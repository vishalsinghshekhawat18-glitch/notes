### 2. Topic & Concept Breakdown

#### TOPIC 21: Part IX & IX-A: Rural & Urban Local Governance (73rd & 74th CAAs)
- `CON-T21-01`: **73rd Constitutional Amendment Act: Panchayati Raj Architecture & 3-Tier Devolution**
  - *Articles:* 243 to 243-O, Eleventh Schedule (29 matters).
  - *Key Doctrines:* Mandatory vs Voluntary provisions, Gram Sabha (registered voters), min 1/3rd women quota, 5-year tenure with 6-month residual term rule, State Election Commission (Art 243K, removed like HC Judge), State Finance Commission (Art 243I), and the 3Fs (Funds, Functions, Functionaries) devolution reality.
- `CON-T21-02`: **PESA Act (1996) & Tribal Self-Governance in Fifth Schedule Areas**
  - *Statutory Basis:* PESA 1996 (Bhuria Committee recommendations) across 10 Fifth Schedule States.
  - *Key Doctrines:* Gram Sabha ownership of Minor Forest Produce (MFP), mandatory prior consultation for land acquisition and mineral leases, anti-alienation powers, and *Niyamgiri Hills (2013)* consent doctrine.
- `CON-T21-03`: **74th Constitutional Amendment Act: Urban Local Bodies & Metropolitan Planning**
  - *Articles:* 243-P to 243-ZG, Twelfth Schedule (18 matters).
  - *Key Doctrines:* 3 municipal tiers (Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation), Wards Committees (population $\ge$ 3 Lakhs), District Planning Committee (Art 243ZD, $\ge$ 4/5th elected members), Metropolitan Planning Committee (Art 243ZE, $\ge$ 2/3rd elected members for population $\ge$ 10 Lakhs).
- `CON-T21-04`: **Local Governance in Rajasthan: Nagaur Inception, 5-Tier Devolution & Rajasthan Acts**
  - *State Landmarks:* Historic Oct 2, 1959 inauguration at Nagaur by PM Jawaharlal Nehru, committee trajectory (Balwant Rai Mehta 1957, Ashok Mehta 1977, GVK Rao 1985, LM Singhvi 1986), Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act 1994, 50% women reservation in PRIs/ULBs, Ward Sabhas, and India's first Gram Nyayalaya at Bassi (Jaipur, 2010).

#### TOPIC 22: Constitutional Institutions: Election Commission of India & Comptroller and Auditor General
- `CON-T22-01`: **Election Commission of India (Article 324): Composition, Independence & CEC/EC Appointment Act 2023**
  - *Constitutional Mandate:* Article 324, multi-member body since 1993 (1 CEC + 2 ECs, equal power under *T.N. Seshan 1995*), removal asymmetry (CEC removed as SC Judge under Art 324(5); ECs removed on CEC recommendation).
  - *Current Law Verification:* *Anoop Baranwal (2023)* interim bench $\rightarrow$ Chief Election Commissioner and other ECs (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act 2023 establishing 3-member Selection Committee (PM, Union Cabinet Minister, Leader of Opposition).
  - *Plenary Powers:* *Mohinder Singh Gill (1978)* inherent executive reservoir.
- `CON-T22-02`: **Electoral Reforms & Representation of the People Acts (1950 & 1951)**
  - *Statutory Division:* RPA 1950 (seats allocation, delimitation, electoral rolls preparation, 18+ voting age) vs RPA 1951 (election conduct, candidate qualifications, Section 8 disqualifications, Section 29A party registration, Section 123 corrupt practices).
  - *Landmark Jurisprudence:* *Lily Thomas (2013)* instant disqualification for 2+ yr sentence; *ADR v. Union of India (2024)* unconstitutionality of Electoral Bonds under Article 19(1)(a).
- `CON-T22-03`: **Comptroller and Auditor-General of India (Articles 148–151): Independence & Duties**
  - *Constitutional Safeguards:* Appointed by warrant and seal, removed as SC Judge, post-retirement job ban, charged salary on CFI.
  - *Audit vs Accounts:* 1976 reform separated Union accounting (now with CGA in Finance Ministry) from audit; CAG maintains state accounts and audits Union/State Consolidated Funds.
  - *Financial Reports:* 3 reports under Article 151 submitted to President $\rightarrow$ examined by Public Accounts Committee (PAC).
- `CON-T22-04`: **Public Audit Architecture & Financial Oversight: Regulatory, Propriety & Performance Audit**
  - *3-Tier Spectrum:* Compliance/Regulatory Audit, Propriety Audit (canons of financial prudence), Performance Audit (3Es: Economy, Efficiency, Effectiveness).
  - *Boundaries:* Post-mortem audit, secret service expenditure limitations, private CA audit for RBI/SBI/LIC, and *Association of Unified Telecom Service Providers (2014)* extending CAG audit to private revenue-sharing entities.

#### TOPIC 23: Statutory & Non-Constitutional Regulatory Authorities: Transparency, Human Rights & Integrity
- `CON-T23-01`: **Central Information Commission (CIC) & RTI Act (2005): Architecture, Exemptions & 2019 Amendments**
  - *Statutory Mandate:* RTI Act 2005 (derived from Art 19(1)(a) in *Raj Narain 1975*), Section 4 proactive disclosures, Section 7 timelines (30 days; 48 hours for life/liberty), Section 8 exemptions & Section 8(2) public interest override.
  - *Current Law Verification:* 2019 RTI Amendment Act removed 5-year tenure and EC salary parity, giving Central Govt power to fix 3-year term and rules.
- `CON-T23-02`: **Central Vigilance Commission (CVC): Santhanam Committee to Statutory Apex Anti-Corruption Body**
  - *Genesis & Act:* Santhanam Committee (1962–64), 1964 executive resolution, *Vineet Narain (1997)* mandate, CVC Act 2003 statutory status.
  - *Tenure & Panel:* 1 CVC + max 2 VCs, 3-member panel (PM, Home Minister, LoP), unique 4-year / 65-year non-renewable tenure.
  - *Superintendence:* Oversees CBI for Prevention of Corruption Act offenses; designated whistleblower authority.
- `CON-T23-03`: **National Human Rights Commission (NHRC): Protection of Human Rights Act 1993 & 2019 Reforms**
  - *Paris Principles:* PHRA 1993, 2019 Amendments (SC Judge eligible as Chair, 3-year / 70-year term renewable).
  - *6-Member Selection Committee:* PM, Speaker LS, Deputy Chairman RS (RS Chairman excluded), Home Minister, LoP LS, LoP RS.
  - *Civil Court Powers & Limits:* 1-year limitation bar (Sec 36(2)), limited military purview (Sec 19), advisory recommendations.
  - *Rajasthan SHRC:* Notified 1999, functional 2000, 1st Chair Justice Kanta Bhatnagar.
- `CON-T23-04`: **Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act (2013): Anti-Corruption Ombudsman & State Ombudsman**
  - *ARC 1966 Genesis:* 1 Chair + max 8 members (50% judicial, 50% diversity quota).
  - *5-Member Panel:* PM, Speaker LS, LoP LS, CJI/nominee, Eminent Jurist.
  - *PM Jurisdiction:* Covered with in-camera 2/3rd full bench safeguards (excluding foreign relations, atomic energy, space, public order).
  - *Rajasthan Lokayukta (1973):* 1st Lokayukta Justice I.D. Dua; **Chief Minister is strictly excluded**.
- `CON-T23-05`: **NITI Aayog vs Planning Commission: Think Tank Architecture & Cooperative Federalism**
  - *Jan 1, 2015 Resolution:* Non-constitutional, non-statutory executive think tank.
  - *Governing Council:* PM + all State CMs + Lt Governors (bottom-up cooperative federalism).
  - *Structural Distinction:* Zero financial allocation powers (transferred to Finance Ministry under Art 282).
  - *Flagship Tools:* Team India Hub, Knowledge & Innovation Hub, SDG India Index, Aspirational Districts Programme.
