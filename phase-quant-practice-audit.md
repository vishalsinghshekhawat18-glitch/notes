## 2. Explanation Quality Audit (The 4 Solved Examples)

The 4 solved examples present in the corpus follow a **3-Tier Pedagogical Model**:
1. **Tier 1 (First Principles / Academic Foundation):** Step-by-step algebraic or fractional derivation.
2. **Tier 2 (Banking Standard / Tabular Extraction):** Standard competitive method (LCM unit method, formula invariant, matrix grid).
3. **Tier 3 (10-Second Mental Elimination / Unit Heuristic):** Rapid examination shortcut (ratio inspection, scaled triple, parity check).

**Pedagogical Evaluation:**
- When fully written, the **3-Tier Model is exceptionally strong**, providing a clear learning bridge from conceptual understanding to speed.
- However, because **84% of the problems (21 out of 25) are empty stubs**, the practice repository cannot be used in its current state without extensive problem creation and complete solution authoring.
