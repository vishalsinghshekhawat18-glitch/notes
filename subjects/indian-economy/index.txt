14:T436,The institutional framework, market failures, and digital unification reforms of agricultural marketing in India. Dissects the traditional state Agricultural Produce Market Committee (APMC) architecture: established under State APMC Acts to protect farmers from moneylender exploitation, which gradually degenerated into monopsonistic cartels characterized by high entry barriers for private buyers, compounding market fees (mandi fees, commission charges), lack of transparency in open auctions, and farm-to-retail price spreads exceeding 60%. Analyzes structural reform pathways: (1) The Model APLM Act, 2017 (proposing unified single state trading licenses, single-point levy of market fee, and establishment of private wholesale markets), (2) The electronic National Agriculture Market (e-NAM, launched in April 2016 by SFAC): a pan-India electronic trading portal integrating physical APMC mandis via transparent online bidding, scientific assaying/grading, and electronic payments, and (3) Development of Gramin Agricultural Markets (GrAMs) as primary aggregation centers.15:T450,The infrastructure economics, quality standards, and financial instruments of post-harvest management in India. Dissects the magnitude and economic cost of post-harvest food losses (estimated by CIPHET at ₹92,000+ Crore annually, with 5%–15% losses in grains and 15%–30% in fruits/vegetables) driven by acute shortages in integrated cold chains, modern silos, and primary pack-houses. Analyzes: (1) The Warehousing (Development and Regulation) Act, 2007 (WDRA): regulating registered warehouses and creating **electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipts (e-NWRs)** issued through digital repositories (NeRL, CCRL) enabling farmers to pledge stored produce as collateral for immediate post-harvest pledge financing from banks, preventing distress sales, (2) The **Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)**: ₹1.00 Lakh Crore financing facility providing 3% interest subvention and CGTMSE credit guarantee for post-harvest farm-gate infrastructure, and (3) Cold-Chain Logistics and the Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY) for mega food parks, integrated cold chains, and agro-processing clusters.16:T45f,The legal entitlements, targeting mechanisms, and digital logistics of India's Public Distribution System (PDS). Traces the historical evolution from Universal PDS (pre-1997) to Targeted PDS (TPDS, 1997: bifurcating BPL and APL households), culminating in the landmark **National Food Security Act, 2013 (NFSA)**. Dissects NFSA statutory architecture: (1) Legally binding entitlement covering **up to 75% of rural population and up to 50% of urban population (~67% of total national population or 81.35 Crore persons)**, (2) Beneficiary categorization: **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** poorest of the poor households (entitled to **35 kg of foodgrains per household per month**) vs **Priority Households (PHH)** (entitled to **5 kg of foodgrains per person per month**), (3) **Women Empowerment Mandate**: eldest woman aged 18+ designated as Head of Household for ration card issuance, (4) Maternity benefit (₹6,000 under PMMVY), and (5) **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)**: enabling nationwide inter-state and intra-state ration portability for migrant workers via biometric Aadhaar authentication (e-PoS devices).17:T426,The microeconomic principles, actuarial pricing, and operational architecture of agricultural risk mitigation in India.
