### 2. Topic & Concept Breakdown

#### TOPIC 24: Rajasthan State Polity: Executive, Assembly, Demography & Apex Institutions
- `CON-T24-01`: **Political Demography, Electoral Geography & Social Stratification of Rajasthan**
  - *Census 2011 Baseline Facts:* Total population 6.85 Crore, density 200/sq km (Jaisalmer 17 vs Jaipur 595), sex ratio 928 (Dungarpur 994 vs Dholpur 846), child sex ratio 888, literacy 66.11% (Kota 76.6% vs Jalore 54.9%; female literacy 52.12%), Scheduled Castes 17.83% (Ganganagar highest), Scheduled Tribes 13.48% (Banswara 76.4% highest).
  - *Electoral Geography:* 200 Vidhan Sabha constituencies (141 General, 34 SC, 25 ST); 25 Lok Sabha constituencies (18 General, 4 SC: Bharatpur, Bikaner, Ganganagar, Karauli-Dholpur; 3 ST: Banswara, Dausa, Udaipur); 10 Rajya Sabha seats.
  - *5 Regional Divisions:* Marwar (Desert), Mewar/Vagad (Tribal/TSP), Dhundhar/Matsya (Eastern Plains), Hadoti (Plateau), Shekhawati (North-Central).
- `CON-T24-02`: **State Executive of Rajasthan: Governor, Chief Minister & Council of Ministers**
  - *Gubernatorial Inception:* Office of Governor created on 1 November 1956 via 7th CAA 1956 / State Reorganisation Act, replacing the office of *Rajpramukh* (Maharaja Sawai Man Singh II); First Governor: Sardar Gurumukh Nihal Singh; First woman Governor: Pratibha Patil; Four Governors who died in office: Darbara Singh (1998), Nirmal Chandra Jain (2003), S.K. Singh (2009), Prabha Rau (2010); 4 President's Rule invocations: 1967, 1977, 1980, 1992.
  - *Chief Ministership:* First nominated CM Pandit Hiralal Shastri (1949); First elected CM Tikaram Paliwal (1952); Jai Narayan Vyas (both nominated and elected); Mohan Lal Sukhadia (longest serving, ~17 years, 1954–1971); Barkatullah Khan (only CM dying in office); Vasundhara Raje (first woman CM).
  - *Cabinet Ceiling:* Article 164(1A) 91st CAA limits Rajasthan Council of Ministers to max 15% (30 Ministers) and min 12 Ministers.
- `CON-T24-03`: **Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha): Evolution, Committees & Unicameral Procedure**
  - *Seat Expansion Trajectory:* 1st Assembly (1952) = 160 seats $\rightarrow$ 2nd Assembly (1957) = 176 seats $\rightarrow$ 4th Assembly (1967) = 184 seats $\rightarrow$ 6th Assembly (1977 onwards) = **200 seats** (delimited on 1971 census).
  - *First Assembly Officers:* Speaker Narottam Lal Joshi, Deputy Speaker Lal Singh Shaktawat, Leader of Opposition Jaswant Singh.
  - *4 Financial Standing Committees:* Public Accounts Committee (PAC), Estimates Committee 'A', Estimates Committee 'B', Public Undertakings Committee (PUC).
- `CON-T24-04`: **Judicial & Constitutional Bodies of Rajasthan: High Court (Jodhpur/Jaipur) & RPSC (Ajmer)**
  - *Rajasthan High Court (Article 214):* Inaugurated 29 August 1949 at Jodhpur by Rajpramukh Sawai Man Singh; First CJ Justice Kamala Kant Verma; Satyanarayan Rao Committee (1957) $\rightarrow$ Jaipur Permanent Bench established 31 January 1977; Sanctioned strength of 50 Judges (38 Permanent + 12 Additional).
  - *RPSC (Article 315):* Constituted 20 August 1949 at Jaipur $\rightarrow$ shifted to Ajmer in 1956; First Chairman Sir S.K. Ghosh; Composition: 1 Chairman + 7 Members (Total 8); 6-year or 62-year tenure.
  - *Critical Removal Asymmetry (Article 317):* Appointed by the **Governor**, but removable ONLY by the **President of India** after a Supreme Court reference report!

#### TOPIC 25: Comparative Constitutional Systems: UK, USA, Canada, Germany & Switzerland
- `CON-T25-01`: **Constitutional Models & Sovereignty: UK Parliamentary Sovereignty vs USA Judicial Supremacy vs Indian Synthesis**
  - *UK Model:* Uncodified constitution, Dicey's Parliamentary Sovereignty, fusion of executive and legislature, zero judicial invalidation of primary statutes.
  - *US Model:* Written rigid constitution (1787), strict separation of powers (Montesquieu), substantive Due Process of Law (*Marbury v. Madison 1803*).
  - *Indian Synthesis:* Constitutional Supremacy combining the British responsible parliamentary executive with American judicial review, harmonized through the indigenous **Basic Structure Doctrine** (*Kesavananda Bharati 1973*) and procedural-substantive justice under Article 21 (*Maneka Gandhi 1978*).
- `CON-T25-02`: **Comparative Federal Architectures: USA, Canada, Australia, Germany & Indian Asymmetric Federalism**
  - *USA:* Bottom-up aggregation, Indestructible Union of Indestructible States, 10th Amendment state residuary powers, dual citizenship.
  - *Canada:* Top-down federation with strong centre, central residuary powers (Sec 91 borrowed in Art 248), centrally appointed Governors (borrowed in Art 155).
  - *Australia:* Concurrent list, joint sittings (Art 108), inter-state trade freedom (Arts 301–307).
  - *Germany:* Administrative federalism (Länder civil services execute federal statutes), Bundesrat upper house composed of state cabinet delegates voting en bloc.
  - *India:* Quasi-federal "Indestructible Union of Destructible States" (Art 3), single citizenship, asymmetric federalism (Art 371A-J, 5th/6th Schedules).
- `CON-T25-03`: **Instruments of Direct Democracy & Plural Executive: The Swiss Constitutional Model**
  - *Direct Democracy:* Mandatory Referendum (double majority for constitutional changes), Optional Referendum (50,000 signatures challenging laws), Popular Initiative (100,000 signatures proposing constitutional amendments), Recall, Landsgemeinde open-air cantonal assemblies.
  - *Plural Executive:* 7-member collegial Federal Council (Bundesrat) with a 1-year rotating Federal President (*primus inter pares*), operating with zero extra executive power.
