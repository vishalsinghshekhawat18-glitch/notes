## 3. Topic-by-Topic Breakdown of Batch 3

### Topic 40: Financial System & Financial Intermediation (Order 40)
- **`CON-ECO-33`**: The Economics of Financial Intermediation: Asymmetric Information, Agency Friction & Transformation Mechanisms
  - *Core Ideas*: Saver $\ne$ Borrower frictions, Adverse Selection (Akerlof 1970 ex-ante lemon problem), Moral Hazard (Diamond 1984 ex-post agency friction), Delegated Monitoring, The 3 Transformations (Maturity, Liquidity, Risk), Diamond-Dybvig (1983) bank run coordination failure.
- **`CON-ECO-34`**: Structure of the Financial System: Direct vs Indirect Finance, Bank-Based vs Market-Based Architectures & Financial Markets
  - *Core Ideas*: Direct (capital markets) vs Indirect (intermediated banking), Comparative institutional tradeoffs, Money Market ($\le 1$ yr under RBI) vs Capital Market ($> 1$ yr under SEBI), Primary Market (capital formation) vs Secondary Market (liquidity provider).

### Topic 41: Commercial Banking: Structure, Functions & Balance Sheet (Order 41)
- **`CON-ECO-35`**: Commercial Bank Functions: Statutory Definition, Deposit Typology, CASA Ratio & Credit Facilities
  - *Core Ideas*: Section 5(b) & Section 6 Banking Regulation Act 1949, Deposit hierarchy (Demand: Current [0% interest] + Savings; Time: Fixed + Recurring), CASA ratio impact on Cost of Funds and Net Interest Margin (NIM), Funded (Cash Credit, Overdraft, Term Loans) vs Non-Funded contingent facilities (Letters of Credit, Bank Guarantees).
- **`CON-ECO-36`**: The Commercial Bank Balance Sheet: Assets, Liabilities, Net Worth & Asset-Liability Management (ALM)
  - *Core Ideas*: Form A 12-Schedule accounting (S1-5 Liabilities, S6-11 Assets, S12 Contingent Liabilities), Bank Capital as equity buffer, Structural Liquidity Gaps across time buckets, Net Interest Income (NII) sensitivity to interest rate shifts.

### Topic 42: Indian Banking Institutional Architecture & Typology (Order 42)
- **`CON-ECO-37`**: The Commercial Banking Landscape: Scheduled Banks (Second Schedule), PSBs, Private Banks & Foreign Bank Modes
  - *Core Ideas*: Section 42(6)(a) RBI Act 1934 criteria (Paid-up capital $\ge$ ₹5 Lakh, depositor safety), 12 Public Sector Banks (SBI under SBI Act 1955 + 11 Nationalized Banks under 1970/1980 Acts), Old vs New Private Banks, Foreign Bank Branch vs Wholly Owned Subsidiary (WOS) mode.
- **`CON-ECO-38`**: Differentiated Banking Architecture: Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), Small Finance Banks (SFBs) & Payments Banks
  - *Core Ideas*: Nachiket Mor Committee (2014), SFBs (75% PSL target, 50% loans $\le$ ₹25 Lakh, 15% CRAR), Payments Banks (Demand deposits up to ₹2 Lakh, ATM/debit cards, strictly prohibited from lending/credit cards, 75% in G-Secs/T-Bills), RRBs (RRB Act 1976, 50:15:35 equity structure, 75% PSL target).
- **`CON-ECO-39`**: Cooperative Banking Architecture: Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs), The 3-Tier Rural Cooperative Structure & Dual Regulation Reforms
  - *Core Ideas*: Democratic "one member, one vote" principle, Short-term rural 3-tier pyramid (PACS at village $\to$ DCCBs at district $\to$ StCBs at apex state level), UCB 4 tiers under N.S. Vishwanathan Committee (Tier 1: $\le$ ₹100 Cr, 9% CRAR; Tiers 2-4: 12% CRAR), Banking Regulation (Amendment) Act 2020 resolving dual regulation gridlock.

### Topic 43: RBI Regulation & Banking Supervision (Order 43)
- **`CON-ECO-40`**: Statutory & Regulatory Architecture: Banking Regulation Act, 1949, Licensing, Ownership Caps & Board Governance
  - *Core Ideas*: Section 22 licensing, Section 35A binding directions, Section 36AA/36AAA managerial removal & board supersession, Section 12(2) voting rights cap (26%), 15-year promoter dilution glidepath (26%), MD/CEO max tenure (15 years) and age limit (70 years), Fit & Proper criteria.
- **`CON-ECO-41`**: Banking Supervision & Enforcement: CAMELS Rating Model, Risk-Based Supervision (RBS) & The Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) Framework
  - *Core Ideas*: Board for Financial Supervision (BFS 1994), CAMELS model (Capital, Asset Quality, Management, Earnings, Liquidity, Systems), Revised PCA Framework (2022: Capital [CET1/CRAR], Asset Quality [Net NPA $\ge 6\%$], Leverage Ratio; RoA removed), Mandatory sanctions (dividend ban, branch freeze).

### Topic 44: Bank Capital, Basel Framework, NPAs & Asset Quality (Order 44)
- **`CON-ECO-42`**: Bank Capital Adequacy & The Basel III Framework: Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA), Capital Tiers, CRAR & Buffers
  - *Core Ideas*: Equity buffer theory, Capital Tiers (CET1 5.5%, AT1 1.5%, Tier 2 2.0%), Minimum CRAR in India (9% + 2.5% CCB = 11.5%; 12% for PSBs), Capital Conservation Buffer (2.5% pure CET1), Leverage Ratio (3.5% min / 4.0% for D-SIBs), Countercyclical Capital Buffer (CCyB: 0–2.5%), RWA credit/market/operational calculations.
- **`CON-ECO-43`**: Non-Performing Assets (NPAs): Asset Classification Stages, Special Mention Accounts (SMA), Provisioning Norms & PCR
  - *Core Ideas*: 90-day overdue rule (Agri: 2 crop seasons short, 1 season long), Early warning tiers (SMA-0: 1–30d, SMA-1: 31–60d, SMA-2: 61–90d), Asset stages (Sub-Standard $\le 12$ mos: 15% sec / 25% unsec; Doubtful D1 25%/100%, D2 40%/100%, D3 100%; Loss: 100%), Gross vs Net NPA formulas, Provisioning Coverage Ratio (PCR).
- **`CON-ECO-44`**: Stressed Asset Resolution Architecture: SARFAESI Act, 2002, The Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC, 2016) & NARCL (Bad Bank)
  - *Core Ideas*: SARFAESI Section 13 notice (60 days, no court intervention, excludes agricultural land and loans < ₹1 Lakh), IBC 2016 CIRP (180+90 days, CoC 66% vote, Section 14 moratorium, Section 29A disqualification, Section 53 liquidation waterfall), RBI June 7 2019 Circular (ICA: 75% value / 60% number), NARCL-IDRCL Bad Bank (15:85 cash/SR structure with 5-year sovereign guarantee).

### Topic 45: Banking Reforms & Structural Evolution in India (Order 45)
- **`CON-ECO-45`**: Historical Evolution & Structural Reforms: Bank Nationalization, Priority Sector Lending (PSL) & Narasimham Committees
  - *Core Ideas*: 1969 Nationalization (14 banks, $\ge$ ₹50 Cr) & 1980 Nationalization (6 banks, $\ge$ ₹200 Cr), PSL Master Direction (40% ANBC for domestic/foreign $\ge 20$ branches, 75% for RRBs/SFBs; Agriculture 18%, SMF 10%, Micro 7.5%, Weaker Sections 12%), PSLC trading on e-Kuber, RIDF shortfall deposits with NABARD, Narasimham I (1991) & II (1998).
- **`CON-ECO-46`**: Modern Banking Sector Reforms: Indradhanush Mission, PSB Mega-Mergers, EASE Reforms & FSIB Governance
  - *Core Ideas*: Mission Indradhanush (7 pillars: A to G), PSB consolidation (reducing 27 PSBs to 12 anchor institutions), EASE Agenda (EASE 1.0 to EASE 6.0 digital transformation), Financial Services Institutions Bureau (FSIB, established July 2022 replacing BBB).

### Topic 46: Financial Inclusion, Digital Banking & Payment Systems (Order 46)
- **`CON-ECO-47`**: Financial Inclusion Architecture: Access vs Usage vs Quality, PMJDY, Business Correspondents & The JAM Trinity
  - *Core Ideas*: RBI FI-Index (Access 35%, Usage 45%, Quality 20%), PMJDY (Zero balance BSBDA, RuPay debit card with ₹2 Lakh accident cover, ₹10,000 Overdraft), Business Correspondent (BC / Bank Mitra) Micro-ATM model, Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) & JAM Trinity (Jan Dhan, Aadhaar, Mobile).
- **`CON-ECO-48`**: Payment & Settlement Systems: PSSA 2007, NPCI Architecture, UPI Ecosystem & Wholesale Settlement (RTGS vs NEFT)
  - *Core Ideas*: Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2007 (RBI as regulator), NPCI (Section 8 not-for-profit company), UPI, IMPS (max ₹5 Lakh), AePS, NACH, BBPS, Wholesale settlement: RTGS (Gross real-time, min ₹2 Lakh, no ceiling, 24x7) vs NEFT (Deferred Net Settlement in 48 half-hourly batches, no min/max limit, 24x7).

### Topic 47: Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) & Shadow Banking (Order 47)
- **`CON-ECO-49`**: Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs): Statutory Architecture, Bank vs NBFC Bifurcation & Shadow Banking Risks
  - *Core Ideas*: Chapter III-B RBI Act 1934 (Section 45-IA, NOF $\ge$ ₹10 Crore), 3 Core Legal Differences (No demand deposits, cannot issue cheques/participate in clearing, no DICGC insurance), Zero CRR, ALM maturity mismatch, 2018 IL&FS liquidity contagion.
- **`CON-ECO-50`**: Scale-Based Regulation (SBR) Framework for NBFCs & Specialized Typologies (HFCs, ARCs, MFIs)
  - *Core Ideas*: SBR 4 Tiers (Base Layer $< ₹1,000$ Cr; Middle Layer $\ge ₹1,000$ Cr, ALL deposit-taking NBFCs, HFCs; Upper Layer top 10-15 with 9% CET1 and 3-year mandatory listing; Top Layer empty), Housing Finance Companies (HFCs under RBI since 2019), ARCs (NOF ₹300 Cr), Harmonized Microfinance 2022 (Household income $\le$ ₹3,00,000, 50% repayment limit).

### Topic 48: Financial Stability, Banking Crises, Safety Nets & AIFIs (Order 48)
- **`CON-ECO-51`**: Financial Stability Framework: Systemic Risk, Solvency vs Liquidity Risk, FSDC & Macro-Stress Testing
  - *Core Ideas*: Solvency Risk (Net worth $< 0$) vs Liquidity Risk (Cash timing crunch), Bagehot's Dictum (LOLR strictly for solvent illiquid banks), FSDC (Apex Council chaired by Union Finance Minister; Sub-Committee chaired by RBI Governor), RBI Financial Stability Report (biannual macro-stress testing).
- **`CON-ECO-52`**: Banking Safety Nets: Lender of Last Resort (LOLR), DICGC Deposit Insurance & Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs)
  - *Core Ideas*: LOLR under Sections 17 & 18 RBI Act, DICGC Act 1961 (₹5 Lakh insurance per depositor per bank in same right/capacity; 90-day mandatory payout under 2021 Amendment), D-SIBs (SBI Bucket 4 $+0.80\%$ CET1, HDFC Bank Bucket 2 $+0.40\%$ CET1, ICICI Bank Bucket 1 $+0.20\%$ CET1).
- **`CON-ECO-53`**: All-India Financial Institutions (AIFIs) & Specialized Development Finance: NABARD, SIDBI, NHB, EXIM Bank, NaBFID & IFSCA
  - *Core Ideas*: 5 AIFIs regulated under Section 45L of RBI Act (NABARD [1982, Sivaraman Comm], SIDBI [1990], NHB [1988], EXIM Bank [1982], NaBFID [2021 DFI for National Infrastructure Pipeline]), IFSCA (IFSCA Act 2019 at GIFT City Gandhinagar, single unified offshore regulator).
