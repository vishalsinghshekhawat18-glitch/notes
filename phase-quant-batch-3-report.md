## 2. Reusable Quant Error & Examiner Trap Bank

| Trap Category | Examiner Trap Mechanism | Concrete Example & Mathematical Risk | Remedial Solving Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Percentage Base Error** | Applying percentage change to SP or wrong CP base. | $20\%$ profit on Article 1 and $20\%$ loss on Article 2 with identical SP ($₹9,600$). Thinking "no profit no loss". | Use $(x/10)^2\%$ Loss formula ($4\%$ loss $= ₹800$). Never add percentages across different base values. |
| **Fraction Conversion Error** | Approximating non-terminating percentages in decimal form. | Computing $14.28\%$ as $0.14 \times 4900 = 686$ instead of $1/7 \times 4900 = 700$. | Always use canonical fraction table ($1/1$ to $1/25$). |
| **Alternate Leak Threshold Error** | Linearly extrapolating cycle rates through full capacity. | Inflow $3\text{ u/h}$, Leak $2\text{ u/h}$, Capacity $30\text{ u}$. Calculating $30 \times 2 = 60\text{ hours}$. | Terminate cycle iterations at $V_{\text{threshold}} = \text{Capacity} - \text{Inflow} = 27\text{ u}$. Tank fills in $55\text{ hours}$. |
| **Moving Observer Train Length Error** | Adding lengths of both trains when crossing a passenger. | Passing train $180\text{m}$, passenger's train $120\text{m}$. Setting distance to $300\text{m}$. | Passenger is a point object: Distance $= L_1 = 180\text{m}$ only. |
| **Dual Pie Chart Base Mismatch** | Comparing sector percentages directly without total expenditure scaling. | Company Alpha $22.5\%$ vs $18\%$. Concluding $5:4$ ratio without weighting base expenditures ($₹80\text{Cr}$ vs $₹120\text{Cr}$). | Multiply percentage by total base: $80(0.225) : 120(0.18) = 18 : 21.6 = 5:6$. |
| **Data Sufficiency Over-Calculation** | Calculating exact numerical values when uniqueness is already proven. | Finding exact roots in quadratic DS instead of checking discriminant and uniqueness of signs. | Solve for sufficiency (unique YES or unique NO), never calculate beyond uniqueness. |
| **Crossroad Overlap Omission** | Summing rectangular strip areas without subtracting central intersection square. | $60\text{m} \times 40\text{m}$ lawn with $2\text{m}$ roads. Calculating $2(60) + 2(40) = 200\text{ m}^2$. | Use $w(l+b-w) = 2(60+40-2) = 196\text{ m}^2$. Subtract $w^2 = 4\text{ m}^2$ once. |
