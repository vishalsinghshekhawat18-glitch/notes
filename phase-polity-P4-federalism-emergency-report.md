## 2. Canonical Concepts Created (9 Concepts across Topics 19 & 20)

### Topic 19: Part XI & XII — Centre–State Relations & Federal Architecture (`centre-state-relations-and-federalism`, Order: 19)
1. **`CON-T19-01`**: *Nature of Indian Federalism: Quasi-Federal Character & Asymmetric Provisions*
   - Article 1 "Union of States" (indestructible union of destructible states; no right to secede; Dr. Ambedkar's rationale); K.C. Wheare's "quasi-federal" classification and Granville Austin's "cooperative federalism"; Unitary/centripetal features (single constitution, integrated judiciary, Governor, All India Services, emergency provisions); Asymmetric Federalism via special provisions (**Articles 371 to 371-J** for Maharashtra, Gujarat, Nagaland, Assam, Manipur, AP/Telangana, Sikkim, Mizoram, Arunachal, Goa, Karnataka) and 5th/6th Schedules; Federalism recognized as an inviolable **Basic Structure** doctrine (*S.R. Bommai 1994*).
2. **`CON-T19-02`**: *Legislative Relations: Three Lists (7th Schedule), Residuary Powers & Exceptions*
   - Article 245 territorial legislative competence and extra-territorial reach of Parliament (Doctrine of Territorial Nexus in *R.M.D. Chamarbaugwala 1957*); Article 246 Seventh Schedule (Union List 100, State List 61, Concurrent List 52; 42nd CAA 1976 transfer of 5 subjects); Article 248 Residuary Powers exclusively in Parliament; Five extraordinary circumstances for Parliamentary law on State List (**Article 249** RS 2/3rd resolution, **Article 250** National Emergency, **Article 252** Consent of 2+ States where **states surrender amending/repealing power**, **Article 253** International Treaties, **Article 356** President's Rule); Article 254 Repugnancy and Article 254(2) Presidential assent exception; Doctrines of *Pith and Substance* (*Prafulla Kumar 1947*), *Colourable Legislation* (*Gajapati Narayan Deo 1953*), and *Severability*.
3. **`CON-T19-03`**: *Administrative Relations: Directives (Arts 256-257), Sanctions (Art 365) & All India Services*
   - Articles 256 and 257 Union executive directives to States (compliance with Union laws, railways, communications of national/military importance, mother tongue primary instruction under Art 350A, ST schemes under Art 339(2)); **Article 365** sanction: non-compliance deemed breakdown of constitutional machinery enabling Article 356; **Article 355** duty of Union to protect States against external aggression and internal disturbance; **Article 312** All India Services (Rajya Sabha 2/3rd resolution creates new AIS, e.g. IFoS 1966; dual control); Inter-State River Water Disputes under **Article 262** and 1956 Act (ouster of Supreme Court jurisdiction).
4. **`CON-T19-04`**: *Inter-State Council (Art 263), Zonal Councils & Federal Commissions*
   - **Inter-State Council (Article 263)**: Constitutional body established in 1990 by Presidential Order on Sarkaria Commission advice (chaired by Prime Minister, all CMs, 6 Union Cabinet Ministers); **Zonal Councils**: Statutory bodies under *States Reorganisation Act 1956* (5 zones) + North-Eastern Council (1971), with **Union Home Minister as common Chairman** (Rajasthan in Northern Zone); Landmark Federal Commissions: **Sarkaria Commission (1983–88)** (Art 356 as last resort, outside Governor), **Punchhi Commission (2007–10)** (Localized emergency under Arts 355/356, fixed 5-yr Governor tenure), and **NCRWC (2002)**.
5. **`CON-T19-05`**: *Financial Relations: Division of Taxes, Finance Commission (Art 280) & GST Council*
   - Vertical fiscal imbalance; 101st CAA 2016 (Article 246A concurrent GST power and **Article 279A GST Council** weighted voting formula: **Union 1/3rd + States 2/3rd, requiring 3/4th (75%) weighted approval**, creating mutual federal veto); **Article 280 Finance Commission** (every 5 years, Chairman + 4 members; vertical divisible pool sharing + horizontal distribution criteria); Grants-in-Aid: **Statutory Grants (Article 275)** on FC recommendation charged on CFI vs **Discretionary Grants (Article 282)**; Borrowing: Union (Art 292) vs States (Art 293: **States cannot borrow abroad and require Centre consent if outstanding central debt exists**).

### Topic 20: Part XVIII — Emergency Provisions & Constitutional Safeguards (`emergency-provisions-and-safeguards`, Order: 20)
6. **`CON-T20-01`**: *National Emergency (Article 352): Grounds, Approval & 44th CAA Safeguards*
   - Part XVIII emergency architecture; Grounds: War, External Aggression, Armed Rebellion (**"internal disturbance" deleted by 44th CAA 1978**); Mandatory written communication of the **Union Cabinet** (not PM alone); Parliamentary approval within **1 Month** (down from 2 months) by **Special Majority** in both Houses (majority total membership + 2/3rd present and voting); Periodic renewal every **6 months**; Disapproval motion in **Lok Sabha only by Simple Majority** (1/10th members requisition special sitting within 14 days).
7. **`CON-T20-02`**: *National Emergency Impact: Federal Balance & Fundamental Rights (Art 358 vs Art 359)*
   - Federal effects: Union directs States on ANY matter; Parliament acquires concurrent power over State List (State governments and assemblies NOT dissolved); Lok Sabha term extendable by law for 1 year at a time (max 6m after emergency); **Article 358 vs Article 359 comparison**: Article 358 **automatically suspends Article 19 freedoms ONLY during External Emergency** (War/Aggression, NOT Armed Rebellion); Article 359 suspends court enforcement of specified FRs by Presidential Order, but **Articles 20 (Protection in conviction) and 21 (Life and liberty) CAN NEVER BE SUSPENDED under any emergency** (44th CAA 1978).
8. **`CON-T20-03`**: *President's Rule (Articles 356 & 365): Grounds, Procedure & S.R. Bommai Doctrine*
   - Breakdown of constitutional machinery (Art 356 on Governor report or *suo motu*; Art 365 non-compliance with central directions); Parliamentary approval within **2 Months** by **Simple Majority**; Valid for 6 months, ordinary max 1 year; Extendable up to **max 3 years ONLY IF National Emergency is on AND Election Commission certifies difficulty in holding elections** (44th CAA); State executive dismissed, assembly suspended/dissolved; Landmark ***S.R. Bommai v. Union of India (1994)*** 9-judge bench principles: Proclamation subject to judicial review, burden on Centre, **Assembly CANNOT be dissolved before Parliamentary approval**, **floor test is sole test of majority**, secularism is basic structure, and **court has power to revive dissolved assembly and restore dismissed ministry**.
9. **`CON-T20-04`**: *Financial Emergency (Article 360): Invocation, Indefinite Duration & Distinctive Powers*
   - Proclaimed on threat to financial stability or credit of India; Parliamentary approval within **2 Months** by **Simple Majority**; **⚠️ CRITICAL INDEFINITE DURATION RULE**: Once approved, **it continues indefinitely without periodic parliamentary approval** until revoked by President; Powers: directions to observe canons of financial propriety, **reduction of salaries of all public officials INCLUDING Supreme Court and High Court Judges**, reservation of all state money bills for President; **Never declared in India**.

## 5. Outdated Legacy Claims Corrected & Misconception Shields

1. **Internal Disturbance vs Armed Rebellion**: Eliminated outdated legacy references to "internal disturbance" as a ground for National Emergency, clarifying that the 44th CAA 1978 substituted "armed rebellion".
2. **Cabinet Written Advice**: Clarified that the President acts only on the **written communication of the full Union Cabinet**, preventing unilateral Prime Ministerial declarations (as occurred in 1975).
3. **Article 358 vs 359 Scope**: Shielded the misconception that Article 19 is suspended during all emergencies (it is auto-suspended under Art 358 **only during External Emergencies**, not Armed Rebellion), and reinforced that Articles 20 and 21 are **strictly non-derogable under Article 359**.
4. **Article 252 Surrender of Power**: Emphasized the critical UPSC trap that once state legislatures request Parliament to legislate on a State subject under Article 252, **states surrender their amending/repealing power to Parliament**.
5. **GST Council Decision Threshold**: Corrected common errors by confirming that decisions require a **3/4th (75%) weighted majority**, with Centre commanding 1/3rd (33.33%) and States commanding 2/3rd (66.67%).
6. **Financial Emergency Duration**: Highlighted the unique constitutional rule that **Article 360 continues indefinitely once approved**, requiring zero periodic renewals.
7. **Article 356 Dissolution Safeguard**: Reinforced *S.R. Bommai* rule that a State Assembly can only be suspended initially and **cannot be dissolved until Parliament approves the proclamation**.
