11:T5d0,### Concave Mirror: All 6 Object Positions & Image Characteristics ($f = R/2$)

| Object Position | Image Position | Image Nature | Image Size / Magnification | Everyday Technological Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **At Infinity** | At Focus ($F$) | Real & Inverted | Point-sized / Highly diminished ($m \ll -1$) | Solar furnace heat concentrator. |
| **Beyond $C$** | Between $F$ and $C$ | Real & Inverted | Diminished ($|m| < 1$) | Optical sensors. |
| **At Centre of Curvature ($C$)** | **At $C$** | **Real & Inverted** | **Same size as object ($m = -1$)** | Standard 1:1 image projection. |
| **Between $C$ and $F$** | Beyond $C$ | Real & Inverted | Enlarged ($|m| > 1$) | Cinema projectors. |
| **At Focus ($F$)** | At Infinity | Real & Inverted | Infinitely large ($m \to -\infty$) | **Searchlights, torches, car headlights** (bulb at focus produces parallel beam). |
| **Between Pole ($P$) and Focus ($F$)** | **Behind Mirror** | **Virtual & Erect** | **Enlarged / Magnified ($m > +1$)** | **Dentist mirror, Shaving mirror, Makeup mirror**. |
