### Agricultural Loan NPA Rules
- **Short Duration Crops** (Paddy, Wheat): NPA if installment is unpaid for **two crop seasons**.
- **Long Duration Crops** (Sugarcane, Tea): NPA if installment is unpaid for **one crop season**.12:T465,To evaluate a bank's true balance sheet health, analysts look at Gross NPA, Net NPA, and PCR:

### 1. Gross NPA vs Net NPA
- **Gross NPA (GNPA)**: The total face value of all loans classified as NPAs on the bank's balance sheet.
$$\text{GNPA Ratio} = \left(\frac{\text{Gross NPAs}}{\text{Gross Total Advances}}\right) \times 100$$
- **Net NPA (NNPA)**: The real residual default risk remaining on the bank's books after deducting loan loss provisions, claims received from ECGC/DICGC, and partial write-offs:
$$\text{Net NPA} = \text{Gross NPA} - (\text{Cumulative Provisions} + \text{ECGC/DICGC Claims} + \text{Interest in Suspense})$$
$$\text{NNPA Ratio} = \left(\frac{\text{Net NPAs}}{\text{Net Total Advances}}\right) \times 100$$

### 2. Provisioning Coverage Ratio (PCR)
$$\text{PCR} = \left(\frac{\text{Total Loan Loss Provisions Held}}{\text{Gross NPAs}}\right) \times 100$$
- **Why PCR is Vital**: A high PCR (e.g., $75\% - 85\%$) means the bank has already set aside cash from past profits to absorb $80\%$ of its bad loans. Even if borrowers default completely, future bank profits and capital will not be damaged.13:T417,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: SMA-0 vs SMA-1 vs SMA-2 Days**:
  - SMA-0 = **1 to 30 days** overdue.
  - SMA-1 = **31 to 60 days** overdue.
  - SMA-2 = **61 to 90 days** overdue.
  - NPA = **Over 90 days** overdue.
- **Trap 2: Sub-Standard Duration**: An asset remains Sub-Standard for **up to 12 months** from the date it became an NPA. On Day 366 as an NPA, it automatically downgrades to **Doubtful (D1)**.
- **Trap 3: Agricultural Crop Seasons**: Short duration crops require **2 crop seasons**; Long duration crops require **1 crop season** to become an NPA.
- **Trap 4: Doubtful Asset Provisioning on Unsecured Portion**: In Doubtful assets (D1, D2, D3), the **unsecured portion is ALWAYS provisioned at 100%**, regardless of whether it is D1, D2, or D3!
