13:T530,### Comprehensive Matrix: Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic Cells

| Cellular Dimension | Prokaryotic Cell (e.g. Bacteria, Blue-Green Algae) | Eukaryotic Cell (e.g. Amoeba, Fungi, Plants, Animals) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cell Size** | Small ($mathbf{1 - 10 \, \mu\text{m}}$) | Large ($mathbf{10 - 100 \, \mu\text{m}}$) |
| **Nuclear Organization**| **NO Nuclear Membrane**; naked circular DNA in **NUCLEOID**. | **True Nucleus** enclosed by double nuclear envelope with pores. |
| **Chromosome Count** | **Single circular chromosome** (Plasmids also present). | **Multiple linear chromosomes** with histone proteins. |
| **Membrane-Bound Organelles**| **STRICTLY ABSENT** (No Mitochondria, ER, Golgi, Lysosomes).| **PRESENT** (Mitochondria, ER, Golgi, Lysosomes, Vacuoles). |
| **Ribosome Subunit Type**| **70S Ribosomes** ($50S + 30S$ subunits). | **80S Cytoplasmic Ribosomes** ($60S + 40S$ subunits). |
| **Cell Division Mode** | Simple **Binary Fission** or Budding (No spindle fibers).| **Mitosis** and **Meiosis** with mitotic spindle. |
| **Cell Wall Chemistry** | Contains **Peptidoglycan (Murein)** in bacteria. | **Cellulose** in plants, **Chitin** in fungi, absent in animals. |
| **Photosynthetic Pigments**| Associated with membranous vesicles/lamellae (No plastids).| Enclosed inside membrane-bound **Chloroplasts**.
