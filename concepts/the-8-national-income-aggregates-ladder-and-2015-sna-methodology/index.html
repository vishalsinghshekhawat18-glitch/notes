The 8 National Income Aggregates Ladder & 2015 SNA Methodology

The mathematical conversion step-ladder connecting the 8 macroeconomic aggregates (GDP, NDP, GNP, NNP) across Gross/Net, Domestic/National, and Market Price/Factor Cost switches, and the MoSPI 2015 SNA methodology.

Intuition & Context

The 3 Binary Switches of National Accounting

National income accounting is governed by three universal mathematical switches:

  1. The Depreciation Switch (Gross vs Net):
    Net=Gross−Consumption of Fixed Capital (Depreciation)\text{Net} = \text{Gross} - \text{Consumption of Fixed Capital (Depreciation)}Net=Gross−Consumption of Fixed Capital (Depreciation)
  2. The Geographic Switch (Domestic vs National):
    National=Domestic+Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA)\text{National} = \text{Domestic} + \text{Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA)}National=Domestic+Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA)
    NFIA=Factor Income earned by Indian residents abroad−Factor Income earned by foreigners in India\text{NFIA} = \text{Factor Income earned by Indian residents abroad} - \text{Factor Income earned by foreigners in India}NFIA=Factor Income earned by Indian residents abroad−Factor Income earned by foreigners in India
  3. The Government Tax Switch (Factor Cost vs Market Price):
    Market Price=Factor Cost+Net Indirect Taxes (NIT)\text{Market Price} = \text{Factor Cost} + \text{Net Indirect Taxes (NIT)}Market Price=Factor Cost+Net Indirect Taxes (NIT)
    NIT=Indirect Taxes (GST, Excise, Customs)−Government Subsidies\text{NIT} = \text{Indirect Taxes (GST, Excise, Customs)} - \text{Government Subsidies}NIT=Indirect Taxes (GST, Excise, Customs)−Government Subsidies
Core Principle

The 8 Macroeconomic Aggregates Master Ladder

Using these 3 switches, we derive the 8 Canonical Macroeconomic Aggregates:

The 8 Step-Ladder Equations:

  1. GDPMP\text{GDP}_{\text{MP}}GDPMP​ = Gross Domestic Product at Market Price (Gross value of final goods produced within domestic territory).
  2. NDPMP=GDPMP−Depreciation\text{NDP}{\text{MP}} = \text{GDP}{\text{MP}} - \text{Depreciation}NDPMP​=GDPMP​−Depreciation
  3. GNPMP=GDPMP+NFIA\text{GNP}{\text{MP}} = \text{GDP}{\text{MP}} + \text{NFIA}GNPMP​=GDPMP​+NFIA
  4. NNPMP=GNPMP−Depreciation=NDPMP+NFIA\text{NNP}{\text{MP}} = \text{GNP}{\text{MP}} - \text{Depreciation} = \text{NDP}_{\text{MP}} + \text{NFIA}NNPMP​=GNPMP​−Depreciation=NDPMP​+NFIA
  5. GDPFC=GDPMP−NIT\text{GDP}{\text{FC}} = \text{GDP}{\text{MP}} - \text{NIT}GDPFC​=GDPMP​−NIT
  6. NDPFC=NDPMP−NIT\text{NDP}{\text{FC}} = \text{NDP}{\text{MP}} - \text{NIT}NDPFC​=NDPMP​−NIT (Domestic Factor Income).
  7. GNPFC=GNPMP−NIT\text{GNP}{\text{FC}} = \text{GNP}{\text{MP}} - \text{NIT}GNPFC​=GNPMP​−NIT
  8. NNPFC=NNPMP−NIT\text{NNP}{\text{FC}} = \text{NNP}{\text{MP}} - \text{NIT}NNPFC​=NNPMP​−NIT = NATIONAL INCOME (NI)\mathbf{\text{NATIONAL INCOME (NI)}}NATIONAL INCOME (NI).
How It Works / Mechanism

The 2015 MoSPI SNA Methodology: Basic Prices Framework

In January 2015, MoSPI aligned India's National Accounts Statistics (NAS) with the international UN System of National Accounts (SNA 2008), introducing the concept of Basic Prices:

The Production vs Product Tax Distinction:

  • Production Taxes / Subsidies: Paid or received independent of the volume of actual production (e.g. Land revenue, stamp duty, factory registration fee, municipal tax; Subsidies: Railways subsidies, small-scale enterprise subsidies).
  • Product Taxes / Subsidies: Paid or received per unit of good produced/sold (e.g. GST, Central Excise Duty, Customs Duty; Subsidies: Food subsidy, fertilizer subsidy, petroleum subsidy).

Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Headline Growth vs Sectoral GVA & Exam Traps

Why Did India Switch Headline GDP in 2015?

  • Pre-2015: India reported headline economic growth using GDP at Factor Cost\text{GDP at Factor Cost}GDP at Factor Cost (Constant Prices).
  • Post-2015: India reports headline economic growth using GDP at Market Prices\mathbf{\text{GDP at Market Prices}}GDP at Market Prices (Constant Prices), matching IMF and global standard reporting.
  • Sectoral performance (Agriculture, Manufacturing, Services) is reported as GVA at Basic Prices\mathbf{\text{GVA at Basic Prices}}GVA at Basic Prices.

Examiner Traps & High-Yield Pitfalls

  • ⚠️ Trap 1: NFIA for India is Historically Negative: Because foreign multinational investments and foreign debt in India historically earn more factor income than Indian investments abroad, NFIA for India is negative (NFIA < 0). Consequently, GNP<GDP\text{GNP} < \text{GDP}GNP<GDP for India.
  • ⚠️ Trap 2: Canonical National Income is ALWAYS NNP_FC: When an exam asks for "National Income", it is strictly NNPFC\text{NNP}_{\text{FC}}NNPFC​, NOT GDP, NDP, or NNP_MP.
