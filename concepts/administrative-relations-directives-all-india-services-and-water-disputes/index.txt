11:T4fa,### 1. Executive Directions from Centre to States:
• **Article 256:** State executive power must ensure compliance with laws made by Parliament. Centre can give necessary directions.
• **Article 257:** State executive power must not impede or prejudice the executive power of the Union. Centre can direct States to:
  1. Construct and maintain **means of communication of national or military importance**.
  2. Take measures for the **protection of railways** within the State.
  3. Provide facilities for instruction in the **mother tongue at the primary stage** to linguistic minority children (Article 350A).
  4. Draw up and execute schemes for the welfare of **Scheduled Tribes** (Article 339(2)).

### 2. Constitutional Sanction (Article 365):
• If a State fails to comply with any lawful Union direction, **Article 365 empowers the President to deem it a breakdown of constitutional machinery in the State**, justifying the imposition of **President's Rule under Article 356**.
