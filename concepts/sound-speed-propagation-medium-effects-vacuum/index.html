Sound Propagation in Media, Speed Variations & Vacuum Impossibility

The material dependency of sound speed governed by medium elasticity and density, environmental influences (temperature, humidity), and the bell jar vacuum proof.

Intuition & Context

Why Astronauts Cannot Talk to Each Other on the Moon Without Radios

In Hollywood movies, space battles roar with loud explosions. In reality, deep space is utterly silent. On the Moon, two astronauts standing two feet apart shouting at the top of their lungs cannot hear a whisper without radio headsets. Why? Because the Moon has no atmosphere: without air molecules to collide and pass along pressure pulses, sound cannot exist.

How It Works / Mechanism

Environmental Drivers: Temperature, Humidity & Pressure Invariance

1. Effect of Temperature (TTT)

Sound speed in a gas is proportional to the square root of absolute temperature:
v=γRTM  ⟹  v∝Tv = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma R T}{M}} \implies v \propto \sqrt{T}v=MγRT​​⟹v∝T​

  • In air, speed increases by ≈0.61 m/s\approx \mathbf{0.61 , \text{m/s}}≈0.61m/s for every 1∘C1^\circ\text{C}1∘C temperature rise:
    vT≈331+0.61⋅T (where T is in ∘C)v_T \approx 331 + 0.61 \cdot T , (\text{where } T \text{ is in } ^\circ\text{C})vT​≈331+0.61⋅T(where T is in ∘C)

2. Effect of Humidity (Why Sound Travels Faster on Rainy/Humid Days)

  • Water vapor (H2OH_2OH2​O, molar mass =18 g/mol= 18 , \text{g/mol}=18g/mol) is lighter than dry air (dominated by N2=28N_2 = 28N2​=28 and O2=32O_2 = 32O2​=32, average molar mass ≈29 g/mol\approx 29 , \text{g/mol}≈29g/mol).
  • As air becomes humid, light water vapor molecules displace heavier nitrogen/oxygen molecules   ⟹  \implies⟹ Moist air is LESS dense than dry air (ρmoist<ρdry\rho_{\text{moist}} < \rho_{\text{dry}}ρmoist​<ρdry​).
  • Since v=γPρv = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma P}{\rho}}v=ργP​​, lower density yields higher sound speed in humid air.

3. The Pressure Independence Principle

  • If atmospheric pressure PPP increases, gas density ρ\rhoρ increases in exact proportion (P/ρ=constantP/\rho = \text{constant}P/ρ=constant at constant TTT).
  • Changing atmospheric pressure has ZERO effect on the speed of sound if temperature remains constant.
