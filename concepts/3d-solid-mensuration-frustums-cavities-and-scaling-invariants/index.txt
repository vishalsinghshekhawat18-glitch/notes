### 2. Frustum of a Right Circular Cone
For a frustum (bucket) of top radius $R$, bottom radius $r$, and vertical height $h$:
- **Slant Height ($l$):** $l = sqrt{h^2 + (R - r)^2}$
- **Curved Surface Area (CSA):** $	ext{CSA} = pi(R + r)l$
- **Total Surface Area (TSA - Closed Bucket):** $	ext{TSA} = pi(R + r)l + pi R^2 + pi r^2$
- **Volume ($V$):** $V = rac{1}{3}pi h (R^2 + r^2 + Rcdot r)$
