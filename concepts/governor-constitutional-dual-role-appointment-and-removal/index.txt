11:T506,### Constitutional Provisions (Articles 153–158):
• **Article 153:** There shall be a Governor for each State (7th CAA 1956 allows same person for 2+ states).
• **Article 155:** Appointed by the **President of India** by warrant under hand and seal (Canadian model of appointed Governor, rejecting the US elected model to prevent dual power centers in the state).
• **Article 156 (Tenure):** Holds office during the **pleasure of the President**. Normal term is 5 years, but no security of tenure is guaranteed by the text.
• **Qualifications (Article 157):** Citizen of India, completed **35 years of age**.
• **Conventions (Sarkaria & Punchhi Commissions):** Should be an outsider to the state; should not be intimately connected with local state politics; Chief Minister of the state should be consulted before appointment.

### Landmark Removal Jurisprudence (*B.P. Singhal v. UOI, 2010*):
• A change in the central government cannot be a valid ground to remove Governors en masse.
• The President need not state reasons in the removal order, but must have valid, compelling, and non-arbitrary reasons.
