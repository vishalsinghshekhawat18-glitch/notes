- **Trap 1: Agricultural Income Taxation**: The Central Government **CANNOT tax agricultural income** (barred by Entry 82 List I). Only State Governments have the constitutional power under Entry 46 List II (though most states exempt it).
- **Trap 2: Article 246A is NOT in Concurrent List**: Article 246A is a **standalone sui generis constitutional provision** giving simultaneous power. GST is NOT an entry in List III (Concurrent List)!
- **Trap 3: Cesses and Surcharges (Article 271)**: Cesses and surcharges are **EXCLUDED from the Divisible Pool** of Article 270, meaning the Union retains 100% of cess collections.
- **Trap 4: Alcoholic Liquor for Human Consumption**: States retain **exclusive taxation power** over alcohol under Entry 51 List II (State Excise) and Entry 54 List II (State VAT); it was **strictly excluded from GST**.
