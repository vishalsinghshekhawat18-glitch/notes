11:T4a4,### The VIBGYOR Spectral Hierarchy

$$\mathbf{V \quad I \quad B \quad G \quad Y \quad O \quad R}$$
$$\text{(Violet} \longrightarrow \text{Indigo} \longrightarrow \text{Blue} \longrightarrow \text{Green} \longrightarrow \text{Yellow} \longrightarrow \text{Orange} \longrightarrow \text{Red)}$$

### Physical Parameters Comparison Table

| Spectral Parameter | Violet Light | Red Light | Mathematical Relationship |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Wavelength ($\lambda$)** | **Shortest ($\approx 400 \, \text{nm}$)** | **Longest ($\approx 700 \, \text{nm}$)** | $\lambda_{\text{red}} \approx 1.75 \times \lambda_{\text{violet}}$ |
| **Frequency ($f$)** | **Highest ($\approx 7.5 \times 10^{14} \, \text{Hz}$)**| **Lowest ($\approx 4.3 \times 10^{14} \, \text{Hz}$)** | $f = c / \lambda$ |
| **Speed in Glass/Water ($v$)** | **Slowest** | **Fastest** | $v = c / n$ |
| **Refractive Index ($n$)** | **Highest ($n_{\text{violet}} \approx 1.53$)** | **Lowest ($n_{\text{red}} \approx 1.51$)** | Cauchy's law: $n(\lambda) = A + B/\lambda^2$ |
| **Angle of Deviation ($\delta$)**| **MAXIMUM Deviation (Bends the most)** | **MINIMUM Deviation (Bends the least)**| $\delta \approx (n - 1) A$ |12:T5b6,### The 3 Step Optical Cycle in a Raindrop (Primary Rainbow):
1. **Entry & Dispersion (Refraction 1)**:
   - Sunlight enters the upper hemisphere of a spherical raindrop.
   - Refraction bends the light and **disperses white light into VIBGYOR colors** (Violet bends more than Red).
2. **Back Wall Bounce (Internal Reflection)**:
   - The dispersed rays strike the back surface of the water droplet and undergo **Internal Reflection**.
   - *(Note: It is ordinary internal reflection at water-air boundary, NOT total internal reflection since angle is $\approx 40^\circ - 42^\circ < \theta_c = 48.6^\circ$).*
3. **Exit Refraction (Refraction 2)**:
   - Rays refract as they exit the droplet toward the observer's eye.
   - Red light emerges at an angle of **$42^\circ$** relative to sunlight; Violet emerges at **$40^\circ$**.
   - **Observer Position Rule**: The Sun must be **BEHIND the observer**, and raindrops in front.

### Primary vs Secondary Rainbow:

| Feature | Primary Rainbow | Secondary Rainbow |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Internal Reflections** | **1 Internal Reflection** inside droplet. | **2 Internal Reflections** inside droplet. |
| **Color Sequence** | **Red on OUTSIDE / top; Violet on INSIDE**. | **Violet on OUTSIDE / top; Red on INSIDE** (Reversed!). |
| **Angular Radius** | $40^\circ - 42^\circ$ | $50^\circ - 53^\circ$ (Appears higher in sky). |
| **Brightness** | Very Bright. | Much Fainter (due to double reflection light loss).
