11:T57e,### Progression of Section 13 Enforcement under SARFAESI Act 2002

```
[Account turns NPA + Charge Registered in CERSAI]
                       │
                       ▼
[Step 1: Section 13(2) Statutory Demand Notice]
- Gives borrower 60 DAYS to discharge full liabilities.
- Sets out exact details of debt, interest, and secured assets.
- Bar on Borrower: Cannot transfer/sell assets without bank NOC (Sec 13(13)).
                       │
         ┌─────────────┴─────────────┐
         ▼                           ▼
[Borrower Repays within 60 Days]  [Borrower Submits Representation / Objection]
- Notice discharged; loan closed.    │
                                     ▼
                        [Step 2: Section 13(3A) Reply within 15 DAYS]
                        - Bank MUST consider objection.
                        - If rejected, Bank must send written reasons within 15 DAYS.
                        - (Rejection does not give immediate right of appeal to DRT).
                                     │
                                     ▼
                        [Step 3: Section 13(4) Enforcement Measures]
                        - Triggered after 60 days expire.
                        - Take physical / symbolic possession (Rule 8).
                        - Issue 30-day public auction notice (Rule 8(6)).
