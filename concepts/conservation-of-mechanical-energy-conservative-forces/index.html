Law of Conservation of Mechanical Energy & Conservative Forces

The universal principle stating that total mechanical energy remains constant in an isolated conservative system, exchanging between kinetic and potential energy.

Intuition & Context

The Rollercoaster Energy Seesaw

When a rollercoaster car is slowly cranked up to the highest peak, the motor packs it full of Potential Energy (mghmghmgh). As it plunges down the steep drop, height disappears while roaring speed appears—potential energy converts smoothly into Kinetic Energy (12mv2\frac{1}{2}mv^221​mv2). At the bottom, kinetic energy is at its absolute maximum. As it climbs the next loop, speed bleeds away as height is regained. Energy is never destroyed; it merely changes costumes.

Core Principle

Conservation of Mechanical Energy in Free Fall

Stepwise Proof of Energy Conservation in Free Fall (Mass mmm, Height HHH):

Point A (Height H, Top):
E_p = mgH
E_k = 0
Total E_A = mgH + 0 = mgH
       │
       ▼ (Falls distance x)
Point B (Height H − x, Midway):
E_p = mg(H − x)
E_k = ½ m v_B² = ½ m (2gx) = mgx   [since v_B² = 0 + 2gx]
Total E_B = mg(H − x) + mgx = mgH
       │
       ▼ (Strikes ground)
Point C (Height 0, Ground):
E_p = 0
E_k = ½ m v_C² = ½ m (2gH) = mgH   [since v_C² = 2gH]
Total E_C = 0 + mgH = mgH

ETotal=EA=EB=EC=mgH=CONSTANT\mathbf{E_{\text{Total}} = E_A = E_B = E_C = mgH = \text{CONSTANT}}ETotal​=EA​=EB​=EC​=mgH=CONSTANT

2. Simple Pendulum Energy Transformations

  • At Extreme Positions (maximum swing height hhh): Velocity v=0  ⟹  Ek=0,Ep=mghv = 0 \implies E_k = 0, E_p = mghv=0⟹Ek​=0,Ep​=mgh (Maximum).
  • At Mean Position (lowest equilibrium point h=0h = 0h=0): Height h=0  ⟹  Ep=0,Ek=12mv2h = 0 \implies E_p = 0, E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2h=0⟹Ep​=0,Ek​=21​mv2 (Maximum).
  • A pendulum in a vacuum swings forever without stopping. In air, air drag (non-conservative) slowly dissipates mechanical energy into atmospheric heat.
