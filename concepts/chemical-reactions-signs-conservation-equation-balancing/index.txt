$$\text{Skeletal Equation: } \text{Fe} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \longrightarrow \text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4 + \text{H}_2$$

1. **Count Atoms on Both Sides**:
   - Reactants: $\text{Fe} = 1, \text{H} = 2, \text{O} = 1$
   - Products: $\text{Fe} = 3, \text{H} = 2, \text{O} = 4$
2. **Step 1: Balance Oxygen Atoms (Maximum count = 4)**:
   - Put coefficient **$4$** in front of $\text{H}_2\text{O}$:
   $$\text{Fe} + \mathbf{4}\text{H}_2\text{O} \longrightarrow \text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4 + \text{H}_2$$
3. **Step 2: Balance Hydrogen Atoms ($4 \times 2 = 8$ H on left)**:
   - Put coefficient **$4$** in front of $\text{H}_2$ on right:
   $$\text{Fe} + 4\text{H}_2\text{O} \longrightarrow \text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4 + \mathbf{4}\text{H}_2$$
4. **Step 3: Balance Iron Atoms (3 Fe on right)**:
   - Put coefficient **$3$** in front of $\text{Fe}$ on left:
   $$\mathbf{3}\text{Fe (s)} + \mathbf{4}\text{H}_2\text{O (g)} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Fe_3O_4 \text{ (s)}} + \mathbf{4H_2 \text{ (g)}}$$
