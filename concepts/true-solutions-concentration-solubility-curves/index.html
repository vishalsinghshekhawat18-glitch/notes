True Solutions, Solute-Solvent Dynamics, Concentration & Solubility Curves

The thermodynamics of homogeneous liquid solutions, solute and solvent roles, mathematical concentration formulas (mass %, volume %), saturation thresholds, and solubility curves.

Intuition & Context

Why Trout Die in Warm Water and Soda Goes Flat When Warm

If you want to dissolve $100 , \text{g}$ of sugar in tea, you heat the tea up—hot water holds way more solid sugar. But if you want to keep carbon dioxide gas fizz in your soda, or keep oxygen dissolved in a mountain stream for trout fish, THE RULES REVERSE: hot water expels dissolved gases! When factory thermal pollution dumps warm water into rivers, dissolved oxygen drops sharply, suffocating fish (Gas Solubility Law).

How It Works / Mechanism

Temperature & Pressure Effects: Henry's Law on Gases

1. Solubility of Solids in Liquids

  • Dissolving sugar/salt is usually an endothermic process (ΔHsol>0\Delta H_{\text{sol}} > 0ΔHsol​>0).
  • By Le Chatelier's Principle: Increasing Temperature INCREASES the solubility of solids in liquids.

2. Solubility of Gases in Liquids (Henry's Law):

P=KH⋅xP = K_H \cdot xP=KH​⋅x
(The solubility of a gas in a liquid is directly proportional to the partial pressure of the gas above the liquid).

  • Effect of Pressure: Bottling soda under high CO2CO_2CO2​ pressure forces gas to dissolve. When you pop the cap, pressure drops   ⟹  \implies⟹ Gas solubility collapses   ⟹  \implies⟹ CO2CO_2CO2​ bubbles rush out.
  • Effect of Temperature: Gas dissolution is exothermic (ΔH<0\Delta H < 0ΔH<0). Increasing Temperature DECREASES gas solubility. Hot water holds less dissolved O2O_2O2​ than cold water!
Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps: Mass of Solution Denominator Trap

Top Solution Traps in Competitive Exams:

  1. The Denominator Trap:
    • Trap: Candidates divide solute mass by solvent mass: 40320×100=12.5%\frac{40}{320} \times 100 = 12.5%32040​×100=12.5% (WRONG!).
    • Correct: Must divide by TOTAL SOLUTION ($40 + 320 = 360 , \text{g}), yielding $11.11%.
  2. Tincture of Iodine Composition:
    • Solute: Solid Iodine (I2I_2I2​).
    • Solvent: Ethyl Alcohol (Ethanol).
    • Widely used as an antiseptic for cuts and wounds.
  3. Aerated Drinks (Soda Water):
    • Solute: Carbon Dioxide gas (CO2CO_2CO2​).
    • Solvent: Liquid Water (H2OH_2OH2​O).
