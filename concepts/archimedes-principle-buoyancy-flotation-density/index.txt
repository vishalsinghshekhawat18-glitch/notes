12:T4dc,### Archimedes' Principle Formula:
$$\mathbf{F_B = W_{\text{fluid displaced}} = V_{\text{submerged}} \cdot \rho_{\text{fluid}} \cdot g}$$

### The Three States of Immersion:

| Condition | Relationship between Forces | Densities | Physical Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Case 1: Sinking** | Weight of body $>$ Buoyant force ($W > F_B$). | $\rho_{\text{object}} > \rho_{\text{fluid}}$ | Object accelerates downward and **sinks to the bottom** (e.g. iron nail in water). |
| **Case 2: Neutral Buoyancy** | Weight of body $=$ Buoyant force ($W = F_B$). | $\rho_{\text{object}} = \rho_{\text{fluid}}$ | Object **floats fully submerged** anywhere inside the liquid (e.g. fish, submarine). |
| **Case 3: Floating with Partial Immersion** | Weight of body $=$ Buoyant force ($W = F_B$). | $\rho_{\text{object}} < \rho_{\text{fluid}}$ | Object **floats partially above surface** (e.g. wooden block, ice cube). |

### Fraction of Floating Volume Submerged:
$$\frac{V_{\text{submerged}}}{V_{\text{total}}} = \frac{\rho_{\text{object}}}{\rho_{\text{fluid}}}$$
- Ice density $\rho_{\text{ice}} \approx 0.9 \, \text{g/cm}^3$, Water density $\rho_w = 1.0 \, \text{g/cm}^3 \implies \mathbf{90\%}$ of an iceberg lies hidden beneath the water line!13:T583,### 1. Anomalous Expansion of Water at $4^\circ\text{C}$
- Unlike almost all substances that contract continuously upon cooling, water has **Maximum Density ($1.000 \, \text{g/cm}^3 = 1000 \, \text{kg/m}^3$) at exactly $4^\circ\text{C}$**.
- Below $4^\circ\text{C}$, water expands as it freezes into ice ($ho_{\text{ice}} = 0.917 \, \text{g/cm}^3$).
- **Aquatic Life Survival in Frozen Lakes**: Surface water cools to $0^\circ\text{C}$ and freezes into floating insulating ice, while denser $4^\circ\text{C}$ liquid water remains at the lake bottom, preserving fish and aquatic ecosystems.

### 2. Swimming in Fresh Water vs Ocean vs Dead Sea
- Salt water has higher density than fresh water ($\rho_{\text{sea}} \approx 1.025 \, \text{g/cm}^3$).
- **It is much easier to swim in the sea than in a fresh water swimming pool** because dense sea water provides greater upward buoyant force ($F_B = V \rho g$).
- In the **Dead Sea** (salinity ~34%, $\rho \approx 1.24 \, \text{g/cm}^3$), the buoyant force exceeds human body density ($~1.06 \, \text{g/cm}^3$), making it impossible for a human to sink.

### 3. Dedicated Archimedean Measuring Instruments
- **Lactometer**: Measures purity of milk by testing milk density (higher density of pure milk causes lactometer to float higher; watered-down milk sinks deeper).
- **Hydrometer**: Directly measures specific gravity of industrial liquids and car battery
