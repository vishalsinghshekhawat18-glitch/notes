Mirror Formula (1/f = 1/v + 1/u), Cartesian Sign Convention & Magnification

The mathematical coordinate system of geometrical optics, the Cartesian sign convention, the mirror formula, and linear magnification equations.

Intuition & Context

The Number Line of Optics: Why Signs Prevent Disasters

In optical physics, you cannot simply say "the image is $20 , \text{cm}$ away." Is it $20 , \text{cm}$ in front of the mirror where light rays physically converge onto a screen (Real image)? Or is it $20 , \text{cm}$ trapped behind the glass where no real light reaches (Virtual image)? The Cartesian Sign Convention is the rigorous coordinate grid that prevents surgeons and telescope designers from confusing real focal points with phantom virtual reflections.
