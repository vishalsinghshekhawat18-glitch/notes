12:T478,### Worked Example:
A concave mirror has a focal length of $15 \, \text{cm}$. An object of height $4 \, \text{cm}$ is placed $30 \, \text{cm}$ in front of the mirror. Find the position, nature, and height of the image.

1. **Assign Cartesian Signs**:
   - $f = -15 \, \text{cm}$ (Concave mirror).
   - $u = -30 \, \text{cm}$ (Object in front).
   - $h = +4 \, \text{cm}$ (Erect object).
2. **Apply Mirror Formula**:
   $$\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u} \implies \frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{u}$$
   $$\frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{-15} - \frac{1}{-30} = -\frac{1}{15} + \frac{1}{30} = \frac{-2 + 1}{30} = -\frac{1}{30}$$
   $$\mathbf{v = -30 \, \text{cm}}$$
3. **Calculate Magnification & Image Height**:
   $$m = -\frac{v}{u} = -\frac{-30}{-30} = \mathbf{-1.0}$$
   $$h' = m \times h = (-1.0) \times 4 = \mathbf{-4 \, \text{cm}}$$
4. **Conclusion**:
   - Image is formed **$30 \, \text{cm}$ in front of the mirror** ($v < 0$).
   - Nature is **Real and Inverted** ($m < 0$).
   - Size is **$4 \, \text{cm}$ high (Same size as object)** because the object was positioned exactly at the Centre of Curvature ($C = 2f = 30 \,
