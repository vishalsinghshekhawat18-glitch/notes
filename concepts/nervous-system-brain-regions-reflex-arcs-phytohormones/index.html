Nervous System, Brain Anatomy, Spinal Reflex Arcs & Plant Phytohormones

Neural and plant coordination systems: tripartite brain architecture (Cerebrum, Cerebellum, Medulla), involuntary spinal reflex circuits, and the five phytohormone master regulators (Auxins, Gibberellins, Cytokinins, Abscisic Acid, Ethylene).

Intuition & Context

Why You Jerk Your Hand from a Hot Iron Before You Even Feel the Pain

If you accidentally touch a scorching hot iron, your hand violently yanks backward in 0.05 seconds before your brain even realizes what happened! If your hand had to wait for the signal to travel all the way up to your conscious cerebral cortex, think about it, and send a command back down, your skin would be burnt to the bone. Your body solves this with a short-circuit emergency loop in your spinal cord (The Reflex Arc)!

How It Works / Mechanism

The Master Plant Phytohormones Reference Table

The 5 Cardinal Phytohormones of Botany

Phytohormone Name Chemical Category / State Primary Site of Synthesis Master Botanical Functions & Real-World Roles
1. Auxin (IAA) Indole derivative Shoot Tips (Apical buds) • Phototropism (bends plants toward light by elongating shaded side).
• Apical Dominance (inhibits lateral branch buds).
• Root initiation in stem cuttings.
2. Gibberellins (GA3GA_3GA3​) Terpene acid Young leaves & seeds • Internodal Stem Elongation (bolting in rosette plants).
• Breaking seed dormancy & stimulating alpha-amylase in malting barley.
• Increases grape cluster size.
3. Cytokinins (Zeatin) Adenine / Purine base Root tips, Germinating seeds, Fruits • Rapid Cell Division (Cytokinesis).
• Overcomes apical dominance (promotes bushy lateral branches).
• Delays Leaf Aging / Senescence (Richmond-Lang effect).
4. Abscisic Acid (ABA) Sesquiterpenoid Leaves, stems, green fruits • "THE STRESS HORMONE": Triggers Rapid Stomatal Closure during drought.
• Inhibits Growth & induces seed dormancy.
• Promotes leaf wilting and fruit drop (Abscission).
5. Ethylene (C2H4C_2H_4C2​H4​) ONLY GASEOUS Hormone Ripening fruits, aging leaves • COMMERCIAL FRUIT RIPENING (bananas, mangoes, tomatoes).
• Promotes leaf and flower senescence and abscission.
Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps: Cerebellum Alcohol Effect & Gaseous Hormone

Top 4 Nervous & Phytohormone Traps:

  1. Alcohol Impairment of the Brain:
    • Question: "Which part of the brain is temporarily impaired by excessive alcohol consumption, causing a person to stumble and lose physical body balance?"
    • Answer: CEREBELLUM (responsible for muscle coordination and bodily equilibrium).
  2. The ONLY Gaseous Plant Hormone:
    • Question: "Which plant hormone exists in a GASEOUS state at room temperature and accelerates fruit ripening?"
    • Answer: ETHYLENE (C2H4C_2H_4C2​H4​).
  3. The Plant Stress Hormone:
    • Question: "Which phytohormone is termed the 'Stress Hormone' because it closes stomata during water deficiency?"
    • Answer: ABSCISIC ACID (ABA).
  4. Reflex Arc Correct Sequence:
    • Receptor ⟶\longrightarrow⟶ Sensory Neuron ⟶\longrightarrow⟶ Spinal Relay Neuron ⟶\longrightarrow⟶ Motor Neuron ⟶\longrightarrow⟶ Effector Muscle.
