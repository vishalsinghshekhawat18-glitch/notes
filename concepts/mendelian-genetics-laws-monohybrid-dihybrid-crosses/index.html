Mendelian Genetics: Pea Experiments, Monohybrid (3:1) & Dihybrid (9:3:3:1) Laws

The foundational laws of classical inheritance discovered by Gregor Mendel: 7 garden pea traits, monohybrid phenotypic (3:1) and genotypic (1:2:1) ratios, dihybrid assortment (9:3:3:1), Law of Dominance, Segregation, and Independent Assortment.

Intuition & Context

How an Austrian Monk Breeding Peas Cracked the Code of All Heredity

In 1856, in the quiet garden of an Austrian monastery, a monk named Gregor Mendel crossed pure-breeding giant tall pea plants with tiny dwarf plants. He expected the children to be medium-sized. Instead, every single baby in the F1F_1F1​ generation was 100% giant Tall! Where did the dwarf trait go? When he crossed those tall babies together, the dwarf trait suddenly re-appeared in the grandchildren in a perfect mathematical ratio: 3 Tall to 1 Dwarf ($3:1$)! Mendel proved that genes are discrete particles that never blend or dilute.

Core Principle

Mendel's 7 Contrasting Pea Characters & Ratios Matrix

The 7 Contrasting Characters Studied by Mendel in Pisum sativum

Botanical Character Dominant Trait (Expressed in F1F_1F1​) Recessive Trait (Suppressed in F1F_1F1​)
1. Plant Height Tall (TTT) Dwarf / Short (ttt)
2. Seed Shape Round (RRR) Wrinkled (rrr)
3. Seed Color Yellow (YYY) Green (yyy)
4. Flower Color Violet / Purple (WWW) White (www)
5. Pod Shape Inflated / Full (III) Constricted (iii)
6. Pod Color Green (GGG) Yellow (ggg) (Note: Opposite of seed color!)
7. Flower Position Axial (along stem) Terminal (at tip of stem)

Master Mendelian Cross Ratios:

Cross Type Traits Studied F2F_2F2​ Phenotypic Ratio F2F_2F2​ Genotypic Ratio
Monohybrid Cross 1 Pair (e.g. Height T/tT/tT/t) 3:1\mathbf{3 : 1}3:1 (3 Tall : 1 Dwarf) 1:2:1\mathbf{1 : 2 : 1}1:2:1 ($1 , TT : 2 , Tt : 1 , tt$)
Dihybrid Cross 2 Pairs (R/rR/rR/r and Y/yY/yY/y) 9:3:3:1\mathbf{9 : 3 : 3 : 1}9:3:3:1 $1:2:2:4:1:2:1:2:1$ (9 genotypes)
How It Works / Mechanism

The Monohybrid Punnett Square Walkthrough ($Tt \times Tt$)

Punnett Square for F2F_2F2​ Generation:

                  Female Gametes ──►     T (50%)          t (50%)
           Male Gametes ▼
              T (50%)                    TT (Tall)        Tt (Tall)
              t (50%)                    Tt (Tall)        tt (Dwarf)
  1. Phenotypic Count:
    • TTTTTT (Tall) +++ TtTtTt (Tall) +++ TtTtTt (Tall) =3 Tall= \mathbf{3 \text{ Tall}}=3 Tall (75%75%75%).
    • tttttt (Dwarf) =1 Dwarf= \mathbf{1 \text{ Dwarf}}=1 Dwarf (25%25%25%).
    •   ⟹  Phenotypic Ratio =3:1\implies \mathbf{\text{Phenotypic Ratio } = 3 : 1}⟹Phenotypic Ratio =3:1.
  2. Genotypic Count:
    • $1 , TT$ (Homozygous Dominant) : $2 , Tt$ (Heterozygous Dominant) : $1 , tt$ (Homozygous Recessive).
    •   ⟹  Genotypic Ratio =1:2:1\implies \mathbf{\text{Genotypic Ratio } = 1 : 2 : 1}⟹Genotypic Ratio =1:2:1.
Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps: Phenotypic vs Genotypic 3:1 vs 1:2:1 & Pod vs Seed Color

Top 4 Mendelian Genetics Traps:

  1. Phenotypic vs Genotypic Ratio Distinction:
    • Monohybrid PHENOTYPIC Ratio =3:1= \mathbf{3 : 1}=3:1 (Physical appearance).
    • Monohybrid GENOTYPIC Ratio =1:2:1= \mathbf{1 : 2 : 1}=1:2:1 (Genetic allele makeup).
    • Dihybrid PHENOTYPIC Ratio =9:3:3:1= \mathbf{9 : 3 : 3 : 1}=9:3:3:1.
  2. Seed Color vs Pod Color Inversion:
    • Seed Color: Yellow is Dominant, Green is Recessive.
    • Pod Color: Green is Dominant, Yellow is Recessive.
    • (A tricky trap: candidates often confuse seed color dominance with pod color dominance!)
  3. Law of Segregation has NO Exceptions: The Law of Segregation is universally true in all diploid sexually reproducing organisms because homologous chromosomes must segregate in Meiosis I.
  4. Father of Genetics: Gregor Johann Mendel (Austrian Augustinian monk).
