11:T466,### The 7 Contrasting Characters Studied by Mendel in *Pisum sativum*

| Botanical Character | Dominant Trait (Expressed in $F_1$) | Recessive Trait (Suppressed in $F_1$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Plant Height** | **Tall ($T$)** | **Dwarf / Short ($t$)** |
| **2. Seed Shape** | **Round ($R$)** | **Wrinkled ($r$)** |
| **3. Seed Color** | **Yellow ($Y$)** | **Green ($y$)** |
| **4. Flower Color** | **Violet / Purple ($W$)** | **White ($w$)** |
| **5. Pod Shape** | **Inflated / Full ($I$)** | **Constricted ($i$)** |
| **6. Pod Color** | **Green ($G$)** | **Yellow ($g$)** *(Note: Opposite of seed color!)* |
| **7. Flower Position** | **Axial (along stem)** | **Terminal (at tip of stem)** |
