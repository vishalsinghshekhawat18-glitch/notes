Excretion: Kidney Nephron Ultrastructure, Hemodialysis & Plant Waste

The renal filtration apparatus: nephron ultrafiltration in glomerulus/Bowman's capsule, tubular selective reabsorption of glucose, artificial kidney hemodialysis, and botanical excretion mechanisms (resins, gums).

Intuition & Context

Why Your Kidneys Filter 180 Litres of Blood but You Only Urinate 1.5 Litres

Every 24 hours, your two kidneys filter a staggering $180 , \text{Litres}$ of fluid out of your bloodstream—enough water to fill an entire bathtub! If you urinated all of it out, you would die of extreme dehydration in 15 minutes. Fortunately, your nephron tubules are equipped with microscopic biological vacuum cleaners (Selective Reabsorption), sucking 99%99%99% of the water, salt, and every single molecule of valuable glucose right back into your veins, leaving just $1.5 , \text{Litres}$ of concentrated waste!

How It Works / Mechanism

How an Artificial Kidney Machine (Hemodialysis) Cleans Blood

The Biophysics of Hemodialysis:

  1. The Circuit: Blood is drawn from the patient's radial artery, cooled to 0∘C0^\circ\text{C}0∘C, and treated with anticoagulant Heparin.
  2. The Dialysing Tank:
    • Blood flows through coiled semi-permeable cellophane tubes submerged in Dialysing Fluid.
    • Dialysing fluid contains the exact same electrolyte and glucose concentration as normal plasma, BUT ZERO UREA / ZERO URIC ACID.
  3. The Diffusion:
    • High urea concentration in patient's blood diffuses down its concentration gradient across the cellophane pores into the dialysing fluid.
  4. The Return: Clean blood is warmed to body temperature, treated with anti-heparin, and pumped back into the patient's vein. (Note: Unlike natural nephrons, dialysis does NOT perform tubular reabsorption).
Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps: Yellow Urine Pigment & Plant Resins Storage

Top 4 Excretory Traps:

  1. Why Normal Urine is Pale Yellow:
    • The characteristic yellow amber color of normal urine is caused by the pigment UROCHROME (Urobilin), a breakdown degradation byproduct of dead hemoglobin from recycled RBCs.
  2. Glomerular Filtrate vs Urine Volume:
    • Initial Glomerular Filtrate produced daily =180 Litres= \mathbf{180 , \text{Litres}}=180Litres.
    • Actual Urine excreted daily =1.5 Litres= \mathbf{1.5 , \text{Litres}}=1.5Litres (99%99%99% is reabsorbed!).
  3. Plant Excretory Products in Old Xylem:
    • Plants store metabolic waste products like Resins and Gums in non-functional Old Xylem (Heartwood).
  4. Site of Urea Synthesis:
    • Trap: Candidates think urea is made in the kidneys.
    • Fact: Urea is synthesized in the LIVER (via the Urea / Ornithine Cycle) and merely filtered out by the Kidneys!
