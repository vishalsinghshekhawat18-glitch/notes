11:T748,### 1. The Three Liability Regimes

```
UNAUTHORIZED ELECTRONIC TRANSACTION DETECTED
├── REGIME 1: ZERO LIABILITY
│   ├── Bank negligence / system fault (Zero liability always, regardless of reporting delay)
│   └── Third-Party Breach (Customer reports within ≤ 3 working days)
├── REGIME 2: LIMITED LIABILITY
│   └── Third-Party Breach (Customer reports with delay of 4 to 7 working days)
│       └── Capped at ₹5,000 / ₹10,000 / ₹25,000 (or tx value, whichever lower)
└── REGIME 3: CUSTOMER NEGLIGENCE / DELAYED REPORTING
    ├── Customer shared OTP/PIN -> 100% Customer Liability until bank is notified
    └── Third-Party Breach reported after > 7 working days -> As per Bank Board Policy
```

### 2. Comprehensive Statutory Customer Liability Matrix

| Account & Credit Facility Type | Transaction Value / Credit Limit Tier | Customer Liability if Reported within ≤ 3 Working Days | Customer Liability if Reported within 4 to 7 Working Days | Customer Liability if Reported after > 7 Working Days |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BSBDA (Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account)** | All BSBDA / PMJDY Accounts | **ZERO** | **₹5,000** (or transaction value, whichever is lower) | As per Bank Board approved policy |
| **All Other Savings Accounts, Overdraft Accounts, Credit Cards** | Credit Limit / Balance **up to ₹5 Lakhs** | **ZERO** | **₹10,000** (or transaction value, whichever is lower) | As per Bank Board approved policy |
| **Current / OD / Cash Credit Accounts, Credit Cards** | Credit Limit / Balance **> ₹5 Lakhs** | **ZERO** | **₹25,000** (or transaction value, whichever is lower) | As per Bank Board approved policy |
| **Bank System Negligence / Internal Fraud** | Any Account Type / Any Amount | **ZERO (Always)** | **ZERO (Always)** | **ZERO (Always)** |12:T4f3,The institutional workflow for handling unauthorized electronic transactions operates under strict statutory deadlines:

```
[Customer Receives SMS / Email Alert of Unauthorized Electronic Debit]
                    ↓
[Customer Notifies Bank via 24x7 Helpline, App, SMS, or Branch]
                    ↓
[Bank Immediately Freezes Channel (Card / UPI / NetBanking) & Acknowledges Incident]
                    ↓
[Within 10 Working Days: Bank Mandatorily Credits Shadow / Reversal Amount to Customer Account]
                    ↓
[Bank Forensic & Fraud Monitoring Cell Investigates Incident]
                    ↓
[Within 90 Days: Final Resolution & Settlement of Claim]
  ├── If Customer Innocent / Zero Liability -> Shadow Credit Made Permanent
  └── If Customer Negligence Proven with Digital Evidence -> Liability Adjusted
```
