### Evolution of Atomic Models:
1. **Thomson's Plum Pudding Model (1904)**: Atom is a uniform sphere of positive charge with electrons embedded like raisins in a pudding. (Failed alpha scattering test).
2. **Rutherford's Planetary Nuclear Model (1911)**: Discovered dense central **Nucleus**; electrons orbit like planets. (Failed stability test: classical electrons would spiral into nucleus).
3. **Bohr's Quantized Shell Model (1913)**: Electrons occupy stable **Quantized Energy Shells ($K, L, M, N$)** with zero radiation unless jumping between
