12:T563,International economists utilize five distinct metrics to analyze terms of trade:\n\n### 1. Net Barter (Commodity) Terms of Trade ($N$)\n$$N = \\left(\\frac{P_x}{P_m}\\right) \\times 100$$\n- $P_x$ = Export Price Index, $P_m$ = Import Price Index.\n- *Limitation*: Measures only price ratios, ignoring export volume expansion.\n\n### 2. Income Terms of Trade ($I$ / Capacity to Import)\n$$I = N \\times Q_x = \\left(\\frac{P_x \\times Q_x}{P_m}\\right)$$\n- $Q_x$ = Export Volume Index. Represents the total purchasing power of export earnings over imports. If export prices fall by $10\\%$ but export volume expands by $50\\%$, $N$ deteriorates but $I$ significantly improves!\n\n### 3. Single Factoral Terms of Trade ($S$)\n$$S = N \\times Z_x$$\n- $Z_x$ = Productivity Index in the domestic export sector. Measures the volume of imports a nation can purchase per unit of domestic factor (labor/capital) input.\n\n### 4. Double Factoral Terms of Trade ($D$)\n$$D = N \\times \\left(\\frac{Z_x}{Z_m}\\right)$$\n- $Z_m$ = Productivity Index in foreign export sectors. Compares the productivity efficiency of domestic factors against foreign factors.\n\n### 5. Gross Barter Terms of Trade ($G$)\n$$G = \\left(\\frac{Q_m}{Q_x}\\right) \\times 100$$\n- Measures physical quantities exchanged.
