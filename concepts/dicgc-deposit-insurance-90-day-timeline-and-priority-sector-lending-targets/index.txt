11:T52d,### Part 1: DICGC Deposit Insurance Architecture
- **Coverage Limit:** ₹5,00,000 per depositor per bank (raised from ₹1 Lakh in Feb 2020).
- **Premium:** 12 paise per ₹100 of assessable deposits per annum (paid entirely by the bank, cannot be passed on to depositors).
- **90-Day Payout Rule (Section 18A DICGC Act):**
  - Days 1 to 45: Stressed bank collects depositor claims and submits to DICGC.
  - Days 46 to 75: DICGC processes and verifies claims.
  - Days 76 to 90: Direct payout credited to depositors' Aadhaar-linked accounts.
- **Insured Entities:** All commercial banks, SFBs, Payments Banks, Local Area Banks, and State/Central/Primary Cooperative Banks.
