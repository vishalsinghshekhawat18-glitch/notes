- **Devolution Quantum**: **7.5% of Net Own Tax Revenue** of Rajasthan Government.
- **Vertical Split between Rural & Urban**:
  - **Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs)**: **75.1%**
  - **Urban Local Bodies (ULBs)**: **24.9%** (reflecting rural-urban population ratio per Census 2011).
- **Sub-Devolution among PRIs**:
  - Zila Parishads: 5%
  - Panchayat Samitis: 20%
  - Gram Panchayats: **75%** (Lion's share to village tier).

```13:T487,### Critical RPSC RAS Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: Who Conducts Assembly Elections in Rajasthan?**: The **Election Commission of India (ECI)** conducts elections for the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) and Lok Sabha. The **Rajasthan State Election Commission conducts ONLY Panchayat and Municipality elections**.
- **Trap 2: Satyanarayan Rao Committee (1956)**: When Rajasthan was reorganized on Nov 1, 1956, this committee recommended: Capital $\to$ **Jaipur**, High Court $\to$ **Jodhpur**, RPSC $\to$ **Ajmer**, Education Dept $\to$ **Bikaner**, Mining & Forest $\to$ **Kota**, Agriculture $\to$ **Bharatpur**.
- **Trap 3: RPSC Member Age Limit vs UPSC**: RPSC members retire at **62 years** (Article 316(2)), whereas UPSC members retire at **65 years**.
