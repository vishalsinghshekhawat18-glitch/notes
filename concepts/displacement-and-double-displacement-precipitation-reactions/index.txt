| Dimension | Single Displacement ($A + BC \to AC + B$) | Double Displacement ($AB + CD \to AD + CB$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Driving Mechanism** | **Reactivity Differential** (More reactive element displaces less reactive). | **Mutual Ion Exchange** between two aqueous salts. |
| **Species Involved** | One free Element $+$ One compound solution. | Two ionic salt aqueous solutions. |
| **Electron Transfer** | **Redox Reaction** (Electron transfer occurs). | **Non-Redox** (Valencies / oxidation states remain unchanged). |
