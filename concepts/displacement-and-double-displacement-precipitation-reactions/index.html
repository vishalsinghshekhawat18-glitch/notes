Displacement & Double Displacement (Precipitation & Neutralization) Reactions

Single displacement governed by metal reactivity series versus double displacement involving mutual ion exchange, insoluble precipitate formation, and acid-base neutralization.

Intuition & Context

Why an Iron Nail Steals Copper's Blue Coat in a Beaker

If you dip a shiny grey iron nail into a beautiful sky-blue beaker of Copper Sulphate (CuSO4CuSO_4CuSO4​), come back an hour later: the solution has turned an eerie pale green, and the iron nail is coated in a thick, furry layer of reddish-brown copper metal! Because Iron is higher up on the chemical power ladder than Copper, Iron muscles in, takes Copper's sulphate partner, and kicks copper out of solution (Single Displacement).

Precipitation Mechanics: Net Ionic Equations & Lattice Energy

How Insoluble Precipitates Form:

  1. In Solution: Na2SO4\text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4Na2​SO4​ and BaCl2\text{BaCl}_2BaCl2​ completely dissociate into free hydrated ions:
    Solution contains: 2Na+,SO42−,Ba2+,2Cl−\text{Solution contains: } 2\text{Na}^+, \text{SO}_4^{2-}, \text{Ba}^{2+}, 2\text{Cl}^-Solution contains: 2Na+,SO42−​,Ba2+,2Cl−
  2. The Insoluble Collision:
    • Na+\text{Na}^+Na+ and Cl−\text{Cl}^-Cl− have high hydration energy and remain dissolved as spectator ions.
    • Ba2+\text{Ba}^{2+}Ba2+ and SO42−\text{SO}_4^{2-}SO42−​ have an enormous Lattice Energy that overwhelms water hydration.
    • They snap together into an insoluble ionic crystal lattice:

    Ba2+ (aq)+SO42− (aq)⟶BaSO4↓ (s)\mathbf{\text{Ba}^{2+} \text{ (aq)} + \text{SO}_4^{2-} \text{ (aq)} \longrightarrow BaSO_4 \downarrow \text{ (s)}}Ba2+ (aq)+SO42−​ (aq)⟶BaSO4​↓ (s)

Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps: "No Reaction" Traps & Precipitate Colors

Top Competitive Exam Traps:

  1. The "No Reaction" Reactivity Trap:
    • Question: "What happens when a piece of Copper wire is placed in a solution of Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4FeSO_4FeSO4​)?"
    • Trap: Writing Cu+FeSO4→CuSO4+FeCu + FeSO_4 \to CuSO_4 + FeCu+FeSO4​→CuSO4​+Fe.
    • Correct: NO REACTION OCCURS! (Copper is LESS reactive than Iron; CuCuCu cannot displace FeFeFe).
  2. Precipitate Colors to Memorize:
    • Barium Sulphate (BaSO4BaSO_4BaSO4​): White Precipitate.
    • Lead Iodide (PbI2PbI_2PbI2​): Bright Yellow Precipitate.
    • Silver Chloride (AgClAgClAgCl): White Curdy Precipitate.
    • Copper Hydroxide [Cu(OH)2Cu(OH)_2Cu(OH)2​]: Pale Blue Precipitate.
    • Ferric Hydroxide [Fe(OH)3Fe(OH)_3Fe(OH)3​]: Reddish-Brown Precipitate.
