Electric Charge (Q=ne), Potential Difference (V=W/Q) & Current Flow Mechanics

The foundational electrodynamic quantities: quantized electric charge, electromotive driving pressure (voltage), electric current, and the historical conventional vs electron flow duality.

Intuition & Context

The Water Tank Analogy: Voltage, Current and Wires

Think of an electric circuit as a water pipe system: Electric Charge (QQQ) is the water molecules; Current (III) is the rate of water flowing out of the tap (litres per second); Voltage / Potential Difference (VVV) is the height of the overhead water tank creating hydrostatic pressure to push water through the pipes. Without a pressure difference (Voltage), water (charges) sits completely motionless.

The Electron vs Conventional Flow Duality:

  • Conventional Current Direction: Flows from Positive (+++) →\to→ Negative (−-−) terminal (Established in the 18th century before the discovery of electrons).
  • Actual Electron Drift Flow: Negatively charged free electrons are repelled by negative terminal and flow from Negative (−-−) →\to→ Positive (+++) terminal.
How It Works / Mechanism

Drift Velocity & Why Bulbs Glow Instantly When Switched On

The Instant Light Paradox:

  • In a copper wire, free electrons drift forward at a surprisingly sluggish crawl called Drift Velocity (vd≈0.1 mm/sv_d \approx 0.1 , \text{mm/s}vd​≈0.1mm/s)—it takes an electron hours to travel 1 metre!
  • Why does the bulb turn on instantaneously across a 10-metre room?
  • When you flip the switch, an Electromagnetic Wave (Electric Field) propagates along the wire at nearly the speed of light (c≈3×108 m/sc \approx 3 \times 10^8 , \text{m/s}c≈3×108m/s).
  • This electric field sets all 102310^{23}1023 electrons throughout the entire wire in motion simultaneously like a line of packed marbles; the electron already inside the bulb filament moves immediately.
