```12:T4d1,### 1. Article 279A Voting Mathematics
- **Quorum**: At least **one-half ($50\%$)** of total members must be present.
- **Decision Threshold**: At least **three-fourths ($75\%$)** of the weighted votes of members present and voting.
- **Weighting Schema**:
$$\text{Total Weighted Vote} = \left( \text{Vote of Central Govt} \times \frac{1}{3} \right) + \left( \sum \text{Votes of State Govts} \times \frac{2}{3 \times N} \right)$$
- *Implication*: Centre cannot pass a resolution alone (needs at least $\approx 62.5\%$ of state votes); States cannot pass a resolution without the Centre.

### 2. Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN)
- The non-profit, state-of-the-art IT backbone managing invoice matching, return filing (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B), registration of 1.4+ Crore taxpayers, and automated ITC settlement.

### 3. GST Compensation Cess History (2017–2022)
- Guaranteed States a **$14\%$ annual growth rate** in tax revenues from 2015-16 base.
- Funded by a cess on demerit goods (tobacco, pan masala, coal, luxury SUVs).
- The 5-year guarantee expired in June 2022, but the cess collection was extended to March 2026 **solely to repay the ₹2.69 Lakh Crore back-to-back market loans** taken during COVID-19 to compensate states.13:T4a7,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: GST Council Voting Weights**: The Central Government holds **1/3rd (33.33%)** of the weighted vote; all State Governments together hold **2/3rd (66.67%)**. A decision requires **3/4th (75%)** majority.
- **Trap 2: IGST Levying Authority**: Integrated GST (IGST) is levied and collected **EXCLUSIVELY by the Central Government** under Article 269A (NOT by state governments).
- **Trap 3: GST Compensation Period**: The 5-year compensation guarantee ran from **July 1, 2017 to June 30, 2022** (the extension of the cess to 2026 is strictly to repay borrowing principal/interest, NOT to pay new compensation to states).
- **Trap 4: UTGST vs SGST**: UTGST applies only in the Union Territories **without legislature** (Ladakh, Chandigarh, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep). UTs with legislatures (Delhi, Puducherry, J&K) levy **SGST**.

### Cross-Exam Focus
- **RBI Grade B & UPSC APFC**: Article 279A voting formula, Article 269A IGST apportionment, and GST compensation loan architecture.
