Cellular Respiration (Aerobic vs Anaerobic) & Respiratory Gas Transport

The bioenergetic pathways of glucose breakdown: aerobic respiration (36-38 ATP in mitochondria) versus anaerobic glycolysis (yeast alcoholic fermentation vs muscle lactic acid cramps), alveolar gas exchange, and blood gas transport (hemoglobin, carbon monoxide toxicity).

Intuition & Context

Why 100-Metre Sprinters Double Over with Stabbing Leg Cramps

When you walk casually in a park, your muscle cells burn oxygen cleanly to produce water, carbon dioxide, and 38 ATP coins of biological energy (Aerobic Respiration). But if you suddenly sprint at maximum speed away from a barking dog, your lungs cannot supply oxygen fast enough to your legs. In desperation, your muscle cells flip an emergency switch: they burn sugar without oxygen, producing Lactic Acid (Anaerobic Glycolysis). The rapid buildup of lactic acid crystals locks your muscle fibers into excruciating Muscle Cramps!

Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps: Trachea Cartilage Rings & Lactic Acid Cramps

Top 4 Respiratory Traps:

  1. Rings of Cartilage in Trachea:
    • Question: "Why are C-shaped rings of cartilage present along the human trachea (windpipe)?"
    • Answer: To prevent the tracheal air-passage from COLLAPSING when there is negative pressure / less air in the tube.
  2. Cause of Muscle Cramps After Heavy Exercise:
    • Question: "Heavy muscular exercise often results in painful cramps due to the accumulation of which chemical?"
    • Answer: LACTIC ACID (formed by anaerobic breakdown of pyruvate in muscle cells).
  3. Form of CO2CO_2CO2​ Transport in Blood:
    • Trap: Guessing that CO2CO_2CO2​ is mostly carried by hemoglobin.
    • Fact: 70%70%70% of CO2CO_2CO2​ is transported as dissolved BICARBONATE IONS (HCO3−HCO_3^-HCO3−​) in Blood Plasma (because CO2CO_2CO2​ is highly soluble in water).
