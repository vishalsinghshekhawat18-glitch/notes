Advanced Time & Work: Multi-Agent Schedules, Dynamic Efficiency Shifts & Work-Wages

Core Principle

Multi-Agent Dynamic Work Modeling

Advanced Time & Work in Banking PO Mains moves beyond simple pairwise addition into Dynamically Shifted Work Systems.

Key problem topologies include:

  1. Dynamic Efficiency Modulation: Workers operating at fractional capacity (Enew=E0×(1±k%)E_{\text{new}} = E_0 \times (1 \pm k%)Enew​=E0​×(1±k%)) due to fatigue, technological assistance, or partial days.

3-Tier Worked Problem (SBI PO Mains Benchmark)

Problem: A, B, and C can complete a project in $12, 15,$ and $20$ days respectively. They work on alternate days: A on Day 1, B on Day 2, C on Day 3, A on Day 4, and so on. Find the total number of days required to complete the project and A's share if the total payment is ₹$3,600$.

  • Tier 1 (First Principles / Cycle Decomposition):
    1. Total Work =LCM(12,15,20)=60 units= \text{LCM}(12, 15, 20) = \mathbf{60\text{ units}}=LCM(12,15,20)=60 units.
    2. EA=60/12=5 u/day;EB=60/15=4 u/day;EC=60/20=3 u/dayE_A = 60/12 = 5\text{ u/day}; E_B = 60/15 = 4\text{ u/day}; E_C = 60/20 = 3\text{ u/day}EA​=60/12=5 u/day;EB​=60/15=4 u/day;EC​=60/20=3 u/day.
    3. Work in 1 full 3-day cycle =5+4+3=12 units= 5 + 4 + 3 = \mathbf{12\text{ units}}=5+4+3=12 units.
    4. Number of complete cycles =60/12=5 cycles= 60 / 12 = \mathbf{5\text{ cycles}}=60/12=5 cycles.
    5. Total time =5×3=15 days= 5 \times 3 = \mathbf{15\text{ days}}=5×3=15 days.

      • Work done by A =5×5=25 units= 5 \times 5 = 25\text{ units}=5×5=25 units.
