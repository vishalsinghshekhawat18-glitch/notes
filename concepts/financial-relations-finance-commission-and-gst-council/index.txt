12:T40e,• **Constitutional Insertion:** 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016 (along with **Article 246A** granting concurrent legislative power on GST to Parliament and State Legislatures).
• **Composition:**
  - **Chairperson:** Union Finance Minister
  - **Members:** Union Minister of State for Finance + State Finance / Taxation Ministers from each State.
• **Decision-Making Formula (Article 279A(9)):**
  - Quorum: Half of total members.
  - **Weighted Voting Formula:**
    1. Central Government vote weight = **1/3rd (33.33%)** of total votes cast.
    2. All State Governments combined vote weight = **2/3rd (66.67%)** of total votes cast (each state has equal vote share among states).
    3. **Passing Threshold:** Every decision requires a majority of **not less than 3/4th (75%) of the weighted votes cast**.
