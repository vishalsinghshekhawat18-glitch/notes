11:T75b,### 1. Criteria for Recognition as a NATIONAL PARTY (Must fulfill ANY ONE)
1. **6% Votes + 4 MPs**: Secures at least **6% of valid votes** polled in **4 or more States** in a Lok Sabha or Assembly election, **AND wins at least 4 seats in the Lok Sabha** from any State(s).
2. **2% Lok Sabha Seats from 3 States**: Wins at least **2% of total Lok Sabha seats (11 seats out of 543)**, and these elected members come from **at least 3 different States**.
3. **State Party in 4 States**: Is recognized as a State Party in **at least 4 States** (the "Four-State Rule", which elevated AAP to National Party status in 2023).

### 2. Criteria for Recognition as a STATE PARTY (Must fulfill ANY ONE)
1. **6% Assembly Votes + 2 MLAs**: Secures at least **6% of valid votes** in a State Assembly election AND wins **at least 2 seats** in that Legislative Assembly.
2. **6% Lok Sabha Votes + 1 MP**: Secures at least **6% of valid votes** in the State in a Lok Sabha election AND wins **at least 1 Lok Sabha seat** from that State.
3. **3% Assembly Seats / 3 Seats**: Wins **at least 3% of total Assembly seats** OR **3 seats** in the Legislative Assembly (whichever is greater).
4. **1 MP per 25 MLAs**: Wins **at least 1 Lok Sabha seat for every 25 seats** allotted to the State in the Lok Sabha.
5. **8% Total State Vote Share**: Secures **at least 8% of total valid votes** polled in the entire State in either a Lok Sabha or Assembly election (introduced in 2011; does NOT require winning any seat).

### Privileges of Recognized Parties
- Exclusive **Reserved Symbol** across the State/Country (unrecognized parties get free/unreserved symbols).
- Free broadcast and telecast time on Doordarshan and All India Radio during general elections.
- Right to nominate up to **40 Star Campaigners** (unrecognized parties get only 20), whose travel expenses are not added to candidate expenditure.12:T573,### ECI Power to Derecognize vs Deregister (The 2002 SC Principle)
- **Derecognition (Allowed)**: If a recognized party fails to satisfy the quantitative thresholds in consecutive elections, ECI can strip its "National" or "State" status and withdraw its reserved symbol.
- **Deregistration (Strictly Limited)**: In *Indian National Congress v. Institute of Social Welfare (2002)*, the Supreme Court held Section 29A is administrative. ECI CANNOT cancel a party's registration except under 3 narrow exceptions:
  1. Where registration was obtained by **fraud or forgery**.
  2. Where the party has been declared unlawful by the Central Government under UAPA.
  3. Where the party amends its constitution to renounce allegiance to the Constitution of India.

### Pressure Groups Typology (Gabriel Almond Framework in India)
1. **Associational Groups**: Formally structured groups representing specific economic interests (e.g. FICCI, ASSOCHAM, CII for business; AITUC, BMS, INTUC for labour; BKU, AIKS for farmers).
2. **Institutional Groups**: Professional groups embedded inside state apparatus (e.g. IAS Association, Police Officers Association).
3. **Non-Associational Groups**: Informal, kinship/caste/linguistic groupings (e.g. caste panchayats, community sabhas).
