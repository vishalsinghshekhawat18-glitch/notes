Refraction of Light, Snell's Law, Refractive Index & Apparent Depth Phenomena

The bending of light rays when crossing an interface between media of differing optical densities, Snell's law, absolute refractive index, and apparent depth/bending of sticks in water.

Intuition & Context

Why a Straight Straw Looks Broken in a Glass of Lemonade

If you place a straight plastic straw into a transparent glass of water, the straw appears sharply bent or broken at the water's surface. If you try to grab a coin resting at the bottom of a bucket of water, your fingers reach down and grab empty water above the coin! Why? Because light rays originating from the submerged straw or coin bend away from the normal as they exit the dense water into thin air, tricking your brain into seeing the object at a shallower Apparent Position.

Optical Density vs Mass Density & Apparent Depth Mathematics

1. Optical Density ≠\ne= Mass Density (The Kerosene Paradox)

  • Mass Density: Mass per unit volume (kg/m3\text{kg/m}^3kg/m3). Water (ρ=1.00 g/cm3\rho = 1.00 , \text{g/cm}^3ρ=1.00g/cm3) is heavier than Kerosene (ρ≈0.81 g/cm3\rho \approx 0.81 , \text{g/cm}^3ρ≈0.81g/cm3). Hence kerosene floats on water.
  • Optical Density: Refractive index (nnn). Kerosene (n=1.44n = 1.44n=1.44) has a HIGHER refractive index than water (n=1.33n = 1.33n=1.33).
  • Crucial Fact: Kerosene is OPTICALLY DENSER than water, even though its physical mass density is lower!

Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps & Refraction Through Glass Slabs

Top Refraction Traps:

  1. Parallel Glass Slab Refraction: When a light ray passes through a rectangular glass slab:
    • It refracts toward normal at entry, and away from normal at exit.
    • The emergent ray is strictly PARALLEL to the incident ray (Angle of Emergence === Angle of Incidence: ∠e=∠i\angle e = \angle i∠e=∠i).
    • There is ZERO net angular deviation, but the ray undergoes a perpendicular sideways shift called Lateral Displacement.
  2. Normal Incidence (∠i=0∘\angle i = 0^\circ∠i=0∘): A ray entering perpendicular to the surface goes straight through without bending (sin⁡0∘=0  ⟹  r=0∘\sin 0^\circ = 0 \implies r = 0^\circsin0∘=0⟹r=0∘), though its speed still drops from ccc to c/nc/nc/n.
