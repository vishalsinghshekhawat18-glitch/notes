Intuition & Context

Why "AMBULANCE" is Spelled Backwards on Emergency Vehicles

When you look at the front hood of an emergency vehicle, the word AMBULANCE is painted in reverse mirror letters (ƎƆИA⅃UBMA). Why? When a driver ahead looks into their rear-view plane mirror, the mirror performs a horizontal flip called Lateral Inversion. The reversed letters flip back to normal ("AMBULANCE") in the driver's mirror, allowing them to read it instantly and yield the lane.

Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps: Relative Speed in Plane Mirrors & Periscope

Top Plane Mirror Traps:

  1. Relative Velocity of Image:
    • If a person runs toward a stationary plane mirror at speed uuu: The image moves toward the mirror at speed uuu.
    • The speed of the image RELATIVE TO THE RUNNING PERSON is $2u$ (approaching at twice the running speed!).
  2. Rotating a Mirror by Angle θ\thetaθ: If a plane mirror is rotated by angle θ\thetaθ about an axis in its plane, the reflected ray rotates by 2θ2\theta2θ.
  3. Periscope Construction: Uses two plane mirrors placed parallel to each other at a 45∘45^\circ45∘ angle inside a Z-tube (used in submarines to see above water levels; image is upright and unmagnified).
  4. Kaleidoscope: Uses three narrow rectangular plane mirror strips inclined at 60∘60^\circ60∘ to form beautiful symmetrical hexagonal patterns.
