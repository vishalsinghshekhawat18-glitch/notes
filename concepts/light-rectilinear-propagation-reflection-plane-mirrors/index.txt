11:T426,### Formula for Multiple Images (Angle $\theta$ between two mirrors):
When an object is placed between two plane mirrors inclined at angle $\theta$:
1. Calculate the ratio: $n_0 = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta}$
2. **If $n_0$ is EVEN**: Number of images formed is **$N = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta} - 1$** (for all object positions).
   - E.g. Mirrors at $90^\circ$: $N = \frac{360}{90} - 1 = 4 - 1 = \mathbf{3 \text{ images}}$.
   - E.g. Mirrors at $60^\circ$: $N = \frac{360}{60} - 1 = 6 - 1 = \mathbf{5 \text{ images}}$.
3. **If $n_0$ is ODD**:
   - Placed symmetrically on angle bisector: $N = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta} - 1$.
   - Placed asymmetrically: $N = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta}$.
4. **Parallel Mirrors ($	heta = 0^\circ$)**:
   - $N = \frac{360}{0} - 1 = \mathbf{\infty \text{ (Infinite images)}}$, as seen in barber shops and trial rooms.

### Minimum Mirror Height to See Full Body:
To see one's full height $H$, the plane mirror must have a minimum vertical length of:
$$L_{\text{min}} = \frac{H}{2}$$
