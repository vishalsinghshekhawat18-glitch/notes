Reactions of Carbon Compounds: Ethanol, Ethanoic Acid, Esterification & Saponification

The major chemical reactions of organic compounds (combustion, alkaline KMnO₄ oxidation, nickel-catalysed hydrogenation, halogen substitution) and the industrial chemistry of Ethanol and Ethanoic Acid (Esterification ⇌ Saponification).

Intuition & Context

How Rotten Apples Make Nail Polish Smell Sweet and How Soap is Born

If you mix sour vinegar (Ethanoic Acid) with drinking alcohol (Ethanol) and add a drop of sulphuric acid, a magical transformation happens: the foul vinegar smell vanishes, replaced by a delicious, sweet fruity aroma of fresh apples and bananas! You just synthesized an Ester (CH3COOC2H5CH_3COOC_2H_5CH3​COOC2​H5​), the exact chemical used in perfumes and fruit flavorings. And if you boil that ester with caustic lye (NaOHNaOHNaOH), it turns into real Soap (Saponification)!

Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps: Glacial Acetic Acid & Denatured Alcohol

Top Competitive Exam Traps:

  1. Glacial Acetic Acid Freezing Point:
    • Freezing point is $16.6^\circ\text{C}$ ($290 , \text{K}$).
    • In cold winter climates, pure ethanoic acid freezes solid into clear ice-like sheets, which is why it is called Glacial Acetic Acid.
  2. Vinegar Concentration: Vinegar is strictly a 5% to 8%5% \text{ to } 8%5% to 8% solution of Ethanoic Acid in water (used as preservative in pickles).
  3. Denatured Alcohol Poison: Industrial ethanol is intentionally contaminated with toxic Methanol (CH3OHCH_3OHCH3​OH) and blue copper sulphate dye to render it poisonous and prevent workers from drinking industrial alcohol (ingesting methanol oxidizes into formaldeyde in the liver, causing blindness and death).
  4. Hydrogenation Catalyst: Industrial hydrogenation of vegetable oils universally uses NICKEL (NiNiNi) or Palladium (PdPdPd) as catalyst.
