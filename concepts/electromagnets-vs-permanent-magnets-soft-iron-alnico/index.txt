11:T48e,### Comparison of Magnet Types

| Dimension | Electromagnet (Temporary Magnet) | Permanent Magnet |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Magnetism Nature** | **Temporary**: Lasts only while electric current flows. | **Permanent**: Retains magnetism indefinitely. |
| **Control of Strength**| Easily adjusted by varying current ($I$) or turns ($N$). | Fixed strength; cannot be easily varied. |
| **Control of Polarity** | North and South poles can be reversed by reversing current direction. | Fixed polarity; cannot be reversed easily. |
| **Core Material Used** | **Soft Iron** (Pure Iron / Silicon steel). | **Steel, ALNICO** (Al-Ni-Co), **Neodymium (NdFeB)**. |
| **Magnetic Retentivity**| **Very LOW Retentivity** (Demagnetizes instantly when current stops). | **Very HIGH Retentivity** (Retains strong magnetic flux). |
| **Coercivity** | **Very LOW Coercivity** (Small energy loss in AC cycles). | **Very HIGH Coercivity** (Resists demagnetization from heat/impact). |
| **Master Applications** | Scrapyard cranes, Electric bells, Relays, MRI, Maglev, Particle accelerators. | Magnetic compasses, Loudspeaker cones, Refrigerator door gaskets, Electric guitar pickups.
