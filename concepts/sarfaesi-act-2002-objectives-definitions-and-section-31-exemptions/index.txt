12:T54a,### The Three Statutory Pillars of the SARFAESI Act 2002
1. **Securitisation (Sections 7 & 8):** Packaging illiquid financial assets (mortgage loans) into marketable tradeable securities.
2. **Asset Reconstruction (Sections 3 to 10):** Acquisition of distressed debt portfolios by specialized Asset Reconstruction Companies (ARCs).
3. **Enforcement of Security Interest (Sections 13 to 19):** Extra-judicial repossession and sale of collateral securities by secured creditors.

### Master Section 31 Exemption Checklist (SARFAESI CANNOT BE INVOKED)

| Section 31 Clause | Statutory Exemption | Key Practical Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Section 31(i)** | **Agricultural Land** | Farmland, agricultural fields, orchards cannot be auctioned under SARFAESI |
| **Section 31(h)** | **Amount Due < ₹1,00,000** | Micro debts below ₹1 Lakh are excluded (must use civil courts/Lok Adalat) |
| **Section 31(j)** | **Remaining Debt < 20%** | If borrower has already repaid $>80\%$ of principal + interest, SARFAESI cannot be invoked |
| **Section 31(b)** | **Pledge of Movables** | Governed strictly by Section 176 Indian Contract Act 1872 |
| **Section 31(a)** | **Lien on Goods / Money** | General and Particular liens under Contract Act |
