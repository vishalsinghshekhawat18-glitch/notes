International System of Units (SI): Base Quantities, Derived Units & Dimensions

The universal decimal measurement framework defining 7 fundamental base physical quantities and coherent derived units.

Intuition & Context

Why Global Standards of Measurement Exist

Imagine trading grain where the "cup" is defined by the seller's palm size, or measuring distance by human footsteps. Before global scientific standardization, commerce, engineering, and astronomy suffered from chaotic regional variations (such as the cubit, yard, or seer). To establish unambiguous universal communication, scientists established seven fundamental, immutable physical standards—the SI Base Units—from which every other measurable phenomenon in the universe (from the thrust of a rocket to the voltage of a battery) can be systematically derived.

How It Works / Mechanism

Dimensional Homogeneity & Unit Conversion Workflows

1. Principle of Dimensional Homogeneity

In every valid physical equation (A=B+CA = B + CA=B+C), every additive term must possess identical physical dimensions. You cannot add mass to velocity:

Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps, Dimensionless Quantities & Rapid Recall

Critical Examiner Traps:

  1. The Temperature Scale Trap: The SI unit of temperature is Kelvin (K), NOT Celsius (∘C^\circ\text{C}∘C). Never write "∘K^\circ\text{K}∘K" (the degree symbol is illegal with Kelvin).
  2. Dimensionless Physical Quantities:
    • Relative Density / Specific Gravity: Ratio of densities   ⟹  \implies⟹ No unit, no dimensions.
    • Refractive Index (n=c/vn = c/vn=c/v): Ratio of speeds   ⟹  \implies⟹ Dimensionless.
    • Strain (ΔL/L\Delta L / LΔL/L): Ratio of lengths   ⟹  \implies⟹ Dimensionless.
    • Plane Angle (Radian) & Solid Angle (Steradian): Have units but NO physical dimensions ([M0L0T0][\text{M}^0\text{L}^0\text{T}^0][M0L0T0]).
  3. Weight vs Mass Unit Trap: Mass is measured in kg\text{kg}kg (scalar). Weight is a gravitational force measured in Newtons (N) (vector).
