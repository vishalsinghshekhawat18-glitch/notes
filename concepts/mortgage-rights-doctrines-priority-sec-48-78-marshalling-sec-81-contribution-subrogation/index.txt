11:T8b5,### 1. Rule of Priority & The Section 78 Gross Neglect Exception
- **General Rule (Section 48)**: *Qui prior est tempore, potior est jure* (He who is earlier in time has the superior right). The first registered/created mortgage takes precedence over subsequent mortgages.
- **The Exception (Section 78 - Postponement of Prior Mortgage)**: A prior mortgagee loses priority to a subsequent mortgagee if the prior mortgagee was guilty of:
  1. **Fraud**
  2. **Misrepresentation**
  3. **Gross Neglect** (e.g. returning the original title deeds to the borrower, allowing the borrower to create a fraudulent second mortgage by showing clean possession of deeds).

### 2. Doctrine of Marshalling Securities (Section 81)
- **Rule**: If Common Debtor mortgages Property 1 + Property 2 to Creditor A, and subsequently mortgages only Property 1 to Creditor B:
  - Creditor B can insist that Creditor A satisfy its debt first out of **Property 2**.
- **Essential Safeguard**: Marshalling cannot prejudice the rights of the prior mortgagee (Creditor A must not suffer loss) or any subsequent purchaser for value.

### 3. Doctrine of Contribution (Section 82)
- **Rule**: Where several properties are mortgaged together to secure a single common debt, and the ownership of the properties subsequently vests in different persons, each property must contribute to the debt **rateably according to its relative market value** on the date of the mortgage.
- **Formula**: $\text{Contribution of Property A} = \text{Total Debt} \times \frac{\text{Value of Property A}}{\text{Value of Property A} + \text{Value of Property B}}$.

### 4. Doctrine of Subrogation (Section 92)
- **Meaning**: 'Stepping into the shoes of the redeemed creditor'.
- **Legal Subrogation (Sec 92 Para 1)**: Arises by operation of law when a person with an interest (e.g. second mortgagee, co-mortgagor, surety) pays off a prior mortgage.
- **Conventional Subrogation (Sec 92 Para 3)**: Arises by written registered contract when a third-party lender advances money to the mortgagor specifically to pay off a prior mortgage.
- **Full Subrogation Rule**: Subrogation is permitted ONLY when the prior mortgage debt is paid off **in FULL** (partial subrogation is prohibited).12:T402,### High-Yield Traps for Banking Exams
1. **Section 78 Gross Neglect Trap**: If Bank A grants a loan and carelessly leaves the original title deeds with the borrower (or returns them for 'safe custody'), and the borrower uses those deeds to take an equitable mortgage from Bank B, **Bank A loses its priority to Bank B under Section 78** due to gross neglect.
2. **The Mortgagor Cannot Claim Subrogation**: Section 92 explicitly states that the **Mortgagor cannot claim subrogation** by paying their own debt (paying one's own debt merely extinguishes the mortgage; it does not subrogate).
3. **Full Payment Requirement in Subrogation**: A person paying only 80% or 90% of a prior mortgage cannot claim partial subrogation. Subrogation operates **strictly upon full and final discharge** of the prior mortgage.
4. **Marshalling vs Contribution Conflict**: Marshalling (Sec 81) regulates the rights of *competing mortgagees*, while Contribution (Sec 82) regulates the liabilities of *different properties / co-owners* rateably.13:T415,### Architectural Framework: Mortgage Equity Doctrines (TP Act 1882)
- **Rule of Priority (Sections 48 & 78)**:
  - General Rule: Chronological priority (*qui prior est tempore potior est jure*).
  - Section 78 Exception: Postponement of prior mortgagee for Fraud, Misrepresentation, or Gross Neglect.
- **Doctrine of Marshalling (Section 81)**:
  - Beneficiary: Subsequent mortgagee.
  - Mandate: Prior lender must exhaust unencumbered collateral first.
  - Limitation: Must not prejudice prior lender or third-party bona fide purchasers.
- **Doctrine of Contribution (Section 82)**:
  - Beneficiary: Co-owners / Distinct property mortgagors.
  - Mandate: Debt shared rateably in proportion to property values at mortgage date.
- **Doctrine of Subrogation (Section 92)**:
  - Meaning: Stepping into the priority shoes of the paid-off creditor.
  - Types: Legal Subrogation (Sec 91 interested party) & Conventional Subrogation (registered contract).
  - Prerequisite: Must pay off the entire prior mortgage debt in FULL (no partial
