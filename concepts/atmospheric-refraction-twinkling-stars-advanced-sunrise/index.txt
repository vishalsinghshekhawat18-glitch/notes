12:T5d7,### Master Register of Atmospheric Refraction Phenomena

| Natural Phenomenon | Underlying Optical Mechanism | Key Scientific Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Twinkling of Stars** | **Atmospheric Refraction** of point sources through turbulent air layers. | Point source light ray is bent randomly; apparent brightness and position fluctuate rapidly. |
| **Why Planets Do Not Twinkle** | **Extended Source Averaging** | Planets are close $\implies$ disk of billions of point sources; random fluctuations cancel out to steady total light. |
| **Apparent Higher Position of Stars**| Continuous bending **TOWARDS the normal** as light descends into denser air. | Starlight curves down $\implies$ tangent to ray points **higher than true position**. |
| **Advanced Sunrise (~2 mins early)** | Atmospheric refraction bending sunlight over the horizon curvature. | Sun is seen when it is still **$0.5^\circ$ below the real geometric horizon**. |
| **Delayed Sunset (~2 mins late)** | Atmospheric refraction keeping sun rays bent over horizon. | Sun is visible for 2 minutes after dipping below true geometric horizon. |
| **Net Day Length Extension** | $2 \text{ mins at sunrise} + 2 \text{ mins at sunset} = \mathbf{\approx 4 \text{ MINUTES}}$. | Actual daylight is lengthened by 4 minutes every single day! |
| **Oval / Flattened Sun at Horizon** | **Differential Atmospheric Refraction** | Bottom edge of solar disc is refracted more than top edge $\implies$ vertical compression into an oval.
