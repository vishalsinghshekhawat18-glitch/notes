11:T500,NITI Aayog was conceptualized to transition India from centralized "one-size-fits-all" socialist planning to competitive and cooperative federalism:

1. Organizational Structure:
   • Chairperson: Prime Minister of India.
   • Vice-Chairperson: Appointed by the PM (holds Cabinet Minister rank).
   • Full-Time Members: Hold Minister of State rank.
   • Ex-Officio Members: Max 4 Union Cabinet Ministers nominated by the PM.
   • Special Invitees: Experts and specialists nominated by the PM.
   • Chief Executive Officer (CEO): Appointed by the PM in the rank of Secretary to the Government of India for a fixed tenure.

2. Key Operational Hubs:
   • Team India Hub: Leads state-centre engagement and acts as a direct interface for states in New Delhi.
   • Knowledge and Innovation Hub: Builds think-tank research capabilities, innovation ecosystems (Atal Innovation Mission), and evidence-based policy benchmarking.

3. Flagship Monitoring Frameworks:
   • SDG India Index (tracks state-level progress on 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals).
   • Aspirational Districts Programme (data-driven convergence and competitive ranking of 112 underdeveloped districts across India).
