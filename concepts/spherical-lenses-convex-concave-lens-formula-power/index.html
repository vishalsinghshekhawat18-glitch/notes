Spherical Lenses (Convex & Concave): Ray Tracing, Lens Formula & Power (Dioptres)

The geometrical refraction through curved transparent lenses, image formation rules for convex (converging) and concave (diverging) lenses, and lens power in Dioptres.

Intuition & Context

How a Simple Glass Lens Can Start a Fire With Sunlight

If you hold a convex magnifying glass over dry leaves on a sunny day, parallel rays of sunlight passing through the broad glass lens are bent and forced to converge into a single tiny, searing dot of blinding light. That hot dot is the Principal Focus (FFF) of the lens. The shorter the distance to that dot, the more violently the lens bends light, and the higher its optical Power (Dioptres).

Convex vs Concave Lens Image Formation Rules

1. Convex (Converging) Lens Image Behavior

  • Object at ∞  ⟹  \infty \implies∞⟹ Real, inverted, point image at F2F_2F2​.
  • Object beyond 2F1  ⟹  2F_1 \implies2F1​⟹ Real, inverted, diminished image between F2F_2F2​ and 2F22F_22F2​ (Camera lens).
  • Object at 2F1  ⟹  2F_1 \implies2F1​⟹ Real, inverted, same size image at 2F22F_22F2​ (m=−1m = -1m=−1).
  • Object between F1F_1F1​ and 2F1  ⟹  2F_1 \implies2F1​⟹ Real, inverted, enlarged image beyond 2F22F_22F2​ (Slide projector).
  • Object at F1  ⟹  F_1 \impliesF1​⟹ Real, inverted, infinitely large image at ∞\infty∞ (Searchlight collimator).
  • Object between Optical Centre (OOO) and Focus (F1F_1F1​):
    • Forms a Virtual, Erect, and Enlarged image on the same side as the object (m>+1m > +1m>+1).
    • This is the Simple Microscope / Hand Magnifying Glass!

2. Concave (Diverging) Lens Image Behavior

  • For ALL object positions anywhere in front of the lens:
    • ALWAYS forms a Virtual, Erect, and Diminished image between optical centre OOO and focus F1F_1F1​ on the same side as object ($0 < m < +1$).
    • Used for Myopia (Near-sightedness) spectacles.
