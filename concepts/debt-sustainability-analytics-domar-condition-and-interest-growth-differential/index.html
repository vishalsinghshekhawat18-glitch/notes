Debt Sustainability Analytics: The Domar Condition, (r - g) Differential & The Fundamental Debt Dynamics Equation

The mathematical derivations, analytical conditions, and macroeconomic mechanisms governing sovereign Debt Sustainability. Derives the Fundamental Equation of Debt Dynamics: $\Delta d_t = \left(\frac{r - g}{1 + g}\right) d_{t-1} - pb_t$, where $d$ is the Debt-to-GDP ratio, $r$ is the effective real interest rate on public debt, $g$ is the real GDP growth rate, and $pb_t$ is the Primary Balance as a share of GDP. Analyzes the Domar Debt Sustainability Condition ($g > r$ or $r - g < 0$: an economy where real growth exceeds real interest rate can run primary deficits while its debt ratio automatically stabilizes) versus a debt spiral scenario ($r > g$).

Intuition & Context

The Treadmill of Public Debt: Outrunning the Interest Rate

Imagine you are running on a fitness treadmill:

  • The treadmill speed is the Interest Rate (r=7%r = 7%r=7%) pushing you backwards into debt.
  • Your running speed is your Income / GDP Growth Rate (g=11%g = 11%g=11%) moving you forward.

Because you are running faster than the treadmill (g>rg > rg>r, a negative r−gr - gr−g differential of −4%-4%−4%), you are constantly pulling ahead! Even if you stop and eat a snack (run a small primary deficit), your body weight relative to your wealth naturally declines!

India has enjoyed a negative (r−g)(r - g)(r−g) differential for over four decades, meaning India's high economic growth has been the primary engine keeping its national debt sustainable without harsh austerity.

The Two Levers of Debt Stability

  1. The Snowball Effect: (r−g1+g)dt−1\left( \frac{r - g}{1 + g} \right) d_{t-1}(1+gr−g​)dt−1​ (Driven by macroeconomic growth vs borrowing cost).
  2. The Primary Balance: pbtpb_tpbt​ (Driven by discretionary tax collections and expenditure control).

Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Exam Traps & Multi-Exam Crosswalks

Critical Examiner Traps

  • Trap 1: Domar Condition Inequality: Debt ratio stabilizes when g>rg > rg>r (Growth rate is GREATER than interest rate). If r>gr > gr>g, the debt ratio explodes unless there is a primary surplus!
  • Trap 2: Primary Balance vs Primary Deficit: In the debt equation, a Primary Deficit is entered as a negative primary balance (−pbt-pb_t−pbt​), which increases debt (Δd>0\Delta d > 0Δd>0). A Primary Surplus (pbt>0pb_t > 0pbt​>0) reduces debt.
  • Trap 3: Real vs Nominal in (r−g)(r - g)(r−g): You can use Nominal rrr and Nominal ggg, OR Real rrr and Real ggg. The differential (r−g)(r - g)(r−g) is mathematically identical because inflation cancels out in both terms!
  • Trap 4: Economic Survey 2020-21 Focus: The Economic Survey dedicated a flagship chapter arguing that for India, growth leads to debt sustainability, making public investment in CapEx self-financing.

Cross-Exam Focus

  • RBI Grade B & SEBI Grade A: Mathematical derivations of Δd\Delta dΔd, Domar steady-state debt ratio (d∗d^*d∗), and Economic Survey debt sustainability analytics.
  • RPSC RAS & UPSC APFC: 2-mark definitions of Domar Condition / (r−g)(r - g)(r−g) differential, and 5-mark essays on Debt Sustainability in India.
