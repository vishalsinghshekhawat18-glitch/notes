11:T49a,### The Scientific Work Formula:
$$W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s} = F \cdot s \cdot \cos\theta$$

### Variable Definitions:
- $W = \text{Work done (Joules, J)}$
- $F = \text{Magnitude of applied force (Newtons, N)}$
- $s = \text{Magnitude of displacement (metres, m)}$
- $\theta = \text{Angle between the force vector } \vec{F} \text{ and displacement vector } \vec{s}$

### Classification by Angle $\theta$:

| Work Type | Angle Range | Sign of $\cos\theta$ | Real-World Physical Example |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Positive Work** | $0^\circ \le \theta < 90^\circ$ | Positive ($>0$) | • A horse pulling a cart forward along a road.<br>• Gravity doing work on a falling apple ($\theta = 0^\circ$). |
| **Negative Work** | $90^\circ < \theta \le 180^\circ$ | Negative ($<0$) | • Frictional force opposing sliding motion ($\theta = 180^\circ, \cos 180^\circ = -1$).<br>• Gravity doing work when throwing a ball upward. |
| **Zero Work** | $\theta = 90^\circ$ OR $s = 0$ | Zero ($=0$) | • Pushing a stationary wall ($s = 0$).<br>• Porter walking horizontally with luggage ($\theta = 90^\circ$).<br>• Centripetal force in circular orbits ($\theta = 90^\circ$). |12:T41d,### 1. The Railway Porter Paradox (Why $W = 0$)
- Porter lifts suitcase upward onto his head: **Positive Work** ($W = +mgh$).
- Porter holds suitcase stationary on head: **Zero Work** ($s = 0$).
- Porter walks horizontally with velocity $v$:
  - Downward gravitational force: $\vec{F}_g = mg \hat{j}$ (vertically downward).
  - Horizontal displacement: $\vec{s} = s \hat{i}$ (horizontal).
  - Angle between force and displacement: $\theta = 90^\circ$.
  $$W_{\text{gravity}} = F \cdot s \cdot \cos 90^\circ = F \cdot s \cdot 0 = \mathbf{0 \, \text{Joules}}$$

### 2. Work Done by Gravitational Force on Orbiting Satellites
At every point along a circular orbit:
- Gravitational force $\vec{F}_g$ acts radially **inward towards Earth's centre**.
- Instantaneous displacement $d\vec{s}$ is **tangential** to the circular orbit.
- Angle $\theta = 90^\circ$ at all times $\implies W = \int F_g \cdot ds \cdot \cos 90^\circ = \mathbf{0}$.
- *Consequence*: Earth's gravity does zero work on the Moon; the Moon circles forever without losing mechanical
