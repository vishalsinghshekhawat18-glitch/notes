3. **The Systemic Contagion Channel**:

```

• Short-term CP rollover      • Debt mutual funds suffer     • Banks hold 60% of total
  fails completely              redemption runs                NBFC wholesale bank loans
• Construction & capex loans  • Liquidity drying up across   • Risk of fresh systemic
  freeze nationwide             commercial paper market        NPA wave across banks
```
