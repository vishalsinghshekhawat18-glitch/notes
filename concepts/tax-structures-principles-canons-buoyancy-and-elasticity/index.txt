11:T45f,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: Tax Buoyancy vs Elasticity**: **Tax Buoyancy includes discretionary changes** (rate hikes, new cesses). **Tax Elasticity excludes discretionary changes** (pure automatic response).
- **Trap 2: Regressive Tax Definition**: A regressive tax does NOT mean rich people pay less nominal money; it means the tax represents a **lower percentage of income** as income increases.
- **Trap 3: Adam Smith's Canons**: The four original canons are **Equality, Certainty, Convenience, Economy**. (Canons like Productivity and Elasticity were added later by Charles Bastable).
- **Trap 4: Benefit Principle vs Ability to Pay**: The Benefit Principle (paying based on benefits received, like highway tolls) cannot be used to fund redistributive welfare, because the poor who need welfare have zero ability to pay for it.

### Cross-Exam Focus
- **RBI Grade B & SEBI Grade A**: Tax buoyancy vs elasticity formulas, Harberger efficiency, and progressive rate mathematics.
