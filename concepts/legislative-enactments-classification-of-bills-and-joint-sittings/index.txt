12:T5d6,### Joint Sitting (Article 108):
• **Trigger:** If after a bill is passed by one House and sent to the other: (a) rejected by the other House, (b) Houses disagree on amendments, or (c) more than **6 months elapse** without being passed.
• **Summoned by:** The **President of India** by notification.
• **Presided by:** **Speaker of Lok Sabha** $\rightarrow$ in absence, Deputy Speaker $\rightarrow$ in absence, Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha (⚠️ **Chairman of Rajya Sabha NEVER presides**).
• **Majority Required:** **Simple Majority** of the total number of members of both Houses present and voting (Lok Sabha’s numerical superiority of 543 vs 245 usually guarantees passage).
• **Strict Bars:** **NEVER held for Money Bills (Art 109) or Constitutional Amendment Bills (Art 368)**.

### Rules on Lapse of Bills upon Lok Sabha Dissolution (Article 107):
• **BILLS THAT LAPSE:**
  1. A Bill pending in the **Lok Sabha** (whether originated in LS or transmitted from RS).
  2. A Bill passed by the **Lok Sabha** but pending in the **Rajya Sabha**.
• **BILLS THAT DO NOT LAPSE:**
  1. A Bill originated in the **Rajya Sabha and pending in the Rajya Sabha** (not yet passed by Lok Sabha).
  2. A Bill passed by **BOTH Houses** and waiting for the **Assent of the President**.
  3. A Bill passed by **BOTH Houses** and returned by the President for reconsideration.
  4.
