Corrosion of Metals (Rusting, Patina & Tarnishing), Prevention & Food Rancidity

The electrochemical deterioration of metals by atmospheric moisture and gases (rusting of iron, copper patina, silver tarnishing), corrosion prevention methods (galvanization, sacrificial anodes), and oxidative food spoilage (rancidity).

Intuition & Context

Why the Statue of Liberty is Green and Potato Chip Bags are Puffy

The Statue of Liberty in New York was originally shiny reddish-brown because its skin is made of pure Copper metal. Over 100 years of exposure to salty air and rain, copper reacted with atmospheric CO2CO_2CO2​ and moisture to form a protective green crust (Basic Copper Carbonate Patina). Meanwhile, when you open a bag of potato chips, it is inflated like a balloon with Nitrogen gas (N2N_2N2​)—not to cheat you on chips, but to displace all oxygen so fatty oils don't turn stinking and sour (Rancidity Prevention)!

How It Works / Mechanism

Electrochemical Rusting & The Zinc Galvanization Shield

1. Electrochemical Mechanism of Rusting:

  • An iron surface acts as a tiny electrochemical cell:
    • Anode: Fe⟶Fe2++2e−Fe \longrightarrow Fe^{2+} + 2e^-Fe⟶Fe2++2e−
    • Cathode: O2+4H++4e−⟶2H2OO_2 + 4H^+ + 4e^- \longrightarrow 2H_2OO2​+4H++4e−⟶2H2​O
    • Overall Rust Formation: 4Fe2++O2+4H2O+2xH2O⟶2Fe2O3⋅xH2O (Rust)+8H+4Fe^{2+} + O_2 + 4H_2O + 2xH_2O \longrightarrow \mathbf{2Fe_2O_3 \cdot xH_2O \text{ (Rust)}} + 8H^+4Fe2++O2​+4H2​O+2xH2​O⟶2Fe2​O3​⋅xH2​O (Rust)+8H+

2. Why Zinc Galvanization Protects Even When Scratched:

  • Zinc is more electropositive and more reactive than Iron in the reactivity series (Zn>FeZn > FeZn>Fe).
  • If a scratch exposes iron to moist air, Zinc oxidizes preferentially (Zn→Zn2++2e−Zn \to Zn^{2+} + 2e^-Zn→Zn2++2e−), pumping electrons into iron to keep iron reduced in its metallic state (Cathodic / Sacrificial Protection).
  • If iron were coated with Tin (SnSnSn) instead, a scratch would cause iron to rust faster because iron is more reactive than tin!
Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps: Potato Chip Gas & Rust Chemical Formula

Top Competitive Exam Traps:

  1. The Potato Chip Packaging Gas:
    • Question: "Which gas is flushed into plastic bags of potato chips to prevent rancidity?"
    • Answer: NITROGEN Gas (N2N_2N2​) (It is an unreactive, inert gas that displaces oxygen).
  2. Chemical Formula of Iron Rust:
    • Trap: Answering FeOFeOFeO or anhydrous Fe2O3Fe_2O_3Fe2​O3​.
    • Correct: Fe2O3⋅xH2O\mathbf{Fe_2O_3 \cdot xH_2O}Fe2​O3​⋅xH2​O (Hydrated Ferric Oxide) where xxx is variable water of hydration.
  3. Rusting Essential Conditions:
    • In dry air (with anhydrous calcium chloride desiccant): Zero rusting.
    • In boiled distilled water covered with oil film (zero dissolved oxygen): Zero rusting.
    • Rusting STRICTLY requires BOTH Air (O2O_2O2​) AND Moisture (H2OH_2OH2​O) simultaneously!
