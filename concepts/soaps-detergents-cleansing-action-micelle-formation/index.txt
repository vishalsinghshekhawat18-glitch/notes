13:T405,### Comparison: Soaps vs Synthetic Detergents

| Parameter / Feature | Soaps (Traditional Cleansers) | Synthetic Detergents (Modern Cleansers) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chemical Definition** | **Sodium or Potassium salts of long-chain fatty acids** ($C_{17}H_{35}COONa$). | **Sodium salts of long-chain Alkyl Sulphonic acids** ($-SO_3^- Na^+$). |
| **Active Ionic Head** | **Carboxylate ion ($-COO^- Na^+$)** | **Sulphonate ($-SO_3^- Na^+$) or Sulphate ($-OSO_3^- Na^+$)** |
| **Source Material** | Natural plant oils & animal fats. | Petroleum hydrocarbons. |
| **Action in Hard Water** | **FAILS: Forms sticky insoluble SCUM** with $Ca^{2+}$ and $Mg^{2+}$. | **EXCELLENT: Lathers easily in hard water** (no scum formation). |
| **Action in Acidic Water**| Fails (precipitates as free fatty acids). | Cleans effectively even in mildly acidic water. |
| **Biodegradability** | **100% Biodegradable** (Eco-friendly; bacteria consume fatty chains). | Linear alkyl chains are biodegradable; branched chains cause river foaming. |14:T578,### 1. Anatomy of a Soap Molecule ($C_{17}H_{35}COO^- Na^+$):

```
       Hydrophobic Tail                          Hydrophilic Head
     (Non-polar hydrocarbon)                    (Polar ionic group)
   ┌───────────────────────────┐                     ┌──────┐
   │ ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ │ ─────────────────── │ -COO⁻│ Na⁺
   └───────────────────────────┘                     └──────┘
       • Oil-soluble / Lipophilic                        • Water-soluble
       • Repelled by water                               • Attracted to water
````

### 2. The Spherical Micelle Cluster:
- In water, hundreds of soap molecules orient radially into a sphere:
  - **Tails point inwards**, dissolving into the central oily dirt droplet.
  - **Heads point outwards**, forming a negatively charged exterior shell.
  - Mutual electrostatic repulsion between negative head shells prevents micelles from clumping, forming a stable **Emulsion** washed away by rinsing!
