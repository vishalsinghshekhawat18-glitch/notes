14:T696,Public Service Commissions in India are bifurcated across three distinct legal entities:

| Feature / Dimension | Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) | State Public Service Commission (SPSC) | Joint State PSC (JSPSC) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Constitutional Status** | Constitutional Body (Article 315) | Constitutional Body (Article 315) | **Statutory Body** (Created by Act of Parliament) |
| **Appointing Authority** | President of India | **Governor of the State** | President of India |
| **Tenure & Age Limit** | **6 Years or 65 Years** of age | **6 Years or 62 Years** of age | **6 Years or 62 Years** of age |
| **Removal Authority** | **President** (on SC inquiry per Art 317) | **President exclusively** (NOT Governor) | **President** (on SC inquiry per Art 317) |
| **Resignation Submitted To** | President of India | Governor of the State | President of India |
| **Expense Charged On** | **Consolidated Fund of India** (Non-votable) | **Consolidated Fund of State** (Non-votable) | Respective Consolidated Funds of participating States |
| **Annual Report Submitted To** | President $\to$ Tabled in Parliament | Governor $\to$ Tabled in State Legislature | Governors of participating States |

### Post-Retirement Employment Disqualifications (Article 319)
- **UPSC Chairman**: Ineligible for any further employment under the Government of India or a State.
- **UPSC Member**: Eligible only for appointment as Chairman of UPSC or Chairman of an SPSC.
- **SPSC Chairman**: Eligible for appointment as Chairman/Member of UPSC or Chairman of another SPSC.
- **SPSC Member**: Eligible for appointment as Chairman/Member of UPSC or Chairman of that or another SPSC.15:T5ce,Under Article 320(3), the Executive is constitutionally mandated to consult the Public Service Commission on:
1. All matters relating to methods of recruitment to civil services and civil posts.
2. Principles to be followed in making appointments, promotions, and transfers from one service to another.
3. All disciplinary matters affecting a person serving under the government (censure, withholding increments, demotion, compulsory retirement, dismissal).
4. Any claim for reimbursement of legal expenses incurred by a civil servant defending official actions.

### The Advisory Nature vs Accountability Loop
```
                      UPSC / SPSC RECOMMENDATION WORKFLOW
                                       │
   1. Government seeks advice on recruitment, promotions, or disciplinary penalty
                                       │
   2. Commission investigates record and submits independent statutory advice
                                       │
   3. Government evaluates advice (UPSC advice is ADVISORY, not legally binding)
                                       │
   4. IF GOVERNMENT REJECTS ADVICE:
      Article 323 mandates Cabinet Appointments Committee must record reasons
                                       │
   5. Annual Report + Explanatory Memorandum of Non-Acceptance placed before Parliament
                                       │
                                       ▼
          PARLIAMENTARY SCRUTINY & PUBLIC ACCOUNTABILITY ENFORCED
```16:T477,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: Who Removes an SPSC Member?**: While an SPSC Chairman/member is **appointed by the Governor**, they can be **removed ONLY by the President of India** under Article 317 (after a Supreme Court inquiry). The Governor has power only to suspend them pending inquiry, NOT remove them.
- **Trap 2: Is a JSPSC a Constitutional Body?**: **NO**. A Joint State Public Service Commission is a **Statutory Body** created by an Act of Parliament (e.g. for Punjab and Haryana), whereas UPSC and SPSCs are directly created by Article 315.
- **Trap 3: Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) Overlap**: In disciplinary matters, when CVC and UPSC advice conflict, UPSC (as a constitutional body) advice takes precedence over statutory bodies, but the final decision rests with the Disciplinary Authority.
