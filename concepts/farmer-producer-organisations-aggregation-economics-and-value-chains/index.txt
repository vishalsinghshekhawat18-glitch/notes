Each farmer grows 5 tons of tomatoes on a 1-acre plot:\n1. **Input Disadvantage**: When buying fertilizer, each farmer buys 5 bags at retail price (₹1,500/bag). They have zero negotiating power.\n2. **Output Tragedy**: When harvesting, 300 farmers rush to the local mandi simultaneously with overloaded tempo trucks. Wholesale traders cartelize and offer a distressed price of ₹3 per kg.
