12:T5df,The six members of the MPC are structured as follows:

```
                         MONETARY POLICY COMMITTEE (6 Members)
                                          │
        ┌─────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                                                   ▼
  3 INTERNAL RBI MEMBERS                                           3 EXTERNAL EXPERTS
1. Governor, RBI (Ex-Officio Chairperson)                 4. Appointed by Central Government
2. Deputy Governor in charge of Monetary Policy           5. Appointed by Central Government
3. One RBI Officer nominated by Central Board             6. Appointed by Central Government
```

### The Search-cum-Selection Committee (Section 45ZC)
External members are selected by a high-powered 6-member committee comprising:
1. **Cabinet Secretary** (Chairperson)
2. **Governor, Reserve Bank of India**
3. **Secretary, Department of Economic Affairs (DEA)**, Ministry of Finance
4. **Three external experts in economics/banking** nominated by the Central Government.

### Tenure & Eligibility Rules
- **Tenure of External Members**: **4 Years**.
- **Reappointment**: **Strictly prohibited** (Section 45ZD(2)).
- **Qualifications**: Persons of ability, integrity, and standing having knowledge and experience in economics, banking, finance, or monetary policy.13:T558,The statutory operating rules governing MPC meetings under Sections 45ZI to 45ZM are strictly defined:

### 1. Meeting Frequency & Quorum (Section 45ZI)
- **Frequency**: The RBI must organize at least **4 MPC meetings every year** (in practice, RBI conducts 6 bi-monthly meetings per year).
- **Quorum**: **4 members**, of which at least one must be the Governor (or Deputy Governor in Governor's absence).

### 2. Voting Mechanics (One-Person-One-Vote & Casting Vote)
- Every member has **one vote**.
- Decisions are taken by a **majority of votes** of the members present and voting.
- **Casting Vote**: If there is a tie ($3-3$), the **Governor of RBI exercises a second or casting vote** (e.g., breaking the tie to hike or hold rates).

### 3. Statutory Transparency & Publication Calendar
- **Day of Decision (End of Meeting)**: The MPC publishes its formal **Resolution** (including the policy repo rate decision and the vote count of each member).
- **14th Day after Meeting (Section 45ZK)**: The RBI must publish the detailed **Minutes of the Proceedings**, containing: (a) the resolution adopted, (b) the vote of each member, and (c) the statement of each member explaining their voting rationale.
- **Every Six Months (Section 45ZL)**: RBI publishes the **Monetary Policy Report (MPR)** explaining inflation sources and forecasts for the next 6 to 18 months.14:T454,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: Who heads the Search Committee vs Who heads the MPC?**:
  - The **MPC is headed by the Governor of RBI**.
  - The **Search-cum-Selection Committee for external members is headed by the Cabinet Secretary**.
- **Trap 2: Can External Members be Reappointed?**: No. External members hold office for **4 years and are NOT eligible for reappointment** (Section 45ZD).
- **Trap 3: MPC vs RBI Institutional Powers**: The MPC **only sets the Policy Repo Rate**. It does **NOT** set CRR, SLR, regulatory capital requirements, or foreign exchange interventions—those remain under the sole jurisdiction of the RBI as a central banking institution.
- **Trap 4: When are Minutes Published?**: On the **14th day** after the meeting, NOT immediately and NOT at the next meeting.

### Cross-Exam Focus
- **RBI Grade B & SEBI Grade A**: Complete mastery of Sections 45ZB, 45ZC, 45ZI, 45ZK, quorum (4), casting vote rules, and 14-day minute publication rule.
