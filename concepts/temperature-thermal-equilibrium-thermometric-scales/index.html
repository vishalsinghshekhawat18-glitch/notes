Temperature, Thermal Equilibrium, Zeroth Law & Thermometric Scales (C, F, K)

The thermal measure of average molecular kinetic energy, the Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics, and interconversion across Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin temperature scales.

Intuition & Context

Why a Hot Metal Spoon in Ice Water Changes Everything

If you place a red-hot iron spoon into a bowl of ice water, heat flows spontaneously from the hot spoon into the cold water until both reach the exact same temperature—a state called Thermal Equilibrium. You can feel heat, but your hand cannot accurately measure degrees (if you touch cold water with a warm hand it feels freezing; with a colder hand it feels warm). To measure temperature objectively, scientists created calibrated Thermometric Scales.
