### Detailed Institutional Matrix
1. **NABARD (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development - 1982)**:
   - Established under **NABARD Act, 1981** based on the **B. Sivaraman Committee (CRAFICARD)**.
   - Mandate: Apex refinance institution for agriculture, allied activities, and rural development; manages the **Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF)**; supervises RRBs and Cooperative Banks.
2. **SIDBI (Small Industries Development Bank of India - 1990)**:
   - Established under **SIDBI Act, 1989**.
   - Mandate: Promotion, financing, and development of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs); operates **MUDRA (Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency)** and **CGTMSE**.
3. **NHB (National Housing Bank - 1988)**:
   - Established under **NHB Act, 1987** based on the **C. Rangarajan High-Level Group**.
   - Mandate: Apex housing finance refinancing institution (supervisory powers over HFCs transferred to RBI in 2019; NHB remains primary refinancer).
4. **EXIM Bank (Export-Import Bank of India - 1982)**:
   - Established under **EXIM Bank Act, 1981**.
   - Mandate: Financing, facilitating, and promoting India's international trade and overseas Lines of Credit (LOCs).
5. **NaBFID (National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development - 2021)**:
   - Established under **NaBFID Act, 2021**.
   - Mandate: Dedicated infrastructure DFI to fund the **₹111 Lakh Crore National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP)**; granted a 10-year income-tax concession and sovereign credit guarantees.12:T404,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: How many AIFIs are regulated by RBI?**: There are currently **FIVE AIFIs: NABARD, SIDBI, NHB, EXIM Bank, and NaBFID** (NaBFID was added as the 5th AIFI in 2021 under Section 45L).
- **Trap 2: NABARD Committee**: NABARD was created on the recommendations of the **B. Sivaraman Committee (CRAFICARD)** in 1979/1981 (NOT the Narasimham Committee!).
- **Trap 3: NHB Ownership**: The RBI sold its entire 100% equity stake in NHB (and NABARD) to the **Government of India in 2019** to eliminate conflict of interest (RBI cannot own the banks it regulates).
- **Trap 4: IFSCA Headquarters**: The International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) is headquartered at **GIFT City, Gandhinagar, Gujarat**.
