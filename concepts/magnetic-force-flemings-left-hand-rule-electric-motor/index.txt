11:T447,### Magnetic Force Equation:
$$\mathbf{F = B \cdot I \cdot L \cdot \sin\theta}$$

- $F = \text{Mechanical Force (Newtons, N)}$
- $B = \text{Magnetic Field Strength (Tesla, T)}$
- $I = \text{Electric Current (Amperes, A)}$
- $L = \text{Length of conductor inside magnetic field (metres, m)}$
- $\theta = \text{Angle between Current direction and Magnetic Field lines}$

### Critical Angle Cases:
- **Perpendicular ($\theta = 90^\circ$)**: $\sin 90^\circ = 1 \implies \mathbf{F_{\text{max}} = B I L}$ (**Maximum Force**).
- **Parallel ($\theta = 0^\circ \text{ or } 180^\circ$)**: $\sin 0^\circ = 0 \implies \mathbf{F = 0}$ (**Zero Force**).

---

### Fleming's Left-Hand Rule (The "FBI" Motor Rule):

| Left Hand Finger | Physical Quantity | Standard Symbol | Direction |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Thumb** | **Force / Motion** | $\mathbf{F}$ | Direction in which wire is pushed. |
| **Forefinger (Index)** | **Magnetic Field** | $\mathbf{B}$ | Points from North ($N$) to South ($S$). |
| **Middle Finger** | **Electric Current** | $\mathbf{I}$ | Conventional current flow ($+$ to $-$). |12:T4df,### Core Components of a DC Electric Motor:
1. **Armature Coil**: A rectangular loop of insulated copper wire ($ABCD$) mounted on an axle.
2. **Permanent Field Magnets**: Horseshoe magnet providing a strong horizontal magnetic field from North to South.
3. **Split-Ring Commutator ($P$ and $Q$)**: Two semi-cylindrical brass rings that rotate with the coil.
4. **Carbon Brushes ($X$ and $Y$)**: Stationary graphite blocks that slide against the rotating split rings to feed DC current from the battery.

### Step-by-Step Rotational Mechanism:
- **0° to 180° (First Half-Cycle)**: Current flows from $A \to B$ on left arm and $C \to D$ on right arm.
  - Applying Fleming's Left-Hand Rule: Arm $AB$ is pushed **DOWN**, arm $CD$ is pushed **UP** $\implies$ Coil rotates **Anticlockwise**.
- **At 180° (The Commutator Reversal)**:
  - If current continued in the same direction, the forces would reverse and snap the coil backward.
  - The **Split-Ring Commutator reverses contact with the carbon brushes**, inverting the current flow ($D \to C$ and $B \to A$).
- **180° to 360° (Second Half-Cycle)**: The new left arm is pushed DOWN and new right arm is pushed UP $\implies$ **Coil continues rotating in the EXACT SAME anticlockwise
