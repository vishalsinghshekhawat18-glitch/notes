12:T56c,A famous mnemonic to remember the 12 Schedules in exact order:

• **T — Territory:** 1st Schedule (Names of States & UTs with boundaries — Arts 1 & 4)
• **E — Emoluments:** 2nd Schedule (Salaries of President, Governors, Judges, CAG — Arts 59, 65, 75, 97, 125, 148, 158, 164, 186, 221)
• **A — Affirmations / Oaths:** 3rd Schedule (Oaths for Ministers, MPs, Judges, CAG — Arts 75, 84, 99, 124, 146, 173, 188, 219)
• **R — Rajya Sabha:** 4th Schedule (Allocation of Rajya Sabha seats to States/UTs — Arts 4(1) & 80(2))
• **S — Scheduled Areas:** 5th Schedule (Administration of Scheduled Areas/Tribes in 10 states — Art 244(1))
• **O — Other Tribal Areas:** 6th Schedule (Tribal Areas in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram — Arts 244(2) & 275(1))
• **F — Federal Lists:** 7th Schedule (Union, State, Concurrent Lists — Art 246)
• **O — Official Languages:** 8th Schedule (22 recognized languages — Arts 344(1) & 351)
• **L — Land Reforms:** 9th Schedule (Validation of certain Acts & Regulations — Art 31B, 1st Amendment 1951)
• **D — Defection:** 10th Schedule (Anti-Defection provisions — Arts 102(2) & 191(2), 52nd Amendment 1985)
• **P — Panchayats:** 11th Schedule (29 functional items — Art 243G, 73rd Amendment 1992)
