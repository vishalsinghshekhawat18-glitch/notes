11:T818,### The Double Fertilisation Mathematical Architecture:

```
                          POLLEN GRAIN (Discharges 2 Male Gametes)
                                             │
             ┌───────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┐
             ▼                                                               ▼
   1st Male Gamete (Haploid, n)                                   2nd Male Gamete (Haploid, n)
             │                                                               │
             ▼  [ FUSES WITH ]                                               ▼  [ FUSES WITH ]
     Female Egg Cell (Haploid, n)                                   2 Polar Central Nuclei (2n)
             │                                                               │
             ▼  [ 1. SYNGAMY ]                                               ▼  [ 2. TRIPLE FUSION ]
     DIPLOID ZYGOTE (2n)                                            TRIPLOID ENDOSPERM NUCLEUS (3n)
             │                                                               │
             ▼                                                               ▼
       PLANT EMBRYO                                                   NUTRITIVE FOOD SUPPLY
````

| Floral Part Before Fertilisation | Post-Fertilisation Fate / Transformation | Real-World Culinary Analog |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ovary** | **Matures into the FRUIT** | The sweet fleshy mango pulp, tomato flesh. |
| **Ovule(s)** | **Transforms into the SEED(S)** | Mango seed stone, pea seeds in a pod. |
| **Zygote ($2n$)** | **Develops into the EMBRYO** | The baby root (radicle) and shoot (plumule). |
| **Endosperm ($3n$)**| **Develops into Food Storage tissue**| **Coconut Water & White Kernel ($3n$ Endosperm)**. |
| **Petals, Sepals, Stigma** | **Shrivel, wither, and fall away** | Dry dead caps on brinjals/apples.
