- **Crossing a Point Object (Pole, Standing Person, Tree):** Distance traveled equals the **Length of the Train ($L_{	ext{train}}$)**:
  $$	ext{Time} = rac{L_{	ext{train}}}{	ext{Speed}}$$

### 2. Boats & Streams Formulations
Let $u$ be the speed of the boat in still water, and $v$ be the speed of the water stream/current:
- **Downstream Speed ($S_d$):** $S_d = u + v$ (with current).
- **Upstream Speed ($S_u$):** $S_u = u - v$ (against current).
- **Fundamental Resolving Formulas:**
  $$	ext{Speed of Boat in Still Water } (u) = rac{S_d + S_u}{2}$$
  $$	ext{Speed of Stream / Current } (v) = rac{S_d - S_u}{2}$$
