Time, Speed & Distance, Relative Velocity, Trains, Boats & Moving Escalators

Kinematic arithmetic models: speed unit conversions (km/h to m/s), harmonic mean average speed, relative speed in same vs opposite directions, train platform crossing, boat-stream equations, moving escalators, and circular track meeting points.

Core Principle

The Motion Dynamics Architecture

Time, Speed & Distance (TSD) governs all motion problems in banking examinations.

Key structural principles include:

  1. Unit Consistency: Always convert units before calculating ($ ext{km/h} imes rac{5}{18} = ext{m/s}$; $ ext{m/s} imes rac{18}{5} = ext{km/h}$).
  2. Harmonic Average Speed: When covering equal distances at speeds xxx and yyy, the average speed is NOT $ rac{x+y}{2}$, but the Harmonic Mean: Savg=2xyx+yS_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2xy}{x+y}Savg​=x+y2xy​.
  3. Relative Velocity: Moving bodies in opposite directions add speeds (S1+S2S_1 + S_2S1​+S2​); moving in the same direction subtract speeds (∣S1−S2∣|S_1 - S_2|∣S1​−S2​∣).
SYSTEMATIC CATALOGUE

Train Dynamics, Boat-Stream Equations, Escalators & Circular Tracks

1. Train Crossing Invariants

  • Crossing a Point Object (Pole, Standing Person, Tree): Distance traveled equals the Length of the Train (LtrainL_{\text{train}}Ltrain​):
    extTime=LtrainSpeedext{Time} = \frac{L_{\text{train}}}{\text{Speed}}extTime=SpeedLtrain​​
  • Crossing an Extended Object (Platform, Bridge, Tunnel, another Train): Distance traveled equals the Sum of Lengths (Ltrain+LplatformL_{\text{train}} + L_{\text{platform}}Ltrain​+Lplatform​):
    extTime=Ltrain+LplatformRelative Speedext{Time} = \frac{L_{\text{train}} + L_{\text{platform}}}{\text{Relative Speed}}extTime=Relative SpeedLtrain​+Lplatform​​

2. Boats & Streams Formulations

Let uuu be the speed of the boat in still water, and vvv be the speed of the water stream/current:

  • Downstream Speed (SdS_dSd​): Sd=u+vS_d = u + vSd​=u+v (with current).
  • Upstream Speed (SuS_uSu​): Su=u−vS_u = u - vSu​=u−v (against current).
  • Fundamental Resolving Formulas:
    extSpeedofBoatinStillWater(u)=Sd+Su2ext{Speed of Boat in Still Water } (u) = \frac{S_d + S_u}{2}extSpeedofBoatinStillWater(u)=2Sd​+Su​​
    extSpeedofStream/Current(v)=Sd−Su2ext{Speed of Stream / Current } (v) = \frac{S_d - S_u}{2}extSpeedofStream/Current(v)=2Sd​−Su​​

3. Moving Walkways & Escalators

Let NNN be the total visible steps, vpv_pvp​ be person's walking rate (steps/sec), and vev_eve​ be escalator speed:

  • Walking WITH Escalator Movement (Same Direction):
    extTotalStepsS=Nwalked×(1+vevp)ext{Total Steps } S = N_{\text{walked}} \times \left(1 + \frac{v_e}{v_p}\right)extTotalStepsS=Nwalked​×(1+vp​ve​​)
  • Walking AGAINST Escalator Movement (Opposite Direction):
    extTotalStepsS=Nwalked×(1−vevp)ext{Total Steps } S = N_{\text{walked}} \times \left(1 - \frac{v_e}{v_p}\right)extTotalStepsS=Nwalked​×(1−vp​ve​​)

4. Races & Circular Track Meeting Points

Let two runners AAA and BBB run on a circular track of length LLL with speed ratio $ rac{S_A}{S_B} = rac{a}{b}$ in simplest coprime terms (gcd(a,b)=1gcd(a, b) = 1gcd(a,b)=1):

  1. Time for First Meeting Anywhere on Track:
    • Opposite Directions: T1=LSA+SBT_1 = \frac{L}{S_A + S_B}T1​=SA​+SB​L​
    • Same Direction: T1=L∣SA−SB∣T_1 = \frac{L}{|S_A - S_B|}T1​=∣SA​−SB​∣L​
  2. Number of Distinct Meeting Points on Track:
    • Opposite Directions: a+b\mathbf{a + b}a+b distinct points.
    • Same Direction: ∣a−b∣\mathbf{|a - b|}∣a−b∣ distinct points.
  3. Time to Meet at the Exact Starting Point for First Time:
    Tstart=LCM(LSA,LSB)T_{\text{start}} = \text{LCM}\left(\frac{L}{S_A}, \frac{L}{S_B}\right)Tstart​=LCM(SA​L​,SB​L​)

3-Tier Worked Problem (RBI Grade B / SBI PO Mains Benchmark)

Problem: Two runners A and B start simultaneously from the same point on a circular track of length $1,200 ext{ m}. A runs clockwise at $15\text{ m/s} and B runs counter-clockwise at $25 ext{ m/s}$. Find:

  1. Time for their first meeting anywhere on track.
  2. Number of distinct meeting points on the track.
  3. Time taken to meet at the starting point for the first time.
  • Tier 1 (First Principles):
    1. First Meeting: Running in opposite directions   ⟹  \implies⟹ Relative Speed =15+25=40 m/s= 15 + 25 = 40\text{ m/s}=15+25=40 m/s.

    3. Distinct Meeting Points:
      extDistinctPoints=TstartT1=24030=8 distinct pointsext{Distinct Points} = \frac{T_{\text{start}}}{T_1} = \frac{240}{30} = \mathbf{8\text{ distinct points}}extDistinctPoints=T1​Tstart​​=30240​=8 distinct points
