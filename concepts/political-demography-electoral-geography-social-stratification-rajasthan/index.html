Political Demography, Electoral Geography & Social Stratification of Rajasthan

Structural demographic and electoral geography of Rajasthan based on Census 2011 baseline data (6.85 Cr population, density 200/km2, sex ratio 928, literacy 66.1%), regional divisions (Marwar, Mewar, Dhundhar, Hadoti, Shekhawati), 200 Assembly seats (34 SC, 25 ST), and 25 Lok Sabha seats (4 SC, 3 ST).

Intuition & Context

Geographical Dichotomy & Political Geography

Rajasthan is India’s largest state by area (3.42 lakh sq km, comprising 10.41% of India’s geographical area). The Aravalli Range runs diagonally from southwest (Khedbrahma/Mount Abu) to northeast (Khetri/Delhi), dividing Rajasthan into two distinct geographical and demographic zones:

  1. Western Arid Zone (Thar Desert — ~61% area, ~40% population): Sparse density, pastoral/arid settlement patterns, water scarcity politics (Indira Gandhi Canal project).
  2. Eastern Semi-Arid & Sub-Humid Plains (~39% area, ~60% population): High agricultural productivity, dense population settlement, industrial corridors.
Core Principle

Five Historical-Cultural Political Regions of Rajasthan

Rajasthan's electoral behavior and political mobilization operate across five historical-cultural sub-regions:

  1. Marwar (Western Desert: Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, Barmer, Nagaur, Pali, Bikaner, Jalore):
    • Largest landmass, agrarian-pastoral politics, border security dynamics.
  2. Mewar / Vagad (Southern Aravalli & TSP Zone: Udaipur, Rajsamand, Chittorgarh, Bhilwara, Banswara, Dungarpur, Pratapgarh):
    • High concentration of Scheduled Tribes (Bhil, Meena, Damor, Garasia), tribal sub-plan governance, PESA implementation.
  3. Dhundhar & Matsya (Eastern Plains: Jaipur, Dausa, Alwar, Bharatpur, Dholpur, Karauli):
    • High demographic density, rapid urbanisation (Jaipur), Eastern Rajasthan Canal Project (ERCP) water politics.
  4. Hadoti (Southeastern Plateau: Kota, Bundi, Baran, Jhalawar):
    • Industrial/education hubs (Kota), fertile Chambal river basin, tribal presence in Sahariya pocket (Baran).
  5. Shekhawati (North-Central: Sikar, Jhunjhunu, Churu):
    • Highest rural education/defence recruitment belt, cooperative banking networks.
