### 1. Imperial Chronology & Major Historical Milestones

| Monarch | Regnal Period | Royal Titles | Major Historical & Cultural Contributions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dantidurga** | 735–756 CE | *Khadgavaloka*, *Maharajadhiraja* | Overthrew Chalukya Kirtivarman II; performed *Hiranyagarbha* ritual at Ujjain; founder of dynasty. |
| **Krishna I** | 756–774 CE | *Shubhatunga*, *Akalavarsha* | Excavated the monolithic **Kailasanatha Temple (Cave 16) at Ellora**; completely crushed remaining Chalukya power. |
| **Govinda III** | 793–814 CE | *Jagattunga*, *Prabhutavarsha* | Military titan; marched into North India, defeated Gurjara-Pratihara Nagabhata II and Pala king Dharmapala; received submission of Chakrayudha at Kannauj. |
| **Amoghavarsha I** | 814–878 CE | *Nrupatunga*, *Atishayadhavala* | • Longest reign (64 years); moved capital to **Manyakheta** (Malkhed).\n• Great patron of Jainism (disciple of Acharya Jinasena); authored *Prashnottara Ratnamalika* (Sanskrit) and *Kavirajamarga* (Kannada).\n• Arab traveler **Sulaiman** ranked him among the 4 greatest world rulers. |
| **Indra III** | 914–929 CE | *Nityavarsha* | Marched north in 915 CE, completely captured and sacked **Kannauj**, routing Gurjara-Pratihara Mahipala I. |
| **Krishna III** | 939–967 CE | *Akalavarsha* | Last great emperor; defeated Chola king Parantaka I at the famous **Battle of Takkolam (949 CE)**; erected a victory pillar at Rameswaram. |

### 3. The Marvel of Ellora Cave 16 (Kailasanatha)
* **Top-Down Excavation**: Carved out of a single basalt cliff starting from the top down to the floor, eliminating the need for scaffolding.
