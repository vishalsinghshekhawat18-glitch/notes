Human Endocrine System: Pituitary, Thyroid, Pancreas, Adrenals & Feedback

The ductless chemical communication network: master pituitary control (GH, gigantism/dwarfism), thyroid thyroxine (iodine & goitre), pancreatic insulin/glucagon (diabetes mellitus), adrenal adrenaline (fight-or-flight), and homeostatic negative feedback loops.

Intuition & Context

The Super-Human Adrenaline Surge and The Sugar Thermostat in Your Blood

If you suddenly turn a corner in a dark alley and come face-to-face with a roaring tiger, your brain doesn't waste time sending electrical nerve wires to 50 different organs. Your adrenal glands dump a chemical flood of Adrenaline directly into your bloodstream! Instantly, your heart hammers like a drum, your lung airways open wide, blood drains from your pale stomach to supercharge your leg muscles, and you sprint faster than an Olympic athlete (Fight-or-Flight)!

How It Works / Mechanism

The Negative Feedback Thermostat: How Insulin Regulates Blood Sugar

The Pancreatic Glucose Feedback Loop:

                      High Blood Sugar (After Eating a Meal)
                                      │
                                      ▼
                      Pancreatic BETA Cells Detect High Sugar
                                      │
                                      ▼
                      INSULIN Secreted into Bloodstream
                                      │
                                      ▼
             Liver & Muscles Convert Excess Glucose ──► GLYCOGEN
                                      │
                                      ▼
                       Blood Sugar Returns to Normal (100 mg/dL)
                                      │
                                      ▼  [ NEGATIVE FEEDBACK ]
                       Insulin Secretion Automatically SHUTS OFF!
  • If blood glucose drops too low (during fasting), Alpha cells secrete Glucagon, which breaks stored glycogen back into glucose, maintaining healthy equilibrium!
Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps: Iodized Salt Goitre, Master Gland & 3F Hormone

Top 4 Endocrine Traps:

  1. Why Salt is Iodized in India:
    • Question: "Why is the use of Iodized salt strongly recommended in our daily diet?"
    • Answer: Iodine is essential for the Thyroid gland to synthesize Thyroxine hormone. Iodine deficiency causes Simple Goitre (enlargement of the thyroid gland causing neck swelling).
  2. The "Fight, Flight or Fright (3F)" Emergency Hormone:
    • ADRENALINE (Epinephrine), secreted by the Adrenal Medulla.
  3. The "Master Gland" of the Human Body:
    • PITUITARY GLAND (controls thyroid, adrenals, and gonads via trophic hormones; itself regulated by the Hypothalamus).
  4. Islets of Langerhans Cell Specificity:
    • Beta (β\betaβ) Cells   ⟹  \implies⟹ INSULIN (lowers blood glucose).
    • Alpha (α\alphaα) Cells   ⟹  \implies⟹ GLUCAGON (raises blood glucose).
