11:T574,### The Chemical Dualism of Amphoteric Oxides:
- Most non-metal oxides ($SO_2, CO_2$) are strictly acidic.
- Most metal oxides ($Na_2O, CaO, MgO$) are strictly basic.
- **Amphoteric Oxides ($Al_2O_3, ZnO, PbO$)** possess the unique ability to act as a **Base** when reacting with acids, and act as an **Acid** when reacting with strong bases!

### Aluminium Oxide Dual Equations:
1. **Acting as Base (with Acid $HCl$)**:
   $$\mathbf{\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 + 6\text{HCl} \longrightarrow 2\text{AlCl}_3 \text{ (Salt)} + 3\text{H}_2\text{O}}$$
2. **Acting as Acid (with Base $NaOH$)**:
   $$\mathbf{\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 + 2\text{NaOH} \longrightarrow \mathbf{2NaAlO_2 \text{ (Sodium Aluminate Salt)}} + \text{H}_2\text{O}}$$

---

### Metal Reactivity with Water Continuum:

| Reactant Water Form | Metals Reacting | Reaction Behavior & Visual Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cold Water** | **$K, Na, Ca$** | • $K$ and $Na$: Violent explosive reaction, catches fire instantly ($H_2$ ignites).<br>• $Ca$: Less violent, calcium floats on water. |
| **Hot Water** | **$Mg$ (Magnesium)** | Reacts with hot water; floats as $H_2$ bubbles stick to metal surface. |
| **Steam Only ($H_2O$ gas)**| **$Al, Zn, Fe$** | React only at red-hot temperatures: $3Fe + 4H_2O \to Fe_3O_4 + 4H_2$. |
| **Zero Reaction** | **$Pb, Cu, Ag, Au$** | Unreactive with cold water, hot water, or superheated steam.
