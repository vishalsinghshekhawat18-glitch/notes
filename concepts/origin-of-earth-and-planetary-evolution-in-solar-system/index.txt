12:T606,### Terrestrial Planets vs. Jovian Planets Matrix

| Attribute | Terrestrial (Inner) Planets | Jovian (Outer / Gas Giant) Planets |
|---|---|---|
| **Planets Included** | Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars | Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune |
| **Location Relative to Sun** | Inside Asteroid Belt (close to Sun) | Beyond Asteroid Belt (far from Sun) |
| **Composition & State** | Dense rock and metal (silicates, iron core) | Low-density hydrogen, helium, methane, ammonia gases |
| **Average Density** | High ($3.9 - 5.5\text{ g/cm}^3$) | Low ($0.7 - 1.6\text{ g/cm}^3$; Saturn is less dense than water!) |
| **Atmospheric Thickness** | Thin primary atmosphere stripped by solar winds | Massive, thick, high-pressure primordial envelopes |
| **Moons & Rings** | Few or no moons (Earth 1, Mars 2); no rings | Numerous moons (Saturn >140, Jupiter >90); prominent ring systems |

### The Hierarchy of Geological Time
- **Eon (Largest division):** Hadean (4.6–4.0 Ga, molten earth), Archean (4.0–2.5 Ga, first unicellular life), Proterozoic (2.5–0.54 Ga, oxygenation, multicellular life), Phanerozoic (541 Ma–present, visible macroscopic life).
- **Phanerozoic Eras:**
  1. *Paleozoic Era (541–252 Ma):* Cambrian explosion, fish, amphibians, coal forests (Carboniferous).
  2. *Mesozoic Era (252–66 Ma):* Age of Reptiles/Dinosaurs (Triassic, Jurassic, Cretaceous - Deccan traps eruption).
  3. *Cenozoic Era (66 Ma–Present):* Age of Mammals & Humans (Tertiary: Himalayan orogeny; Quaternary: Pleistocene ice ages & Holocene modern epoch).13:T509,### The Three Stages of Atmospheric Evolution
1. **Stage 1 (Loss of Primordial Atmosphere):** The primordial envelope of hydrogen and helium was stripped away from inner planets by violent early **Solar Winds** (T-Tauri phase of the young Sun).
2. **Stage 2 (Volcanic Degassing):** As the interior of Earth cooled, intense volcanism expelled gases trapped within the mantle:
   $$\text{Volcanic Emissions} \to \text{H}_2\text{O (Vapour)} + \text{CO}_2 + \text{N}_2 + \text{CH}_4 + \text{NH}_3$$
   *Note:* Free molecular oxygen ($O_2$) was essentially **ZERO** in this reducing atmosphere.
3. **Stage 3 (Photosynthetic Modification & Ocean Sink):**
   - As surface temperatures cooled below $100^\circ\text{C}$, torrential rains washed atmospheric $CO_2$ into oceans, forming limestone ($CaCO_3$) sediment sinks.
   - Around 3.5–3.0 billion years ago, marine cyanobacteria evolved oxygenic photosynthesis ($6\text{CO}_2 + 6\text{H}_2\text{O} \to \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\text{O}_2$).
   - Oxygen first oxidized dissolved iron in oceans (Band Iron Formations - BIFs), and around 2.4–2.0 billion years ago saturated the oceans to escape into the atmosphere (The **Great Oxidation Event** or Oxygen Catastrophe), creating the protective stratospheric **Ozone Layer ($O_3$)**.14:T445,### Frequent Examiner Traps
1. **Free Oxygen in Early Atmosphere Trap:** Early volcanic degassing **DID NOT produce free molecular oxygen ($O_2$)**. Oxygen accumulated only hundreds of millions of years later through biological cyanobacterial photosynthesis in oceans.
2. **Density of Saturn:** Saturn has an average density of **$0.69\text{ g/cm}^3$**, which is **LESS than the density of liquid water ($1.0\text{ g/cm}^3$)**; if placed in a sufficiently giant bathtub, Saturn would float.
3. **Solar Wind Stripping:** Terrestrial planets lack thick hydrogen envelopes because: (a) higher temperatures near the Sun prevented light gases from condensing, (b) their lower gravity could not retain light gases, and (c) intense early solar winds stripped them away.
4. **Age of Earth:** Authoritative radiometric dating of meteorites and lunar samples establishes Earth's age at **$4.54 \pm 0.05$ billion years (4.6 Ga)**.
