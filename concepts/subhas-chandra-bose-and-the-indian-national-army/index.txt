12:T4b8,Could non-violent moral persuasion alone compel a ruthlessly entrenched global imperial power to relinquish the "Jewel in the Crown"?

Subhas Chandra Bose believed it could not. Netaji represented the supreme synthesis of uncompromising anti-imperialism and international realpolitik:
* While Mahatma Gandhi viewed the struggle through the prism of absolute ethical means and moral purification, Bose operated on the classical axiom of foreign policy: **"The enemy of my enemy is my friend"** and **"Britain's difficulty is India's opportunity"**.
* Bose recognized that World War II created a unique, non-repeatable geopolitical window. By escaping colonial custody, traveling across continents in submarines, building a provisional government recognized by world powers, and mobilizing an army of Indian prisoners of war and diaspora civilians, Netaji demonstrated that Indians were capable of organizing a modern military force to liberate their own homeland.
* Even in military defeat, the INA achieved its ultimate strategic objective: by putting the loyalty of the British Indian Armed Forces into question, the INA made the continued British occupation of India logistically and militarily impossible.13:T1838,### 1. The Congress Years & The Ideological Rift (1938–1939)
* **Haripura Session (1938):** Bose elected Congress President unopposed. Pioneered modern economic planning by establishing the **National Planning Committee** with **Jawaharlal Nehru** as its Chairman.
* **Tripuri Crisis (1939):**
  * Bose contested the presidency for a second term against the wishes of Gandhi, who nominated **Dr. Pattabhi Sitaramayya**.
  * Bose defeated Sitaramayya by 1,580 to 1,377 votes. Gandhi publicly declared: *"Pattabhi's defeat is my defeat"*.
  * The Old Guard moved the **Govind Ballabh Pant Resolution**, mandating the President to appoint the Congress Working Committee (CWC) strictly according to Gandhi's wishes. Gandhi refused to nominate members or cooperate.
  * To avoid crippling factional deadlock, Bose **resigned** as Congress President in April 1939. **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** was elected in his place.
* **Formation of the Forward Bloc (May 3, 1939):** Formed by Bose at Makur, Unnao (UP) as a radical leftist faction within the Congress to unite all anti-imperialist progressive forces. Later in August 1939, Bose was disqualified from holding Congress executive offices for 3 years due to his protest against CWC resolutions.

### 2. The Great Escape & The European Odyssey (1941–1943)
* **The Escape (January 16–17, 1941):** Under house arrest at Elgin Road, Calcutta, Bose disguised himself as an insurance agent named **"Maulvi Ziauddin"** (later as "Orlando Mazzotta").
* Aided by Kirti Kisan Party operative **Bhagat Ram Talwar** (code-named "Silver"), Bose trekked through Peshawar to Kabul, and secured an Italian passport to reach Moscow, flying to Berlin in April 1941.
* **Free India Centre & Indian Legion (Germany):**
  * Established the *Zentrale Freies Indien* (Free India Centre) in Berlin and set up the **Indian Legion** (*Indische Legion*) of 3,000 Indian POWs captured in North Africa.
  * Founded the **Azad Hind Radio** broadcasting in seven languages.
  * Here, Indian soldiers first hailed him with the honorific **"Netaji"** and adopted the universal greeting **"Jai Hind"** (coined by Zain-ul-Abedin Hasan).
* **The Submarine Rendezvous (Feb–May 1943):**
  * Realizing Nazi Germany was bogged down in Russia and incapable of aiding India militarily, Bose undertook an epic 90-day submarine voyage.
  * Travelled on German submarine **U-180** around the Cape of Good Hope, rendezvoused in rough seas off Madagascar, and transferred to Japanese submarine **I-29**, reaching Tokyo in May 1943 to meet Prime Minister Hideki Tojo.

### 4. The Post-War INA Red Fort Trials & The RIN Mutiny (1945–1946)
* **The Joint Red Fort Court Martial (November 1945):**
  * The British colonial government placed three INA officers on joint trial at the Red Fort, Delhi on charges of treason and murder:
    1. **Colonel Prem Kumar Sahgal** (Hindu)
    2. **Colonel Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon** (Sikh)
    3. **Major General Shah Nawaz Khan** (Muslim)
  * This joint trial unwittingly created unprecedented communal solidarity across the country under the slogan: *"Lal Qile se aayi aawaz: Sahgal, Dhillon, Shahnawaz!"*.
* **The INA Defence Committee:**
  * Formed by the Congress, led by brilliant legal counsel **Bhulabhai Desai**, supported by **Jawaharlal Nehru** (who donned his barrister's gown after 25 years), Sir Tej Bahadur Sapru, Kailash Nath Katju, and Asaf Ali.
  * Desai argued that the INA was the regular armed force of a sovereign, recognized provisional government waging a legitimate war of national liberation, and could not be tried under domestic municipal treason laws.
* **The Spark of National Mutiny:**
  * Although the court martial found them guilty, Commander-in-Chief Claude Auchinleck was forced by public fury to remit their sentences of transportation for life.
  * The INA agitation directly catalyzed the **Royal Indian Navy (RIN) Mutiny (Feb 18–23, 1946)**, when 20,000 ratings on HMIS Talwar and 78 other ships struck against racial discrimination and trials of INA heroes. This proved to British Prime Minister Clement Attlee that the Indian armed forces could no longer be relied upon to suppress an Indian revolt.14:T67e,### The Legitimacy Blueprint of the Arzi Hukumat-e-Azad Hind

Bose understood that armed liberation without sovereign institutional legitimacy would be branded by international law as mere "banditry" or "mercenary rebellion". Therefore, he constructed a complete de jure state structure:

1. **Constitutional Sovereignty:**
   * Proclaimed on October 21, 1943 in Singapore, Netaji assumed the roles of Head of State, Prime Minister, Minister of War, and Minister of Foreign Affairs.
   * Established a national bank (**Azad Hind Bank** in Rangoon, 1944) issuing currency and promissory notes, a civil legal code, postage stamps, and diplomatic missions.

2. **The "Father of the Nation" Address:**
   * On **July 6, 1944**, broadcasting from Azad Hind Radio in Rangoon, Bose addressed Mahatma Gandhi:
   > *"Father of our Nation! In this holy war for India's liberation, we ask for your blessings and good wishes."*
   * This was the very first time in history that Gandhi was addressed as the **"Father of the Nation"**, proving that Netaji's political divergence with Gandhi never degraded into personal bitterness.

3. **Demolishing British Military Hegemony:**
   $$\text{British Empire in India} = \frac{\text{Administrative Bureaucracy}}{\text{Indian Armed Sepoys' Loyalty}}$$
   * By convincing 40,000 Indian sepoys to break their oath of loyalty to the British King-Emperor and fight under the Indian national flag, the INA detonated the foundational pillar of the British Raj.    * *Trap:* Claiming Subhas Chandra Bose founded the Indian National Army (INA).
   * *Fact:* The INA was originally conceived and formed in **1942 by Captain Mohan Singh** along with Major Fujiwara and Pritam Singh. Subhas Chandra Bose arrived in Singapore in July 1943 and reorganized the *Second INA*.

2. **Shaheed Dweep vs Swaraj Dweep Inversion Trap:**
   * *Trap:* Examiners flip the names of Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

   * *Trap:* Believing Pattabhi Sitaramayya became President after Bose resigned at Tripuri in 1939.
   * *Fact:* When Bose resigned in April 1939, **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** was elected Congress President (NOT Sitaramayya!).
