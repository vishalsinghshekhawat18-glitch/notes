11:T81b,### Digambara vs Svetambara: 6 Key Doctrinal Differences
| Dimension | Digambara Sect ('Sky-Clad') | Svetambara Sect ('White-Clad') |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monastic Attire** | Complete nudity for monks (discard all clothes). | Wear simple unstitched white robes (*Shveta-Vastra*). |
| **Historical Leader** | **Bhadrabahu** (migrated to Shravanabelagola during famine). | **Sthalabahu** (stayed in Magadha). |
| **Attainment of Kevalin** | An omniscient Kevalin does not require physical food. | A Kevalin continues to consume physical morsels of food. |
| **Female Liberation** | Women cannot attain Moksha in female body; must be reborn as men. | Women are fully capable of attaining Moksha in their current lifetime. |
| **19th Tirthankara Mallinatha** | Mallinatha was a **man** (*Malli*). | Mallinatha was a **woman** (*Princess Malli*). |
| **Canonical Literature** | Reject Svetambara Agamas as lost/corrupted; accept *Shatkhandagama* & *Kashayaprabhrata*. | Accept the **12 Angas** and written Agamas compiled at Vallabhi. |

### The Two Jain Councils
- **1st Jain Council (c. 300 BCE, Pataliputra):** Presided over by **Sthalabahu**. Compiled the 12 Angas to reconstruct oral canon; Digambaras boycotted and rejected the compilation.
- **2nd Jain Council (c. 512 CE, Vallabhi, Gujarat):** Presided over by **Devardhi Kshamasramana**. Finally committed the Svetambara canonical texts (Agamas) into written palm-leaf manuscripts.

### The 6 Major Shramana Thinkers of the *Samannaphala Sutta*
1. **Purana Kassapa:** *Akriyavada* (Amoralism / Actions produce neither merit nor demerit).
2. **Makkhali Gosala:** *Ajivika / Niyativada* (Absolute Determinism / Fate governs all transmigration).
3. **Ajita Kesakambalin:** *Ucchedavada / Materialism* (Annihilation at death; body dissolves into 4 elements).
4. **Pakudha Kaccayana:** *Sasvatavada* (Seven eternal, uncreated elemental substances).
5. **Sanjaya Belatthiputta:** *Ajnanavada* (Radical Skepticism / Agnosticism).
6. **Vardhamana Mahavira (Nigantha Nataputta):** *Kiriyavada / Chaturyama-Pancha Mahavrata*.12:T415,### 1. The Ajivikas & Mauryan Cave Inscriptions
- Despite their fatalism, the Ajivikas commanded tremendous popular and royal following for over a millennium.
- Emperor **Ashoka** dedicated the **Sudama Cave** and **Nyagrodha/Karna Chaupar Cave** in the **Barabar Hills** (Gaya, Bihar) specifically to Ajivika ascetics.
- Ashoka's grandson **Dasharatha Maurya** dedicated the **Gopika**, **Vapiyaka**, and **Vadathika** caves in the **Nagarjuni Hills** to the Ajivikas.

### 2. Charvaka Epistemology (*Lokayata*)
- **Rejection of Inference (*Anumana*) and Testimony (*Shabda*):** Charvakas argued that universal causal connection (*Vyapti*) cannot be verified beyond doubt; therefore, only direct sensory perception (**Pratyaksha**) is a valid means of knowledge (*Pramana*).
- **Four Elements Ontology:** The world consists only of Earth, Water, Fire, and Air (rejecting Ether / *Akasha* as non-perceivable).
