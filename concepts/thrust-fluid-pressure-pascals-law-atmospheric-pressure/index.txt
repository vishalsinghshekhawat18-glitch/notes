11:T4fa,### 1. Pascal's Principle & The Hydraulic Force Multiplier
In an enclosed hydraulic system filled with oil:
$$P_1 = P_2 \implies \frac{F_1}{A_1} = \frac{F_2}{A_2} \implies F_2 = F_1 \left(\frac{A_2}{A_1}\right)$$
- If the output piston area $A_2$ is $100$ times larger than input piston $A_1$, applying a modest $100 \, \text{N}$ force on $A_1$ generates a massive **$10,000 \, \text{N}$ force** on $A_2$, lifting a heavy automobile with one foot!

### 2. Hydrostatic Depth Pressure
$$P = \rho g h$$
- Dams are built **much thicker at the bottom than at the top** because water pressure increases linearly with depth ($h$).
- Submarines must have thick titanium hulls to withstand extreme deep-sea pressure.

### 3. Atmospheric Pressure & Torricelli's Barometer (1643)
- Atmospheric pressure arises from the weight of the air column above Earth.
$$P_{\text{atm}} = \rho_{\text{Hg}} \cdot g \cdot h = (13,600 \, \text{kg/m}^3)(9.8 \, \text{m/s}^2)(0.76 \, \text{m}) = \mathbf{1.013 \times 10^5 \, \text{Pa}}$$
- **Why Mercury is Used in Barometers**: Mercury has extreme high density ($13.6 \, \text{g/cm}^3$), requiring only a compact $76 \, \text{cm}$ column.
