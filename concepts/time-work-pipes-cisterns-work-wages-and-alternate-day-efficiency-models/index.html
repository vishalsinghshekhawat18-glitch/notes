3. Alternate Working Days / Hours Protocol

When workers operate on alternate days (e.g. AAA on Day 1, BBB on Day 2, AAA on Day 3...):

  1. Define the Base Cycle: 1 full cycle =2 days= 2\text{ days}=2 days; Work in 1 cycle =EA+EB= E_A + E_B=EA​+EB​.
  2. Compute Integer Cycles: $ ext{Number of full cycles } k = lfloor rac{ ext{Total Work}}{E_A + E_B}
    floor$.
  3. Calculate Remaining Work: $ ext{Work Left} = ext{Total Work} - k(E_A + E_B)$.
  4. Fractional Final Day: Assign remaining work to the scheduled worker whose turn it is: $ ext{Time} = rac{ ext{Work Left}}{E_{ ext{turn}}}$.

4. Pipes & Cisterns Invariants & The Negative Leak Trap

  • Inlets possess positive efficiency (+E+E+E); Draining outlets possess negative efficiency (−E-E−E).
  • The Alternate Hour Filling Trap: When an inlet pipe and a leak operate on alternate hours:

    [!IMPORTANT]
    Calculate cycle progress ONLY UP TO (Total Capacity−Inlet Capacity)(\text{Total Capacity} - \text{Inlet Capacity})(Total Capacity−Inlet Capacity).
    The tank becomes completely full during the positive inlet's turn before the draining leak operates, preventing overflow!

3-Tier Worked Problem: Worker Leaving Midway in Time & Work

3-Tier Worked Problem

Problem: A can complete a piece of work in $20$ days and B can complete it in $30$ days. They start working together, but B leaves after $6$ days. In how many more days will A alone complete the remaining work, and what is the total time taken?

  • Tier 1 (First Principles / Fractional Balance):
    1. AAA's 1-day work =120= \frac{1}{20}=201​; BBB's 1-day work =130= \frac{1}{30}=301​.
    2. In first 6 days, work done by A+B=6×(120+130)=6×560=3060=12A+B = 6 \times \left(\frac{1}{20} + \frac{1}{30}\right) = 6 \times \frac{5}{60} = \frac{30}{60} = \frac{1}{2}A+B=6×(201​+301​)=6×605​=6030​=21​ of total work.
    3. Remaining work =1−12=12= 1 - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}=1−21​=21​.
    4. Time taken by AAA alone =1/21/20=202=10 days= \frac{1/2}{1/20} = \frac{20}{2} = \mathbf{10\text{ days}}=1/201/2​=220​=10 days.
    5. Total time taken =6+10=16 days= 6 + 10 = \mathbf{16\text{ days}}=6+10=16 days.
  • Tier 2 (Banking Standard / LCM Unit Method):
    • Total Work =LCM(20,30)=60 units= \text{LCM}(20, 30) = 60\text{ units}=LCM(20,30)=60 units.
    • EA=3 u/day,EB=2 u/dayE_A = 3\text{ u/day}, E_B = 2\text{ u/day}EA​=3 u/day,EB​=2 u/day.
    • Work done in first 6 days =6×(3+2)=30 units= 6 \times (3 + 2) = 30\text{ units}=6×(3+2)=30 units.
    • Remaining Work =60−30=30 units= 60 - 30 = 30\text{ units}=60−30=30 units.
    • AAA's extra time =303=10 days= \frac{30}{3} = \mathbf{10\text{ days}}=330​=10 days.
    • Total Time =6+10=16 days= 6 + 10 = \mathbf{16\text{ days}}=6+10=16 days.
  • Tier 3 (10-Second Mental Elimination): AAA works all TTT days, BBB works 6 days. $3T + 2(6) = 60 implies 3T = 48 implies T = 16 ext{ days}$. Extra days =16−6=10 days= 16 - 6 = 10\text{ days}=16−6=10 days.
