### 3. Alternate Working Days / Hours Protocol
When workers operate on alternate days (e.g. $A$ on Day 1, $B$ on Day 2, $A$ on Day 3...):
1. **Define the Base Cycle:** 1 full cycle $= 2	ext{ days}$; Work in 1 cycle $= E_A + E_B$.
2. **Compute Integer Cycles:** $	ext{Number of full cycles } k = lfloor rac{	ext{Total Work}}{E_A + E_B} floor$.
3. **Calculate Remaining Work:** $	ext{Work Left} = 	ext{Total Work} - k(E_A + E_B)$.
4. **Fractional Final Day:** Assign remaining work to the scheduled worker whose turn it is: $	ext{Time} = rac{	ext{Work Left}}{E_{	ext{turn}}}$.
