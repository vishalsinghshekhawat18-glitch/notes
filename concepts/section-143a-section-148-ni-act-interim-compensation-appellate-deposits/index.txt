11:T5c2,### Comparative Matrix: Section 143A vs Section 148 NI Act

| Dimension | Section 143A (Interim Compensation at Trial) | Section 148 (Appellate Court Deposit) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage of Proceeding** | **Trial Stage** (Summary trial / Summons case) | **Appellate Stage** (Appeal against conviction) |
| **Trigger Point** | Accused pleads **'not guilty'** / charges framed | Convicted drawer files an appeal in Sessions/HC |
| **Prescribed Amount** | **Not exceeding 20%** of the cheque amount (Upper cap = 20%) | **Minimum of 20%** of fine/compensation (Floor = 20%, can be higher) |
| **Deposit Timeline** | **60 Days** + extendable by **30 Days** (Total max 90 days) | **60 Days** + extendable by **30 Days** (Total max 90 days) |
| **Discretion vs Mandate** | Directory / Discretionary (*Rakesh Ranjan Shrivastava v. State of Jharkhand 2024*) | Normally Mandatory unless exceptional grounds (*Surinder Singh Deswal 2019*) |
| **Repayment on Acquittal** | Complainant must refund full amount **+ RBI Bank Rate interest** within 60+30 days | Complainant must refund full amount **+ RBI Bank Rate interest** within 60+30 days |
| **Recovery Mechanism** | Recoverable as a fine under Section 421 CrPC / Section 461 BNSS | Recoverable as a fine under Section 421 CrPC / Section 461 BNSS |
| **Prospective vs Retrospective** | Strictly **Prospective** (*GJ Raja v. Tejraj Surana 2019*) | Applies **Retrospectively** to pending appeals (*Surinder Singh Deswal 2019*) |12:T43d,```mermaid
graph TD
    A[Trial Begins: Drawer Pleads Not Guilty] --> B[Complainant Applies for Sec 143A Interim Compensation]
    B --> C[Trial Court Exercises Discretion: Orders up to 20% of Cheque Amount]
    C --> D[Drawer Must Deposit Sum within 60 Days + 30 Days Extension]
    D --> E{Trial Conclusion}
    E -- Drawer Convicted --> F[Interim Compensation Adjusted against Final Fine / Award]
    E -- Drawer Acquitted --> G[Court Directs Complainant to Repay Sum + RBI Bank Rate Interest within 60+30 Days]
    F --> H[Drawer Appeals Conviction in Sessions Court]
    H --> I[Appellate Court Mandates Minimum 20% Deposit under Sec 148]
    I --> J[Sum Released to Complainant Pending Appeal Outcome]
```

- **Trigger**: Plea of not guilty (Sec 143A) or Filing of Appeal (Sec 148).
- **Timeline**: 60 days standard + 30 days grace on sufficient cause = 90 days maximum.
- **Interest Rate on Refund**: Bank rate published by the Reserve Bank of India prevailing at the beginning of the relevant financial year.
- **Enforcement**: Attachment of property as fine under CrPC / BNSS.13:T460,### High-Yield Traps for Banking Exams
1. **'Maximum 20%' vs 'Minimum 20%' Trap**:
   - Under **Section 143A** (Trial stage), the interim compensation is capped at a **MAXIMUM of 20%** of the cheque amount.
   - Under **Section 148** (Appellate stage), the deposit is floored at a **MINIMUM of 20%** of the fine or compensation awarded.
2. **Prospective vs Retrospective Application (Landmark Rulings)**:
   - *GJ Raja v. Tejraj Surana (2019)*: Section 143A is **substantive** and applies only **prospectively** to offences committed after the 2018 amendment (Sept 1, 2018).
   - *Surinder Singh Deswal v. Virender Gandhi (2019)*: Section 148 is **procedural** and applies **retrospectively** even to pending appeals where complaint was filed before 2018.
3. **Interest on Refund Rule**: If the drawer is acquitted, the complainant cannot just return the principal; they must pay interest at the **RBI Bank Rate**.
4. **Recovery Method Trap**: If the drawer refuses to pay interim compensation under Sec 143A, the court cannot cancel bail immediately, but recovers it as a fine under Section 421 CrPC / Section 461
