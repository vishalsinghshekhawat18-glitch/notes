11:T435,India’s constitutional architects pragmatically curated features from global federal systems:

1. From Canada (The Primary Structural Template):
   • Federation with a Strong Centre (Unitary bias).
   • Vesting of Residuary Powers in the Centre (Article 248).
   • Appointment of State Governors by the Centre (Article 155).
   • Advisory Jurisdiction of the Supreme Court (Article 143).

2. From Australia:
   • Concurrent List (Seventh Schedule List III).
   • Freedom of Inter-State Trade, Commerce and Intercourse (Part XIII, Articles 301–307).
   • Joint Sitting of both Houses of Parliament to resolve deadlocks (Article 108).

3. From the USA:
   • Written Fundamental Rights (Part III).
   • Independence of the Judiciary & Judicial Review (Article 13).
   • Impeachment procedure of the President (Article 61) & removal of SC/HC Judges.
   • Office of the Vice-President as ex-officio Chairman of Upper House (Article 64).
