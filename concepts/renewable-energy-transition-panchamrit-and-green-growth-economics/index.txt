11:T478,Imagine the national electricity grid on a hot April afternoon:
- At **1:00 PM**, Rajasthan and Gujarat solar panels flood the national grid with **70,000 MW of cheap clean electricity**. The wholesale price of electricity drops to nearly zero.
- At **7:00 PM**, the sun sets, but 1.4 billion Indians return home, switch on air conditioners, TVs, and induction stoves (**Peak Electricity Demand**).

This creates the famous **"Duck Curve"** problem:
- Solar energy vanishes exactly when the nation needs electricity the most.
- If you don't have massive **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)** or **Pumped Hydro Dams**, you are forced to burn thousands of tons of dirty coal to prevent blackouts.

Furthermore, what happens to **Dhanbad in Jharkhand**, where 80% of local families depend on Coal India paychecks?

If India closes coal mines without creating new green manufacturing jobs, entire mining regions will collapse into catastrophic poverty.

This is why India's green growth strategy balances **₹19,744 Crore Green Hydrogen incentives** with a **"Just Transition"** that protects coal-mining workers and invests in energy storage.12:T61c,### 1. India's "Panchamrit" Five Climate Commitments (Glasgow COP26 & Updated NDCs)

| Panchamrit Pillar | Official Metric & Baseline | Milestone Target Year | Core Strategic Pathway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Non-Fossil Capacity** | **500 GW Installed Non-Fossil Power** | **By 2030** | Massive solar parks, offshore wind, and nuclear reactors. |
| **2. Renewable Energy Share** | **50% of Total Electric Power Capacity** | **By 2030** | Surpassed 44% in 2024; accelerated via PM-Surya Ghar (1 Cr rooftop solar). |

### 2. National Green Hydrogen Mission (SIGHT) Architecture
- **Total Budget Outlay**: **₹19,744 Crore** (Approved January 2023).
- **Target by 2030**: Minimum **5 Million Metric Tonnes (MMT)** of Green Hydrogen per annum.
- **SIGHT Sub-Schemes**: 
  - Component I: ₹4,440 Crore for domestic Electrolyser Manufacturing.
  - Component II: ₹13,050 Crore for Green Hydrogen Production.
- **Decarbonization Impact**: Replaces grey hydrogen (made from imported natural gas) in **oil refineries, urea fertilizer plants, and steel mills**.13:T4fe,### 1. The Intermittency "Duck Curve" and Storage Economics
```
  Grid Load (GW)
     │              Evening Peak Demand Surge (7 PM - 10 PM)
     │                       /\  <── Dispatched from BESS & Pumped Hydro
     │                      /  \
     │   Solar Midday Drop /    \     Normal Baseload
     │  (The Belly of Duck)      \──────────────────────────
     └────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Time (24 Hrs)
        6 AM       12 Noon       6 PM        12 Midnight
```
- **Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS)**: The economic barrier to 100% renewables. Falling lithium-ion battery and pumped-storage capital costs are critical to making Round-the-Clock (RTC) clean power competitive with coal baseload.

### 2. Statutory Renewable Purchase Obligations (RPO)
Under the Electricity Act 2003 and Ministry of Power mandates, all electricity distribution companies (DISCOMs) and open-access consumers are legally required to purchase a minimum percentage of electricity from renewable sources (Wind RPO, Hydro RPO, and Other RPO), progressing from ~27% in 2023 to $>43\%$ by 2030.14:T738,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: Net-Zero Target Year**: India's target for Net-Zero greenhouse gas emissions is strictly **2070** (NOT 2050 as committed by Western nations or 2060 as committed by China).
- **Trap 2: Emissions Intensity Baseline Year**: The 45% reduction in GDP emissions intensity by 2030 is measured against the **2005 baseline year**, NOT 2015 or 1990.
- **Trap 3: Green Hydrogen Production Target**: The National Green Hydrogen Mission targets **5 MMT per annum by 2030**, with a budget outlay of **₹19,744 Crore**.
- **Trap 4: Green vs Grey vs Blue Hydrogen**:
  - *Grey Hydrogen*: Produced from fossil fuels (natural gas) via Steam Methane Reforming $\implies$ emits $CO_2$.
  - *Blue Hydrogen*: Produced from fossil fuels with Carbon Capture and Storage (CCS).
  - *Green Hydrogen*: Produced strictly by splitting water via electrolysis using **100% renewable electricity** $\implies$ ZERO emissions.
