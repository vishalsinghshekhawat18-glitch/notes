11:T58a,The 73rd Amendment establishes a sharp constitutional boundary between mandatory provisions (which every state must enact) and voluntary provisions (left to state legislative discretion):

1. Mandatory Constitutional Provisions:
   • Gram Sabha as the foundational democratic assembly of registered village voters (Art 243A).
   • 3-tier structure: Gram Panchayat (village), Panchayat Samiti (intermediate/block), and Zilla Parishad (district) under Art 243B (states with population below 20 lakhs may skip intermediate tier).
   • Direct elections for all members at all three levels (Art 243C).
   • Indirect elections for chairpersons at intermediate and district levels; chairperson election mode at village level left to state law.
   • Fixed 5-year tenure (Art 243E) with mandatory fresh elections within 6 months of dissolution.
   • Minimum 1/3rd reservation for women across seats and chairpersons (Art 243D).
   • Independent State Election Commission (Art 243K) with removal equivalence to a High Court Judge.
   • State Finance Commission (Art 243I) constituted every 5 years to review PRI finances.

2. Voluntary Discretionary Provisions:
   • Extent of functional devolution from the 29 subjects in the Eleventh Schedule.
   • Granting taxation and financial powers (Art 243H).
   • Reservation for Backward Classes (OBCs).
   • Giving voting rights to MPs and MLAs in
