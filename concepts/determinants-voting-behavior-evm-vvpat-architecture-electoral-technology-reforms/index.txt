11:T532,The Indian EVM system comprises three interconnected standalone units:

```
[ Balloting Unit (BU) ]  <───>  [ VVPAT Unit ]  <───>  [ Control Unit (CU) ]
(Voter presses button)      (Displays paper slip        (Presiding Officer activates;
                             for 7 seconds & drops)      records & tallies vote totals)
```

### Core Engineering & Security Features
1. **Air-Gapped & Non-Networked:** EVMs operate on independent battery power. They possess **zero network interfaces** (no Wi-Fi, no Bluetooth, no cellular modems, no internet connectivity).
2. **One-Time Programmable (OTP) Microcontrollers:** The firmware is burned permanently into the microcontrollers during manufacturing at BEL / ECIL and cannot be modified, reprogrammed, or rewritten.
3. **Randomization Process:**
   * **First Randomization:** EVMs allocated randomly from district warehouse to assembly constituencies using computerized software in the presence of recognized political party representatives.
   * **Second Randomization:** EVMs allocated randomly to specific polling stations just prior to poll day.
4. **Mock Poll Protocol:** A mandatory mock poll of at least 50 votes is conducted by the Presiding Officer on election morning in the presence of polling agents of all candidates, and mock poll certificates are signed.12:T5e2,### Determinants of Voting Behavior in India
Indian voting behavior is shaped by an ongoing interplay between traditional primordial identities and emerging performance metrics:

1. **Caste & Sub-Caste (*Jati* Networks):** Mobilization around social identity, community coalitions, and representation.
2. **Governance & Welfare Delivery (*Labharthi* Phenomenon):** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), subsidized food security, housing, and infrastructure influencing voting choices regardless of traditional caste lines.
3. **Gender Participation & The Silent Voter Surge:** Significant increase in female voter turnout, creating an independent women voter constituency focused on safety, prohibition, welfare pensions, and LPG subsidies.
4. **Leadership & National Narrative:** The role of prime ministerial / chief ministerial face in presidential-style electoral campaigns.

### ECI Digital Governance Platforms
* **cVIGIL App:** Mobile application allowing citizens to report Model Code of Conduct (MCC) violations with geo-tagged photographic evidence, guaranteeing response within 100 minutes.
* **ENCORE (Enabling Communications on Real-time Environment):** Comprehensive software suite for Returning Officers to manage candidate nomination, scrutiny, voter turnout trends, and round-wise counting.
* **Suvidha Portal:** Single-window digital interface for political parties and candidates to request rally permissions, vehicle permits, and campaign venue allocations on a first-come, first-served basis.13:T582,### 1. Subramanian Swamy v. ECI (2013)
The Supreme Court ruled that a "paper trail is an indispensable requirement of free and fair elections" because it enables a voter to verify that their vote was recorded accurately. The court directed the Union Government to provide necessary financial support to equip all EVMs with VVPAT units.

### 2. Mandatory 5 Polling Station Verification (2019 SC Order)
In 2019, the Supreme Court directed the ECI to increase the physical verification of VVPAT slips from 1 polling station per assembly constituency to **5 randomly selected polling stations per assembly constituency / segment**.

### 3. Association for Democratic Reforms (ADR) v. ECI (April 2024)
A two-judge bench (Justice Sanjiv Khanna and Justice Dipankar Datta) dismissed petitions seeking 100% VVPAT slip counting or a return to paper ballots, holding:
* EVMs have proven to be robust, secure, and resilient against tampering.
* **Symbol Loading Units (SLU)** used to load candidate names and symbols into VVPATs must be sealed in containers and stored in the strongroom along with EVMs for **at least 45 days** post-election declaration.
* Candidates securing 2nd or 3rd positions can submit a written request within 7 days of results to verify the microcontroller firmware of **up to 5% of EVMs** per assembly segment, with verification
