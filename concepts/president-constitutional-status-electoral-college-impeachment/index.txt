12:T50c,### Who Votes vs Who Does NOT Vote:
• **VOTES:**
  1. **Elected MPs** of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.
  2. **Elected MLAs** of all 28 State Legislative Assemblies.
  3. **Elected MLAs** of Union Territories with assemblies (Delhi & Puducherry added by the **70th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992**).
• **DOES NOT VOTE:**
  1. Nominated members of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha (12 in RS).
  2. Nominated members of State Assemblies.
  3. **All members (elected & nominated) of State Legislative Councils (Vidhan Parishad)**.

### The Value of Votes (Federal Parity Formula):
1. **Value of 1 MLA Vote:**
   $$\text{Value of MLA Vote} = \frac{\text{Total Population of State (1971 Census)}}{\text{Total Elected MLAs in State Assembly}} \times \frac{1}{1000}$$
2. **Value of 1 MP Vote:**
   $$\text{Value of MP Vote} = \frac{\text{Total Value of Votes of all MLAs of all States}}{\text{Total Number of Elected MPs (LS + RS)}}$$
3. **Electoral Quota Required to Win:**
   $$\text{Electoral Quota} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Number of Valid Votes Polled}}{2} \right) + 1$$
