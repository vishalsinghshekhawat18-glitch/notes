Insurance Jurisprudence: The 6 Core Doctrines & Flagship National Social Security Initiatives

Fundamental legal doctrines governing insurance contracts (Uberrimae Fidei, Insurable Interest timing, Indemnity, Subrogation, Contribution, Causa Proxima) and the operational architecture of India's flagship social welfare initiatives (PMJJBY, PMSBY, Atal Pension Yojana, PM-SYM, and e-Shram).

Intuition & Context

Why Insurance is Not Gambling and Life Cannot be Measured in Cash

If you bet ₹1,000 on a horse race, you hope to walk away richer than when you entered. Insurance is the exact opposite:

  1. The Principle of Indemnity: You insure your car for ₹5,00,000. If an accident damages the bumper costing ₹15,000 to repair, the insurer pays exactly ₹15,000. You cannot make a profit from insurance; indemnity merely restores you to where you were a second before the disaster struck.
  2. Why Life Insurance is NOT Indemnity: Can a court assign an exact rupee value to a mother's life? No. Therefore, Life Insurance is a Contingent Contract, NOT a contract of indemnity. The insurer agrees to pay a fixed pre-agreed sum on death.
  3. Insurable Interest Timing: You can buy a life insurance policy on your spouse during marriage; even if you divorce later, the policy remains legally valid because insurable interest is tested strictly at the time the policy was signed.

Flagship Social Security Initiatives Matrix

  • PMJJBY (Jeevan Jyoti Bima): Age 18 to 50 years; Annual premium = ₹436; Benefit = ₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh) on death due to ANY cause.
  • PMSBY (Suraksha Bima): Age 18 to 70 years; Annual premium = ₹20; Benefit = ₹2,00,000 for accidental death/total disability, ₹1,00,000 for partial disability.
  • Atal Pension Yojana (APY): Entry age 18 to 40 years (Non-taxpayers); Monthly pension = ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 from age 60 for life.
  • e-Shram Portal: Age 16 to 59 years; Issues 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN) for unorganized workers.
How It Works / Mechanism

The Subrogation Mechanism & Insurable Interest Timing Case Law

1. Subrogation Mechanics in Property Insurance

Suppose an arsonist sets fire to your factory warehouse causing ₹10,00,000 damage:

  • The insurer indemnifies you and pays the full ₹10,00,000 claim.
  • Under the Doctrine of Subrogation, your legal right to sue the arsonist for damages transfers entirely to the insurance company.
  • Rationale: If you could collect ₹10,00,000 from the insurer AND sue the arsonist for another ₹10,00,000, you would collect ₹20,00,000, violating the fundamental principle of Indemnity.

2. Landmark Precedent on Insurable Interest Timing

Dalby v. The India and London Life-Assurance Company (1854):

  • The court ruled that under the Life Assurance Act 1774, insurable interest in Life Insurance is required strictly at the moment the contract is entered into. It need not continue to exist at the time of death.
Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Competitive Examination Traps & Scheme Thresholds

Frequent Examiner Traps

  1. Insurable Interest Timing Triad:
    • Life Insurance: At INCEPTION only.
    • Marine Insurance: At TIME OF LOSS only.
    • Fire Insurance: At BOTH inception AND loss.
      Examiners repeatedly test this exact comparative triad in UPSC APFC papers!
  2. Is Life Insurance a Contract of Indemnity? NO! A human life cannot be financially valued. Life insurance is a contingent contract.
  3. PMJJBY vs PMSBY Premiums:
    • PMJJBY = ₹436/year (revised from ₹330 in 2022).
    • PMSBY = ₹20/year (revised from ₹12 in 2022).
  4. APY Age Eligibility: Minimum entry age is 18 years, maximum entry age is 40 years (w.e.f. Oct 2022, income taxpayers are barred from joining APY).
  5. e-Shram Age Limits: Age eligibility is 16 to 59 years (not 18 to 60).

  • RBI Grade B & IBPS PO: General insurance and government financial inclusion initiatives.
