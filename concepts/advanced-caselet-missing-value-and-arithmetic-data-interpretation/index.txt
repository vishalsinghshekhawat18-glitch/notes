- In **Agriculture**, the bank recovers $40%$ of NPAs, writes off $25%$, and the remainder is restructured.
- In **MSME**, total recovered amount is $1.5$ times the Agricultural recovery, while the restructured amount is $20%$ of total MSME NPAs.
- In **Corporate**, total NCLT recovery is ₹1,800 Crore.
Find the ratio of **Total Restructured NPAs across Agriculture and MSME combined** to **Total Corporate NPAs**.

- **Tier 1 (First Principles / Sequential Matrix Deduction):**
  1. *Total Distribution:* Ratio $3:4:5 implies 3x + 4x + 5x = 12x = 12,000	ext{ Cr} implies x = 1,000	ext{ Cr}$.
     - Agriculture NPAs $= ₹3,000	ext{ Cr}$.
     - MSME NPAs $= ₹4,000	ext{ Cr}$.
     - Corporate NPAs $= ₹5,000	ext{ Cr}$.
  2. *Agriculture Breakdown ($₹3,000	ext{ Cr}$):*
     - Recovery $= 40% 	imes 3,000 = ₹1,200	ext{ Cr}$.
     - Write-off $= 25% 	imes 3,000 = ₹750	ext{ Cr}$.
     - Restructured $= 3,000 - (1,200 + 750) = 3,000 - 1,950 = mathbf{₹1,050	ext{ Cr}}$.

     - Recovery $= 1.5 	imes 1,200 = ₹1,800	ext{ Cr}$.
     - Restructured $= 20% 	imes 4,000 = mathbf{₹800	ext{ Cr}}$.
  4. *Combined Agri + MSME Restructured:*
     $$	ext{Total Restructured} = 1,050 + 800 = mathbf{₹1,850	ext{ Cr}}$$
  5. *Required Ratio to Corporate NPAs ($₹5,000	ext{ Cr}$):*
     $$	ext{Ratio} = rac{1,850}{5,000} = rac{185}{500} = mathbf{rac{37}{100} = 37 : 100}$$
