Core Social Security Statutes: EPF 12% Split, ESI 4% Premium, Gratuity (15/26) & Maternity Benefit

The mathematical and legal mechanics of India's core formal social security statutes: Employees' Provident Funds & Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952 (EPF, EPS 1995, EDLI 1976 contribution split), Employees' State Insurance Act 1948 (4.0% contribution w.e.f. 2019), Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 (15/26 formula, ₹20 Lakh cap), and Maternity Benefit Act 2017 (26 weeks paid leave, crèche mandate).

Intuition & Context

The Four Pillars of Worker Contingency Insurance

A worker faces four great life hazards: becoming too old to work (EPF/EPS), sudden sickness or factory accident (ESIC), lifelong employment termination (Gratuity), and pregnancy/childbirth (Maternity):

  1. The EPF 12% Split: Both the worker and boss put 12% into the pot. But the employer's 12% is cleverly split: 8.33% is directed into a pension pool to pay monthly pensions after age 58, while 3.67% goes into the lump-sum provident fund.
  2. The 26-Day Month Gratuity Formula: When calculating gratuity severance, why does the formula divide by 26 instead of 30? Because a standard working month contains 26 working days (excluding 4 Sundays), giving the worker a higher daily wage multiplier for each of the 15 days.
  3. Maternity Protection: The 2017 amendment elevated India's paid maternity leave from 12 weeks to 26 weeks, placing India among the top 3 countries globally in paid maternity protection.

Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Competitive Examination Traps & Statutory Thresholds

Frequent Examiner Traps

  1. The 12% Employer Split Trap: Out of the employer's 12% contribution, 8.33% goes to Pension (EPS) and 3.67% goes to Provident Fund (EPF). (Examiners often reverse these numbers: 3.67% to pension and 8.33% to PF!).
  2. ESI Current Rates: The total ESI rate is 4.00% (Employer: 3.25%, Employee: 0.75%), reduced from the legacy 6.5% rate (4.75% + 1.75%) in 2019.
  3. Gratuity Ceiling: The current statutory gratuity limit is ₹20,00,000 (₹20 Lakh), raised from the earlier ₹10 Lakh ceiling.
  4. Crèche Threshold: Crèche facility is mandatory in establishments employing 50 or more employees (not 30 or 100).
  5. Maternity Duration Slabs: 26 weeks for first two surviving children; strictly 12 weeks for the third child onwards.

Exam Alignment
