12:T825,### Statutory Immunities: Trade Unions Act, 1926

| Statutory Section | Legal Subject Matter | Precise Statutory Requirement / Legal Immunity |
|---|---|---|
| **Section 4 & 9A** | Registration Thresholds | Minimum **7 members** can apply; registered union must retain **≥10% or 100 workers** (whichever is less) |
| **Section 16** | Political Fund | Separate voluntary fund; **STRICTLY VOLUNTARY**; refusing to pay cannot lead to expulsion or discrimination |
| **Section 17** | **Criminal Immunity** | Immunity from criminal conspiracy (Sec 120B IPC) for acts done in furtherance of a trade dispute |
| **Section 18** | **Civil Immunity** | Immunity from civil tort liability for inducing breach of contract of employment in a trade dispute |
| **Section 21A** | Office-Bearer Disqualification | Conviction involving moral turpitude with imprisonment; Minimum age: **18 years** (15 years for general member) |

### Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Settlement Architecture
- **Works Committee (Section 3):** Mandatory in establishments with **≥100 workers**. Equal employer-worker representation; promotes day-to-day bipartite cordiality (consultative).
- **Conciliation Officer (Section 4):** Appointed by appropriate Government; investigates dispute and facilitates settlement; submits conciliation failure report within **14 days**.
- **Board of Conciliation (Section 5):** Independent Chairman + 2 or 4 equal representatives; submits report within **2 months**.
- **Voluntary Arbitration (Section 10A):** Mutual written agreement prior to reference to court; arbitrator's award is legally binding after government gazette publication (within 30 days).
- **Labour Court (Section 7):** Adjudicates Schedule II matters (legality of strikes, dismissal, standing orders).
- **Industrial Tribunal (Section 7A):** Presiding officer qualified as High Court judge; adjudicates Schedule II and III matters (wages, hours of work, bonus, retrenchment, closure).
- **National Tribunal (Section 7B):** Appointed by Central Government for disputes of national importance across multiple states.13:T41f,### 1. Layoff Compensation (Section 25C)
- **Applicability:** Units employing $\ge 50$ workmen.
- **Eligibility:** Workman with $\ge 1$ year continuous service.
- **Compensation Formula:** **50% of Basic Wages + Dearness Allowance (DA)** for the period of layoff (up to 45 days in a 12-month period).

### 2. Retrenchment Compensation (Section 25F)
- **Notice:** 1 month written notice stating reasons (or wages in lieu of notice).
- **Statutory Severance Pay:**
  $$\text{Compensation} = 15\text{ Days' Average Pay} \times \text{Completed Years of Continuous Service}$$
  *(Any period exceeding 6 months is rounded up to a full completed year).*

### 3. Chapter V-B Special Provisions (Mines, Factories, Plantations)
- **Threshold:** Establishments employing **$\ge 100$ workmen** on an average working day.
- **Mandatory Government Clearance:** Prior permission from the appropriate Government is mandatory **before** executing any layoff, retrenchment (Section 25N), or closure (Section 25O).
- **Notice:** 3 months written notice required for closure.14:T421,### Frequent Examiner Traps
1. **Section 17 vs 18 Inversion:** Section 17 is **CRIMINAL Immunity**; Section 18 is **CIVIL Immunity**. Examiners constantly invert these two sections in UPSC APFC and EPFO EO/AO questions.
2. **Political Fund Voluntariness:** Contribution to the Political Fund under Section 16 is **STRICTLY VOLUNTARY**. A trade union cannot deduct it from general dues or expel a member for non-payment.
3. **Trade Union Age Slabs:** A person must be **at least 15 years old** to be an ordinary member of a registered trade union, but must be **at least 18 years old** to be elected as an office-bearer.
4. **Works Committee Mandate:** Works Committees are legally constituted in establishments employing **100 or more workers** (Section 3), NOT 50 or 20.
5. **Retrenchment Pay Fraction:** Retrenchment compensation is **15 days' average pay** (not 30 days or 1 month).

The Trade Unions Act allows 7 workers to apply for registration, requiring registered bodies to represent at least 10% or 100 workers in the establishment. Section 16 establishes that contributions to the political fund are strictly voluntary. Sections 17 and 18 provide vital immunities: Section 17 confers criminal immunity against Section 120B IPC conspiracy charges for trade disputes, while Section 18 shields office-bearers from civil liability for inducing contractual breaches during strikes. The Industrial Disputes Act 1947 institutes a tiered dispute settlement machinery: Works Committees in units with ≥100 workers, Conciliation Officers (reporting in 14 days), Voluntary Arbitration, and adjudication via Labour Courts and Industrial Tribunals. Section 25F guarantees retrenchment compensation equal to 15 days' average pay per year of service with 1 month notice, while Chapter V-B mandates 3 months notice and prior government clearance for layoff, retrenchment, or closure in factories, mines, and plantations employing 100 or more workers.16:T4f3,# Architecture of Industrial Relations & Dispute Resolution

1. **Trade Unions Act 1926 Statutory Architecture:**
   - Application: Minimum 7 workers.
   - Sustained Registration: 10% of workers or 100 workers (whichever is less).
   - Section 16: Voluntary Political Fund (no penalty for non-contribution).
   - **Section 17: Criminal Conspiracy Immunity (Section 120B IPC).**
   - **Section 18: Civil Suits Immunity (Tortious breach of employment contract).**
   - Age Bounds: 15 years for member, 18 years for office-bearer.
2. **ID Act 1947 Dispute Settlement Escalation:**
   - Bipartite: Works Committee (Section 3, ≥100 workers).
   - Conciliation: Conciliation Officer (Sec 4, 14-day report) -> Board of Conciliation (Sec 5, 2-month report).
   - Voluntary: Arbitration (Section 10A, award published in 30 days).
   - Adjudication: Labour Court (Sec 7, Sched II) -> Industrial Tribunal (Sec 7A, Sched II & III) -> National Tribunal (Sec 7B).
3. **Compensation & Chapter V-B Thresholds:**
   - Layoff (Sec 25C, ≥50 workers): 50% Basic + DA for first 45 days.
   - Retrenchment (Sec 25F): 1 month notice + 15 days' average pay per completed year.
