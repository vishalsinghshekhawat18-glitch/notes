| Regulatory Dimension | Non-Resident External (NRE) | Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank) [FCNR(B)] | Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Account Denomination** | **Indian Rupee (INR)** | **Designated Foreign Currency** (USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, CAD, AUD, etc.) | **Indian Rupee (INR)** |
| **Permissible Account Types** | Savings, Current, Recurring, Term Deposit | **Term Deposit ONLY** (Fixed Deposit) | Savings, Current, Recurring, Term Deposit |
| **Tenor / Maturity Period** | Min: **1 Year**; Max: Bank discretion (typically 10 yrs) | Min: **1 Year**; Max: **5 Years** | Min: **7 Days**; Max: 10 Years |
| **Foreign Exchange Risk** | Borne by **Depositor** (Conversion on deposit & withdrawal) | Borne by **Bank** (Deposit & interest held in foreign currency) | Borne by **Depositor** (if repatriated) |

```
[NRI Client Earns Foreign Exchange Overseas]
                    ↓
[Transfers Foreign Currency via SWIFT to Bank in India]
  ├── Stream 1: Converted to INR -> Credited to NRE Savings/FD -> Tax-Free Interest -> 100% Repatriable
  └── Stream 2: Kept in USD/GBP -> Credited to FCNR(B) FD (1-5 Yrs) -> Zero Forex Risk -> 100% Repatriable

[NRI Earns Domestic Rupee Income in India (Rent, Dividends, Sale of Ancestral House)]
                    ↓
[Credited to NRO Account in INR]
                    ↓
[TDS Deducted under Section 195 (30% + Surcharge or DTAA Rates)]
                    ↓
[Outward Repatriation: Form 15CA (Self Declaration) + Form 15CB (CA Certificate)]
                    ↓
[AD Bank Executes Outward Remittance up to USD 1,000,000 per Financial Year]
```

2. **Redesignation upon Return to India:** When an NRI returns to India permanently, NRE/FCNR accounts must be redesignated as resident domestic accounts or converted into **Resident Foreign Currency (RFC) accounts**.13:T469,### High-Yield Distinctions & Examiner Traps

- **Trap 1: FCNR(B) Account Types & Tenor:** FCNR(B) accounts can **ONLY be Term Deposits** (Fixed Deposits). There is **no such thing as an FCNR savings or current account**. Furthermore, the tenor is strictly **1 year to 5 years** (neither less than 1 year nor more than 5 years).
- **Trap 2: Taxation of NRO vs NRE/FCNR:** Interest on NRE and FCNR(B) is **100% tax-free in India**. Interest and credits on NRO accounts are **fully taxable in India** at standard TDS rates (30% plus cess, unless reduced under Double Tax Avoidance Agreement / DTAA).
- **Trap 3: NRO Repatriation Ceiling:** The statutory repatriation limit for NRO balances is **USD 1,000,000 ($1 Million) per financial year** (do NOT confuse with the LRS limit of $250,000).
- **Trap 4: Joint Account with Resident Relative:** An NRI can open an NRE, FCNR(B), or NRO account jointly with a resident Indian relative (as defined under Section 2(77) of Companies Act), provided the mode of operation is strictly **'Former or Survivor'** (the resident relative operates only upon the demise of the NRI primary holder).14:T4c2,### NRI Banking Deposit Accounts Architecture

1. **Non-Resident External (NRE) Account**
   - Currency: Indian Rupee (INR).
   - Account Formats: Savings, Current, Recurring, Term Deposit (min 1 year).
   - Taxation: 100% Tax-Exempt in India (Income Tax & Wealth Tax).
   - Repatriation: Fully and freely repatriable without any dollar limits.

2. **Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank) [FCNR(B)] Account**
   - Currency: Designated foreign currencies (USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, AUD, CAD).
   - Account Formats: Term Deposit ONLY (Strictly 1 to 5 years maturity).
   - Forex Risk: Borne completely by the Bank.
   - Taxation: 100% Tax-Exempt in India; Principal & interest fully repatriable.

3. **Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) Account**
   - Currency: Indian Rupee (INR).
   - Purpose: Domestic income management (rent, dividends, pensions, local asset sales).
   - Taxation: Taxable in India (Subject to 30% TDS / DTAA rates).
   - Repatriation: Capped at USD 1,000,000 ($1 Million) per FY via Form 15CA/15CB.

4. **Operational Rules & Redesignation**
   - Joint accounts with resident relatives permitted on 'Former or Survivor' basis.
   - Redesignation to Resident accounts / RFC accounts mandatory upon return to
