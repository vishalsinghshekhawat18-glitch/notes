12:T45f,The legal entitlements, targeting mechanisms, and digital logistics of India's Public Distribution System (PDS). Traces the historical evolution from Universal PDS (pre-1997) to Targeted PDS (TPDS, 1997: bifurcating BPL and APL households), culminating in the landmark **National Food Security Act, 2013 (NFSA)**. Dissects NFSA statutory architecture: (1) Legally binding entitlement covering **up to 75% of rural population and up to 50% of urban population (~67% of total national population or 81.35 Crore persons)**, (2) Beneficiary categorization: **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)** poorest of the poor households (entitled to **35 kg of foodgrains per household per month**) vs **Priority Households (PHH)** (entitled to **5 kg of foodgrains per person per month**), (3) **Women Empowerment Mandate**: eldest woman aged 18+ designated as Head of Household for ration card issuance, (4) Maternity benefit (₹6,000 under PMMVY), and (5) **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)**: enabling nationwide inter-state and intra-state ration portability for migrant workers via biometric Aadhaar authentication (e-PoS devices).13:T41a,Before 2013, if a poor person in India went to a government ration shop and the shopkeeper said *"I have no grain today, come back next month,"* the citizen had no legal recourse. Food was a government welfare charity, not an enforceable legal right.\n\nOn **September 10, 2013**, Parliament enacted the **National Food Security Act (NFSA)**, fundamentally shifting the paradigm:\n- **From Welfare to a Legal Right**: Food became a justiciable statutory entitlement. If the state fails to provide foodgrains, it is legally mandated to pay a **Food Security Allowance** in cash!\n- **Gender Revolution**: For centuries, ration cards had the name of the male patriarch. NFSA legally mandated that the **eldest woman of the household (aged 18 or above)** MUST be the official Head of the Household on the ration card!\n- **National Portability (ONORC)**: A construction worker from Rajasthan working in Mumbai can walk into any local ration shop in Mumbai, place his thumb on an electronic machine, and withdraw his family's subsidized grains instantly!14:T5d9,```\n                      NATIONAL FOOD SECURITY ACT (NFSA 2013)\n                                        │\n     ┌──────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┐\n     ▼                                  ▼                                  ▼\n1.
