### Key Statutory Rules:
1. **Single Nominee Rule:** In deposit accounts, nomination can be made in favor of **only ONE individual person** (multiple nominees are prohibited for a single deposit account).
2. **Minor as Nominee:** A minor can be appointed as a nominee, provided the depositor appoints an adult guardian to receive funds on the minor's behalf in Form DA-1.
3. **Accounts Ineligible for Nomination:** Accounts opened in the name of sole proprietorships (can have nomination as individual), partnerships, trusts, companies, and HUFs **cannot** have nomination
