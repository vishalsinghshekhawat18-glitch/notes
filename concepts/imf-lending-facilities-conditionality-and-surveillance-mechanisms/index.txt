### Reserve Tranche vs Upper Credit Tranches
- **Reserve Tranche (Automatic Drawing)**: The 25% of a member's quota paid in foreign currency. A member can draw this down **instantly on demand with zero interest and zero policy conditions**.
- **Upper Credit Tranches & EFF**: Borrowings exceeding the reserve tranche. Subject to strict, phased **macroeconomic conditionality** and quarterly performance reviews.13:T639,Beyond crisis lending, the IMF performs continuous global surveillance:

### India's Historic Interaction with the IMF
India was a frequent borrower from the IMF between 1957 and 1991 (including Stand-By loans in 1981 and the famous 1991 EFF program). Following the 1991 reforms and the build-up of foreign exchange reserves, **India fully repaid all IMF loans ahead of schedule by May 2000**.
