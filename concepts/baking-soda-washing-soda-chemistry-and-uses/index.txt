| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Common Commercial Name**| **Baking Soda / Meetha Soda** | **Washing Soda / Dhoban Soda** |

Water**.<br>• Manufacturing **Glass, Soap, Paper, Borax**.<br>• Household laundry cleaning agent.
