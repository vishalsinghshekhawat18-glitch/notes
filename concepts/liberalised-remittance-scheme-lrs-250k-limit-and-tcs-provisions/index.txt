11:Tae4,### 1. The LRS Annual Limit Evolution & Coverage

Introduced in 2004 with an initial limit of $25,000, the LRS cap was progressively expanded to its current ceiling of **USD 250,000 ($250,000)** per financial year (April 1 to March 31). In a family of four (two parents and two minor children), each individual has an independent $250,000 limit, allowing a consolidated household remittance of up to **$1,000,000 per financial year** (provided funds originate from each individual's independent bank account).

### 2. Permissible vs Prohibited LRS Use Cases

| Permissible Transactions under LRS | Strictly Prohibited Transactions under LRS |
| :--- | :--- |
| • **Current Account:** Private visits abroad, gift/donation to non-resident, going abroad for employment, emigration, maintenance of close relatives abroad, business trips, medical treatment, overseas studies | • Remittance for any purpose specifically prohibited under **Schedule I** (e.g., lottery tickets, sweepstakes, banned magazines) |
| • **Capital Account:** Opening and maintaining foreign currency bank accounts abroad, acquisition of immovable property overseas, investment in foreign equity shares, debt securities, mutual funds, venture capital funds | • Purchase of foreign currency **margin trading**, overseas derivatives, or forex spread betting |
| • Extending loans in INR to Non-Resident Indian (NRI) relatives (subject to specific FEMA terms) | • Remittances to entities or bank accounts in **FATF Non-Cooperative / Blacklisted jurisdictions** |
| • Capital contribution to Overseas Direct Investment (ODI) in joint ventures / wholly owned subsidiaries (as per ODI guidelines) | • Direct purchase of overseas virtual digital assets / cryptocurrencies or gifting capital to unrelated non-residents |

### 3. Tax Collected at Source (TCS) Regime under Section 206C(1G)

| Purpose of Remittance under LRS | Threshold (Per FY per Individual) | TCS Rate (Effective Current Regime) |
