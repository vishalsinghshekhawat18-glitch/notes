Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS): $250,000 Cap, Permissible Uses & TCS Regime

The flagship foreign exchange facilitation facility established by the Reserve Bank of India on February 4, 2004, enabling Resident Individuals (including minors) to freely remit up to USD 250,000 ($250,000) per financial year (April to March) across any combination of permissible Current and Capital Account transactions (overseas education, medical treatment, private travel, foreign equity investments, property acquisition abroad, gifts/donations), subject to strict statutory negative lists (lottery, margin trading, crypto) and the Tax Collected at Source (TCS) regime under Section 206C(1G) of the Income Tax Act.

• Current Account: Private visits abroad, gift/donation to non-resident, going abroad for employment, emigration, maintenance of close relatives abroad, business trips, medical treatment, overseas studies • Remittance for any purpose specifically prohibited under Schedule I (e.g., lottery tickets, sweepstakes, banned magazines)
• Capital Account: Opening and maintaining foreign currency bank accounts abroad, acquisition of immovable property overseas, investment in foreign equity shares, debt securities, mutual funds, venture capital funds • Purchase of foreign currency margin trading, overseas derivatives, or forex spread betting

How It Works / Mechanism

Operational Mechanism: Form A2, PAN Validation & Centralised LRS Surveillance

The execution of an outward remittance under LRS operates through a real-time regulatory pipeline:

[Remitter Approaches AD Category-I Bank with Outward Wire Request]
                    ↓
[Submission of Form A2 cum Application & Mandatory PAN (Permanent Account Number)]
                    ↓
[Bank Queries RBI Centralized LRS Verification Database in Real Time]
                    ↓
[Surveillance Check: Verifies Total Cumulative Remittances under PAN ≤ $250,000 in FY]
                    ↓
[Calculation & Debit of Applicable TCS under Section 206C(1G) of IT Act]
                    ↓
[SWIFT MT103 Message Generated -> Foreign Exchange Transmitted to Beneficiary Bank]
                    ↓
[Bank Reports Transaction Record to RBI in Daily / Monthly LRS Return]
  1. Mandatory PAN: Permanent Account Number (PAN) is legally mandatory for all outward remittances under LRS without exception.
  2. Documentation for Large Studies/Medical: For overseas education and medical expenses, remittances exceeding $250,000 in a year are permitted if backed by an official estimate from the foreign hospital or educational institution.
Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Exam Application & Examiner Traps

High-Yield Distinctions & Examiner Traps

  • Trap 1: Eligibility of HUFs and Corporates: LRS is available only to resident natural individual human beings (including minors). HUFs, Partnership firms, Companies, and Trusts are strictly NOT eligible to remit under LRS.
  • Trap 2: LRS Financial Year vs Calendar Year: The $250,000 limit operates on an Indian Financial Year basis (April 1 to March 31), NOT a calendar year.
  • Trap 3: Margin Trading Prohibition: An individual cannot use LRS funds to engage in forex margin trading or CFD trading overseas, even if within the $250,000 limit.
  • Trap 4: TCS on Education Loan vs Self-Funded: Education remittances funded by an education loan taken from an approved financial institution carry a concessional TCS rate of 0.5% above ₹7 Lakhs, whereas self-funded education carries a 5.0% TCS above ₹7 Lakhs.
