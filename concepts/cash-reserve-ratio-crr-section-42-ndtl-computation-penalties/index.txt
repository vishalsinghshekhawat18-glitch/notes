### Mathematical Formulation of NDTL
$$\text{NDTL} = \text{Demand Liabilities (DL)} + \text{Time Liabilities (TL)} + \text{Other Demand & Time Liabilities (ODTL)} + \text{Net Inter-Bank Liabilities (IBL)}$$

$$\text{Net IBL} = \max(0, \text{Liabilities to other banks} - \text{Assets / balances with other banks})$$

### CRR Framework Matrix

| Parameter | Statutory / Regulatory Rule | Numerical Norm / Standard | Operational Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Governing Section** | Section 42(1) RBI Act 1934 | Current CRR: **4.50%** of NDTL | Statutory floor (3%) and ceiling (20%) were abolished in 2006. RBI can set CRR at any level. |
| **Reference Base Date** | Second preceding fortnight | Alternate Friday NDTL | NDTL of Friday 2 weeks prior serves as the benchmark for the upcoming 14-day fortnight. |
| **Fortnight Average Rule** | Average of 14 days | Must equal $\ge 100\%$ of required CRR | Sum of daily balances over 14 days $\div 14 \ge$ Required CRR. |
| **Daily Minimum Rule** | Every single day of fortnight | **Minimum 90%** of required CRR | Prevents banks from draining cash mid-fortnight and depositing huge sums on the last day. |
| **Interest Remuneration** | Section 42(1B) RBI Act | **0% (Zero Interest)** | RBI pays no interest on CRR balances since March 31, 2007. |
| **Day 1 Default Penalty** | Section 42(3) RBI Act | **Bank Rate + 3.00% p.a.** | Levied on the exact daily deficit amount. |
| **Subsequent Days Default** | Section 42(3) RBI Act | **Bank Rate + 5.00% p.a.** | Levied on the deficit if default continues on subsequent days. |
| **Incremental CRR (I-CRR)** | Section 42(1A) RBI Act | Temporary discretionary levy | Imposed to absorb transient surplus liquidity (e.g. 10% I-CRR during ₹2000 note withdrawal in 2023). |12:T60a,```mermaid

### Step-by-Step Maintenance Protocol:
1. **NDTL Computation:** On Reporting Friday, bank sums up: (a) Savings & Current account balances, (b) Fixed & Recurring deposits, (c) Margins against LCs/BGs, (d) Unclaimed deposits, minus interbank assets.
2. **Lagged Maintenance:** This NDTL serves as the required base for the 14-day maintenance cycle that begins 15 days later (Saturday to second Friday).
3. **Daily Liquidity Operations:** Bank treasury monitors intraday settlement via e-Kuber. If balances drop below 90% of required CRR on any day, treasury immediately borrows from Call Money Market, TREPS, or MSF to bridge the deficit.
4. **End-of-Fortnight Reconciliation:** If total fortnight average falls short, RBI system automatically calculates the penal interest and debits the bank's current account with
