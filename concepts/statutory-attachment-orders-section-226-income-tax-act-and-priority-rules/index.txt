| Dimension | Garnishee Order (Order XXI CPC) | Income Tax Attachment (Sec 226(3) IT Act) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Issuing Authority** | Civil Court of Competent Jurisdiction | Assessing Officer / Tax Recovery Officer (TRO) |
| **Governing Statute** | Code of Civil Procedure 1908 | Income Tax Act 1961 (and Sec 83 CGST Act 2017) |
| **Attachment of Future Inflows** | **NO** (attaches only funds at exact service time) | **YES** (attaches present funds + all future credits) |
| **Joint Account Attachment** | **NO** (cannot attach joint account for individual debt) | **YES** (attaches the proportionate share of assessee under Sec 226(3)(vi)) |
| **Two-Stage Process?** | **YES** (Order Nisi followed by Order Absolute) | **NO** (Single direct statutory demand notice) |
| **Priority in Conflict** | Subordinate to sovereign tax dues | **Takes statutory priority over civil decrees** |

### Sovereign Debt Priority Rules:
1. **Pre-Existing Set-Off:** If a bank's loan was already overdue before the IT notice arrived, the bank's contractual Right of Set-Off prevails over the IT department for the existing balance.
2. **Banker's Personal Liability:** Under Section 226(3)(x), if a branch manager allows the customer to withdraw funds after receiving an IT notice, the bank manager is personally treated as an
