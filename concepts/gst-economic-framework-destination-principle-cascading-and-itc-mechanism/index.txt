11:T871,Here is how the Input Tax Credit (ITC) mechanism operates across a 3-stage supply chain at an $18\%$ GST rate:

```
                             THE INPUT TAX CREDIT (ITC) FLOW
                                            │
┌───────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┐
▼                                           ▼                                           ▼
STAGE 1: RAW MATERIAL SUPPLIER              STAGE 2: SHOE MANUFACTURER                  STAGE 3: RETAIL SHOP
• Buys raw leather: ₹1,000                  • Buys raw material: ₹1,000 + ₹180 GST      • Buys shoes from factory: ₹2,000 + ₹360
• Sells to Manufacturer for: ₹1,000         • Adds Value (Labour/Profit): ₹1,000        • Adds Value (Retail margin): ₹1,000
• Output Tax charged ($18\%$): ₹180         • Selling Price to Retailer: ₹2,000         • Final Consumer Price: ₹3,000
• Input Tax Credit (ITC): ₹0                • Output Tax ($18\%$ of ₹2,000): ₹360       • Output Tax ($18\%$ of ₹3,000): ₹540
• Net Tax Remitted to Govt:                 • Minus ITC (Tax paid in Stage 1): ₹180     • Minus ITC (Tax paid in Stage 2): ₹360
  ₹180 - ₹0 = ₹180                          • Net Tax Remitted to Govt:                 • Net Tax Remitted to Govt:
                                              ₹360 - ₹180 = ₹180                          ₹540 - ₹360 = ₹180
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TOTAL TAX COLLECTED BY GOVERNMENT = ₹180 (Stage 1) + ₹180 (Stage 2) + ₹180 (Stage 3) = ₹540
FINAL TAX PAID BY CONSUMER = 18% of final ₹3,000 price = ₹540 (ZERO CASCADING / PURE VALUE-ADDED TAX!)
```12:T461,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: Origin vs Destination Principle**: GST is a **Destination-Based Tax**. If a car is manufactured in Tamil Nadu and sold to a consumer in Rajasthan, the GST revenue belongs to **Rajasthan (the consumption state)**, NOT Tamil Nadu.
- **Trap 2: Items Excluded from GST**:
  - **Alcoholic liquor for human consumption**: **Permanently excluded** from GST by Article 366(12A) (taxed by States via State Excise & VAT).
  - **5 Petroleum Products** (Petrol, Diesel, Crude, ATF, Gas): **Temporarily deferred** under Section 9(2) (can be brought under GST whenever GST Council decides).
  - **Electricity & Real Estate Stamp Duty**: Taxed exclusively by States outside GST.
- **Trap 3: E-Way Bill Threshold**: The statutory threshold for generating an inter-state E-Way Bill is consignment value **exceeding ₹50,000**.

### Cross-Exam Focus
- **RBI Grade B & SEBI Grade A**: Destination principle, ITC utilization order, RCM rules, and cascading elimination.
