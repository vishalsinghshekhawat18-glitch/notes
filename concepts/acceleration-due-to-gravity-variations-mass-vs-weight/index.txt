11:T4b3,### 1. Shape of Earth (Poles vs Equator)
Since Earth is flattened at poles: $R_{\text{equator}} > R_{\text{pole}}$ (by $\approx 21 \, \text{km}$).
$$g = \frac{GM}{R^2} \implies \mathbf{g_{\text{pole}} > g_{\text{equator}}}$$
*(A gold bar bought at the equator by weight weighs slightly more at the poles).*

### 2. Variation with Altitude (Height $h$)
At height $h$ above Earth surface ($h \ll R$):
$$g_h = g \left(1 - \frac{2h}{R}\right) \implies g \text{ decreases as you go higher}.$$

### 3. Variation with Depth (Depth $d$)
Inside a mine at depth $d$ below surface:
$$g_d = g \left(1 - \frac{d}{R}\right) \implies g \text{ decreases as you go deeper}.$$
- **At Earth's Centre ($d = R$)**: $g_{\text{centre}} = g(1 - 1) = \mathbf{0}$.

### 4. Apparent Weight in an Elevator / Lift (Mass $m$)
- **Lift at rest or moving at uniform speed ($a = 0$)**: $W_{\text{app}} = mg$ (Normal weight).
- **Lift accelerating UPWARD with acceleration $a$**: $W_{\text{app}} = m(g + a)$ (Feels heavier).
- **Lift accelerating DOWNWARD with acceleration $a$**: $W_{\text{app}} = m(g - a)$ (Feels lighter).
- **Free Fall (Cable snaps, $a = g$)**: $W_{\text{app}} = m(g - g) = \mathbf{0}$ (State of
