Acceleration Due to Gravity (g), Free Fall, Altitude/Depth Variations & Mass vs Weight

The local gravitational acceleration of Earth, its geographical and spatial variations, and the fundamental distinction between invariant mass and variable weight.

Intuition & Context

Why You Weigh Less at the Equator and Zero at Earth's Center

Earth is not a perfect sphere; it is an oblate spheroid bulging at the equator and flattened at the poles. Because you are ≈21 km\approx 21 , \text{km}≈21km closer to Earth's center at the North Pole than at the Equator, Earth's gravity pulls on you noticeably harder at the poles. If you dig a tunnel to the very center of the Earth, all surrounding magma and rock pulls on you equally in every direction, canceling out completely—leaving you weightless (g=0g = 0g=0)!

Mathematical Variations of g: Poles, Altitude, Depth & Rotation

1. Shape of Earth (Poles vs Equator)

Since Earth is flattened at poles: Requator>RpoleR_{\text{equator}} > R_{\text{pole}}Requator​>Rpole​ (by ≈21 km\approx 21 , \text{km}≈21km).
g=GMR2  ⟹  gpole>gequatorg = \frac{GM}{R^2} \implies \mathbf{g_{\text{pole}} > g_{\text{equator}}}g=R2GM​⟹gpole​>gequator​
(A gold bar bought at the equator by weight weighs slightly more at the poles).

2. Variation with Altitude (Height hhh)

At height hhh above Earth surface (h≪Rh \ll Rh≪R):
gh=g(1−2hR)  ⟹  g decreases as you go higher.g_h = g \left(1 - \frac{2h}{R}\right) \implies g \text{ decreases as you go higher}.gh​=g(1−R2h​)⟹g decreases as you go higher.

3. Variation with Depth (Depth ddd)

Inside a mine at depth ddd below surface:
gd=g(1−dR)  ⟹  g decreases as you go deeper.g_d = g \left(1 - \frac{d}{R}\right) \implies g \text{ decreases as you go deeper}.gd​=g(1−Rd​)⟹g decreases as you go deeper.

  • At Earth's Centre (d=Rd = Rd=R): gcentre=g(1−1)=0g_{\text{centre}} = g(1 - 1) = \mathbf{0}gcentre​=g(1−1)=0.

4. Apparent Weight in an Elevator / Lift (Mass mmm)

  • Lift at rest or moving at uniform speed (a=0a = 0a=0): Wapp=mgW_{\text{app}} = mgWapp​=mg (Normal weight).
  • Lift accelerating UPWARD with acceleration aaa: Wapp=m(g+a)W_{\text{app}} = m(g + a)Wapp​=m(g+a) (Feels heavier).
  • Lift accelerating DOWNWARD with acceleration aaa: Wapp=m(g−a)W_{\text{app}} = m(g - a)Wapp​=m(g−a) (Feels lighter).
  • Free Fall (Cable snaps, a=ga = ga=g): Wapp=m(g−g)=0W_{\text{app}} = m(g - g) = \mathbf{0}Wapp​=m(g−g)=0 (State of Weightlessness).
