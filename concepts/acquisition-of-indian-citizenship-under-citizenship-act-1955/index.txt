11:T790,### 1. By Birth (Section 3 — Evolution of Law):
• **Jan 26, 1950 to July 1, 1987:** Pure *Jus Soli* (born in India = automatic citizen, irrespective of parents’ nationality).
• **July 1, 1987 to Dec 3, 2004:** Born in India + **at least one parent must be an Indian citizen** at the time of birth (1986 Amendment).
• **After Dec 3, 2004 (2003 Amendment):** Born in India + **both parents are citizens of India**, OR **one parent is a citizen and the other is NOT an illegal migrant** at birth.

### 2. By Descent (Section 4 — Born Outside India):
• Born outside India on or after Jan 26, 1950 if father was an Indian citizen.
• **1992 Amendment (Gender Equality):** Either **father OR mother** was an Indian citizen at the time of birth.
• **Post-2004:** Birth must be registered at an Indian consulate within **1 year** with an undertaking that the minor does not hold a foreign passport.

### 3. By Registration (Section 5 — Prescribed Categories):
• Persons of Indian Origin (PIO) ordinarily resident in India for **7 years** before applying.
• Persons married to an Indian citizen and resident in India for **7 years**.
• Minor children of Indian citizens.
• Persons of full age whose parents were registered as citizens of independent India.

### 4. By Naturalisation (Section 6 — Foreigners):
• Qualifications: Not an illegal migrant; renounces foreign citizenship; resident in India for **12 continuous months** preceding application, plus **11 years aggregate residence out of the preceding 14 years** (shortened to 5 years for CAA 2019 beneficiaries); good character; adequate knowledge of an **Eighth Schedule language**; intends to reside in India.
