```
                      THE 8 STAGES OF THE UNION BUDGET
                                     │
1. PRESENTATION OF BUDGET ─────► Feb 1: Finance Minister presents AFS (Art 112) & Budget Speech
                                     │
2. GENERAL DISCUSSION     ─────► 3-4 days general economic debate in Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha
                                     │
3. COMMITTEE SCRUTINY     ─────► Houses adjourn for 3-4 weeks; 24 Standing Committees
                                 examine Demands for Grants of all Ministries in detail
                                     │
4. VOTING ON DEMANDS      ─────► Lok Sabha exclusively debates & votes on Demands for Grants.
                                 Opposition can move Cut Motions (Policy, Economy, Token).
                                 On final day, all remaining demands are passed via "GUILLOTINE".
                                     │
5. APPROPRIATION BILL     ─────► Introduced under Article 114 to legalize withdrawals from CFI.
                                     │
6. FINANCE BILL           ─────► Introduced under Article 110 (Money Bill) to legalize tax proposals.
                                 Must be passed within 75 days (Provisional Collection of Taxes Act).
                                     │
7. BUDGET EXECUTION       ─────► Ministries spend allocated funds from April 1 to March 31.
                                     │

                                 Committee (PAC) investigates irregularities and excess spending.
```12:T68e,### 1. The Three Types of Cut Motions in Lok Sabha
- **Policy Disapproval Cut**: *"The amount of the demand be reduced to ₹1."* (Represents complete disapproval of the underlying government policy; if passed, the government falls!).
- **Economy Cut**: *"The amount of the demand be reduced by a specified lump sum (e.g., ₹100 Crore)."* (Argues that the ministry can save money without harming policy).
- **Token Cut**: *"The amount of the demand be reduced by ₹100."* (Used to voice a specific local grievance within the sphere of the government's responsibility).

### 2. Extraordinary Budgetary Grants (Articles 115 & 116)

| Grant Name | Constitutional Article | Operational Trigger / Scenario |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Supplementary Grant** | **Article 115(1)(a)** | When the amount authorized in the budget is found **insufficient** during the year |
| **Additional Grant** | **Article 115(1)(a)** | When a need arises for spending on a **new service** not contemplated in the budget |
| **Excess Grant** | **Article 115(1)(b)** | When money spent **exceeds the amount granted** for that service; must be voted by Lok Sabha after financial year ends, **only after approval by the PAC** |
| **Vote of Credit** | **Article 116(b)** | Granted to meet an unexpected demand arising from an emergency where details cannot be specified in a budget (**"Parliament's Blank Cheque"**) |
| **Exceptional Grant** | **Article 116(c)** | Granted for a special purpose that forms no part of the current service of any financial year |
| **Token Grant** | **Article 115** | Granted when funds are re-appropriated from existing schemes for a new service via a nominal ₹1 demand |13:T4d8,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: The Word "Budget" in the Constitution**: The word "Budget" is **NOWHERE mentioned in the Constitution**! The formal constitutional term is **"Annual Financial Statement" (Article 112)**.
- **Trap 2: Rajya Sabha Powers over Budget**:
  - Rajya Sabha can **discuss** the budget and the Appropriation/Finance bills.
  - Rajya Sabha has **ZERO power to vote on Demands for Grants** (Demands for Grants are voted exclusively by the Lok Sabha under Article 113(2)).
  - Rajya Sabha cannot reject or amend a Money Bill (Finance Bill); it can only make recommendations within **14 days**.
- **Trap 3: Excess Grant Requirement**: An Excess Grant can ONLY be introduced in the Lok Sabha **AFTER the Public Accounts Committee (PAC)** has examined and recommended it.
- **Trap 4: Policy Cut Motion vs Token Cut Motion**: Policy Cut reduces the demand to **₹1**; Economy Cut reduces it by a **specified amount**; Token Cut reduces it by **₹100**.
