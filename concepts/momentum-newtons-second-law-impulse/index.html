Linear Momentum, Newton's Second Law (F=ma) & Impulse Dynamics

The quantitative law of dynamics relating applied force to the time rate of change of momentum, and impulse as force integrated over time.

Intuition & Context

Why Catching a Cricket Ball Stings Without Pulling Back

A tiny table tennis ball hit at $50 , \text{km/h}$ bounces off your palm harmlessly, but a heavy leather cricket ball at that same speed can fracture your fingers. Why? Because the cricket ball carries far more Momentum (m×vm \times vm×v).
