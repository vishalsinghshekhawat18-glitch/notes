### The 7 Major Pillar Edicts Sites
All 7 Major Pillar Edicts are carved on monolithic **Chunar sandstone** pillars with mirror-like polish and written in **Brahmi script**:
1. **Delhi-Topra:** Originally at Topra (Ambala, Haryana); transported to Delhi by **Sultan Feroz Shah Tughlaq** in 1356 CE; **ONLY pillar containing all 7 Pillar Edicts** (others contain only 6).
2. **Delhi-Meerut:** Originally at Meerut (UP); transported to Delhi by Feroz Shah Tughlaq; damaged by explosion in 1713 and reassembled.
3. **Allahabad / Kosam (Prayagraj):** Originally at Kausambi; shifted to Allahabad Fort by Akbar/Jahangir; contains Ashoka’s Schism Edict, **Queen’s Edict (Karuvaki & Prince Tivara)**, Samudragupta's *Prayag Prashasti* (by Harishena), and Jahangir's Persian inscription.
4. **Lauriya-Araraj:** East Champaran, Bihar (contains 6 edicts, no capital animal remains).
5. **Lauriya-Nandangarh:** West Champaran, Bihar (superb complete pillar topped with a single majestic lion capital).
6. **Rampurva:** West Champaran, Bihar (two pillars discovered: one topped with a Lion capital, now in Indian Museum Kolkata, and one with a magnificent Bull capital, now in Rashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi).
