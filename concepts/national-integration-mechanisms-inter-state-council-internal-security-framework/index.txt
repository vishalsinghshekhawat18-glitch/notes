11:T6ab,### 1. Inter-State Council (Article 263) — Constitutional Body
* **Constitutional Basis:** Article 263 empowers the President to create the Council if public interest is served.
* **Establishment:** Set up on **May 28, 1990** upon the recommendation of the Sarkaria Commission.
* **Composition:**
  - **Chairman:** Prime Minister of India.
  - **Members:** Chief Ministers of all States and UTs with Assemblies, Administrators of UTs without Assemblies, and 6 Union Cabinet Ministers nominated by the PM.
* **Standing Committee:** Chaired by the Union Home Minister with 5 Union Cabinet Ministers and 9 Chief Ministers.
* **Mandate:** Inquiring into disputes between States, investigating subjects of common interest, and deliberating policy coordination.

### 2. Zonal Councils (States Reorganisation Act 1956) — Statutory Bodies
* **Status:** Created under Part III of the *States Reorganisation Act 1956* (NOT a constitutional body).
* **Five Zones:** Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, and Southern.
* **Leadership:**
  - **Chairman:** Union Home Minister (common chairman for all five Zonal Councils).
  - **Vice-Chairman:** Chief Ministers of member States by annual rotation.
* **North Eastern Council (NEC):** Created separately under the *North Eastern Council Act 1971* covering the 8 North-Eastern states (Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura, and Sikkim added in 2002).

### 3. National Integration Council (NIC)
* **Origin:** Constituted in **1961 by Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru** following the National Integration Conference.
* **Role:** Extra-constitutional advisory body to address communalism, casteism, regionalism, and linguistic chauvinism.12:T595,### Article 355: The Protective Duty of the Union
Article 355 acts as the constitutional bridge between peace-time federalism and emergency intervention. It imposes a two-fold duty on the Union:
1. Protect every State against **external aggression and internal disturbance**.
2. Ensure that state governance is conducted **in accordance with the Constitution**.
