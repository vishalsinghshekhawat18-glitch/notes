### Chronology of Vardhamana Mahavira (599–527 BCE / 540–468 BCE)
- **Lineage:** Father Siddhartha (Head of **Jnatrika** Kshatriya clan); Mother **Trishala** (Sister of Lichchhavi King Chetaka of Vaishali); Wife Yashoda; Daughter Anojja/Priyadarshana.
- **Renunciation:** At age 30, with permission of elder brother Nandivardhana.
- **Austerity:** 12 years of severe penance; discarded clothes in the 13th month.
- **Enlightenment (*Kevala Jnana*):** At age 42, on the bank of River **Rijupalika** near Jrimbhikagrama under a **Sala** tree.
- **First Sermon:** At Vipulachala hill near Rajagriha; first disciple (*Gandhara*) was **Gautama Indrabhuti** (head of 11 Gandharas).
