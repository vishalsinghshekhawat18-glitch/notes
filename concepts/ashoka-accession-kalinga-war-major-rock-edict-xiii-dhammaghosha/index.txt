12:T6e5,### Chronological Milestones of Ashoka’s Reign
| Year BCE / Regnal Year | Historical Milestone | Primary Epigraphic / Textual Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **c. 273 BCE** | Demise of Bindusara; Ashoka seizes de facto power with help of **Radhagupta**. | *Mahavamsa*, *Divyavadana* |
| **c. 269 BCE** | Formal Royal Coronation (*Abhisheka*) after 4-year interregnum. | Ashokan Inscriptions (calculated from coronation year) |
| **c. 261 BCE (8th Regnal Year)** | **The Kalinga War**; slaughter of 100k, capture of 150k; conversion to Buddhism. | **Major Rock Edict XIII** |
| **c. 259 BCE (10th Regnal Year)** | First pilgrimage to Bodh Gaya (**Dhammayatra** replaces *Viharayatra*). | **Major Rock Edict VIII** |
| **c. 256 BCE (14th Regnal Year)** | Creation of the new administrative cadre of **Dhamma Mahamattas**. | **Major Rock Edict V** |
| **c. 250 BCE (18th Regnal Year)** | Convening of the **Third Buddhist Council** at Pataliputra; overseas Dhamma missions. | *Mahavamsa* V, *Dipavamsa* VII |
| **c. 249 BCE (20th Regnal Year)** | Pilgrimage to Lumbini (Rummindei) & Nigali Sagar (Kanakamuni Stupa). | **Rummindei & Nigali Sagar Pillar Edicts** |

### The Five Contemporary Hellenistic Kings in Major Rock Edict XIII
Ashoka explicitly claims that his *Dhammavijaya* (moral conquest) succeeded 600 yojanas away in the realms of five Greek kings:
1. **Amtiyoka:** **Antiochus II Theos** of Syria (Seleucid Empire, r. 261–246 BCE)
2. **Turamaya:** **Ptolemy II Philadelphus** of Egypt (r. 285–246 BCE)
3. **Amtekina / Antakini:** **Antigonus Gonatas** of Macedonia (r. 276–239 BCE)
4. **Maka / Magas:** **Magas** of Cyrene (modern Libya, d. c. 250 BCE)
5. **Alikasudara:** **Alexander II** of Epirus (or Alexander of Corinth, r. 272–255 BCE)13:T4b1,### 1. Geopolitical & Strategic Impetus for Invading Kalinga
- Kalinga (modern coastal Odisha) was strategically positioned between the Mauryan Gangetic heartland and the southern Deccan provinces.
- It controlled vital overland trade routes to the South (*Dakshinapatha*) and rich **maritime trade routes across the Bay of Bengal** to Southeast Asia (Suvarnabhumi/Java/Sumatra).
- A powerful, independent, hostile naval and mercantile power right next to Pataliputra was an intolerable strategic threat.

### 2. The Omission of Edict XIII in Kalinga: The Separate Kalinga Edicts
- Highly revealingly, **Major Rock Edict XIII is DELIBERATELY OMITTED** from the rock inscriptions located inside Kalinga itself at **Dhauli** and **Jaugada**.
- Instead, Ashoka substituted it with the **Two Separate Kalinga Rock Edicts**:
  - *Separate Edict I:* Addressed to the city magistrates of Tosali and Samapa, declaring: **'All men are my children (*Sarve munisa me paja*)'**, instructing judges to avoid arbitrary imprisonment and torture.
  - *Separate Edict II:* Reassuring the unconquered border forest tribes (*Atavikas*) that the Emperor desires not their destruction, but their welfare and fatherly
