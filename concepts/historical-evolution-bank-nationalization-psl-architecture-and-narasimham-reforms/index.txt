### PSL Eligible Categories (8 Broad Sectors)
1. Agriculture | 2. Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) | 3. Export Credit | 4. Education | 5. Housing | 6. Social Infrastructure | 7. Renewable Energy | 8. Others (SHGs/JLGs).

### PSL Shortfall Mechanics & PSLCs
- **Priority Sector Lending Certificates (PSLCs)**: A market-driven trading mechanism launched by RBI on the CBS (e-Kuber) platform. Banks with surplus PSL achievement sell PSLCs to deficit banks without transferring the underlying loan risk. (4 Types: PSLC-Agriculture, PSLC-SF/MF, PSLC-Micro, PSLC-General).
- **Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF)**: If a bank still fails to meet its PSL target, the shortfall must be deposited with **NABARD (RIDF)** or SIDBI/NHB/MUDRA at punitive below-market interest rates.12:T4b0,The twin reports of the M. Narasimham Committee designed the structural blueprint of modern Indian banking:

### Narasimham Committee I (1991: Committee on Financial System)
1. **Phased Reduction of Pre-emptions**: Recommended slashing statutory pre-emptions (CRR and SLR) to unlock funds for commercial credit.
2. **Deregulation of Lending Rates**: Replaced administered interest rates with market-determined pricing.
3. **Prudential Accounting (IRAC Norms)**: Mandatory classification of assets into Standard, Sub-standard, Doubtful, and Loss assets with mandatory provisioning.
4. **Entry of New Private Banks**: Allowed licensing of modern private sector banks (spawning HDFC, ICICI, Axis).
5. **Asset Reconstruction Fund (ARF)**: Recommended establishing specialized bad-loan recovery funds.

### Narasimham Committee II (1998: Committee on Banking Sector Reforms)
1. **Capital Adequacy Enhancement**: Raising CRAR to Basel standards ($9\%$).
2. **NPA Convergence**: Shortening the NPA recognition norm to the global standard of **90 days**.
