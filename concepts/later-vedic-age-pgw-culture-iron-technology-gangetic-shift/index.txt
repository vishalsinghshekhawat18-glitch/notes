11:T9dd,The Later Vedic period (c. 1000–600 BCE) is reconstructed from the Sama, Yajur, and Atharva Vedas, the Brahmanas, and the archaeology of **Painted Grey Ware (PGW)**:

### 1. The Eastward Shift & Composite Kingdoms:
| Realm / Region | Composite Tribes | Capital Cities | Textual & Archaeological Landmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kuru Realm** | Merger of **Bharata** + **Puru** tribes | **Asandivat**, **Hastinapur**, **Kaushambi** | Formed the premier Vedic kingdom under King **Parikshit** and King **Janamejaya**; celebrated in the Atharvaveda and Mahabharata traditions. |
| **Panchala Realm** | Merger of five smaller Vedic clans (Krivi, Turvasha, Keshin, etc.) | **Ahichchhatra** (Bareilly, UP) & **Kampilya** (Farrukhabad, UP) | Renowned for royal patron-philosophers like King **Pravahana Jaivali**; center of Upanishadic debates. |
| **Kosala Realm** | Eastward expansion along the Sarayu River | **Ayodhya** / **Shravasti** | Emerged as a major agricultural monarchy. |
| **Videha Realm** | Expansion across the **Sadanira (Gandak) River** | **Mithila** | King **Janaka**’s court hosted the greatest philosophical debates (featuring Sage Yajnavalkya, Gargi, and Maitreyi). |

### 2. Painted Grey Ware (PGW) Ceramic Culture:
- **Material Markers:** Wheel-turned, fine-grained, high-temperature reduction-fired grey fabric with smooth surfaces painted in black with simple geometric designs (swastikas, concentric circles, sigmas, floral dots).
- **Primary Excavated Sites:** **Hastinapur** (Meerut), **Atranjikhera** (Etah), **Ahichchhatra** (Bareilly), **Noh** (Bharatpur, Rajasthan), **Jakhera** (UP), **Bhagwanpura** (Kurukshetra, Haryana — where PGW overlaps with Late Harappan).
- **Life in PGW Settlements:** Mud-brick and wattle-and-daub houses, circular hearths, domestic horse breeding, terracotta discs, and iron toolkits.

### 3. Iron Technology (*Syama Ayas*):
- **Terminology:** 
  * Early Vedic: *Ayas* = Copper / Bronze.
  * Later Vedic: *Lohita Ayas* = Red metal (Copper); *Syama Ayas* / *Krishna Ayas* = Black metal (**Iron**).
- **Atranjikhera Evidence (c. 1000 BCE):** Excavations by R.C. Gaur yielded the earliest clear stratigraphic evidence of iron weapons (arrowheads, spearheads, daggers) and craft tools (axes, chisels, tongs, smelting slag), followed later by agricultural implements.
- **Jakhera Discovery:** Yielded an early iron socketed ploughshare and iron sickle dating to the late PGW phase (~800–700 BCE), confirming iron's transition into agricultural production.12:T470,The transformation across the Later Vedic horizon followed a multi-step causal chain:

```
[Discovery of Iron Ore Deposits in Aravalli & Vindhyan Outcrops (c. 1000 BCE)]
  --> [Smelting Iron Tools: Heavy Axes & Socketed Hoes/Ploughshares (*Phala*)]
  --> [Massive Deforestation of Gangetic Monsoon Jungle (*Videgha Mathava Legend*)]
  --> [Deep Tillage of Heavy Gangetic Alluvium -> Transition from Barley to Wet Rice (*Vrihi*)]
  --> [Massive Agrarian Surplus -> Permanent Villages -> Emergence of *Janapadas*]
```

### The Legend of Videgha Mathava (Satapatha Brahmana 1.4.1.14):
The text describes prince **Videgha Mathava** accompanied by his family priest **Gautama Rahugana**. They followed **Agni Vaishvanara** (the sacrificial fire deity), who rolled eastwards from the sacred Sarasvati, burning all forests until he reached the **Sadanira (modern Gandak River in Bihar)**. Agni refused to cross the river until Videgha Mathava established a new kingdom on its eastern bank (**Videha**).
