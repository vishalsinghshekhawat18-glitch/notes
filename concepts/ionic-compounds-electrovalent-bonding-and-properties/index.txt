11:T4a5,### Comparative Profile of Ionic and Covalent Compounds

| Property / Parameter | Ionic (Electrovalent) Compounds | Covalent (Molecular) Compounds |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bonding Mechanism** | **Complete Electron Transfer** (Metal $\to$ Non-metal). | **Electron Pair Sharing** (Between Non-metals). |
| **Constituent Units** | **Oppositely charged Ions** ($Na^+, Cl^-$). | Neutral discrete **Molecules** ($CH_4, H_2O, CO_2$). |
| **Physical State at 25°C**| **Hard, crystalline solids**. | Gases, liquids, or soft solids. |
| **Melting & Boiling Points**| **VERY HIGH** ($NaCl = 801^\circ\text{C}, CaO = 2572^\circ\text{C}$).| **Low to Moderate** ($CH_4 = -182^\circ\text{C}, C_2H_5OH = 78^\circ\text{C}$).|
| **Solubility in Water** | **Generally Highly Soluble** (Polar). | Generally Insoluble (except sugar/alcohol). |
| **Solubility in Kerosene**| **INSOLUBLE** in organic solvents. | **Highly Soluble** in non-polar solvents (petrol). |
| **Solid State Conductivity**| **Zero (Insulator)** (Ions locked in lattice).| **Zero (Insulator)** (No free ions or electrons). |
| **Molten/Aqueous State** | **EXCELLENT CONDUCTOR** (Free moving ions). | **Insulator** (No ions present).
