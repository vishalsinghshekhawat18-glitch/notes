11:T45b,Imagine how two different organizations manage 28 branch offices:
- **The Old Planning Commission Model (Command Allocation)**: The head office treats state Chief Ministers like schoolboys who must travel to New Delhi every year with begging bowls. The Planning Commission dictates: *"You will spend ₹500 Crore on building canals, whether your desert state needs canals or solar power. If you don't obey our standard scheme guidelines, we will withhold your funds."*
- **The NITI Aayog Model (Think-Tank & Federal Enabler)**: NITI Aayog holds **no money bag**. The Ministry of Finance and the Finance Commission disburse the funds directly to the states. Instead, NITI Aayog acts as a cutting-edge management consultancy and federal platform:
  1. It sits all 28 Chief Ministers around the same table (**Cooperative Federalism**).
  2. It publicly publishes rankings: *"District X in Rajasthan improved child immunization by 24% this month, while District Y in another state fell behind"* (**Competitive Federalism**).
  3. It identifies best-practice innovations from one district and replicates them nationwide.12:T75d,### 1. Institutional Architecture: Planning Commission vs NITI Aayog

| Structural Dimension | Legacy Planning Commission (1950–2014) | NITI Aayog (2015–Present) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Status** | Non-statutory, extra-constitutional body (Cabinet Resolution March 1950). | Non-statutory, extra-constitutional body (Cabinet Resolution Jan 1, 2015). |
| **Core Philosophy** | **Top-Down ("One-Size-Fits-All") Command Planning**. | **Bottom-Up ("Cooperative & Competitive Federalism")**. |
| **Financial Disbursal Powers** | Allocated Plan Funds to Central Ministries and State Governments. | **ZERO financial allocation powers** (transferred to Ministry of Finance). |
| **State Participation** | States participated passively through the National Development Council (NDC). | States are permanent co-architects via the **Governing Council**. |
| **Policy Horizon** | Rigid 5-Year Plans + Annual Schematic Approvals. | **15-Year Vision Document**, **7-Year Strategy**, and **3-Year Action Agenda**. |
| **Role in Policy** | Imposed centralized schemes and bureaucratic approvals. | Serves as an apex **Think Tank**, knowledge catalyst, and performance evaluator. |\n
### 2. NITI Aayog's 3 Pillar Transformation Engines
1. **Cooperative Federalism**: The Governing Council enables Chief Ministers to set national agendas collaboratively.
2. **Competitive Federalism**: Ranking states and districts via indices (**SDG India Index**, **Export Preparedness Index**, **State Energy & Climate Index**, **Multidimensional Poverty Index**).
3. **Targeted Micro-Development**: 
   - **Aspirational Districts Programme (ADP, 2018)**: 112 backward districts monitored monthly on 49 KPIs (Health 30%, Education 30%, Agri/Water 20%, Financial Inclusion/Skills 10%, Infrastructure 10%).

### 2. Multi-Tier Planning Horizon replacing Five-Year Plans
- **15-Year Vision Document (2017–2032)**: Long-term national goals, structural transformation targets, and global positioning.
- **7-Year Strategic Framework (2017–2024)**: Medium-term operational roadmap aligning national priorities with SDGs (e.g. *Strategy for New India @ 75*).
- **3-Year Action Agenda**: Short-term tactical allocation and legislative action plans.14:T581,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: Fund Allocation Authority**: NITI Aayog does **NOT** allocate funds or grant schematic financial approvals to states. That power belongs exclusively to the Ministry of Finance and the Finance Commission.
- **Trap 2: Constitutional Status**: NITI Aayog is **NEITHER a Constitutional body NOR a Statutory body**. It was created via an executive **Cabinet Resolution** on January 1, 2015.
- **Trap 3: Governing Council vs NDC**: The National Development Council (NDC) was the legacy body under the Planning Commission. NITI Aayog operates through its **Governing Council**, which includes all Chief Ministers as voting members from Day 1.
