Dissects the market failures inherent to agricultural insurance: Covariant Risk (systemic crop failure across entire regions), Information Asymmetry, Moral Hazard, and Adverse Selection. Analyzes **Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)**, launched in Kharif 2016 (replacing NAIS and MNAIS): (1) Uniform low premium rates payable by farmers: **2.0% of sum insured for Kharif crops**, **1.5% for Rabi foodgrains and oilseeds**, and **5.0% for annual Commercial / Horticultural crops**, with the remaining actuarial premium split equally (50:50) between Central and State Governments (90:10 for North-Eastern States), (2) Comprehensive risk coverage from pre-sowing to post-harvest losses, (3) Use of Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) and remote sensing technology, (4) Voluntary enrollment for all farmers (revamped in 2020), and (5) Contrasting PMFBY area-yield approach with the **Restructured Weather-Based Crop Insurance Scheme (RWBCIS)**.13:T4e5,| Crop Category | Season / Sowing Cycle | Maximum Premium Payable by Farmer (% of Sum Insured) | Balance Premium Subsidy Sharing |\n| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |\n| **1.
