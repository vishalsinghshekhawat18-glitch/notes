### Consolidation of the Deccan & Administrative Crises
- **Deccan Annexation:** According to Tibetan historian Taranatha, Bindusara (aided by aged Chanakya in his early reign) subdued sixteen states between the Eastern and Western oceans, extending Mauryan boundaries to the border of the Tamil country (Sangam kingdoms: Cholas, Pandyas, Cheras/Keralaputras, and Satyaputras remained independent buffer states).
- **Rebellion at Taxila (*Uttarapatha*):** The citizens of Taxila revolted against oppressive royal ministers (*Dushtamatya*). Bindusara dispatched Prince **Ashoka** (then governor of *Avanti* at Ujjain), who peacefully pacified the province through wise and just
