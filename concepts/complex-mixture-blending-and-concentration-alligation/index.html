3-Tier Worked Problem: 3-Vessel Blending with Capacity Ratios

3-Tier Worked Problem (SBI PO Mains Benchmark)

Problem: Three vessels of capacities in the ratio $3 : 2 : 1$ contain milk and water in the ratios $5 : 2, 4 : 1,$ and $4 : 3$ respectively. If the contents of all three vessels are mixed into a single large container, find the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture.

  • Tier 1 (First Principles / Fractional Capacity Integration):
    1. Let capacities be $3V, 2V, 1V$.
    2. Vessel 1 (Milk fraction =5/7= 5/7=5/7, Water fraction =2/7= 2/7=2/7):
      • Milk =3V×57=15V7= 3V \times \frac{5}{7} = \frac{15V}{7}=3V×75​=715V​; Water =3V×27=6V7= 3V \times \frac{2}{7} = \frac{6V}{7}=3V×72​=76V​.
    3. Vessel 2 (Milk fraction =4/5= 4/5=4/5, Water fraction =1/5= 1/5=1/5):
      • Milk =2V×45=8V5= 2V \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{8V}{5}=2V×54​=58V​; Water =2V×15=2V5= 2V \times \frac{1}{5} = \frac{2V}{5}=2V×51​=52V​.
    4. Vessel 3 (Milk fraction =4/7= 4/7=4/7, Water fraction =3/7= 3/7=3/7):
      • Milk =1V×47=4V7= 1V \times \frac{4}{7} = \frac{4V}{7}=1V×74​=74V​; Water =1V×37=3V7= 1V \times \frac{3}{7} = \frac{3V}{7}=1V×73​=73V​.
    5. Total Milk =15V7+8V5+4V7=19V7+8V5=95V+56V35=151V35= \frac{15V}{7} + \frac{8V}{5} + \frac{4V}{7} = \frac{19V}{7} + \frac{8V}{5} = \frac{95V + 56V}{35} = \frac{151V}{35}=715V​+58V​+74V​=719V​+58V​=3595V+56V​=35151V​.
    6. Total Water =6V7+2V5+3V7=9V7+2V5=45V+14V35=59V35= \frac{6V}{7} + \frac{2V}{5} + \frac{3V}{7} = \frac{9V}{7} + \frac{2V}{5} = \frac{45V + 14V}{35} = \frac{59V}{35}=76V​+52V​+73V​=79V​+52V​=3545V+14V​=3559V​.
    7. Final Ratio Milk : Water =15135:5935=151:59= \frac{151}{35} : \frac{59}{35} = \mathbf{151 : 59}=35151​:3559​=151:59.
  • Tier 2 (Banking Standard / LCM Capacity Normalization):
    • Sums: S1=7,S2=5,S3=7  ⟹  LCM=35S_1 = 7, S_2 = 5, S_3 = 7 \implies \text{LCM} = 35S1​=7,S2​=5,S3​=7⟹LCM=35.
    • Multiply by capacities (3,2,1)  ⟹  (3, 2, 1) \implies(3,2,1)⟹ Vessel $1 = 105, Vessel $2 = 70, Vessel $3 = 35$.
    • Vessel 1 ($105$): Milk =105×5/7=75= 105 \times 5/7 = 75=105×5/7=75, Water =30= 30=30.
    • Vessel 2 ($70$): Milk =70×4/5=56= 70 \times 4/5 = 56=70×4/5=56, Water =14= 14=14.
    • Vessel 3 ($35$): Milk =35×4/7=20= 35 \times 4/7 = 20=35×4/7=20, Water =15= 15=15.
    • Total Milk =75+56+20=151= 75 + 56 + 20 = \mathbf{151}=75+56+20=151; Total Water =30+14+15=59  ⟹  151:59= 30 + 14 + 15 = \mathbf{59} \implies \mathbf{151:59}=30+14+15=59⟹151:59.
