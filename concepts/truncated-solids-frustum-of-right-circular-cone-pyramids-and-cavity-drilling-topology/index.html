Mensuration & Geometric Invariants · Concept 5 of 6

Truncated Solids: Frustum of Right Circular Cone, Pyramids & Cavity Drilling Topology

Advanced spatial mensuration: frustum of a cone (slant height, CSA, TSA, capacity volume), bucket geometry, composite solids, and cavity drilling topological changes in surface area.

Intuition & Context

The Geometry of a Bucket: Slicing a Cone Parallel to Its Base

If you take a party hat (a right circular cone) and slice off its sharp pointed top with a horizontal cut parallel to the base, the shape left at the bottom is a Frustum (or truncated cone). In daily life, everyday objects like drinking glasses, buckets, flower pots, and lamp shades are frustums.

When working with a frustum, you are dealing with two distinct circular faces of different sizes: a larger base of radius RRR and a smaller base of radius rrr, separated by a vertical height hhh.

A frequent point of conceptual confusion in competitive exams occurs when a cavity is drilled into a solid object. Intuitively, students assume that because material is removed, the surface area must decrease. In reality, drilling a hole or cavity increases total surface area, because the newly exposed inner walls create additional surface area that must now be painted, polished, or plated!

Exam Trap & High-Yield Analysis

Examiner Traps, Slant Height Invariants & Cavity Traps

High-Yield Examiner Traps in Frustums & Cavities

  1. The Slant Height Formula Trap:

    • For a standard cone: l=h2+r2l = \sqrt{h^2 + r^2}l=h2+r2​.
    • For a frustum: l=h2+(R−r)2l = \sqrt{h^2 + (R - r)^2}l=h2+(R−r)2​.
    • Trap: Students often mistakenly write l=h2+(R+r)2l = \sqrt{h^2 + (R + r)^2}l=h2+(R+r)2​ or h2+R2−r2\sqrt{h^2 + R^2 - r^2}h2+R2−r2​. The horizontal leg of the right triangle is strictly the difference in radii: (R−r)(R - r)(R−r).

  2. The Open Top Bucket Sheet Trap:

    • A bucket is open at the top and closed at the bottom.
    • Area of metal sheet = CSA+πrbottom2\text{CSA} + \pi r_{\text{bottom}}^2CSA+πrbottom2​.
    • Trap: Adding both circular bases (πR2+πr2\pi R^2 + \pi r^2πR2+πr2) adds a metal lid, which ruins the bucket calculation and wastes time!

  3. Cavity Surface Area Direction Trap:

    • When a conical cavity is scooped out of a cylinder:
      Total Surface Area increases by (πrl−πr2)\text{Total Surface Area increases by } (\pi r l - \pi r^2)Total Surface Area increases by (πrl−πr2)
    • Because the flat base circle (πr2\pi r^2πr2) is removed, but the conical curved surface (πrl\pi r lπrl) is exposed. Since l>rl > rl>r, πrl>πr2\pi r l > \pi r^2πrl>πr2, meaning TSA strictly increases.
