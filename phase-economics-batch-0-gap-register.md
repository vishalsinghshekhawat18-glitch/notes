## 2. Granular Gap Analysis by Examination Universe

### 1. SEBI Grade A Gaps (Priority: P0)
- **Microeconomics Core**: Completely missing from existing corpus. Requires full coverage of Consumer Theory (Indifference Curves, Budget Constraints, MRS, Price/Income Effect, Giffen Goods), Producer Theory (Isoquants, Returns to Scale, Cobb-Douglas, Cost Curves), Market Structures (Perfect Competition, Monopoly, Price Discrimination, Monopolistic Competition, Oligopoly, Cournot, Sweezy Kinked Demand Curve), and Market Failures (Public goods, Negative externalities, Pigouvian taxes, Coase Theorem, Asymmetric Information, Moral Hazard, Adverse Selection).
- **Capital Market Microstructure**: Lacks deep mechanics of primary markets (IPO, FPO, Rights Issue, Book Building, ASBA), secondary markets (Clearing corporations, T+1 settlement cycle, margin trading, circuit breakers), Bond valuation, Yield to Maturity (YTM), Duration/Convexity, and Derivative instruments (Futures, Options, Greeks, Swaps).

### 2. NABARD Grade A Gaps (Priority: P0)
- **Rural Economy & Priority Credit**: Existing corpus treats agriculture in 2 generic notes. Missing: Detailed Priority Sector Lending agriculture sub-targets (18% Total Agri, 10% Small & Marginal Farmers), RIDF (Rural Infrastructure Development Fund), LTIF (Long-Term Irrigation Fund), Micro Irrigation Fund (MIF), Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme mechanics & interest subvention (MISS/AIF), Self-Help Group Bank Linkage Programme (SHG-BLP), Producer Organizations (FPO 10,000 scheme), and Dairy/Fisheries/Livestock allied sectors.
- **Agricultural Economics**: Missing CACP statutory framework, formulaic cost concept derivations ($A_2$, $A_2+FL$, $C_2$), Swaminathan Commission recommendations, PM-AASHA procurement mechanisms (PSS, PDPS, PPPS), e-NAM integration, and Model APMC Acts.

### 3. UPSC APFC Gaps (Priority: P0)
- **Labour Economics & Social Security**: Existing corpus has only 2 general unemployment notes. Missing: Theoretical wage determination (Subsistence, Wages Fund, Marginal Productivity, Collective Bargaining), Economic analysis of the 4 Labour Codes 2020 (Code on Wages 2019, Industrial Relations Code 2020, Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code 2020, Code on Social Security 2020), Statutory frameworks of EPFO (EPF Act 1952, EPS 1995, EDLI 1976), ESIC (ESI Act 1948), Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, Maternity Benefit Act 2017 amendments, and Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act 2008 / e-Shram portal.

### 4. RPSC RAS Gaps (Priority: P0)
- **Rajasthan State Economy**: Existing corpus dumped the entire state economy into 1 superficial note. Missing: Complete Rajasthan Economic Review breakdown, GSDP constant vs current prices, GSVA sectoral contributions over a 5-year trend, Arid & semi-arid agriculture, Major irrigation projects (Indira Gandhi Canal Project - IGNP, Eastern Rajasthan Canal Project - ERCP, Bisalpur Project, Narmada Canal Project - mandatory drip irrigation), Livestock Census 20th round and indigenous breeds, Mineral endowments (Lead-Zinc-Silver at Rampura Agucha/Zawar, Rock Phosphate at Jhamarkotra, Barmer-Sanchore oilfields, Pachpadra Refinery), RIICO industrial parks and Neemrana Japanese Zone, Solar energy roadmap (Bhadla Solar Park), and State Budget / Public Finance / 6th State Finance Commission.

### 5. RBI Grade B Gaps (Priority: P1)
- **Advanced Macroeconomic Models**: Solow-Swan growth model, Harrod-Domar model, IS-LM open economy (Mundell-Fleming), Expectations-augmented Phillips Curve (Friedman-Phelps accelerationist hypothesis), Taylor Rule for policy rates.
- **Monetary Policy & Liquidity Architecture**: Operating target vs intermediate target, Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) evolution (Corridor narrowing, Standing Deposit Facility - SDF introduction, Marginal Standing Facility - MSF), Monetary transmission lags and frictions.

### 6. SBI PO / IBPS PO / IIBF DBF Gaps (Priority: P1)
- **Banking Operations & Regulations**: Detailed Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) framework revised thresholds (Capital, Asset Quality, Leverage), Priority Sector Lending Certificates (PSLCs) market trading mechanics, Integrated RBI Ombudsman Scheme 2021, and NPCI retail payment systems infrastructure.

## 3. Actionable Mitigation Strategy for Rebuilding

To close all P0, P1, and P2 gaps without creating fragmented or duplicate content, the curriculum reset will:
1. **Consolidate Common Knowledge**: Teach universal economic theories (Macro, Money, Fiscal, Trade, Development) once as first-principles canonical concepts.
2. **Layer Specialized Exam Modules**:
   - Add a dedicated **Microeconomics Module** (Domain B) for SEBI Grade A & RBI.
   - Add a dedicated **Agriculture & Rural Credit Module** (Domain K) for NABARD Grade A.
   - Add a dedicated **Labour Economics & Social Security Module** (Domain M) for UPSC APFC.
   - Add a dedicated **Rajasthan State Economy Module** (Domain O) for RPSC RAS.
3. **Multi-Exam Overlay**: Attach exam lenses (Exam Concept Mappings) to every concept, explaining how the topic is tested across Prelims and Mains in each target exam.
