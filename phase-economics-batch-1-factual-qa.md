- **Claim `CLM-ECO-01-01` (Robbinsian Scarcity):** Verified against Lionel Robbins (1932) *An Essay on the Nature and Significance of Economic Science*, Chapter 1. Economics defined strictly by ends and scarce means with alternative uses.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-01-02` (Opportunity Cost & PPF Concavity):** Verified against Paul A. Samuelson (1948) *Economics*, Chapter 2. - **Claim `CLM-ECO-01-03` (Efficiency Criteria):** Verified against Hal Varian (2014) *Intermediate Microeconomics*, Chapter 31. Points on frontier are productively efficient; optimal tangency with social indifference curve defines allocative efficiency ($MRS = MRT$).

- **Claim `CLM-ECO-02-01` (Economic Systems Triad):** Verified against Samuelson & Nordhaus (2009) *Economics*, Chapter 1. Market vs Command vs Mixed systems differentiated by ownership of factors and allocation mechanisms.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-02-02` (Positive vs Normative):** Verified against Milton Friedman (1953) *Essays in Positive Economics*, Part I. Positive economics is value-neutral and testable; normative economics incorporates subjective value judgments.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-02-03` (Goods Matrix):** Verified against Richard Musgrave (1959) *The Theory of Public Finance*. 4-quadrant classification by Excludability and Rivalry (Private, Public, Common Pool, Club Goods).

### Domain B: Microeconomics (Topics 28, 29, 30, 31)

#### Concept `CON-ECO-03`: Theory of Demand, Shifts vs Movements, Giffen & Veblen Goods
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-03-01` (Law of Demand):** Verified against Alfred Marshall (1890) *Principles of Economics*, Book III. Inverse price-quantity relationship verified under ceteris paribus.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-03-02` (Shift vs Movement):** Verified against Varian (2014), Chapter 6. Own-price changes generate movements along the curve; income, substitute/complement prices, and tastes shift the curve.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-03-03` (Giffen & Veblen Paradoxes):** Verified against J.R. Hicks (1939) *Value and Capital* & Thorstein Veblen (1899) *The Theory of the Leisure Class*. Giffen goods require negative income effect to dominate substitution effect; Veblen goods derive utility from conspicuous price signaling.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-04`: Theory of Supply & Market Equilibrium
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-04-01` (Law of Supply):** Verified against Marshall (1890), Book V. Upward sloping supply curve derived from rising marginal cost.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-04-02` (Market Equilibrium):** Verified against Léon Walras (1874) *Elements of Pure Economics*. Stable equilibrium occurs at $Q_D(P^*) = Q_S(P^*)$ with self-correcting surplus/shortage dynamics.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-04-03` (Simultaneous Shifts):** Verified against Mankiw (2020) *Principles of Economics*, Chapter 4. Mathematical ambiguity rules validated for concurrent demand and supply shifts.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-05`: Elasticity of Demand & Supply
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-05-01` (Price Elasticity Measures):** Verified against Marshall (1890), Book III, Chapter IV. Point elasticity ($e_p = \frac{dQ}{dP} \cdot \frac{P}{Q}$) and Midpoint Arc elasticity formulas audited.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-05-02` (Cross & Income Elasticity):** Verified against Varian (2014), Chapter 15. $e_{xy} > 0$ for substitutes, $e_{xy} < 0$ for complements; $e_y > 1$ for luxury, $0 < e_y \le 1$ for normal/necessity, $e_y < 0$ for inferior.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-05-03` (Total Outlay Method):** Verified against Marshall (1890). Verified: $e > 1 \implies P \uparrow, TR \downarrow$; $e = 1 \implies P \uparrow, TR \text{ unchanged}$; $e < 1 \implies P \uparrow, TR \uparrow$.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-06`: Welfare Economics, Price Controls & Deadweight Loss
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-06-01` (Consumer & Producer Surplus):** Verified against Marshall (1890) & Jules Dupuit (1844). CS is area below demand curve and above price; PS is area above supply curve and below price.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-06-02` (Price Ceilings & Floors):** Verified against Mankiw (2020), Chapter 6. Price ceiling below $P^*$ causes chronic shortages and black markets; price floor above $P^*$ causes unsold surpluses.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-06-03` (Deadweight Loss):** Verified against Arnold Harberger (1964) *Taxation, Resource Allocation, and Welfare*. Deadweight loss triangle audited ($DWL = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \Delta P \cdot \Delta Q$).

#### Concept `CON-ECO-07`: Cardinal Utility Theory & Consumer Equilibrium
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-07-01` (Law of Diminishing Marginal Utility):** Verified against H.H. Gossen (1854) & Marshall (1890). First Law of Gossen: marginal utility declines monotonically with successive units.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-07-02` (Law of Equi-Marginal Utility):** Verified against Marshall (1890), Book III, Chapter V. Multi-commodity equilibrium: $\frac{MU_x}{P_x} = \frac{MU_y}{P_y} = MU_m$.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-07-03` (Water-Diamond Paradox):** Verified against Adam Smith (1776) & Marshall (1890). Value in use determined by Total Utility, value in exchange determined by Marginal Utility.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-08`: Ordinal Utility & Indifference Curve Analysis
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-08-01` (Properties of Indifference Curves):** Verified against J.R. Hicks & R.G.D. Allen (1934) *A Reconsideration of the Theory of Value*. Downward sloping, convex to origin (diminishing MRS), non-intersecting.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-08-02` (Consumer Equilibrium Tangency):** Verified against Hicks (1939), Chapter 2. Tangency condition: $MRS_{xy} = \frac{P_x}{P_y}$.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-08-03` (Hicksian vs Slutsky Decomposition):** Verified against Evgeny Slutsky (1915) & J.R. Hicks (1939). Substitution effect is unambiguously negative; total effect = substitution + income effect.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-09`: Theory of Production & Returns to Scale
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-09-01` (Law of Variable Proportions):** Verified against Joan Robinson (1933) *The Economics of Imperfect Competition*. Stage I ($MP > AP$), Stage II ($MP < AP, MP \ge 0$), Stage III ($MP < 0$). Rational production restricted to Stage II.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-09-02` (Isoquants & MRTS):** Verified against Varian (2014), Chapter 18. Isoquant slope is $MRTS_{LK} = \frac{MP_L}{MP_K}$; cost minimization condition is $\frac{MP_L}{w} = \frac{MP_K}{r}$.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-09-03` (Cobb-Douglas Production Function):** Verified against Charles Cobb & Paul Douglas (1928) *A Theory of Production*. $Q = A K^\alpha L^\beta$; returns to scale determined by $\alpha + \beta$.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-10`: Theory of Costs & Revenue
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-10-01` (Short-Run Cost Curves):** Verified against Jacob Viner (1931) *Cost Curves and Supply Curves*. $MC$ intersects $AVC$ and $ATC$ at their lowest points.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-10-02` (Long-Run Average Cost & Economies of Scale):** Verified against Viner (1931). LAC is the lower envelope of all SAC curves; U-shape governed by internal/external economies of scale.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-10-03` (Marginal Revenue & Elasticity Nexus):** Verified against Joan Robinson (1933). Mathematical relationship: $MR = P(1 - \frac{1}{|e_d|})$.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-11`: Market Structures: Perfect Competition vs Monopoly
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-11-01` (Perfect Competition Equilibrium):** Verified against Frank Knight (1921) *Risk, Uncertainty and Profit*. Short-run condition: $P = MC \ge AVC$; Long-run condition: $P = MR = MC = \min ATC$ (zero economic profit).
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-11-02` (Monopoly Pricing & Lerner Index):** Verified against Abba Lerner (1934) *The Concept of Monopoly and the Measurement of Monopoly Power*. Lerner Index: $L = \frac{P - MC}{P} = \frac{1}{|e_d|}$.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-11-03` (Price Discrimination Degrees):** Verified against A.C. Pigou (1920) *The Economics of Welfare*. 1st degree (perfect CS extraction), 2nd degree (volume discounts), 3rd degree (market segmentation by elasticity: $P_1(1 - 1/|e_1|) = P_2(1 - 1/|e_2|) = MC$).

#### Concept `CON-ECO-12`: Monopolistic Competition, Oligopoly & Market Failures
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-12-01` (Monopolistic Competition & Excess Capacity):** Verified against Edward Chamberlin (1933) *The Theory of Monopolistic Competition*. Tangency to downward sloping demand yields long-run excess capacity ($P > \min ATC$).
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-12-02` (Kinked Demand & Game Theory):** Verified against Paul Sweezy (1939) *Demand Under Conditions of Oligopoly* & John Nash (1950) *Equilibrium Points in N-person Games*. Asymmetric price response causes rigid prices and discontinuous MR curve; Prisoner's Dilemma explains cartel instability.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-12-03` (Market Failures & Coase Theorem):** Verified against Ronald Coase (1960) *The Problem of Social Cost*, George Akerlof (1970) *The Market for Lemons*, and Pigou (1920). Negative externalities cause overproduction; well-defined property rights and zero transaction costs solve externalities through bargaining.

### Domain C: National Income Accounting & Core Macro Foundations (Topic 32)

#### Concept `CON-ECO-13`: Foundations of National Income Accounting
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-13-01` (Domestic Territory & Normal Residents):** Verified against United Nations SNA 2008 & MoSPI (2015) *Methodological Guidelines on National Accounts*. Embassies abroad belong to home domestic territory; normal residents include individuals/enterprises with center of economic interest for $\ge 1$ year.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-13-02` (Factor vs Transfer Payments):** Verified against MoSPI National Accounts Statistics (NAS). Factor payments (rent, wages, interest, profit) correspond to factor services and are included; transfer payments (pensions, subsidies, scholarships) are unrequited and excluded.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-13-03` (Intermediate vs Final Goods & Double Counting):** Verified against MoSPI NAS 2015. Goods for resale or single-use consumption in production within the same financial year are intermediate and excluded from final GDP.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-14`: The Eight Macroeconomic Aggregates & 2015 MoSPI Methodology
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-14-01` (8 Aggregates Step-Ladder):** Verified against MoSPI NAS (2015). Systematic derivation bridges: Gross/Net ($\pm \text{CFC/Depreciation}$), Domestic/National ($\pm \text{NFIA}$), Factor Cost/Market Price ($\pm \text{Net Indirect Taxes}$).
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-14-02` (2015 MoSPI Methodological Reset):** Verified against MoSPI Press Release (January 30, 2015) & Central Statistical Office. Headline GDP was shifted from $\text{GDP}_{\text{FC}}$ to $\text{GDP}_{\text{MP}}$, GVA is calculated at Basic Prices ($\text{GVA}_{\text{BP}} = \text{GVA}_{\text{FC}} + \text{Production Taxes} - \text{Production Subsidies}$), and base year was revised from 2004-05 to 2011-12.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-14-03` (Basic Prices to Market Prices Bridge):** Verified against MoSPI (2015) National Accounts Revision. $\text{GDP}_{\text{MP}} = \sum \text{GVA}_{\text{BP}} + \text{Product Taxes} - \text{Product Subsidies}$.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-15`: Methods of Calculating National Income
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-15-01` (Production/Value Added Method):** Verified against MoSPI NAS (2015). $\text{GVA} = \text{Value of Output} - \text{Intermediate Consumption}$.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-15-02` (Income Method & Mixed Income):** Verified against MoSPI NAS (2015). $\text{NDP}_{\text{FC}} = \text{Compensation of Employees (CEE)} + \text{Operating Surplus (OS)} + \text{Mixed Income of Self-Employed (MI)}$.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-15-03` (Expenditure Method):** Verified against Keynes (1936) & MoSPI NAS (2015). $\text{GDP}_{\text{MP}} = C + I + G + (X - M) + \text{Discrepancies}$.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-16`: Real vs Nominal GDP, GDP Deflator & Green GDP
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-16-01` (Real vs Nominal GDP & Deflator):** Verified against MoSPI NAS (2015). $\text{GDP Deflator} = \frac{\text{Nominal GDP}}{\text{Real GDP}} \times 100$. Deflator reflects prices of all domestically produced goods with changing Paasche weights.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-16-02` (Base Year Revisions):** Verified against MoSPI (2015). Base year revisions capture structural economic shifts, new commodities (digital services), and improve MCA21 corporate filings integration.
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-16-03` (Welfare Limitations & Green GDP):** Verified against Stiglitz-Sen-Fitoussi Commission Report (2009) & Partha Dasgupta Review (2021). GDP omits unpaid domestic labor, income inequality, and environmental degradation; $\text{Green GDP} = \text{GDP} - \text{Depreciation of Natural Capital} - \text{Environmental Pollution Costs}$.

#### Concept `CON-ECO-17`: Circular Flow of Income & National Accounting Identities
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-17-01` (Two-Sector Circular Flow):** Verified against Paul Samuelson (1948). In closed economy without government: Real flow of factor services/goods equals Monetary flow of factor payments/expenditures ($Y \equiv E \equiv O$).
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-17-02` (Leakages and Injections Equilibrium):** Verified against J.M. Keynes (1936) *The General Theory of Employment, Interest and Money*. Equilibrium in four-sector economy requires total leakages to equal total injections ($S + T + M = I + G + X$).
- **Claim `CLM-ECO-17-03` (Fundamental Twin Deficit Identity):** Verified against Mankiw (2020) & RBI Macroeconomic Reports. Derived identity: $(S - I) + (T - G) = (X - M) \iff (I - S) + (G - T) = \text{CAD}$.
