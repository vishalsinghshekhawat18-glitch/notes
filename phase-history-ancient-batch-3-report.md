## 3. Historiographical Uncertainties, Nuances & Corrections

| Popular / Coaching Distortion | Forensic Historical Reality | Canonical Treatment & Qualification Applied |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Kautilya wrote the Arthashastra entirely in 321 BCE"** | **COMPOSITE DATING DEBATE** | Scholarly consensus (Trautmann, Olivelle, Kangle) establishes that the surviving text is a **composite work with a 4th Century BCE Mauryan core redacted/expanded between 3rd BCE and 2nd CE**. Presented as a normative theoretical model rather than an uncritical mirror of historical practice. |
| **"Megasthenes gives a perfect picture of Mauryan society"** | **HELLENISTIC DISTORTIONS** | Explicitly analyzed Megasthenes\' foreign misinterpretations: confusing Varna with 7 occupational classes, erroneously denying the existence of slavery (missing domestic *Dasa-Karmakaras*), and claiming Indians did not know usury or suffer famine. |
| **"Chandragupta Maurya died by Sallekhana at Shravanabelagola"** | **LATER RELIGIOUS TRADITION** | Presented as an influential, celebrated traditional narrative preserved in 10th–12th century Jain texts (*Parishishtaparvan*) and Karnataka epigraphs, while explicitly noting the absence of 3rd Century BCE contemporary epigraphic proof. |
| **"Ashoka killed 99 brothers to seize the throne"** | **THEOLOGICAL LORE** | Refuted using Ashoka\'s own contemporary inscriptions (**Major Rock Edict V**), which mention the households and welfare of his living brothers and sisters in Pataliputra and provincial cities, exposing the 99-brother story as later Sri Lankan chronicle exaggeration. |
| **"Ashoka became an absolute pacifist after Kalinga"** | **HISTORICAL ANCHOR WITH LIMITS** | Ashoka renounced aggressive expansionist warfare (*Dhammavijaya* instead of *Digvijaya*), but **retained his standing army, preserved the death penalty (with a 3-day reprieve in PE IV), and sternly warned forest tribes (*Atavikas*) in MRE XIII of royal power**. |
| **"Ashoka's Dhamma was Buddhism imposed on the empire"** | **CATEGORICAL ERROR** | Strictly distinguished **Ashoka's personal Buddhism** (pilgrimages to Bodh Gaya/Lumbini, Triratna in Bhabru edict) from his **public state Dhamma** (a universal, non-sectarian civic ethic emphasizing Ahimsa, filial respect, kindness to servants, and mutual religious tolerance in MRE XII). |
| **"Ashokan pacifism or Brahmanical reaction caused the empire's collapse"** | **OVERSIMPLIFIED MONOCAUSALITY** | Evaluated H.P. Sastri\'s "Brahmanical reaction" and Raychaudhuri\'s "Pacifism" theories as historiographically flawed. Established modern consensus: **structural hyper-centralization around the person of the emperor, fiscal strain (debased punch-marked coins), succession crises, vast geographic distances, and rising regional forces**, ending in the 185 BCE military coup by Pushyamitra Sunga. |

## 5. Canonical Concepts Created (9 Concepts)

1. **`CON-HIST-21`:** *Sources for Mauryan History: Inscriptions, Texts, Archaeology & Historiographical Limits*
   - Critical evaluation of Ashokan epigraphy, *Arthashastra* composite dating (3rd BCE–2nd CE), Megasthenes\' *Indica* (7 classes, denial of slavery/usury), Buddhist (*Mahavamsa*) and Jain (*Parishishtaparvan*) traditions, and Kumrahar 80-pillar hypostyle hall.
2. **`CON-HIST-22`:** *Chandragupta Maurya, Imperial Expansion & The Seleucid Settlement*
   - Overthrow of Nandas (c. 321 BCE) with Chanakya; 305 BCE treaty with Seleucus I Nicator (cession of 4 satrapies: Aria/Herat, Arachosia/Kandahar, Gedrosia/Baluchistan, Paropamisadae/Kabul in exchange for 500 war elephants and Megasthenes); Sudarshana Lake (Pushyagupta); Shravanabelagola Jain tradition.
3. **`CON-HIST-23`:** *Bindusara Amitraghata & Imperial Continuity*
   - Accession (c. 297–273 BCE); title *Amitraghata / Amitrochates*; diplomatic exchanges with Antiochus I (wine, figs, philosopher) and Ptolemy II (ambassador Dionysius); suppression of Taxila revolt via Prince Ashoka; patronage of Ajivika sect (astrologer Pingalavatsa).
4. **`CON-HIST-24`:** *Mauryan Imperial Administration: Centralization, Bureaucracy & Espionage*
   - Central authority (King, Mantriparishad); Top officials (*Samaharta* Tax Collector General, *Sannidhata* Treasurer, 27 *Adhyakshas*); 5 Provinces (*Taxila, Ujjain, Suvarnagiri, Tosali, Pataliputra*) under *Kumaras*; District *Rajukas* and *Yuktas*; Pataliputra 30-member municipal commission (6 boards $\times$ 5 members, 1/10th sales tax); *Gudhapurushas* espionage (*Sanstha* and *Sanchara*).
5. **`CON-HIST-25`:** *Mauryan Economy, Revenue System, Trade & State Enterprise*
   - Dual agrarian base: Crown lands (*Sita* under *Sitadhyaksha*) vs private peasant land (*Bhaga* 1/6th to 1/4th); irrigation tax (*Udakabhaga*); state monopolies (mining *Akara*, salt, forests, liquor); *Uttarapatha* and *Dakshinapatha* highways; Tamralipti seaport; silver *Karshapana* coinage; Rummindei tax relief; Romila Thapar\'s differentiated imperial control model.
6. **`CON-HIST-26`:** *Ashoka\'s Accession, Kalinga War & The Transformation of Imperial Policy*
   - Accession c. 273 BCE, Coronation c. 269 BCE; 99-brother legend refuted by MRE V; Kalinga War (c. 261 BCE / 8th regnal year) recorded in Major Rock Edict XIII (100,000 slain, 150,000 deported); transition from *Bherighosha* (war drums) to *Dhammaghosha* (sound of Dhamma); *Dhammavijaya*; retention of army, death penalty, and warning to *Atavika* forest tribes.
7. **`CON-HIST-27`:** *Ashoka\'s Dhamma: Ethical Code, Religious Harmony & Moral Governance*
   - Precise definition of Dhamma as a universal civic ethic (distinct from Buddhist monastic doctrine); four pillars: Ahimsa (MRE I & PE V game laws), filial piety & kindness to servants (*Dasa-Karmakaras*), moral moderation, and **Major Rock Edict XII on Universal Religious Concord (*Samavāya*)** condemning sectarian pride (*Atmapasanda*).
8. **`CON-HIST-28`:** *The Epigraphic Corpus: Major & Minor Edicts, Scripts, Languages & Geography*
   - James Prinsep\'s 1837 decipherment of Brahmi; identity confirmation at Maski (1915), Gujarra, Nittur, Udegolam; Multilingual geography: Prakrit in Brahmi (pan-India), Kharosthi (Shahbazgarhi & Mansehra), Greek-Aramaic bilingual (Kandahar Shar-i-Kuna); 14 Major Rock Edicts (Separate Kalinga Edicts at Dhauli/Jaugada), 7 Pillar Edicts (Topra/Meerut to Delhi), Bairat/Bhabru Edict in Rajasthan (Triratna), Barabar Caves (Ajivika dedications).
9. **`CON-HIST-29`:** *Ashokan State Policy, Mauryan Art, Imperial Decline & Historiography*
   - Creation of *Dhamma Mahamatras* in 14th regnal year (MRE V); public welfare (medical centers for humans and animals across India and 5 Hellenistic kings: Antiochus II, Ptolemy II, Antigonus, Magas, Alexander); Chunar sandstone pillars and Mauryan polish; Sarnath Lion Capital (4 lions + Elephant, Horse, Bull, Lion + Dharma Chakra); multi-causal decline historiography (evaluating H.P. Sastri, D.D. Kosambi, Romila Thapar); 185 BCE coup by Pushyamitra Sunga slaying Brihadratha.
