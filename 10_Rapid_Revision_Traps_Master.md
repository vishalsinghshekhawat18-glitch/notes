### 📖 2. Medieval Administrative & Land Tenure Trap Matrix


| System | Period / Empire | Nature of Grant | Key Prelims Trap / Distinction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Iqta System** | Delhi Sultanate (Iltutmish) | Revenue assignment to *Iqtadar / Muqti* for maintaining troops | **NOT private land ownership!** Non-hereditary revenue collection right (*Fawazil* = excess revenue transferred to treasury). |
| **Mansabdari & Jagirdari** | Mughal Empire (Akbar) | Dual rank (*Zat* = personal rank/salary, *Sawar* = cavalry troops required) | **Mansab was NOT hereditary!** A Jagir was a revenue assignment, NOT land ownership. |
| **Nayakattana / Amaram** | Vijayanagara Empire | Land grant (*Amaram*) given to Military Chiefs (*Nayakas*) | Nayakas maintained troops and paid annual tribute + military service to Raya. |
| **Watan Jagir** | Mughal Empire | Jagir granted to local autonomous rajas in their ancestral lands | **ONLY type of Jagir that WAS hereditary and non-transferable!** |


### 🧮 3. Maratha Taxation & Economic Terms Flash Matrix


• **Chauth:** $1/4$th ($25\%$) of land revenue claimed by Marathas from neighboring non-Maratha territories in exchange for protection against external plunder.
• **Sardeshmukhi:** Additional $10\%$ tax levied on top of Chauth based on Shivaji's legal claim as the *Sardeshmukh* (hereditary head deshmukh) of Maharashtra.
• **Mokasa:** Jagir/land grant given to Maratha commanders out of Chauth collections.


### 🏛️ 4. Temple Architecture Distinction Grid


• **Nagara Style (North India):** Built on raised platform (*Jagati*); Curvilinear tower (*Shikhara*) topped by *Amalaka* & *Kalasha*; **NO elaborate boundary walls or Gopurams**; No water tank inside complex.
• **Dravida Style (South India):** Enclosed within high compound walls; Majestic entrance gateway (*Gopuram*); Pyramidal step-tower (*Vimana*) topped by *Shikhara* (octagonal/dome structure); **Mandatory water tank inside complex**.
• **Vesara Style (Deccan):** Hybrid style developed under Chalukya & Hoysala dynasties (Star-shaped *Stellate* ground plan, e.g. Chennakesava Temple Belur).


### ⚖️ 5. Common Prelims & Mains Traps


> 🎯 **Exam Anchor & Trap:**
> ⚠️ **Exam Trap 1:** Asokan Edicts in the North-West (Mansehra & Shahbazgarhi) were written in **Kharosthi script** (right to left), NOT Brahmi! Kandahar inscription was bilingual (**Greek & Aramaic**).


> 🎯 **Exam Anchor & Trap:**
> ⚠️ **Exam Trap 2:** The *Iqta* system in Delhi Sultanate was NOT hereditary under Iltutmish or Alauddin Khalji; Firoz Shah Tughlaq later made Iqtas hereditary (a major cause of Sultanate decline!).
