### 🧠 Visual Mind Map & Structural Diagram


🗺️ Homi Bhabha 3-Stage Nuclear Energy Roadmap


Stage 1: Pressurised Heavy Water Reactors (PHWR)


Fuel: Natural Uranium (0.7% U-235 + 99.3% U-238). Heavy Water ($D_2O$) moderator. Byproduct: Plutonium-239 ($Pu^{239}$).


Stage 2: Fast Breeder Reactors (FBR - Kalpakkam PFBR)


Fuel: Mixed Oxide (Plutonium-239 + U-238 blanket). Liquid Sodium coolant. Breeds MORE fissile $Pu^{239}$ and converts Thorium-232 into Uranium-233 ($U^{233}$).


Stage 3: Advanced Heavy Water Reactors (AHWR)


Fuel: Thorium-232 + Uranium-233 breeder system. Unlocks India's ~25% of global Thorium reserves in Kerala monazite sands!


### ⚡ 1. Beginner Jargon Unpack


• **Nuclear Fission:** Splitting of heavy nucleus (U-235, Pu-239) into lighter fragments by neutron bombardment, releasing huge energy ($E=mc^2$).
• **Fissile Material:** Isotopes capable of undergoing fission by slow thermal neutrons (U-235, U-233, Pu-239).
• **Fertile Material:** Isotopes that cannot fission directly, but absorb neutrons to transform into fissile isotopes (U-238 $ightarrow$ Pu-239; Th-232 $ightarrow$ U-233).


### 📖 2. Layer 1: India 3-Stage Nuclear Program Master Matrix


| Stage | Reactor Type | Primary Fuel | Coolant & Moderator | Fissile Output / Breeding Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Stage 1** | PHWR (Pressurised Heavy Water Reactor) | Natural Uranium ($U^{235} + U^{238}$) | Heavy Water ($D_2O$) for both | Produces Electricity + Spent fuel containing $Pu^{239}$ |
| **Stage 2** | FBR (Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor - Kalpakkam) | $Pu^{239} + U^{238}$ & Thorium blanket | Liquid Sodium (No moderator!) | Breeds more $Pu^{239}$ & converts $Th^{232}$ into $U^{233}$ |
| **Stage 3** | AHWR (Advanced Heavy Water Reactor) | $Th^{232} + U^{233}$ fuel cycle | Light water / Heavy water | Self-sustaining $Th-U^{233}$ power generation |

### 🧠 3. Layer 2: Underlying Reasoning ("The Why")


Why does **Stage 2 Fast Breeder Reactor use Liquid Sodium**, and **NO Moderator**?


• Fast breeder reactors require **FAST neutrons** (high kinetic energy) to convert Fertile $U^{238}$ and $Th^{232}$ into fissile material.
• Moderators (like water or graphite) slow down neutrons. Therefore, FBR eliminates moderators and uses **Liquid Sodium** which cools the core without slowing neutrons!


### 🧮 4. Layer 3: Worked Diagnostic Scenario (Half-Life Calculation)


**Scenario:** A radioactive isotope $I^{131}$ used in thyroid treatment has a half-life of 8 days. Calculate the remaining percentage after 24 days.


**Step-by-Step Resolution:**


• Total time elapsed $t = 24$ days. Half-life $T_{1/2} = 8$ days.
• Number of half-lives $n = t / T_{1/2} = 24 / 8 = 3$.
• Remaining Fraction = $(1/2)^n = (1/2)^3 = 1/8 = 12.5%$.
