## 1. Evidence & Epistemic Classification Standard

Every reviewed historical claim is evaluated against authoritative archaeological, epigraphic, and textual references using the following 5-tier classification standard:
- **A — Well Established:** Confirmed by direct archaeological excavations, epigraphic records, or universal scholarly consensus (e.g. ASI reports, NCERT, peer-reviewed monographs).
- **B — Reasonable but Qualified:** Historically sound and widely accepted, but requires precise qualification to avoid overstatement or modern anachronism (e.g. "democratic" assemblies, "proto-Shiva").
- **C — Scholarly Debate:** Active academic divergence exists; canonical text must present major perspectives with epistemic humility rather than manufacturing certainty (e.g. script decipherment, nature of Harappan governance).
- **D — Incorrect / Needs Correction:** Factually inaccurate, anachronistic, or contradicted by modern evidence (must be corrected or replaced).
- **E — Unsupported / Remove:** Unverified coaching myth, synthetic hallucination, or unevidenced claim (must be purged).
