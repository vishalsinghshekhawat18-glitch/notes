Bank Capital Adequacy & The Basel III Framework: Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA), Capital Tiers, CRAR & Buffers

The core risk-sensitive capital architecture of commercial banking under the Basel III framework (codified in RBI Master Circular on Capital Regulations). Covers Risk-Weighted Assets (Credit, Market, Operational Risk), the Capital Tiers hierarchy (Common Equity Tier 1 / CET1, Additional Tier 1 / AT1, Tier 2 / Gone-Concern Capital), minimum CRAR requirements in India (9% minimum CRAR, 11.5% with 2.5% Capital Conservation Buffer / CCB; 12% for PSBs), the non-risk-based Leverage Ratio (minimum 3.5% / 4% for D-SIBs), and Countercyclical Capital Buffers (CCyB).

Non-Performing Assets (NPAs): Asset Classification Stages, Special Mention Accounts (SMA), Provisioning Norms & PCR

The statutory asset classification and loan-loss provisioning framework of the Reserve Bank of India (Income Recognition and Asset Classification / IRAC Norms). Covers the 90-day overdue rule for Non-Performing Assets (NPAs), early warning Special Mention Account tiers (SMA-0: 1–30 days, SMA-1: 31–60 days, SMA-2: 61–90 days), the 4-tier asset classification (Standard, Sub-Standard, Doubtful [D1, D2, D3], Loss Assets), statutory provisioning percentages (0.25% to 100%), the Gross NPA vs Net NPA calculation, and the Provisioning Coverage Ratio (PCR).

Stressed Asset Resolution Architecture: SARFAESI Act, 2002, The Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC, 2016) & NARCL (Bad Bank)

The legal, institutional, and structural toolkit for resolving non-performing loans and stressed corporate assets in India. Covers the SARFAESI Act, 2002 (enforcement of security interest without court intervention under Section 13), the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (IBC - Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process / CIRP timeline, Committee of Creditors / CoC, Section 14 moratorium, Section 29A disqualification, Section 53 liquidation waterfall), the RBI Prudential Framework for Resolution of Stressed Assets (June 7, 2019 Circular / Inter-Creditor Agreement), and the National Asset Reconstruction Company Limited (NARCL / "Bad Bank" with sovereign Security Receipt guarantees).
