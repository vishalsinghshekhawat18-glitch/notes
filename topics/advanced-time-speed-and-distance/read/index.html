Core Principle

Kinematic Systems & The Post-Meeting Theorem

Advanced Kinematics in Banking examinations tests Simultaneous Moving Bodies and Non-Uniform Velocity Profiles.

Two fundamental theorems unlock high-speed solving in Mains:

  1. The Post-Meeting Destination Theorem: If two bodies start simultaneously from points XXX and YYY towards each other, meet at an intermediate point, and take aaa hours and bbb hours respectively to reach their destinations after meeting:
    S1S2=ba\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}S2​S1​​=ab​​
  2. Moving Observer in a Train: When a train of length L1L_1L1​ crosses a passenger sitting inside another train of length L2L_2L2​, the distance covered is STRICTLY L1L_1L1​ (the passing train's length), because the passenger is treated as a moving point object!
SYSTEMATIC CATALOGUE

Relative Speeds, Staggered Departures & Post-Meeting Formulas

1. The Post-Meeting Destination Invariant

  • Speed Ratio: S1S2=ba\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}S2​S1​​=ab​​
  • Time Elapsed until Meeting: Tmeet=a⋅bT_{\text{meet}} = \sqrt{a \cdot b}Tmeet​=a⋅b​
  • Total Distance: D=(S1+S2)×a⋅b=S1(a+ab)=S2(b+ab)D = (S_1 + S_2) \times \sqrt{a \cdot b} = S_1(a + \sqrt{ab}) = S_2(b + \sqrt{ab})D=(S1​+S2​)×a⋅b​=S1​(a+ab​)=S2​(b+ab​).

2. Staggered Departures Between Two Stations

Let Station AAA and BBB be separated by distance DDD. Train 1 leaves AAA at time t1t_1t1​ with speed S1S_1S1​; Train 2 leaves BBB at time t2t_2t2​ (t2>t1t_2 > t_1t2​>t1​) with speed S2S_2S2​:

  1. Calculate distance traveled by Train 1 before Train 2 starts:
    Dlead=S1×(t2−t1)D_{\text{lead}} = S_1 \times (t_2 - t_1)Dlead​=S1​×(t2​−t1​)
  2. Remaining distance between trains: Drem=D−DleadD_{\text{rem}} = D - D_{\text{lead}}Drem​=D−Dlead​.
  3. Time taken to meet after Train 2 starts:
    Tmeet=DremS1+S2T_{\text{meet}} = \frac{D_{\text{rem}}}{S_1 + S_2}Tmeet​=S1​+S2​Drem​​

3. Moving Observer Crossing Invariant

  • Train 1 (Length L1L_1L1​) crossing a person sitting in Train 2 (Length L2L_2L2​):
    Crossing Time=L1Relative Speed\text{Crossing Time} = \frac{L_1}{\text{Relative Speed}}Crossing Time=Relative SpeedL1​​
    (Do NOT add L2L_2L2​! L2L_2L2​ is irrelevant because only the person is being crossed).

Boats, Streams, Moving Walkways, Escalators & Circular Races Topologies

Applied kinematic frameworks: multi-stage boat-stream round trips, stream rate shifts, moving walkway/escalator relative passenger step formulations, and multi-runner circular track synchronization theorems.

Core Principle

Fluid & Mechanical Kinematics Framework

Applied Kinematics in Banking examinations synthesizes three specialized frameworks:

  1. Boats & Streams: Fluid current assisting downstream (u+vu+vu+v) and impeding upstream (u−vu-vu−v).
  2. Moving Walkways / Escalators: Dynamic mechanical frame adding or subtracting velocity relative to stationary steps:
    S=Nwalked×(1±vevp)S = N_{\text{walked}} \times \left(1 \pm \frac{v_e}{v_p}\right)S=Nwalked​×(1±vp​ve​​)
  3. Circular Races: Continuous cyclic tracks where meeting points are determined by the coprime ratio of speeds (a+ba+ba+b opposite, ∣a−b∣|a-b|∣a−b∣ same).
SYSTEMATIC CATALOGUE

Escalator Step Invariants & Circular Synchronization

1. Moving Escalator Equations

Let SSS be total stationary visible steps, vpv_pvp​ be person walking rate (steps/sec), and vev_eve​ be escalator speed (steps/sec):

  • Walking UP an UP-Escalator (Same Direction):
    S=N1(1+vevp)S = N_1 \left(1 + \frac{v_e}{v_p}\right)S=N1​(1+vp​ve​​)
  • Walking DOWN an UP-Escalator (Opposite Direction):
    S=N2(1−vevp)S = N_2 \left(1 - \frac{v_e}{v_p}\right)S=N2​(1−vp​ve​​)
  • Equating Total Steps: If person takes N1N_1N1​ steps walking up and N2N_2N2​ steps walking down:
    S=2N1N2N1+N2[when walking speeds are equal]S = \frac{2 N_1 N_2}{N_1 + N_2} \quad \text{[when walking speeds are equal]}S=N1​+N2​2N1​N2​​[when walking speeds are equal]

2. Multi-Runner Circular Track Synchronization

For 3 runners A,B,CA, B, CA,B,C on track LLL with speeds s1,s2,s3s_1, s_2, s_3s1​,s2​,s3​:

  • Time to meet at starting point for first time:
    Tstart=LCM(Ls1,Ls2,Ls3)T_{\text{start}} = \text{LCM}\left(\frac{L}{s_1}, \frac{L}{s_2}, \frac{L}{s_3}\right)Tstart​=LCM(s1​L​,s2​L​,s3​L​)
  • Time for first meeting anywhere on track (same direction):
    Tmeet=Lgcd⁡(∣s1−s2∣,∣s2−s3∣)T_{\text{meet}} = \frac{L}{\gcd(|s_1 - s_2|, |s_2 - s_3|)}Tmeet​=gcd(∣s1​−s2​∣,∣s2​−s3​∣)L​
