Scientific Conception of Work: Scalar Dot Product, Positive, Negative & Zero Work

The scientific definition of work as the scalar product of force and displacement in the direction of force, and conditions for zero, positive, and negative work.

Intuition & Context

Why Holding a 50 kg Weight for 2 Hours is "Zero Work" in Physics

If you push against a solid brick wall with all your strength for three hours until you are drenched in sweat and exhausted, everyday language says you "worked very hard." But in physics, you did EXACTLY ZERO WORK because the wall never moved (s=0s = 0s=0). Similarly, when a railway porter carries a heavy suitcase horizontally across a platform, his muscles burn, but the downward force of gravity did zero work on the bag because the displacement was strictly perpendicular (90∘90^\circ90∘) to gravity.

1. The Railway Porter Paradox (Why W=0W = 0W=0)

  • Porter lifts suitcase upward onto his head: Positive Work (W=+mghW = +mghW=+mgh).
  • Porter holds suitcase stationary on head: Zero Work (s=0s = 0s=0).
  • Porter walks horizontally with velocity vvv:
    • Downward gravitational force: F⃗g=mgj^\vec{F}_g = mg \hat{j}Fg​=mgj^​ (vertically downward).
    • Horizontal displacement: s⃗=si^\vec{s} = s \hat{i}s=si^ (horizontal).

2. Work Done by Gravitational Force on Orbiting Satellites

At every point along a circular orbit:

  • Gravitational force F⃗g\vec{F}_gFg​ acts radially inward towards Earth's centre.
  • Instantaneous displacement ds⃗d\vec{s}ds is tangential to the circular orbit.
  • Angle θ=90∘\theta = 90^\circθ=90∘ at all times   ⟹  W=∫Fg⋅ds⋅cos⁡90∘=0\implies W = \int F_g \cdot ds \cdot \cos 90^\circ = \mathbf{0}⟹W=∫Fg​⋅ds⋅cos90∘=0.
  • Consequence: Earth's gravity does zero work on the Moon; the Moon circles forever without losing mechanical energy!

Mechanical Energy Forms: Kinetic Energy (½mv²) & Potential Energy (mgh)

The capacity of a physical system to perform work, categorized into energy of motion (kinetic energy) and energy of configuration/position (potential energy).

Intuition & Context

Energy: The Universal Currency of Work

If Work is the act of buying something, Energy is the cash sitting in your bank account. Energy is simply the stored capacity to do work. A stationary boulder on the ground has zero kinetic energy, but if a crane lifts it to the 10th floor of a building, the crane deposited gravitational potential energy into the boulder. If dropped, that stored energy transforms into lethal kinetic motion.

Law of Conservation of Mechanical Energy & Conservative Forces

The universal principle stating that total mechanical energy remains constant in an isolated conservative system, exchanging between kinetic and potential energy.

Intuition & Context

The Rollercoaster Energy Seesaw

When a rollercoaster car is slowly cranked up to the highest peak, the motor packs it full of Potential Energy (mghmghmgh). As it plunges down the steep drop, height disappears while roaring speed appears—potential energy converts smoothly into Kinetic Energy (12mv2\frac{1}{2}mv^221​mv2). At the bottom, kinetic energy is at its absolute maximum. As it climbs the next loop, speed bleeds away as height is regained. Energy is never destroyed; it merely changes costumes.

Core Principle

Conservation of Mechanical Energy in Free Fall

Stepwise Proof of Energy Conservation in Free Fall (Mass mmm, Height HHH):

E_p = mgH
E_k = 0
Total E_A = mgH + 0 = mgH

E_p = 0

Work-Energy Theorem, Power (P=W/t), Horsepower & Commercial Energy Accounting

The equivalence of net work done to kinetic energy change, time rate of energy expenditure (power), horsepower ratings, and electrical kilowatt-hour accounting.

Intuition & Context

The Difference Between Energy and Power

Imagine moving 1,000 bricks to the roof of a house. Both a human laborer and a motorized crane perform the EXACT SAME WORK (mghmghmgh) and consume the exact same amount of energy. But the laborer takes 8 hours, while the crane takes 2 minutes. The crane has vastly higher Power (P=W/tP = W/tP=W/t)—it delivers that energy at a blisteringly fast rate.
