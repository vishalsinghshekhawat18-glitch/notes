12:T48f,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: Mining Royalties Classification**: Mining Royalties are **State's Own NON-TAX Revenue (SONTR)**, NOT tax revenue!
- **Trap 2: Major Mineral Royalty Power**: Under the MMDR Act, 1957, the **Central Government fixes royalty rates** for Major Minerals, but the **State Government collects and retains 100% of the royalty revenue**.
- **Trap 3: Rajasthan's 15th FC Share**: Rajasthan receives **~6.026%** of the 41% Divisible Pool under the 15th Finance Commission formula (benefiting from its large geographic area of 10.4% of India).
- **Trap 4: Alcohol Taxation**: Alcohol for human consumption is taxed exclusively by the State via **State Excise Duty** (Entry 51 List II) and State VAT; it is strictly excluded from GST (Article 366(12A)).
