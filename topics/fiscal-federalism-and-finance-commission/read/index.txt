```15:T402,How central funds flow to the States:

### 1. Central Sector Schemes (100% Central Funding)
- Directly funded and executed by the Union Government on Union List subjects.
- **Examples**: PM-KISAN (₹6,000/yr direct income support), Central Sector Crop Insurance, Space research, Urea fertilizer subsidies.

### 2. Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS - Cost-Shared)
- Implemented by State Governments on State and Concurrent List subjects, with joint funding:
  - **General States**: 60% Centre : 40% State
  - **NE & Himalayan States (Uttarakhand, HP, J&K, NE)**: 90% Centre : 10% State
  - **UTs without legislature**: 100% Centre
- **The 3-Tier CSS Classification (NITI Aayog 2015)**:
  1. **Core of the Core Schemes** (Mandatory, first charge on funds: MGNREGA, National Social Assistance Programme / NSAP).
  2. **Core Schemes** (Major national missions: Jal Jeevan Mission, PM Awas Yojana, Samagra Shiksha, Ayushman Bharat - PMJAY).
  3. **Optional Schemes** (States can choose to implement or substitute with own schemes).16:T467,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: Cesses and Surcharges Exclusion**: Under Article 271, Cesses (e.g., Health & Education Cess, Road & Infrastructure Cess) and Surcharges are **NOT shared with States**; they are retained 100% by the Centre. (A major source of Centre-State friction!).
- **Trap 2: CSS Funding Ratios**: Normal CSS funding ratio is **60:40** for general states and **90:10** for North-Eastern and Himalayan States.
- **Trap 3: Central Sector vs Centrally Sponsored**: Central Sector Schemes are **100% funded by Centre** (e.g. PM-KISAN). Centrally Sponsored Schemes are **cost-shared** with states (e.g. Samagra Shiksha, PMAY).
- **Trap 4: Article 270 vs Article 271**: Article 270 creates the Divisible Pool; Article 271 allows the Centre to levy surcharges outside the divisible pool.
```

- **Trap 1: 42% vs 41% Devolution**: The 14th Finance Commission recommended **42%**. The 15th Finance Commission reduced it to **41%** (a 1% adjustment to provide funds for the newly formed Union Territories of **J&K and Ladakh** from the Centre's share).
