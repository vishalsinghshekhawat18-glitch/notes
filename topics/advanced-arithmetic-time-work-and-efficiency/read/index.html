Advanced Time & Work: Multi-Agent Schedules, Dynamic Efficiency Shifts & Work-Wages

Advanced work-rate modeling: staggered joining/leaving intervals, percentage efficiency degradation and boost factors, multi-agent rotating alternate day cycles, fractional work packets, and wage distribution proportional to completed work units.

Core Principle

Multi-Agent Dynamic Work Modeling

Advanced Time & Work in Banking PO Mains moves beyond simple pairwise addition into Dynamically Shifted Work Systems.

Key problem topologies include:

  1. Dynamic Efficiency Modulation: Workers operating at fractional capacity (Enew=E0×(1±k%)E_{\text{new}} = E_0 \times (1 \pm k%)Enew​=E0​×(1±k%)) due to fatigue, technological assistance, or partial days.
  2. Rotating Multi-Agent Alternate Schedules: 3 or more agents (A,B,CA, B, CA,B,C) rotating cyclically. The work completed in a base cycle of NNN days is Wcycle=∑EiW_{\text{cycle}} = \sum E_iWcycle​=∑Ei​.
  3. Proportional Wage Allocation Law: Wages are distributed strictly according to the Total Work Units Contributed (Ei×TiE_i \times T_iEi​×Ti​), completely independent of nominal days present on site.

Advanced Pipes & Cisterns: Variable Draining Leaks & Partial Capacity Cycling

Complex fluid dynamics for competitive examinations: multiple inlet/outlet networks, variable leak discharge rates, partial capacity filling/emptying sequences, and non-overflow alternate hour threshold calculations.

Core Principle

Fluid Dynamics & Non-Linear Leak Models

Advanced Pipes & Cisterns problems in Banking examinations test Directional Flow Balance and Cycle Threshold Constraints.

The most critical distinction from standard Time & Work:

  • Inlet Pipes (+E+E+E): Add fluid volume.
  • Outlet Pipes / Leaks (−E-E−E): Subtract fluid volume.
  • The Non-Overflow Principle: When an inlet and a leak operate on alternate time intervals, the tank fills completely on an inlet interval BEFORE the leak can operate, meaning cycle iterations must terminate at (Capacity−Inflow)(\text{Capacity} - \text{Inflow})(Capacity−Inflow).

Formulas & The Alternate Hour Threshold Protocol

1. The Alternate Hour Filling & Emptying Invariant

Let Inlet Pipe AAA fill a tank in t1t_1t1​ hours (+EA+E_A+EA​) and Leak BBB empty it in t2t_2t2​ hours (−EB-E_B−EB​), with EA>EBE_A > E_BEA​>EB​:

  2. Net Hourly Cycle: In 1 cycle of 2 hours2\text{ hours}2 hours, net fluid added =EA−EB= E_A - E_B=EA​−EB​.
  3. Threshold Check: Calculate cycle iterations ONLY UP TO:
    Vthreshold=V−EAV_{\text{threshold}} = V - E_AVthreshold​=V−EA​
  4. Number of Complete Cycles:
    k=⌈V−EAEA−EB⌉k = \left\lceil \frac{V - E_A}{E_A - E_B} \right\rceilk=⌈EA​−EB​V−EA​​⌉
  5. Volume at End of kkk Cycles: Vk=k×(EA−EB)V_k = k \times (E_A - E_B)Vk​=k×(EA​−EB​).

2. Variable Height-Dependent Discharge

If a leak is situated at height h=13Hh = \frac{1}{3}Hh=31​H from the bottom:
