11:T80b,### The 3 Stages of Renal Urine Formation

```
             GLOMERULAR CAPILLARY CLUSTER (High Blood Pressure)
                                   │
                                   ▼  [ 1. ULTRAFILTRATION ]
                      BOWMAN'S CUP-SHAPED CAPSULE
                  (Initial Filtrate: 180 L/day containing Glucose, Urea, Salts)
                                   │
                                   ▼  [ 2. SELECTIVE REABSORPTION (PCT & Loop of Henle) ]
                     PROXIMAL CONVOLUTED TUBULE (PCT)
             (100% Glucose & Amino Acids + 85% Water reabsorbed into blood)
                                   │
                                   ▼  [ 3. TUBULAR SECRETION (DCT) ]
                     DISTAL CONVOLUTED TUBULE (DCT)
                      (Extra H⁺, K⁺ ions & drugs secreted)
                                   │
                                   ▼
                       COLLECTING DUCT ──► URETER
                     (Final Urine: 1.5 L/day containing Urea + Urochrome)
````
