| Stage & Style | Regnal Epoch & Key Kings | Architectural Innovations | Classic Monumental Examples |

### 2. The Pancha Rathas of Mamallapuram
Carved out of whale-backed granite outcrops on the Coromandel coast, the five rathas demonstrate diverse roof prototypes:
1. **Dharmaraja Ratha**: Largest ratha; square plan with a three-storeyed pyramidal Vimana with octagonal Shikhara (direct prototype of the Dravidian Vimana).
2. **Bhima Ratha**: Oblong wagon-vaulted roof (*Shala* roof), imitating barrel-vaulted timber halls.
3. **Arjuna Ratha**: Square plan, two-tiered Vimana; shares plinth with Draupadi ratha.
4. **Draupadi Ratha**: Smallest ratha; square hut with curved Bengali-style thatched roof dedicated to Goddess Durga.
5. **Nakula-Sahadeva Ratha**: Apsidal / horseshoe-ended roof (*Gajaprishtha* - elephant-back shape).13:Tfdf,The Medieval Chola dynasty, revived by **Vijayalaya** in 850 CE when he captured Thanjavur from the Muttaraiyars, grew into the greatest maritime empire in Indian history.

### 2. The Uttaramerur Inscriptions: Kudavolai Democratic System
Engraved on the walls of the Vaikuntha Perumal temple at Uttaramerur (Tamil Nadu), these epigraphs provide an unrivaled blueprint of grassroots local governance:
* **Village Structure**: The village was divided into **30 wards (*Kudumbus*)**.
* **The Kudavolai Method**: Eligible candidates' names were inscribed on palm-leaf tickets (*olai*), placed inside an earthen pot (*kuda*), and drawn in full public assembly by a young, innocent boy.
* **Strict Eligibility Criteria**:
  - Must own at least $\frac{1}{4}$ *veli* (approx. 1.5 acres) of taxable land.
  - Must reside on own house built on own site.
  - Age between **35 and 70 years**.
  - Must know the Vedic mantras and Brahmanas.
* **Strict Disqualifications**:
  - Anyone who failed to submit committee accounts (*Kanakku*) during the previous 3 years.
  - Anyone guilty of the *Panchamapatakas* (five great sins: murder, theft, adultery, alcoholism, association with sinners).
  - Close relatives of disqualified members.
* **Specialized Executive Committees (*Variyams*)**:
  1. **Samvatsara-variyam**: Annual executive oversight committee.
  2. **Eri-variyam**: Tank and irrigation committee (vital for water security).
  3. **Totta-variyam**: Garden, orchard, and public commons committee.
  4. **Pancha-vara-variyam**: Standing committee / taxation assessment.
  5. **Pon-variyam**: Gold standardization and currency committee.
  6. **Nyayattar**: Judicial and disputes resolution committee.

### 3. The Lost-Wax Bronze Nataraja
The Cholas brought the art of bronze metal casting to world perfection using the **lost-wax (*cire-perdue*) technique**:
* **Cosmic Dance (*Ananda Tandava*)**: Depicts Shiva as the Lord of Dance inside a circular ring of flames (*Prabhamandala* / fire of cosmic creation and destruction).
* **Iconographic Symbolism**:
  - *Upper Right Hand*: Holds the hourglass drum (*Damaru*) -> Sound of Creation.
  - *Upper Left Hand*: Holds the flame (*Agni*) -> Fire of Cosmic Destruction.
  - *Lower Right Hand*: Raised in *Abhaya Mudra* -> Protection and assurance of refuge.
  - *Lower Left Hand*: Points downward to raised left foot -> Salvation and spiritual liberation.

   - **Ur**: The general assembly of common, non-Brahmin tax-paying agricultural villages (*Vellanvagai*).
