12:T426,### Formula for Multiple Images (Angle $\theta$ between two mirrors):
When an object is placed between two plane mirrors inclined at angle $\theta$:
1. Calculate the ratio: $n_0 = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta}$
2. **If $n_0$ is EVEN**: Number of images formed is **$N = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta} - 1$** (for all object positions).
   - E.g. Mirrors at $90^\circ$: $N = \frac{360}{90} - 1 = 4 - 1 = \mathbf{3 \text{ images}}$.
   - E.g. Mirrors at $60^\circ$: $N = \frac{360}{60} - 1 = 6 - 1 = \mathbf{5 \text{ images}}$.
3. **If $n_0$ is ODD**:
   - Placed symmetrically on angle bisector: $N = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta} - 1$.
   - Placed asymmetrically: $N = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta}$.
4. **Parallel Mirrors ($	heta = 0^\circ$)**:
   - $N = \frac{360}{0} - 1 = \mathbf{\infty \text{ (Infinite images)}}$, as seen in barber shops and trial rooms.

### Minimum Mirror Height to See Full Body:
To see one's full height $H$, the plane mirror must have a minimum vertical length of:
$$L_{\text{min}} = \frac{H}{2}$$
*(Independent of the person's distance from the mirror).*13:T5d0,### Concave Mirror: All 6 Object Positions & Image Characteristics ($f = R/2$)

| Object Position | Image Position | Image Nature | Image Size / Magnification | Everyday Technological Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **At Infinity** | At Focus ($F$) | Real & Inverted | Point-sized / Highly diminished ($m \ll -1$) | Solar furnace heat concentrator. |
| **Beyond $C$** | Between $F$ and $C$ | Real & Inverted | Diminished ($|m| < 1$) | Optical sensors. |
| **At Centre of Curvature ($C$)** | **At $C$** | **Real & Inverted** | **Same size as object ($m = -1$)** | Standard 1:1 image projection. |
| **Between $C$ and $F$** | Beyond $C$ | Real & Inverted | Enlarged ($|m| > 1$) | Cinema projectors. |
| **At Focus ($F$)** | At Infinity | Real & Inverted | Infinitely large ($m \to -\infty$) | **Searchlights, torches, car headlights** (bulb at focus produces parallel beam). |
| **Between Pole ($P$) and Focus ($F$)** | **Behind Mirror** | **Virtual & Erect** | **Enlarged / Magnified ($m > +1$)** | **Dentist mirror, Shaving mirror, Makeup mirror**. |

### Convex Mirror: Universal Image Behavior

| Object Position | Image Position | Image Nature | Image Size | Practical Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Anywhere in front of mirror** (Infinity to Pole) | Between $P$ and $F$ (behind mirror) | **Virtual & Erect** | **Always Diminished ($0 < m < +1$)** | **Vehicle Rear-View Mirrors**, Blind corner road mirrors, ATM surveillance mirrors. |14:T478,### Worked Example:
A concave mirror has a focal length of $15 \, \text{cm}$. An object of height $4 \, \text{cm}$ is placed $30 \, \text{cm}$ in front of the mirror. Find the position, nature, and height of the image.

1. **Assign Cartesian Signs**:
   - $f = -15 \, \text{cm}$ (Concave mirror).
   - $u = -30 \, \text{cm}$ (Object in front).
   - $h = +4 \, \text{cm}$ (Erect object).
2. **Apply Mirror Formula**:
   $$\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u} \implies \frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{u}$$
   $$\frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{-15} - \frac{1}{-30} = -\frac{1}{15} + \frac{1}{30} = \frac{-2 + 1}{30} = -\frac{1}{30}$$
   $$\mathbf{v = -30 \, \text{cm}}$$
3. **Calculate Magnification & Image Height**:
   $$m = -\frac{v}{u} = -\frac{-30}{-30} = \mathbf{-1.0}$$
   $$h' = m \times h = (-1.0) \times 4 = \mathbf{-4 \, \text{cm}}$$
4. **Conclusion**:
   - Image is formed **$30 \, \text{cm}$ in front of the mirror** ($v < 0$).
   - Nature is **Real and Inverted** ($m < 0$).
   - Size is **$4 \, \text{cm}$ high (Same size as object)** because the object was positioned exactly at the Centre of Curvature ($C = 2f = 30 \, \text{cm}$).15:T47c,### The Two Mandatory Conditions for Total Internal Reflection (TIR):
1. **Direction Rule**: Light must travel from **Denser Medium $\to$ Rarer Medium** (e.g. Glass $\to$ Air, Water $\to$ Air, Core $\to$ Cladding). *(TIR is IMPOSSIBLE from air to water!)*
2. **Angle Rule**: Angle of incidence must exceed the **Critical Angle**: $\mathbf{i > \theta_c}$.

### The Critical Angle Formula:
$$\mathbf{\sin\theta_c = \frac{1}{n} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad n = \frac{1}{\sin\theta_c}}$$

### Critical Angles of Key Materials (relative to air):

| Material | Refractive Index ($n$) | Critical Angle ($\theta_c$) | Optical Consequence |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |

| **Flint Glass** | $1.65$ | $37.3^\circ$ | High dispersion and high TIR reflection. |

| Feature | Spherical Lenses (Refraction) | Spherical Mirrors (Reflection) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Formula** | $\mathbf{\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u}}$ | $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$ |
| **Magnification ($m$)** | $\mathbf{m = +\frac{v}{u}}$ | $m = -\frac{v}{u}$ |
| **Convex Element** | **Converging ($f > 0, P > 0$)** | Diverging ($f > 0, P = 0$) |
| **Concave Element** | **Diverging ($f < 0, P < 0$)** | Converging ($f < 0, P = 0$) |

### Power of a Lens Formula:
$$\mathbf{P = \frac{1}{f \text{ (in metres)}}} \quad \left[\text{SI Unit: Dioptre (D)} = \text{m}^{-1}\right]$$
- If focal length $f$ is given in centimetres: $P = \frac{100}{f \text{ (in cm)}}$.
- **Convex Lens of $f = +50 \, \text{cm} = +0.5 \, \text{m}$**: $P = \frac{1}{+0.5} = \mathbf{+2.0 \, \text{D}}$.
- **Concave Lens of $f = -20 \, \text{cm} = -0.2 \, \text{m}$**: $P = \frac{1}{-0.2} = \mathbf{-5.0 \, \text{D}}$.
- **Combination of Lenses in Contact**: $P_{\text{total}} = P_1 + P_2 = (+2.0) + (-5.0) = \mathbf{-3.0 \, \text{D}}$.17:T40d,### 1. Convex (Converging) Lens Image Behavior
