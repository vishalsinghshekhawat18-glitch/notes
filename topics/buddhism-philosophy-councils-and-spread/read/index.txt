16:T644,Siddhartha Gautama was born c. 563 BCE in the garden grove of **Lumbini** (modern southern Nepal) to Chief Shuddhodana of the Shakya clan of Kapilavastu and Queen Maya (who died seven days after childbirth; Siddhartha was raised by foster-mother Mahaprajapati Gautami). At age 29, deeply disturbed by the 'Four Sights' (an Old Man, a Sick Man, a Corpse, and an Ascetic), he renounced his luxurious princely life (**The Great Renunciation / Mahabhinishkramana**).

### The Five Great Life Events & Their Canonical Symbols
| Life Event | Pali/Sanskrit Nomenclature | Canonical Symbol | Sacred Geographical Location |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Birth** | *Janma / Jati* | **Lotus & Bull** | Lumbini (Rummindei, Nepal) |
| **Great Renunciation** | *Mahabhinishkramana* | **Horse** (*Kanthaka*) | Kapilavastu (Piprahwa) |
| **Enlightenment** | *Sambodhi / Bodhi* | **Bodhi Tree** (*Pipal / Ficus religiosa*) | Bodh Gaya (Uruvela, on River Niranjana/Phalgu) |
| **First Sermon** | *Dhammacakkappavattana* | **Dharmachakra (Wheel of Law)** | Sarnath (Isipatana / Mrigadava near Varanasi) |
| **Final Passing** | *Mahaparinirvana* | **Stupa / Footprints** | Kushinagar (Kusinara, Malla Republic, UP) |

### The Eight Great Holy Places (*Ashtamahapratiharya*)
1. **Four Primary Sites:** Lumbini (Birth), Bodh Gaya (Bodhi), Sarnath (First Sermon), Kushinagar (Mahaparinirvana).
2. **Four Secondary Miracle Sites:** Sravasti (Twin Miracle / *Yamaka-patihariya*), Sankasya (Descent from Trayastrimsa Heaven), Rajagriha (Taming of wild elephant Nalagiri), Vaishali (Offering of honey by the monkey collective).17:T46e,### 1. Iron Metallurgy, Agricultural Surplus & The Middle Gangetic Urbanisation
The expansion of wet-rice transplantation (*Ropa*) and deep ploughing with iron ploughshares generated substantial agricultural surplus. This supported the **Second Urbanisation** (emergence of cities like Rajagriha, Kausambi, Sravasti, Varanasi, Champa).

### 2. The Rise of the Gahapatis & Merchant Discontent
- The Vedic orthodoxy branded maritime trade and usury (*Kusida*) as polluting and degraded.
- Buddhism, in contrast, praised frugal accumulation of wealth through ethical trade and endorsed investment (*Sigalovada Sutta*).
- Wealthy merchants (*Setthis*, *Gahapatis* such as Anathapindika and Mendaka) and powerful royal monarchs (Bimbisara, Ajatashatru, Prasenajit) lavishly endowed Buddhist monastic dwellings (*Viharas*).

### 3. Democratic Monastic Governance (*Sangha*)
The Buddhist Sangha was structured along the democratic lines of the oligarchic *Gana-Sanghas* (such as the Vrijis/Lichchhavis), featuring open deliberation, consensus voting through wooden ballot sticks (*Salaka*), and periodic confessional assemblies (*Uposatha*).18:T857,### The Three Marks of Existence (*Trilakkhana*)
- **Anicca (Impermanence):** All compounded phenomena (*Sankharas*) are in perpetual flux (*Kshanikavada*).
- **Dukkha (Unsatisfactoriness/Suffering):** Because things are impermanent, clinging to them inevitably produces pain.
- **Anatta (Non-Self):** There is no unchanging, immortal soul (*Atman*); what we call 'I' is merely a bundle of Five Aggregates (**Pancha Skandha**: *Rupa* [Form], *Vedana* [Sensation], *Sanna* [Perception], *Sankhara* [Mental Volitions], and *Vinnana* [Consciousness]).

### The Noble Eightfold Path (*Arya Ashtangika Marga*)
Grouped canonically into the **Threefold Training (*Tri-Sikkha*)**:
| Division (*Sikkha*) | Eightfold Path Component | Practical Meaning & Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prajna (Wisdom)** | 1. **Samyak Drishti** (Right View)<br>2. **Samyak Sankalpa** (Right Resolve) | Understanding Four Noble Truths; intention of renunciation, loving-kindness, and non-cruelty. |
| **Shila (Ethical Conduct)** | 3. **Samyak Vacha** (Right Speech)<br>4. **Samyak Karmanta** (Right Action)<br>5. **Samyak Ajiva** (Right Livelihood) | Refraining from falsehood/slander; non-violence/non-stealing; ethical livelihood (no weapon/slave/meat/poison trade). |
| **Samadhi (Mental Discipline)** | 6. **Samyak Vyayama** (Right Effort)<br>7. **Samyak Smriti** (Right Mindfulness)<br>8. **Samyak Samadhi** (Right Concentration) | Cultivating wholesome mental states; four foundations of mindfulness (*Satipatthana*); meditative absorptions (*Jhanas*). |

### The 12-Linked Chain of Dependent Origination (*Dvadasa Nidana*)
1. *Avidya* (Ignorance) $\rightarrow$ 2. *Sankhara* (Mental Formations) $\rightarrow$ 3. *Vijnana* (Consciousness) $\rightarrow$ 4. *Nama-Rupa* (Mind and Body) $\rightarrow$ 5. *Shadayatana* (Six Sense Bases) $\rightarrow$ 6. *Sparsha* (Contact) $\rightarrow$ 7. *Vedana* (Feeling/Sensation) $\rightarrow$ 8. *Tanha* (Thirst/Craving) $\rightarrow$ 9. *Upadana* (Clinging/Attachment) $\rightarrow$ 10. *Bhava* (Becoming/Process of Existence) $\rightarrow$ 11. *Jati* (Birth) $\rightarrow$ 12. *Jaramarana* (Old Age, Decay & Death).19:T7c9,### Matrix of the Four Great Buddhist Councils

- **Vinaya Pitaka (Discipline):** Suttavibhanga (*Patimokkha* rules for monks and nuns), Khandhaka (*Mahavagga* & *Cullavagga*), Parivara.
