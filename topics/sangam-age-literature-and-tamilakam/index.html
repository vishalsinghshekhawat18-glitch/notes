Sangam Age Literature, The Three Crowned Kings (Muventar) & The Fivefold Eco-Zones (Aintinai)

The classical Sangam era of Tamilakam (c. 3rd C. BCE - 3rd C. CE), structured by three literary assemblies at Madurai, the canonical anthologies (Ettuttokai, Pattuppattu, Tolkappiyam), the Muventar polities (Cheras, Cholas, Pandyas), and the fivefold ecological landscape theory (Aintinai).
