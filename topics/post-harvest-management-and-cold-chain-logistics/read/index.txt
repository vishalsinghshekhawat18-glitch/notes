12:T5ca,### The Integrated Cold Chain Protocol

| Stage in Value Chain | Infrastructure Unit | Operational Process / Technology | Primary Preservation Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Farm-Gate Aggregation** | **Pre-Cooling Unit** | Rapid forced-air cooling within 2–4 hours of harvest | Extracts latent 'field heat'; slows enzymatic respiration rate by 80% |
| **2. Post-Harvest Cleaning** | **Integrated Pack-House** | Mechanized washing, sorting, grading, and corrugation packing | Standardizes size/weight; eliminates diseased fruits; prevents mechanical bruising |
| **3. Temperature-Controlled Storage** | **Controlled Atmosphere (CA) Store** | Oxygen ($O_2$) lowered to 1-2%, $CO_2$ modulated, temperature kept at 0-4°C | Puts fruits (e.g. apples, kinnow) into biological 'dormancy'; extends shelf-life from weeks to 9 months |
| **4. Cold Transit** | **Reefer Vans / Containers** | Refrigerated trucks and rail containers with GPS temperature telemetry | Maintains unbroken cold chain from pack-house to wholesale mandi or export port |

### Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) Key Financial Terms
- **Total Fund Size:** **₹1,00,000 Crore (₹1 Lakh Crore)**.
- **Scheme Tenure:** FY2020 to FY2032-33 (Disbursement up to FY2025-26).
- **Interest Subvention:** **3.0% per annum** for loans up to **₹2,00,000,000 (₹2 Crore)**.
- **Subvention Period:** Maximum **7 Years**.
- **Credit Guarantee:** Free CGTMSE coverage for loans up to ₹2 Crore (fee paid by Government).13:T463,### Frequent Examiner Traps
1. **AIF Financial Parameters:**
   - Fund corpus: **₹1 Lakh Crore** (not ₹10,000 Cr).
   - Interest subvention: **3% per annum** (not 1.5% or 4%).
   - Loan limit eligible for subvention: **Up to ₹2 Crore**.
   - Maximum subvention tenure: **7 Years**.
2. **Operation Greens Evolution:** Originally launched strictly for **TOP** (Tomato, Onion, Potato); later extended to **TOTAL** (all 22 perishable fruits and vegetables).
3. **Pre-cooling Timing:** Pre-cooling must be executed **within hours of harvest at the farm gate**, NOT after produce arrives at destination wholesale urban markets.
4. **Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY):** Implemented by MoFPI for Mega Food Parks, Agro-Processing Clusters, and Integrated Cold Chains. (Never confuse PM Kisan SAMPADA Yojana under Food Processing with PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana under Jal Shakti/Agri!).
