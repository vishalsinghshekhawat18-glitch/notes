12:T75e,### Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Financial Mechanics

| Parameter | Statutory Rule / Specification | Analytical Rationale / Governing Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| **Founding Genesis** | Formulated in **August 1998** by RBI & NABARD | Based on the **R.V. Gupta Committee** recommendations |
| **Card Validity Period** | **5 Years** | Revolving cash credit account; no need to apply afresh each crop season |
| **Limit Fixation Formula** | Scale of Finance (SOF) × Cropped Area + 10% Post-harvest + 20% Asset Maintenance | First year limit ($L_1$); scales up by **10% compound per annum** for 2nd to 5th years ($L_5 = L_1 \times 1.46$) |
| **Loan Ceiling for MISS** | Up to **₹3,00,000 (₹3 Lakh)** | Extended up to **₹2,00,000** for Animal Husbandry and Fisheries (within the overall ₹3 Lakh ceiling) |
| **Collateral-Free Limit** | Up to **₹1,60,000 (₹1.6 Lakh)** | Waived up to ₹3,00,000 for loans with tie-up arrangements |
| **Benchmark Interest Rate** | **7.0% per annum** | Standard commercial rate subsidized by Government |
| **Prompt Repayment Incentive** | **3.0% per annum** | Given to farmers who repay within 1 year of disbursement |
| **Effective Net Interest Rate** | **4.0% per annum** ($7\% - 3\% = 4\%$) | The cheapest institutional credit in the Indian economy |

### Agricultural Landholding Sizing Classes (Agriculture Census of India)
- **Marginal Farmer:** Operational landholding of **less than 1.0 hectare (< 2.47 acres)**. (Accounts for ~68.5% of total holdings).
- **Small Farmer:** Operational landholding of **1.0 to 2.0 hectares (2.47 to 4.94 acres)**. (Accounts for ~17.7% of total holdings).
- **Small & Marginal Farmers (SMF Combined):** Account for **86.2% of all operational farm holdings** in India!
- **Semi-Medium:** 2.0 to 4.0 hectares.
- **Medium:** 4.0 to 10.0 hectares.
- **Large Farmer:** Above 10.0 hectares (barely ~0.6% of holdings).13:T509,### Frequent Examiner Traps
1. **KCC Interest Calculation Trap:**
   - Benchmark rate: **7%**
   - Government subvention to banks: **1.5%**
   - Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) to farmers: **3%**
   - **Effective net interest to farmer: 4%** (Never confuse the 1.5% bank subvention with the 3% farmer discount!).
2. **KCC Validity & Escalation:** Card validity is **5 years** (not 3 years). Annual limit escalation is **10%** (not 5% or 15%).
3. **KCC Animal Husbandry & Fisheries Cap:** KCC limit for allied animal husbandry/fisheries activities is capped at **₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh)** within the overall ₹3 Lakh ceiling for interest subvention.
4. **Small vs Marginal Landholding Boundaries:** Marginal is **< 1 ha**; Small is **1 to 2 ha**. Together, SMFs represent **86.2%** of all farmers in India.
5. **Gupta Committee vs Nair Committee:** KCC was designed by the **R.V. Gupta Committee (1998)**. Priority Sector Lending was restructured by the **M.V. Nair Committee (2012)**.

Rural credit institutional architecture and financial inclusion.14:T41d,Institutional farm credit in India is anchored by the Kisan Credit Card (KCC) and RBI Priority Sector Lending (PSL) mandates. Formulated in 1998 following the R.V. Gupta Committee, KCC provides a revolving credit line valid for 5 years with a 10% annual compound limit escalation based on the Scale of Finance. Under the Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS), short-term crop loans up to ₹3,00,000 are disbursed at a benchmark rate of 7.0% p.a., with a 1.5% interest subvention to banks and a 3.0% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) to farmers, resulting in an effective net borrowing rate of exactly 4.0% p.a. (allied animal husbandry/fisheries loans are capped at ₹2,00,000 within this limit). For commercial banks, RBI mandates that 18% of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC) must be deployed to agriculture, with a ring-fenced 10% sub-target for Small and Marginal Farmers (Marginal <1 ha, Small 1–2 ha, comprising 86.2% of Indian farmers). Shortfalls in PSL achievement are channeled into NABARD's Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF).15:T426,# Architecture of Indian Agricultural Credit & PSL

   - Committee: R.V. Gupta Committee (1998).
   - Validity: 5 Years revolving line.
   - Escalation: 10% annual increase ($L_5 = L_1 \times 1.46$).
   - Limit Ceilings: ₹3,00,000 for crop loans; ₹2,00,000 for animal husbandry/fisheries.
