- **Trap 1: Default Tax Regime**: The New Tax Regime under **Section 115BAC is the DEFAULT regime** in India. If a taxpayer does not actively opt into the Old Regime, the New Regime applies automatically.
- **Trap 2: Section 87A Rebate Threshold**: Under the New Regime, income up to **₹7,00,000 pays ZERO tax** due to the Section 87A full tax rebate.
- **Trap 3: Corporate Tax Rate Under Section 115BAA**: The base rate is **$22\%$**, but with mandatory $10\%$ surcharge and $4\%$ Health & Education cess, the **effective corporate tax rate is $25.17\%$**.
- **Trap 4: Capital Gains Rationalization (2024)**: Listed equity STCG is **$20\%$** (raised from $15\%$); LTCG is **$12.5\%$** (raised from $10\%$) with an exemption limit of **₹1,25,000 per year** (raised from ₹1,00,000).
