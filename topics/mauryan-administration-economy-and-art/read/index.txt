12:Tecd,### The 18 Tirthas (Apex Executive Ministers)
Foremost among the 18 Tirthas (*Mahamatras*):
1. **Mantri / Pradhana:** Prime Minister / Chief Imperial Counsellor.
2. **Purohita:** Chief Royal Priest and moral-religious adviser.
3. **Senapati:** Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces.
4. **Yuvaraja:** Crown Prince and heir-apparent.
5. **Samaharta:** **Collector General of Revenue** (supervises total imperial budget, taxation, assessment, and provincial audits).
6. **Sannidhata:** **Chief Treasurer / Storekeeper General** (custodian of royal treasury /*Kosha*/, granaries, and state stores).
7. **Pradeshtha:** Chief Judge of Criminal Courts (*Kantakashodhana*).
8. **Vyavaharika:** Chief Judge of Civil Courts (*Dharmasthiya*).
9. **Dandapala:** Head of Police and Law Enforcement.
10. **Durgapala:** Commander of Internal Fortresses.
11. **Antapala:** Warden of Frontier / Border Fortresses.
12. **Akshapataladhyaksha:** Accountant General and Keeper of Royal Records.

### Key Adhyakshas (Departmental Directors / Superintendents)
| Departmental Director | Sanskrit Nomenclature | Administrative Portfolio & Regulatory Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agriculture / Crown Lands** | **Sitadhyaksha** | Direct cultivation of state crown lands (*Sita*), distribution of seeds, farm laborers, irrigation. |
| **Weights & Measures** | **Pautavadhyaksha** | Standardization of balances, weights (*Pautava*), and linear measures; stamping commercial weights. |
| **Tolls & Customs** | **Shulkadhyaksha** | Collection of custom duties (*Shulka*) at city gates on imported and exported commodities. |
| **Mines & Metallurgy** | **Akaradhyaksha** | State exploration of mineral mines, smelting, and extraction of gold, silver, copper, and iron. |
| **Coinage & Mint** | **Lakshanadhyaksha** | Minting of official punch-marked silver and copper coins (*Rupadarshaka* inspected authenticity). |
| **Commerce & Markets** | **Panyadhyaksha** | Regulating supply, pricing, distribution of commodities, and preventing black-marketing. |
| **Navigation & Shipping** | **Navadhyaksha** | Overseeing ocean vessels, river transport, harbor tolls, and river ferries. |
| **Passports & Visas** | **Mudradhyaksha** | Issuing imperial passport seals (*Mudra*) required for domestic travel and foreign entry. |
| **Forests & Wildlife** | **Kupyadhyaksha** | Managing timber resources, forest products, and royal elephant sanctuaries (*Hasti-vana*). |
| **Excise & Liquor** | **Suradhyaksha** | State monopoly over distillation and sale of liquor (*Sura*); regulating drinking houses. |
| **Weaving & Textiles** | **Sutradhyaksha** | State spinning and weaving workshops employing widows, destitute women, and female prisoners. |

### Provincial Administrative Hierarchy
- **1. Central Empire:** Imperial Capital at **Pataliputra**.
- **2. Provinces (*Chakras*):** Governed by royal princes (**Kumaras / Aryaputras**):
  - *Uttarapatha* (Northwestern Province): Capital at **Taxila**.
  - *Avantiratha* (Western Province): Capital at **Ujjain**.
  - *Dakshinapatha* (Southern Province): Capital at **Suvarnagiri** (Kanakagiri, Karnataka).
  - *Prachya / Magadha* (Eastern Heartland): Capital at **Pataliputra**.
  - *Kalinga* (Eastern Province, post-261 BCE): Capital at **Tosali** (with secondary base at Samapa).
- **3. District (*Ahara / Vishaya*):** Headed by **Pradesikas** (executive head) and **Rajukas** (revenue assessment & rural judiciary).
- **4. Intermediate Units:** *Sthaniya* (800 villages) $\rightarrow$ *Dronamukha* (400 villages) $\rightarrow$ *Kharvatika* (200 villages) $\rightarrow$ *Sangrahana* (10 villages).

### 2. The Dual Court System
- **Dharmasthiya Courts (Civil Judiciary):** Handled contracts, inheritance, property boundaries, debts, and domestic disputes presided over by three *Dharmasthas*.
- **Kantakashodhana Courts (Criminal Judiciary / 'Removal of Thorns'):** Handled anti-state conspiracies, banditry, corrupt officials, treason, and market fraud presided over by three *Pradeshthas*.14:Tb6d,### Taxonomy of Mauryan Imperial Taxes & Levies
| Tax Term | Nature & Source of Levy | Assessment Rate & Operational Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bhaga** | **Principal Land Revenue** on private peasant lands. | Standard rate was **1/6th (*Shadbhaga*)** of agricultural produce; could be raised to 1/4th in fertile tracts or reduced to 1/8th (*Athabhagiya* as at Lumbini). |
| **Sita** | Income / produce generated exclusively from **State Crown Lands**. | Managed directly by *Sitadhyaksha* using state seeds, state ploughs, and hired farmhands (*Karmakaras*). |
| **Bali** | Traditional religious cess / voluntary tribute converted into regular state levy. | Levied on fertile villages; completely exempted at Lumbini (*Ubalike kate*) by Ashoka. |
| **Kara** | Periodic tax levied on orchards, gardens, and commercial fruit-bearing trees. | Assessed by *Samaharta* based on yield. |
| **Hiranya** | Tax paid in **cash (gold/silver)** rather than in kind. | Levied on specific cash crops and commercial trades. |
| **Shulka** | Customs duties, tolls, and octroi on commercial goods. | Collected at city entry gates by *Shulkadhyaksha* (ranging from 1/5th to 1/20th of value). |
| **Vishti** | **Compulsory unpaid forced labor (Corvee)**. | Demanded from artisans, weavers, and landless laborers for royal defense works, road construction, and state logistics. |
| **Pranaya** | **Emergency 'Benevolence' / Gift Tax**. | A voluntary-turned-mandatory surcharge (1/3rd to 1/4th of produce) levied **only once** during extreme fiscal or military crises. |
| **Udakabhaga** | **Water Cess / Irrigation Tax**. | Ranged from 1/5th to 1/3rd of produce depending on whether water was lifted manually, by bullocks, or via state water canals. |

### The Monetary System: Punch-Marked Coins (*Ahat Sikke*)
- **Silver Pana:** The standard imperial currency unit (~3.5 grams / 32 rattis), punched with five standard symbols (Sun, Six-armed wheel/Chakra, Hill with crescent, Tree-in-railing, Bull/Elephant).
- **Sub-denominations:** *Ardha-Pana* (1/2), *Pada* (1/4), *Ashta-Bhaga* (1/8).
- **Copper Coinage:** *Mashaka*, *Ardha-Mashaka*, *Kakini* (1/4th Mashaka), and *Ardha-Kakini*.
- **Mint Administration:** *Lakshanadhyaksha* (Superintendent of Mint) minted coins; *Rupadarshaka* (Assayer / Examiner of Coins) verified purity and regulated circulation.

### The Great Trade Highways (*Mahapathas*)
1. **Uttarapatha (The Grand Trunk Road of Antiquity):** Extended from **Taxila / Pushkalavati** in the northwest through Hastinapura, Mathura, Kausambi, Varanasi, Pataliputra, and Champa to the maritime port of **Tamralipti** (in coastal Bengal).
2. **Dakshinapatha (The Southern Trans-Peninsular Highway):** Extended from **Pataliputra and Varanasi** southwards through Vidisha, Ujjain, Mahishmati, Pratishthana (Paithan), and Suvarnagiri, terminating at the western ports of **Bhrigukachcha (Bharuch)** and **Surparaka (Sopara)**.15:Te22,### Court Art vs Popular Art Matrix

### The Sarnath Lion Capital: Anatomy of the National Emblem
1. **Shaft:** Monolithic polished Chunar sandstone column (broken).
2. **Lotus Base:** Bell-shaped inverted lotus (*Padma*).
3. **Abacus (Circular Drum):** Carved with four dynamic animals in continuous clockwise motion:
   - **Lion** (*Simha* - courage/royalty)
   - **Elephant** (*Gaja* - Buddha's conception)
   - **Bull** (*Vrisha* - Taurus / Buddha's zodiac)
   - **Horse** (*Kanthaka* - Buddha's Great Renunciation)
   - Separated by four **24-spoked Dharmachakras**.
4. **Crowing Apex:** Four majestic Asiatic lions seated back-to-back roaring across the four cardinal directions (originally topped by a large crowning Dharmachakra of 32 spokes, fragments in Sarnath Museum).
