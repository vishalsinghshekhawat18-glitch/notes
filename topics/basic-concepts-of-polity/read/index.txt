12:T544,### 1. The Four Modern Elements of Statehood
• **Definite Territory:** A demarcated geographical space over which jurisdiction is exercised.
• **Permanent Population:** An aggregate of individuals residing within the territory.
• **Government:** The political apparatus through which the will of the state is formulated, expressed, and executed.
• **Sovereignty:** The supreme, absolute power free from external control (external sovereignty) and superior to all internal associations (internal sovereignty).

### 2. Kautilya’s Saptanga Theory (Arthashastra)
Kautilya conceptualized the state as a living organism with 7 interdependent organs (*Saptanga*):
1. **Swami (The King / Head):** The apex decision-maker providing vision and moral leadership.
2. **Amatya (Ministers / Eyes):** The administrative apparatus and bureaucracy executing governance.
3. **Janapada / Rashtra (Territory & People / Limbs):** Fertile land populated by industrious subjects.
4. **Durga (Fortified Capital / Arms):** Defensive infrastructure securing the realm.
5. **Kosha (Treasury / Mouth):** Legitimate fiscal resources derived from just taxation.
6. **Danda or Bala (Armed Forces / Brain):** Military might ensuring domestic order and external defense.

Dual Legislature & Dual Executive
• **Union Level:** Parliament (Lok Sabha: 543 elected + Rajya Sabha: 245) & Union Council of Ministers headed by PM.
• **State Level:** State Legislative Assembly (MLA) & Legislative Council (MLC, where bicameral) & State Council of Ministers headed by CM.

### 2. Single Integrated Judiciary
Unlike the USA (which has separate Federal and State court hierarchies), India has a **unified, pyramid judiciary** enforcing both central and state laws:
$$\text{Supreme Court} \longrightarrow \text{High Courts} \longrightarrow \text{District \& Subordinate Courts}$$

### 3. Parliamentary Sovereignty (UK) vs Constitutional Supremacy (India)
• **UK Model:** Parliament is sovereign; no written constitution can limit statutory acts (*De Lolme: Parliament can do anything except make a man into a woman*).
