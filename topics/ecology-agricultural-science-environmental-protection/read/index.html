Intuition & Context

Why Eagles at the Top of the Food Chain Die from Farm Spray at the Bottom

If a farmer sprays a tiny, harmless dose of DDT pesticide (0.000003 ppm0.000003 , \text{ppm}0.000003ppm) on a crop field, rain washes it into a lake. Microscopic plankton eat it, small minnows eat 1,000 plankton, big fish eat 100 minnows, and an apex fish-eagle eats 10 big fish. Because DDT is fat-soluble and indestructible, it never leaves the body—it multiplies millions of times up the ladder until the eagle's body contains a lethal concentration of 25 ppm25 , \text{ppm}25ppm (10,000,000-fold increase10,000,000\text{-fold increase}10,000,000-fold increase) (Biological Magnification)!

Exam Application & Traps

Examiner Traps: 10% Energy Calculation & Montreal Protocol Year

Top 4 Environmental Science Traps:

  1. The 10% Energy Numerical Problem:
    • Question: "If 20,000 J of energy is available at the Producer grass level, how much energy is transferred to the secondary consumer (Fox)?"
    • Step 1: Grass (20,000 J20,000 , \text{J}20,000J) →\to→ Deer (Primary consumer) =10% of 20,000=2,000 J= 10% \text{ of } 20,000 = \mathbf{2,000 , \text{J}}=10% of 20,000=2,000J.
    • Step 2: Deer (2,000 J2,000 , \text{J}2,000J) →\to→ Fox (Secondary consumer) =10% of 2,000=200 J= 10% \text{ of } 2,000 = \mathbf{200 , \text{J}}=10% of 2,000=200J.
    • Trap: Candidates often apply the 10% rule only once instead of tracking every trophic step!
  2. Biological Magnification Maximum Site:
    • Question: "In an ecosystem contaminated with DDT or heavy metals, which organism will accumulate the highest concentration?"
    • Answer: THE APEX PREDATOR / TOP CONSUMER (e.g. Eagle, Tiger, or Human).
  3. The Montreal Protocol (1987):
    • Historic international treaty signed in 1987 to freeze and phase out the production of Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) to protect the Stratospheric Ozone Layer.
  4. Endoscope Optical Principle: Endoscopy operates exclusively via Total Internal Reflection (TIR) through flexible glass optical fibers.
