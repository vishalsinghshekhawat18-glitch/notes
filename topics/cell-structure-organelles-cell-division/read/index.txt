1. **Robert Hooke vs Leeuwenhoek**:
   - **Robert Hooke (1665)**: Observed **DEAD cells** (empty cellulose cell walls of dead cork).
   - **Leeuwenhoek (1674)**: First to observe **LIVING cells** in pond water.
2. **The "Omnis Cellula-e Cellula" Attribution**:
   - Question: *"Who proposed that new cells arise from pre-existing cells?"*
   - Answer: **RUDOLF VIRCHOW (1855)**.
3. **The Master Exception to Cell Theory**:
   - **VIRUSES are the universal exception to the Cell Theory!**
   - Viruses lack cellular organization (they are acellular packages of DNA/RNA wrapped in protein capsids), showing zero metabolic life signs outside a living host cell.

   - **Smallest Free-Living Cell**: *Mycoplasma gallisepticum* / PPLO ($\approx 0.1 - 0.3 \, \mu\text{m}$).
