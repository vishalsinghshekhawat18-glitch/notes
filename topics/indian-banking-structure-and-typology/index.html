The Commercial Banking Landscape: Scheduled Banks (Second Schedule), PSBs, Private Banks & Foreign Bank Modes

The statutory classification and structural hierarchy of commercial banks in India under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 and Banking Regulation Act, 1949. Covers Scheduled Commercial Banks (Second Schedule inclusion criteria: paid-up capital & reserves >= ₹5 Lakh and non-detrimental affairs), Public Sector Banks (Nationalized Banks and State Bank of India), Private Sector Banks (Old vs New generation post-1993/2001), and Foreign Banks (Branch mode vs Wholly Owned Subsidiary / WOS mode).

Differentiated Banking Architecture: Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), Small Finance Banks (SFBs) & Payments Banks

The economic rationale, regulatory design, and operational mandates of differentiated (niche) banks in India: Regional Rural Banks (RRB Act 1976, 50:15:35 ownership model, 75% PSL mandate), Small Finance Banks (Nachiket Mor Committee 2014, financial inclusion of unserved/underserved, 75% PSL target, 50% loans <= ₹25 Lakh), and Payments Banks (high-volume low-value payments, ₹2 Lakh deposit cap, prohibited from lending/issuing credit cards).

Cooperative Banking Architecture: Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs), The 3-Tier Rural Cooperative Structure & Dual Regulation Reforms

The cooperative credit architecture in India operating on the principle of mutual cooperation and democratic member control ("one member, one vote"). Examines the Urban Cooperative Banking structure (4-tiered regulatory categorization: Tier 1 to Tier 4), the Three-Tier Short-Term Rural Cooperative Credit Structure (Primary Agricultural Credit Societies / PACS at village level, District Central Cooperative Banks / DCCBs at district level, and State Cooperative Banks / StCBs at state apex), and the historical dual regulation conflict resolved by the Banking Regulation (Amendment) Act, 2020.
