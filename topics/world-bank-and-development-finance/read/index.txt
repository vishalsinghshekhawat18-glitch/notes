### The Concept of the "World Bank"
In official United Nations terminology:
- **"World Bank"** refers strictly to **IBRD + IDA** combined.
- **"World Bank Group"** refers to all five institutions: **IBRD + IDA + IFC + MIGA + ICSID**.13:T475,| Feature | International Monetary Fund (IMF) | World Bank Group (IBRD/IDA) | World Trade Organization (WTO) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Headquarters** | Washington D.C., USA | Washington D.C., USA | Geneva, Switzerland |
| **Establishment** | 1944 (Bretton Woods) | 1944 (Bretton Woods) | 1995 (Marrakesh Agreement / GATT 1947) |
| **Core Mandate** | **Macroeconomic & BoP Stability** | **Long-Term Economic Development & Poverty Reduction** | **Multilateral Trade Rules & Dispute Settlement** |
| **Primary Activity** | Emergency liquidity loans to central banks/govts | Long-term project finance, infrastructure, health/education loans | Enforcing trade treaties; adjudicating trade disputes |
| **Lending Nature** | Short-to-medium term (SBA, EFF) | Long-term (IBRD: 15–20 yrs; IDA: 30–40 yrs) | **ZERO lending** (Pure regulatory body) |
| **Governance** | Weighted voting by Quota shares | Weighted voting by Share Capital | **Consensus-based** (One Country = One Vote) |
| **India's Status** | Founding member; Net Creditor | Member of 4 out of 5 institutions (Non-signatory to ICSID) | Founding member (1995 / GATT 1947) |14:T41c,### Critical Examiner Traps
- **Trap 1: India and ICSID**: India is a member of IBRD, IDA, IFC, and MIGA, but is **NOT a member of ICSID**. India opted out because ICSID arbitrations do not allow appeals to domestic sovereign courts and are seen as biased toward Western corporations.
- **Trap 2: World Bank vs World Bank Group**: The term *"World Bank"* legally includes only **IBRD and IDA**. The *"World Bank Group"* includes all five (IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID).
- **Trap 3: IDA "Graduation"**: When a developing nation's per capita GNI crosses the operational cutoff ($> \\sim \\text{US \$1,335}$), it "graduates" from IDA concessional soft loans to market-rate IBRD borrowing. India graduated from IDA in 2014, but received transitional assistance.
