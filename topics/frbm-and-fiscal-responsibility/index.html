The FRBM Act, 2003: Statutory Framework, Mandatory Budget Documents & Escape Clauses

The statutory framework of the Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act, 2003 enacted to institutionalize fiscal discipline, ensure inter-generational equity, and eliminate fiscal deficits. Analyzes the four mandatory fiscal documents laid before Parliament alongside the budget (Macroeconomic Framework Statement, Medium-Term Fiscal Policy Statement, Fiscal Policy Strategy Statement, and Medium-Term Expenditure Framework Statement), historical statutory targets (phasing Gross Fiscal Deficit to 3% of GDP and eliminating Revenue Deficit), and the statutory Escape Clauses (Section 4(2)) governing permissible deficit deviations.

FRBM Evolution & Modern Reforms: N.K. Singh Committee, Debt-to-GDP Anchor & Elimination of Off-Budget Borrowings

The modern evolution of India's fiscal responsibility framework from the 2017 N.K. Singh FRBM Review Committee to post-pandemic fiscal consolidation. Analyzes the structural paradigm shift from a sole annual fiscal deficit target to a dual Debt-to-GDP Anchor (General Government Debt 60% of GDP: 40% for Centre, 20% for States), the post-COVID fiscal glidepath aiming for a Fiscal Deficit below 4.5% of GDP by FY 2025-26, and the landmark transparency reform that eliminated Off-Budget / Extra-Budgetary Resources (EBR) (bringing FCI food subsidy borrowings from the National Small Savings Fund / NSSF directly into the Union Budget).
