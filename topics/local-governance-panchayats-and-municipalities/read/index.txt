12:T58a,The 73rd Amendment establishes a sharp constitutional boundary between mandatory provisions (which every state must enact) and voluntary provisions (left to state legislative discretion):

1. Mandatory Constitutional Provisions:
   • Gram Sabha as the foundational democratic assembly of registered village voters (Art 243A).
   • 3-tier structure: Gram Panchayat (village), Panchayat Samiti (intermediate/block), and Zilla Parishad (district) under Art 243B (states with population below 20 lakhs may skip intermediate tier).
   • Direct elections for all members at all three levels (Art 243C).
   • Indirect elections for chairpersons at intermediate and district levels; chairperson election mode at village level left to state law.
   • Fixed 5-year tenure (Art 243E) with mandatory fresh elections within 6 months of dissolution.
   • Minimum 1/3rd reservation for women across seats and chairpersons (Art 243D).
   • Independent State Election Commission (Art 243K) with removal equivalence to a High Court Judge.
   • State Finance Commission (Art 243I) constituted every 5 years to review PRI finances.

2. Voluntary Discretionary Provisions:
   • Extent of functional devolution from the 29 subjects in the Eleventh Schedule.
   • Granting taxation and financial powers (Art 243H).
   • Reservation for Backward Classes (OBCs).
   • Giving voting rights to MPs and MLAs in Panchayats.13:T49f,PESA applies to Fifth Schedule areas in 10 states: Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, and Rajasthan.

Key Statutory Mandates under PESA:
1. Customary Law Primacy: State legislation must conform with customary law, social and religious practices, and traditional management practices of community resources (Sec 4(a)).
2. Natural Resource Sovereignty:
   • Ownership of Minor Forest Produce (MFP) vests directly in the Gram Sabha.
   • Mandatory Prior Consultation before land acquisition for development projects and resettlement (Sec 4(i)).
   • Mandatory Recommendation before granting prospecting licenses or mining leases for minor minerals (Sec 4(k)).
3. Social & Institutional Safeguards:
   • Power to enforce prohibition or regulate/restrict the sale of intoxicants.
   • Power to prevent alienation of tribal land and restore unlawfully alienated land.
   • Power to manage village markets and control local money lending to STs.
