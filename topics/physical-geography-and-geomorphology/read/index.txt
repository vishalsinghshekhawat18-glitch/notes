### 2. High-Yield Topographical Terminology Pairs (Amit Sengupta Visual Ledger)

1. **Isthmus vs. Strait:**
   - **Isthmus:** A narrow neck of **land** connecting two large landmasses and separating two bodies of water. *Examples:* Isthmus of Panama (connects North & South America; separates Atlantic & Pacific), Isthmus of Suez (connects Africa & Asia; separates Mediterranean & Red Sea).
   - **Strait:** A narrow navigable channel of **water** connecting two large bodies of water and separating two landmasses. *Examples:* Palk Strait (connects Bay of Bengal & Gulf of Mannar; separates India & Sri Lanka), Strait of Gibraltar (connects Atlantic Ocean & Mediterranean Sea; separates Europe & Africa), Strait of Malacca (connects Andaman Sea & South China Sea).
2. **Peninsula vs. Gulf vs. Bay:**
   - **Peninsula:** A piece of land surrounded by water on three sides (e.g., Indian Peninsula, Arabian Peninsula, Iberian Peninsula).
   - **Gulf:** A large, deep inlet of sea almost surrounded by land, with a narrow mouth (e.g., Persian Gulf, Gulf of Mexico, Gulf of Kutch).
   - **Bay:** A broad curved indentation of the sea where the land curves inward, typically with a wider open entrance than a gulf (e.g., Bay of Bengal, Hudson Bay).
3. **Plateau $\to$ Mesa $\to$ Butte (Denudational Evolution):**
   - **Plateau:** Broad, expansive elevated tract of flat rock capped by a resistant caprock layer (e.g., Colorado Plateau, Deccan Plateau).
   - **Mesa:** As rivers and weathering dissect a plateau, large, flat-topped tablelands with vertical cliff-sides separate (Spanish *mesa* = table).
   - **Butte:** Continued lateral cliff retreat and erosion reduce a mesa to an isolated, tall, narrow rock spire whose height exceeds its surface diameter.
4. **Fjord (Fiord) vs. Ria:**
   - **Fjord:** A deeply carved, steep-sided, submerged U-shaped **glacial valley** flooded by the sea, featuring a shallow rock threshold at its mouth (e.g., Norway, New Zealand, Alaska).
   - **Ria:** A funnel-shaped drowned **river valley** (fluvial), which deepens seaward without a shallow rock sill (e.g., Coast of Galicia in Spain, SW Ireland).
5. **Archipelago vs. Atoll:**
   - **Archipelago:** An extensive chain, cluster, or collection of scattered islands (e.g., Indonesian Archipelago, Andaman & Nicobar, Japanese Archipelago).
   - **Atoll:** A continuous or broken ring-shaped coral reef surrounding a central shallow lagoon without an emergent central volcanic island (e.g., Lakshadweep Atolls,
