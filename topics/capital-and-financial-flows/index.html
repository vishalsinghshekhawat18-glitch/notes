The institutional taxonomy and macroeconomic dynamics of cross-border capital flows in the Balance of Payments Financial Account. Distinguishes Foreign Direct Investment (FDI: long-term, $\\ge 10\\%$ equity, managerial control, technology transfer, illiquid) from Foreign Portfolio Investment (FPI: short-term, $< 10\\%$ equity in listed securities, highly liquid, "hot money"). Currency Convertibility: Current vs Capital Account Convertibility & Tarapore Committee Preconditions

The economic theory, legal framework, and sequencing of Currency Convertibility. Distinguishes Current Account Convertibility (the freedom to convert domestic currency into foreign currency for goods, services, and remittances at market exchange rates, achieved by India in August 1994 under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement) from Capital Account Convertibility (CAC: the freedom to convert local financial assets into foreign financial assets and vice versa). Analyzes the S.S. Tarapore Committee reports (1997 & 2006) and the essential macroeconomic preconditions required for full CAC.
