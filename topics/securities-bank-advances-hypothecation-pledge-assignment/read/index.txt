12:T430,### Rights of the Pawnee under Indian Contract Act 1872

| Section | Statutory Right | Legal Scope & Limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Section 173** | **Right of Retainer** | Retain goods not only for the debt, but also for interest and all necessary expenses incurred in preserving the goods |
| **Section 174** | **No Retainer for Subsequent Debts** | Pawnee cannot retain goods for any separate loan, unless a contract to the contrary exists |
| **Section 175** | **Extraordinary Expenses** | Right to recover extraordinary expenses incurred for preservation of goods (e.g., cold storage, veterinary costs) |
| **Section 176** | **Right of Sale on Default** | Sell goods after **reasonable notice of sale**; surplus goes to pawnor; deficit remains recoverable |
| **Section 178** | **Pledge by Mercantile Agent** | Valid if agent possesses goods/documents with owner's consent and pawnee acts in good faith |
| **Section 178A** | **Pledge under Voidable Contract** | Valid if goods obtained under voidable contract (fraud/coercion) before contract is rescinded
