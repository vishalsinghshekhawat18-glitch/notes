- At room temperature, electrons are bound to atomic nuclei.
- At extreme temperatures ($> 10^4 \, \text{K}$), violent collisions strip electrons away, creating a mix of **free electrons and positively charged ions**.
- Because it is packed with free charges, plasma is an **exceptional electrical conductor** and reacts intensely to magnetic fields.
- **Neon Signs**: When high-voltage electricity passes through neon gas, it ionizes the gas into glowing neon plasma.

### 2. Bose-Einstein Condensate (BEC): The Zero-Point Quantum State
- Named after Indian genius **Satyendra Nath Bose** and **Albert Einstein** (1920).
- According to de Broglie, matter behaves as waves ($\lambda = h / mv$).
- Near Absolute Zero ($T \to 0 \, \text{K}$), thermal velocity vanishes $\implies$ de Broglie wavelength expands until it becomes larger than the distance between atoms.
- Atoms overlap and lose individual identity, collapsing into a single **macroscopic quantum wave ("Super-Atom")**.17:T4dc,### Evaporation vs Boiling Comparison:

| Feature | Evaporation | Boiling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Temperature** | Occurs at **ANY temperature below boiling point**. | Occurs strictly at a **fixed Boiling Point** ($100^\circ\text{C}$ for water at $1 \text{ atm}$). |
| **Physical Extent** | **Surface Phenomenon** (only top surface molecules escape). | **Bulk Phenomenon** (bubbles form throughout entire liquid). |
| **Cooling Effect** | **Always causes cooling** of the remaining liquid. | Does not cause cooling (temperature remains constant). |
| **Noise & Bubbles** | Silent, zero bubble formation. | Vigorous boiling bubbles with noise. |

### The 4 Factors Governing Rate of Evaporation:
1. **Surface Area ($\uparrow$)**: Spreading out wet clothes to dry increases surface molecules exposed $\implies$ Evaporation speeds up.
2. **Temperature ($\uparrow$)**: Hot summer days provide more thermal energy $\implies$ Evaporation speeds up.
3. **Humidity ($\downarrow$)**: Dry air has room for water vapor $\implies$ Evaporation speeds up (sweat evaporates fast in Rajasthan, but stays sticky in humid Mumbai).
4. **Wind Speed ($\uparrow$)**: Wind carries away evaporated vapor molecules from the surface $\implies$ Evaporation speeds up.18:T4ba,### The Master Triad of Mixtures Comparison
