Auditing Jurisprudence: True & Fair View, Companies Act 2013 Governance & Audit Reports

Foundational auditing principles (True and Fair View, Independence), statutory auditor governance under Companies Act 2013 (Section 139 appointment and mandatory rotation: individual 1 term of 5 yrs, firm 2 terms of 10 yrs with 5-yr cooling; Section 141 disqualifications including ₹5 Lakh indebtedness), the four audit report opinions (Unqualified, Qualified, Adverse, Disclaimer), and Companies (Auditor's Report) Order (CARO 2020).

Intuition & Context

The Financial Watchdog and the Four Grades of Corporate Truth

If a student graded their own exam paper, they would give themselves 100% every single time. That is why companies cannot audit their own balance sheets:

  1. The Watchdog, Not a Bloodhound: As Justice Lopes famously ruled in Kingston Cotton Mill (1896), an auditor is a watchdog to protect shareholders, not a detective hunting for fraud unless suspicious clues appear.
  2. True and Fair View: An auditor does not guarantee that a company is profitable or well-managed. They only certify that the financial accounts are free from material lies (they show a 'True and Fair View').
  3. The Four Grades of Audit Opinion:
    • Clean / Unqualified: Everything looks good.
    • Qualified: There is a problem with one specific inventory item, but the rest of the company's books are sound (material, but NOT pervasive).
    • Adverse: The whole balance sheet is fabricated and completely unreliable (both material AND pervasive).
    • Disclaimer of Opinion: Management locked the auditor out of the records and refused to provide bank statements; the auditor cannot verify anything (unable to obtain evidence, pervasive impact).
Core Principle

Master Architecture: Audit Types, Rotation Rules & The 4 Opinions

Types of Audits in the Indian Corporate Framework

Audit Type Governing Statute / Mandate Primary Scope / Objective Appointment Authority
Statutory Financial Audit Companies Act 2013 (Section 139) Verifies compliance with Ind AS / GAAP; expresses opinion on True & Fair view Shareholders in AGM (or CAG for Government Companies under Sec 139(5))
Internal Audit Companies Act 2013 (Section 138) Evaluates internal financial controls, risk management, operational workflows Board of Directors / Audit Committee
Cost Audit Companies Act 2013 (Section 148) Verifies cost records in manufacturing companies to prevent predatory pricing Board on recommendation of Audit Committee
Secretarial Audit Companies Act 2013 (Section 204) Verifies compliance with corporate, labour, SEBI, and FEMA laws Practicing Company Secretary (PCS) appointed by Board
Social Audit Statutory under MGNREGA / CSR rules Assesses community impact of public schemes and corporate CSR expenditure Gram Sabha / Independent Social Audit Units

The 4 Types of Audit Opinions (SA 700 / SA 705)

Audit Opinion Type Nature of Financial Misstatement Pervasiveness in Financial Statements Auditor's Verdict Summary
1. Unqualified (Clean) Financial statements comply with GAAP in all material aspects None Statements reflect a True & Fair view
2. Qualified Opinion Misstatements exist or evidence is lacking for specific items Material, but NOT Pervasive "Except for the effects of matter X, statements give a True & Fair view"
3. Adverse Opinion Misstatements are severe, widespread, and misleading BOTH Material AND Pervasive Financial statements DO NOT present a True and Fair view
4. Disclaimer of Opinion Auditor is prevented from obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence Pervasive limitation of scope Auditor refrains from expressing any opinion due to inability to verify
Mechanism & Operational Rule

Auditor Disqualifications & CARO 2020 Reporting Clauses

Statutory Disqualifications: Section 141(3) Companies Act 2013

A person CANNOT be appointed as company auditor if:

  1. It is a Body Corporate (other than a Limited Liability Partnership - LLP).
  2. An officer or employee of the company.
  3. A person who is a partner or employee of an officer/employee of the company.
  4. A person (or partner/relative) who:
    • Holds security/shares in the company (relative may hold shares up to face value of ₹1,00,000 / ₹1 Lakh).
    • Is indebted to the company in excess of ₹5,00,000 (₹5 Lakh).
    • Has given a guarantee/security in excess of ₹1,00,000 (₹1 Lakh).
  5. A person convicted of an offence involving fraud (disqualified for 10 years from conviction date).

CARO 2020 (Companies Auditor's Report Order)

Mandates reporting on 21 specific operational clauses: physical inventory verification, benami property proceedings, undisclosed income, Evergreening of loans, and internal audit compliance.

Exam Application & Traps

Competitive Examination Traps & Statutory Governance

Frequent Examiner Traps

  1. Auditor Rotation Slabs Trap:
    • Individual Auditor: Maximum 1 term of 5 consecutive years.
    • Audit Firm: Maximum 2 terms of 5 consecutive years (10 consecutive years).
    • Cooling-off Period: Mandatory 5 years before reappointment.
  2. Indebtedness Disqualification Ceiling: A person indebted to the company for more than ₹5,00,000 (₹5 Lakh) is disqualified.
  3. Qualified vs Adverse vs Disclaimer:
    • Material but NOT pervasive = Qualified Opinion.
    • Material AND pervasive = Adverse Opinion.
    • Scope limitation preventing evidence collection with pervasive impact = Disclaimer of Opinion.
  4. Removal of Auditor Before Expiry of Term (Section 140(1)): Requires a Special Resolution of the company AND the prior approval of the Central Government.

🏛️ 5-Minute Architecture & Key Revision

Architecture of Auditing Jurisprudence & Corporate Governance

  1. Companies Act 2013 Statutory Framework:
    • Section 139: Appointment & Mandatory Rotation (Individual: 1x5 yrs; Firm: 2x5 yrs; 5-yr cooling).
    • Section 140: Removal before term requires Special Resolution + Central Government approval.
    • Section 141: Qualifications (CA with COP) & Disqualifications (Indebtedness >₹5 Lakh, shares >₹1 Lakh face value).
    • Section 143: Powers and Duties of Auditors (CAG supplementary audit under Sec 143(6)).
    • Section 148: Cost Audit.
  2. The 4 Audit Opinions Grid (SA 700 / 705):
    • Unqualified: Clean True & Fair view.
    • Qualified: Material, but NOT Pervasive error.
    • Adverse: BOTH Material AND Pervasive error.
    • Disclaimer of Opinion: Severe scope limitation; evidence unobtainable; pervasive impact.
  3. CARO 2020 Reporting Invariants:
    • 21 Diagnostic reporting clauses.
    • Physical inventory verification, benami properties, loan evergreening, and internal audit.
