• The President nominates **12 members** having special knowledge or practical experience in:
  1. **Literature**
  2. **Science**
  3. **Art**

### Parliamentary Sessions & Quorum (Articles 85 & 100):
• **Sessions:** President summons each House. Maximum permissible gap between two sessions **shall not exceed 6 months** (Parliament must meet at least twice a year; conventionally meets in 3 sessions: Budget, Monsoon, Winter).
• **Adjournment vs Prorogation vs Dissolution:**
  - **Adjournment:** Suspends sitting for hours/days/weeks; done by the **Presiding Officer (Speaker/Chairman)**.
  - **Adjournment Sine Die:** Terminates sitting without naming a day for reassembly; done by the **Presiding Officer**.
  - **Prorogation:** Terminates a session of the House; done by the **President of India** under Article 85(2)(a).
  - **Dissolution:** Ends the very life of the Lok Sabha; done by the **President of India** under Article 85(2)(b) (Rajya Sabha cannot be dissolved).
• **Quorum (Article 100(3)):** Minimum **10% (1/10th) of the total membership** of the House required to constitute a meeting (55 members in Lok Sabha, 25 members in Rajya Sabha).13:T41c,### Critical Traps & Multi-Statement Pitfalls for Aspirants:\n\n1. **Article & Nomenclature Confusion:** Examiners frequently confuse identical sounding powers across Union and State levels (e.g. Art 123 vs Art 213; Art 72 vs Art 161). • **Trigger:** If after a bill is passed by one House and sent to the other: (a) rejected by the other House, (b) Houses disagree on amendments, or (c) more than **6 months elapse** without being passed.
• **Summoned by:** The **President of India** by notification.
• **Presided by:** **Speaker of Lok Sabha** $\rightarrow$ in absence, Deputy Speaker $\rightarrow$ in absence, Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha (⚠️ **Chairman of Rajya Sabha NEVER presides**).
• **Majority Required:** **Simple Majority** of the total number of members of both Houses present and voting (Lok Sabha’s numerical superiority of 543 vs 245 usually guarantees passage).
• **Strict Bars:** **NEVER held for Money Bills (Art 109) or Constitutional Amendment Bills (Art 368)**.

• **Question Hour (1st hour of sitting, 11 AM – 12 PM):**
  1. **Starred Questions:** Require an **oral answer**; supplementary questions **CAN be asked**.
  2. **Unstarred Questions:** Require a **written answer**; supplementary questions **CANNOT be asked**.
  3. **Short Notice Questions:** Asked on matters of urgent public importance with less than 10 days notice; answered orally.
• **Zero Hour (Starts at 12 PM immediately after Question Hour):**
  - An **informal Indian parliamentary innovation (introduced in 1962)**; not mentioned in Rules of Procedure. Members raise urgent matters without prior notice.
