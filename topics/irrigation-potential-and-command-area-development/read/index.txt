12:T559,### Structural Drivers of the IPC-IPU Divide

| Structural Bottleneck | Field Reality / Failure Mode | Corrective CADWM Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| **Missing Field Channels** | Main canals built by state engineers; field-level ditches left to impoverished farmers | Government constructs lined pucca field channels or underground pipes to 1-ha outlets |
| **Heavy Canal Siltation & Breaches** | Unlined earthen canals choke with weeds and silt; transmission water losses reach 40–50% | Canal relining and participatory de-silting by Water Users' Associations (WUAs) |
| **Head-End vs Tail-End Inequity** | Head-reach farmers illegally siphon water for paddy/sugarcane, leaving tail ends dry | Strict enforcement of **Warabandi rotational schedules** and volumetric metering |
| **Undulating Topography & Waterlogging** | Uneven fields prevent gravity flow, causing localized pools and waterlogging | Micro-topographical land leveling and installation of subsurface drainage networks |

### Participatory Irrigation Management (PIM) Hierarchy
- **Tier 1: Water Users' Association (WUA):** Minor canal/outlet level covering 300 to 500 hectares of command area. Enforces Warabandi and collects water tariffs.
- **Tier 2: Distributary Committee:** Federation of WUAs along a distributary.
- **Tier 3: Project Committee:** Apex body representing the entire dam
