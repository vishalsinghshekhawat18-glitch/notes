12:T455,A **Citizen** is a full member of the Indian State owing allegiance to it and enjoying all civil and political rights. **Aliens** (citizens of other states) enjoy certain basic human rights under Article 21 and 14, but are excluded from political participation.

### Rights Guaranteed ONLY to Indian Citizens (Exam High-Yield):
1. **Article 15:** Prohibition of discrimination on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth.
2. **Article 16:** Equality of opportunity in matters of public employment.
3. **Article 19:** Six fundamental democratic freedoms (speech, assembly, association, movement, residence, profession).
4. **Articles 29 & 30:** Cultural and educational rights of minorities.
5. **Article 326:** Right to vote in elections to the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies.
6. **Right to Contest:** Eligibility to become MP, MLA, MLC.
7. **Constitutional Offices:** Eligibility to hold the office of President (Art 58), Vice-President (Art 66), Supreme Court Judge (Art 124), High Court Judge (Art 217), Governor (Art 157), Attorney General (Art 76), Advocate General (Art 165).13:T536,Articles 5 to 11 dealt only with the initial citizenship snapshot on **January 26, 1950**:

• **Article 6 (Migrants from Pakistan):** Persons who migrated from Pakistan before **July 19, 1948** (ordinarily resident) or after July 19, 1948 (registered after 6 months residence).

• **Article 10 (Continuance of Rights):** Every citizen continues to be a citizen, subject to any law made by Parliament.

By Birth (Section 3 — Evolution of Law):
• **Jan 26, 1950 to July 1, 1987:** Pure *Jus Soli* (born in India = automatic citizen, irrespective of parents’ nationality).
• **July 1, 1987 to Dec 3, 2004:** Born in India + **at least one parent must be an Indian citizen** at the time of birth (1986 Amendment).
• **After Dec 3, 2004 (2003 Amendment):** Born in India + **both parents are citizens of India**, OR **one parent is a citizen and the other is NOT an illegal migrant** at birth.

### 2. By Descent (Section 4 — Born Outside India):
• Born outside India on or after Jan 26, 1950 if father was an Indian citizen.
• **1992 Amendment (Gender Equality):** Either **father OR mother** was an Indian citizen at the time of birth.
• **Post-2004:** Birth must be registered at an Indian consulate within **1 year** with an undertaking that the minor does not hold a foreign passport.

### 3. By Registration (Section 5 — Prescribed Categories):
• Persons of Indian Origin (PIO) ordinarily resident in India for **7 years** before applying.
• Persons married to an Indian citizen and resident in India for **7 years**.
• Minor children of Indian citizens.
• Persons of full age whose parents were registered as citizens of independent India.

### 4. By Naturalisation (Section 6 — Foreigners):
• Qualifications: Not an illegal migrant; renounces foreign citizenship; resident in India for **12 continuous months** preceding application, plus **11 years aggregate residence out of the preceding 14 years** (shortened to 5 years for CAA 2019 beneficiaries); good character; adequate knowledge of an **Eighth Schedule language**; intends to reside in India.

By Renunciation (Section 8 — Voluntary Surrender):
• Any Indian citizen of full age and capacity who is also a citizen/national of another country can make a formal declaration renouncing Indian citizenship.
• **Wartime Rule:** The Central Government can withhold registration of renunciation if made during a war in which India is engaged.
• **Effect on Minor Children:** When a person renounces citizenship, **every minor child of that person also ceases to be an Indian citizen**. However, such a child may resume Indian citizenship within **one year of attaining full age (18 years)**.

### 2. By Termination (Section 9 — Automatic Loss):
• If an Indian citizen **voluntarily acquires the citizenship of another country**, their Indian citizenship **automatically terminates** by operation of law.
• Does not apply during wartime without the central government’s explicit approval.
