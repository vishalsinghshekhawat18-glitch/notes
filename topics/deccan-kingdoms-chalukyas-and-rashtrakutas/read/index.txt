```12:T403,1. **Aihole Durga Temple Misconception**: The famous **Durga Temple at Aihole** is NOT dedicated to Goddess Durga! It was a **Surya/Vishnu temple** situated near the city ramparts or fort (*Durg*), which led to its local name "Durga Temple". Architecturally, it has an **apsidal (horseshoe/Gajaprishtha)** ground plan imitating Buddhist chaitya halls.
2. **Aihole Prashasti Poet Authorship**: The Aihole Prashasti was composed by **Ravikirti** (a devout Jain poet), NOT Kalidasa or Bharavi. Ravikirti boasts at the end of the inscription that his poetic skill equaled both Kalidasa and Bharavi.
3. **Meguti Temple Dedication**: The Aihole Prashasti is carved on the outer wall of the **Meguti Temple**, which is a **Jain temple** dedicated to Jinendra, NOT a Hindu temple.
4. **Pattadakal Virupaksha vs. Kanchi Kailasanatha**: Queen Lokamahadevi brought sculptors from Kanchi (headed by architect *Gunda*) to build the Virupaksha Temple at Pattadakal as a conscious direct replica of the **Kailasanatha Temple at Kanchipuram**.13:Tc0e,The Rashtrakutas dominated the political and cultural landscape of the Deccan from 753 to 973 CE.
