## 2. Approved Phase 10.1 Human-Learner Protocol

The pilot must be administered interactively to the real human participant across 6 distinct stages:

### Stage A — Baseline Diagnostic (Pre-Reading)
* Administer 8 diagnostic questions testing existing mental models before reading.
* Record the learner's actual answer, reasoning, and confidence (1–5). Zero coaching or immediate answer reveals allowed.

### Stage B — Reading Session Observation
* Participant reads the 5 canonical Inflation concepts in Reading Hub.
* Record actual elapsed time, voluntary rereads, and observed friction codes (F1 to F8).

### Stage C — Immediate Closed-Book Teach-Back
* 7 core explanatory prompts administered without access to notes.
* Scored on a 0–2 rubric across 5 dimensions: Concept, Causal reasoning, Scope, Example, Terminology (Max 10 pts per prompt).

### Stage D — Unseen Scenario Transfer Assessment
* 8 novel macroeconomic scenarios not found in the notes.
* Evaluate whether the participant applies underlying causal models or memorized keywords.

### Stage E — Competitive Exam Application
* Unfamiliar questions tested across UPSC CSE / APFC, RPSC RAS, and IIBF DBF lenses.

### Stage F — Delayed Retrieval Durability
* Administered after a true, elapsed time interval (e.g. 24 hours).
* Reconstruct 30s, 2m, and 5m mental models from `FLASH_30S` retrieval pegs.
