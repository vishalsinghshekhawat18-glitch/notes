## 2. Topic-by-Topic Canonical Concept Breakdown

### Topic 5: Part I — The Union and Its Territory (`part-1-union-and-its-territory`)
1. **`CON-T5-01`**: **Article 1: Union of States, Federal Nature & Territory of India**
   - *Core Doctrine*: "India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States." Contrast between "Union of India" (only 28 states sharing sovereign powers) vs "Territory of India" (wider: states + 8 UTs + future acquired territories).
   - *Pedagogy*: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar's dual rationale for choosing "Union" over "Federation" (not an agreement; no right to secede).
2. **`CON-T5-02`**: **Articles 2 and 3: Admission vs Internal Reorganisation of States**
   - *Core Mechanism*: Article 2 (admission/establishment of external new territories) vs Article 3 (internal realignment of existing state boundaries).
   - *Constitutional Safeguards*: Presidential prior recommendation, mandatory State Legislature reference (time-bound, purely non-binding), and Simple Majority approval in Parliament ("Indestructible Union of Destructible States" vs USA's "Indestructible Union of Indestructible States").
3. **`CON-T5-03`**: **Article 4 & Cession of Indian Territory: The Berubari Doctrine**
   - *Judicial Precedents*: *In re Berubari Union (1960)* (Article 3 does NOT authorize ceding territory; ceding to foreign power requires Article 368 Constitutional Amendment, leading to 9th Amendment 1960) vs *Maganbhai Ishwarbhai Patel v. Union of India (1969)* (settling boundary disputes without ceding territory can be done by Executive Action).
4. **`CON-T5-04`**: **Union Territories: Constitutional Architecture & Enclaves Exchange**
   - *Evolution & Classification*: Part VIII (Articles 239–241), administrator designations (Lt. Governors vs Chief Commissioners), legislative assemblies in UTs (Puducherry, Delhi, J&K).
   - *100th Amendment (2015)*: Enclaves exchange with Bangladesh (India transferred 111 enclaves to Bangladesh and received 51 enclaves).

### Topic 6: Unification and Integration of India (`unification-of-india`)
1. **`CON-T6-01`**: **Lapse of Paramountcy & The Patel-Menon Integration Architecture**
   - *Legal Framework*: Section 7(1)(b) of Indian Independence Act 1947; Sardar Patel & V.P. Menon's diplomatic tri-part mechanism: Standstill Agreement, Instrument of Accession (Defence, External Affairs, Communications), and Privy Purses (later abolished by 26th Amendment 1971).
2. **`CON-T6-02`**: **Integration of Challenging States: Junagadh, Hyderabad & Jammu & Kashmir**
   - *Junagadh*: Nawab accession to Pakistan -> *Aarzi Hukumat* revolt -> Feb 1948 Plebiscite (190,870 votes for India vs 91).
   - *Hyderabad*: Nizam & Razakar terror -> **Operation Polo** (Police Action, Sept 13–18, 1948, Maj Gen J.N. Chaudhuri).
   - *Jammu & Kashmir*: Pakistani tribal invasion (*Operation Gulmarg*) -> Maharaja Hari Singh signed IoA on October 26, 1947 -> 2019 Reorganisation into UTs of J&K and Ladakh.
3. **`CON-T6-03`**: **Liberation of Foreign Enclaves: French and Portuguese Territories**
   - *French Settlements*: Chandernagore (1949 plebiscite), Puducherry/Karikal/Mahe/Yanam (Nov 1, 1954 de facto transfer, Aug 16, 1962 de jure treaty, 14th Amendment 1962).
   - *Portuguese Settlements*: Dadra & Nagar Haveli (1954 liberation, 10th Amendment 1961), Goa/Daman/Diu (**Operation Vijay**, Dec 18–19, 1961, 12th Amendment 1962, Goa Statehood via 56th Amendment 1987).
4. **`CON-T6-04`**: **Integration of Sikkim: From Associate State to Full Statehood**
   - *Constitutional Milestones*: Chogyal protectorate -> 35th Amendment 1974 (created novel "Associate State" status via Article 2A and 10th Schedule) -> 36th Amendment 1975 (repealed 2A/10th Schedule, made Sikkim the 22nd full state, inserted Article 371F).
   - *1950 Four-Fold Classification*: Part A (9 Governor Provinces), Part B (9 Princely States with Rajpramukhs), Part C (10 Chief Commissioner Provinces), Part D (Andaman & Nicobar).

### Topic 7: Reorganisation of States & Zonal Councils (`reorganisation-of-states`)
1. **`CON-T7-01`**: **Linguistic Demands & Early Commissions: Dhar Commission & JVP Committee**
   - *1920 Nagpur Session Pledge* vs post-partition security anxieties.
   - *S.K. Dhar Commission (1948)*: Rejected language; advocated administrative convenience.
   - *JVP Committee (1948–49)*: Jawaharlal Nehru, Vallabhbhai Patel, Pattabhi Sitaramayya; formally rejected linguistic statehood.
2. **`CON-T7-02`**: **Andhra Crisis & Fazal Ali Commission (SRC 1953–55)**
   - *Potti Sreeramulu's 56-day fast* -> Creation of Andhra State on October 1, 1953 (Kurnool capital).
   - *States Reorganisation Commission (SRC)*: Justice Fazal Ali, K.M. Panikkar, Hriday Nath Kunzru. Accepted language criterion while rejecting "One Language, One State". Laid down 4 core pillars (National Unity/Security, Linguistic/Cultural Homogeneity, Financial/Administrative Viability, National Welfare Planning).
3. **`CON-T7-03`**: **States Reorganisation Act 1956 & Evolution of States (1956–2020)**
   - *1956 Baseline*: 7th Amendment abolished 4-fold classification; created 14 States and 6 UTs.
   - *Post-1956 Milestones*: 1960 (Bombay -> Maharashtra & Gujarat 15th), 1963 (Nagaland 16th), 1966 (Punjab -> Punjab & Haryana 17th + Chandigarh UT via Shah Commission), 1971 (Himachal Pradesh 18th), 1972 (Manipur 19th, Tripura 20th, Meghalaya 21st), 1975 (Sikkim 22nd), 1987 (Mizoram 23rd, Arunachal Pradesh 24th, Goa 25th), 2000 (Chhattisgarh 26th, Uttarakhand 27th, Jharkhand 28th), 2014 (Telangana 29th), 2019 (J&K Reorganisation into 2 UTs), 2020 (DNH & DD merger). Current Count: **28 States and 8 Union Territories**.
4. **`CON-T7-04`**: **Zonal Councils & North-Eastern Council: Inter-State Cooperative Architecture**
   - *Statutory Nature*: Created under Part III of States Reorganisation Act 1956 (NOT constitutional under Art 263).
   - *5 Zones*: Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern. Chaired by **Union Home Minister**; Vice-Chairs are CMs on 1-year rotation.
   - *North-Eastern Council (NEC)*: Created under separate *North-Eastern Council Act, 1971* (covers 8 states, including Sikkim added in 2002).

### Topic 8: Part II — Citizenship (`part-2-citizenship`)
1. **`CON-T8-01`**: **Citizenship: Meaning, Civil Rights & Constitutional Articles (5–11)**
   - *Exclusive Rights for Citizens*: Fundamental Rights (Articles 15, 16, 19, 29, 30), Right to Vote (Article 326), eligibility for constitutional offices (President, VP, Judges, Governor, AG).
   - *Transitional Scheme*: Articles 5 to 11 (snapshot at Jan 26, 1950); Article 9 (Single Citizenship / auto-termination on acquiring foreign nationality); Article 11 (plenary power of Parliament to regulate citizenship).
2. **`CON-T8-02`**: **Acquisition of Indian Citizenship under Citizenship Act 1955**
   - *Five Modes*:
     1. **Birth (*Jus Soli*)**: Evolution from unconditional (1950–1987) to either parent Indian (1987–2004) to both parents Indian or one Indian and other not illegal migrant (post-2004).
     2. **Descent (*Jus Sanguinis*)**: Gender-neutralized by 1992 amendment; mandatory birth registration at Indian consulate within 1 year post-2004.
     3. **Registration**: 7 years residence for PIOs; spouses of Indian citizens; minor children.
     4. **Naturalisation**: 12 years aggregate residence (12 months immediately preceding + 11 of 14 years), good character, knowledge of 8th Schedule language.
     5. **Incorporation of Territory**: Automatic conferment upon notified residents (e.g. Goa 1962, Pondicherry 1962).
3. **`CON-T8-03`**: **Loss of Indian Citizenship: Renunciation, Termination & Deprivation**
   - *Three Modes*:
     1. **Renunciation (Section 8)**: Voluntary declaration; minor children lose citizenship until attaining 18 years (1-year resumption window).
     2. **Termination (Section 9)**: Automatic operation of law upon voluntarily acquiring foreign citizenship (suspended during war).
     3. **Deprivation (Section 10)**: Compulsory cancellation by Central Government (fraud, disloyalty to Constitution, unlawful trade/communication with enemy during war, 2 years imprisonment within 5 years of registration/naturalisation, ordinary residence outside India for 7 continuous years).
4. **`CON-T8-04`**: **Single Citizenship vs Dual Citizenship & Constitutional Unity**
   - *Doctrinal Contrast*: Indian Single Citizenship (*D.P. Joshi v. State of Madhya Bharat, 1955* distinguishes state domicile for education from national citizenship) vs US/Swiss Dual Citizenship (separate state & national citizenships).
   - *Constitutional Unity*: Unified rights across all states; Article 16(3) Parliamentary residence exception for employment.
5. **`CON-T8-05`**: **Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) Scheme & Rights Framework**
   - *Origin*: L.M. Singhvi Committee (2000–2002); Citizenship Amendments 2003 & 2005; PIO scheme merged into OCI on Jan 9, 2015.
   - *Section 7B Rights Granted*: Lifelong multiple-entry visa, FRRO exemption, parity with NRIs in economic/financial/education spheres, non-agricultural real estate ownership.
   - *Section 7B(2) Rights Strictly Denied*: No public employment equality (Art 16), No voting rights (RPA 1950), No contesting elections (RPA 1951), Ineligible for constitutional offices.
   - *Comparison Matrix*: Structured NRI (Indian citizen) vs OCI (foreign citizen with card) vs PIO (merged).
6. **`CON-T8-06`**: **Contemporary Citizenship Dynamics: Renunciation Trends & Policy Debate**
   - *Empirical Trends*: 1.6+ million citizens renounced Indian citizenship between 2011 and 2023 (peaked at 225,620 in 2022).
   - *Root Causes*: Global mobility, tax residency, career opportunities in OECD countries.
   - *Policy Debate*: Constitutional and security implications of dual citizenship vs economic benefits of diaspora capital and diaspora voting rights.
