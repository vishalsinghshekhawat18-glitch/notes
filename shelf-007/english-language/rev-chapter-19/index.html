2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

TCS iON Environment: Plain text only ➔ 0 Bold, 0 Italics, 0 Tab indents ➔ Double Enter for paragraphs 
➔ Copy/Paste Disabled ➔ Spellcheck Disabled ➔ Practicing on Notepad is MANDATORY 
➔ Word Count Discipline: Budget ±5% (Never exceed ±10%) 
➔ Punctuation Hygiene: Comma/Period touches word ➔ exactly ONE space after 
➔ Time Pacing (90 Min paper): 10m Plan ➔ 30m Essay ➔ 25m Précis ➔ 20m Letter/RC ➔ 5m Proofread 
➔ Précis Title: 3 to 5 marks ➔ Letters: NEVER write "Respected Sir" or "Your's".

3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

~1m read

Card 1

  • Diagnostic Sentence: What happens to word count tracking on the TCS iON console if a candidate types "economic,stability" without a space after the comma?
  • Answer: The system's automated word-counting algorithm detects spaces; omitting the space after the comma causes the engine to count "economic,stability" as a single garbled word, artificially depressing the reported word count and introducing a typo.

Card 2

  • Diagnostic Question: In SBI PO Mains (30 minutes total), what is the optimal time allocation between Letter and Essay?
  • Answer: 12 minutes for the Letter (including planning and drafting), 15 minutes for the Essay, and a mandatory 3-minute buffer for manual proofreading and typo correction.
