## 5. Historiographical Consensus & Resolved Debates

1. **Pushyamitra Shunga & Buddhism:** Reconciled literary accusations in the *Divyavadana* with physical archaeological evidence from Bharhut and Sanchi, establishing royal non-interference alongside substantial community patronage.
2. **Kanishka's Chronology:** Presented the traditional 78 CE Shaka Era affiliation alongside the modern epigraphic consensus of c. 127 CE derived from the Rabatak inscription.
3. **Satavahana Matronymics:** Clarified that naming kings after their mothers (*Gautamiputra*, *Vashishthiputra*) was an honorific lineage marker and did not alter patrilineal succession.
4. **Fourth Buddhist Council:** Distinguished the northern Sarvastivada council at Kundalvana (Kashmir) from the southern Theravada council at Aluvihara (Sri Lanka).
5. **Indo-Roman Trade Dynamics:** Reconciled Pliny's lament regarding the 100-million sesterce drain with archaeological finds of Roman gold coins, amphorae, and Arretine ware at Arikamedu, Muziris, and Keeladi.
