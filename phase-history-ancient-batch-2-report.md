## 3. Claims Corrected / Overstatements Nuanced

| Popular / Coaching Simplification | Forensic Historical Reality | Canonical Correction Applied |

| **"Buddhism and Jainism were founded merely as anti-Brahmana social protest movements"** | **OVERSIMPLIFICATION** | Contextualized within the broad socio-economic transformation of the Second Urbanisation: iron-plough agricultural demand for cattle draft power (protected by Ahimsa), social elevation of urban merchant classes (*Vaishyas/Setthis*) against orthodox usury taboos, and Kshatriya intellectual assertion in *Gana-Sanghas*. |
| **"Buddhism was an atheistic religion with modern constitutional egalitarianism"** | **ANACHRONISM** | Buddhism is non-theistic and rejects an uncaused creator God (*Ishvara*), but operates within karma, rebirth, and celestial realms. | **"Jainism was founded by Mahavira"** | **INCORRECT** | Mahavira was the **24th Tirthankara** and reformer who added *Brahmacharya* to the 4-fold restraint (*Chaturyama*) taught by the 23rd Tirthankara *Parshvanatha* (c. 8th Century BCE). |
| **"Magadha's rise was caused solely by iron mines"** | **MONOCAUSAL REDUCTION** | Corrected into a multi-causal model: natural defensive capitals (Rajgir hills, Pataliputra *Jaladurga* confluence), rich iron ore deposits, fertile Gangetic alluvium with high rice yields, pioneering deployment of war elephants, and an unorthodox, socially flexible society. |
| **"Gana-Sanghas were modern democratic republics"** | **MISLEADING ANALOGY** | Gana-Sanghas were **Kshatriya oligarchic republics**: while decisions were debated in the *Santhagara* using *Salaka* voting sticks among clan heads (*Rajas*), political citizenship strictly excluded women, slaves (*Dasa-Karmakaras*), and Shudras. |

## 5. Canonical Concepts Created (11 Concepts)

1. **`CON-HIST-10`:** *From Janapadas to Mahajanapadas: Second Urbanisation, Iron & Agrarian Expansion*
   - Material catalysts: Iron axes, wet-rice seedling transplantation (*Ropa*), Northern Black Polished Ware (NBPW), Punch-Marked Coins (*Karshapanas*), merchant guilds (*Srenis* under *Sreshthins*), *Uttarapatha* and *Dakshinapatha* highways, and statutory 1/6th *Bhaga* taxation.
2. **`CON-HIST-11`:** *The Sixteen Mahajanapadas: Political Geography, Monarchies & Gana-Sanghas*
   - Textual concordances (*Anguttara Nikaya* and *Bhagavati Sutra*); 16-state master matrix with capitals; *Ashmaka* (Godavari) as sole southern state; *Matsya* (Viratnagar/Bairat) in Rajasthan; constitutional dichotomy of Monarchies (*Rajyas*) vs Oligarchic Republics (*Gana-Sanghas*); *Santhagara* assembly voting with *Salakas* (*Salaka-Gahapaka*).
3. **`CON-HIST-12`:** *Magadha's Geo-Strategic Hegemony: Geography, Forests, Minerals & Warfare Innovations*
   - Multi-causal synthesis: Rajgir 5-hill fortress, Pataliputra 4-river *Jaladurga*, Chota Nagpur/Singhbhum iron ore, Middle Ganga alluvial rice surplus, first large-scale war elephant deployment, and unorthodox *Bratya* social flexibility.
4. **`CON-HIST-13`:** *From Haryankas to Nandas: Pre-Mauryan Dynastic Consolidation*
   - Haryankas: Bimbisara (*Seniya*, standing army, marriage diplomacy, Anga annexation), Ajatashatru (*Kunika*, 16-year Vajji war, *Mahashilakantaka* catapult, *Rathamusala* scythed chariot, 1st Buddhist Council 483 BCE), Udayin (Pataliputra capital founder); Shishunagas: Shishunaga (Avanti annexation), Kalashoka (2nd Buddhist Council 383 BCE); Nandas: Mahapadma Nanda (*Ekarat*, first empire builder), Dhana Nanda (mighty army deterring Alexander 326 BCE, overthrown 321 BCE).
5. **`CON-HIST-14`:** *Siddhartha Gautama & The Shramana Intellectual Milieu*
   - 6th BCE Shramana ferment; Six Heterodox Teachers (Makkhali Gosala *Niyati*, Ajita Kesakambali materialism, etc.); Buddha's life landmarks: Lumbini birth (Sakya clan), Four Sights, *Mahabhinishkramana* (Horse), *Sambodhi* at Bodh Gaya (Bodhi Tree), First Sermon at Sarnath (Dharma Wheel), *Mahaparinirvana* at Kushinagar (Stupa).
6. **`CON-HIST-15`:** *Core Buddhist Philosophy: Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, Pratityasamutpada & Nirvana*
   - *Cattari Ariyasaccani* (Dukkha, Samudaya/Tanha, Nirodha/Nirvana, Magga); Threefold Training (*Trishiksha*: Prajna, Sila, Samadhi); *Pratityasamutpada* (Dependent Origination / 12 Nidanas); Three Marks of Existence (*Anicca, Dukkha, Anatta* / 5 Skandhas); Rebirth as causal stream without eternal soul.
7. **`CON-HIST-16`:** *The Buddhist Sangha, Canonical Councils, Pali Tipitaka & Sectarian Evolution*
   - Democratic Sangha code (*Uposatha, Vassa, Pavarana*); Bhikkhuni Sangha (*Mahapajapati Gotami, Garudhammas*); Four Buddhist Councils matrix (Rajgir 483 BCE, Vaishali 383 BCE, Pataliputra 250 BCE, Kashmir 72 CE); Pali Tipitaka (*Vinaya, Sutta, Abhidhamma*); *Therigatha*; Hinayana, Mahayana (*Bodhisattva* ideal), and Vajrayana.
8. **`CON-HIST-17`:** *Vardhamana Mahavira & The Tirthankara Lineage*
   - 24 Tirthankaras; Rishabhanatha (1st, Bull), Parshvanatha (23rd, 4 Vows); Mahavira biography: Kundagrama birth (Jnatrika clan, Siddhartha/Trishala), 12 years penance, *Kevala Jnana* under Sal tree at Rijupalika River, 5th Vow *Brahmacharya*, 11 *Gandharas* (*Indrabhuti Gautama*), Nirvana at Pavapuri.
9. **`CON-HIST-18`:** *Core Jain Metaphysics: Triratna, Ahimsa, Anekantavada, Syadvada & Moksha*
   - Dualism: *Jiva* and *Ajiva* (Pudgala, Dharma, Adharma, Akasha, Kala); *Triratna* (Right Faith, Knowledge, Conduct); 5 Vows (*Maha-Vratas* vs *Anu-Vratas*); *Anekantavada* (Multi-sided reality / Elephant parable); *Syadvada* (Seven-fold conditional predication / *Saptabhangi Naya*); Karma as physical matter (*Asrava, Bandha, Samvara, Nirjara, Moksha*); *Sallekhana / Santhara*.
10. **`CON-HIST-19`:** *Jain Community, Agamas, Digambara-Svetambara Schism & Historical Legacy*
    - 12-year Magadha famine; Digambaras (*Bhadrabahu*, Shravanabelagola, strict nudity, women cannot attain direct Kevala Jnana) vs Svetambaras (*Sthulabhadra*, white robes, women can attain Moksha / Mallinatha); Canonical Councils (1st Pataliputra 300 BCE, 2nd Vallabhi 512 CE under *Devardhi* in Ardhamagadhi); Architectural heritage: Gommateshwara statue (981 CE), Dilwara Temples (Mount Abu), Ranakpur Temple.
11. **`CON-HIST-20`:** *Buddhism and Jainism: Comparative Synthesis, Socio-Economic Drivers & Historical Impact*
    - Master comparative matrix (Founder, Soul: Anatta vs Jiva, Asceticism: Middle Path vs Tapas, Ahimsa: Practical vs Absolute, Languages: Pali vs Ardhamagadhi, Global spread vs Subcontinental roots); Four material catalysts of Shramana revolution (Agrarian cattle preservation, merchant dignity, Kshatriya assertion, cosmopolitan urban ethics).
