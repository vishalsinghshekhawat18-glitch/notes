## 2. Canonical Concepts Created (10 Concepts across Topics 14 & 15)

### Topic 14: Part V — The Union Legislature (`the-union-legislature`, Order: 14)
1. **`CON-T14-01`**: *Parliament Architecture: Bicameralism (Art 79), Composition & Sessions*
   - Tripartite structure (President, RS, LS under Art 79); Rajya Sabha permanent house (Art 80: 238 elected + 12 nominated in Literature/Science/Art/Social Service, 6-yr term, 1/3rd retire every 2 yrs); Lok Sabha (Art 81: 543 elected via FPTP, 104th CAA 2019 Anglo-Indian repeal); Article 85 sessions (max 6-month gap; Prorogation/Dissolution by President vs Adjournment by Presiding Officer); Article 100(3) 10% quorum.
2. **`CON-T14-02`**: *Presiding Officers: Speaker vs Chairman (Arts 89, 93) & Disqualifications*
   - Speaker (elected MP, resigns to Deputy Speaker, sole Money Bill certifier under Art 110(3), presides Joint Sittings under Art 118(4), casting vote under Art 100); Chairman RS (Vice-President *ex-officio*, non-member, resigns to President); Dual Disqualification: Article 102 decided by President on binding ECI opinion (Art 103) vs 10th Schedule decided by Presiding Officer as Tribunal subject to judicial review (*Kihoto Hollohan 1992*).
3. **`CON-T14-03`**: *Legislative Enactments: Classification of Bills (Arts 107–110, 117) & Joint Sittings (Art 108)*
   - Four-tier classification (Ordinary, Money, Financial Type I, Financial Type II); Article 110 Money Bill (7 exclusive tax/debt/CFI matters; LS only; prior President nod; RS 14-day delay; President cannot return); Article 108 Joint Sitting (Speaker presides; passed by Simple Majority; **strictly barred for Money Bills and Constitutional Amendment Bills**); Article 107 rules on lapse of bills upon Lok Sabha dissolution.
4. **`CON-T14-04`**: *Parliamentary Financial Control: Budget Procedure & Cut Motions (Arts 112–116)*
   - Power of purse (Arts 265, 266(3)); Annual Financial Statement (Art 112); 6 stages (Presentation Feb 1, General Discussion, 24 DRSC Scrutiny, Voting on Demands for Grants in LS only under Art 113, Appropriation Bill under Art 114, Finance Bill); Charged vs Voted expenditure (**High Court Judges Salaries charged on State, but Pensions charged on Consolidated Fund of India**); 3 Cut Motions (Policy Disapproval ₹1, Economy specified amount, Token ₹100; passage amounts to No-Confidence); Guillotine mechanism; Vote on Account (Art 116(1)(a)).
5. **`CON-T14-05`**: *Parliamentary Standing Committees: PAC (1921), Estimates (1950), COPU (1964) & DRSCs*
   - PAC (est. 1921, 22 mem: 15 LS + 7 RS, Opposition chairman convention since 1967, examines CAG reports); Estimates Committee (est. 1950 John Mathai, **30 members ALL from Lok Sabha only**, largest committee, continuous economy); COPU (est. 1964 Krishna Menon, 22 mem: 15 LS + 7 RS); Universal rule: **Ministers strictly barred from membership**; 24 DRSCs (31 mem: 21 LS + 10 RS).
6. **`CON-T14-06`**: *Parliamentary Privileges (Art 105), Motions & Procedural Devices*
   - Article 105 individual privileges (freedom of speech, civil arrest immunity 40 days before/after session, **no immunity for criminal offences or preventive detention**); Collective privileges (in-camera sittings, contempt power upheld in *Searchlight 1959* & *Raja Ram Pal 2007*); Question Hour (Starred = oral + supplementaries; Unstarred = written, no supplementaries); Zero Hour (Indian innovation 1962); No-Confidence Motion (Rule 198, LS only, 50 MPs support, forces cabinet resignation).

### Topic 15: Part VI — The State Legislature (`the-state-legislature`, Order: 15)
7. **`CON-T15-01`**: *State Legislature Architecture & Creation/Abolition of Councils (Art 169)*
   - Article 168 (6 bicameral states: UP, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana); Vidhan Sabha strength (Art 170: max 500, min 60; exceptions Sikkim 32, Goa 40, Mizoram 40); Article 169 two-stage creation/abolition procedure: (1) State Assembly passes resolution by **Special Majority** (majority total membership + 2/3rd present & voting), (2) Parliament passes law by **Simple Majority** (**not an Article 368 amendment**); Rajasthan 2012 resolution context.
8. **`CON-T15-02`**: *Composition of Legislative Council (Art 171) & 5-Tier Electoral Formula*
   - Size limits: Max 1/3rd of Assembly strength, Minimum floor of 40 members; 5-tier composition formula: (1) 1/3rd elected by Local Authorities, (2) 1/12th by 3-year Graduates, (3) 1/12th by 3-year Teachers (secondary+), (4) 1/3rd by MLAs from non-MLAs, (5) 1/6th nominated by Governor from 5 fields: Literature, Science, Art, **Cooperative Movement** (exclusive to Governor; absent in RS Art 80(3)), Social Service.
9. **`CON-T15-03`**: *State Legislative Procedure: 4-Month Delaying Powers & Absence of Joint Sitting*
   - Structural asymmetry: Legislative Council is merely a delaying chamber with no equal powers; Article 197 two-stage ordinary bill delay (max 3 months in 1st instance + 1 month in 2nd instance = **4 months total maximum delay**); Assembly overrides Council by passing bill a second time; **Total absence of Joint Sittings in State Legislatures**; Council origin bill dies immediately if rejected by Assembly.
10. **`CON-T15-04`**: *State Legislature: Privileges (Art 194), Disqualifications (Art 191) & Rajasthan Assembly*
    - Article 194 state privileges; Article 191 disqualifications decided by Governor on binding ECI opinion (Art 192); Rajasthan Legislative Assembly: Unicameral chamber of **200 seats** (34 SC, 25 ST, 141 General); Historical evolution: 160 seats (1952) $\rightarrow$ 176 (1957) $\rightarrow$ 184 (1967) $\rightarrow$ 200 (1977); First Speaker Narottam Lal Joshi; First Leader of Opposition Jaswant Singh.

## 5. Outdated Legacy Claims Corrected & Misconception Shields

1. **Anglo-Indian Nominated Seats**: Corrected outdated claims stating Lok Sabha has 545 members (now 543 elected members following the 104th CAA 2019).
2. **Estimates Committee Composition Trap**: Reinforced that the Estimates Committee has 30 members drawn **exclusively from Lok Sabha** with zero representation from Rajya Sabha.
3. **High Court Judges Salary vs Pension**: Shielded the classic UPSC trap where High Court Judges' salaries are charged on the State Consolidated Fund, but their pensions are charged on the Consolidated Fund of India.
4. **Cooperative Movement Field in Upper Chambers**: Clarified that Governor nomination under Article 171 includes "Cooperative Movement" (5 fields), whereas Presidential nomination to Rajya Sabha under Article 80(3) includes only 4 fields (omitting Cooperative Movement).
5. **Absence of Joint Sitting in States**: Emphasized that unlike Parliament (Article 108), State Legislatures have zero constitutional provision for a Joint Sitting; the Legislative Assembly completely overrides the Council after 4 months.
6. **Civil Arrest Immunity Boundary**: Clarified that the 40-day immunity before/after sessions under Article 105/194 applies strictly to civil suits and provides zero protection against criminal charges or preventive detention.
