## 2. Granular Exam Profiles & Specific Analytical Lenses

### 1. RBI Grade B (Officers in Grade 'B' - DR)
- **Primary Papers**: Phase 1 (General Awareness), Phase 2 (Paper 1: Economic & Social Issues - ESI, Paper 3: Finance & Management - FM).
- **Depth Requirement**: **Very High / Advanced Analytical**.
- **Pedagogical Lens**: Deep macroeconomic frameworks, growth models (Solow, Harrod-Domar), IS-LM curve equilibrium, Monetary policy operational frameworks (LAF, SDF, MSF, Corridor), Inflation targeting mechanisms, Financial stability indicators (CRAR, GNPA, PCR, Liquidity Coverage Ratio - LCR), Fiscal policy debt sustainability (FRBM / NK Singh), External sector vulnerability (CAD, Forex cover, ECBs), and structural Indian economic reforms.
- **Exam Style**: 50% Objective MCQs + 50% Descriptive Analytical Writing in ESI.

### 2. SEBI Grade A (Assistant Manager - General / Stream)
- **Primary Papers**: Phase 1 (Paper 2: Economics, Commerce, Management, Finance), Phase 2 (Paper 2: Deep Economics & Financial Markets).
- **Depth Requirement**: **High / Advanced Microeconomics & Capital Markets**.
- **Pedagogical Lens**: Microeconomics core (Consumer behavior, Indifference curves, Elasticity of demand/supply, Isoquants, Cost curves, Market structures: Perfect competition, Monopoly, Monopolistic competition, Oligopoly, Cournot, Kinked demand), Capital market microstructure (Primary/Secondary markets, Book building, Clearing corporations, T+1 settlement cycle, SEBI LODR, PIT Regulations, Takeover Code), Bond mathematics (YTM, Duration, Convexity), Derivatives (Futures, Options, Swaps).
- **Exam Style**: 100% Objective Multiple Choice Questions with negative marking.

### 3. NABARD Grade A (Assistant Manager - Generalist)
- **Primary Papers**: Phase 1 (Economic & Social Issues - ESI, Agriculture & Rural Development - ARD), Phase 2 (Paper 2: ESI & ARD).
- **Depth Requirement**: **High / Specialized Rural & Agricultural Economics**.
- **Pedagogical Lens**: Agricultural economics (Landholding patterns, Agrarian distress, Capital formation in agriculture), CACP cost derivations ($A_2, A_2+FL, C_2$), MSP operational architecture, PM-AASHA procurement mechanisms, Agricultural credit flow, NABARD statutory mandate & refinance schemes (RIDF, LTIF, MIF, FSPF), Priority Sector Lending agriculture sub-targets (18% Total, 10% SF/MF), Self-Help Group Bank Linkage (SHG-BLP), Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Allied sectors (Dairy, Fisheries, Livestock census, Agro-climatic zoning).
- **Exam Style**: 50% Objective MCQs + 50% Descriptive Writing in ESI & ARD.

### 4. SBI PO & IBPS PO (Probationary Officers / Management Trainees)
- **Primary Papers**: Prelims (Quantitative Aptitude / Reasoning), Mains (General / Economy / Banking Awareness), Group Discussion / Interview.
- **Depth Requirement**: **Moderate to High / Fast Factual & Practical Banking Awareness**.
- **Pedagogical Lens**: Key economic terminology, Banking structure (Scheduled Commercial Banks, SFBs, Payment Banks, RRBs), Monetary policy rates and tools (Repo, Reverse Repo, SDF, MSF, CRR, SLR), Inflation indices (CPI, WPI, Headline vs Core), Priority Sector Lending categories, NPA classification rules (SMA-0/1/2, Sub-standard, Doubtful, Loss), Capital adequacy (Basel III minimum capital requirements), Financial inclusion schemes (PMJDY, PMSBY, PMJJBY, APY, Stand-Up India), NPCI payment systems (UPI, IMPS, NACH, AePS, NETC FASTag, CBDC e-Rupee).
- **Exam Style**: Fast-paced Objective MCQs in Mains General Awareness.

### 5. RPSC RAS (Rajasthan State & Subordinate Services)
- **Primary Papers**: Prelims (Paper 1: General Knowledge & General Science), Mains (Paper 1: Unit 2 - Indian Economy, World Economy & Economy of Rajasthan).
- **Depth Requirement**: **Advanced / Comprehensive Indian + Full Rajasthan State Economy**.
- **Pedagogical Lens**: Complete Indian Economy syllabus + Exhaustive Rajasthan State Economy:
  - **Macro Profile**: Rajasthan GSDP (current vs constant prices), GSVA sectoral distribution (Agriculture, Industry, Services), Per capita income trends.
  - **State Agriculture**: Arid & semi-arid cropping, Bajra, Mustard, Gram, Guar production, Drought proofing.
  - **Irrigation & Water**: Indira Gandhi Canal Project (IGNP), Eastern Rajasthan Canal Project (ERCP), Bisalpur Dam, Narmada Canal drip irrigation mandate, Jal Jeevan Mission.
  - **Animal Husbandry & Dairy**: Livestock Census 20th round, Indigenous breeds (Tharparkar, Rathi, Gir, Chokla), Saras Dairy cooperative network, Camel conservation.
  - **Mineral & Mining Economy**: Lead-Zinc-Silver (Rampura Agucha, Zawar), Copper (Khetri), Rock Phosphate (Jhamarkotra), Petroleum basin (Barmer-Sanchore), Pachpadra Refinery project.
  - **Industrial Infrastructure**: RIICO industrial estates, Neemrana & Ghiloth Japanese Zones, DMIC corridors, MSME Policy 2022, ODOP.
  - **Renewable Energy**: Solar Policy 2019, Bhadla Solar Park, Wind energy, Green hydrogen.
  - **State Public Finance**: Rajasthan State Budget latest trends, Fiscal deficit, State debt-to-GSDP, 6th State Finance Commission recommendations, State flagship welfare schemes (Chiranjeevi/MAA, Indira Rasoi, Indira Gandhi Urban Employment, Social pensions).
- **Exam Style**: Prelims MCQs + Mains Descriptive (2-mark 15 words, 5-mark 50 words, 10-mark 100 words short/long analytical answers).

### 6. IIBF DBF (Diploma in Banking & Finance / JAIIB IE&IFS)
- **Primary Papers**: Paper 1: Indian Economy & Indian Financial System (IE&IFS).
- **Depth Requirement**: **High / Banking Institutional & Regulatory Fundamentals**.
- **Pedagogical Lens**: Economic environment, National income aggregates, Money supply and credit creation, Monetary policy instruments, Financial markets architecture (Money market: Call, T-Bills, CDs, CPs vs Capital market: Equities, Bonds), Banking regulation (BR Act 1949, RBI Act 1934), Asset classification and provisioning norms, Basel III framework, Merchant banking, NBFC regulations, Export credit and foreign exchange regulations (FEMA 1999).
- **Exam Style**: 100% Objective Multiple Choice Questions with situational/caselet questions.

### 7. UPSC APFC (Assistant Public Provident Commissioner - EPFO)
- **Primary Papers**: Recruitment Test (Objective) + Interview.
- **Depth Requirement**: **Advanced / Core Labour Economics, Social Security & Welfare Architecture**.
- **Pedagogical Lens**: Labour economics & wage determination, Unemployment typology & measurement (PLFS indicators: UPS, UPSS, CWS, LFPR, WPR), Informal & gig economy worker protections, 4 Labour Codes 2020 (Code on Wages, Industrial Relations, OSH, Social Security Code 2020), Statutory frameworks of EPFO (EPF Act 1952, EPS 1995, EDLI 1976), ESIC (ESI Act 1948), Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, Maternity Benefit Act 2017 amendments, Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act 2008, e-Shram portal, Gender dynamics in employment (Female LFPR, wage gap, care economy), and Social security financing.
- **Exam Style**: High-level Conceptual & Statutory Objective MCQs.
