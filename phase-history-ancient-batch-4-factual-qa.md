## 1. Epistemic Classification Scheme
- **Class A (Well Established / Primary Corroboration):** Direct contemporary epigraphic, numismatic, archaeological, or undisputed classical textual evidence.
- **Class B (Reasonable but Qualified / Source-Attributed):** Scholarly consensus with necessary context, scope boundaries, or explicit source-level qualifiers.
- **Class C (Scholarly Debate / Competing Tradition):** Competing historical traditions, sectarian variants, or differing historiographical interpretations.
- **Class D (Incorrect / Historiographically Refuted):** Factual errors or superseded myths that must be rejected.
- **Class E (Unsupported / Removed):** Unverifiable speculation or generic coaching distortions.
