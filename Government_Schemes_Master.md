🪝 Context Hook — The ultimate 10-Bucket Government Schemes Masterfile for SBI PO, RBI Grade B, and IBPS PO. Includes Master Classification Framework, Central Sector vs Centrally Sponsored, Ministry Quick-Maps, Umbrella Schemes, Funding Ratios, and Master Trap Tables.


> 🧠 **Key Concept — Government Schemes Master Classification Rule**
> Central Sector Schemes are 100% funded and directly implemented by the Central Government. Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS) are funded jointly (60:40 or 90:10 for NE/Hills) and implemented by State Governments.


### 🏥 BUCKET 1: Master Framework & Core Problems


Think of the Indian government like a **hospital with many departments**:
- **Central Government:** Hospital management — sets policy, funds big programs, monitors outcomes.
- **State Governments:** Ward doctors — implement on the ground.
- **Schemes:** Treatment plans — each designed for a specific problem.


| Core Problem | Example Flagship Scheme |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Unbanked citizens (no bank accounts) | PMJDY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana) |
| 2. Farmers have no income security | PM-KISAN (₹6,000/year DBT) |
| 3. Poor have no insurance cover | PMJJBY (Life) / PMSBY (Accident) |
| 4. No housing for the poor | PMAY-Urban & PMAY-Gramin |
| 5. No skills / no employment | PMKVY 4.0 / MGNREGS |
| 6. No healthcare access | Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY |
| 7. No digital access | Digital India / BharatNet / PM WANI |


### 📊 Classification & Funding Pattern Cheat Sheet


| Scheme Type | Centre : State Funding Ratio | Implementation Body |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central Sector Schemes (e.g. PM-KISAN, MUDRA) | 100 : 0 (Centre pays all) | Central Government / Nodal Agency |
| Core Schemes - General States (e.g. PMAY, Samagra Shiksha) | 60 : 40 | State Governments |
| Core Schemes - NE & Hill States (e.g. PMGSY, NHM) | 90 : 10 | State Governments |
| Core of Core Schemes (e.g. MGNREGS wages) | 90 : 10 (100% for NE/UTs) | State Governments |

### ⚠️ Master Trap Table (Top Exam Mistakes)


| # | Common Exam Trap | Correct Exam Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | MUDRA lends directly to borrowers | MUDRA is an NBFC — banks/MFIs lend, MUDRA refinances |
| 2 | PMJJBY = accident insurance | PMJJBY = LIFE insurance (any cause death, ₹436/yr) |
| 3 | PMAY-Urban = Rural Dev Ministry | PMAY-Urban = Housing & Urban Affairs Ministry |
| 4 | PM-KUSUM = Agriculture Ministry | PM-KUSUM = Ministry of New & Renewable Energy |
| 5 | SVAMITVA = Rural Dev Ministry | SVAMITVA = Ministry of Panchayati Raj |
| 6 | UPI operated by RBI | UPI operated by NPCI, regulated by RBI |
| 7 | ONDC = MeitY | ONDC = DPIIT (Ministry of Commerce & Industry) |
| 8 | BharatNet = MeitY | BharatNet = DoT (Ministry of Communications) |
| 9 | MGNREGS = 100 days per person | MGNREGS = 100 days per HOUSEHOLD (150 days in drought areas) |
| 10 | PM-SYM = ESIC managed | PM-SYM = LIC managed (₹3,000/month pension at age 60) |
