# 🏦 BUCKET 1: Master Governance Architecture & Financial Inclusion

> 🧠 **Key Concept — Classification & Funding First Principles**
> **Central Sector Schemes (CS):** 100% funded and executed directly by the Central Government apparatus or designated central PSUs/NBFCs.
> **Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS):** Funded jointly by Centre and States (standard ratio **60:40** for general states; **90:10** for 8 North-Eastern states and 2 Himalayan states [Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh] + J&K; **100:0** for Union Territories without legislature). CSS are implemented exclusively through State Government machinery.

> 🎯 **Top Civil Services Traps for Bucket 1:**
> - **Trap 1:** Believing MUDRA lends directly to citizens. *Reality:* Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency (MUDRA) is an NBFC/refinance agency; commercial banks, RRBs, and MFIs issue the actual loans, which MUDRA refinances.
> - **Trap 2:** Confusing PMJJBY and PMSBY premium and age brackets:
>   - **PMJJBY:** Age **18–50** (exit at 55), Premium **₹436/year**, covers death by **ANY cause**.
>   - **PMSBY:** Age **18–70**, Premium **₹20/year**, covers **Accidental death/disability ONLY**.
> - **Trap 3:** Assuming Income Tax payers can subscribe to Atal Pension Yojana. *Reality:* Effective **1 October 2022**, anyone who is or has been an income tax payer is **disqualified** from opening an APY account.

> 🎯 **Top Civil Services Traps for Bucket 3:**
> - **Trap 1:** Assuming MGNREGS guarantees 100 days of work per individual. *Reality:* The guarantee is 100 days **per rural household**, not per individual worker.
> - **Trap 2:** Confusing SVAMITVA's nodal ministry. *Reality:* SVAMITVA is executed by the **Ministry of Panchayati Raj**, with technical support from the **Survey of India**, NOT the Ministry of Rural Development.
> - **Trap 3:** PMAY-G beneficiary selection. *Reality:* Selection is not done by local politicians; it is strictly audited via the **Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC 2011)** and verified by the Gram Sabha.
