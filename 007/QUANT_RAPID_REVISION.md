## 2. Master Formula Ledger (With Exact Boundary Conditions)

### 2.1 Commercial Arithmetic
1. **The Golden Pricing Bridge**:
   $$\frac{\text{MP}}{\text{CP}} = \frac{100 + P\%}{100 - D\%}$$
   *Condition*: Applicable whenever profit percentage $P\%$ and discount percentage $D\%$ are specified on the same transaction.
2. **Two Articles with Same SP and Equal $\pm x\%$ Gain/Loss**:
   $$\text{Net Loss}\% = \left(\frac{x}{10}\right)^2\% = \frac{x^2}{100}\%, \quad \text{Loss in ₹} = \frac{2 \cdot \text{SP} \cdot x^2}{10000 - x^2}$$
   *Condition*: Strictly requires **Selling Prices (SP) to be equal**. If Cost Prices are equal, net result is strictly $0\%$ (Breakeven)!
3. **Dishonest Merchant Universal Multiplier**:
   $$\text{Overall Profit Multiplier} = \left(\frac{100 + m}{100}\right) \times \left(\frac{100 - d}{100}\right) \times \left(\frac{\text{Claimed Weight}}{\text{Actual Weight}}\right)$$
   *Condition*: Valid for any combination of markup $m\%$, discount $d\%$, and faulty weight.
4. **Simple Interest Debt Installment**:
   $$n \cdot x + \frac{x \cdot R \cdot n(n-1)}{200} = A \implies x = \frac{200 \cdot A}{200n + R \cdot n(n-1)}$$
   *Condition*: $A$ is the **debt due at the end of $n$ years**. Not applicable to present borrowed loans (which require discounting).
5. **CI – SI Difference Invariants**:
   $$D_2 = P\left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2, \quad \frac{D_3}{D_2} = 3 + \frac{R}{100}$$
   *Condition*: Constant annual rate $R$ compounded annually for 2 and 3 years respectively.
6. **Repeated Dilution / Replacement Invariant**:
   $$Q_{\text{final}} = Q_{\text{initial}}\left(1 - \frac{x}{V}\right)^n$$
   *Condition*: Total volume $V$ remains constant throughout all $n$ replacement cycles.

### 2.2 Rates, Work & Motion
7. **Phantom Worker Rule (Leaving Before Completion)**:
   $$T = \frac{W_{\text{actual}} + \eta_{\text{leaver}} \cdot d}{\sum \eta_{\text{all workers}}}$$
   *Condition*: When a worker leaves $d$ days *before* total project completion. Add their hypothetical work to total work!
8. **Alternating Work / Cistern Monkey Cycle Threshold**:
   $$q = \left\lceil \frac{V - a}{a - b} \right\rceil, \quad \text{Total Time} = 2q + \frac{V - q(a - b)}{a}$$
   *Condition*: Fill rate $a$, empty rate $b$. Must evaluate cycle threshold before the final forward leap!
9. **Cross-Meeting Kinematic Law**:
   $$\frac{v_A}{v_B} = \sqrt{\frac{T_B}{T_A}}, \quad t_{\text{meeting}} = \sqrt{T_A \cdot T_B}$$
   *Condition*: Two bodies start simultaneously, meet along the path, and take $T_A, T_B$ to reach destinations.
10. **Stream Vectors & Round-Trip Average Speed**:
    $$u = \frac{v_d + v_u}{2}, \quad v = \frac{v_d - v_u}{2}, \quad v_{\text{avg}} = \frac{u^2 - v^2}{u} = \frac{v_d \cdot v_u}{u}$$
    *Condition*: Round trip over identical distance upstream and downstream in steady current.

## 3. Top 20 Instant Killer Traps to Avoid in Exam Hall

1. **BODMAS "Of" Trap**: Evaluating $20 \div 5 \text{ of } 2$ as $(20 \div 5) \times 2 = 8$. "Of" takes grouping precedence: $20 \div (5 \times 2) = 2$. But for $20 \div 5 \times 2$, evaluate left-to-right $= 8$.
2. **Modulo 100 $k=1$ Trap**: Assuming $76 \times 2^k$ ends in $2^k$ for $k=1$. $76 \times 2 = 152 \equiv 52 \neq 02$. Condition is strictly $k \ge 2$.
3. **Same SP vs Same CP Trap**: Applying $(x/10)^2\%$ loss when items have the *same CP*. If CP is equal, $+x\%$ and $-x\%$ net exactly $0\%$.
4. **Dishonest Merchant Cheated Grams Base**: Calculating gain as $\frac{200}{1000} = 20\%$ when giving $800\text{g}$ for $1\text{kg}$. Correct base is actual weight delivered: $\frac{200}{800} = 25\%$.
5. **SI Debt Installment vs Borrowed Loan**: Treating $A$ as principal borrowed today instead of future debt due at year $n$.
6. **Compound Interest Nominal vs Half-Yearly**: For half-yearly compounding, rate is $R/2$ and time periods are $2n$. Do not double the rate!
7. **Pipes and Cisterns Final Leap Overshoot**: Multiplying net cycle progress blindly when the tank fills on an inlet hour before the drain opens.
8. **Relative Speed Collinear vs Opposite**: Subtracting speeds when trains travel towards each other. Opposite directions $\implies v_1 + v_2$; Same direction $\implies |v_1 - v_2|$.
9. **Average Speed Arithmetic Mean Fallacy**: Calculating average speed as $\frac{v_1 + v_2}{2}$ for equal distances. Must use harmonic mean: $\frac{2v_1 v_2}{v_1 + v_2}$.
10. **Trains Crossing Length Omission**: Forgetting that crossing a platform or bridge covers distance $L_{\text{train}} + L_{\text{platform}}$, whereas crossing a pole covers $L_{\text{train}}$ only.
11. **Boats Still Water Speed Confusion**: Assuming upstream speed is $u$ instead of $u - v$.
12. **Semicircle Perimeter Diameter Omission**: Writing $\pi r$ instead of $\pi r + 2r = \frac{36}{7}r$.
13. **Solid Hemisphere Flat Face Omission**: Writing curved surface area $2\pi r^2$ instead of Total Surface Area $3\pi r^2$.
14. **Quadratic Leading Coefficient Scaling Omission**: Comparing raw roots $(x_1, x_2)$ and $(y_1, y_2)$ when leading coefficients $a_1 \neq a_2$. Must scale by opposing $a_i$ or divide.
15. **Data Sufficiency "Sufficiency" vs "Truth"**: Rejecting a statement because it proves the answer is definitively "NO". In DS, a definitive "NO" is 100% SUFFICIENT!
16. **Data Sufficiency Combining Without Checking Alone**: Combining Statement 1 and 2 before verifying whether Statement 1 or 2 is individually sufficient.
17. **Number Series Two-Level Difference Overlook**: Stopping at the first difference when a sequence appears erratic. Check second and third differences!
18. **Approximation Aggressive Rounding with Close Options**: Rounding $14.8$ to $15$ and $24.2$ to $24$ when options differ by only $1$ unit. Check option spacing first!
19. **Dual Pie Chart Base Mismatch**: Subtracting or adding percentages directly from two pie charts that represent different absolute totals.
20. **At Least One Probability Complement Omission**: Summing multiple complicated probability cases instead of calculating $1 - P(\text{None})$.
