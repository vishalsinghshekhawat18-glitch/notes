## 6. The 7-Step Issue Lifecycle Architecture (Standard Chapter Template)

Every contemporary topic synthesized must follow this immutable 7-step cognitive scaffolding:

1. **§ 1. Genesis & Statutory / Constitutional Foundation**:
   - The bare constitutional articles, statutory acts, or international treaties that form the bedrock of this issue.
   - Historical precedents and previous attempts/failures.
2. **§ 2. The Contemporary Catalyst & Core Trigger**:
   - What exact event occurred? (e.g. A Supreme Court verdict, a parliamentary bill passed, an RBI master direction issued, an international treaty signed).
   - Date, official title, and statutory authority involved.
3. **§ 3. First-Principles Institutional Mechanics (How it Works)**:
   - Step-by-step structural transmission mechanism.
   - Institutional flowchart and regulatory architecture.
4. **§ 4. Multidimensional Impact Matrix**:
   - Side-by-side analysis across 4 dimensions: Constitutional/Legal, Economic/Fiscal, Geopolitical/Strategic, and Societal/Ethical.
5. **§ 5. Critical Dialectic & High-Level Recommendations**:
   - Objective arguments in favor vs. counter-arguments / institutional concerns.
   - Specific recommendations of official commissions (Sarkaria, Punchhi, 2nd ARC, Law Commission, NITI Aayog).
6. **§ 6. The Multi-Exam Elimination Engine & Trap Alerts**:
   - **Prelims Traps**: Linguistic tricks, misleading ministry attributions, false voluntary vs. mandatory claims, incorrect quantitative thresholds.
   - **Mains Analytical Framework**: 200-word model structuring (Introduction $\rightarrow$ Institutional pillars $\rightarrow$ Challenges $\rightarrow$ Way forward).
7. **§ 7. 60-Second Rapid Retrieval Skeleton & Active Recall Flashcards**:
   - High-density bulleted keyword chain for instant recall.
   - 3 high-yield diagnostic questions testing edge cases.
