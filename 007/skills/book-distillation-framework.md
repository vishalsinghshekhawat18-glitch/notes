# Universal Book Distillation & Synthesis Framework (Codex Engine)

## 1. Epistemic Screening & Demarcation (Pass 0)
Classify every source into one of four epistemic tiers:
1. **Tier 1: Seminal / Empirical / Clinical Treatises**: Extract deep causal mechanics, primary data, and diagnostic criteria.
2. **Tier 2: Practical / Applied Systems**: Extract actionable workflows, step-by-step algorithms, and boundary tests.
3. **Tier 3: Repetitive / Introductory Material**: Extract the 1-2 novel ideas, discard repetitive anecdotes and filler, link to Tier 1 foundations.
4. **Tier 4: Pseudoscience / Unsubstantiated Claims**: Exclude from factual notes; flag in cautionary callouts detailing why the claim fails empirically.

---

## 2. The 6-Dimensional Distillation Matrix
For every major work distilled, incorporate:
- **Dimension 1: The Core Thesis & Paradigm Shift**: What common intuition does this overturn?
- **Dimension 2: Causal Mechanics Over Motivation**: Why does it occur at a systemic/incentive level?
- **Dimension 3: Concrete Master Analogy**: Physical or mechanical models that lock understanding.
- **Dimension 4: Diagnostic Nuances, Typologies & Edge Cases**: Comparative tables and failure modes.
- **Dimension 5: Actionable Protocols & Concrete Application**: Systematic procedures and algorithms.
- **Dimension 6: Skeptic Auditor's Demarcation Box**: Pop-culture distortion vs. empirical/statutory reality.

---

## 3. Structural & Editorial Rules
- **Self-Contained Completeness**: Define every technical term in context. Never use unexplained jargon.
- **High Signal-to-Noise**: Cut publisher filler, autobiographical fluff, and redundant examples.
- **Active Recall**: Every distillation embeds causal diagnostic questions.
