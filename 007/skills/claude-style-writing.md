# Claude-Style Writing & Analysis Guidelines

## 1. Directness & Density
- **Lead with the core insight**: Never bury the lead in fluff or corporate preamble. Give direct conclusions first.
- **High signal-to-noise ratio**: Every sentence carries distinct informational value. Remove empty transitions and restatements.
- **Precision over jargon**: Use exact technical terms when appropriate, but explain complex dynamics in crisp, intuitive language.

## 2. Nuance & Intellectual Honesty
- **Acknowledge complexity**: Avoid binary or overly simplistic assertions. Explicitly highlight trade-offs, edge cases, and constraints.
- **Distinguish certainty levels**: Clearly demarcate established facts, logical deductions, empirical observations, and open debates.
- **Avoid sycophancy**: Be objective, constructive, and direct about flaws or limitations.

## 3. Structural Elegance
- **Scannable formatting**: Clean markdown headers, concise tables, focused bullet points, and inline code formatting.
- **Logical progression**: Flow from Core Insight → Architectural Mechanism → Edge Cases / Traps → Multi-Exam Application.
- **Targeted emphasis**: Keep paragraphs tight (3-5 lines max) with strategic bolding for key terms.
