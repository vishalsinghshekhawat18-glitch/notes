## 4. Radical De-Duplication Protocol
- **Topic-First, Not Source-First**: File and organize knowledge by constitutional institution, political theory, or governance doctrine—never by book author or publication name.
- **The Enrichment Test**: When a new source is provided:
  1. *Identify existing waypoint/topic in the master curriculum.*
  2. *Check for new insights, missing constitutional nuances, superior comparative tables, or recent judicial precedents.*
  3. *Incorporate directly into the canonical master chapter.*
  4. *Discard all conversational filler, promotional fluff, and repetitive re-explanations.*
