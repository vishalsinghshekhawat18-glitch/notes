- **The Enrichment Test**: When a new source is provided:
  1. *Identify existing waypoint/era in the master curriculum.*
  2. *Check for new historical insights, missing primary evidence, superior comparative tables, or recent archaeological discoveries.*

  4. *Discard all conversational filler, promotional fluff, and repetitive re-narrations.*

## 5. Publication-Grade Book Architecture (Print-Ready Standard)
Every subject synthesized in `007/` is constructed not as casual markdown notes, but as an **elite, publication-grade academic treatise** designed for both screen and physical printouts:

### A. The Front Matter
1. **The Book Cover (`00_COVER.md`)**:
   - Aesthetic typography, formal academic subtitle, volume/edition metadata.
   - Domain insignia / decorative border and sovereign publication seal.
   - Epistemic Pledge (*"Zero Unaccounted-For Omission • Primary Source Grounding"*).
2. **Editorial Table of Contents (`01_TABLE_OF_CONTENTS.md`)**:
   - Divided into logical **Academic Parts / Modules** (e.g. *Part I: Ancient India*, *Part II: Medieval India*, *Part III: Modern India & Freedom Struggle*, *Part IV: Rajasthan History & Heritage*, *Part V: World History*, *Part VI: Capstone Grand Synthesis*).
   - Chapter titles, thematic subtitles, waypoint counts, and page/reading-time anchors.

### B. The Body Chapters
- **Running Headers & Strict Section Hierarchy**: (`# Chapter`, `## Section`, `### Historical Causation`, `#### Micro-Evidence`).
- **Publication Callout Boxes**:
  - `[HISTORICAL CAUSATION & TRANSMISSION MECHANISM]` — Causal explanation of why institutions rose, evolved, or collapsed (material conditions, agrarian surplus, technological shifts, geopolitics).
  - `[PRIMARY SOURCE & EPIGRAPHIC EVIDENCE]` — Verbatim inscriptions, archaeological sites, traveler quotes, and archival documents.
  - `[DISTINCTION MATRIX]` — Side-by-side comparative matrices contrasting easily confused dynasties, administrative ranks, architectural styles, or revolutionary factions.
  - `[EXAM TRAP & WARNING]` — Chronological inversions, false attributions, deceptively similar administrative titles, and negative-stem triggers.
- **Print Optimization**: Formatted with print page-break boundaries (`page-break-before: always`) so chapters never break awkwardly across pages when printed.

### C. The Consolidated Master Revision Chapter (The Capstone)
Every completed subject finishes with a dedicated **Final Revision Book**:
