HOW TO USE THIS BOOK?
First of all, I want you to know that it is not expected to learn each and every
words / vocabulary given in this book, so try not to mug up all the words
without thinking. This book has been divided into several parts, which
serves distinct objectives. Therefore, you need to refer and learn
vocabulary according to your needs and the requirement of the
examination.
One of the main objective of compiling this book was to provide all One Word
Substitutions asked by SSC till the very date for SSC CGL / CHSL 2018.
Hence, I have added all the new vocabulary asked in SSC exams even
conducted in the years 2019 (SSC CPO 2018 Exam, SSC GD Constable 2018,
SSC Stenographer 2018, SSC Selection Posts VI, and SSC JHT 2018).
PART A:
This is very essential part, as an SSC aspirant, you need to cover this part
compulsorily. Almost 75% plus questions asked in SSC exams are from this
part only. Recently SSC Conducted CPO, GD Constable etc exams and I
found that more than 75% of the OWS were repeated and had been asked in
previous years.
 	Part A2 gives you almost 3500 OWS, sorted alphabetically. The last
column #A indicated the number of times this word has been asked / used
in SSC Exams.
 	Part A1 is for last minute revision, which includes Top 500 OWS which
have been asked repetitively in SSC exams. This list has been
sorted based on number of times the vocabulary has been appeared.
Symbols used:
SN., SNo. SR. No. etc are denoting Serial Number.
#A: Number of times a word has been appeared / asked in SSC Exams
// : Alternate Phrases used to ask same word substitution
/ : used with either of the words.
--- [PAGE 7] ---
PART B:
This section contain vocabulary frequently used in The Hindu Newspaper.
It is often seen that many of these vocabularies appear in one exam or other.
Moreover, it contains some of the legal or technical words that you are
expected to know as an educated person. A great knowledge of this part not
only increases your vocabulary but it will help you to read faster. Yes, you
will read the Hindu much faster and this will serve a boon for your Reading
Comprehension (Passage, Cloze Tests etc) part of Competitive exams.
PART C:
This section contains a list of special technical vocabularies which are
frequently asked in SSC and other competitive examinations. These terms
are technical and you need to pay little attention in this section. It may be
noted that though some of the vocabularies are more frequent, while some
are rarely used. It is advisable to use selective reading, if you have time
constraint. Do not attempt to mug up all the vocabulary (in one go).
PART D:
This is practice section and includes 50 short tests to gauge your learning.
This is mandatory part for SSC aspirants and includes recently asked One
Word Substitution MCQs of SSC.
PART E:
This is an appendix and is very useful to understand etymology. Once you
learn the meaning of a root word it would be very easy to learn similar words
and even to guess the words in exam when you have not studied it. This part
is again for self-learning which must be gradual. Do not try to cover this
part completely for Tier 1 of CGL 2018, though your gradual learning will
help you in Tier 2 and many other exams.
