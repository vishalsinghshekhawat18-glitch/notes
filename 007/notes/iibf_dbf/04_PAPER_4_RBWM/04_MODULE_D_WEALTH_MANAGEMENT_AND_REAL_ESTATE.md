## 📊 1. Master Matrix: SEBI AIF Categories & Portfolio Management Services

| Investment Vehicle | Governing Regulator & Min Ticket Size | Investment Focus & Strategy | Examples / Sponsoring Structures |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PMS (Discretionary) | SEBI (**Minimum ₹50 Lakhs**) | Portfolio manager manages funds with full discretion on security selection and timing | HNWI customized equity/debt portfolios |
| PMS (Non-Discretionary) | SEBI (**Minimum ₹50 Lakhs**) | Portfolio manager suggests ideas; trades executed strictly upon client's prior approval | Advisory portfolios |
| AIF Category I | SEBI (**Minimum ₹1 Crore**) | Invests in start-ups, early stage ventures, social ventures, infrastructure, and SMEs | Venture Capital Funds (VCF), Angel Funds, Social Venture Funds |

| AIF Category III | SEBI (**Minimum ₹1 Crore**) | Funds employing diverse or complex trading strategies, arbitrage, and leverage | Hedge Funds, PIPE (Private Investment in Public Equity) Funds |

## 🏗️ 2. Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA)

| Dimension | RERA Statutory Rule / Requirement | Impact on Housing Finance & Home Buyers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mandatory Registration | All commercial and residential projects with land area **\ge 500\text{ sq meters}** or **\ge 8\text{ apartments}** must be registered with RERA | Banks cannot finance unregistered real estate projects. |
| Dedicated Escrow Account | Promoter must deposit **70% of all amounts realized from buyers** in a separate dedicated bank account | Funds can be withdrawn only in proportion to project percentage completion certified by architect/engineer/CA. |
| Carpet Area Mandate | Units must be sold strictly based on **Carpet Area** (usable floor area inside walls), NOT super-built-up area | Eliminates inflated builder area claims. |
| Defect Liability Period | Promoter is liable to rectify structural defects without charge for **5 years** from date of handing over possession | Enhanced physical security for bank mortgage collateral. |

> 🎯 **Exam Anchor & Trap:**
> 🎯 Top Exam Traps on Wealth Management & RERA:
1. **PMS Minimum Ticket Size:** Under SEBI norms, the minimum investment ticket size for Portfolio Management Services (PMS) is **₹50 Lakhs** (revised from ₹25L in 2020).
2. **AIF Minimum Ticket Size:** Minimum investment in Alternate Investment Funds (AIF) is **₹1 Crore**.
3. **RERA Escrow Account Rule:** RERA mandates that **70% of funds realized from buyers** must be deposited in a separate dedicated bank account used strictly for construction and land costs.
4. **RERA Registration Thresholds:** Mandatory registration applies to projects exceeding **500 sq meters** or **8 apartments**.
