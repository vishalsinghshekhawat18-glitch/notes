## 74. IIBF RBWM Unit 1: Retail Banking: Characteristics, Business Models & Segmentation

> 🧠 **Key Concept — Pivotal Concept: Retail Banking Architecture**
> Retail banking involves banking services provided directly to individual consumers and small businesses rather than corporate entities or institutional clients.

## 📊 1. Master Matrix: Retail vs Corporate / Wholesale Banking

| Parameter | Retail Banking | Corporate / Wholesale Banking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target Customer | Individual consumers, Households, Small Businesses | Large Corporates, Multinational Companies, PSUs, Institutional Borrowers |
| Transaction Volume & Ticket Size | High transaction volume, Small individual ticket size | Low transaction volume, Very large ticket size |
| Risk Concentration | **Granular / Low** (Risk spread across millions of borrowers) | **High** (Default of single corporate borrower impacts bank capital) |
| Pricing & Spreads | Higher interest spreads / margins | Competitive, finer pricing / lower interest spreads |
| Delivery Mode | Multi-channel (Branch, ATM, Mobile, Internet, BCs) | Dedicated Relationship Managers (RMs) and specialized corporate branches |
| Processing Cost | Higher per-unit administrative and processing cost | Lower per-unit processing cost due to bulk scale |

## 👥 2. Customer Segmentation Tiers in Retail Banking

| Segment | Annual Income / Wealth Range | Primary Products & Delivery Channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mass Banking | Annual Income up to ₹10 Lakhs | Basic savings accounts, PMJDY, micro-loans, digital channels (UPI, Mobile Banking) |
| Mass Affluent | Annual Income ₹10 Lakhs to ₹50 Lakhs | Housing loans, Auto loans, Credit cards, Term deposits, Mutual fund SIPs |
| Super Affluent / Emerging HNWIs | Annual Income ₹50 Lakhs to ₹2 Crores | Wealth management, Priority banking lounges, customized investment portfolios |
| High Net Worth Individuals (HNWIs) | Investible Surplus ₹2 Crores to ₹25 Crores | Dedicated Private Banking, Portfolio Management Services (PMS), Estate Planning |
| Ultra HNWIs | Investible Surplus > ₹25 Crores | Family Office services, Alternate Investment Funds (AIFs), Global asset diversification |

> 🎯 **Exam Anchor & Trap:**
> 🎯 Top Exam Traps on Retail Banking Characteristics:
1. **Risk Concentration in Retail:** Retail banking has **LOW credit concentration risk** because advances are distributed across a massive base of individual borrowers.
2. **SBU Model:** The Strategic Business Unit (SBU) approach creates autonomous profit centers with dedicated operational heads for retail, corporate, and treasury divisions.
3. **CASA Deposits Significance:** Retail deposits (CASA) provide **low-cost, sticky funds** that reduce interest rate sensitivity compared to volatile wholesale bulk deposits.
