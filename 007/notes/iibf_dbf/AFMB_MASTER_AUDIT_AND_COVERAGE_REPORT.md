| **ERR-05** | **NPV vs IRR Rule** | "NPV must ALWAYS be adopted." | "For mutually exclusive projects with equal scale and life, NPV is generally preferred because it measures absolute economic value creation; however, method selection depends on project constraints, capital rationing, and specific decision context." | Brealey, Myers & Allen Corporate Finance | `[CONCEPTUAL FACT]` |
| **ERR-06** | **Statutory Reserve Transfer** | Ambiguous 20% vs 25% transfer to Statutory Reserve. | Section 17(1) Banking Regulation Act 1949 establishes a statutory minimum of 20% of net profits; RBI prudential Master Direction enforces an operational benchmark of 25%. | Banking Regulation Act 1949 §17(1); RBI Master Direction on Financial Statements | `[CURRENT REGULATION]` |
| **ERR-07** | **Working Capital Benchmark** | "Current Ratio must be strictly 1.33:1 under RBI rules." | Current ratio of 1.33:1 is a HISTORICAL / EXAM-CONVENTIONAL benchmark originating from the Tandon Committee (Method 2), now utilized by commercial banks as an internal underwriting guideline rather than a universal statutory RBI minimum. | Tandon Committee Report (1975); RBI Master Circular on Working Capital | `[EXAM-CONVENTIONAL]` |
| **ERR-08** | **Cash Budget & Depreciation** | "Depreciation is completely excluded from cash budgets." | Depreciation is a non-cash expense and therefore does not represent a cash outflow in cash budgets; however, cash budgets directly reflect the actual cash inflows and outflows from asset acquisitions, capital expenditures, and salvage realizations. | Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA); IIBF Courseware | `[CONCEPTUAL FACT]` |
