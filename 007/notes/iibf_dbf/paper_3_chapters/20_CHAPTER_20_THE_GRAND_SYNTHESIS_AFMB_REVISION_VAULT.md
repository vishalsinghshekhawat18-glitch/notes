### Matrix A: Core Mathematical & Corporate Finance Formulas

| Domain | Parameter / Metric | Mathematical Formula | Examination Anchor & Key Caveat |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TVM** | Effective Annual Rate (EAR) | $\text{EAR} = \left(1 + \frac{r}{m}\right)^m - 1$ | Intra-year compounding makes EAR strictly greater than nominal rate $r$. |
| **TVM** | Continuous Compounding FV | $\text{FV} = \text{PV} \times e^{rt}$ | As $m \to \infty$, compounding factor converges to $e^{rt}$ ($e \approx 2.71828$). |
| **TVM** | Rule of 72 / 114 / 144 | $t_2 \approx \frac{72}{r}, \quad t_3 \approx \frac{114}{r}, \quad t_4 \approx \frac{144}{r}$ | Approximation shortcuts for doubling, tripling, and quadrupling capital. |
| **Annuity** | Ordinary Annuity FV | $\text{FVA} = C \times \left[ \frac{(1+r)^n - 1}{r} \right]$ | Cash flows occur at the end of each payment period. |
| **Annuity** | Annuity Due Factor | $\text{Value (Annuity Due)} = \text{Value (Ordinary Annuity)} \times (1 + r)$ | Cash flows occur at the beginning of each period; earns 1 extra interval. |
| **Annuity** | Perpetuity PV | $\text{PV} = \frac{C}{r} \quad \left(\text{Growing: } \frac{C_1}{r - g}\right)$ | Stream of perpetual payments. Capitalization rate must exceed growth ($r > g$). |
| **Loans** | Equated Monthly Installment | $\text{EMI} = P \times r \times \frac{(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}$ | $r$ must be monthly interest ($\frac{\text{annual rate}}{1200}$); $n$ in months. |
| **Bonds** | YTM Approximation | $\text{YTM} \approx \frac{C + \frac{M - P}{n}}{\frac{M + P}{2}} \times 100$ | $C$ = annual coupon, $M$ = face/par value, $P$ = market price, $n$ = years. |
| **Bonds** | Modified Duration (MD) | $MD = \frac{\text{Macaulay Duration}}{1 + YTM}$ | Strictly positive measure. Price volatility: $\frac{\Delta P}{P} \approx -MD \times \Delta y$. |
| **Bonds** | Zero-Coupon Bond Duration | $D = n$ | Macaulay duration of zero-coupon bond equals its exact maturity period. |
| **Capital** | After-Tax Cost of Debt | $K_d = I \times (1 - t)$ | Reflects debt tax shield. Preference share dividend is NOT tax deductible. |
| **Capital** | Cost of Equity (CAPM) | $K_e = R_f + \beta \times (R_m - R_f)$ | $R_f$ = risk-free rate, $\beta$ = systematic risk, $(R_m - R_f)$ = market risk premium. |
| **Leverage** | Degree of Operating Leverage | $\text{DOL} = \frac{\text{Contribution}}{\text{EBIT}} = \frac{S - V}{\text{EBIT}}$ | Quantifies business risk. If fixed costs = 0, $\text{DOL} = 1.0$. |
| **Leverage** | Degree of Financial Leverage | $\text{DFL} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{EBT}} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{EBIT} - I}$ | Quantifies financial risk. If interest = 0, $\text{DFL} = 1.0$. |
| **Leverage** | Combined Leverage (DCL) | $\text{DCL} = \text{DOL} \times \text{DFL} = \frac{\text{Contribution}}{\text{EBT}}$ | Total risk amplification. |
| **Working Cap** | Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) | $\text{CCC} = \text{ICP} + \text{RCP} - \text{PDP}$ | Inventory Conversion + Receivables Collection - Payables Deferral Period. |
| **Working Cap** | Tandon Method 2 MPBF | $\text{MPBF} = 0.75 \times (\text{CA} - \text{CL other than bank borrowings})$ | Imposes minimum Current Ratio benchmark of 1.33:1. |
| **Working Cap** | Nayak Turnover Method | $\text{Total WC} = 25\% \times \text{Turnover}; \quad \text{Bank Loan} \ge 20\%$ | Applies to SSI / MSE units with credit limits up to ₹5 Crores. |
| **Costing** | P/V Ratio & Break-Even | $\text{P/V} = \frac{C}{S} \times 100; \quad \text{BEP (value)} = \frac{F}{\text{P/V}}$ | Fundamental marginal costing equilibrium: $S - V = F + P$. |
| **Costing** | Margin of Safety (MoS) | $\text{MoS} = \text{Actual Sales} - \text{BEP Sales} = \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{P/V}}$ | Cushion against operational losses. Total Sales = BEP + MoS. |
| **Variances** | Material Cost Variance | $MCV = (SQ \times SP) - (AQ \times AP)$ | $MCV = MPV [AQ \times (SP - AP)] + MUV [SP \times (SQ - AQ)]$. |
| **Variances** | Labour Cost Variance | $LCV = (SH \times SR) - (AH \times AR)$ | $LCV = LRV [AH \times (SR - AR)] + LEV [SR \times (SH - AH)]$. |

## 2. 100 VERIFIED EXAMINER TRAPS & PITFALLS FOR AFMB

1. **Closing Stock Valuation:** Valuing closing stock at cost or net realizable value, whichever is lower, is mandated by the **Prudence (Conservatism) Concept**, not the Materiality Concept.
2. **Business Entity Concept:** The owner's capital is recorded as a liability in the balance sheet strictly because the enterprise is recognized as a legal entity distinct from its proprietor.
3. **Representative Personal Account:** Outstanding Rent, Prepaid Insurance, Accrued Interest, and Unearned Commission are **Representative Personal Accounts**, never Nominal Accounts.
4. **Father of Bookkeeping:** Luca Pacioli published *Summa de Arithmetica* in Venice in **1494**, documenting the double-entry accounting method.
5. **Error of Principle:** Charging installation wages of a new generator to the Wages Account is an Error of Principle; it **does NOT cause the Trial Balance to disagree**.
6. **Compensating Errors:** Two independent errors of equal amount on opposite sides offset each other and **do NOT disturb the Trial Balance tally**.
7. **Suspense Account Placement:** A debit balance in the Suspense Account represents an excess of credits over debits in the ledger and is presented on the **Assets side** of the Balance Sheet.
8. **Rectification in Subsequent Period:** Rectification of nominal accounts after the close of the financial year is routed through the **Profit & Loss Adjustment Account** to protect current-year operating profit.
9. **Bank Passbook Debit Balance:** A debit balance in the customer's passbook indicates a liability to the bank, representing an **Overdraft**.
10. **Adjusted Cash Book Rule:** Bank charges, direct collections by bank, and dishonored cheques must be corrected first in the **Adjusted Cash Book** before preparing the BRS; timing differences (unpresented/uncredited cheques) remain in the BRS.
11. **Freehold Land Depreciation:** Freehold land has an indefinite economic life and is **never depreciated** under AS 10 / Ind AS 16. Leasehold land is amortized over the lease term.
12. **Income Tax Depreciation Method:** Under direct tax statutes (Income-tax Act 1961 / 2025 framework), depreciation is admissible strictly on the **Written Down Value (WDV) method on Blocks of Assets**, except for power-generating units.
13. **The 180-Day Rule:** Under Section 32 of direct tax law, if a new asset is put to use for **fewer than 180 days** in the acquisition year, only **50% of the normal depreciation rate** is deductible.
14. **Depreciation Estimate Change:** Under Ind AS 16 and revised AS 10, a change in depreciation method or useful life is treated as a **change in accounting estimate** and applied **prospectively**, not retrospectively.
15. **Days of Grace:** Section 22 of the NI Act allows **3 days of grace** exclusively for usance promissory notes and bills of exchange; cheques and demand bills receive **zero grace days**.
16. **Maturity on Public Holiday:** When the maturity of a negotiable usance instrument falls on a public holiday (Sunday or gazetted holiday under §25), it becomes due on the **Immediately Preceding Business Day**.
17. **Maturity on Emergency Holiday:** When maturity falls on an unexpected public holiday declared under emergency conditions, the instrument becomes due on the **Immediately Succeeding Business Day**.
18. **Accommodation Bill Status:** An accommodation bill is drawn, accepted, or endorsed without consideration to extend financial accommodation; an accommodation party is liable to a holder in due course, but not to the accommodated party.
19. **Rebate on Bills Discounted Schedule:** In a bank balance sheet, Rebate on Bills Discounted (unearned discount) is disclosed under **Schedule 5 (Other Liabilities & Provisions)**.
20. **Rebate on Bills Discounted Calculation:** Discount is calculated strictly for the **unexpired period falling in the subsequent financial year**: $\text{Face Value} \times \text{Rate} \times \frac{\text{Days after 31 March}}{365}$.
21. **Effective Annual Rate vs Nominal Rate:** The Effective Annual Rate (EAR) is **strictly higher** than the nominal stated interest rate whenever compounding occurs more frequently than once a year ($m > 1$).
22. **Continuous Compounding Factor:** As compounding intervals approach infinity, the future value factor converges to $e^{rt}$ ($e \approx 2.71828$).
23. **Rule of 72 Applicability:** The Rule of 72 provides doubling time $t \approx \frac{72}{r}$; for tripling time, use the **Rule of 114** ($t \approx \frac{114}{r}$); for quadrupling time, use the **Rule of 144** ($t \approx \frac{144}{r}$).
24. **Annuity Due Timing:** Cash flows in an Annuity Due occur at the **beginning of each interval**; its present or future value is obtained by multiplying the ordinary annuity value by $(1 + r)$.
25. **Perpetuity PV Formula:** The present value of an ordinary perpetuity is $PV = \frac{C}{r}$; for a perpetuity growing at constant rate $g$, $PV = \frac{C_1}{r - g}$ (valid only when $r > g$).
26. **Sinking Fund Factor:** A Sinking Fund represents an ordinary annuity accumulated to replace an asset or redeem debt at maturity; its periodic payment is $C = \text{Target Future Sum} \times \frac{r}{(1+r)^n - 1}$.
27. **Zero-Coupon Bond Duration:** The Macaulay duration of a zero-coupon bond **always equals its exact time to maturity** ($D = n$) because there are no intermediate coupon cash flows.
28. **Bond Price vs Yield Relationship:** Bond prices and market interest yields have an **inverse relationship**. When market yields rise, bond prices fall.
29. **Modified Duration Sign:** Modified Duration is mathematically **positive**: $MD = \frac{D}{1 + YTM}$. The negative sign is applied only when expressing percentage price change: $\frac{\Delta P}{P} \approx -MD \times \Delta y$.
30. **Coupon Rate vs YTM:** When a bond sells at par, Coupon Rate = Current Yield = YTM. When selling at discount, $\text{Coupon Rate} < \text{Current Yield} < \text{YTM}$. When selling at premium, $\text{Coupon Rate} > \text{Current Yield} > \text{YTM}$.
31. **Form A Schedules:** Commercial banks prepare their Balance Sheet under Form A comprising **Schedules 1 through 12**.
32. **Form B Schedules:** Commercial banks prepare their Profit & Loss Account under Form B comprising **Schedules 13 through 16**.
33. **Bills for Collection Status:** Bills for Collection are **not added to the bank balance sheet totals**; they appear as a memorandum footnote beneath Schedule 12.
34. **Letters of Credit and Guarantees:** Contingent liabilities such as Letters of Credit and Bank Guarantees appear exclusively in **Schedule 12**, outside the balance sheet footings.
35. **Non-Banking Assets Holding Period:** Non-Banking Assets acquired in satisfaction of claims (Section 9 BR Act) are recorded in **Schedule 11** and must be disposed of within **7 years**.
36. **Section 17 Statutory Reserve:** Section 17(1) of the BR Act mandates transferring at least **20% of net profits** to Statutory Reserve; RBI operational guidelines enforce **25%**.
37. **Provisions and Contingencies Line:** In Form B Profit & Loss Account, Provisions and Contingencies is an **unnumbered direct line item** in Section II (Expenditure).
38. **Direct Forex Quotation Mandate:** Effective **2 August 1993**, India adopted the **Direct Quotation** system, where exchange rates express the variable amount of home currency (INR) per fixed unit of foreign currency.
39. **Ascending Forward Margins:** In direct quotation, ascending forward margins (e.g., 0.20 / 0.25) indicate the foreign currency is at a **Premium** and must be **ADDED** to spot rates.
40. **Descending Forward Margins:** In direct quotation, descending forward margins (e.g., 0.30 / 0.20) indicate the foreign currency is at a **Discount** and must be **SUBTRACTED** from spot rates.
41. **Bank Buying vs Selling Rates:** The fundamental forex operational maxim: **"Bank Buys Low, Bank Sells High"**. In a quotation USD/INR 84.10 / 84.20, bank buys at 84.10 and sells at 84.20.
42. **Capital Budgeting Cash Flows:** Project appraisal evaluates **Cash Flow After Tax** ($\text{CFAT} = \text{PAT} + \text{Depreciation}$), never accounting profit.
43. **Sunk Cost Exclusion:** Historical sunk costs (e.g., past feasibility survey fees) are **strictly excluded** from capital budgeting appraisal.
44. **NPV vs IRR Conflict Resolution:** When evaluating mutually exclusive projects where NPV and IRR rankings conflict, **NPV must always be preferred** because it maximizes absolute shareholder wealth.
45. **Reinvestment Rate Assumptions:** NPV assumes cash inflows are reinvested at the **Cost of Capital ($k$)**, whereas IRR assumes reinvestment at the project's **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**.
46. **Profitability Index Benchmark:** A project is acceptable under the Profitability Index (PI) rule if $PI = \frac{\text{PV of Inflows}}{\text{Initial Outlay}} > 1.0$.
47. **Payback Period Flaw:** The traditional Payback Period ignores cash flows occurring after the payback cutoff and disregards the **time value of money**.
48. **After-Tax Cost of Debt:** Because corporate interest is tax-deductible, after-tax cost of debt is $K_d = I \times (1 - t)$. Preference share dividends are not tax-deductible, so $K_p = \frac{D_p}{P_0}$.
49. **CAPM Beta Definition:** In the CAPM formula $K_e = R_f + \beta(R_m - R_f)$, Beta ($\beta$) measures **systematic (non-diversifiable) risk**, not unsystematic risk.
50. **Operating Leverage at Zero Fixed Cost:** When fixed operating costs are zero, the Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL) is **1.0**, never zero.
51. **Financial Leverage at Zero Interest:** When debt interest is zero, the Degree of Financial Leverage (DFL) is **1.0**, never zero.
52. **Combined Leverage Formula:** Combined Leverage equals Operating Leverage multiplied by Financial Leverage ($\text{DCL} = \text{DOL} \times \text{DFL} = \frac{\text{Contribution}}{\text{EBT}}$).
53. **EBIT-EPS Indifference Point:** The level of EBIT at which Earnings Per Share (EPS) is identical under two alternative financing structures is the **Indifference Point**.
54. **Current Ratio Benchmark:** The historical Tandon Committee Method 2 sets a minimum benchmark **Current Ratio of 1.33:1**.
55. **Quick Assets Composition:** Quick Assets exclude **Inventories and Prepaid Expenses** from Current Assets ($\text{Quick Assets} = \text{CA} - \text{Inventories} - \text{Prepaids}$).
56. **DSCR Inclusion:** Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) includes **both Interest and Principal Installments** in the denominator, whereas Interest Coverage evaluates Interest alone.
57. **Tangible Net Worth Computation:** Tangible Net Worth (TNW) equals Paid-up Capital plus Free Reserves **minus Intangible Assets (Goodwill, Patents) and accumulated losses**.
58. **Operating Cycle vs Cash Cycle:** The Cash Conversion Cycle equals Operating Cycle minus Payables Deferral Period ($\text{CCC} = \text{ICP} + \text{RCP} - \text{PDP}$).
59. **Nayak Committee Working Capital:** For SSI/MSE units with credit limits up to ₹5 Crores, total working capital requirement is assessed at minimum **25% of projected annual turnover**, with bank financing at least **20%**.
60. **Factoring vs Forfaiting Recourse:** Factoring can be with or without recourse and applies to short-term receivables; Forfaiting applies to medium/long-term export bills and is **strictly 100% without recourse**.
61. **Financial Lease Asset Ownership:** In a Finance Lease under Ind AS 116, the lessee recognizes a **Right-of-Use (RoU) Asset** and a lease liability on its balance sheet.
62. **Operating Lease Characteristics:** An operating lease is a short-term, cancellable lease where the lessor bears maintenance, taxes, and risks of obsolescence.
63. **Forward vs Futures Settlement:** Forward contracts are private OTC bilateral agreements with credit risk; Futures contracts are exchange-traded, standardized, and settled daily via **Mark-to-Market (MTM)** through a clearinghouse.
64. **Interest Rate Swap Cash Flow:** In a plain-vanilla Interest Rate Swap (IRS), only **net differential interest cash flows** are exchanged based on a notional principal; the principal is never exchanged.
65. **Call Option In-the-Money:** A Call Option is In-the-Money (ITM) when the spot market price of the underlying asset exceeds the strike price ($S > X$).
66. **Put Option In-the-Money:** A Put Option is In-the-Money (ITM) when the strike price exceeds the spot market price ($X > S$).
67. **Option Buyer vs Writer Risk:** An option buyer has **limited loss (premium paid)** and unlimited profit potential; an option writer (seller) has **limited profit (premium received)** and unlimited loss exposure.
68. **Fixed Cost per Unit Behavior:** Total fixed cost remains constant within a relevant range, but **fixed cost per unit decreases** as production volume expands.
69. **Variable Cost per Unit Behavior:** Variable cost per unit remains **constant**, whereas total variable cost changes in direct proportion to production volume.
70. **Contribution Equation:** Contribution equals Sales minus Variable Cost ($C = S - V$) and also equals Fixed Cost plus Profit ($C = F + P$).
71. **P/V Ratio Invariant:** P/V Ratio remains unchanged if selling price per unit and variable cost per unit remain constant, regardless of production volume shifts.
72. **P/V Ratio Two-Period Formula:** Across two financial periods, $\text{P/V Ratio} = \frac{\Delta \text{Profit}}{\Delta \text{Sales}} \times 100 = \frac{\Delta \text{Contribution}}{\Delta \text{Sales}} \times 100$.
73. **Break-Even Point Units vs Value:** $\text{BEP (units)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{Contribution per unit}}$; $\text{BEP (value)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{P/V Ratio}}$.
74. **Cash Break-Even Point:** Cash BEP covers only cash fixed costs: $\text{Cash BEP (units)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost} - \text{Depreciation}}{\text{Contribution per unit}}$.
75. **Margin of Safety Formula:** $\text{Margin of Safety (value)} = \text{Actual Sales} - \text{BEP Sales} = \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{P/V Ratio}}$.
76. **Sales Decomposition:** Total Sales equals Break-Even Sales plus Margin of Safety ($\text{Sales} = \text{BES} + \text{MoS}$).
77. **Profit via MoS:** Operating profit equals Margin of Safety multiplied by P/V Ratio ($\text{Profit} = \text{MoS} \times \text{P/V}$).
78. **Key Factor Allocation:** When production resources are constrained, prioritize products yielding the highest **Contribution per unit of Limiting Factor**.
79. **Shutdown Point Formula:** $\text{Shutdown Sales (units)} = \frac{\text{Avoidable Fixed Costs}}{\text{Contribution per unit}} = \frac{\text{Total Fixed Cost} - \text{Unavoidable Fixed Cost}}{\text{Contribution per unit}}$.
80. **Absorption vs Marginal Profit Equilibrium:** When production exceeds sales, closing stock exceeds opening stock, and **Absorption Costing Profit > Marginal Costing Profit** because fixed overhead is capitalized in inventory.
81. **Profit Reconciliation Identity:** $\text{Absorption Profit} - \text{Marginal Profit} = (\text{Closing Inventory Units} - \text{Opening Inventory Units}) \times \text{Fixed Overhead Absorption Rate per unit}$.
82. **Material Price Variance Basis:** Material Price Variance is evaluated strictly on **Actual Quantity consumed/purchased**: $\text{MPV} = \text{AQ} \times (\text{SP} - \text{AP})$.
83. **Material Usage Variance Basis:** Material Usage Variance is valued strictly at the **Standard Price**: $\text{MUV} = \text{SP} \times (\text{SQ} - \text{AQ})$.
84. **Material Cost Variance Identity:** $\text{MCV} = \text{MPV} + \text{MUV} = (\text{SQ} \times \text{SP}) - (\text{AQ} \times \text{AP})$.
85. **Labour Rate Variance Basis:** Labour Rate Variance is calculated on **Actual Hours worked/paid**: $\text{LRV} = \text{AH} \times (\text{SR} - \text{AR})$.
86. **Labour Efficiency Variance Basis:** Labour Efficiency Variance is valued at the **Standard Rate**: $\text{LEV} = \text{SR} \times (\text{SH} - \text{AH})$.
87. **Idle Time Variance Nature:** Idle Time Variance ($\text{Abnormal Idle Hours} \times \text{Standard Rate}$) is **always Adverse (A)**.
88. **Flexible Budget Purpose:** A Flexible Budget recalculates revenue and costs based on the **actual level of activity achieved**, enabling meaningful variance analysis.
89. **Zero-Based Budgeting Origin:** Zero-Based Budgeting (ZBB) was introduced by **Peter Pyhrr** and requires justifying every budget rupee from scratch via Decision Packages.
90. **Cash Budget Depreciation Rule:** Depreciation is a non-cash expense and is **strictly excluded from Cash Budgets**.
91. **Section 194A General TDS Limit:** Annual bank interest threshold for mandatory TDS under Section 194A is **₹40,000** for general individual depositors across all branches of a bank.
92. **Section 194A Senior Citizen Limit:** Annual bank interest threshold for Senior Citizens (age 60+ years) under Section 194A read with Section 80TTB is **₹50,000**.
93. **Section 206AA PAN Default:** If a payee fails to furnish a valid PAN, TDS must be deducted at the penal rate of **20%** (or applicable rate, whichever is higher).
94. **Form 15G Qualification:** Form 15G is valid for resident individuals aged **under 60 years** whose estimated total taxable income does not exceed the basic tax exemption limit.
95. **Form 15H Qualification:** Form 15H is strictly for **Senior Citizens (60+ years)** whose estimated final tax liability on total income is **NIL**, even if interest exceeds the basic exemption limit.
96. **Section 194N Cash TDS General:** TDS at **2%** applies to aggregate cash withdrawals exceeding **₹1 Crore** in a financial year from one or more accounts in a bank.
97. **Section 194N Non-Filer Rates:** For non-filers of income tax returns (preceding 3 assessment years), TDS is **2% between ₹20 Lakhs and ₹1 Crore**, and **5% above ₹1 Crore**.
98. **Bank GST Standard Rate:** Taxable fee-based banking services (processing fees, locker rent, documentation charges, ATM fees beyond cap) attract standard GST at **18%**.
99. **GST Exemption for Pure Interest:** Interest or discount earned on loans, advances, or deposits is **completely exempt from GST**.
100. **Section 17(4) CGST Bank Option:** Commercial banks have an annual statutory option to claim a **flat 50% of eligible monthly Input Tax Credit (ITC)**, with the remaining 50% lapsing.

## 4. 50 NUMERICAL TRIGGERS & CALCULATION SHORTCUTS

1. **Doubling Time:** At $r\%$, doubling period $t \approx \frac{72}{r}$. At $8\%$, $t \approx 9 \text{ years}$; at $12\%$, $t \approx 6 \text{ years}$.
2. **Tripling Time:** At $r\%$, tripling period $t \approx \frac{114}{r}$. At $10\%$, $t \approx 11.4 \text{ years}$.
3. **Quadrupling Time:** At $r\%$, quadrupling period $t \approx \frac{144}{r}$. At $12\%$, $t \approx 12 \text{ years}$.
4. **Annuity Due Value:** Take Ordinary Annuity result and multiply directly by $(1 + r)$.
5. **Perpetuity Present Value:** Divide periodic annual cash flow by annual discount rate: $PV = \frac{C}{r}$.
6. **Growing Perpetuity PV:** Divide first period cash flow by $(r - g)$: $PV = \frac{C_1}{r - g}$.
7. **Bond Market Price at Par:** When Coupon Rate = YTM, $\text{Price} = \text{Face Value}$.
8. **Bond Duration Zero Coupon:** Macaulay Duration equals bond maturity ($D = n$).
9. **Bond Price Change via Modified Duration:** $\%\Delta P \approx -MD \times \Delta y$. For $MD = 4.5$ and $+50\text{ bps}$ shift, $\Delta P \approx -4.5 \times 0.5\% = -2.25\%$.
10. **After-Tax Debt Cost:** $K_d = I(1 - t)$. For $12\%$ coupon and $30\%$ tax, $K_d = 12 \times 0.70 = 8.4\%$.
11. **Cost of Equity via CAPM:** $K_e = R_f + \beta(R_m - R_f)$. If $R_f = 7\%$, $\beta = 1.25$, Equity Risk Premium $= 8\%$, $K_e = 7 + 1.25(8) = 17\%$.
12. **Operating Leverage:** $\text{DOL} = \frac{\text{Contribution}}{\text{EBIT}}$. If Sales = 100, Var Cost = 60, Fixed Cost = 20: $\text{Contrib} = 40$, $\text{EBIT} = 20 \implies \text{DOL} = 2.0$.
13. **Financial Leverage:** $\text{DFL} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{EBT}}$. If EBIT = 20, Interest = 5: $\text{EBT} = 15 \implies \text{DFL} = \frac{20}{15} = 1.33$.
14. **Combined Leverage:** $\text{DCL} = \text{DOL} \times \text{DFL} = 2.0 \times 1.33 = 2.67$.
15. **Break-Even Units:** $\text{Fixed Cost} / \text{Contribution per unit}$. For $F = 50,000$, $C = 10 \implies 5,000 \text{ units}$.
16. **Break-Even Sales Value:** $\text{Fixed Cost} / \text{P/V Ratio}$. For $F = 50,000$, $\text{P/V} = 25\% \implies ₹2,00,000$.
17. **Margin of Safety Value:** $\text{Actual Sales} - \text{BEP Sales}$. If Actual = 3,00,000 and BEP = 2,00,000, $\text{MoS} = ₹1,00,000$.
18. **Operating Profit from MoS:** $\text{Profit} = \text{MoS} \times \text{P/V Ratio} = 1,00,000 \times 25\% = ₹25,000$.
19. **P/V Ratio Two Periods:** $\frac{\Delta \text{Profit}}{\Delta \text{Sales}} \times 100$. If Sales rise by 1,00,000 and Profit rises by 25,000, $\text{P/V} = 25\%$.
20. **Required Sales for Target Profit:** $\frac{\text{Fixed Cost} + \text{Target Profit}}{\text{P/V Ratio}}$.
21. **Cash Break-Even Point:** $\frac{\text{Cash Fixed Cost}}{\text{Contribution per unit}} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost} - \text{Depreciation}}{\text{Contribution per unit}}$.
22. **Material Price Variance:** $AQ \times (SP - AP)$. If actual price exceeds standard, variance is Adverse.
23. **Material Usage Variance:** $SP \times (SQ - AQ)$. If actual quantity exceeds standard, variance is Adverse.
24. **Material Cost Variance:** $MCV = MPV + MUV$. Sum must match $(SQ \times SP) - (AQ \times AP)$.
25. **Labour Rate Variance:** $AH \times (SR - AR)$.
26. **Labour Efficiency Variance:** $SR \times (SH - AH)$.
27. **Labour Cost Variance:** $LCV = LRV + LEV$.
28. **Idle Time Variance:** $\text{Abnormal Idle Hours} \times \text{Standard Rate}$. Always Adverse.
29. **Economic Batch Quantity (EBQ):** $\sqrt{\frac{2DS}{C}}$. For $D = 10,000$, $S = 200$, $C = 4 \implies \sqrt{\frac{4,000,000}{4}} = 1,000 \text{ units}$.
30. **Section 194A TDS on Senior Citizens:** Threshold is ₹50,000. Interest ₹55,000 triggers TDS on entire ₹55,000 at 10% = ₹5,500.
31. **Section 194A TDS on General Citizens:** Threshold is ₹40,000. Interest ₹45,000 triggers TDS on entire ₹45,000 at 10% = ₹4,500.
32. **Section 206AA Penal TDS:** No PAN furnished $\implies 20\%$ TDS flat.
33. **Section 194N Cash TDS:** Regular filer withdrawing ₹1.20 Crores $\implies 2\%$ on ₹20 Lakhs = ₹40,000.
34. **Section 194N Non-Filer Cash TDS:** Non-filer withdrawing ₹1.20 Crores $\implies 2\%$ on ₹80 Lakhs (₹20L to ₹1Cr) + $5\%$ on ₹20 Lakhs (above ₹1Cr) = ₹1,60,000 + ₹1,00,000 = ₹2,60,000.
35. **Bank GST ITC Claim:** Under §17(4), Bank claims exactly $50\%$ of monthly eligible ITC.
36. **Statutory Reserve Transfer:** Section 17 BR Act min 20%; RBI mandate 25%. On profit ₹80 Cr, transfer $25\% = ₹20 \text{ Cr}$.
37. **Rebate on Bills Discounted:** Discount rate 12%, Face Value ₹10,00,000, unexpired days 73: $\text{Rebate} = 10,00,000 \times 12\% \times \frac{73}{365} = ₹24,000$.
38. **Current Ratio:** Current Assets / Current Liabilities. Benchmark 1.33:1.
39. **Quick Ratio:** $(CA - \text{Stock} - \text{Prepaid}) / CL$. Benchmark 1.00:1.
40. **Debt-Equity Ratio:** Long-Term Debt / Tangible Net Worth.
41. **DSCR:** $(\text{PAT} + \text{Depr} + \text{Interest}) / (\text{Interest} + \text{Principal})$. Safe range: 1.50 to 2.00.
42. **Interest Coverage Ratio:** $\text{EBIT} / \text{Interest}$.
43. **N walk MPBF Turnover Method:** Turnover ₹400 Lakhs $\implies$ Total WC requirement = $25\% = ₹100 \text{ Lakhs}$; Bank financing = $20\% = ₹80 \text{ Lakhs}$; Margin = ₹20 Lakhs.
44. **Tandon Method 1 MPBF:** $0.75 \times (CA - CL)$.
45. **Tandon Method 2 MPBF:** $(0.75 \times CA) - CL = 0.75 \times (CA - CL \text{ other than bank loans})$.
46. **Cash Conversion Cycle:** $ICP + RCP - PDP$.
47. **Forex Ascending Margin:** Spot USD/INR 84.10 / 84.20, forward premium 0.15 / 0.25 $\implies$ Forward Rate = 84.25 / 84.45.
48. **Forex Descending Margin:** Spot USD/INR 84.10 / 84.20, forward discount 0.25 / 0.15 $\implies$ Forward Rate = 83.85 / 84.05.
49. **Contract Costing 50-90% Profit:** $\frac{2}{3} \times \text{Notional Profit} \times \frac{\text{Cash Received}}{\text{Work Certified}}$.
50. **Total CRAR + CCB Target:** $9.0\% + 2.5\% = 11.5\%$ minimum capital ratio in India.
