## 16. IIBF AFMB Unit 9: Fundamentals of Costing, Marginal Costing & Break-Even Analysis

> 🧠 **Key Concept — Pivotal Concept: Marginal Costing & Contribution Principle**
> Marginal costing segregates total costs strictly into Fixed Costs (period costs that remain constant regardless of production volume) and Variable Costs (product costs that vary directly with output volume).

## 🧮 1. Master Mathematical Formulas for Marginal Costing

$\text{P/V Ratio} = \frac{\text{Contribution}}{\text{Sales}} \times 100 = \frac{\text{Sales} - \text{Variable Cost}}{\text{Sales}} \times 100$

*Explanation:* Expresses profitability per unit of sales volume. Higher P/V ratio indicates high profit-earning capacity.

$\text{BEP (Units)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{Contribution per Unit}}, \quad \text{BEP (Value)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{P/V Ratio}}$

*Explanation:* The sales volume at which Total Revenue equals Total Cost (Profit = 0).

$\text{Margin of Safety (MoS)} = \text{Actual Sales} - \text{Break-Even Sales} = \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{P/V Ratio}}$

*Explanation:* The safety cushion by which sales can decline before the business begins incurring losses.

> 🎯 **Exam Anchor & Trap:**
> 🎯 Top Exam Traps on Marginal Costing:
1. **Fixed Cost per Unit Trap:** Total Fixed Cost remains constant, but **Fixed Cost per unit DECREASES as production volume increases**.
2. **Variable Cost per Unit Trap:** Variable cost per unit remains **CONSTANT**, but total variable cost increases proportionally with volume.
3. **Margin of Safety Formula:** \(\text{MOS} = \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{P/V Ratio}}\).

## 17. IIBF AFMB Unit 10: Direct & Indirect Taxation, GST & TDS in Banking

> 🧠 **Key Concept — Pivotal Concept: Tax Architecture in Indian Banking**
> Banks operate as major tax deduction, collection, and reporting intermediaries under both direct (Income Tax Act 1961) and indirect (GST Act 2017) tax regimes.

## 📊 1. TDS on Bank Term Deposits (Section 194A, Income Tax Act)

| Depositor Category | Annual Interest Threshold for TDS | TDS Rate with Valid PAN | TDS Rate WITHOUT PAN (Sec 206AA) | Exemption Declaration Form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General / Non-Senior Citizens | **₹40,000** per financial year per bank | **10%** | **20%** | **Form 15G** (if total taxable income is below basic exemption limit and age < 60) |
| Senior Citizens (Age 60+ Years) | **₹50,000** per financial year per bank (under Sec 80TTB) | **10%** | **20%** | **Form 15H** (if tax liability on total income is NIL) |
| Time of TDS Deduction | Deducted at the time of credit of interest to account or payment, whichever is earlier | Deposited with Central Govt by **7th of succeeding month** | TDS Certificate issued in **Form 16A** quarterly | Annual information reflected in **Form 26AS & AIS** |

## 🏛️ 2. Goods and Services Tax (GST) Architecture

| GST Component | Levying Authority | Applicability / Transaction Nature | Input Tax Credit (ITC) Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CGST (Central GST) | Central Government | Intra-State supply of goods and services | Can be utilized against CGST and IGST |
| SGST (State GST) / UTGST | State Government / Union Territory | Intra-State supply of goods and services | Can be utilized against SGST and IGST |
| IGST (Integrated GST) | Central Government (shared with States) | Inter-State supplies and Imports into India | Can be utilized against IGST, CGST, and SGST |
| GST Council | Article 279A of Constitution | Chaired by Union Finance Minister; decisions taken by **75% weighted majority** | Determines tax slabs (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and banking exemptions |

> 🎯 **Exam Anchor & Trap:**
> 🎯 Top Exam Traps on Taxation & TDS:
1. **TDS Threshold for Senior Citizens:** Under Section 194A / 80TTB, the TDS threshold for Senior Citizens (60+ years) is **₹50,000**, compared to **₹40,000** for general citizens.
2. **TDS Rate without PAN:** If a depositor fails to submit PAN, TDS must be deducted at **20%** under Section 206AA.
3. **Form 15G vs 15H:** **Form 15G** is for individuals below 60 years. **Form 15H** is strictly for Senior Citizens (60 years and above).
