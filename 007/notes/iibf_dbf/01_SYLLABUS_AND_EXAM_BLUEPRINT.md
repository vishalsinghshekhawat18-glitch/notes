# IIBF DBF / JAIIB: Examination Blueprint, Passing Rules & Syllabus Mapping

> **Target Examination:** Diploma in Banking & Finance (DB&F) / JAIIB  
> **Conducting Authority:** Indian Institute of Banking & Finance (IIBF)  
> **Pattern:** 100 Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs) per Paper (100 Marks, 2 Hours per Paper)  
> **Standard Passing Criteria:** Minimum 50 marks out of 100 in each paper, OR a minimum of 45 marks in each paper with an aggregate of 200 marks (50%) across all 4 papers in a **single attempt**.

## 2. Paper-by-Paper Weightage & High-Yield Focus Areas

### Paper 1: Indian Economy & Indian Financial System (IE&IFS)
* **Module A (Indian Economic Architecture)**: Structural transformation, NITI Aayog strategy, PSL targets (40% for domestic banks, 75% for RRBs/SFBs), infrastructure financing (NIP, NMP, PM GatiShakti), and foreign trade policy.
* **Module B (Economic Concepts Related to Banking)**: 2015 NSO GDP/GVA methodology, Money supply aggregates ($M_1, M_2, M_3, M_4$), Demand-Supply elasticity formulas, Hicks-Hansen IS-LM curve, business cycle indicators, and FRBM fiscal deficit caps.
* **Module C (Indian Financial Architecture)**: Regulatory hierarchy (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA, IFSCA), Commercial banks Basel III norms, 4-tier UCB framework, NBFC Scale-Based Regulation (Base, Middle, Upper, Top), and NaBFID.
* **Module D (Financial Products and Services)**: Money market tenors (T-Bills, CPs ₹5L, CDs ₹1L, TREPS), Fixed income yield calculations, Derivatives (Options Greeks, Swaps), TReDS factoring mechanics, Forex quotes, and ESG/BRSR green finance.

---

### Paper 2: Principles & Practices of Banking (PPB)
* **Module A (General Banking Operations)**: Banker-customer legal relationships (Bailor/Bailee for safe deposit, Trustee/Beneficiary for safe custody, Debtor/Creditor for deposits), AML/KYC/PMLA norms, new 2025 nomination rules (up to 4 nominees; simultaneous vs successive), Garnishee vs Attachment orders, and the Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS 2026).
* **Module B (Functions of Banks & Lending)**: Credit appraisal (Tandon/Chore committee MPBF methods, Turnover method for working capital), Collateral charges (Pledge vs Hypothecation vs Mortgage vs Lien vs Assignment), NPA classification (Sub-standard 15%, Doubtful 25–100%, Loss 100%), SARFAESI 60-day notice rules, IBC corporate insolvency process, and Letters of Credit under UCPDC 600.
* **Module C (Banking Technology)**: CBS architecture, Data Centers (RPO & RTO metrics), NPCI payment rails (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI, AePS, BBPS, CBDC), ISO 27001, and RBI Cyber Security Framework.
* **Module D (Ethics in Banks & Financial Institutions)**: Ethical dilemma resolution, whistleblower protections, BCSBI codes, and corporate governance principles.

---

### Paper 3: Accounting & Financial Management for Bankers (AFMB)
* **Module A (Accounting Principles and Processes)**: GAAP concepts (Accrual, Going Concern, Conservatism/Prudence, Materiality), Golden rules of journal entry, Rectification of errors (Suspense account handling), Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS favorable vs overdraft balances), and Depreciation (Straight Line Method vs Written Down Value).
* **Module B (Financial Mathematics & Statements)**: Time Value of Money (TVM: Present Value, Future Value, Ordinary Annuity, Annuity Due), Bond valuation and Yield to Maturity (YTM) approximations, Bank final accounts under the Third Schedule of the Banking Regulation Act 1949 (Form A Balance Sheet: Schedules 1–12; Form B P&L: Schedules 13–16), and Foreign Exchange Arithmetic.
* **Module C (Financial Management)**: Capital Budgeting techniques (Net Present Value - NPV, Internal Rate of Return - IRR, Payback Period, Profitability Index), Cost of Capital (Weighted Average Cost of Capital - WACC), Leverages (Operating Leverage, Financial Leverage, Combined Leverage), and Financial Ratios for Credit Assessment (Current Ratio $\ge 1.33$, Quick Ratio $\ge 1.0$, Debt-Equity, Debt Service Coverage Ratio - DSCR $\ge 1.5$ to $2.0$).
* **Module D (Taxation and Fundamentals of Costing)**: GST applications in banking fees and commissions, TDS under Sections 194A (interest on deposits) and 194N (cash withdrawals), Marginal Costing equations, Contribution, $P/V$ Ratio, Break-Even Point (BEP in units and sales value), and Margin of Safety.

---

### Paper 4: Retail Banking & Wealth Management (RBWM)
* **Module A (Retail Banking Overview)**: Characteristics and business models (Horizontally Integrated, Vertically Integrated, Predominantly Vertically Integrated), Retail vs Wholesale/Corporate banking, and Branch profitability metrics (Return on Assets - ROA, Return on Equity - ROE).
* **Module B (Retail Products, Recovery & Credit Scoring)**: Maslow's hierarchy of needs applied to banking products, Home loans and PMAY interest subvention slabs (EWS, LIG, MIG), FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) and LTV (Loan-to-Value) caps, Credit card charge cards, CIBIL credit scoring architecture (300 to 900 score bands; $\ge 750$ prime threshold), Recovery mechanisms (DRT pecuniary limit ₹20 Lakhs, Lok Adalat limit ₹20 Lakhs, SARFAESI without court intervention), and Direct Recovery Agents (DRA) code of conduct.
* **Module C (Marketing of Banking Services)**: The 7 Ps of Services Marketing in Banking (Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, Process, Physical Evidence), and Delivery channels (Branches, ATMs, POS, Internet & Mobile Banking, Direct Selling Agents - DSAs).
* **Module D (Wealth Management & Real Estate)**: Wealth management cycle (Accumulation, Preservation, Distribution), Risk profiling of investors, Asset allocation, and Real Estate Regulation & Development Act (RERA 2016: mandatory 70% project realization deposit in dedicated escrow account).
