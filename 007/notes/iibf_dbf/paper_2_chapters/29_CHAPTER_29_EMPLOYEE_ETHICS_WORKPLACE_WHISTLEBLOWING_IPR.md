## 29.1 Ethics at the Employee Level: Fiduciary Conduct & Insider Trading

Employees of commercial banking institutions are custodians of public trust. Fiduciary duty requires that employees subordinate their private interests to the legitimate financial interests of the bank, its depositors, and regulatory mandates.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        EMPLOYEE ETHICAL INTEGRITY TAXONOMY                        |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                                   |
|  [ Abuse of Official Position ]                                                   |
|  * Diverting bank resources, vendor contracts, or credit approvals for personal    |
|    enrichment or to benefit family, associates, or related parties.               |
|                                                                                   |
|  [ Bribery, Graft & Undue Hospitality ]                                           |
|  * Acceptance of gifts, kickbacks, entertainment, or financial accommodations from|
|    borrowers, contractors, or empanelled professionals. Governed by internal staff|
|    conduct rules and the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 (for public banks).   |
|                                                                                   |
|  [ Moonlighting & Conflict of Interest ]                                          |
|  * Engaging in secondary commercial employment, directorships, or paid consulting |
|    without explicit prior institutional sanction.                                 |
|                                                                                   |
|  [ Insider Trading & Information Arbitrage ]                                      |
|  * Exploiting material Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI) gathered    |
|    during credit appraisal or treasury operations to trade in securities.          |
|                                                                                   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### SEBI Insider Trading Regulations and Chinese Walls
* **SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015:**
  * **Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI):** Any non-public information relating directly or indirectly to a company which, if published, is likely to materially affect securities prices (e.g., quarterly financial results, mergers, debt defaults, credit rating changes).
  * **Insider Definition:** Any person who is a connected person (e.g., credit underwriters, investment bankers, auditors, senior executives) or in possession of or having access to UPSI.
  * **Chinese Walls / Information Barriers:** Physical, procedural, and technological barriers erected inside a bank to isolate departments possessing confidential market-sensitive information (e.g., Wholesale Lending, Merchant Banking, Syndication) from public-facing departments (e.g., Treasury Dealing Desk, Wealth Management, Equity Research).
  * **Trading Windows and Pre-Clearance:** Mandatory closure of trading windows around financial disclosures; mandatory pre-clearance of trades exceeding specified monetary thresholds by compliance officers.

## 29.2 Workplace Conduct and the POSH Act, 2013

A safe, dignified, and inclusive workplace environment is both an ethical necessity and a statutory requirement under Indian law.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               POSH ACT, 2013: STATUTORY ARCHITECTURE & TIMELINES                  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                                   |
|  [ Internal Committee (IC) Mandate ]                                              |
|  * Mandatorily constituted at all workplaces / administrative units with          |
|    TEN (10) OR MORE EMPLOYEES.                                                    |
|                                                                                   |
|  [ Composition of the Internal Committee ]                                        |
|  1. Presiding Officer: A woman employed at a senior level in the organization.    |
|  2. Employee Members: Not less than two members committed to women's causes,      |
|     social work, or having legal knowledge.                                       |
|  3. External Member: One member from an NGO or association committed to women's   |
|     causes, or familiar with sexual harassment issues.                            |
|  * STATUTORY GENDER RATIO: At least 50% OF TOTAL MEMBERS MUST BE WOMEN.           |
|                                                                                   |
|  [ Critical Inquiry & Implementation Timelines ]                                  |
|  * Filing Complaint: Within 3 MONTHS of incident (extendable by 3 months by IC).  |
|  * Inquiry Completion: Must be completed within 90 CALENDAR DAYS.                 |
|  * Report Submission: IC must submit findings report to employer within 10 DAYS.  |
|  * Employer Action: Employer must implement IC recommendations within 60 DAYS.    |
|  * Conciliation: Permitted before inquiry, but NO MONETARY SETTLEMENT permitted.  |
|                                                                                   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 29.3 Whistleblower Mechanisms & Protection

Whistleblowing is the disclosure by an employee or stakeholder of corrupt, illegal, unethical, or fraudulent activities occurring within an organization to authorities who can enforce corrective action.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                    VIGIL MECHANISM / WHISTLEBLOWER FRAMEWORK                      |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                                   |
|  [ Statutory Foundations ]                                                        |
|  * Section 177(9) of the Companies Act, 2013: Every listed company, companies     |
|    accepting deposits from public, and companies borrowing > ₹50 Cr from banks.   |
|  * Regulation 22 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for listed entities.            |
|                                                                                   |
|  [ Core Structural Protections ]                                                  |
|  * Direct Access to Audit Committee: Provides direct, unhindered access to the    |
|    Chairperson of the Board's Audit Committee in appropriate or exceptional cases.|
|  * Protection Against Victimization: Total statutory protection against retaliatory|
|    discharge, demotion, suspension, harassment, or adverse performance appraisals.|
|  * Confidentiality of Identity: Whistleblower identity is protected; anonymous    |
|    complaints containing verifiable, concrete evidence must be investigated.      |
|                                                                                   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 29.6 Practice Drill: Examination Diagnostic Questions

### Question 1
Under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH Act), what is the statutory requirement regarding the composition of the Internal Committee (IC)?
A) The IC must be headed by a judicial magistrate, and at least 30% of members must be women.
B) The IC must be chaired by a senior woman employee, include an independent external member from an NGO, and at least 50% of the total members must be women.
C) The IC must be chaired by the Chief Human Resources Officer, and all members must be confirmed permanent officers.
D) An IC is required only if the administrative unit employs more than 100 workers.

### Question 2
Under Section 177(9) of the Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI Listing Regulations, what critical safeguard is guaranteed to a corporate employee invoking the Vigil Mechanism (Whistleblower policy)?
A) An immediate cash reward equivalent to 10% of the fraudulent amount uncovered.
B) Absolute immunity from any future transfers across branch locations.
C) Direct access to the Chairperson of the Audit Committee in appropriate cases, and complete statutory protection against retaliatory victimization.
D) The automatic filing of an FIR with the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI).

### Question 3

A) Up to ₹10 Crore
B) Up to ₹50 Crore
C) Up to ₹250 Crore

### Question 4
In corporate treasury and investment banking, what is the function of an institutional "Chinese Wall"?
A) A reinforced physical vault structure designed to withstand ballistic cyber attacks.
B) Information barriers and procedural firewalls that isolate departments possessing confidential material non-public information from departments executing public trading or research.

### Question 5

A) 10 years; yes, software algorithms are freely patentable.
B) 20 years; no, Section 3(k) explicitly excludes a mathematical method, a business method, or a computer program per se from patentability.
C) 50 years; yes, provided the algorithm generates corporate revenue.
D) Author's lifetime plus 60 years; only if registered through the RBI.

## 29.7 Diagnostic Solutions & Analysis

1. **Correct Answer: B**
   * *Analysis:* Under Section 4 of the POSH Act, 2013, every workplace employing 10 or more workers must constitute an Internal Committee (IC). The Presiding Officer must be a senior woman employee; at least two employee members must be committed to the cause of women; one member must be drawn from an NGO or familiar with harassment issues; and **at least 50% of the total committee members must be women**.

2. **Correct Answer: C**
   * *Analysis:* The Vigil Mechanism mandated by the Companies Act, 2013 (Section 177) and SEBI LODR Regulations provides for direct access to the Chairperson of the Audit Committee in exceptional cases. It guarantees strict confidentiality and protection against victimization or adverse retaliatory employment action.

3. **Correct Answer: C**
   * *Analysis:* Under the Schedule to the DPDP Act, 2023, the maximum statutory financial penalty is **₹250 Crore** for significant failure to observe reasonable security safeguards. (Note for examinations: Under the 13 November 2025 commencement notification, substantive adjudication and penalty powers under Sections 28–34 follow an 18-month phased rollout timeline).

4. **Correct Answer: B**
   * *Analysis:* "Chinese Walls" are ethical, procedural, and technological barriers implemented within multi-service financial institutions to prevent the flow of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI) from private advisory/lending arms to public trading or wealth management desks, mitigating conflicts of interest and illegal insider trading.

5. **Correct Answer: B**
   * *Analysis:* The term of a patent under the Indian Patents Act, 1970, is **20 years** from the international/domestic filing date. Under Section 3(k) of the Act, a mathematical or business method, or a computer program *per se*, or algorithms are non-patentable subject matter (though software embedded in unique physical hardware providing a technical effect may be eligible).
