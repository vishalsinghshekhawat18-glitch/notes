# FINANCIAL INCLUSION, CUSTOMER SERVICE GUIDELINES & SECRECY

Financial inclusion drives institutional banking penetration to unserved segments through technology-enabled delivery channels. Concurrently, customer service architecture enforces disciplined branch standards, transparent fee schedules, and strict adherence to statutory secrecy, balanced against modern regulatory disclosure obligations.

---

## § 11.1 Unit 16: Financial Inclusion & Financial Literacy Architecture

> **Curriculum Alignment — Official IIBF Paper 2 Benchmark (Unit 16)**  
> **Core Proposition:** Financial Inclusion is the process of ensuring access to financial services and timely, adequate credit where needed by vulnerable groups at an affordable cost (Rangarajan Committee, 2008). Anchored in the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), the framework utilizes Business Correspondents (BCs) and digital tools.

### 1. Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) & BSBDA Norms

• **Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA):**
  - **Zero Minimum Balance:** No requirement of maintaining any minimum balance.
  - **No Annual Maintenance Charges:** Basic RuPay Debit Card issued completely free of cost.
  - **Free Services:** Minimum 4 cash withdrawals per month free of charge (including ATM withdrawals). Unlimited free cash deposits.
  - **BSBDA-Small Accounts:** Relaxed KYC for individuals lacking standard OVDs: aggregate credits $\le$ ₹1 Lakh/year, aggregate withdrawals $\le$ ₹10,000/month, balance at any point $\le$ ₹50,000. Valid for 12 months, extendable by 12 months if applied for an OVD.
• **Special PMJDY Features:**
  - **Overdraft (OD) Facility:** Up to **₹10,000** available to one adult per eligible household (preferably the woman of the house) after 6 months of satisfactory account operation; OD up to **₹2,000 without any conditions**. Age limit: **18 to 65 years**.
  - **Accidental Insurance Cover:** Free accidental insurance cover of **₹2 Lakh** on RuPay cards issued to PMJDY accounts (provided the card was used at least once in 90 days).

### 2. Business Correspondents (BC) & RSETI Architecture

• **Business Correspondent (BC) Model:** Retail agents engaged by banks to provide doorstep banking services in unbanked rural/semi-urban areas using micro-ATMs and biometric Aadhaar authentication.
• **Rural Self Employment Training Institutes (RSETI):** Dedicated training centers managed by lead banks with Ministry of Rural Development support to provide free skill development and entrepreneurship training to rural youth.

## § 11.2 Units 17 & 18: Customer Service Framework, Secrecy & BCSBI Historical Status

### 1. Key Customer Service Committees

• **Goiporia Committee (1990):** Recommended 15-minute counter service benchmarks, display of service charges, specialized teller systems, and mandatory training of frontline branch staff.
• **Damodaran Committee (2011):** Recommended zero penal charges on inoperative accounts, standardized basic services, third-party ATM fee limitations, and establishment of Chief Customer Service Officers (Internal Ombudsman) in banks.

### 2. Historical Status of BCSBI (Banking Codes and Standards Board of India)

> **Auditor Finding Fix — Historical Status Qualifier (H-06)**  
> • The **Banking Codes and Standards Board of India (BCSBI)** was set up in 2006 as an independent watchdog following the Tarapore Committee recommendations. It formulated two non-statutory voluntary codes:
>   1. *Code of Bank's Commitment to Customers*
>   2. *Code of Bank's Commitment to Micro and Small Enterprises*
> • **Current Legal Reality:** **BCSBI was formally dissolved and wound up in 2019**. The voluntary codes have been retired. Customer protection in Indian banking is now directly enforced through **statutory RBI Master Directions on Customer Service**, the **Internal Ombudsman scheme**, and the **Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021 (RB-IOS)**.

### 3. Banker's Duty of Confidentiality & Legal Exceptions

Section 13 of the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970/1980 mandates strict secrecy. Disclosures are permitted only under:
• **Compulsion of Law:** Section 29 BR Act (financial statements inspection), Section 12 PMLA 2002 (reporting suspicious transactions to FIU-IND), Section 226(3) Income Tax Act.
• **Credit Information Sharing:** Mandatory monthly submission of credit records to Credit Information Companies (CICs: CIBIL, Equifax, Experian, CRIF High Mark) under the Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005 without requiring separate consent for statutory reporting.

## § 11.3 High-Yield Examination Drill

**Q1. Under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), what is the maximum Overdraft (OD) facility available to an eligible account holder after satisfactory operation of the account for six months?**  
A. ₹5,000  
B. ₹10,000  
C. ₹20,000  
D. ₹50,000  

**Q2. What is the current regulatory status of the Banking Codes and Standards Board of India (BCSBI) and its voluntary customer codes in Indian banking operations?**  
A. It remains the supreme statutory judicial tribunal for adjudicating banking service disputes  
B. It is a mandatory wing of the Ministry of Corporate Affairs  
C. The BCSBI was formally dissolved in 2019; customer protection is now directly governed by statutory RBI Master Directions and the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme  
D. It was merged into the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)  

**Q3. Under RBI guidelines on Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts (BSBDA), what is the minimum number of free cash withdrawals permitted per month across branch counters and ATMs?**  
A. Exactly 1 withdrawal  
B. Maximum 2 withdrawals  
C. At least 4 withdrawals  
D. Unlimited free withdrawals  

---

## § 11.4 Answer Key & Detailed Explanatory Rationale

• **Q1 — Answer: B.** Under revised PMJDY guidelines, the maximum Overdraft (OD) limit is **₹10,000** for accounts operated satisfactorily for 6 months (with up to ₹2,000 granted without condition). The upper age limit for the OD facility was enhanced from 60 to 65 years.  
• **Q2 — Answer: C.** BCSBI was an independent voluntary board established in 2006 but was formally **dissolved in 2019**. Its voluntary codes are no longer active; customer service and protection standards are now statutorily regulated by RBI Master Directions and the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.  
• **Q3 — Answer: C.** RBI mandates that every BSBDA account holder must be provided **at least four (4) free cash withdrawals per month** across ATMs and branch counters without any fee. Cash deposits are completely free and unlimited.
