# CASH OPERATIONS, CLEAN NOTE POLICY & COUNTERFEIT CURRENCY

Cash management represents the operational frontline of retail banking governed by RBI Clean Note Policy, Section 39 of the RBI Act 1934, and RBI (Note Refund) Rules 2009. Detection, impounding, and reporting of Fake Indian Currency Notes (FICN) are statutory duties enforceable under the Indian Penal Code (IPC) and PMLA.

## § 7.1 Comprehensive Operational & Legal Analysis

Cash Operations, Clean Note Policy & Counterfeit Currency

> **Key Concept — Pivotal Concept: Clean Note Policy & Currency Integrity**
> Under Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act 1949 and RBI Act 1934, the Reserve Bank implements policies to maintain the quality, authenticity, and hygiene of circulating Indian currency.

## 1. Detection and Impounding of Counterfeit / Forged Banknotes

Under RBI Master Directions on Counterfeit Currency, failure by a bank teller to impound a forged note is treated as a severe compliance failure:

| Parameter | Single Transaction: Up to 4 Forged Notes | Single Transaction: 5 or More Forged Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impounding Action | Teller stamps 'COUNTERFEIT BANKNOTE IMPOUNDED' and issues signed receipt to presenter | Teller stamps 'COUNTERFEIT BANKNOTE IMPOUNDED' and issues signed receipt to presenter |
| Return of Note to Customer |  **Strictly Prohibited.** No credit/refund given under any circumstances |  **Strictly Prohibited.** No credit/refund given under any circumstances |
| Police Reporting Protocol | Send consolidated report with impounded notes to Nodal Police Station at the **end of the month** | File an **immediate First Information Report (FIR)** with local police station for criminal investigation |
| Preservation of Forged Notes | Maintained in safe custody under joint custodianship until handed over to police | Handed over to Police Investigation Officer against official acknowledgement |

## 2. RBI (Note Refund) Rules, 2009: Soiled vs Mutilated Notes

| Category | Definition / Characteristics | Value Payable (Adjudication Norms) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soiled Note | A banknote which has become dirty due to normal usage, or a two-piece note pasted together where both pieces belong to the same note and form an entire note without any essential feature missing | Full Value payable across all bank branches free of charge |
| Mutilated Note | A banknote of which a portion is missing or which is composed of more than two pieces pasted together | Adjudicated based on the **surface area of the single largest undivided piece** according to RBI Note Refund Rules |
| Extremely Brittle / Charred / Burnt Notes | Notes severely burnt, charred, or stuck together that cannot be handled without crumbling | Cannot be accepted across bank counters; sent directly to RBI Issue Department for special adjudication |
| Deliberately Cut / Slogans Inscribed | Notes bearing political/religious slogans or intentionally cut/defaced |  **Zero Value.** Rejected and impounded under Note Refund Rules |

## 3. Area Benchmarks for Mutilated Note Refund (Mahatma Gandhi New Series)

• **For Denominations of ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500:**.
• • **Full Value:** Single largest undivided piece has an area (ge 80%) of the original note dimensions.
• • **Half Value:** Single largest undivided piece has an area (ge 40%) and (< 80%) of the original note dimensions.
• • **Zero Value:** Single largest undivided piece has an area (< 40%).
• **For Lower Denominations (₹5, ₹10, ₹20):**.
• • **Full Value:** Single largest undivided piece has an area (ge 50%). (No half-value refund for denominations below ₹50).

> **Exam Anchor & Trap:**
> Top Exam Traps on Cash Operations:
1. **Police Reporting Slabs for Fake Notes:** Up to 4 notes = **Monthly consolidated report**. 5 or more notes in a single transaction = **Immediate FIR**.
2. **Returning Fake Notes:** A bank employee who returns a counterfeit note to the customer without impounding can be prosecuted under Section 39 of the CrPC for concealing a cognizable offence.
3. **Half-Value Refund Rule:** Half-value refund is available ONLY for denominations of **₹50 and above**. For ₹5, ₹10, and ₹20 notes, there is NO half value (it is either full value if (ge 50%) area or zero value if (< 50%)).

### Practice Questions & Solved Numerical Drills (IIBF Pattern)

**Q1:** When a forged or counterfeit banknote is detected across the counter in a bank branch, what is the mandatory immediate statutory action required?
• (A) Return the note immediately to the presenter
• (B) Tear or destroy the note in the customer’s presence
• (C) Impound the note, stamp "COUNTERFEIT BANKNOTE IMPOUNDED", and issue a dated receipt
• (D) Deduct the amount from the customer's savings account

**Q2:** Under RBI guidelines on Counterfeit Notes, when must a bank lodge a First Information Report (FIR) with the local police?
• (A) For even a single detected forged note of ₹10 denomination
• (B) When 5 or more pieces of counterfeit notes are detected in a single transaction
• (C) Only when counterfeit currency exceeds ₹1,00,000
• (D) Only upon written order from the Currency Officer, RBI

**Q3:** Under RBI (Note Refund) Rules 2009, what is the definition of a "Soiled Note"?
• (A) A note which has become dirty due to normal circulation, including two pieces pasted together provided both belong to the same note
• (B) A note of which a portion is missing
• (C) A note which is completely burnt or brittle
• (D) A note containing political or religious slogans

#### Solutions & Detailed Explanations

1. **(C) Impound the note, stamp "COUNTERFEIT BANKNOTE IMPOUNDED", and issue a dated receipt** — Bank notes detected as counterfeit must never be returned to the presenter or destroyed; they must be impounded and receipted immediately.

2. **(B) When 5 or more pieces of counterfeit notes are detected in a single transaction** — For 1 to 4 pieces, consolidated monthly reporting is sent to police; for 5 or more pieces in a single transaction, an FIR must be lodged immediately.

3. **(A) A note which has become dirty due to normal circulation, including two pieces pasted together provided both belong to the same note** — Soiled notes can be exchanged freely over bank counters for full value without payment of adjudication fees.

## § 7.2 Active Recall Diagnostic Vault

<details>
<summary>Distinguish between a Mutilated Note and an Imperfect Note under RBI (Note Refund) Rules.</summary>
Mutilated Note: A banknote of which a portion is missing or which is composed of more than two pieces pasted together. Adjudicated based on minimum area percentages (single largest undivided piece). Imperfect Note: A banknote which is wholly or partially, obliterated, shrunk, washed, altered, or indecipherable, but of which no essential part is missing.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the minimum undivided surface area benchmarks required for full value refund on a mutilated note of ₹50 and above (Mahatma Gandhi New Series)?</summary>
For denominations of ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500, and ₹2000: (1) Full Value: If the single largest undivided piece has an area ≥ 80% of the original note area; (2) Half Value: If the undivided area is ≥ 40% and < 80%; (3) Zero Value: If the undivided area is < 40%.
</details>
