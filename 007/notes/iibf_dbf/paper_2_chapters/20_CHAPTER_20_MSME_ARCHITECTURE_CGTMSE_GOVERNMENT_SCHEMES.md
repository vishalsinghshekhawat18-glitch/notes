# MSME ARCHITECTURE, CGTMSE & GOVERNMENT-SPONSORED SCHEMES

Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) drive industrial output, exports, and nationwide employment generation. Governed by the MSMED Act 2006 and the revised 2025 composite classification, MSME financing is reinforced by the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE), Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS), and targeted Government-sponsored programs including MUDRA, DAY-NRLM, and the SHG-Bank Linkage Programme.

---

## § 20.1 Unit 39: MSME Classification & Statutory Safeguards

> **Curriculum Alignment — Official IIBF Paper 2 Benchmark (Unit 39)**  
> **Core Proposition:** MSME classification uses a composite criterion combining investment in plant, machinery, or equipment with annual turnover. Both criteria must be simultaneously satisfied to maintain enterprise status.

### 1. Revised Composite Classification (Effective 1 April 2025)

| Enterprise Category | Investment in Plant & Machinery / Equipment | Annual Net Turnover Ceiling | Mandatory Statutory Invariant |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Micro Enterprise** | **$\le$ ₹2.50 Crore** *(₹25 Million)* | **$\le$ ₹10.00 Crore** *(₹100 Million)* | **Export Turnover is STRICTLY EXCLUDED** from turnover calculation. Both investment and turnover tests must be satisfied. |
| **Small Enterprise** | **$\le$ ₹25.00 Crore** *(₹250 Million)* | **$\le$ ₹100.00 Crore** *(₹1,000 Million)* |
| **Medium Enterprise** | **$\le$ ₹125.00 Crore** *(₹1,250 Million)* | **$\le$ ₹500.00 Crore** *(₹5,000 Million)* |

### 2. Statutory Penalties for Delayed Payments (Section 15 & 16, MSMED Act, 2006)

• **Statutory Credit Period (Section 15):** The buyer must pay the MSME supplier within the agreed date, which **CANNOT exceed forty-five (45) days** from the date of acceptance of goods or services. If no period is agreed, payment is due within **15 days**.
• **Compound Penal Interest (Section 16):**
  - If the buyer fails to pay within 45 days, the buyer is legally liable to pay compound interest with **monthly rests** at **three (3) times the Bank Rate** notified by the RBI.
  - Penal interest paid by a buyer to an MSME **CANNOT be claimed as a tax-deductible business expense** under the Income Tax Act, 1961!

### 3. CGTMSE & TReDS Architecture

• **Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE):**
  - Jointly set up by Ministry of MSME and SIDBI.
  - Provides credit guarantee coverage up to **₹10 Crore** per borrower without requiring collateral or third-party guarantees.
  - Guarantee cover extends up to 85% for micro enterprises (loans up to ₹5 Lakh), women entrepreneurs, SC/ST, and aspirational districts; 75% for general category MSEs up to ₹10 Crore (and up to 90% for select special initiatives).
• **Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS):**
  - Electronic institutional platform authorized by RBI to facilitate financing/discounting of trade receivables of MSMEs from corporate buyers and PSUs through multiple financiers.
  - **Operator Net Worth:** Entities setting up and operating a TReDS platform must have a minimum paid-up equity capital / net worth of **₹25 Crore** as mandated by the RBI.
  - **Non-Recourse Factoring:** Discounting on TReDS is strictly **without recourse to the MSME seller**; the financier assumes credit risk directly on the corporate buyer.

## § 20.2 Units 40 & 41: Government Schemes & Self-Help Groups (SHGs)

### 1. Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

• Refinancing institution set up under SIDBI to finance non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises:
  1. **Shishu:** Loans up to **₹50,000** (for micro-entrepreneurs at start-up phase).
  2. **Kishore:** Loans from **₹50,001 up to ₹5,00,000** (for expanding businesses).
  3. **Tarun:** Loans from **₹5,00,001 up to ₹10,00,000** (for established businesses).
  4. **Tarun Plus (Budget 2024/2025):** Enhanced limit up to **₹20 Lakh** for entrepreneurs who have previously taken and successfully repaid a Tarun loan.
• **Zero Collateral:** No collateral security or third-party guarantee can be demanded by banks on MUDRA loans.

### 2. Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - NRLM & Self-Help Groups (SHGs)

• **DAY-NRLM (National Rural Livelihoods Mission):** Focuses on organizing rural poor women into Self-Help Groups (SHGs) of 10 to 20 members (special groups 5 to 20 for disabled/remote areas).
• **SHG-Bank Linkage:**
  - Revolving Fund (RF) and Community Investment Support Fund (CIF).
  - Credit linkage in multiple doses (up to 4 times of group savings or specified slabs).
  - **Collateral-Free Limit:** **Zero collateral up to ₹10 Lakh**, enhanced to **₹20 Lakh for eligible SHGs** under DAY-NRLM.
  - Interest subvention on credit up to ₹3 Lakh bringing the effective rate down to **7.0% p.a.** (with prompt repayment incentive further reducing it to **4.0% p.a.**).

## § 20.3 High-Yield Examination Drill

**Q1. An industrial enterprise has an investment in plant and machinery of ₹20 Crore, an annual domestic turnover of ₹80 Crore, and export proceeds of ₹50 Crore. Under the revised MSME classification criteria effective 1 April 2025, how is this enterprise legally classified?**  
A. Micro Enterprise  
B. Small Enterprise  
C. Medium Enterprise  
D. Large Corporate Enterprise  

**Q2. Under Section 16 of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006, what is the statutory penal interest payable by a corporate buyer who fails to make payment to an MSME supplier within the maximum prescribed period of 45 days?**  
A. Simple interest at the bank's Base Rate  
B. Compound interest with quarterly rests at the prevailing Repo Rate  
C. Compound interest with monthly rests at three (3) times the Bank Rate notified by the RBI  
D. A flat penalty of 18% per annum without rests  

**Q3. Under the Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY), what is the loan sanction slab designated under the 'Kishore' category?**  
A. Up to ₹50,000  
B. From ₹50,001 up to ₹5,00,000  
C. From ₹5,00,001 up to ₹10,00,000  
D. From ₹10,00,001 up to ₹20,00,000  

---

## § 20.4 Answer Key & Detailed Explanatory Rationale

• **Q1 — Answer: B.** Under the revised criteria effective 1 April 2025, a Small Enterprise has investment $\le$ ₹25 Crore and turnover $\le$ ₹100 Crore. Because **export turnover is statutorily excluded**, the relevant turnover is only the domestic sales of ₹80 Crore (which is $\le$ ₹100 Crore). With investment at ₹20 Crore ($\le$ ₹25 Crore), the unit is classified as a **Small Enterprise**.  
• **Q2 — Answer: C.** Section 16 of the MSMED Act, 2006 explicitly provides that for delayed payments beyond 45 days, the buyer must pay compound interest with **monthly rests at three times the Bank Rate** notified by the Reserve Bank. This interest is not tax-deductible.  
• **Q3 — Answer: B.** PMMY loan categories are: **Shishu** (up to ₹50,000), **Kishore** (₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000), **Tarun** (₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000), and **Tarun Plus** (up to ₹20 Lakh for seasoned borrowers).
