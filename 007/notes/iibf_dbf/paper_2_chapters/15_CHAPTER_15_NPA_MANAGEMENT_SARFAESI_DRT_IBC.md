# NPA MANAGEMENT, ASSET CLASSIFICATION, SARFAESI ACT & DRT

Asset classification adheres to the 90-day overdue benchmark, progressing from Special Mention Accounts (SMA-0/1/2) to Sub-standard, Doubtful, and Loss assets. Recovery enforcement utilizes the SARFAESI Act 2002 (enforcement without court intervention), Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy Act 1993 (DRT), and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016 (IBC).

## § 15.1 Comprehensive Operational & Legal Analysis

NPA Management, SARFAESI Act & Recovery Mechanisms

> **Key Concept — Pivotal Concept: Prudential Norms on Income Recognition & Asset Classification (IRAC)**
> Under RBI IRAC norms, an asset becomes Non-Performing when it ceases to generate income for the bank.

## 1. Master Matrix of Asset Classification & Mandatory Provisioning Norms

| Asset Category | Period / Criteria | Mandatory Provision on Secured Portion | Mandatory Provision on Unsecured Portion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Asset | Performing normally without default | 0.40% (General) / 0.25% (Direct Agri & SME) / 1.00% (Commercial Real Estate) | 0.40% to 1.00% |
| Sub-Standard Asset | NPA for a period (le 12	ext{ months}) | **15%** | **25%** (20% for infrastructure loans) |
| Doubtful-1 (D1) | Remained in Sub-standard category for 12 months (NPA up to 1 yr) | **25%** | **100%** |
| Doubtful-2 (D2) | NPA for more than 1 year and up to 3 years | **40%** | **100%** |
| Doubtful-3 (D3) | NPA for more than 3 years | **100%** | **100%** |
| Loss Asset | Identified as uncollectible by bank, internal/external auditors, or RBI inspection | **100%** (Write-off or full provision) | **100%** |

## 2. SARFAESI Act, 2002 Enforcement Architecture

| Section in SARFAESI | Enforcement Stage | Statutory Timelines & Powers | Borrower / Guarantor Rights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Section 13(2) | Demand Notice to Borrower | Mandatory **60-day notice** issued to discharge liabilities in full after account is classified as NPA. | Borrower can submit representation/objection within 60 days. |
| Section 13(3A) | Disposal of Objections | Bank must reply to borrower's representation within **15 days** of receipt. | Rejection of objection gives no immediate right to appeal in DRT. |
| Section 13(4) | Enforcement / Possession Measures | Upon failure to pay after 60 days, bank takes symbolic/physical possession of secured asset, takes over management, or appoints manager. | Borrower can appeal to DRT within **45 days** under Section 17. |
| Section 14 | Magistrate Assistance | Chief Metropolitan Magistrate (CMM) or District Magistrate (DM) takes physical possession of asset and hands over to bank. | Administrative act; Magistrate cannot adjudicate dispute on merits. |
| Section 17 | Appeal to DRT (Securitisation Application) | Borrower files application against Sec 13(4) measures within 45 days. | Appeal to Appellate Tribunal (DRAT) under Sec 18 requires **50% pre-deposit** (can be reduced to 25%). |

## 3. Comparative Recovery Forums: Lok Adalats vs DRT vs IBC

| Recovery Forum | Governing Act | Monetary Claim Jurisdiction | Nature of Order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lok Adalat | Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 | Up to **₹20 Lakhs** (for bank compromise settlements) | Consent decree; No appeal lies against award |
| Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT) | RDB Act, 1993 (Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy) | **₹20 Lakhs and above** | Issues Recovery Certificate; Appeal to DRAT within 30 days |
| Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC) | Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (NCLT) | Default threshold of **₹1 Crore and above** | CIRP process: Resolution within 180 + 90 days (max 330 days) |

> **Exam Anchor & Trap:**
> Top Exam Traps on NPA Management & SARFAESI:
1. **SARFAESI Agricultural Land Exemption:** Under Section 31(i), the SARFAESI Act does **NOT apply to agricultural land**.
2. **SARFAESI Debt Thresholds:** SARFAESI cannot be invoked if: (a) remaining debt is less than **₹1 Lakh**, or (b) remaining principal and interest is less than **20% of the original principal debt**.
3. **DRAT Pre-Deposit Requirement:** To appeal before the Debt Recovery Appellate Tribunal (DRAT) under Section 18 of SARFAESI, the borrower must deposit **50% of the debt determined by DRT** (DRAT can reduce it to minimum 25%).

### Practice Questions & Solved Numerical Drills (IIBF Pattern)

**Q1:** Under RBI Prudential Norms, an account is classified as a "Sub-standard Asset" when it has remained in the Non-Performing Asset (NPA) category for a period of up to:
• (A) 90 days
• (B) 12 months (≤ 12 months)
• (C) 18 months
• (D) 3 years

**Q2:** Under Section 13(2) of the SARFAESI Act 2002, what is the mandatory notice period granted to a defaulting borrower to discharge liabilities before the bank takes enforcement measures?
• (A) 15 days
• (B) 30 days
• (C) 60 days
• (D) 90 days

**Q3:** What is the minimum pecuniary jurisdiction threshold for commercial banks and financial institutions to file an original recovery application before the Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT)?
• (A) ₹5 Lakh
• (B) ₹10 Lakh
• (C) ₹20 Lakh
• (D) ₹1 Crore

#### Solutions & Detailed Explanations

1. **(B) 12 months (≤ 12 months)** — An asset remains Sub-standard for ≤ 12 months, after which it slips into the Doubtful category.

2. **(C) 60 days** — Section 13(2) requires a 60-day demand notice. If unpaid, Section 13(4) empowers the bank to take physical possession of secured assets.

3. **(C) ₹20 Lakh** — Central Government raised the pecuniary threshold for filing original applications before DRTs from ₹10 Lakh to ₹20 Lakh.
