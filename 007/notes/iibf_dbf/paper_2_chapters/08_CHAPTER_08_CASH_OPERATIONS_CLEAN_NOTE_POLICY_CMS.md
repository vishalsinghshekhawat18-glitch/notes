# CASH OPERATIONS, CLEAN NOTE POLICY & CASH MANAGEMENT SERVICES

Cash operations in commercial banking demand rigorous physical custody protocols, strict adherence to the Clean Note Policy, statutory impounding of counterfeit currency, and sophisticated Cash Management Services (CMS) to manage corporate collections, disbursements, and liquidity pooling.

---

## § 8.1 Unit 06b: Cash Custody, Clean Note Policy & Counterfeit Management

> **Curriculum Alignment — Official IIBF Paper 2 Benchmark (Unit 06b)**  
> **Core Proposition:** Section 22 of the RBI Act gives the Reserve Bank the sole right to issue banknotes. The Clean Note Policy mandates circulation of good quality notes, banning stapling and writing on banknotes. Counterfeit note detection requires mandatory impounding and reporting.

### 1. Currency Chests & Clean Note Policy

• **Currency Chests:** Operating branches authorized by RBI to hold sovereign cash reserves. Funds in currency chests belong to the Reserve Bank of India, not the holding commercial bank.
• **Clean Note Policy Rules:**
  - Banning of stapling, pinning, or stitching of banknote packets.
  - Banks must issue only clean notes to the public and withdraw soiled/mutilated notes from circulation.
  - Bank staff and public are prohibited from scribbling or writing on the watermark area or security threads of banknotes.

### 2. Soiled, Mutilated & Imperfect Notes (RBI Note Refund Rules)

| Note Classification | Physical Condition Definition | Operational Exchange / Refund Entitlement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Soiled Note** | Notes that have become dirty due to normal circulation or cut into two pieces with both pieces belonging to the same note (forming a complete note). | **Exchanged at full value** at any bank branch across the counter without question. |
| **Mutilated Note** | Notes with a portion missing or composed of more than two pieces pasted together. | • **Full Value:** If undivided single largest piece area is **$\ge$ 80%** (for denominations $\ge$ ₹50) or **$\ge$ 50%** (for denominations $\le$ ₹20).<br>• **Half Value:** If area is **$\ge$ 40% but < 80%** for denominations $\ge$ ₹50. |
| **Extremely Mutilated / Charred / Inseparable** | Notes burnt, charred, or stuck together that cannot be separated. | Cannot be exchanged across branch counters; must be forwarded to the designated Issue Department of the RBI for special adjudication. |

### 3. Detection & Impounding of Counterfeit Banknotes

• **Mandatory Impounding:** No bank branch or teller can return a suspected counterfeit note to the presenter or destroy it. The note must be stamped with **"COUNTERFEIT BANKNOTE IMPOUNDED"** in prescribed dimensions.
• **Receipt:** A designated receipt must be issued to the presenter, signed by both the customer and the bank cashier.
• **Police Reporting Framework:**
  - **Up to 4 Counterfeit Notes in a Single Transaction:** Consolidated monthly report sent to the local Nodal Police Station at the end of the month.
  - **5 or More Counterfeit Notes in a Single Transaction:** The bank must immediately lodge a First Information Report (**FIR**) with the local police for criminal investigation.

## § 8.2 Unit 11: Cash Management Services (CMS) Architecture

Cash Management Services represent high-value transaction banking provided to corporate clients, non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), and government departments:
• **Collections Management:** Centralized local and outstation collection of cheques, Electronic NACH mandates, and Virtual Account Numbers (VAN) enabling instant reconciliation of institutional receivables.
• **Disbursements Management:** Bulk payroll processing, vendor payments, dividend distribution via automated host-to-host ERP integration.
• **Liquidity Management:**
  - **Sweeping & Pooling:** Automated transfer of idle credit balances from subsidiary/branch accounts across India to a central master account at End of Day (EOD) to maximize interest yield.
  - **Two-Way Sweep:** Reverse sweep from central pool to subsidiary accounts during day to meet liquidity requirements.

## § 8.3 High-Yield Examination Drill

**Q1. A customer tenders a mutilated ₹500 banknote at a bank counter. Upon physical evaluation under the RBI Note Refund Rules, the undivided single largest piece of the banknote measures 65% of the total area of a normal ₹500 note. What is the customer's legal refund entitlement?**  
A. Full face value (₹500)  
B. Half value (₹250)  
C. Zero value (note is rejected)  
D. ₹100 nominal compensation  

**Q2. During cash verification at a bank counter, a cashier detects six (6) counterfeit notes of ₹500 denomination in a single deposit slip tendered by a customer. What is the mandatory immediate regulatory procedure?**  
A. Return the notes to the customer and issue a stern verbal warning  
B. Impound the notes, issue an acknowledgment receipt, and immediately lodge a First Information Report (FIR) with the local police  
C. Tear the counterfeit notes into pieces in front of the customer  
D. Credit the account with real money and claim reimbursement from the RBI Currency Chest  

**Q3. Under the Reserve Bank of India's Clean Note Policy, which of the following operational practices is STRICTLY BANNED across all bank branches?**  
A. Counting banknotes using electronic currency sorting machines  
B. Banding note bundles using paper or plastic wrappers  
C. Stapling, stitching, or pinning banknote packets  
D. Depositing soiled notes into currency chests  

---

## § 8.4 Answer Key & Detailed Explanatory Rationale

• **Q1 — Answer: B.** Under the RBI (Note Refund) Rules, for denominations of ₹50 and above, a mutilated note qualifies for full value if its single largest piece area is **$\ge$ 80%**. If the area is **$\ge$ 40% but < 80%**, it is eligible for **half value**. With 65% area, the ₹500 note receives half value (₹250).  
• **Q2 — Answer: B.** When **5 or more counterfeit notes** are detected in a single transaction, the bank is legally required to impound the notes, provide an impounding receipt, and lodge an immediate **FIR** with the police. (For up to 4 notes, a consolidated monthly report suffices).  
• **Q3 — Answer: C.** The Clean Note Policy explicitly bans the **stapling, stitching, or pinning** of banknote packets, as it damages the paper fibers and reduces banknote longevity. Packets must be secured exclusively with paper or plastic bands.
