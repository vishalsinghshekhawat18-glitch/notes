# PRINCIPLES OF LENDING, CREDIT TAXONOMY & LOAN MANAGEMENT

Credit and advances constitute the principal earning assets of commercial banking. Sound lending requires adherence to established cardinal principles of credit, rigorous operational facility taxonomy, transparent benchmark-linked interest pricing, and strict adherence to the Fair Practices Code and statutory recovery agent regulations.

---

## § 13.1 Unit 22: Principles of Lending & Credit Facilities Taxonomy

> **Curriculum Alignment — Official IIBF Paper 2 Benchmark (Unit 22)**  
> **Core Proposition:** The canonical principles of lending balance safety of depositors' funds with commercial profitability. Credit facilities are bifurcated into Fund-Based (direct cash outflow) and Non-Fund-Based (contingent liability converting to fund-based only upon borrower default).

### 1. The Canonical Principles of Lending

1. **Safety:** The foremost cardinal principle. Money lent belongs to depositors; ensuring timely repayment of principal and interest is paramount.
2. **Liquidity:** The borrower's ability to maintain sufficient cash flows to meet contractual repayment schedules as and when due.
3. **Profitability:** Yield on advances must cover cost of funds, operational overheads, credit risk provisioning, and yield an adequate net interest margin (NIM).
4. **Purpose:** The purpose of the advance must be productive, legally permissible, and capable of generating cash flow. Lending for speculative activities (gambling, hoarding, speculative stock buybacks) is prohibited.
5. **Spread of Risk (Diversification):** Avoiding concentration of credit in single borrowers, groups, or industrial sectors through RBI Large Exposure Framework caps.

### 2. Credit Facility Taxonomy

| Facility Classification | Structural Nature & Drawal Mechanism | Repayment Architecture | Primary Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cash Credit (CC)** | Fund-based revolving credit against pledge/hypothecation of working capital assets (stocks/debtors). | Running account; interest charged on daily utilized debit balance. | Financing working capital gap in manufacturing and trading. |
| **Overdraft (OD)** | Permitted drawing beyond credit balance in a current account against collateral (FD, shares, property). | Running account; fluctuating balance within sanctioned limit. | Temporary liquidity adjustments for business or professionals. |
| **Demand Loan (DL)** | Fixed loan amount disbursed in lump sum or stages; repayable on demand. | No revolving drawal; interest on entire sanctioned debit. | Purchasing specific machinery, paying taxes, bridge financing. |
| **Term Loan (TL)** | Medium or long-term loan disbursed for capital expenditure. | Repaid in fixed installments (monthly/quarterly) over 3 to 15+ years. | Acquiring fixed assets (land, building, plant, equipment). |
| **Non-Fund Based (NFB)** | Letters of Credit (LC), Bank Guarantees (BG), Deferred Payment Guarantees (DPG). | Bank earns commission; fund outflow occurs only on invocation/crystallization. | International trade, government contracts, capital goods import. |

## § 13.2 Unit 24: Operational Loan Account Management & Recovery Code

### 1. Interest Rate Architecture: MCLR vs. EBLR Framework

• **Marginal Cost of Funds Based Lending Rate (MCLR):**
  - Internal benchmark computed on marginal cost of borrowings, CRR negative carry, operating costs, and tenor premium.
  - Reset periodicity typically 1 year.
• **External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR - Mandatory since October 2019):**
  - All new floating-rate personal/retail loans (home, auto, personal) and floating-rate loans to Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) **MUST be linked to an External Benchmark**:
    1. RBI Policy Repo Rate, OR
    2. Government of India 91-Day or 182-Day Treasury Bill yield published by FBIL.
  - Reset Frequency: Mandated reset at least **once in three months (quarterly)**.
  - Spread: Fixed at loan inception; credit spread can be reset only if borrower's credit assessment undergoes a documented structural downgrade.

### 2. Fair Practices Code & Recovery Agent Regulations

> **Regulatory Compliance — RBI Fair Practices Code for Lenders**  
> Banks must govern operational loan collection through transparent, humane standards:
> 1. **Permitted Calling Hours:** Recovery agents and bank staff can contact borrowers only between **08:00 AM and 07:00 PM**. Calling before 8 AM or after 7 PM is strictly prohibited.
> 2. **Prohibition of Harassment:** Strict ban on threats, verbal abuse, public shaming, calling anonymous numbers, calling unlisted family members/friends, or visiting workplaces without notice.
> 3. **Due Diligence on Recovery Agents:** Mandatory police verification, standard Indian Institute of Banking & Finance (IIBF) Debt Recovery Agent (DRA) certification, and display of authorization identity card.
> 4. **Penal Charges vs. Penal Interest:** Under revised RBI guidelines, penal charges for non-compliance with loan terms must be reasonable and **cannot be capitalized (compounded)** into the principal debt.

## § 13.3 High-Yield Examination Drill

**Q1. Under Reserve Bank of India directives on the External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR) framework, how frequently must commercial banks reset the interest rate on floating-rate retail home loans linked to the RBI Policy Repo Rate?**  
A. Daily at close of business  
B. At least once in three months (quarterly)  
C. Strictly on an annual basis on 1 April  
D. Only when the customer requests a reset in writing  

**Q2. Under the RBI's Fair Practices Code and regulatory instructions on Recovery Agents, during which hours are loan recovery agents legally permitted to contact a borrower at their residence or telephone?**  
A. Between 06:00 AM and 10:00 PM  
B. Between 08:00 AM and 07:00 PM  
C. Between 09:00 AM and 05:00 PM  
D. Round the clock, provided prior SMS notice was delivered  

**Q3. Which of the following credit facilities is classified as a 'Non-Fund Based' banking advance?**  
A. Export Packing Credit  
B. Clean Demand Overdraft  
C. Irrevocable Financial Bank Guarantee  
D. Medium-Term Corporate Loan  

---

## § 13.4 Answer Key & Detailed Explanatory Rationale

• **Q1 — Answer: B.** Under RBI's EBLR framework, commercial banks are mandated to reset the interest rate on external-benchmark linked floating-rate loans at least **once every three months (quarterly)**, ensuring rapid transmission of monetary policy changes.  
• **Q2 — Answer: B.** RBI guidelines strictly stipulate that recovery agents and bank personnel are permitted to contact delinquent borrowers for recovery **only between 08:00 AM and 07:00 PM**. Contacting outside this statutory window is a severe regulatory violation.  
• **Q3 — Answer: C.** A **Bank Guarantee** is a Non-Fund Based facility. The bank does not disburse funds upon sanction; it undertakes a contingent liability that crystallizes into an actual fund outflow only if the applicant defaults on the underlying obligation.
