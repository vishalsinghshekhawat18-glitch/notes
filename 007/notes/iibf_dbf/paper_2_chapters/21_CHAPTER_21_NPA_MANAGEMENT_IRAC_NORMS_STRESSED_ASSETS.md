# NPA MANAGEMENT, IRAC NORMS & STRESSED ASSET RESOLUTION

Prudential norms on Income Recognition, Asset Classification, and Provisioning (IRAC) ensure the transparency and solvency of commercial banking balance sheets. Governed by RBI's Master Circular on IRAC and the Prudential Framework for Resolution of Stressed Assets (June 7, 2019), banks must classify credit assets into distinct risk buckets, allocate mandatory capital provisions, and execute time-bound resolution plans.

---

## § 21.1 Unit 28: Asset Classification & Provisioning Norms (IRAC Framework)

> **Curriculum Alignment — Official IIBF Paper 2 Benchmark (Unit 28)**  
> **Core Proposition:** An asset becomes non-performing when it ceases to generate income for the bank. Under the 90-day delinquency norm, an advance is classified as an NPA if interest and/or installment of principal remains overdue for more than 90 days in respect of a term loan, or if the account remains 'out of order' in cash credit / overdraft.

### 1. Classification Categories & Provisioning Matrix

| Asset Classification Category | Delinquency / Default Criteria | Secured Portion Provision | Unsecured Portion Provision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Assets** | Fully performing; normal servicing. | • Direct Agri / SME: **0.25%**<br>• Commercial Real Estate (CRE): **1.00%**<br>• Other Advances: **0.40%** | Not applicable |
| **Substandard Assets** | Remained as NPA for a period **$\le$ 12 months**. | **15%** of outstanding balance | **25%** (ab initio unsecured: 100%) |
| **Doubtful Asset — D1** | Remained in substandard category for up to **1 year**. | **25%** of secured value | **100%** of unsecured exposure |
| **Doubtful Asset — D2** | Remained doubtful for **1 to 3 years**. | **40%** of secured value | **100%** of unsecured exposure |
| **Doubtful Asset — D3** | Remained doubtful for **more than 3 years**. | **100%** of secured value | **100%** of unsecured exposure |
| **Loss Assets** | Identified as uncollectible by bank, auditors, or RBI inspection, but not written off entirely. | **100% Provision** | **100% Provision** |

### 2. Operational Invariants in NPA Identification

• **Out of Order Status (Cash Credit / Overdraft):**
  - An account is treated as 'out of order' if:
    1. The outstanding balance exceeds the sanctioned limit / drawing power continuously for **90 days**, OR
    2. There are no credits continuously for 90 days, or credits are insufficient to cover interest debited during the same period.
• **Agricultural Advances NPA Norm:**
  - **Short Duration Crops:** Loan becomes NPA if installment of principal or interest remains unpaid for **two (2) crop seasons** beyond the due date.
  - **Long Duration Crops (crops with season > 1 year, e.g., sugarcane):** Loan becomes NPA if unpaid for **one (1) crop season** beyond due date.
• **Borrower-Wise Classification:** NPA classification is **borrower-wise, not facility-wise**. If one credit facility of a borrower turns NPA, all other facilities granted to that same borrower across all branches must be classified as NPA.

## § 21.2 Resolution of Stressed Assets: June 7, 2019 Framework & Recovery Modes

### 1. RBI Prudential Framework for Resolution of Stressed Assets (June 7, 2019)

• **Early Identification (SMA Slabs):**
  - **SMA-0:** Principal or interest overdue for **1 to 30 days**.
  - **SMA-1:** Principal or interest overdue for **31 to 60 days**.
  - **SMA-2:** Principal or interest overdue for **61 to 90 days**.
• **Review Period & Resolution Plan (RP):**
  - Upon default by a borrower with aggregate exposure $\ge$ ₹2,000 Crore (and subsequently extended to lower tiers), lenders are given a **Review Period of 30 days** to decide on a resolution strategy.
  - Lenders must sign an **Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA)**. Decisions agreed to by lenders representing **75% by value of total outstanding credit** and **60% by number of lenders** are binding on all signatories.
  - The Resolution Plan must be implemented within **180 days** from the end of the Review Period.
  - If RP is not implemented within 180 days, banks must make an **additional provision of 20%**; if not implemented within 365 days, additional provision rises to **35%**!

### 2. Modes of NPA Clean-Up & Resolution

> **Auditor Finding Fix — Precision in Write-Offs & Settlements (H-12)**  
> 1. **Technical Write-Off (Prudential Write-Off):** The bank removes the bad loan from its balance sheet assets by debiting its provisioning reserves to optimize capital adequacy and reduce Gross NPAs, **without relinquishing its legal right to recover the debt**. Recovery actions continue unabated.
> 2. **Compromise / One-Time Settlement (OTS):** A negotiated consensual settlement where the bank agrees to accept a lump-sum amount less than the full ledger dues, granting a complete legal release to the borrower. Governed strictly by Board-approved policies.
> 3. **Prudential Restructuring:** Modification of loan terms (extending repayment tenor, reducing interest rate, converting debt to equity) granted to a financially stressed borrower with a viable business model. Asset classification downgrades immediately to NPA unless specific regulatory dispensations apply.

## § 21.3 High-Yield Examination Drill

**Q1. A commercial term loan has an outstanding balance of ₹100 Lakh. It has remained in the 'Doubtful' category for two and a half years (D2 stage). The realisable market value of the tangible security held by the bank is evaluated at ₹60 Lakh. What is the total provisioning requirement for this loan under RBI IRAC norms?**  
A. ₹40 Lakh  
B. ₹55 Lakh  
C. ₹64 Lakh  
D. ₹100 Lakh  

**Q2. Under the Reserve Bank of India's asset classification norms for agricultural crop loans, when is a short-duration crop loan officially classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA)?**  
A. If interest or installment remains overdue for 90 days  
B. If interest or installment remains overdue for two (2) crop seasons beyond the due date  
C. If interest or installment remains overdue for one (1) crop season beyond the due date  
D. If the crop suffers damage certified by the local revenue officer  

**Q3. Under the RBI Prudential Framework for Resolution of Stressed Assets (7 June 2019), what voting threshold is legally required among participating lenders to make an Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA) resolution decision binding on all lenders?**  
A. 51% by value and 51% by number  
B. 66% by value and 66% by number  
C. 75% by value of total outstanding credit and 60% by number of lenders  
D. 100% unanimous consent of all lenders  

---

## § 21.4 Answer Key & Detailed Explanatory Rationale

• **Q1 — Answer: C.** In the **Doubtful-2 (D2)** stage (doubtful for 1 to 3 years), the provision on the **secured portion is 40%**, and the provision on the **unsecured portion is 100%**:
  - $\text{Secured Portion} = \text{₹60 Lakh} \implies \text{Provision (40%)} = 0.40 \times 60 = \text{₹24 Lakh}$
  - $\text{Unsecured Portion} = 100 - 60 = \text{₹40 Lakh} \implies \text{Provision (100%)} = 1.00 \times 40 = \text{₹40 Lakh}$
  - $\text{Total Provision Required} = 24 + 40 = \mathbf{₹64\text{ Lakh}}$.  
• **Q2 — Answer: B.** For **short-duration crops** (crops whose harvest cycle does not exceed one year), the loan turns into an NPA if unpaid for **two (2) crop seasons** beyond the due date. For long-duration crops, the delinquency norm is one crop season.  
• **Q3 — Answer: C.** Under the 7 June 2019 Prudential Framework, an Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA) resolution plan is binding if approved by lenders representing at least **75% by value** of the total outstanding facilities and at least **60% by number** of participating lenders.
