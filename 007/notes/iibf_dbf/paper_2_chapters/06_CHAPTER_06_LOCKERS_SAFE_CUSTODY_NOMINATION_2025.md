# LOCKERS, SAFE CUSTODY & BANKING LAWS 2025 NOMINATION ARCHITECTURE

Safe deposit lockers and safe custody facilities represent essential para-banking customer services governed by the Transfer of Property Act, Contract Act, and RBI Master Directions. The nomination architecture—comprehensively amended by the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025—provides a streamlined mechanism for asset transmission without succession complications.

---

## § 6.1 Unit 05b: The Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025 — Modernized Nomination

> **P0 Audit Fix — Stop-Ship Regulatory Clearance (C-01)**  
> Previously, Section 45ZA of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 permitted only a **single nominee** for an individual deposit account. On **1 November 2025**, the **Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025** came into force, radically modernizing nomination rights.

### 1. Reconciled Nomination Rules: 2025 Statutory Baseline

| Deposit / Facility Type | Previous Statutory Rule (Pre-2025) | REVISED STATUTORY LAW (Banking Laws Amendment Act, 2025) | Operational Guidelines |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Deposit Accounts** (Savings, Current, FD, RD) | Only **ONE** nominee permitted per individual account. | • **Simultaneous Nomination:** Up to **FOUR (4) NOMINEES** permitted simultaneously.<br>• **Successive Nomination:** Account holder may specify nominees in succession.<br>• Customer defines percentage share for each simultaneous nominee (totaling 100%). | Nominee gets valid discharge of bank's liability; acts as trustee for legal heirs. |
| **Safe Deposit Lockers (Sole Hirer)** | Only **ONE** nominee permitted. | Up to **FOUR (4) nominees** permitted simultaneously or successively. | Nominee receives access to locker contents and can inventory/remove items. |
| **Safe Deposit Lockers (Joint Hirers)** | Maximum **TWO** nominees permitted. | Modernized survivor/nominee access framework allows seamless handover. | Access granted jointly to survivor(s) and nominee(s) of deceased hirer. |
| **Safe Custody Articles** | Only **ONE** nominee permitted. | Successive and simultaneous nomination framework extended. | Bank obtains full legal discharge upon delivery to validated nominee. |

### 2. Legal Status of Nominee vs. Legal Heir

• A nominee is **NOT an absolute owner** of the deposit funds or locker contents.
• Settled Supreme Court Law (*Sarojini Amma v. Neelakanta Pillai* & *Ram Chander Talwar v. Devinder Kumar Talwar*):
  - Nomination confers only the **right to receive payment** from the bank.
  - The nominee holds the funds as an **Agent / Trustee for the legal heirs** of the deceased under the applicable law of succession (Hindu Succession Act, Indian Succession Act, etc.).
  - Payment to the nominee gives the bank **complete and valid statutory discharge** against claims by legal heirs under Section 45ZA(2) of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

## § 6.2 Unit 15b: RBI Safe Deposit Locker & Safe Custody Directives

### 1. Banker's Liability & Compensation Framework

• **Lessor-Lessee Relationship:** Hiring a safe deposit locker creates a **Lessor (Bank) and Lessee (Customer)** relationship, NOT bailor-bailee. The customer has exclusive physical access to the contents via his key; the bank holds the master key and has no knowledge of what is stored.
• **100x Liability Cap (RBI Revised Locker Directions):**
  - For incidents such as fire, theft, burglary, robbery, building collapse, or **fraud committed by bank employees**:
  - The bank's liability is strictly capped at **100 times the prevailing annual rent** of the locker.
  - The bank is **NOT liable** for damage/loss caused by natural calamities (floods, earthquakes, lightning) or customer negligence.
• **Term Deposit Security for Lockers:**
  - To ensure prompt payment of locker rent, banks are permitted to obtain a Fixed Deposit covering up to **3 years of locker rent** plus estimated charges for breaking open the locker in case of default.
  - Banks **CANNOT demand excessive term deposits** or insist on fixed deposits from existing customers with satisfactory operations.
• **Settlement of Deceased Claims:**
  - Banks must settle claims and grant access to locker/deposit balances to nominees/survivors within **15 days** of receipt of claim form, death certificate, and identity verification.

## § 6.4 Answer Key & Detailed Explanatory Rationale

• **Q1 — Answer: C.** The Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025 (effective 1 Nov 2025) amended Section 45ZA of the BR Act to permit **simultaneous nomination of up to four (4) persons** for deposit accounts, with defined proportionate percentage shares, as well as successive nominations.  
• **Q2 — Answer: C.** Under RBI's revised locker guidelines, the bank's maximum liability for loss due to fire, theft, burglary, dacoity, building collapse, or staff fraud is capped at **100 times the prevailing annual rent** of the safe deposit locker.  
• **Q3 — Answer: B.** Established Supreme Court jurisprudence affirms that a nominee is merely an authorized receiver and statutory trustee for the legal heirs of the deceased. Handing over funds to the nominee gives the bank full legal discharge from liability, but legal heirs can claim their inheritance from the nominee under succession law.
