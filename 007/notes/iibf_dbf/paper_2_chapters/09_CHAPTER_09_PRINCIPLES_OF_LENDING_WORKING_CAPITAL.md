# PRINCIPLES OF LENDING, WORKING CAPITAL & TERM LOAN APPRAISAL

Commercial lending balances Safety, Liquidity, and Profitability. Working Capital finance assessment transitioned from security-oriented pledge limits to need-based appraisal models pioneered by the Tandon and Chore Committees (Maximum Permissible Bank Finance — MPBF) and the Nayak Committee Turnover Method for MSMEs.

## § 9.1 Comprehensive Operational & Legal Analysis

Principles of Lending, Working Capital Assessment & Term Loans

> **Key Concept — Pivotal Concept: Working Capital vs Term Loan Appraisal**
> Banks provide credit under two distinct operational horizons: Working Capital (short-term liquidity for day-to-day operating cycle) and Term Loans (medium-to-long-term capital expenditure for creating productive fixed assets).

## 1. Master Framework: Tandon Committee MPBF Methods

| Method of Lending | Borrower Minimum Margin (NWC Contribution) | MPBF Calculation Formula | Benchmark Current Ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Method 1 (Tandon) | 25% of the **Working Capital Gap (WCG)** [where WCG = Current Assets (CA) − Current Liabilities (CL excluding bank borrowings)] | MPBF = 0.75 \times (CA - CL) | 1.17 : 1 |
| Method 2 (Tandon / Chore Standard) | 25% of the **Total Current Assets (TCA)** funded from long-term sources / Net Working Capital | MPBF = (0.75 \times CA) - CL | 1.33 : 1 |
| Method 3 (Tandon - Restricted) | 100% of Core Current Assets (CCA) + 25% of remaining Real/Fluctuating Current Assets | MPBF = 0.75 \times (CA - CCA) - CL | 1.79 : 1 |

## 2. Key Mathematical Formulas for Credit Appraisal

$\text{DSCR} = \frac{\text{Net Profit After Tax} + \text{Depreciation} + \text{Interest on Term Loan}}{\text{Interest on Term Loan} + \text{Principal Term Loan Installment}}$

*Explanation:* Measures borrower's ability to service annual term debt obligations. Benchmark: Average DSCR should ideally be between 1.50 and 2.00. Minimum acceptable DSCR is generally 1.20.

$\text{BEP (\% of capacity)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{Installed Capacity Output} \times (\text{Selling Price} - \text{Variable Cost per unit})} \times 100$

*Explanation:* Indicates the level of production/sales where Total Revenue equals Total Cost (Zero Profit, Zero Loss). Lower BEP indicates higher margin of safety for lenders.

> **Exam Anchor & Trap:**
> Top Exam Traps on Working Capital & Term Loans:
1. **Tandon Method 2 Formula Trap:** Method 2 margin is 25% of **Total Current Assets**, NOT 25% of Working Capital Gap. Formula is: \(\text{MPBF} = (0.75 \times CA) - CL\).
2. **Current Ratio Benchmark:** Method 1 gives a Current Ratio of **1.17:1**, whereas Method 2 mandates a minimum Current Ratio of **1.33:1**.
3. **Nayak Committee Parameters:** Nayak Committee applies to credit limits up to **₹5 Crores** for MSEs. Projected annual turnover \(T\) is assessed; Total Working Capital Requirement = \(25\% \text{ of } T\), of which Bank Finance = \(20\% \text{ of } T\) and Borrower Margin = \(5\% \text{ of } T\).

### Practice Questions & Solved Numerical Drills (IIBF Pattern)

**Q1:** Under the Tandon Committee Method II of Maximum Permissible Bank Finance (MPBF), what is the minimum Net Working Capital (NWC) contribution required from the borrower?
• (A) 25% of the Working Capital Gap (WCG)
• (B) 25% of Total Current Assets (TCA)
• (C) 50% of Current Assets
• (D) 10% of Projected Turnover

**Q2:** For working capital limits up to ₹5 Crore for MSME manufacturing units, which appraisal methodology was mandated by the Nayak Committee?
• (A) Tandon Method III
• (B) Cash Budget Method
• (C) Turnover Method (minimum bank finance of 20% of projected annual turnover)
• (D) Chore Committee Peak Deficit System

**Q3:** Which financial ratio serves as the most critical parameter for assessing the repayment capacity of a borrower in Term Loan appraisal?
• (A) Current Ratio
• (B) Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
• (C) Quick Ratio
• (D) Return on Equity (ROE)

#### Solutions & Detailed Explanations

1. **(B) 25% of Total Current Assets (TCA)** — Under Tandon Method II: MPBF = 0.75(TCA) - Other Current Liabilities (OCL). The borrower must finance 25% of TCA from long-term funds, yielding a minimum Current Ratio of 1.33:1.

2. **(C) Turnover Method (minimum bank finance of 20% of projected annual turnover)** — Nayak Committee requires total working capital to be 25% of projected turnover, with borrower contributing 5% and bank financing 20%.

3. **(B) Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)** — DSCR measures earnings available for debt service against principal and interest repayment obligations (ideal benchmark: 1.50 to 2.00).

## § 9.2 Active Recall Diagnostic Vault

<details>
<summary>Formulate the mathematical distinction between Tandon Committee MPBF Method I and Method II.</summary>
Method I: Borrower contributes 25% of the Working Capital Gap (WCG) from long-term surplus funds. Formula: MPBF = 0.75 * (TCA - OCL). Benchmark Current Ratio = 1.17:1. Method II: Borrower contributes 25% of Total Current Assets (TCA) from long-term surplus funds. Formula: MPBF = (TCA - 0.25 * TCA) - OCL = 0.75 * TCA - OCL. Benchmark Current Ratio = 1.33:1.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Define the Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) formula and state the economic implication of a DSCR below 1.0.</summary>
DSCR = (Net Profit after Tax + Depreciation + Term Loan Interest) / (Term Loan Installment Repayments + Term Loan Interest). A DSCR < 1.0 indicates that operating cash flows are insufficient to service contractual debt obligations, signifying imminent default or debt servicing distress.
</details>
