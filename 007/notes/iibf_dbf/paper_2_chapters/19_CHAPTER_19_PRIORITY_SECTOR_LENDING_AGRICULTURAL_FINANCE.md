# PRIORITY SECTOR LENDING & AGRICULTURAL FINANCE

Priority Sector Lending (PSL) directs institutional credit to sectors of high employment, food security, and balanced regional development. Governed by the RBI Master Directions on PSL (2025 Revised Framework), banks must meet overall targets alongside mandatory sub-targets for Agriculture, Small and Marginal Farmers (SMF), Micro Enterprises, and Weaker Sections, complemented by agricultural credit mechanisms such as the Kisan Credit Card (KCC) and crop insurance.

---

## § 19.1 Unit 36: Priority Sector Lending Master Architecture (2025 Baseline)

> **Curriculum Alignment — Official IIBF Paper 2 Benchmark (Unit 36)**  
> **Core Proposition:** Priority sector targets are computed as a percentage of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC) or Credit Equivalent Amount of Off-Balance Sheet Exposure (CEOBE), whichever is higher.

### 1. Master PSL Targets & Sub-Targets Matrix (Reconciled 2025 Directions)

| Category / Sub-Target | Domestic Commercial Banks & Foreign Banks ($\ge$ 20 branches) | Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) | Small Finance Banks (SFBs) | Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs) (2025 Framework) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Priority Sector** | **40% of ANBC** | **75% of ANBC** | **75% of ANBC** | **60% of ANBC** |
| **Total Agriculture** | **18% of ANBC** | **18% of ANBC** | **18% of ANBC** | Not Applicable |
| **Small & Marginal Farmers (SMF)** | **10% of ANBC** | **10% of ANBC** | **10% of ANBC** | Not Applicable |
| **Micro Enterprises Sub-Target** | **7.5% of ANBC** *(P0 Fix C-02)* | **7.5% of ANBC** | **7.5% of ANBC** | **7.5% of ANBC** |
| **Weaker Sections** | **12% of ANBC** | **15% of ANBC** | **12% of ANBC** | **12% of ANBC** |

> **P0 Audit Fix — Stop-Ship Regulatory Clearance (C-02):**  
> The mandated sub-target for advances to **Micro Enterprises is strictly 7.5% of ANBC / CEOBE**, NOT 8.0%. (The earlier temporary trajectory targeting 8% was harmonized at 7.5% in the current directions).

### 2. Treatment of Shortfalls: RIDF Architecture

Banks failing to achieve their priority sector targets or sub-targets are not permitted to retain the shortfall. The Reserve Bank allocates the shortfall amount to be deposited in:
• **Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF)** maintained with NABARD.
• **Urban Infrastructure Development Fund (UIDF)** with National Housing Bank (NHB).
• **Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) / MUDRA funds**.
• Interest rates paid on RIDF deposits are inversely related to the degree of shortfall (banks with higher shortfall earn lower interest yields).

## § 19.2 Unit 37: Agricultural Financing & Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

### 1. Types of Agricultural Credit

• **Short-Term Crop Loans:** Production credit provided for purchasing seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, labor, and harvesting expenses. Repayable after harvest.
• **Medium / Long-Term Investment Credit:** Financing tractors, tube-wells, drip irrigation, poly-houses, dairy, poultry, orchards. Repayable over 3 to 15 years.
• **Scale of Finance:** The credit requirement per acre for each specific crop is determined annually by the **District Level Technical Committee (DLTC)** chaired by the District Collector.

### 2. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Operational Architecture

• **Sanction & Validity:** Sanctioned as a revolving cash credit facility valid for **five (5) years**, with an automatic annual limit increase of **10%** from the second year onwards to account for cost escalation.
• **KCC Limit Computation:**
  $$\text{1st Year Limit} = (\text{Scale of Finance} \times \text{Crop Area}) + \text{10% for Household / Post-Harvest} + \text{20% for Maintenance of Farm Assets}$$
• **Interest Subvention Scheme (Modified Interest Subvention Scheme - MISS):**
  - Standard lending rate on crop loans up to **₹3 Lakh** is 9.0% p.a.
  - Government provides a **1.5% p.a. interest subvention** to lending institutions, lowering basic rate to 7.0%.
  - Farmers ensuring prompt repayment receive an additional **3.0% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI)**.
  - **Effective Interest Rate to Farmers:** Exactly **4.0% p.a.** on crop loans up to ₹3 Lakh!
• **Collateral-Free Agricultural Loan Limit (RBI Enhanced Benchmark):**
  - The mandatory threshold for collateral-free agricultural loans stands enhanced from ₹1.60 Lakh to **₹2.00 Lakh per borrower** (effective December 2024 per RBI circular / Annual Report benchmark; no margin or collateral can be obtained).
  - Can be extended up to ₹3.00 Lakh for loans with tie-up arrangements for recovery.
• **Distinction Between Agriculture Credit Thresholds (Top Examiner Trap):**
  1. *Collateral-Free Mandate:* Exactly **₹2.00 Lakh**.
  2. *Interest Subvention (MISS) Maximum Eligibility:* Up to **₹3.00 Lakh** (yielding 4.0% net rate).
  3. *Overall KCC Limit:* Determined by cropping pattern, acreage, and Scale of Finance for 5 years with 10% annual step-up.

### 3. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Comprehensive crop insurance against non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, pests):
• **Maximum Premium Payable by Farmers:**
  - Kharif Crops (Food & Oilseeds): **2.0% of Sum Insured**.
  - Rabi Crops (Food & Oilseeds): **1.5% of Sum Insured**.
  - Annual Commercial / Horticultural Crops: **5.0% of Sum Insured**.
• Difference between actuarial premium rate and farmer premium is shared 50:50 between Central and State Governments.

B. 3.0% p.a.  
C. 4.0% p.a.  
D. 7.0% p.a.

C. 2.0% of Sum Insured

## § 19.4 Answer Key & Detailed Explanatory Rationale

• **Q1 — Answer: B.** Under RBI's current PSL Master Directions, the mandatory sub-target for advances to **Micro Enterprises is 7.5% of ANBC / CEOBE** for all domestic commercial banks and foreign banks with $\ge$ 20 branches. (An option stating 8% is outdated and incorrect).  
• **Q2 — Answer: C.** The basic lending rate is 9.0%. With the GoI 1.5% interest subvention, the nominal rate is 7.0%. Farmers ensuring prompt repayment receive an additional 3.0% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI), resulting in a net effective borrowing cost of **4.0% p.a.** up to ₹3 Lakh.  
• **Q3 — Answer: C.** Under PMFBY, the farmer premium is statutorily capped at **2.0% of Sum Insured for Kharif food and oilseed crops**, 1.5% for Rabi crops, and 5.0% for commercial/horticultural crops.
