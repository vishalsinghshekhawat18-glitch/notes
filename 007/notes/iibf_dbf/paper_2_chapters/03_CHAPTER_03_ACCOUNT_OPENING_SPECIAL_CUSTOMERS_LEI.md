# ACCOUNT OPENING, SPECIAL CUSTOMER CATEGORIES & LEI

Opening and managing bank accounts requires strict compliance with statutory capacity, contractual law, and RBI accessibility mandates. Special customer classes—including minors, joint accounts, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), illiterate individuals, visually impaired persons, and non-individual entities—operate under specialized legal frameworks designed to balance operational convenience with risk containment.

---

## § 3.1 Unit 04: Customer Due Diligence & Special Customer Types

> **Curriculum Alignment — Official IIBF Paper 2 Benchmark (Unit 04)**  
> **Core Proposition:** Contractual capacity is governed by Section 11 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872. A person must be of the age of majority, sound mind, and not disqualified by any law. In banking, specific operational dispensations allow minors, illiterate persons, and disabled individuals to access institutional financial services under statutory safeguards.

### 1. Special Categories of Individual Customers

| Customer Class | Legal & Contractual Basis | Core Operational Rules & Non-Negotiable Safeguards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minor (Individual Account)** | Sec 11 Contract Act / Hindu Minority & Guardianship Act | • Minors aged **10 years and above** can open and operate independent savings accounts in their own name.<br>• Account must **ALWAYS remain in credit**; overdrafts are void *ab initio*.<br>• Minor cannot be personally sued for debts; cannot ratify debt upon attaining majority. |
| **Minor (Under Guardianship)** | Father / Mother natural guardians; Court-appointed guardian | • Natural guardians: Father and mother have equal status post *Githa Hariharan* Supreme Court verdict.<br>• Guardian ceases to operate account immediately upon minor attaining **18 years** (or **21 years** if court-appointed guardian under Guardians & Wards Act). |
| **Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)** | Mitakshara / Dayabhaga School; Hindu Succession Act | • Governed by **Karta** (senior-most coparcener; post-2005 amendment, **daughters are coparceners by birth and can be Karta**).<br>• Account opened in HUF name; operated exclusively by Karta.<br>• Karta has unlimited liability; other coparceners' liability is limited to their share in joint family property. |
| **Illiterate Customer** | Contract Act (Thumb impression attestation) | • Account opened via **Left Thumb Impression (LTI)** for males / **Right Thumb Impression (RTI)** for females, attested by independent witness.<br>• Modern practice: Physical presence at branch with photo verification; cheque book generally not issued except for direct government benefit credits. |
| **Visually Impaired Customer** | Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 / RBI Directives | • **Strict Non-Discrimination:** Same facilities as sighted customers (cheque book, ATM card, net banking, locker).<br>• **No Mandatory Witness:** Banks **CANNOT compel a third-party witness or force thumb impression** if the visually impaired customer can sign or wishes to operate independently. |
| **Purdahnashin Woman** | Common law doctrine of undue influence | • Contracts presumed to be executed under undue influence unless independent legal advice is established.<br>• Branch practice: Identity verified by a female officer or attested by an acceptable referee. |

### 2. Operational Mandates in Joint Accounts

• **Survivorship Mandates:**
  - **Either or Survivor (E or S):** Permitted in accounts of two individuals. Either person can operate during joint lifetime; survivor gets balance on death.
  - **Former or Survivor (F or S):** Only the Former operates during his lifetime. Survivor operates only upon death of the Former.
  - **Anyone or Survivor(s):** In accounts of three or more persons. Any single holder operates; survivors continue operation upon death of one.
  - **Jointly:** Both/all holders must sign every withdrawal, mandate, or cheque.
• **Revocation of Mandate:** Any single joint account holder can give a **Stop Payment instruction** or revoke an operating authority, requiring the bank to stop operations until all joint holders sign jointly.

### 3. Current Account Opening Framework & LEI Architecture

• **RBI Current Account Opening Norms:**
  - For borrowers with aggregate banking exposure **< ₹5 Crore:** Banks can open current accounts without restriction, subject to an undertaking that the borrower will inform when credit facilities reach ₹5 Crore.
  - Exposure **$\ge$ ₹5 Crore:** Borrowers must route all transactions through Cash Credit / Overdraft (CC/OD) accounts. Non-lending banks cannot open current accounts.
• **Legal Entity Identifier (LEI):**
  - A unique **20-digit alphanumeric code** based on ISO 17442 standard.
  - Identifies distinct legal entities participating in global and domestic financial transactions.
  - **Mandatory Thresholds in India:**
    1. All non-individual transactions **$\ge$ ₹50 Crore** routed through RTGS and NEFT.
    2. All non-individual borrowers with aggregate banking exposure **$\ge$ ₹5 Crore** from banks and financial institutions.

## § 3.2 High-Yield Examination Drill

**Q1. Under the Hindu Succession (Amendment) Act, 2005 and RBI Master Directions, which of the following statements regarding the operation of a Hindu Undivided Family (HUF) bank account is legally accurate?**  
A. Only the eldest male member can act as Karta of the HUF under all circumstances  
B. Daughters are coparceners by birth and are legally competent to act as Karta of the HUF  
C. Coparceners have unlimited personal liability for debts incurred by the Karta for business purposes  
D. An HUF account can be operated by any coparcener upon verbal authorization of the Karta  

**Q2. A bank branch refuses to issue a cheque book and ATM debit card to a visually impaired customer, citing operational risk and internal bank safety rules. Under RBI guidelines and the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, this action of the bank is:**  
A. Completely lawful, because banks have autonomous discretion to protect vulnerable customers  
B. Unlawful and discriminatory; visually impaired customers are legally entitled to all banking facilities on par with sighted customers without mandatory third-party guarantees  
C. Permissible only if the branch manager records reasons in writing and obtains an indemnity bond  
D. Lawful provided the customer is allowed free counter transactions with thumb impression  

**Q3. What is the minimum aggregate exposure threshold mandated by the Reserve Bank of India for non-individual borrowers to compulsorily obtain a 20-digit Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)?**  
A. ₹1 Crore  
B. ₹5 Crore  
C. ₹25 Crore  
D. ₹50 Crore  

---

## § 3.3 Answer Key & Detailed Explanatory Rationale

• **Q1 — Answer: B.** The Hindu Succession (Amendment) Act, 2005 conferred equal coparcenary rights on daughters by birth, enabling a woman to be a coparcener and legally act as Karta. In an HUF, only the Karta has unlimited liability; coparceners' liability is restricted to their share in joint family assets.  
• **Q2 — Answer: B.** RBI guidelines strictly prohibit discrimination against visually impaired persons. They must be offered all standard facilities (cheque books, Net banking, ATM cards, lockers) without forcing them to bring witnesses or execute indemnities.  
• **Q3 — Answer: B.** Under RBI guidelines on Legal Entity Identifier (LEI), non-individual entities having aggregate exposure of **₹5 Crore and above** from scheduled commercial banks are mandated to obtain an LEI code. (The ₹50 Crore threshold applies to single large payment transactions via RTGS/NEFT).
