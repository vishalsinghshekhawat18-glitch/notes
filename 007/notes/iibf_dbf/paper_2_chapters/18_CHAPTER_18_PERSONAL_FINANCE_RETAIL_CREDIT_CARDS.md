# PERSONAL FINANCE, RETAIL LENDING & CONSUMER CREDIT

Retail lending has expanded into a primary asset engine for commercial banks, characterized by diversified risk across salaried and professional individuals. Governed by RBI prudential guidelines, retail advances span home loans, vehicle finance, personal loans, educational loans, and credit cards, subject to mandatory Loan-to-Value (LTV) caps, external benchmarking, and strict consumer protection prohibitions against foreclosure penalties.

---

## § 18.1 Unit 35: Retail Loan Products Architecture

> **Curriculum Alignment — Official IIBF Paper 2 Benchmark (Unit 35)**  
> **Core Proposition:** Retail loans are granular, lower-ticket advances evaluated on individual credit scores (CIBIL/Equifax $\ge$ 750), Debt-to-Income (DTI) or Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR), and regulatory collateral margins.

### 1. Master Home Loan Prudential Framework (RBI LTV Caps)

| Individual Housing Loan Slab | Maximum Permissible Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio | Minimum Borrower Margin | Standard Risk Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Up to ₹30 Lakh** | **Up to 90%** (if LTV $\le$ 80%) / **80%** (if LTV > 80% $\le$ 90%) | 10% to 20% | 35% |
| **Above ₹30 Lakh up to ₹75 Lakh** | **Up to 80%** | 20% | 35% |
| **Above ₹75 Lakh** | **Up to 75%** | 25% | 50% |

> **Critical Regulatory Rule — Stamp Duty & Registration in LTV:**  
> In housing loans up to ₹10 Lakh, banks may include stamp duty, registration charges, and documentation costs in the total project cost for calculating LTV. For housing loans **exceeding ₹10 Lakh**, stamp duty and registration fees **CANNOT be included in the property cost** for determining loan eligibility.

### 2. Vehicle, Education & Personal Loans

• **Vehicle Loans:** Secured by hypothecation of vehicle; charge registered with Regional Transport Authority (RTA) in Form 34/35. Standard margin 10% to 20%; repayment 3 to 7 years.
• **Education Loans (IBA Model Scheme):**
  - **Up to ₹4 Lakh:** Zero margin; No collateral or third-party guarantee required.
  - **₹4 Lakh to ₹7.5 Lakh:** 5% margin for studies in India (15% for abroad); third-party guarantee required.
  - **Above ₹7.5 Lakh:** Tangible collateral security of suitable value.
  - **Moratorium:** Course period + 1 year (or 6 months after getting job, whichever is earlier).
• **Absolute Prohibition on Prepayment Penalties:**
  - The Reserve Bank of India **strictly prohibits banks and NBFCs from levying foreclosure charges or prepayment penalties** on:
    1. All **floating-rate term loans** granted to **individual borrowers** (for purposes other than business).
    2. With or without co-obligants.
    3. Even if the loan is prepaid from third-party balance transfer.

## § 18.2 Credit Card Operations & Regulatory Safeguards

• **Credit Card Billing & Minimum Amount Due (MAD):**
  - Minimum Amount Due is usually 5% of total outstanding balance (comprising all taxes, interest charges, fees, plus 100% of EMIs).
  - Failure to pay MAD attracts late payment charges and adverse reporting to Credit Information Companies.
• **RBI Master Direction on Credit Card Conduct (2022/2024 Rules):**
  - **Unsolicited Cards:** Issuing a credit card without written consent is strictly prohibited. If an unsolicited card is activated without consent, the bank must **pay a penalty of twice the billed amount** to the recipient!
  - **Grace Period (Free Credit Period):** Interest can be charged only if payment is not made by the Payment Due Date. A minimum grace period of **three (3) days** beyond the due date must be provided before reporting the account to credit bureaus as overdue.
  - **Closure of Card:** Request for closure must be honored within **7 working days**. Failure to close within 7 days attracts a penalty of **₹500 per day of delay** payable to the customer.

## § 18.3 High-Yield Examination Drill

**Q1. Under Reserve Bank of India prudential guidelines on individual housing loans, what is the maximum permissible Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio allowed for a home loan of ₹50 Lakh sanctioned to an individual?**  
A. Up to 90%  
B. Up to 80%  
C. Up to 75%  
D. Maximum 60%  

**Q2. Under RBI directives on customer protection, under which of the following circumstances is a commercial bank legally permitted to levy prepayment penalties or foreclosure charges on a term loan?**  
A. When an individual borrower prepays a floating-rate housing loan through internal savings  
B. When an individual borrower takes over a floating-rate car loan through a balance transfer from another bank  
C. When a corporate entity or commercial enterprise prepays a fixed-rate business term loan  
D. When an individual student prepays a floating-rate educational loan  

**Q3. Under RBI's Master Directions on Credit Card Operations, within how many working days must a credit-card-issuing bank complete the closure of a card account upon receiving a customer's request?**  
A. Within 48 hours  
B. Within seven (7) working days  
C. Within 15 working days  
D. Within 30 working days  

---

## § 18.4 Answer Key & Detailed Explanatory Rationale

• **Q1 — Answer: B.** For individual housing loans between **₹30 Lakh and ₹75 Lakh**, the statutory ceiling on the Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio is **80%** (requiring a minimum 20% promoter/borrower margin). (For loans up to ₹30 Lakh, LTV is up to 90%; for loans above ₹75 Lakh, LTV is capped at 75%).  
• **Q2 — Answer: C.** RBI guidelines prohibit prepayment penalties on **all floating-rate term loans granted to individual borrowers** (with or without co-obligants). However, banks **can legally levy prepayment penalties on fixed-rate loans and on loans granted to non-individual corporate/commercial business entities**.  
• **Q3 — Answer: B.** Card issuers must process credit card cancellation requests within **seven (7) working days**. If the bank fails to close the card within 7 days, it must pay a statutory penalty of **₹500 per calendar day of delay** to the customer, provided there are no outstanding dues.
