### Epistemic Stance & Learning Objectives
Human health represents a dynamic state of complete physical, mental, and social equilibrium preserved through the coordinated defenses of the immune system. This chapter provides a rigorous scientific survey of infectious pathology (bacterial, viral, protozoan, helminthic, and fungal etiologies) and non-communicable oncogenesis; the molecular architecture of the immune system (innate defenses, humoral versus cell-mediated immunity, immunoglobulin structures, and graft rejection); the pharmacological classifications of vaccines; and the applied recombinant DNA technologies (recombinant Humulin, gene therapy, RNA interference, and agricultural Bt transgenics) that routinely feature in civil services and competitive examinations.

## 27.1 INFECTIOUS DISEASES & PATHOGENIC ETIOLOGIES

Infectious diseases are transmissible pathologies caused by biological pathogens invading and proliferating within host tissues.

```
                         THE PATHOGENIC SPECTRUM
                                    │
     ┌──────────────┬───────────────┼───────────────┬──────────────┐
     ▼              ▼               ▼               ▼              ▼
```

### 27.1.1 Bacterial Pathologies

| Disease | Etiological Pathogen & Morphology | Transmission & Target Organ | Diagnostic Test & Pathognomonic Signs | Treatment & Prophylaxis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Typhoid (Enteric Fever)** | *Salmonella typhi* (Gram-negative motile bacillus) | Fecal-oral route via contaminated food/water; colonizes small intestine, enters bloodstream. | **Widal Test** (serological tube agglutination detecting O and H antigens); persistent high stepped-ladder fever ($39 - 40^\circ\text{C}$), stomach pain, constipation, rose spots on trunk; severe cases: **Intestinal Perforation & Hemorrhage**. | Fluoroquinolones (Ciprofloxacin), Azithromycin; Typhoid Conjugate Vaccine (TCV). |
| **Pneumonia** | *Streptococcus pneumoniae* (Pneumococcus) & *Haemophilus influenzae* | Droplet inhalation / aerosol transmission; infects **Pulmonary Alveoli**. | Sputum culture; chest X-ray; alveoli fill with inflammatory exudate fluid and WBCs; dyspnea, fever, chills, cough; severe cases: **lips and fingernails turn gray to cyanotic blue**. | Broad-spectrum antibiotics (Amoxicillin, Ceftriaxone); Pneumococcal conjugate vaccine (PCV). |
| **Cholera** | *Vibrio cholerae* (Gram-negative comma-shaped bacillus) | Waterborne via fecal contamination; secretes **Cholera Enterotoxin (Choleragen)** activating adenylate cyclase. | Stool microscopy (darting motility); profuse, painless **"Rice-Water Stools"**; rapid catastrophic dehydration ($>10 - 20\text{ L/day}$ fluid loss), hypovolemic shock, metabolic acidosis. | Prompt aggressive **Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS)**, IV Ringer's Lactate; Doxycycline; oral cholera vaccines (Dukoral, Shanchol). |

| **Tetanus ("Lockjaw")** | *Clostridium tetani* (Anaerobic spore-forming drumstick bacillus) | Spores enter deep penetrating wounds/soil; secretes potent neurotoxin **Tetanospasmin**. | Clinical diagnosis; tetanospasmin blocks release of inhibitory neurotransmitters (GABA and Glycine) in spinal cord $\implies$ uncontrolled spastic muscular contractions; **Trismus (Lockjaw)**, **Risus sardonicus** (grimace), **Opisthotonos** (arched spine). | Tetanus Toxoid (TT) active immunization; Anti-Tetanus Serum (ATS / Tetanus Immunoglobulin, TIG) for post-exposure passive prophylaxis. |
| **Plague ("Black Death")** | *Yersinia pestis* (Gram-negative bipolar rod) | Vector: **Rat Flea (*Xenopsylla cheopis*)** feeding on infected rodents (*Rattus rattus*). | Bubonic plague (painful, acutely swollen regional lymph nodes called **Buboes** in groin/axilla); Pneumonic plague (airborne, lethal hemorrhagic pneumonia); Septicemic plague (acral gangrene, tissue necrosis). | Streptomycin, Gentamicin, Doxycycline. Vector flea eradication. |

### 27.1.2 Viral Pathologies
* **Common Cold:** Caused by **Rhinoviruses** ($>100\text{ serotypes}$) and Coronaviruses. Infects the nasal epithelium and upper respiratory tract, but **DOES NOT infect the pulmonary alveoli** (distinguishing it from pneumonia). Self-limiting ($3–7\text{ days}$).
* **Dengue Fever ("Breakbone Fever"):** Caused by **Dengue Virus (Flavivirus / DENV-1 to 4)**; transmitted by the daytime-biting female **Tiger Mosquito (*Aedes aegypti*)**. Characterized by retro-orbital pain, severe arthralgia/myalgia, high fever. Severe complication: **Dengue Hemorrhagic Fever (DHF)** featuring plasma leakage, vascular collapse, and life-threatening **Thrombocytopenia (Plunging Platelet Count $<50,000/\mu\text{L}$)**.
* **Chikungunya:** Caused by Chikungunya virus (Alphavirus); vectored by ***Aedes aegypti***; causes severe, crippling, persistent symmetrical joint pain (arthralgia) that may persist for months.
* **Poliomyelitis (Infantile Paralysis):** Caused by **Poliovirus** (Enterovirus, single-stranded RNA); fecal-oral transmission. Virus enters bloodstream and selectively destroys **Motor Neurons in the Anterior Horn of the Spinal Cord**, causing irreversible flaccid asymmetric muscle paralysis, typically of the lower limbs:
  * *Salk Vaccine (1954):* **Inactivated Polio Vaccine (IPV)**; killed virus administered by intramuscular injection.
  * *Sabin Vaccine (1961):* **Oral Polio Vaccine (OPV)**; live attenuated virus given orally; induces mucosal **IgA immunity** in the gut, essential for herd elimination.
* **Rabies (Hydrophobia):** Caused by **Rabies Virus (Rhabdovirus)**; bullet-shaped negative-sense ssRNA virus. Transmitted via the bite/saliva of rabid mammals (dogs, bats, foxes). The virus travels via retrograde axonal transport along peripheral nerves to the CNS, inducing fatal encephalomyelitis, laryngeal spasms on attempting to swallow liquids (**Hydrophobia**), maniacal agitation, and death. Post-mortem brain examination reveals pathognomonic intracytoplasmic eosinophilic inclusion bodies called **Negri Bodies** in hippocampal and cerebellar Purkinje neurons. Once neurological symptoms manifest, mortality is $\sim 100\%$. Post-exposure prophylaxis requires immediate thorough wound washing with soap and water, active rabies vaccination, and local infiltration of Rabies Immunoglobulin (RIG).
* **Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome (AIDS):**
  * *Etiology:* **Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV-1 and HIV-2)**; an enveloped **Retrovirus** containing two identical copies of single-stranded RNA (ssRNA) and the enzyme **Reverse Transcriptase (RNA-dependent DNA Polymerase)**.
  * *Replication Dynamics:* Viral envelope glycoprotein **gp120** binds with high affinity to the **CD4 Receptor** and chemokine coreceptors (CCR5 / CXCR4) on **Helper T-Lymphocytes ($\text{T}_\text{H}$ / CD4+ cells)** and Macrophages ("HIV factory"). Reverse transcriptase synthesizes viral dsDNA from RNA, which integrates into host nuclear DNA via **Integrase**.
  * *Pathophysiological Crisis:* Progressive, profound depletion of the peripheral **CD4+ T-cell count** from normal levels ($>1000/\mu\text{L}$) to critical threshold **$<200/\mu\text{L}$**, paralyzing cell-mediated immunity. The host succumbs to lethal opportunistic infections: **Pneumocystis jirovecii** pneumonia, *Mycobacterium avium*, Toxoplasmosis, Cytomegalovirus (CMV), and **Kaposi’s Sarcoma** (vascular malignancy caused by HHV-8).
  * *Diagnostic Protocol:*
    1. *Screening Test:* **ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay)** — detects anti-HIV antibodies.
    2. *Confirmatory Test:* **Western Blot** (detects specific viral protein bands: gp120, gp41, p24) or RT-PCR for HIV RNA viral load.
  * *Therapy:* **Antiretroviral Therapy (ART / HAART)**: Triple-drug combination regimens (Reverse transcriptase inhibitors [Zidovudine/Tenofovir] + Protease inhibitors + Integrase inhibitors). ART suppresses viral replication and prolongs life, but cannot eradicate latent integrated proviruses.

### 27.1.3 Protozoan & Helminthic Pathologies
* **Malaria:** Caused by protozoans of the genus *Plasmodium* (*P. vivax, P. falciparum, P. malariae, P. ovale*):
  * **Vectors & Transmission:** Transmitted strictly by the bite of the infected female **Anopheles** mosquito.
  * **Most Lethal Species:** ***Plasmodium falciparum***; causes **Malignant Tertian Malaria**, cerebral malaria, microvascular sequestration of parasitized RBCs, blackwater fever (severe hemolysis and hemoglobinuria), and high mortality.
  * **Life Cycle Dynamics:**

```
                    THE DUAL MALARIA LIFE CYCLE
                    
  [Female Anopheles Mosquito] ──► Bites human, injects infectious SPOROZOITES with saliva
               │
               ▼  (Within 30 minutes)
  [Human Liver Cells]         ──► Exoerythrocytic Schizogony (Multiply asexually -> Merozoites)
               │
               ▼  Merozoites rupture liver cells, enter bloodstream
  [Human Red Blood Cells]     ──► Erythrocytic Schizogony:
                                  Trophozoite (Signet-ring) -> Schizont -> Rupture of RBC
                                       │
                                       ▼  Releases Merozoites + TOXIC **HEMOZOIN** GRANULES
                                  [Chills & Shivering Fever Spike every 48 hrs]
                                       │
                                       ▼  Some merozoites differentiate into
  [GAMETOCYTES in Blood]      ──► Ingested by female Anopheles during blood meal
               │
               ▼
  [Mosquito Gut Cavity]       ──► Sexual Fertilization -> Motile Ookinete -> Oocyst
               │
               ▼  Oocyst ruptures, releasing thousands of sporozoites
  [Mosquito Salivary Glands]  ──► Ready to infect next human host!
```

  * *Pathognomonic Mechanism:* The rhythmic paroxysm of high fever, teeth-chattering chills, and profuse diaphoresis every $48\text{ hours}$ (in tertian malaria) coincides with the simultaneous synchronous **rupture of millions of RBCs**, releasing free merozoites along with the insoluble iron-pigment **Hemozoin** (digested hemoglobin breakdown product).
  * *Therapeutic Pharmacology:* **Quinine** (extracted from the bark of the *Cinchona* tree); **Chloroquine**; **Artemisinin** (Nobel Prize 2015 to Tu Youyou, extracted from *Artemisia annua*) used in Artemisinin-based Combination Therapy (ACT).
* **Amoebiasis (Amoebic Dysentery):** Caused by **Entamoeba histolytica** (protozoan); fecal-oral transmission via tetranucleate cysts. Invades the mucosal epithelium of the large intestine, secreting histolytic enzymes that erode mucosa, forming characteristic **flask-shaped ulcers**. Causes abdominal cramping, stools containing excess mucus and blood streaks. Complication: amoebic liver abscess. Treated with **Metronidazole / Tinidazole**.
* **Visceral Leishmaniasis (Kala-Azar / Black Fever):** Caused by **Leishmania donovani**; transmitted by the bite of the blood-sucking **Sandfly (*Phlebotomus argentipes*)**. Pathogen multiplies intracellularly within reticuloendothelial macrophages, causing massive **Hepatosplenomegaly**, severe pancytopenia (anemia, leukopenia), hyperpigmentation of skin, and wasting. Treated with Amphotericin B, Miltefosine.
* **Filariasis (Elephantiasis):** Caused by the filarial roundworms **Wuchereria bancrofti** and *Wuchereria malayi*; transmitted by the bite of the female **Culex mosquito**. Adult worms reside within human **deep lymphatic vessels** (especially of the lower limbs and scrotum) for decades, triggering chronic persistent granulomatous inflammation, lymphatic obstruction, and massive fibroelastic induration, leading to grotesque enlargement of the legs and scrotum (**Elephantiasis / Hydrocele**). Treated with **Diethylcarbamazine (DEC)**.

## 27.2 THE HUMAN IMMUNE SYSTEM & TRANSPLANTATION BIOLOGY

Immunity is the overall physiological competence of an organism to recognize, neutralize, and eliminate foreign non-self antigens, pathogenic microorganisms, and neoplastic cells.

```
                           THE IMMUNITY ARCHITECTURE
                                       │
           ┌───────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┐
           ▼                                                       ▼
  [INNATE (Non-Specific)]                                 [ACQUIRED (Adaptive / Specific)]
  - Present from birth                                    - Developed upon antigen encounter
  - No immunological memory                               - High specificity & MEMORY
  - Rapid, first line of defense                          - Self vs Non-self discrimination
            │                                                       │
  ┌─────────┴─────────┐                                   ┌─────────┴─────────┐
  ▼                   ▼                                   ▼                   ▼
[Barriers]      [Cellular/Cytokine]                  [HUMORAL (AMI)]     [CELL-MEDIATED (CMI)]
- Physical      - PMNLs, Macrophages                 - B-Cells -> Plasma - T-Cells (CD4+, CD8+)
  (Skin, Mucus) - NK Cells                             cells -> Antibodies- Graft Rejection &
- Physiological - Interferons                        - Target: Bacteria,   Intracellular bugs
  (HCl, Lysozyme) (Virus defense)                      extracellular toxins
```

### 27.2.1 Innate Immunity Barriers
1. **Physical Barriers:** Intact stratified keratinized **Skin** epidermis (mechanical barrier preventing pathogen penetration); sticky **Mucous Membranes** lining the digestive, respiratory, and urogenital tracts trapping micro-debris.
2. **Physiological Barriers:** Low gastric **Hydrochloric Acid ($\text{pH } 1.5 - 2.0$)** killing ingested microbes; salivary and tear **Lysozyme** hydrolyzing bacterial peptidoglycan walls; bile salts; skin sebum (fatty acids).
3. **Cellular Barriers:** Phagocytic leukocytes circulating in blood—**Polymorphonuclear Leukocytes (PMNL-Neutrophils)** and **Monocytes**; resident tissue macrophages (Kupffer cells in liver, alveolar macrophages, microglia in brain); **Natural Killer (NK) Cells** (large granular lymphocytes that induce apoptosis in virus-infected and neoplastic cells without prior sensitization).
4. **Cytokine Barriers (Interferons):**
   * Discovered by Isaacs & Lindenmann (1957). **Interferons ($\alpha, \beta, \gamma$)** are species-specific glycoproteins synthesized and secreted by **virus-infected cells**.
   * Diffuse to neighboring uninfected cells, binding surface receptors and triggering synthesis of **Translation Inhibitory Proteins (TIP / 2',5'-oligoadenylate synthetase)**, which halt viral replication and degrade viral mRNA, protecting surrounding healthy tissues. Recombinant $\alpha$-interferon is clinically deployed in the adjuvant immunotherapy of Hepatitis B/C and cancers (e.g., Kaposi's sarcoma, hairy cell leukemia).

### 27.2.2 Acquired Immunity: Humoral vs. Cell-Mediated

```
                     HUMORAL VS. CELL-MEDIATED IMMUNITY
                     
  [B-Lymphocytes] ──► Differentiate into PLASMA CELLS ──► Secrete ANTIBODIES into Blood & Lymph
                      (Humoral Immunity / Antibody-Mediated Immunity: AMI)
                      Effective against: Extracellular bacteria, circulating viruses, soluble toxins
                      
  [T-Lymphocytes] ──► CD4+ Helper T-cells (Activate B-cells & Macrophages via cytokines)
                  ──► CD8+ Cytotoxic T-cells (Inject PERFORIN & GRANZYMES -> Lyses target cells)
                      (Cell-Mediated Immunity: CMI)
                      Effective against: Intracellular pathogens (TB, viruses), CANCER CELLS,
                      and responsible for ALLOGRAFT ORGAN REJECTION!
```

* **Transplantation Biology & Graft Rejection:**
  * When a patient receives an allograft tissue or organ transplant (kidney, heart, liver), the recipient's immune system recognizes non-self polymorphic **Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC / HLA)** surface antigens.
  * **Cell-Mediated Immunity (T-Lymphocytes)** is **primarily and decisively responsible for graft rejection**.
  * *Clinical Management:* Requires strict HLA tissue-typing, ABO cross-matching, and lifelong therapeutic administration of immunosuppressive drugs:
    * **Cyclosporin A:** Potent immunosuppressive cyclic peptide produced by the soil fungus **Trichoderma polysporum**; selectively inhibits calcineurin, suppressing T-cell activation without suppressing bone marrow hematopoiesis.

### 27.2.3 Immunoglobulin (Antibody) Architecture
Antibodies are $Y$-shaped serum glycoproteins synthesized exclusively by differentiated **Plasma B-Cells**.

```
                           ANTIBODY MOLECULE (H2L2)
                           
               Antigen-Binding Site            Antigen-Binding Site
               (Paratope)                      (Paratope)
                   \    /                          \    /
                   [VL][VH]                        [VH][VL]
                    |    |                          |    |
                    |    |                          |    |
                   [CL][CH1]                      [CH1][CL]
                     \  /                            \  /
                     -SS-                            -SS-   <-- Disulfide Bonds
                       \                              /
                        \─── HINGE REGION (Flexible) ─/
                                     |  |
                                    [CH2][CH2]
                                     |  |
                                    [CH3][CH3]
                                     |  |
                                 Fc Region
```

* **Structural Subunits ($H_2L_2$):** Composed of four polypeptide chains linked by covalent interchain and intrachain **Disulfide Bonds ($-\text{S}-\text{S}-$):**
  * Two identical **Heavy (H) Chains** ($\approx 440\text{ amino acids}, \text{MW } 50\text{ kDa}$).
  * Two identical **Light (L) Chains** ($\approx 220\text{ amino acids}, \text{MW } 25\text{ kDa}$, either $\kappa$ or $\lambda$).
* **Functional Domains:**
  * **Fab (Fragment Antigen-Binding) Region:** Formed by the variable domains of both heavy ($V_H$) and light ($V_L$) chains. The hypervariable terminal sequences form the **Paratope**, displaying high-affinity stereochemical complementarity to the specific **Epitope (Antigenic Determinant)**. Monomeric antibody has two identical paratopes (bivalent).
  * **Fc (Fragment Crystallizable) Region:** Formed strictly by the constant domains of the heavy chains ($C_H$). Mediates effector functions: binds complement proteins ($C1q$), anchors to Fc receptors on macrophages, neutrophils, and mast cells, and mediates placental transport.

| Class | Heavy Chain | Native Quaternary Structure | Percentage in Serum | Major Physiological & Immunological Roles |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$\text{IgG}$** | $\gamma$ (gamma) | **Monomer** ($H_2L_2$) | $\mathbf{\approx 75 - 80\%}$ (Most abundant) | Predominates in secondary immune response; opsonizes bacteria for phagocytosis; neutralizes toxins; **ONLY immunoglobulin capable of crossing the maternal placenta**, conferring passive immunity to the fetus. |
| **$\text{IgA}$** | $\alpha$ (alpha) | **Dimer** joined by **J-Chain** and **Secretory Component** | $\approx 10 - 15\%$ | **Secretory Immunoglobulin**; found in high concentrations in external secretions: **Colostrum (first breast milk)**, saliva, tears, gastrointestinal and respiratory mucus; shields mucosal surfaces against viral and bacterial attachment. |
| **$\text{IgM}$** | $\mu$ (mu) | **Pentamer** (10 antigen-binding sites) joined by a central **J-Chain** | $\approx 5 - 10\%$ | **First antibody class synthesized during primary immune response** and neonatal life; highest molecular weight ($\sim 900\text{ kDa}$); exceptionally potent agglutinating and classical complement-activating agent. |
| **$\text{IgE}$** | $\epsilon$ (epsilon) | **Monomer** | $< 0.1\%$ | Binds via Fc region to high-affinity receptors on **Mast Cells and Basophils**; antigen cross-linking triggers degranulation releasing **Histamine and Serotonin**, mediating **Type I Anaphylactic Allergic Hypersensitivity** (asthma, hay fever, hives) and defending against parasitic helminths. |
| **$\text{IgD}$** | $\delta$ (delta) | **Monomer** | $< 1\%$ | Functions primarily as a membrane-bound antigen receptor on the surface of naive B-lymphocytes, driving B-cell differentiation. |

### 27.2.4 Active vs. Passive Immunity

| Parameter | Active Immunity | Passive Immunity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Production Source** | Produced actively by the **host's own immune system** upon antigenic stimulation. | Received pre-formed; **antibodies generated in another organism** are administered. |
| **Onset of Protection** | **Delayed onset**; requires a latent period ($1–2\text{ weeks}$) for clonal expansion of lymphocytes. | **Immediate protection**; functional circulating antibodies available instantaneously. |
| **Immunological Memory** | **Generates robust long-lasting Memory Cells**; secondary response is immediate and amplified. | **Zero immunological memory**; no memory cells formed; temporary clearance. |
| **Duration of Efficacy** | Long-lasting (often lifelong, e.g., post-measles infection or yellow fever vaccine). | Short-lived; antibodies metabolized and cleared within weeks to months. |
| **Natural Example** | Developing permanent immunity following natural infection with chickenpox or mumps. | Transfer of maternal **IgG across the placenta** to fetus, or **IgA via Colostrum** to nursing infant. |
| **Artificial Example** | Immunization using **Vaccines** (e.g., BCG, Polio, MMR, Hepatitis B). | Emergency administration of **Anti-Tetanus Serum (ATS)**, Anti-Rabies Serum (ARS), or **Anti-Snake Venom (ASV)**. |

## 27.3 CANCER BIOLOGY & ONCOGENESIS

Cancer is characterized by uncontrolled, autonomous cellular proliferation, evasion of apoptosis, insensitivity to anti-growth signals, sustained angiogenesis, and the lethal hallmark of **Metastasis**.
* **Loss of Contact Inhibition:** Normal animal cells in culture grow as a monolayer, arresting mitosis when touching neighboring cells (**Contact Inhibition**). Cancer cells lose contact inhibition, proliferating indefinitely to form three-dimensional disorganized masses called **Tumors (Neoplasms)**.
* **Tumor Classification:**
  1. *Benign Tumors:* Remain confined to their primary site of origin, encapsulated in connective tissue; do not invade adjacent normal tissue or spread to distant sites; slow-growing, surgically resectable.
  2. *Malignant Tumors:* Composed of rapidly dividing dedifferentiated cancerous cells that invade and destroy surrounding parenchyma. Possess the property of **Metastasis**—cancer cells detach from the primary tumor, travel via blood and lymph vessels, lodge in distant vital organs, and initiate secondary neoplastic foci.
* **Molecular Drivers:**
  * **Proto-oncogenes:** Normal genes regulating physiological cell growth and division. Mutations or chromosomal translocations convert them into hyperactive **Oncogenes** (e.g., *ras*, *myc*, *bcr-abl* in Chronic Myeloid Leukemia).
  * **Tumor Suppressor Genes:** Genes encoding proteins that brake cell division or induce apoptosis in damaged cells. Inactivation of both alleles leads to oncogenesis:
    * **$p53$ Gene ("Guardian of the Genome"):** Senses DNA damage; mutated in $>50\%$ of all human malignant cancers.
    * **$Rb$ Gene (Retinoblastoma gene):** Controls $G_1/S$ checkpoint transition.
* **Carcinogenic Agents:**
  * *Physical Carcinogens:* Ionizing radiation (X-rays, gamma rays) causing double-strand DNA breaks; non-ionizing radiation (UV-B rays causing thymine dimers, leading to melanoma).
  * *Chemical Carcinogens:* Tobacco smoke contains **Benzo(a)pyrene** and $N$-nitrosamines (major cause of bronchogenic lung cancer); asbestos fibers (mesothelioma); coal tar; aflatoxins from *Aspergillus flavus* (hepatocellular carcinoma).
  * *Biological / Viral Oncogenes:* Human Papillomavirus (**HPV 16 and 18**, inducing cervical carcinoma; prevented by HPV vaccine Gardasil); Hepatitis B and C viruses (liver cancer); Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV, Burkitt's lymphoma).

## 27.4 APPLIED BIOTECHNOLOGY IN MEDICINE & AGRICULTURE

Recombinant DNA technology (genetic engineering) combines DNA fragments from diverse biological sources in vitro to create novel chimeric genomes.

```
                    RECOMBINANT DNA TECHNOLOGY (rDNA) CASCADE
                    
  [Foreign DNA with Target Gene]              [Cloning Vector: Plasmid pBR322]
                 │                                            │
                 ▼                                            ▼
          Cleaved by the SAME RESTRICTION ENDONUCLEASE (e.g., EcoRI)
                 │                                            │
                 ▼ (Generates complementary sticky ends)       ▼
                 └───────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                         ▼  (Annealing via DNA LIGASE)
                            [RECOMBINANT PLASMID VECTOR]
                                         │
                                         ▼  Transformation into Host (E. coli)
                            [TRANSGENIC HOST BACTERIA]
                                         │
                                         ▼  Mass industrial fermentation in BIOREACTORS
                            [COMMERCIAL PURIFIED RECOMBINANT PRODUCT]
```

### 27.4.1 Recombinant Human Insulin (Humulin)
Historically, therapeutic insulin administered to diabetic patients was extracted from slaughtered cattle and pig pancreases, triggering severe allergic immunological reactions due to foreign non-human amino acid sequence variations.
* **Molecular Architecture of Proinsulin:** Synthesized by human pancreatic $\beta$-cells as an inactive precursor:
  $$\text{Proinsulin} = \text{A-chain (21 aa)} + \text{B-chain (30 aa)} + \mathbf{\text{C-peptide (connecting peptide, 33 aa)}}$$
  During maturation in secretory vesicles, the **C-peptide is enzymatically cleaved and removed**. Fully active mature insulin consists strictly of **A and B chains linked by two interchain disulfide bonds** (and one intrachain bond in the A-chain).
* **Eli Lilly's Recombinant Breakthrough (1983):**
  * American pharmaceutical company **Eli Lilly** synthesized two independent artificial DNA oligonucleotide sequences corresponding to the human insulin A-chain and B-chain.
  * Inserted these sequences into plasmid vectors of **Escherichia coli** containing $\beta$-galactosidase promoters.
  * The A and B chains were produced separately in distinct bacterial cultures, extracted, purified, and **combined chemically in vitro by establishing disulfide bonds**, creating the world's first genetically engineered human insulin: **Humulin**.

### 27.4.2 Clinical Gene Therapy: ADA Deficiency
Gene therapy is the collection of biomedical methods permitting the correction of a hereditary gene defect diagnosed in a child or embryo.
* **The Pioneer Case (1990):** First clinical somatic gene therapy performed on a 4-year-old girl suffering from **Severe Combined Immunodeficiency (SCID)** caused by the congenital absence of the enzyme **Adenosine Deaminase (ADA)**.
  * *Pathophysiology:* Absence of ADA causes toxic accumulation of deoxyadenosine in lymphocytes, destroying both B- and T-cells, leaving the child with zero functional immune defenses ("bubble baby syndrome").
  * *Therapeutic Protocol:*
    1. Lymphocytes extracted from the patient's peripheral blood were cultured in vitro.
    2. A functional, normal human **ADA-cDNA sequence** was introduced into these cultured lymphocytes using an engineered disarmed **Retroviral Vector**.
    3. The genetically corrected lymphocytes expressing active ADA were re-infused into the patient's circulation.
    4. *Limitation:* Because mature somatic lymphocytes possess a finite lifespan, the patient requires periodic, lifelong re-infusions.
    5. *Permanent Cure:* Introducing the functional ADA gene into **pluripotent stem cells extracted from bone marrow or early embryonic stages**.

### 27.4.3 Agricultural Biotechnology: Bt Transgenics & RNA Interference
1. **Bt Cotton & Cry Endotoxins:**
   * The soil bacterium ***Bacillus thuringiensis*** synthesizes crystal protein endotoxins during sporulation, termed **Bt Toxins** (encoded by **$cry$ genes**).
   * *Mechanism of Specific Insecticidal Action:* The Bt crystal toxin is synthesized as an **inactive protoxin**. When an insect pest (e.g., cotton bollworm) ingests the transgenic plant tissue, the protoxin is solubilized and cleaved into an active toxin by the **alkaline pH of the insect midgut**. The activated toxin binds to surface receptors on midgut epithelial cells, creating micro-pores that cause cell swelling, lysis, and fatal starvation of the larva.
   * *Target Specificity:* Harmless to humans and non-target organisms because mammalian stomach acid ($\text{pH } 1.5$) rapidly degrades the protein and lacks the specific alkaline activation and epithelial receptors.
     * **$cryIAc$ and $cryIIAb$:** Confer resistance against **Cotton Bollworms**.
     * **$cryIAb$:** Confers resistance against **Corn Borer**.
2. **RNA Interference (RNAi) & Pest Resistance:**
   * Operates as an innate eukaryotic cellular defense mechanism mediating **post-transcriptional gene silencing**.
   * Deployed to protect tobacco plant roots against parasitic root-knot nematode ***Meloidogyne incognita***:
     * Using *Agrobacterium tumefaciens* vector, nematode-specific genes were introduced into the tobacco plant genome such that both **sense and anti-sense RNA** strands were transcribed.
     * The two complementary strands hybridize to form a **double-stranded RNA (dsRNA)**.
     * Host enzyme **Dicer** cleaves dsRNA into small interfering RNAs (siRNAs), which assemble into the **RNA-Induced Silencing Complex (RISC)**.
     * When the feeding nematode ingests plant sap, the RISC binds and selectively cleaves the corresponding specific vital nematode mRNA, **silencing its translation**, killing the parasite.

3. The live attenuated Oral Polio Vaccine (Sabin) stimulates secretory IgA mucosal immunity in the intestinal tract.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
A) 1, 2 and 3 only  
B) 2, 3 and 4 only  
C) 3 and 4 only  
D) 1, 2, 3 and 4  

**Analytical Answer with Epistemic Breakdown:**
* Statement 1 is **INCORRECT**: Recombinant human insulin (Humulin) was engineered by Eli Lilly by producing chains A and B separately without C-peptide, followed by chemical disulfide linkage in vitro. C-peptide is present in proinsulin, but absent in mature active insulin.
* Statement 2 is **CORRECT**: Infusion of genetically modified adult peripheral lymphocytes is temporary because lymphocytes have a finite lifespan; introduction into embryonic bone marrow stem cells provides a permanent genetic correction.
* Statement 3 is **CORRECT**: Oral polio vaccine (Sabin) colonizes the gut, stimulating localized secretory mucosal **$\text{IgA}$ antibodies**, neutralizing wild poliovirus before entry into the bloodstream.
* Statement 4 is **CORRECT**: Cell-mediated immunity (CD8+ cytotoxic T-lymphocytes) recognizing non-self foreign HLA/MHC antigens is the primary mediator of tissue and organ graft rejection.
* **Correct Option:** **B) 2, 3 and 4 only**.

### Question 2 (Competitive State PSC Standard)
Which immunoglobulin class is unique in its ability to cross the human placental barrier from maternal blood into fetal circulation, providing passive immunity to the neonate?
A) $\text{IgA}$  
B) $\text{IgM}$  
C) $\text{IgG}$  
D) $\text{IgE}$  

**Analytical Answer:**
* **$\text{IgG}$** is the only immunoglobulin class whose Fc region binds specific syncytiotrophoblast Fc receptors (FcRn), transporting it intact across the placental barrier into fetal blood to confer passive systemic immunity against pathogens during early life.
* **Correct Option:** **C) IgG**.

### Question 3 (Advanced Concept Standard)
In the life cycle of the malaria parasite *Plasmodium*, where does sexual fertilization of gametes and subsequent ookinete formation take place?
A) Human liver hepatocytes  
B) Human red blood corpuscles  
C) Salivary glands of female *Anopheles* mosquito  
D) Gut cavity of female *Anopheles* mosquito  

**Analytical Answer:**
* Gametocytes ingested by the female *Anopheles* mosquito during a blood meal mature into male and female gametes within the **lumen of the mosquito's stomach (gut cavity)**. Fertilization occurs in the gut, forming a motile diploid zygote (**Ookinete**), which penetrates the stomach wall to form an oocyst. The oocyst subsequently ruptures, releasing sporozoites that migrate to the salivary glands.
* **Correct Option:** **D) Gut cavity of female Anopheles mosquito**.
