## 28.1 THE 60-SECOND RETRIEVAL SKELETONS (CHAPTERS 01 TO 27)

### PART I: FOUNDATIONAL & APPLIED PHYSICS
* **Ch 01 (Physical Quantities & Measurement):** 7 SI base units (m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd) + 2 supplementary (rad, sr). Fundamental dimensions: $[G] = M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}$, $[h] = ML^2T^{-1}$, $[\eta] = ML^{-1}T^{-1}$. Astronomical units: $1\text{ ly} \approx 9.46 \times 10^{15}\text{ m}$, $1\text{ pc} \approx 3.26\text{ ly} \approx 3.08 \times 10^{16}\text{ m}$. Vernier caliper LC = $1\text{ MSD} - 1\text{ VSD}$.
* **Ch 02 (Kinematics & Dynamics):** Kinematic formulas: $v = u + at$, $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$, $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$. Projectile at $\theta$: Range $R = \frac{u^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$ (maximum at $45^\circ$, equal for complementary angles $\theta$ and $90^\circ - \theta$). Newton's 2nd Law: $\vec{F} = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt} = m\vec{a}$. Friction: $\mu_s > \mu_k > \mu_r$. Centripetal acceleration: $a_c = \frac{v^2}{r} = \omega^2 r$. Banking angle: $\tan \theta = \frac{v^2}{rg}$.
* **Ch 03 (Gravitation & Orbital Mechanics):** Newton's Law: $F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$ ($G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N m}^2\text{/kg}^2$). Acceleration due to gravity: $g = \frac{GM}{R^2} = 9.8\text{ m/s}^2$; at altitude $h$: $g' = g(1 - \frac{2h}{R})$; at depth $d$: $g' = g(1 - \frac{d}{R})$ ($g = 0$ at Earth's center); at latitude $\lambda$: $g' = g - R\omega^2 \cos^2 \lambda$ ($g$ maximum at poles, minimum at equator). Kepler's 3rd Law: $T^2 \propto a^3$. Orbital velocity: $v_o = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{R}} \approx 7.92\text{ km/s}$. Escape velocity: $v_e = \sqrt{2} v_o = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}} \approx 11.2\text{ km/s}$. Geostationary orbit: $h \approx 35,786\text{ km}$, period $24\text{ hours}$, west-to-east in equatorial plane.
* **Ch 04 (Work, Energy, Power & Elasticity):** $W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s} = F s \cos \theta$ (zero work if $\theta = 90^\circ$, e.g., centripetal force). Work-Energy Theorem: $W_{net} = \Delta K$. Power: $P = \frac{dW}{dt} = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}$ ($1\text{ hp} = 746\text{ W}$, $1\text{ kWh} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J}$). Hooke's Law: Stress $\propto$ Strain; Young's modulus $Y = \frac{F/A}{\Delta L/L}$ (steel is more elastic than rubber).
* **Ch 05 (Fluid Mechanics):** Pascal's Principle: Hydraulic lift $F_2 = F_1 \left(\frac{A_2}{A_1}\right)$. Archimedes' Principle: Buoyant force $F_b = \rho_{fluid} V_{displaced} g$. Continuity: $A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$. Bernoulli: $P + \frac{1}{2}\rho v^2 + \rho g h = \text{constant}$ (Magnus effect, dynamic lift of wings). Torricelli: $v = \sqrt{2gh}$. Surface tension: $T = \frac{F}{L}$; excess pressure in drop $\Delta P = \frac{2T}{R}$, in bubble $\Delta P = \frac{4T}{R}$; capillary rise $h = \frac{2T \cos \theta}{\rho g r}$. Stokes' Law: $F_v = 6\pi \eta r v$; terminal velocity $v_t \propto r^2$.
* **Ch 06 (Thermal Physics & Thermodynamics):** Temperature scales: $\frac{C}{5} = \frac{F - 32}{9} = \frac{K - 273.15}{5}$ ($-40^\circ\text{C} = -40^\circ\text{F}$). Anomalous expansion of water: maximum density at $4^\circ\text{C}$ ($1.000\text{ g/cm}^3$). Heat transfer: Conduction (Fourier's law), Convection (gravity-driven fluid currents), Radiation (Stefan-Boltzmann: $E = \sigma T^4$; Wien's displacement: $\lambda_{max} T = b$). First Law of Thermodynamics: $\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W$. Carnot efficiency: $\eta = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H}$.
* **Ch 07 (Wave Motion, SHM & Sound):** SHM: $T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}$, simple pendulum $T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$ (independent of mass). Sound: Longitudinal mechanical pressure wave; speed $v = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma P}{\rho}}$ (Laplace); $v_{solid} > v_{liquid} > v_{gas}$ ($v_{air} \approx 343\text{ m/s}$, zero in vacuum). Audibility range: $20\text{ Hz} - 20,000\text{ Hz}$. Echo: minimum obstacle distance $d = \frac{v \times 0.1\text{ s}}{2} \approx 17.2\text{ m}$. Doppler Effect: Frequency shifts higher when source approaches observer.
* **Ch 08 (Optics & Human Eye):** Reflection: $i = r$. Refraction (Snell's Law): $n_1 \sin i = n_2 \sin r$. Total Internal Reflection (TIR): $i > \theta_c$ ($\sin \theta_c = \frac{1}{n}$; optical fibers, mirages, diamond brilliance). Mirror formula: $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$; Lens formula: $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u}$. Lens power: $P = \frac{1}{f\text{ (m)}}$ (Diopters). Dispersion: Cauchy formula ($n_{violet} > n_{red} \implies \text{violet bends most, red least}$). Scattering: Rayleigh $I \propto \frac{1}{\lambda^4}$ (blue sky, red sunset). Eye defects: Myopia (nearsightedness, corrected by Concave lens); Hypermetropia (farsightedness, Convex lens); Presbyopia (Bifocal lens); Astigmatism (Cylindrical lens).
* **Ch 09 (Electrostatics & Current Electricity):** Coulomb's Law: $F = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}$. Capacitance: $C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}$ (dielectric increases $C$ by factor $K$). Ohm's Law: $V = IR$. Resistance: $R = \rho \frac{L}{A}$ (temperature coefficient: $\alpha > 0$ for metals, $\alpha < 0$ for semiconductors). Resistors: Series $R_s = R_1 + R_2$, Parallel $\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}$. Joule's Heating: $H = I^2 R t$. Household power: $220\text{ V}, 50\text{ Hz}$ AC; appliances connected in parallel.
* **Ch 10 (Magnetism & Induction):** Biot-Savart & Ampere's Law. Lorentz Force: $\vec{F} = q(\vec{E} + \vec{v} \times \vec{B})$. Magnetic materials: Diamagnetic ($\chi < 0, \mu_r < 1$; water, copper, bismuth; repelled), Paramagnetic ($\chi > 0, \mu_r > 1$; aluminum, oxygen; weakly attracted), Ferromagnetic ($\chi \gg 1$; iron, cobalt, nickel; Curie temperature $T_C$). Faraday's Law: $\varepsilon = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$ (Lenz's Law gives negative sign, conservation of energy). Transformers: $\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p} = \frac{I_p}{I_s}$ (work strictly on AC, never DC).
* **Ch 11 (Modern Physics, Nuclear & Semiconductors):** Photoelectric Effect (Einstein, 1905): $h\nu = \Phi_0 + K_{max}$ (proves photon particle nature; $K_{max}$ depends on frequency, not intensity; photocurrent depends on intensity). Mass-energy: $E = \Delta m c^2$ ($1\text{ amu} \approx 931.5\text{ MeV}$). Radioactivity: $\alpha$ ($^4_2\text{He}$ nucleus), $\beta^-$ (electron), $\gamma$ (high-energy photon); radioactive decay $N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$, half-life $T_{1/2} = \frac{0.693}{\lambda}$. Semiconductors: Intrinsic vs Extrinsic ($n$-type doped with Group 15 pentavalent P/As, electrons major; $p$-type doped with Group 13 trivalent B/Al, holes major). $p-n$ junction diode: Forward bias conducts, reverse bias blocks (rectifier).

## 28.2 THE GRAND CROSS-DOMAIN DISTINCTION MATRIX

| Concept A | Concept B | Definitive Epistemic Criterion / Scientific Distinction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Heat** | **Temperature** | Heat is total thermal energy transferred ($\text{Joules}$); Temperature is average translational kinetic energy of molecules ($\text{Kelvin}$). |

| **Active Transport** | **Facilitated Diffusion** | Active transport pumps solutes *against* electrochemical gradient ($\Delta G > 0$) requiring ATP or ion gradients; Facilitated diffusion moves solutes *down* gradient ($\Delta G < 0$) using carrier/channel proteins without cellular energy expenditure. |

## 28.3 THE 50 DEADLIEST TEST-MAKER TRAPS IN SCIENCE

1. **Acceleration at Maximum Height of Projectile:** At the apex of vertical projectile motion, velocity is instantaneous zero ($v = 0$), but acceleration is **NOT zero**; it remains strictly **$g = 9.8\text{ m/s}^2$ downward**!
2. **Acceleration due to Gravity at Earth's Center:** Inside a solid sphere of uniform density, gravity drops linearly with depth; at the geometric center of the Earth, **$g = 0\text{ m/s}^2$** (a body is weightless, but its mass remains unchanged).
3. **Weightlessness in an Orbiting Satellite:** Astronauts float not because gravity is zero in space ($g$ at $400\text{ km}$ is still $\sim 90\%$ of surface gravity), but because the spacecraft and astronauts are in continuous, synchronized **free-fall toward Earth**!
4. **Centripetal Force Work Done:** In uniform circular motion, centripetal force is perpetually perpendicular to instantaneous displacement ($\theta = 90^\circ$). The work done is **strictly ZERO** ($W = F s \cos 90^\circ = 0$).
5. **Sound Propagation in Vacuum:** Light travels fastest in a vacuum ($3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$), but **sound CANNOT propagate in a vacuum** because it is a mechanical pressure wave requiring an elastic material medium.
