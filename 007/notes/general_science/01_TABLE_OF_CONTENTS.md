## Detailed Chapter Waypoint Breakdown

### Part I: Foundational & Applied Physics

#### Chapter 01: Physical Quantities, Units, Measurement & Error Analysis
* **1.1 Base vs. Derived Quantities**: 7 SI Base Units (meter, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole, candela) & 2 Supplementary Units (radian, steradian).
* **1.2 Systems of Units**: CGS, MKS, FPS, and the SI BIPM standard definitions (post-2019 redefining of kilogram via Planck constant $h$).
* **1.3 Astronomical & Microscopic Units**: Light Year ($9.46 \times 10^{15}\text{ m}$), Astronomical Unit ($1.496 \times 10^{11}\text{ m}$), Parsec ($3.26\text{ light years}$), Fermi/Femtometer ($10^{-15}\text{ m}$), Angstrom ($10^{-10}\text{ m}$).
* **1.4 Dimensional Formulas**: Derivation of dimensions for force, work, pressure, power, surface tension, viscosity, and gravitational constant $G$.
* **1.5 Scalars vs. Vectors**: Directional dependence, vector addition triangle law; common traps (Current, Pressure, and Surface Tension are scalars/tensors, not vectors).
* **1.6 Scientific Instruments & Devices**: Vernier calipers, screw gauge, barometer, altimeter, hygrometer, anemometer, pyrometer, galvanometer.

#### Chapter 02: Kinematics, Dynamics, Friction & Laws of Motion
* **2.1 Motion in One Dimension**: Distance vs. Displacement, Speed vs. Velocity, Acceleration, Uniform vs. Non-uniform motion.
* **2.2 Kinematic Equations**: Derivations and problem applications for $v = u + at$, $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$, $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$; Motion under gravity.
* **2.3 Projectile & Circular Motion**: Trajectory equation, angle of projection for maximum range ($45^\circ$), Centripetal vs. Centrifugal force, Banking of roads.
* **2.4 Newton's Laws of Motion**: 
  - 1st Law (Inertia: rest, motion, direction)
  - 2nd Law (Force as rate of change of momentum $F = \frac{dp}{dt} = ma$, Impulse $J = F\Delta t$)
  - 3rd Law (Action-reaction pair, recoiling of gun, rocket propulsion mechanics)
* **2.5 Momentum & Conservation Laws**: Law of Conservation of Linear Momentum in collisions (Elastic vs. Inelastic).
* **2.6 Friction**: Static, Limiting, Kinetic (Sliding vs. Rolling); Coefficient of friction $\mu$, Angle of repose, Lubrication and ball bearings.

#### Chapter 03: Gravitation, Planetary Motion & Satellite Mechanics
* **3.1 Newton's Universal Law of Gravitation**: Inverse square law, Universal gravitational constant $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$.
* **3.2 Acceleration Due to Gravity ($g$)**: Variations with altitude, depth, latitude (centrifugal reduction and Earth's oblateness), and value at poles vs. equator.
* **3.3 Mass vs. Weight**: Absolute mass vs. variable gravitational pull; Apparent weight in elevators (accelerating upward, downward, and free fall weightlessness).
* **3.4 Kepler's Three Laws of Planetary Motion**: Law of Orbits (ellipses), Law of Areas (conservation of angular momentum), Law of Periods ($T^2 \propto r^3$).
* **3.5 Orbital Velocity & Escape Velocity**: Formula derivation for escape velocity ($v_e = \sqrt{2gR} \approx 11.2\text{ km/s}$ for Earth; why Moon has no atmosphere).
* **3.6 Satellites & Space Mechanics**: Geostationary (35,786 km altitude, 24 hr period, equatorial plane) vs. Geosynchronous vs. Polar Sun-Synchronous orbits.

#### Chapter 04: Work, Energy, Power & Conservation Laws
* **4.1 Scientific Definition of Work**: $W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s} = F s \cos\theta$; Conditions for zero work (satellite circular motion, carrying load on head horizontally, wall push).
* **4.2 Energy Typology**: Kinetic Energy ($E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{p^2}{2m}$), Potential Energy (Gravitational $mgh$, Elastic $\frac{1}{2}kx^2$).
* **4.3 Work-Energy Theorem**: Net work equals change in kinetic energy; Mechanical energy conservation.
* **4.4 Power & Commercial Energy Units**: Definition $P = \frac{W}{t}$, Watt, Horsepower ($1\text{ hp} = 746\text{ W}$), Kilowatt-hour ($1\text{ kWh} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J}$).
* **4.5 Collisions**: Coefficient of restitution $e$, kinetic energy loss in inelastic collisions.

#### Chapter 05: Fluid Mechanics, Hydrostatics, Surface Tension & Viscosity
* **5.1 Pressure & Density**: Hydrostatic pressure $P = \rho gh$, Pascal's Law and hydraulic machines (brakes, lift, press).
* **5.2 Atmospheric Pressure**: Barometer (Torricelli vacuum), variation with altitude, boiling point elevation/depression in pressure cooker vs. mountains.
* **5.3 Archimedes' Principle & Buoyancy**: Buoyant force, Law of Floatation, Relative density, Lactometer, Hydrometer.
* **5.4 Surface Tension**: Molecular origin (cohesive vs. adhesive forces), spherical shape of raindrops, action of detergents, hot soup tasting better, Capillary action (Jurin's law).
* **5.5 Viscosity & Fluid Dynamics**: Terminal velocity, Stokes' Law, Streamline vs. Turbulent flow, Reynolds number.
* **5.6 Bernoulli's Theorem**: Conservation of mechanical energy in ideal fluids; Venturimeter, atomizers/sprayers, Magnus effect, dynamic lift of aircraft wings.

#### Chapter 06: Thermal Physics, Thermodynamics & Modes of Heat Transfer
* **6.1 Temperature & Thermometry**: Scales ($^\circ\text{C}$, $^\circ\text{F}$, $\text{K}$), relation $\frac{C}{5} = \frac{F-32}{9} = \frac{K-273.15}{5}$, Triple point of water ($273.16\text{ K}$).
* **6.2 Thermal Expansion**: Linear ($\alpha$), superficial ($\beta$), and volumetric ($\gamma$) expansion; Anomalous expansion of water between $0^\circ\text{C}$ and $4^\circ\text{C}$ and survival of aquatic life in frozen lakes.
* **6.3 Heat Capacity & Latent Heat**: Specific heat capacity $Q = mc\Delta T$, Latent heat of fusion vs. vaporization, steam burns vs. boiling water burns.
* **6.4 Modes of Heat Transfer**: Conduction (Fourier's law), Convection (sea breeze, land breeze), Radiation (electromagnetic nature, black body radiation, Stefan-Boltzmann law, Wien's displacement law).
* **6.5 Newton's Law of Cooling & Kirchhoff's Law**: Good absorbers are good emitters; Thermos flask design.
* **6.6 Laws of Thermodynamics**:
  - Zeroth Law: Thermal equilibrium and basis of thermometer.
  - First Law: $\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W$ (Conservation of energy).
  - Second Law: Clausius and Kelvin-Planck statements; Entropy concept, Carnot engine efficiency.

#### Chapter 07: Wave Motion, Simple Harmonic Motion & Acoustics (Sound)
* **7.1 Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)**: Restoring force, amplitude, frequency, time period of simple pendulum ($T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}$), pendulum clock in summer vs. winter.
* **7.2 Wave Fundamentals**: Longitudinal vs. Transverse waves, wavelength, frequency ($v = f\lambda$), crests, troughs, compressions, rarefactions.
* **7.3 Physics of Sound**: Nature as mechanical longitudinal wave; Propagation medium dependency ($v_{\text{solid}} > v_{\text{liquid}} > v_{\text{gas}}$; no sound in vacuum).
* **7.4 Factors Affecting Speed of Sound**: Temperature (increases with temp: $+0.61\text{ m/s per }^\circ\text{C}$), humidity (faster in moist air), pressure independence.
* **7.5 Characteristics of Sound**: Loudness/Intensity (decibels dB), Pitch (frequency), Quality/Timbre (wave shape/harmonics).
* **7.6 Acoustic Phenomena**: Reflection, Echo (minimum distance $17.2\text{ m}$), Reverberation, Resonance, Infrasound ($<20\text{ Hz}$) vs. Ultrasound ($>20,000\text{ Hz}$, SONAR, bat navigation, ultrasonography).
* **7.7 Doppler Effect**: Apparent shift in observed frequency due to relative motion; Applications in speed guns and astronomy (red shift).

#### Chapter 08: Ray Optics, Wave Optics & The Human Eye
* **8.1 Reflection of Light**: Laws of reflection, Plane mirrors (virtual, erect, laterally inverted, minimum height for full image $\frac{H}{2}$).
* **8.2 Spherical Mirrors**: Concave vs. Convex mirrors, mirror formula $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$, magnification, practical applications (shaving mirror, vehicle rear-view mirror).
* **8.3 Refraction of Light**: Snell's Law ($n_1 \sin i = n_2 \sin r$), absolute and relative refractive index, apparent depth of water, twinkling of stars.
* **8.4 Total Internal Reflection (TIR)**: Critical angle condition ($i > \theta_c$ traveling denser to rarer medium), Mirage, optical fibers, sparkling of diamond, endoscopy.
* **8.5 Lenses**: Convex (converging) vs. Concave (diverging), lens maker's formula, lens power ($P = \frac{1}{f\text{ (m)}}$, Diopter D).
* **8.6 Dispersion & Scattering of Light**: Prism dispersion, VIBGYOR, Rainbow formation (dispersion + refraction + internal reflection), Rayleigh scattering ($I \propto \frac{1}{\lambda^4}$, blue sky, red sunrise/sunset).
* **8.7 The Human Eye & Vision Defects**:
  - Anatomy: Cornea, iris, pupil, lens, retina, rods (dim light) & cones (color vision), accommodation power.
  - Myopia (Short-sightedness; corrected by Concave lens).
  - Hypermetropia (Long-sightedness; corrected by Convex lens).
  - Presbyopia (Aging eye; corrected by Bifocal lens).
  - Astigmatism (Corneal curvature irregularity; corrected by Cylindrical lens).
  - Cataract (Clouding of lens).

#### Chapter 09: Electrostatics, Capacitance & Current Electricity
* **9.1 Electric Charge & Coulomb's Law**: Quantization of charge ($q = ne$), conservation of charge, Coulomb's inverse square law.
* **9.2 Electric Field & Potential**: Field lines, Electric potential and potential difference ($V = \frac{W}{q}$, Volt), equipotential surfaces.
* **9.3 Current Electricity**: Electric current ($I = \frac{q}{t}$, Ampere), drift velocity, Ohm's Law ($V = IR$), Ohmic vs. Non-ohmic conductors.
* **9.4 Resistance & Resistivity**: Factors affecting resistance ($R = \rho \frac{l}{A}$), temperature coefficient of resistance (conductors vs. semiconductors vs. super-conductors).
* **9.5 Combination of Resistors**: Series ($R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2$) vs. Parallel ($\frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}$); Domestic circuit wiring.
* **9.6 Electric Energy & Power**: Joule's Law of Heating ($H = I^2 R t$), Electric power ($P = VI = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$), Electric fuse (high resistance, low melting point lead-tin alloy).

#### Chapter 10: Magnetism, Electromagnetism & Electromagnetic Induction
* **10.1 Magnetic Field & Lines of Force**: Magnetic poles, properties of magnetic lines of force, Earth's magnetism (magnetic declination, inclination/dip, horizontal component).
* **10.2 Magnetic Materials**: Diamagnetic (bismuth, copper, water), Paramagnetic (aluminum, oxygen), Ferromagnetic (iron, nickel, cobalt), Curie temperature.
* **10.3 Magnetic Effect of Current**: Oersted's experiment, Biot-Savart Law, Right-hand thumb rule, Solenoid, Electromagnets.
* **10.4 Force on a Moving Charge**: Lorentz force ($\vec{F} = q(\vec{v} \times \vec{B})$), Fleming's Left-Hand Rule, Electric motor principle.
* **10.5 Electromagnetic Induction (EMI)**: Faraday's Laws of Induction, Lenz's Law (conservation of energy), Fleming's Right-Hand Rule, AC/DC Generators, Transformers (Step-up vs. Step-down, core eddy currents).

#### Chapter 11: Modern Physics, Nuclear Reactions, Radioactivity & Semiconductors
* **11.1 Dual Nature of Radiation & Matter**: Photoelectric effect (Einstein's equation $E = h\nu - \Phi$, photon concept, Nobel Prize 1921), de Broglie wavelength.
* **11.2 Atomic Models**: Thomson model, Rutherford $\alpha$-particle scattering experiment (discovery of nucleus), Bohr's quantum model.
* **11.3 Nuclear Physics & Radioactivity**: Nuclear composition, isotopes, isobars, isotones, mass defect, binding energy ($E = mc^2$), $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ decays and properties, half-life.
* **11.4 Nuclear Fission & Fusion**:
  - Nuclear Fission: Uranium-235 chain reaction, critical mass, nuclear reactor components (fuel, moderator: heavy water/graphite, control rods: cadmium/boron, coolant).
  - Nuclear Fusion: Proton-proton cycle in Sun and stars, Hydrogen bomb, ITER project, comparison of energy density.
* **11.5 Semiconductors & Electronics**: Intrinsic vs. Extrinsic semiconductors (n-type doped with pentavalent, p-type doped with trivalent), p-n junction diode, rectifiers, Transistors, Solar cells, LEDs.

### Part II: Inorganic, Organic & Applied Chemistry

#### Chapter 12: Nature of Matter, States, Separation Techniques & Colloids
* **12.1 Classification of Matter**: Pure substances (Elements & Compounds) vs. Mixtures (Homogeneous vs. Heterogeneous).
* **12.2 States of Matter**: Solid, Liquid, Gas, and the two extreme states: Plasma (ionized gas in stars/fluorescent tubes) and Bose-Einstein Condensate (BEC at ultra-low temperatures).
* **12.3 Phase Transitions & Latent Heat**: Melting, Vaporization, Sublimation (camphor, ammonium chloride, dry ice, naphthalene), Deposition.
* **12.4 Physical vs. Chemical Changes**: Reversibility, energy profile, examples (burning of candle = both, rusting = chemical, dissolving salt = physical).
* **12.5 Solutions, Suspensions & Colloids**: True solution vs. Colloid vs. Suspension (particle size comparison: $<1\text{ nm}$, $1\text{--}1000\text{ nm}$, $>1000\text{ nm}$); Tyndall effect, Brownian motion.
* **12.6 Separation Techniques**: Distillation, Fractional distillation (petroleum refining, air separation), Chromatography, Centrifugation, Sublimation, Evaporation.

#### Chapter 13: Atomic Structure, Quantum Numbers, Isotopes & Radioisotopes
* **13.1 Historical Atomic Theories**: Dalton's Atomic Theory, J.J. Thomson (electron), Rutherford (nucleus), Chadwick (neutron).
* **13.2 Subatomic Particles**: Electron, Proton, Neutron (mass, charge, discoverers); Positron and quarks overview.
* **13.3 Modern Quantum Mechanical Model**: Orbit vs. Orbital, Quantum Numbers ($n, l, m_l, m_s$), Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion principle, Hund's rule of maximum multiplicity.
* **13.4 Atomic Number, Mass Number & Species**: Atomic number ($Z$), Mass number ($A$), Isotopes (Hydrogen: Protium, Deuterium, Tritium), Isobars, Isotones, Isoelectronic species.
* **13.5 Applications of Radioisotopes**: Carbon-14 (archaeological dating), Cobalt-60 (cancer therapy), Iodine-131 (thyroid disorders), Phosphorus-32 (leukemia/agriculture), Uranium-235 (nuclear fuel).

#### Chapter 14: Chemical Bonding, Molecular Structure & Periodic Classification
* **14.1 Historical Periodic Attempts**: Dobereiner's Triads, Newlands' Law of Octaves, Mendeleev's Periodic Table (merits, gaps, limitations).
* **14.2 Modern Periodic Table (Moseley)**: Modern Periodic Law ($f(Z)$), Periods (7) and Groups (18), s, p, d, f blocks.
* **14.3 Periodic Trends**:
  - Atomic radius / Ionic radius
  - Ionization Enthalpy
  - Electron Gain Enthalpy (Electron Affinity: Fluorine vs. Chlorine trap)
  - Electronegativity (Pauling scale: $F > O > N > Cl$)
  - Metallic vs. Non-metallic character.
* **14.4 Chemical Bonding**: Octet rule, Ionic (electrovalent) bonds, Covalent bonds (single, double, triple), Coordinate (dative) bonds, Hydrogen bonding (intermolecular vs. intramolecular; why water is liquid while $H_2S$ is gas).

#### Chapter 15: Chemical Reactions, Catalysis, Redox & Stoichiometry
* **15.1 Types of Chemical Reactions**: Combination, Decomposition, Displacement, Double displacement, Precipitation.
* **15.2 Oxidation & Reduction (Redox)**: Classical concept vs. Electronic concept (OIL RIG: Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain), Oxidation numbers, Oxidizing and reducing agents.
* **15.3 Corrosion & Rancidity**: Chemistry of rusting of iron ($Fe_2O_3 \cdot xH_2O$), conditions, prevention (galvanization with zinc, tin plating, sacrificial anode); Rancidity of fats/oils and prevention (antioxidants, nitrogen flushing).
* **15.4 Catalysis**: Positive vs. negative catalysts, promoters, poisons, enzymes as biocatalysts, auto-catalysis.
* **15.5 Mole Concept & Stoichiometry**: Avogadro's number ($N_A = 6.022 \times 10^{23}$), molar mass, molar volume at STP ($22.4\text{ L}$).

#### Chapter 16: Acids, Bases, Salts, pH Scale & Buffer Systems
* **16.1 Theories of Acids and Bases**:
  - Arrhenius Concept ($H^+$ and $OH^-$ ions in water).
  - Bronsted-Lowry Concept (Proton donor and proton acceptor, conjugate acid-base pairs).
  - Lewis Concept (Electron pair acceptor and electron pair donor).
* **16.2 The pH Scale & Indicators**: Mathematical definition ($\text{pH} = -\log[H^+]$), neutral $\text{pH} = 7$ at $25^\circ\text{C}$; Litmus, Phenolphthalein, Methyl orange, Universal indicator, Olfactory indicators.
* **16.3 Common Natural Acids**: Formic acid (ant sting), Acetic acid (vinegar), Citric acid (citrus fruits), Tartaric acid (tamarind), Lactic acid (curd/sour milk), Oxalic acid (tomato/spinach).
* **16.4 Important Commercial Salts**:
  - Common Salt ($NaCl$).
  - Bleaching Powder ($CaOCl_2$): preparation, uses.
  - Baking Soda ($NaHCO_3$) vs. Washing Soda ($Na_2CO_3 \cdot 10H_2O$).
  - Plaster of Paris ($CaSO_4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}H_2O$) vs. Gypsum ($CaSO_4 \cdot 2H_2O$).
  - Caustic Soda ($NaOH$), Slaked Lime ($Ca(OH)_2$), Quicklime ($CaO$).

#### Chapter 17: Metals, Non-Metals, Metallurgy & Important Alloys
* **17.1 Physical Properties & Exceptions**: Malleability, ductility, sonority, thermal/electrical conductivity; Exceptions: Mercury (liquid metal), Bromine (liquid non-metal), Iodine (lustrous non-metal), Diamond (hardest natural substance, non-metal), Graphite (conductive non-metal).

* **17.4 Alloys & Amalgams**: Brass ($Cu + Zn$), Bronze ($Cu + Sn$), German Silver ($Cu + Zn + Ni$; contains no silver!), Stainless Steel ($Fe + Cr + Ni + C$), Solder ($Pb + Sn$), Bell metal, Gun metal, Duralumin. Amalgams (mercury alloys, why iron cannot form amalgam).

### Part III: Biological Systems, Physiology & Life Sciences

#### Chapter 20: The Cell: Structural Unit of Life, Organelles & Cell Division
* **20.1 Cell Theory & Discovery**: Robert Hooke (dead cell), Anton van Leeuwenhoek (living cell), Schleiden & Schwann (cell theory), Rudolf Virchow (*Omnis cellula e cellula*).
* **20.2 Prokaryotic vs. Eukaryotic Cells**: Nuclear membrane, circular vs. linear DNA, 70S vs. 80S ribosomes, presence/absence of membrane-bound organelles.
* **20.3 Cell Organelles Architecture & Functions**:
  - Plasma Membrane: Fluid mosaic model (Singer & Nicolson), semi-permeability.
  - Nucleus: Nuclear envelope, chromatin, nucleolus (ribosome synthesis).
  - Mitochondria: "Powerhouse of cell", double membrane, cristae, ATP synthesis, maternal DNA.
  - Endoplasmic Reticulum: Rough (protein synthesis) vs. Smooth (lipid synthesis, detoxification).
  - Golgi Apparatus: Packaging, modification, secretion, formation of lysosomes.
  - Lysosomes: "Suicidal bags", hydrolytic enzymes at acidic pH.
  - Ribosomes: "Protein factories", universal organelle.
  - Plastids (in plants): Chloroplasts ("kitchen of cell"), Chromoplasts (colored pigments), Leucoplasts (storage: amyloplast, elaioplast, aleuroplast).
  - Vacuoles: Tonoplast membrane, turgidity.
  - Centrosome/Centrioles: Cell division spindle formation (absent in higher plant cells).
* **20.4 Cell Division**:
  - Cell Cycle: Interphase ($G_1, S, G_2$) and M Phase.
  - Mitosis: Prophase, Metaphase (chromosome morphology studied), Anaphase (chromatids separate), Telophase, Cytokinesis. Equational division for growth/repair.
  - Meiosis: Meiosis I (Prophase I stages: Leptotene, Zygotene synapsis, Pachytene crossing over, Diplotene chiasmata, Diakinesis) and Meiosis II. Reductional division forming haploid gametes.

#### Chapter 21: Biomolecules: Carbohydrates, Proteins, Lipids & Nucleic Acids (DNA/RNA)
* **21.1 Carbohydrates**: Monosaccharides (glucose, fructose/fruit sugar, galactose), Disaccharides (sucrose/table sugar $= \text{glucose} + \text{fructose}$, lactose/milk sugar $= \text{glucose} + \text{galactose}$, maltose), Polysaccharides (starch, glycogen/animal starch, cellulose, chitin in insect exoskeleton).
* **21.2 Proteins & Amino Acids**: Peptide bonds, Essential vs. Non-essential amino acids, Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, Quaternary structures; Denaturation of proteins.
* **21.3 Lipids**: Fatty acids (saturated vs. unsaturated, essential fatty acids), triglycerides, phospholipids, steroids, cholesterol (HDL vs. LDL).
* **21.4 Nucleic Acids**: Nucleotides (pentose sugar + phosphate group + nitrogenous base: Purines A, G; Pyrimidines C, T, U).
* **21.5 DNA vs. RNA**: Watson-Crick double helix model, complementary base pairing ($A=T, G\equiv C$), Chargaff's rules; RNA types: mRNA, tRNA (clover leaf), rRNA; Central Dogma of Molecular Biology ($\text{DNA} \xrightarrow{\text{Transcription}} \text{mRNA} \xrightarrow{\text{Translation}} \text{Protein}$).

#### Chapter 22: Biological Classification, Taxonomy & Microorganisms
* **22.1 Biological Taxonomy**: Linnaeus binomial nomenclature (Genus species), Taxonomic hierarchy (Domain $\rightarrow$ Kingdom $\rightarrow$ Phylum/Division $\rightarrow$ Class $\rightarrow$ Order $\rightarrow$ Family $\rightarrow$ Genus $\rightarrow$ Species).
* **22.2 Whittaker's Five Kingdom Classification**: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia (cellular type, body organization, mode of nutrition).
* **22.3 Kingdom Monera & Bacteria**: Archaebacteria (extremophiles: methanogens, halophiles, thermoacidophiles) vs. Eubacteria, shapes (coccus, bacillus, spirillum, vibrio), Gram-positive vs. Gram-negative, Cyanobacteria (blue-green algae, nitrogen fixation via heterocysts).
* **22.4 Kingdom Protista & Fungi**: Protozoans (Amoeba, Paramecium, Plasmodium); Fungi cell wall made of chitin, saprophytic nutrition, Lichens (symbiosis of algae/fungi, bio-indicators of $SO_2$ pollution), Mycorrhiza.
* **22.5 Acellular Entities**:
  - Viruses: Obligate intracellular parasites, nucleoprotein composition (DNA or RNA, never both), capsid, bacteriophages.
  - Viroids: Free infectious RNA without protein coat (discovered by T.O. Diener).
  - Prions: Abnormally folded infectious proteins (BSE/Mad Cow Disease, Kuru).

#### Chapter 23: Plant Morphology, Photosynthesis, Transpiration & Plant Hormones
* **23.1 Plant Tissues**: Meristematic (apical, lateral/cambium, intercalary) vs. Permanent (Simple: parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma; Complex: Xylem/water transport & Phloem/food translocation).
* **23.2 Plant Morphology**: Root modifications (tap, fibrous, adventitious, pneumatophores), Stem modifications (rhizome, tuber, bulb, corm), Leaf venation and anatomy.
* **23.3 Photosynthesis Mechanism**: Overall equation ($6CO_2 + 12H_2O \rightarrow C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2 + 6H_2O$); Light Reaction (photolysis of water, generation of ATP and NADPH, thylakoid membrane) vs. Dark Reaction / Calvin Cycle (carbon fixation, stroma, RuBisCO enzyme); $C_3, C_4$ (Kranz anatomy), and CAM plants.
* **23.4 Plant Water Relations**: Osmosis, Plasmolysis, Transpiration (stomata mechanism, cooling effect, transpiration pull theory of Dixon & Joly), Guttation (hydathodes).
* **23.5 Plant Hormones (Phytohormones)**:
  - Growth Promoters: Auxins (apical dominance, phototropism), Gibberellins (internodal elongation, breaking seed dormancy, bolting), Cytokinins (cell division, delay senescence).
  - Growth Inhibitors: Abscisic Acid / ABA ("stress hormone", closing of stomata, dormancy), Ethylene (only gaseous hormone, fruit ripening).
* **23.6 Plant Reproduction & Tropisms**: Pollination (anemophily, entomophily, ornithophily), double fertilization in angiosperms, seed and fruit formation; Phototropism, Geotropism, Thigmotropism, Hydrotropism.

#### Chapter 24: Human Physiology I: Digestion, Respiration & Blood Circulatory System
* **24.1 Digestive System**:
  - Alimentary canal: Mouth (salivary amylase/ptyalin), Pharynx, Esophagus (peristalsis).
  - Stomach: Gastric juice ($HCl$, pepsinogen $\rightarrow$ pepsin, mucus protective layer).
  - Small Intestine: Duodenum, Jejunum, Ileum; Bile juice (emulsification of fats, no enzymes), Pancreatic juice (trypsin, amylase, lipase), Intestinal juice (*succus entericus*), Villi absorption.
  - Large Intestine: Water absorption, rectum, anus.
  - Liver (largest gland, deamination, urea synthesis, glycogen storage) and Gall bladder.
* **24.2 Respiratory System**:
  - Anatomy: Nasal cavity, pharynx, larynx (vocal cords), trachea, bronchi, bronchioles, alveoli (functional unit, gas exchange surface).
  - Breathing Mechanics: Inhalation (diaphragm flattens) vs. Exhalation (diaphragm dome-shaped).
  - Gas Transport: Oxygen transport by hemoglobin (oxyhemoglobin), Carbon dioxide transport (as bicarbonate ions $70\%$, carbamino-hemoglobin $20\text{--}25\%$, dissolved in plasma $7\%$).
  - Cellular Respiration: Glycolysis (cytoplasm, anaerobic), Krebs Cycle (mitochondrial matrix, aerobic), Electron Transport Chain (inner mitochondrial membrane), ATP yield.
* **24.3 Blood & Lymphatic System**:
  - Blood Composition: Plasma ($55\%$, albumin, globulin, fibrinogen) and Formed Elements ($45\%$).
  - Red Blood Cells (Erythrocytes): Biconcave, enucleated in mammals (except camel and llama), 120-day lifespan, spleen as graveyard.
  - White Blood Cells (Leukocytes): Granulocytes (neutrophils, eosinophils, basophils) and Agranulocytes (lymphocytes B and T, monocytes/macrophages).
  - Platelets (Thrombocytes): Blood clotting cascade (Thromboplastin $\rightarrow$ Prothrombin to Thrombin $\rightarrow$ Fibrinogen to Fibrin, role of Vitamin K and $Ca^{2+}$ ions).
  - Blood Groups: ABO system (Karl Landsteiner, antigens on RBC surface, antibodies in plasma), Rh factor (Erythroblastosis fetalis), Universal donor ($O^-$) vs. Universal recipient ($AB^+$).
* **24.4 Circulatory Architecture**:
  - Heart Anatomy: Four chambers, valves (tricuspid, bicuspid/mitral, semilunar), SA Node ("natural pacemaker"), AV Node, Bundle of His, Purkinje fibers.
  - Cardiac Cycle: Systole, Diastole, Heart sounds ("LUB" closure of AV valves, "DUB" closure of semilunar valves).
  - Double Circulation: Pulmonary circulation vs. Systemic circulation.
  - Blood Vessels: Arteries (thick elastic wall, deep seated, no valves) vs. Veins (thin wall, superficial, valves present).
  - Blood Pressure: Sphygmomanometer, normal systolic/diastolic ($120/80\text{ mmHg}$).

#### Chapter 25: Human Physiology II: Excretion, Nervous Control, Senses & Endocrine System
* **25.1 Excretory System**:
  - Types of Excretion: Ammonotelic (bony fishes, aquatic invertebrates), Ureotelic (mammals, humans, amphibians), Uricotelic (birds, reptiles, insects).
  - Kidney Anatomy: Renal cortex, medulla, pelvis, ureters, urinary bladder, urethra.
  - The Nephron: Bowman's capsule, Glomerulus (ultrafiltration), Proximal Convoluted Tubule / PCT (selective reabsorption of glucose/amino acids), Loop of Henle (counter-current multiplier), Distal Convoluted Tubule / DCT, Collecting duct.
  - Osmoregulation & Hormones: ADH / Vasopressin (regulates water reabsorption, diabetes insipidus), Aldosterone, Dialysis / Artificial kidney mechanism.
* **25.2 Nervous System & Neural Transmission**:
  - Neurons: Cell body (cyton), dendrites, axon, myelin sheath (Schwann cells), Nodes of Ranvier, Synapse (neurotransmitters: acetylcholine, dopamine).
  - Central Nervous System (CNS):
    - Forebrain: Cerebrum (intelligence, memory, sensory interpretation), Thalamus (relay center), Hypothalamus (body temperature regulation, hunger, thirst, sleep, master of pituitary).
    - Midbrain: Visual and auditory reflex centers.
    - Hindbrain: Cerebellum (muscular coordination, posture, body balance), Pons, Medulla Oblongata (involuntary cardiac and respiratory reflex center, swallowing, vomiting).
  - Peripheral Nervous System (PNS): Cranial nerves (12 pairs) and Spinal nerves (31 pairs).
  - Autonomic Nervous System (ANS): Sympathetic ("fight or flight", adrenaline release) vs. Parasympathetic ("rest and digest").
  - Reflex Arc: Sensory receptor $\rightarrow$ sensory neuron $\rightarrow$ spinal cord $\rightarrow$ motor neuron $\rightarrow$ effector muscle.
* **25.3 Sense Organs**:
  - Eye mechanics (retina, rod/cone pigments: rhodopsin, iodopsin).
  - Ear mechanics: External ear (pinna), Middle ear (ear ossicles: malleus, incus, stapes / smallest bone in body, Eustachian tube), Internal ear (cochlea for hearing, semicircular canals for balance).
* **25.4 Endocrine System & Hormones**:
  - Pituitary Gland ("Master Gland"): Growth Hormone (GH, gigantism/dwarfism/acromegaly), TSH, ACTH, Prolactin, Oxytocin ("love/birth hormone", milk ejection), ADH.
  - Thyroid Gland: Thyroxine ($T_4, T_3$, contains iodine, regulates BMR), Goitre, Cretinism, Myxedema; Calcitonin (lowers blood calcium).
  - Parathyroid Glands: Parathyroid Hormone / PTH (raises blood calcium; antagonistic to calcitonin).
  - Adrenal Glands: Cortex (cortisol, aldosterone) and Medulla (Adrenaline / Epinephrine "emergency hormone", Noradrenaline).
  - Pancreas (Islets of Langerhans, heterocrine gland): $\alpha$-cells (Glucagon, raises blood sugar) vs. $\beta$-cells (Insulin, lowers blood sugar, Diabetes Mellitus Type 1 and 2).
  - Gonads: Testes (Testosterone) and Ovaries (Estrogen, Progesterone).
  - Pineal Gland: Melatonin (circadian rhythm / biological clock).
  - Thymus Gland: Thymosin (T-cell maturation, shrinks with age).

#### Chapter 26: Human Reproduction, Genetics, Mendel's Laws & Genetic Disorders
* **26.1 Human Reproductive Systems**:
  - Male: Testes (scrotum temperature $2\text{--}2.5^\circ\text{C}$ lower for spermatogenesis), Seminiferous tubules, Epididymis, Vas deferens, Prostate gland, Seminal vesicles.
  - Female: Ovaries, Fallopian tubes / Oviducts (site of fertilization), Uterus (endometrium, site of implantation), Menstrual cycle (follicular, ovulatory, luteal phases, LH surge).
  - Embryonic Development: Zygote $\rightarrow$ Morula $\rightarrow$ Blastocyst $\rightarrow$ Gastrula; Placenta functions; Gestation period ($280\text{ days}$).
* **26.2 Mendelian Genetics**:
  - Gregor Mendel (Father of Genetics, garden pea *Pisum sativum*).
  - Monohybrid Cross: Law of Dominance and Law of Segregation (phenotypic ratio $3:1$, genotypic ratio $1:2:1$).
  - Dihybrid Cross: Law of Independent Assortment (phenotypic ratio $9:3:3:1$).
  - Incomplete Dominance (Mirabilis jalapa pink flowers) and Co-dominance (AB blood group).
* **26.3 Sex Determination & Chromosomes**: Autosomes (22 pairs) vs. Allosomes / Sex chromosomes (1 pair, XX female, XY male); Sex determination in humans, birds (ZZ/ZW), and insects (XO).
* **26.4 Genetic Disorders**:
  - Mendelian / Gene Disorders: Hemophilia ("Royal disease", sex-linked recessive), Color Blindness (red-green, sex-linked recessive), Thalassemia (autosomal recessive), Sickle Cell Anemia (point mutation, substitution of glutamic acid by valine in $\beta$-globin chain), Phenylketonuria (PKU).
  - Chromosomal Disorders: Down Syndrome (Trisomy 21), Turner Syndrome ($45, XO$), Klinefelter Syndrome ($47, XXY$).

#### Chapter 27: Human Health, Pathogens, Immunity, Vaccines, Antibiotics & Lifestyle Diseases
* **27.1 Infectious Pathogens & Diseases**:
  - Bacterial Diseases: Tuberculosis (*Mycobacterium tuberculosis*, BCG vaccine, Mantoux test), Cholera (*Vibrio cholerae*), Typhoid (*Salmonella typhi*, Widal test), Tetanus (*Clostridium tetani*), Diphtheria, Pertussis/Whooping cough, Plague.
  - Viral Diseases: Influenza, Common cold (Rhinovirus), Polio (Salk injectable vs. Sabin oral vaccine), Rabies (hydrophobia), Hepatitis (A, B, C, D, E), Dengue (Aedes mosquito, thrombocytopenia), Chikungunya, AIDS (HIV retrovirus, ELISA and Western Blot tests), COVID-19.
  - Protozoan Diseases: Malaria (*Plasmodium vivax/falciparum*, female Anopheles vector, quinine from Cinchona bark), Amoebic dysentery, Kala-azar / Leishmaniasis (Sandfly vector), Sleeping sickness (Tsetse fly).
  - Fungal & Helminthic Diseases: Ringworm, Athlete's foot; Filariasis / Elephantiasis (*Wuchereria bancrofti*, Culex mosquito), Ascariasis.
* **27.2 The Immune System & Vaccines**:
  - Innate Immunity (Physical barriers: skin/mucus; Physiological: acid in stomach/lysozyme in tears; Cellular: WBCs; Cytokine: interferons).
  - Acquired Immunity: Humoral (B-lymphocytes, antibodies $IgG, IgM, IgA, IgE, IgD$) vs. Cell-mediated (T-lymphocytes, graft rejection).
  - Active vs. Passive Immunity (Colostrum rich in $IgA$, anti-tetanus serum).
  - Vaccine Technologies: Live attenuated, Inactivated/Killed, Toxoids, Subunit/Recombinant (Hepatitis B), mRNA vaccines.
* **27.3 Antibiotics, Antivirals & Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR)**: Mechanism of action (cell wall inhibition, protein synthesis inhibition), why antibiotics fail against viruses, superbugs and AMR crisis.
* **27.4 Non-Communicable & Lifestyle Diseases**: Cancer (oncogenes, metastasis, biopsy, chemotherapy/radiotherapy/immunotherapy), Cardiovascular diseases (atherosclerosis, myocardial infarction, stroke), Diabetes, Hypertension, Autoimmune diseases (Rheumatoid arthritis).
