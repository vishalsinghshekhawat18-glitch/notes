## Detailed Chapter Scope & Learning Matrix

### Chapter 01: Master Static Banking, Regulatory Acts & Prudential Norms Core
* **Statutory Bedrocks**: The Reserve Bank of India Act 1934 (Preamble, Section 7, Section 17, Section 18, Section 42 CRR, Section 45 MPC), Banking Regulation Act 1949 (Section 5 definitions, Section 22 licensing, Section 24 SLR, Section 35A directions, Section 45 moratorium).
* **Legal Recovery & Anti-Fraud Architecture**: SARFAESI Act 2002 (Securitisation, Reconstruction, Asset Reconstruction Companies, SARFAESI notices, CERSAI registry), Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code 2016 (CIRP 180+90 days timeline, Section 29A disqualifications, CoC voting thresholds), Prevention of Money Laundering Act 2002 (PMLA Section 12 reporting, FIU-IND mandate), Negotiable Instruments Act 1881 (Section 138 cheque bouncing penal provisions).
* **Prudential Standards**: Basel III Capital Framework (Minimum CRAR 9% for SCBs / 11.5% with CCB, CET-1 minimum 5.5%, Capital Conservation Buffer 2.5%, Tier-2 limit 2%), Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) Framework (Risk Thresholds 1, 2, 3 across CRAR, Net NPA, and Leverage Ratio), Priority Sector Lending (PSL) Mandates (40% overall for Domestic SCBs, 18% Agriculture with 10% Small & Marginal Farmers, 7.5% Micro-enterprises, 12% Weaker Sections).
