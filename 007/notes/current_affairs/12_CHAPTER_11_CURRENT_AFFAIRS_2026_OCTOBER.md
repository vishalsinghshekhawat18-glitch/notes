[OCT-001] **RBI Enforces FEMA Regulations, 2026 (Effective 1 Oct 2026): Slashed Export Realisation Windows**

- **Supersedes:** FEMA (Export of Goods & Services) Regulations, 2015.
- **Scope:** A unified framework covering exports of goods, services, and software (services defined to include software).
- **Core Realisation Timelines Reduced:** Standard realisation periods cut from 15 to **9 months**; INR-invoiced/settled periods cut from 18 to **12 months** (September 2026 amendment).

### 1. Export Declaration Form (EDF)

| Export category | Declaration requirement |
|---|---|
| Physical goods through an EDI port | EDF is deemed submitted with the electronic shipping bill. |
| Services | EDF within **30 days after the end of the invoice month**. |
| Services other than software | EDF may alternatively be submitted on or before receipt of payment. |

An Authorised Dealer (AD) may extend the service-EDF filing period upon a request explaining the delay, if satisfied with the reasons.

### 2. Export realisation and repatriation deadlines

The **realisation period** is the time allowed to receive export proceeds and repatriate them to India.

| Export category | Deadline | Starting point |
|---|---:|---|
| Physical goods, excluding goods sent to an overseas warehouse | **9 months** | Date of shipment |
| Services, including software | **9 months** | Date of invoice |
| Goods sold from an overseas warehouse | **9 months** | Date of sale from the warehouse |
| Exports invoiced and/or settled in INR | **12 months** | Relevant shipment, invoice or warehouse-sale date |

**Important:** The ordinary period is 9 months, while the INR-related period is 12 months. The previous periods were 15 and 18 months, respectively.

An AD may extend the specified realisation period on the exporter's request if the reasons for the delay are considered satisfactory.

### 3. Simplified closure of EDPMS and IDPMS entries

EDPMS = Export Data Processing and Monitoring System  
IDPMS = Import Data Processing and Monitoring System

For transactions of **₹10 lakh or less**, the regulations permit simplified closure based on a declaration, subject to the prescribed conditions.

- **Exports → EDPMS:** Exporter's declaration that payment against the shipping bill or service invoice has been realised, either in full or otherwise.
- **Imports → IDPMS:** Importer's declaration that payment against the Bill of Entry or service invoice has been made, either in full or otherwise.

The relevant declaration may also be submitted quarterly for bulk closure of entries.

**Exam distinction:** EDPMS = exporter; IDPMS = importer.

### 4. Restriction on exporters with long-overdue proceeds

If export proceeds remain unrealised for **more than one year beyond the due date**, or beyond an extended due date allowed by the AD, the exporter must undertake further exports only against:

- Full advance payment; or
- An irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC).

This is a restriction on the payment terms permitted for subsequent exports, rather than a rule that automatically imposes a monetary penalty.

### 5. Exam revision card

- **Effective date:** 1 October 2026
- **Standard export realisation:** 9 months
- **INR invoicing and/or settlement:** 12 months
- **Service EDF filing:** Within 30 days after invoice month-end, subject to the stated alternatives and extensions
- **Simplified closure threshold:** ₹10 lakh or less
- **Long-overdue export proceeds:** More than 1 year beyond the due date or permitted extended date
- **Permitted terms for further exports:** Full advance or irrevocable LC

[OCT-002] **Small Savings Schemes — Interest Rates Frozen for Q3 FY 2026–27**

### 1. Government decision

The Department of Economic Affairs (DEA), Ministry of Finance, retained the existing interest rates on Small Savings Schemes (SSS) for **Q3 FY 2026–27: 1 October–31 December 2026**.

- **Decision date:** 30 September 2026.
- **Change in rates:** None; rates remain unchanged from Q2 FY 2026–27.
- **Policy framework:** Small-savings rates are linked to Government Securities (G-Sec) yields of comparable maturities, with scheme-specific spreads. The Shyamala Gopinath Committee (2010) informed this market-linked framework.

### 2. Complete interest-rate schedule

All rates are **per annum**.

| Scheme | Rate | Important feature |
|---|---:|---|
| Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme (SCSS) | **8.2%** | Quarterly interest payout; maximum deposit ₹30 lakh; generally for individuals aged 60+ |
| Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA) | **8.2%** | Annual compounding; maximum deposit ₹1.5 lakh per financial year; girl child below 10 at account opening; maturity after 21 years |
| National Savings Certificate (NSC VIII Issue) | **7.7%** | 5-year tenure |
| Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP) | **7.5%** | Investment doubles in 115 months |
| Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS) | **7.4%** | Monthly interest payout; maximum ₹9 lakh for a single account and ₹15 lakh for a joint account |
| Public Provident Fund (PPF) | **7.1%** | 15-year tenure; minimum ₹500 and maximum ₹1.5 lakh deposited annually |
| Post Office Time Deposit — 1 year | **6.9%** | 1-year tenure |
| Post Office Time Deposit — 2 years | **7.0%** | 2-year tenure |
| Post Office Time Deposit — 3 years | **7.1%** | 3-year tenure |
| Post Office Time Deposit — 5 years | **7.5%** | 5-year tenure |
| 5-Year Recurring Deposit (RD) | **6.7%** | Regular instalment-based saving |
| Post Office Savings Account | **4.0%** | Savings deposit |

**SCSS extension:** The account can be extended in blocks of three years, subject to the scheme's rules.

### 3. High-value exam facts

**Highest interest rate:** 8.2%, shared by SCSS and Sukanya Samriddhi.

**KVP doubling period:** 115 months = **9 years and 7 months**, at 7.5%.

**SCSS deposit limit:** ₹30 lakh.

**POMIS account limits:**
- Single: ₹9 lakh
- Joint: ₹15 lakh

**PPF annual deposit ceiling:** ₹1.5 lakh.

**SSS rate-setting framework:** G-Sec yields of comparable maturity plus scheme-specific spreads; Shyamala Gopinath Committee, 2010.

### 4. Quick memory pattern

Remember the leading rates as:

**8.2 → 7.7 → 7.5 → 7.4 → 7.1**

- **8.2%:** SCSS and SSA
- **7.7%:** NSC
- **7.5%:** KVP and 5-year Time Deposit
- **7.4%:** POMIS
- **7.1%:** PPF and 3-year Time Deposit

The remaining key rates are 6.9% for the 1-year Time Deposit, 7.0% for the 2-year Time Deposit, 6.7% for the 5-year RD, and 4.0% for the Savings Account.
