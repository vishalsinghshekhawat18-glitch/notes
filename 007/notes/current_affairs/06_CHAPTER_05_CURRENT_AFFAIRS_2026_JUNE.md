### [JUN-001] RBI Relaxes Capital Requirements for Bank Loans under ECLGS 5.0 (Split Risk-Weight Structure)

> 💡 **MEMORY HOOK:** 0% on 75% – 20% on the rest – only if claim settled within 30 days.

| Portion of Exposure | Regulatory Risk Weight | Statutory Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **75% of the Guaranteed Portion** | **0%** | Guarantee claim settled within 30 days of invocation |
| **Remaining 25% Exposure** | **20%** (concessional) | Standard capital charges apply if 30-day window breached |
| **Breached 30-Day Window** | **Standard capital charges** | 0% benefit revoked |

* **Regulatory Framework & Mandate**: Reserve Bank of India issued an amendment to the *Master Direction – RBI (Commercial Banks – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions, 2025*, significantly easing capital requirements for bank loans backed by the Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (**ECLGS 5.0**).
* **Split Risk-Weight Mechanism**:
  * **75% of the Guaranteed Portion**: Attracts a **0% risk weight**, exempting banks from setting aside regulatory capital against this portion of the exposure.
  * **Remaining 25% Exposure**: Attracts a concessional **20% risk weight** (drastically reduced from standard unrated exposure weights).
* **Settlement Condition for 0% Weight**:
  * Banks can avail of the 0% risk weight benefit only if the guarantee claim is settled within **30 days** from the date of invocation.
  * If the 30-day settlement requirement is breached, the benefit of the 0% risk weight is revoked, and standard capital charges apply.
* **Strategic Intent**: Designed to release trapped bank capital, incentivize fresh liquidity flow to distressed and contact-intensive businesses, and align sovereign-backed portfolio risk with Basel III Pillar 1 norms.

> ⚠️ **EXAM ANGLE:**  
> - **Risk-Weight Split**: 0% on 75% of guaranteed portion vs 20% on remaining 25% (classic matching/statement MCQ).  
> - **Time Boundary for Settlement**: Must be settled within **30 days** of claim invocation to retain 0% risk weight.  
> - **Policy Anchor**: Governed under the *RBI (Commercial Banks – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions*.

### [JUN-002] Launch of ARIFAC: National Industry Platform for AML/CFT Compliance with FIU-IND Oversight

* **Genesis & Institutional Architecture**: India's financial sector formally launched the **Alliance of Reporting Entities in AML/CFT (ARIFAC)** as a premier national industry-led coordination platform to bolster Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Combating the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) frameworks.
* **Administrative Secretariat**: Jointly managed and operated by the **Payments Council of India (PCI)** and the **Fintech Convergence Council (FCC)**.
* **Official Regulatory Observer**: The **Financial Intelligence Unit – India (FIU-IND)** participates in ARIFAC in an official **observer role**, offering high-level guidance, regulatory alignment, and supervisory coordination.
* **Universal Membership Base**: Encompasses all reporting entities regulated under the *Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA)*:
  * Commercial Banks and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).
  * Payment System Operators (PSOs) and Payment Aggregators (PAs).
  * Securities market intermediaries, Mutual Funds, and Depository Participants.
  * Insurance companies and **Virtual Digital Asset (VDA) service providers**.
* **Operational Focus**: Standardizing suspicious transaction reporting (STR) formats, conducting joint capacity-building certifications, and creating early-warning networks to intercept financial mule rings.

> ⚠️ **EXAM ANGLE:**  
> - **Joint Secretariat**: Payments Council of India (PCI) + Fintech Convergence Council (FCC) (NOT IBA alone).  
> - **Observer Agency**: FIU-IND (Financial Intelligence Unit – India).  
> - **Legislative Anchor**: Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002.

### [JUN-003] Credit Guarantee Scheme for Microfinance Institutions 2.0 (CGSMFI-2.0): Expansion to ₹20,000 Crore Pool

> 💡 **MEMORY HOOK:** Bigger MFI → bigger loan cap, but lower guarantee: 80 → 75 → 70.

| MFI Tier | Assets Under Management (AUM) | Maximum Loan (20% of AUM, Capped) | NCGTC Cover | Minimum Bank Allocation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Small** | < ₹500 crore | $le$ ₹100 crore | **80%** | 5% of bank's pool |
| **Medium** | ₹500 crore to < ₹2,000 crore | $le$ ₹200 crore | **75%** | 10% of bank's pool |
| **Large** | $ge$ ₹2,000 crore | $le$ **₹1,000 crore** (raised from ₹300 cr) | **70%** | Remaining available pool |

* **Framework Evolution**: Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services – DFS) and NCGTC operationalized **CGSMFI-2.0**, transitioning from emergency COVID-era relief (launched 28 June 2021) into a permanent, risk-based, tier-structured credit enhancement framework.
* **Pool Size & Validity**: Total guarantee pool expanded to **₹20,000 crore**, with validity up to **31 August 2026** (or until pool is exhausted).
* **NCGTC Institutional Profile**: Implemented by National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Limited (NCGTC), set up under DFS, Ministry of Finance (incorporated 28 March 2014 under Companies Act 1956; paid-up capital ₹10 crore; HQ: Mumbai).
* **Interest Rate & On-Lending Structure**:
  * **Lending Rate to MFIs**: Capped at **EBLR or MCLR + 2.0% per annum**.
  * **On-Lending Rate to Borrowers**: Capped at **1.0% below the MFI's average lending rate** of the preceding six months.
* **Guarantee Fee & Deployment**: Fee fixed at **0.50% per annum** on sanctioned amount in Year 1, and on outstanding balance from Year 2 onwards. Minimum **80% of funds** must be deployed for fresh microfinance assets within 4 months.

> ⚠️ **EXAM ANGLE:**  
> - **Large MFI Max Loan Ceiling**: Enhanced from ₹300 Cr to **₹1,000 crore** (capped at 20% of AUM).  
> - **Tier-wise Guarantee Cover**: Small = 80%, Medium = 75%, Large = 70%.  
> - **Interest Spread Cap**: Lending to MFIs capped at EBLR/MCLR + 2%; on-lending capped at 1% below average rate.  
> - **NCGTC Foundation**: Incorporated 28 March 2014; initial paid-up capital ₹10 crore.

### [JUN-004] RBI Master Directions on Bank Lending to REITs & InvITs (49% Aggregate Exposure Cap)

* **Policy Harmonization**: RBI released the Master Directions on Bank Lending to Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) and Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs), permitting scheduled commercial banks to lend directly to business trusts under strict prudential boundaries.
* **Eligibility & Asset Composition Norms**:
  * Banks can lend only to **SEBI-registered and publicly listed** REITs and InvITs.
  * **Operational Asset Threshold**: At least **80% of the underlying assets** must comprise completed, revenue-generating properties/projects yielding positive cash flows for more than one year.
* **Aggregate Lending & Exposure Limits**:
  * **Aggregate Bank Exposure Cap**: Total bank lending across all commercial banks to any single borrowing REIT or InvIT (including SPVs and holdcos) must not exceed **49% of the value of the trust's total assets**.
  * **Funding Restriction**: Bank loans cannot be used to fund underlying SPVs that are financially distressed or have existing NPAs.
* **Risk Weights & Governance**: Exposure to REITs attracts **100% risk weight** (125% if categorized as capital market exposure); InvITs attract corporate lending risk weights based on external ratings. Migration to full capital charge framework effective **1 April 2027**; directions take effect from **1 October 2026**.

> ⚠️ **EXAM ANGLE:**  
> - **Aggregate Exposure Cap**: $le$**49% of the value of total assets** (NOT 50% or 75%).  
> - **Completed Asset Requirement**: Minimum 80% completed and revenue-generating with >1 year positive cash flow.  
> - **Statutory Dates**: General compliance effective **1 October 2026**; Capital charge migration **1 April 2027**.

### [JUN-005] RBI Master Directions on Authorisation of Payment System Operators (PSOs) — Perpetual Licences & Governance

* **Statutory Authority**: Issued under Section 4 of the Payment and Settlement Systems (PSS) Act, 2007.
* **Perpetual Validity Regime**:
  * All fresh Certificates of Authorisation (CoA) granted to PSOs will carry **perpetual validity** (eliminating the earlier 5-year periodic renewal requirement).
  * Existing authorized PSOs transition to perpetual validity at their next renewal, subject to full compliance.
* **Non-Compliant Entities**: PSOs exhibiting governance or operational deficiencies will receive conditional renewals for a limited period of **1 year only** until rectified.
* **FATF Non-Compliant Jurisdictions**: Fresh investments from FATF non-compliant jurisdictions must account for **less than 20% of the voting power** of the PSO in aggregate.
* **Surrender & Cooling-Off**: Surrender requires a Statutory Auditor's certificate confirming all customer, merchant, and bank liabilities are fully settled. A mandatory **one-year cooling-off period** is imposed before re-application if a CoA was revoked, rejected, or surrendered.

> ⚠️ **EXAM ANGLE:**  
> - **Licence Validity**: Perpetual validity for compliant PSOs; 1 year only for non-compliant entities.  
> - **FATF Non-Compliant Voting Cap**: Strictly below 20% of aggregate voting power.  
> - **Cooling-Off Duration**: 1 year from revocation, surrender, or application rejection.

### [JUN-006] RBI Master Directions on Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS) 2026 & Mandatory CERSAI Registration

* **Framework Objective**: Overhaul of 2014 TReDS guidelines to accelerate MSME invoice monetization, expand participant categories, and eliminate duplicate factoring frauds.
* **Net Worth Requirement & Transition Timeline**:
  * Minimum Net Worth for new entities fixed at **₹25 crore** (continuous maintenance verified by statutory auditor).
  * Existing authorized TReDS platforms must ramp up net worth to ₹25 crore by **31 March 2028**.
* **Expanded Market Participants**: Accommodates MSME Sellers, Corporate/PSU/Government Buyers, Financers (Banks, NBFC-Factors, DFIs), Insurance Companies (for credit risk cover), and notified Credit Guarantee Fund Trusts.
* **Mandatory CERSAI Registration**: Every assignment of receivables executed on TReDS must be mandatorily registered with **CERSAI** to prevent multiple discounting of the same invoice.
* **Secondary Market Re-Discounting**: Authorizes secondary market re-discounting of factoring units between eligible financiers.
* **Current Approved Platforms (5 Entities)**:
  1. Receivables Exchange of India Limited (**RXIL**) — India's 1st operational platform (commenced 9 January 2017).
  2. **M1xchange** (Mynd Solutions Pvt. Ltd.).
  3. **Invoicemart** (A.TREDS Ltd. – Axis Bank & mjunction).
  4. **C2treds** (C2FO Factoring Solutions Pvt. Ltd.).
  5. **DTX** (KredX Platform Pvt. Ltd.).

> ⚠️ **EXAM ANGLE:**  
> - **Minimum Net Worth**: ₹25 crore (existing platforms must comply by 31 March 2028).  
> - **Fraud Prevention Registry**: Mandatory registration of receivables with CERSAI.  
> - **First Live Platform**: RXIL (commenced operations 9 January 2017).  
> - **Total Platforms**: 5 approved platforms as of June 2026.
