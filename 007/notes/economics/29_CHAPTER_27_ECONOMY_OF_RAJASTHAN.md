### Sectoral Composition of Gross State Value Added (GSVA) [2025–26 AE]:
Unlike the national macroeconomic profile (where Services account for >53%), Rajasthan presents a unique, resilient tripartite balance:
1. **Services Sector (47.71% at Current Prices / 44.82% at Constant Prices)**: Tourism, trade, hospitality, real estate, transportation, communication, and public administration.
2. **Industry Sector (26.55% at Current Prices / 27.98% at Constant Prices)**: Manufacturing, mining and quarrying, construction, electricity, gas, and water supply.
3. **Agriculture & Allied Sector (25.74% at Current Prices / 27.20% at Constant Prices)**: Crop production, livestock (dairy, wool), forestry, and fisheries. Displays monsoon-related cyclical swings.

## 27.2 Vision, Strategic Planning & Sustainability

### 1. Viksit Rajasthan @ 2047
Aligned with the Union vision of *Viksit Bharat @ 2047*, Rajasthan has institutionalized a 25-year socio-economic transformation roadmap structured on five core pillars:
* **Economic Scale**: Expanding the State economy to a **US$ 350 Billion GSDP by 2030** and a **US$ 1 Trillion GSDP by 2047** through targeted industrial diversification, infrastructure expansion, and investment corridors.
* **Green Energy & Water Sovereignty**: Becoming India's foremost renewable superpower (solar/wind/green hydrogen) and completing integrated canal link networks (ERCP/PKC and IGNP modernizations).
* **Industrial & Mineral Value Addition**: Shifting from raw mineral extraction to localized high-value smelting, refining, and industrial manufacturing (Pachpadra Petroleum Complex, Electric Vehicle clusters).
* **Inclusive Social Infrastructure**: Universal health safety nets, digitized educational hubs, and drought-resilient livelihood guarantees.

### 2. Rajasthan SDG Implementation Framework
* Institutionalized under the **Planning Department (Centre for SDG Implementation)**.
* Measures district-level progress across all 17 SDGs via the **Rajasthan SDG Index Version 4.0 / 5.0**.
* **Leading Districts**: Sikar, Jhunjhunu, Jaipur consistently rank highest in composite education and health targets.
* **Lagging Districts**: Jaisalmer, Barmer, Jalore (arid desert constraints) and Pratapgarh, Banswara (tribal multi-dimensional deprivation).

## 27.3 State Public Finance & The 6th State Finance Commission

### 1. State Budgetary Architecture & Borrowing Constraints
* **Revenue Receipts**: Comprise State's Own Tax Revenue (SOTR: State GST, State Excise, Stamps & Registration, Tax on Vehicles, Land Revenue), State's Own Non-Tax Revenue (SONTR: Mining royalties, interest receipts), and Central Transfers (Devolution under Article 270 + Grants-in-Aid under Article 275).
* **Fiscal Deficit Ceiling**: Governed by the **Rajasthan Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act, 2005**:
  * Anchored to statutory ceiling of **3.0% of GSDP** (with 0.5% performance-based borrowing leeway for power sector reforms).
  * Governed by **Article 293(3)**: Rajasthan cannot borrow from market without Central consent due to outstanding Central loans.

### 2. The State Finance Commission Architecture & Chronology
* **Constitutional Anchor**: Constituted by the Governor under **Article 243-I** (Panchayati Raj) and **Article 243-Y** (Municipalities).
* **6th State Finance Commission (Award Period: 2020–2025)**:
  * **Chairperson**: **Pradyumn Singh** (Commissioners: Ashok Lahoti, Laxman Singh Rawat).
  * Recommends devolving **6.75% of State's Own Net Tax Revenue** to Local Self-Governments.
  * **Inter-Institutional Ratio**:
    * **75.10%** to Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs).
    * **24.90%** to Urban Local Bodies (ULBs).
  * **Intra-Panchayat Tier Devolution**:
    * **Gram Panchayats (Grassroots Tier)**: **75%** of PRI share (maximum execution burden).
    * **Panchayat Samitis (Block Tier)**: **20%** of PRI share.
    * **Zila Parishads (District Tier)**: **5%** of PRI share.
* **7th State Finance Commission (Subsequent Layer)**: Formulated for the **2025–2026 interim-report layer** to establish post-2025 municipal and PRI transfer guidelines.

### Major Irrigation Infrastructures:
1. **Indira Gandhi Nahar Project (IGNP - Maruganga)**:
   * Originates at **Harike Barrage** (confluence of Satluj and Beas rivers in Punjab).
   * **Feeder Canal**: 204 km (170 km in Punjab/Haryana + 34 km in Rajasthan).
   * **Main Canal**: 445 km ending at Mohangarh (Jaisalmer), extended to Gadra Road (Barmer).
   * **Flow Canals vs. Lift Canals**: Left bank canals operate via natural slope flow; right bank features **7 major lift canals** (e.g., Kanwarsain, Gandheli Sahawa, Gajner, Kolayat, Phalodi, Pokhran, Bangarsar) elevating water onto desert plateaus.
2. **Eastern Rajasthan Canal Project (ERCP) / Modified PKC Interlink**:
   * Intra-state project now integrated into the **Modified Parbati-Kalisindh-Chambal (PKC) Link Project** under a historic Tripartite Agreement (Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Union Ministry of Jal Shakti) declared a **National Project** (90% Central funding).
   * Harvests surplus monsoon waters of Chambal river basin tributaries (Kalisindh, Parbati, Mej, Chakan) and channels them to **21 drought-stressed eastern and central districts** (Jaipur, Alwar, Dausa, Bharatpur, Dholpur, Karauli, Sawai Madhopur, Ajmer, Tonk, Bundi, Kota, Baran, Jhalawar, etc.) providing drinking water and irrigating 2.8+ lakh hectares.
3. **Micro-Irrigation Mandates**:
   * **Narmada Canal Project (Jalore & Barmer)**: India's first irrigation project where **Sprinkler Micro-Irrigation is 100% compulsory by law**!

## 27.5 Livestock, Dairy & Pastoral Economy

With crop failure recurrent due to arid droughts, animal husbandry provides Rajasthan's ultimate sovereign economic shock-absorber, contributing **~8–10% of State GDP** and ~30% of agricultural GSVA.

### Insights from the 20th Livestock Census:
* **Total Livestock Population**: **56.8 Million (5.68 Crore)**, ranking **#2 in India** (after Uttar Pradesh).
* **Camel Population**: **#1 in India** (accounts for ~84% of national camel herd). Declared State Animal (Livestock category) in 2014; governed by the *Rajasthan Camel (Prohibition of Slaughter and Regulation of Temporary Migration or Export) Act, 2015*.
* **Wool Production**: **#1 in India** (produces **~40–45% of India's total wool**).
* **Milk Production**: **#2 in India** (produces **~14–15% of total national milk**).

## 27.6 Industrial Development, MSME Architecture & Investment Schemes

### 1. Key Institutional Enablers:
* **RIICO (Rajasthan State Industrial Development & Investment Corporation)**:
  * Apex state agency for industrial park development, land acquisition, and infrastructure.
  * Formulated dedicated clusters: **Japanese Investment Zones** at Neemrana and Ghiloth (Alwar); Agro-Food Parks at Boranada (Jodhpur), Ranpur (Kota), Sriganganagar, and Matsya Industrial Area (Alwar); Medical Devices Park at Boranada.
* **BIP (Bureau of Investment Promotion)**: Single-window clearance bureau facilitating mega-investments ($> ₹10 \text{ Crore}$).

### 2. Rajasthan MSME (Facilitation of Establishment & Enforcement) Act, 2019
* Historic state legislation to dismantle bureaucratic red-tape for micro, small, and medium startups:
* **Certificate of Intent / Declaration of Intent**: Upon filing an electronic declaration on the MSME portal, entrepreneurs receive an immediate acknowledgment certificate.
* **Statutory Holiday from State Clearances**: Grants **100% statutory exemption from all State department inspections, approvals, and clearances for a period of 3 YEARS** (extended to **5 YEARS** in 2023 amendment).
* Enterprises may commence operations immediately without prior environmental, municipal, or labour department pre-operational clearances!

### 3. Rajasthan Investment Promotion Scheme (RIPS 2022 / RIPS 2024)
* State flagship policy to attract mega manufacturing, green energy, and IT investments:
* **Standard Package**: 100% exemption from Stamp Duty, Land Conversion charges, and Electricity Duty for 7 years.
* **Employment & Capital Subsidy**: Reimbursement of up to 75% of SGST deposited for 7 years, or Capital Subsidy of up to 28% of eligible fixed capital investment.
* **Special Thrust Sectors**: Electric Vehicles (EVs), Green Hydrogen, Agro-processing, Medical Devices, Defense manufacturing.

### Hydrocarbon & Petroleum Architecture:
Rajasthan is India's **second largest crude oil producer** (accounting for **~20–22% of total domestic crude production** after offshore Mumbai High).
* **Four Petroleum Basins**:
  1. **Barmer-Sanchore Basin**: Prolific onshore basin; fields include **Mangala** (commenced production 29 August 2009), **Bhagyam**, **Aishwarya**, **Saraswati**, **Raageshwari**. Operated by Cairn Oil & Gas (Vedanta).
  2. **Jaisalmer Basin**: Primary natural gas basin (Gamnewala, Dandewala).
  3. **Bikaner-Nagaur Basin**: Heavy crude oil reserves (Poonam field).
  4. **Vindhyan Basin**: Kota, Baran, Jhalawar, Bundi (natural gas exploration).
* **HPCL Rajasthan Refinery Limited (HRRL - Pachpadra, Balotra/Barmer)**:
  * Mega refinery-cum-petrochemical complex with a capacity of **9 MMTPA** (Million Metric Tonnes Per Annum).
  * **Ownership Structure**: Joint venture with **74% equity held by HPCL** and **26% equity held by Government of Rajasthan**.
  * Configured to process both indigenous Rajasthan crude and imported heavy crudes, producing BS-VI fuels and 13 downstream petrochemical building blocks (polypropylene, polyethylene).

## 27.9 Social Infrastructure, Livelihood Guarantees & Digital Governance

### 1. Healthcare & Social Safety Nets:
* **Mukhya Mantri Ayushman Arogya Yojana (MAAY)**:
  * Universal health insurance coverage (evolving from the Chiranjeevi health scheme) providing cashless secondary and tertiary hospitalization across affiliated public and private hospitals.
* **Mukhya Mantri Nishulk Dawa & Janch Yojana**:
  * Free distribution of essential medicines and diagnostic medical tests across all State public healthcare institutions.
* **Indira Gandhi Shahari Rozgar Guarantee Yojana (IRGY-Urban)**:
  * First-of-its-kind state urban employment guarantee providing **125 days of guaranteed unskilled manual wage employment** annually to adult members of urban families demanding work.

### 2. Digital Transparency & Good Governance:
* **Jan Soochna Portal (Launched 2019)**:
  * India's first state digital portal operationalizing the true spirit of **Section 4(2) of the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005** (Proactive Voluntary Public Disclosure).
  * Citizens access granular household welfare delivery data across 100+ public schemes without filing formal RTI petitions.
* **Jan Aadhaar Platform**:
  * Unified "One Number, One Card, One Identity" family card serving as the universal database for delivering direct benefit transfers (DBT) and subsidized public services in Rajasthan.
* **Raj-Kaj Platform**:
  * Unified enterprise e-office platform automating digital file movement and administrative governance across all State Secretariats.

## 27.10 Active Recall Diagnostic Cards (RPSC RAS Lens)

#### Card 1 (Inter-Institutional PRI Devolution)
**Question**: Detail the horizontal distribution ratio recommended by the 6th State Finance Commission between PRIs and ULBs, and the internal breakdown across the three tiers of Panchayats.
<details>
<summary>View Rigorous Causal Answer</summary>
The 6th State Finance Commission (chaired by Pradyumn Singh) recommended devolving 6.75% of State's Own Net Tax Revenue. This fund is distributed:<br/>
1. <strong>75.10%</strong> to Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs).<br/>
2. <strong>24.90%</strong> to Urban Local Bodies (ULBs).<br/>
Within the 75.10% PRI allocation, the internal formula divides funds: <strong>75% to Gram Panchayats</strong> (grassroots level), <strong>20% to Panchayat Samitis</strong> (block level), and <strong>5% to Zila Parishads</strong> (district level).
</details>

#### Card 2 (Rajasthan MSME Act 2019 Innovation)
**Question**: What is the most radical statutory exemption provided to new micro, small, and medium industrial units under the Rajasthan MSME Act, 2019?
<details>
<summary>View Rigorous Causal Answer</summary>
Upon electronic submission of a "Declaration of Intent", the entrepreneur receives a statutory Acknowledgment Certificate. Under the Act, new enterprises are granted <strong>100% statutory exemption from all State department approvals, licenses, NOCs, and regulatory inspections for a period of 3 YEARS (extended to 5 YEARS)</strong>. Units can commence construction and manufacturing immediately without waiting for bureaucratic clearances.
</details>

#### Card 3 (Pachpadra Refinery Equity & Output)
**Question**: State the joint venture equity shareholding and processing capacity of HPCL Rajasthan Refinery Limited (HRRL).
<details>
<summary>View Rigorous Causal Answer</summary>
HRRL is located at Pachpadra (Balotra district). The equity structure is: <strong>74% held by HPCL (Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited)</strong> and <strong>26% held by the Government of Rajasthan</strong>. It has a crude processing capacity of <strong>9 MMTPA</strong>, producing BS-VI fuels and integrated petrochemical polymers.
</details>
