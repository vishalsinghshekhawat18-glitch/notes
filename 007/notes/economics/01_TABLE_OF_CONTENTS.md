## Detailed Chapter Breakdown (Chapters 1–3 Preview)

### Chapter 01: Foundations of Economic Organization, Sectors & Goods Typology
* **1.1 The Core Economic Problem**: Scarcity, Opportunity Cost, and the Production Possibility Frontier (PPF).
* **1.2 The Three Fundamental Questions**: What, How, and For Whom to produce.
* **1.3 Systems of Economic Organization**: Free Market (Laissez-faire), Command (Socialist/State-directed), and Mixed Economy.
* **1.4 The Four Sectors of the Macroeconomy**: Households, Producing Enterprises (Firms), Government, and the Rest of the World.
* **1.5 The Circular Flow of Income & Product**: Real Flows vs. Nominal/Money Flows in 2-Sector, 3-Sector, and 4-Sector Open Economies; Leakages and Injections equilibrium.
* **1.6 The Universal Typology of Goods**:
  * Final Goods vs. Intermediate Goods (The double-counting hazard).
  * Consumption Goods vs. Capital Goods (Gross Investment, Depreciation, Net Investment).
  * Excludability & Rivalry Matrix: Pure Public Goods, Private Goods, Common Pool Resources, Club Goods.
  * Merit Goods, Demerit Goods, Veblen Goods, and Giffen Goods.
* **1.7 Examination Lenses & Trap Compendium**: UPSC, RPSC RAS, and Banking distinction checkpoints.
* **1.8 Active Recall Diagnostic Cards**.

### Chapter 02: National Income Accounting, GVA & The 2015 NSO Methodology
* **2.1 The Four Pillars of National Output**: GDP, NDP, GNP, and NNP defined from first principles.
* **2.2 The Geographic vs. National Demarcation**: Domestic Territory vs. National Residency; Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA).
* **2.3 The 2015 Methodological Shift by NSO**:
  * Factor Cost ($FC$) $\longrightarrow$ Basic Price ($BP$) $\longrightarrow$ Market Price ($MP$).
  * Disentangling Product Taxes/Subsidies from Production Taxes/Subsidies.
  * Headline GDP transition from GDP at Factor Cost to GDP at Market Price.
  * The Role of MCA-21 Database.
* **2.4 Gross Value Added (GVA)**: Sectoral compilation and relationship with GDP ($GDP = \sum GVA_{BP} + \text{Net Product Taxes}$).
* **2.5 Price Adjustments**: Nominal GDP, Real GDP, and the GDP Deflator (vs. CPI).
* **2.6 Capital Productivity**: Capital-Output Ratio (COR), Incremental Capital-Output Ratio (ICOR), and the Harrod-Domar growth dynamic.
* **2.7 Potential GDP & Output Gap**: Okun's Law, inflationary vs. recessionary gaps.
* **2.8 Distinction Matrices & Traps**: Factor Cost vs Basic Price vs Market Price; Nominal vs PPP GDP.

### Chapter 03: Economic Growth, Capital Productivity & Human Development Metrics
* **3.1 Economic Growth vs. Economic Development**: Quantitative expansion vs. qualitative structural capability.
* **3.2 The Capabilities Approach of Amartya Sen**: Freedoms, entitlements, and functionings.
* **3.3 Global Indices & Methodologies**:
  * Human Development Index (HDI): Dimensions, minimum/maximum goalposts, Geometric Mean aggregation.
  * Inequality-adjusted HDI (IHDI), Gender Development Index (GDI), Gender Inequality Index (GII).
  * Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) by OPHI & NITI Aayog: 3 dimensions, 12 indicators.
* **3.4 Alternative Welfare Paradigms**: Gross National Happiness (Bhutan), Green GDP, Genuine Progress Indicator.
* **3.5 International Classifications**: World Bank Atlas Method (GNI per capita tiers) and IMF Emerging/Advanced criteria.
