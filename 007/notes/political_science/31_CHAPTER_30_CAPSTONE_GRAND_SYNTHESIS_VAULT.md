## 4. The 6 Forms of Parliamentary Majorities: Mathematical Mechanics

| Majority Type | Mathematical Formulation | Specific Constitutional Triggers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Simple Majority** | $> 50\%$ of members present and voting. | • Passing Ordinary, Money, and Financial Bills.<br>• Confidence / No-Confidence Motions.<br>• Censure Motion, Adjournment Motion.<br>• Approving President's Rule (Art 356).<br>• Approving Financial Emergency (Art 360).<br>• Election of Speaker / Deputy Speaker.<br>• Creation/Abolition of Legislative Council by Parliament (Art 169(3)). |
| **2. Effective Majority** | $> 50\%$ of Effective Strength of House $[\text{Total Seats} - \text{Vacancies}]$. | • Removal of Vice-President in Rajya Sabha (Article 67(b)).<br>• Removal of Speaker / Deputy Speaker of Lok Sabha & State Assemblies.<br>• Removal of Chairman / Deputy Chairman of State Legislative Councils. |
| **3. Absolute Majority** | $> 50\%$ of Total Sanctioned Strength (e.g., $272/543$ in Lok Sabha). | • Formation of Government post-elections (used for government formation and confidence, not for voting on normal legislative bills). |
| **4. Special Majority (Type 1: Art 249 / 312)** | $\ge 2/3$ of members present and voting. | • Rajya Sabha resolution authorizing Parliament to legislate on State List (Article 249).<br>• Rajya Sabha resolution creating All-India Services (Article 312). |
| **5. Special Majority (Type 2: Art 368)** | $\ge 2/3$ of members present and voting **PLUS** $> 50\%$ of Total Membership of the House. | • Constitutional Amendment Bills under Article 368.<br>• Removal of Supreme Court & High Court Judges.<br>• Removal of CAG, CEC (Article 124(4)).<br>• Approval of National Emergency (Article 352).<br>• State Assembly resolution to create/abolish Legislative Council (Art 169(1)). |
| **6. Special Majority (Type 3: Art 61)** | $\ge 2/3$ of **TOTAL SANCTIONED MEMBERSHIP** of the House (e.g., $\ge 362$ in Lok Sabha, $\ge 164$ in Rajya Sabha). | • Impeachment of the President of India strictly under Article 61 on grounds of "Violation of the Constitution". |

## 5. Master Distinction Matrices

### A. President vs. Governor: Constitutional Disparities

| Power / Provision | President of India | Governor of a State |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pardoning Power (Pardon)** | Can pardon court-martial (military) sentences. Can grant full PARDON for death sentences under Article 72. | CANNOT pardon court-martial sentences. CANNOT grant full pardon for death sentences (can only suspend, remit, or commute death sentences under Article 161). |
| **Discretionary Powers** | Possesses ONLY Situational Discretion (no explicit constitutional discretionary powers). | Possesses BOTH Situational Discretion AND Constitutional Discretion (Article 163(1)). |
| **Ordinance Making** | Article 123: Promulgated on Union and Concurrent Lists on advice of Union Cabinet. | Article 213: Promulgated on State and Concurrent Lists; requires Presidential instructions for certain state subjects. |
| **Reservation of Bills** | Cannot reserve bills for anyone; can assent, withhold, or return (except Money Bills). | Article 200: Can reserve State legislative bills for the consideration of the President (Article 201). |

### C. Article 32 vs. Article 226 Writs Matrix
