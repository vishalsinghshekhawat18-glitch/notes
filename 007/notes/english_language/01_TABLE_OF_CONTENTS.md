## Detailed Chapter Breakdown

### Part I: Foundational Grammar & Syntactic Architecture
* **Chapter 01: The 120 Golden Rules of Grammar & Error Spotting Diagnostic Suite**
  * *1.1 Subject-Verb Agreement Mechanics*: Proximity vs. First-subject rules, compound subjects, collective nouns, fractional quantifiers, relative pronoun antecedents.
  * *1.2 Syntactic Inversion & Correlative Conjunctions*: Negative adverb inversion (*Hardly, Scarcely, Seldom, Rarely, Barely, Little*), *No sooner...than*, *Lest...should*, paired coordinators.
  * *1.3 The Conditional Sentences Matrix*: Real (Type 1), Unreal Present/Hypothetical (Type 2, subjunctive *were*), and Unfulfilled Past (Type 3).
  * *1.4 Modifier Mechanics & Participial Traps*: Dangling participles, misplaced adverbs (*only, just*), squirming modifiers.
  * *1.5 Parallelism & Comparative Structures*: Faulty parallelism in compound predicates, correlative conjunctions, and elliptical comparisons.
  * *1.6 Tense Harmony, Gerund vs. Infinitive, and Voice Discipline*: Sequence of tenses, stative verbs, bare infinitives after causative verbs, preposition + gerund dependencies.
  * *1.7 The 60-Second Grammar Memory Skeleton & Active Recall Diagnostic Cards*.

* **Chapter 02: Syntactic Architecture, Sentence Variety & Academic Tone Modulation**
  * *2.1 The Four Sentence Architectures*: Simple, Compound, Complex, and Compound-Complex; rhythm and periodic cadence.
  * *2.2 Eradicating Weak Writing*: Passive voice overuse, nominalization ("zombie nouns"), expletive constructions (*there is / it is*).
  * *2.3 Academic Tone Modulation*: Hedging words, evidentiary language, eliminating emotional and sensational colloquialisms.

  * *3.3 Loqu / Dict / Voc / Phon*: Speech, vocalization, verbal expression (*grandiloquent, loquacious, obloquy, dictate, vocation, equivocal*).
  * *3.4 Phil / Phob / Mis*: Love, fear, hatred (*philanthropy, bibliophile, claustrophobia, misanthrope, misogyny*).
  * *3.5 Path / Sent / Sens*: Feeling, perception, suffering (*apathy, antipathy, empathy, sentimental, sentient*).
  * *3.6 Vert / Vers / Tort*: Turning, twisting, bending (*extrovert, introvert, animadversion, contortion, tortuous*).
  * *3.7 Chron / Temp / Ann*: Time, duration, cycles (*chronology, anachronism, temporal, extempore, perennial*).
