## 3. Agricultural Census & Operational Landholdings Dynamics

The structure of agricultural landholding reflects demographic pressure and the progressive fragmentation of joint families across Rajasthan:

### 3.1 Holding Count and Size Distribution (Agriculture Census Baseline)
- **Total Operational Holdings (कुल प्रचलित भूमि जोतें)**: Increased from 68.88 Lakh (2010–11) to **76.55 Lakh** (an increase of **11.14%**).
- **Total Operated Area (कुल प्रचालित क्षेत्रफल)**: Decreased from 211.36 Lakh ha to **208.73 Lakh ha** (a decline of **1.24%** due to industrialization, urbanization, and highway corridors).
- **Average Landholding Size (औसत जोत आकार)**: Decreased from 3.07 hectares to **2.73 Hectares**. *(Note: Despite fragmentation, Rajasthan's average holding size of 2.73 ha remains substantially higher than the national average of 1.08 ha, driven by massive arid landholdings in Jaisalmer and Bikaner)*.

## 6. Animal Husbandry: The Economic Backbone (पशुपालन)

### 6.1 Macro-Economic Contribution to GSVA
- While traditional crop production fluctuates widely due to erratic monsoonal precipitation, **Animal Husbandry provides structural resilience**.
- **GSVA Contribution (2025–26 at Current Prices)**:
  - Animal Husbandry generates **₹2.17 Lakh Crore**, accounting for **49.35% of total agricultural GSVA** (exceeding the crop sector's 42.61%).
- **Gross Value of Output (GVO) Composition from Livestock**:
  - **Milk (दूध)**: Contributes **79.60%** of total livestock output value.
  - **Meat (including poultry)**: Contributes **7.46%**.
  - **Other Livestock Products (Wool, dung, hides)**: Contributes **12.27%**.
  - **Eggs**: Contributes **0.67%**.

### 6.2 20th Livestock Census Analysis (2019 Baseline)
- Conducted quinquennially by the **Board of Revenue, Ajmer**:
- **Total Livestock Population**: **56.8 Million (568 Lakh)**.
  - Decreased by **1.66%** compared to the 19th Census (57.7 Million in 2012).
- **National Share**: Rajasthan holds **10.60% of India's total livestock** (Ranked **#2 in India** after Uttar Pradesh).

## 7. Institutional Infrastructure & Agricultural Marketing Reforms

### 7.1 Regulated Markets & APMC Architecture
- **Rajasthan Agricultural Produce Markets Act, 1961 (राजस्थान कृषि उपज मंडी अधिनियम)**:
  - Establishes regulated market yards (*Krishi Upaj Mandis*) across the state to eliminate trader monopolies and guarantee fair weighment.
  - **Krishi Upaj Mandis**: 146+ Main Market Yards and 300+ Sub-Market Yards.
  - **National Electronic Agriculture Market (e-NAM)**: Over 140 Mandis integrated on the digital unified e-trading portal.
- **Rajasthan State Agricultural Marketing Board (RSAMB / कृषि विपणन बोर्ड)**: Established in **1974** to construct rural link roads, modern mandi yards, and cold storages.

## 8. State Policy Enactments & The Dedicated Agriculture Budget

### 8.1 The Dedicated Agriculture Budget (कृषि बजट)
- In **2022–23**, Rajasthan became one of the pioneers in India to present a **Separate Agriculture Budget** alongside the General State Budget.
- **Rajasthan Climate Smart Agriculture Missions (12 Key Missions)**:
  1. *Rajasthan Millets Promotion Mission (बाजरा व पोषक अनाज)*: Center of Excellence for Millets at Jodhpur.
  2. *Rajasthan Organic Farming Mission*: 50,000 farmers supported with organic certifications and bio-inputs.
  3. *Rajasthan Seed Production & Distribution Mission*: Free minikits of high-yielding hybrid seeds.
  4. *Rajasthan Micro-Irrigation Mission*: 70–75% subsidy on drip and sprinkler installation.
  5. *Rajasthan Protected Cultivation Mission*: Greenhouses, shade-net houses, and low-tunnels.
  6. *Rajasthan Horticulture Development Mission*: Fruit orchards promotion.
  7. *Rajasthan Agriculture Research & Technology Mission*: Agri-drones and mobile soil labs.
  8. *Rajasthan Food Processing Mission*: Capital subsidies for agro-processing units.
  9. *Rajasthan Crop Protection Mission*: Subsidies for barbed wire fencing (*Tarbandi*) to protect crops from wild strays.
  10. *Rajasthan Farm Pond Mission (खेत तलाई)*: Financial aid for plastic-lined farm ponds.
  11. *Rajasthan Agricultural Machinery Mission*: Custom hiring centers at Gram Panchayat level.
  12. *Rajasthan Young Farmers Skill & Agri-Business Mission*.

### 8.2 Key Farmer & Livestock Welfare Schemes
1. **Mukhyamantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana**:
   - Supplements the Central PM-KISAN grant (₹6,000) with an additional **₹2,000 from the State Government**, providing eligible farmers with **₹8,000 annually**.
2. **Gopal Credit Card Scheme (GCC)**:
   - Provides **short-term interest-free loans up to ₹1 Lakh** to milk producers and livestock farmers for feed, sheds, and veterinary care.
3. **Mukhyamantri Ayushman Dugdh Sambal Yojana**:
   - Provides a direct DBT subsidy of **₹5 per litre** to milk producers supplying to cooperative dairy societies (RCDF / SARAS).
4. **Mukhyamantri Kamdhenu Bima Yojana**:
   - Free livestock insurance covering **up to two milch animals per household** (cow/buffalo) with a maximum insurance cover of **₹40,000 per animal** against accidental death or disease.
5. **Kisan Seva Portal & RajKisan Sathi**:
   - Single-window digital platform for agricultural subsidies, seed minikits, and farm mechanization applications.

### 9.1 Mahatma Gandhi NREGS (The Rajasthan 125-Day Model)
- Under the **Mukhyamantri Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Yojana**, the State Government finances an **additional 25 days of employment** beyond the statutory 100 days under central MGNREGS, guaranteeing **125 days of wage employment**.
- *Vulnerable Communities*: Scheduled Tribes of **Sahariya** (Baran), **Kathodi** (Udaipur), and **Divyangjans** are granted **200 days of guaranteed employment**.

### 9.2 Rajivika & Mahila Nidhi: Financial Independence
- **Rajasthan Grameen Aajeevika Vikas Parishad (RAJIVIKA / राजीविका)**:
  - Autonomous society registered in **October 2010** under the Department of Rural Development.
  - Organizes rural women into a three-tier federation: **Self-Help Groups (SHGs) $\rightarrow$ Village Organizations (VOs) $\rightarrow$ Cluster Level Federations (CLFs)**.
- **Rajasthan Mahila Nidhi**:
  - Established on **26 August 2022** (Women's Equality Day) in collaboration with Telangana's *Stree Nidhi*.
  - **First Women's Cooperative Credit Institution** in Rajasthan, providing fast micro-credit (loans up to ₹40,000 within 48 hours) directly to SHG members.
- **Lakhpati Didi Initiative**: Scaling women SHG members' net household annual income beyond **₹1 Lakh** through digital literacy, micro-enterprises, and dairy collectives.

### 9.3 Watershed Management & Water Harvesting (RGJSY)
- **Rajiv Gandhi Jal Sanchay Yojana (RGJSY)**:
  - Initiated on **20 August 2019** to converge state and central resources for rainwater harvesting, check-dams, anicuts, and percolation tanks across water-stressed non-command blocks.

## 10. Critical Exam Traps & Conceptual Nuances

1. **GVA Composition (The Animal Husbandry Dominance)**:
   - At current prices, **Livestock (49.35%)** contributes more to Agricultural GVA than **Crops (42.61%)**!
   - In GVO of livestock products, **Milk represents 79.60%**.

2. **Landholding Fragmentation**:
   - Holding numbers increased by **11.14%** (to 76.55 Lakh), while total area operated declined by **1.24%** (to 208.73 Lakh ha).
   - Average size: **2.73 Hectares** (down from 3.07 ha).
   - **Marginal holdings (<1 ha)** are the largest group (**40.12%**).
