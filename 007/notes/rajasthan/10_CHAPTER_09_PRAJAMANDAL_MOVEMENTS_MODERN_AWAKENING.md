### 1.1 The Concept of "Praja Mandal" (People's Council)

## 3. Deep-Dive Curricular Analysis: Major Prajamandals

### 3.1 The Jaipur Prajamandal & The Gentlemen's Agreement (1942)
- **Founding & Reorganization (1931 & 1936–1938)**:
  - First founded in 1931 by **Kapur Chand Patni**, making it the earliest Prajamandal in Rajasthan. Due to severe state surveillance, it lapsed into dormancy.
  - Reorganized in 1936–1937 under the active patronship of national treasurer **Seth Jamnalal Bajaj**, along with **Chiranjilal Mishra** and **Hiralal Shastri**.
  - **First Annual Session (8–9 May 1938)**: Convened at Jaipur under the presidency of Jamnalal Bajaj; attended by **Kasturba Gandhi**.
- **The Historic Gentlemen's Agreement (September 1942)**:
  - During the pan-Indian Quit India crisis (August 1942), Jaipur Prime Minister **Sir Mirza Ismail** entered into secret bilateral negotiations with Jaipur Prajamandal President **Hiralal Shastri**.
  - **Bilateral Terms**:
    1. The Prajamandal pledged **NOT to launch Quit India satyagraha or strikes** against Jaipur state.
    2. Jaipur State pledged **not to supply state soldiers or funds to the British imperial war effort**, permitted peaceful anti-British propaganda, and promised gradual transition to responsible government.

### 3.2 The Marwar (Jodhpur) Prajamandal & Jai Narayan Vyas
- **Institutional Genesis (1934 CE)**: Founded in 1934 by **Jai Narayan Vyas**, **Bhanwarlal Saraf** (elected first President), and Abhaymal Jain.
- **Marwar Lok Parishad (May 1938 CE)**:
  - Re-constituted under the presidency of **Ranchhod Das Gattani** to mobilize youth, peasants, and civil servants against royal Prime Minister Sir Donald Field.
- **The Martyrdom of Balmukund Bissa (19 June 1942)**:
  - **Identity**: Revered as the **"Jallianwala / Bhagat Singh of Rajasthan"**, born at Peelwa village (Didwana); established **Jawahar Khadi Bhandar** (1934) in Jodhpur as an underground nationalist center.
  - **The Prison Fast**: Imprisoned during civil disobedience; launched an indefinite hunger strike against unhygienic jail food, lack of medical facilities, and brutal warden assaults in Jodhpur Central Jail.
  - **Martyrdom**: Collapsed due to state medical negligence and force-feeding pneumonia on **19 June 1942**. Over one lakh grief-stricken citizens marched in his funeral procession.
- **Siwana Fort Detention**: During the 1942 Quit India crackdowns, top Marwar leaders including **Jai Narayan Vyas** were imprisoned in the isolated **Siwana Fort** (Barmer).

### 3.3 The Mewar Prajamandal & The 1945 Udaipur AISPC Conference
- **Founding (24 April 1938 CE)**:
  - Established at Udaipur at the personal residence of **Balwant Singh Mehta** (*Sahitya Kuteer*).
  - First President: **Balwant Singh Mehta**; Vice-President: **Bhuralal Baya**; General Secretary: **Manikya Lal Verma**.
  - **State Ban & Devgarh Assault**: Mewar Prime Minister Dharmanarayan banned the organization within days; Verma was banished from Mewar. When Verma attempted to re-enter across the Devgarh border, state police assaulted him into unconsciousness.
  - **The "Kalapani of Mewar"**: Vice-President **Bhuralal Baya** was kept in rigorous solitary confinement in the dread **Sarada Fort (सराड़ा दुर्ग)**, popularly branded the *"Kalapani of Mewar"*.
- **First Annual Session (November 1941 CE)**:
  - Inaugurated at Udaipur by Congress President **Acharya J.B. Kripalani**; keynote address delivered by **Smt. Vijaya Lakshmi Pandit**.
- **The Seventh All-India States' Peoples' Conference (31 Dec 1945 – 1 Jan 1946)**:
  - Convened at **Salotia Ground, Udaipur**; the **first time in Indian history** that the national annual plenary session of the AISPC was convened within a princely state!
  - Presided over by **Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru**; attended by Jammu & Kashmir leader **Sheikh Abdullah**.

### 3.4 The Bikaner Prajamandal & The Birbal Day Sacrifice
- **Founding in Calcutta (4 October 1936 CE)**:
  - Established in **Calcutta** (Machhua Bazar) by **Magharam Vaidya** and Laxmi Das Swami to evade Maharaja Ganga Singh’s secret police.
- **Bikaner Conspiracy Case (1932 CE)**:
  - During the Second Round Table Conference in London, nationalist cadres circulated the explosive pamphlet **"Bikaner: A True Picture" (बीकानेर एक दिग्दर्शन)** exposing Maharaja Ganga Singh’s autocratic governance behind his international modern facade.
  - Ganga Singh arrested Swami Gopal Das, Chandanmal Bahad, and Satyanarayan Saraf under charges of treason in the infamous *Bikaner Conspiracy Case*.
- **Churu Dharm Stupa Tricolor Incident (26 January 1930)**:
  - **Swami Gopal Das** and **Chandanmal Bahad** hoisted the Indian National Tricolor atop the historic **Dharm Stupa** (Red Tower / Dharam Stupa) of Churu amidst intense police vigil.
- **The Martyrdom of Birbal Singh Dhaliwal (1946 CE)**:
  - On **30 June 1946**, at Raisinghnagar (Sri Ganganagar), a peaceful Prajamandal procession led by **Birbal Singh Dhaliwal** was fired upon by state police.
  - Birbal took three bullets in his chest while safeguarding the tricolor flag, dying with the flag aloft on 1 July 1946.
  - On **17 July 1946**, Bikaner Praja Parishad observed **"Birbal Day" (बीरबल दिवस)** across the state.
  - The Indira Gandhi Canal project memorialized his sacrifice by designating the **Birbal Shakha** in his honor.

### 3.5 The Dungarpur Prajamandal & The Rastapal Martyrdom (1947)
- **Founding (1944 CE)**: Established by **Bhogilal Pandya (भोगीलाल पांड्या)** — revered as the **"Gandhi of Vagad" (वागड़ के गांधी)**, alongside Harideo Joshi and Shivlal Kotadia.
- **The Punawada Incident (May 1947)**: State police attacked a Seva Sangh school at Punawada, brutally thrashing Bhil teacher Shivram.
- **The Rastapal Incident (19 June 1947 / रास्तापाल कांड)**:
  - The Dungarpur state police and district magistrate marched to **Rastapal village** to forcibly demolish a nationalist primary school operated by the Seva Sangh.
  - Headmaster **Nana Bhai Khant (नाना भाई खांट)** refused to surrender the keys and was bludgeoned to death on the spot.
  - The police tied teacher **Sengabhai (सेंगाभाई)** to the back of their military truck with thick ropes and drove off, dragging him over gravel and jagged rocks.
  - **The Supreme Sacrifice of Kalibai**:
    - A 13-year-old Bhil schoolgirl, **Kalibai (कालीबाई)**, grazing cattle nearby, witnessed her teacher being dragged toward death.
    - Armed only with her small agricultural sickle (*Danti*), she sprinted past armed constables and slashed the rope, freeing Sengabhai.
    - Police fired point-blank; Kalibai sustained multiple rifle bullet wounds and succumbed on **21 June 1947**.
  - **Memorial**: A statue of Veer Balika Kalibai stands on the banks of **Gaib Sagar Lake (गैब सागर)** in Dungarpur. Rajasthan Government institutes the *Kalibai Bheel Medhavi Chhatra Scooty Yojana* in her honor.

### 3.6 Jaisalmer Prajamandal & The Fiery Ordeal of Sagar Mal Gopa
- **Founding (1945 CE)**: Established at **Jodhpur** by **Mithalal Vyas** because Maharawal Jawahar Singh banned all civil political organizations inside Jaisalmer.
- **Sagar Mal Gopa (1900–1946 CE) — The Lion of the Thar**:
  - Exiled from Jaisalmer for authoring explosive nationalist treatises exposing royal despotism:
    1. **Jaisalmer Ka Gundaraj (जैसलमेर का गुंडाराज — The Mafia Rule of Jaisalmer)**
    2. **Azadi Ke Diwane (आजादी के दीवाने)**
    3. **Raghunath Singh Ka Muqadma (रघुनाथ सिंह का मुकदमा)**
  - Arrested treacherously on 25 May 1941 when visiting his ailing father under false royal safe-conduct assurances.
  - **The Jailhouse Murder (3–4 April 1946)**:
    - Subjected to horrific physical torture for five years in Jaisalmer Central Jail under Jailor **Guman Singh**.
    - On the night of 3 April 1946, Guman Singh poured kerosene over Gopa’s body and set him ablaze inside his cell. Gopa succumbed to his burns on 4 April 1946.
    - Following nationwide outrage, the state appointed the **Gopal Swaroop Pathak Commission**, which submitted a whitewash report declaring the murder a "suicide".

### 3.7 Kota Prajamandal & The 1942 People's Capture of Kota Fort
- **Founding (1939 CE)**: Founded by **Pt. Nayanuram Sharma** and **Abhinna Hari** after the dissolution of Hadoti Prajamandal.
- **First Session (1939)**: Held at **Mangrol (Baran)** under the presidency of Pt. Nayanuram Sharma.
- **The Historic 1942 Quit India Civil Coup**:
  - Following the arrest of national leaders in August 1942, Kota students and Prajamandal cadres led by **Nathulal Jain**, Motilal Agarwal, and Shambhudayal Saksena revolted.
  - Prajamandal volunteers **stormed the Kotwali (Police Station) and captured the city administration**, confining Maharao Bhim Singh II to his palace.
  - **Tricolor on Kota Fort**: Activists hoisted the Indian Tricolor atop Kota Fort and **governed the city administration autonomously for 3 continuous days** until negotiations peacefully restored administrative status without police firing!

### 3.8 Alwar & Bharatpur Prajamandals
- **Alwar Prajamandal (1938 CE)**:
  - Founded by **Pt. Harinarayan Sharma** and **Kunjbihari Lal Modi**.
  - Harinarayan Sharma pioneered Dalit upliftment by founding the *Asprishyata Nivaran Sangh* and *Valmiki Seva Sangh*.
  - **First Session (1944)**: Held at Rajgarh under the presidency of **Bhawani Shankar Sharma**. Agitated vigorously against Jagirdari extortion and the princely administration's ban on nationalist schools.
- **Bharatpur Prajamandal (1938 CE)**:
  - Founded in **Rewari (Haryana)** in 1938 by **Kishen Lal Joshi** to evade state surveillance; first President was **Gopilal Yadav**, Vice-President was **Master Adityendra**, and General Secretary was **Jugal Kishore Chaturvedi**.
  - **Jugal Kishore Chaturvedi**: Celebrated across North India as the **"Jawaharlal Nehru of Rajasthan"** for his socialist ideology and striking physical and rhetorical resemblance to Nehru.
  - **The Bhusawar Martyrdom (1947)**: Prajamandal satyagrahi **Ramesh Swami** was martyred on 5 February 1947 at Bhusawar when state police drove a bus over him during a protest.

### 3.9 Sirohi & Shahpura Prajamandals
- **Sirohi Prajamandal (1934 & 1939 CE)**:
  - Established in **Bombay (1934)** by Vridhi Shankar Trivedi and Samrathmal Singhi; formally reconstituted at Sirohi in **January 1939** under the supreme leadership of **Gokulbhai Bhatt** — celebrated as the **"Gandhi of Rajasthan" (राजस्थान के गांधी)**.
  - Bhatt championed the merger of Sirohi and Mount Abu into Rajasthan against Sardar Patel's initial merger of Sirohi into Bombay State.
- **Shahpura Prajamandal (1938 CE)**:
  - Founded by **Ramesh Chandra Ojha**, **Laduram Vyas**, and **Gokul Lal Asawa**.
  - **First Responsible Government in Rajasthan**: Progressive monarch **Maharaja Sudarshan Dev** granted total responsible government to the people on **14 August 1947**, swearing in a popular democratic cabinet headed by Prime Minister **Gokul Lal Asawa**.

### 3.10 Peripheral & Thikana Prajamandals
1. **Dholpur Prajamandal (1936)**:
   - Founded by **Krishnadutt Paliwal**, Mulchand, and Jwala Prasad Jigyasu.
   - **The Tasimo Firing (11 April 1947 / तसीमों कांड)**:
     - State police raided a Prajamandal gathering at Tasimo village to tear down the Indian Tricolor.
     - Satyagrahis **Chhatar Singh Parmar** and **Pancham Singh Kushwaha** shielded the flag with their bodies and were martyred by police rifle fire.
2. **Karauli Prajamandal (1939)**:
   - Founded by **Trilok Chand Mathur**, Chiranjilal Sharma, and Kunwar Madan Singh (who led an earlier agrarian agitation in 1927).
3. **Kishangarh Prajamandal (1939)**:
   - Founded by **Kantilal Chauthani** and **Jamal Shah**.
4. **Kushalgarh Prajamandal (April 1942)**:
   - Autonomous Thikana Prajamandal founded by **Bhanwarlal Nigam** and celebrated poet **Kanhaiyalal Sethia**.
5. **Banswara Prajamandal (1943)**:
   - Founded by **Bhupendranath Trivedi**, Dhulji Bhai Bhavsar, and Mani Shankar Nagar. Trivedi edited *Sangram* from Bombay; **Vijayabehn Bhavsar** mobilized women.
6. **Pratapgarh Prajamandal (1945)**:
   - Founded by **Amritlal Payak** and **Chunilal Prabhakar**, mobilizing Bhil and Meena cultivators against Jagirdari exploitation.
7. **Jhalawar Prajamandal (25 November 1946)**:
   - Founded by **Mangilal Bhavya** (President), Kanhaiyalal Mittal, and Maqbool Alam.
   - **Unique Constitutional Feature**: Progressive ruler **Maharaja Harishchandra** recognized the Prajamandal legally and conceded responsible government peacefully without requiring public satyagraha.

## 7. The 25 Deadliest Examination Traps (Forensic Autopsy)

1. **Trap 1: The Chronologically First Prajamandal**:
   - *Trap*: Selecting Mewar or Marwar due to their prominence.
   - *Fact*: **Jaipur Prajamandal (1931 CE)** founded by **Kapur Chand Patni** was the earliest, followed by Bundi (1931).
2. **Trap 2: Prajamandals Founded Outside Their Own State**:
   - *Trap*: Assuming all Prajamandals were established within their local boundaries.
   - *Fact*: Four Prajamandals were launched outside:
     1. **Bikaner (1936)**: Founded in **Calcutta** by Magharam Vaidya.
     2. **Bharatpur (1938)**: Founded in **Rewari (Haryana)** by Kishen Lal Joshi.
     3. **Sirohi (1934)**: Founded in **Bombay** by Vridhi Shankar Trivedi.
     4. **Jaisalmer (1945)**: Founded in **Jodhpur** by Mithalal Vyas.
3. **Trap 3: Signatories to the Gentlemen's Agreement (1942)**:
   - *Trap*: Signed between Jamnalal Bajaj and the Maharaja of Jaipur.
   - *Fact*: Concluded between Jaipur Prime Minister **Sir Mirza Ismail** and Prajamandal President **Hiralal Shastri**.
4. **Trap 4: Founders of Azad Morcha (1942)**:
   - *Trap*: Hiralal Shastri formed it to fight the British.
   - *Fact*: Founded by dissident nationalists led by **Baba Harish Chandra**, Ramkaran Joshi, and Hans D. Roy **in rebellion against Hiralal Shastri's Gentlemen's Agreement**.
5. **Trap 5: First President of Mewar Prajamandal**:
   - *Trap*: Manikya Lal Verma.
   - *Fact*: **Balwant Singh Mehta** was the first President; Manikya Lal Verma was the General Secretary!
6. **Trap 6: The "Gandhi of Vagad" vs "Gandhi of Rajasthan"**:
   - *Trap*: Conflating Bhogilal Pandya and Gokulbhai Bhatt.
   - *Fact*: **Bhogilal Pandya** is the *"Gandhi of Vagad"* (Dungarpur); **Gokulbhai Bhatt** is the *"Gandhi of Rajasthan"* (Sirohi).
7. **Trap 7: First State to Establish Responsible Government**:
   - *Trap*: Jaipur, Mewar, or Bikaner.
   - *Fact*: **Shahpura** on **14 August 1947**, instituted under Prime Minister **Gokul Lal Asawa** by progressive monarch **Maharaja Sudarshan Dev**.
8. **Trap 8: The Martyr of Raisinghnagar (1946)**:
   - *Trap*: Balmukund Bissa or Sagar Mal Gopa.
   - *Fact*: **Birbal Singh Dhaliwal**, martyred on 1 July 1946 protecting the national flag; "Birbal Day" observed across Bikaner on 17 July 1946.
9. **Trap 9: The "Jallianwala / Bhagat Singh of Rajasthan"**:
   - *Trap*: Amar Chand Bathiya.
   - *Fact*: **Balmukund Bissa** of Marwar, who died of a hunger strike and prison torture in Jodhpur Central Jail on 19 June 1942.

## 9. RPSC RAS Examination Model Answer Laboratory

### 9.1 Section A: 2-Mark High-Density Formulations (15–20 Words)

#### Q1: What was the primary resolution of the Haripura Congress Session (1938) regarding princely states?
**Answer**: Presided over by Subhas Chandra Bose, Congress ended its non-intervention policy, authorizing the formation of Prajamandals to agitate for responsible self-government (*Uttardayi Shasan*).

#### Q2: What was the Gentlemen's Agreement (1942)?
**Answer**: Signed between Jaipur PM Sir Mirza Ismail and Prajamandal President Hiralal Shastri: Prajamandal boycotted Quit India; state pledged gradual responsible governance and withheld war aid to British.

#### Q3: Who founded Azad Morcha, and why?
**Answer**: Formed in September 1942 by **Baba Harish Chandra**, Ramkaran Joshi, and Hans D. Roy, repudiating the Gentlemen's Agreement to actively participate in the Quit India Movement.

#### Q4: What is the historical significance of the Rastapal Incident (19 June 1947)?
**Answer**: 13-year-old Bhil student **Kalibai** and teacher Nana Bhai Khant were martyred in Dungarpur state while bravely rescuing teacher Sengabhai from being dragged behind a police truck.

#### Q5: Who was Balmukund Bissa?
**Answer**: Revered as the *"Jallianwala / Bhagat Singh of Rajasthan"*, a Marwar freedom fighter who died on 19 June 1942 following an indefinite hunger strike in Jodhpur Central Jail.

#### Q6: Name the revolutionary books authored by martyr Sagar Mal Gopa.
**Answer**: *Jaisalmer Ka Gundaraj*, *Azadi Ke Diwane*, and *Raghunath Singh Ka Muqadma*.

#### Q7: Which was the first princely state in Rajasthan to establish fully responsible government?
**Answer**: **Shahpura** on 14 August 1947, instituted by Maharaja Sudarshan Dev under the prime ministership of **Gokul Lal Asawa**.

#### Q8: What was the Bikaner Conspiracy Case (1932)?
**Answer**: Maharaja Ganga Singh prosecuted Swami Gopal Das, Chandanmal Bahad, and Satyanarayan Saraf for treason after they circulated the exposé pamphlet *Bikaner: A True Picture* at the London Round Table Conference.

### 9.2 Section B: 5-Mark Analytical Blueprints (50 Words)

#### Q9: Analyze the dual mandate and accomplishments of the Prajamandal movement in Rajasthan.
**Answer**:
The Prajamandals bridged local socio-economic grievances with pan-Indian democratic nationalism:
- **Dual Mandate**: Agitated for responsible self-government (*Uttardayi Shasan*) under princely auspices while demanding complete civil liberties (free speech, free press, abolition of Begar and arbitrary cesses).
- **Accomplishments**:
  - Mobilized women, youth, peasants, and marginalized tribals (Kalibai), destroying centuries of feudal political inertia.
  - Trained an articulate democratic leadership that prevented princely balkanization and facilitated Rajasthan’s integration into the Indian Republic (1948–1956).

#### Q10: Detail the martyrdom of Sagar Mal Gopa and its impact on the democratic awakening in Jaisalmer.
**Answer**:
Sagar Mal Gopa (1900–1946 CE) was the intellectual vanguard of anti-feudal resistance in Jaisalmer:
- **Ideological Defiance**: Authored explosive treatises (*Jaisalmer Ka Gundaraj*) exposing royal misrule; arrested treacherously in 1941 under false safe-conduct assurances.
- **Torture & Murder**: Endured five years of horrific police torture; set ablaze with kerosene in his cell by Jailor Guman Singh on 3 April 1946 (succumbed 4 April).
- **Impact**: Sparked nationwide indignation, dismantled the moral legitimacy of Maharawal Jawahar Singh, and galvanized the Jaisalmer Prajamandal (founded at Jodhpur).

#### Q11: Highlight the pivotal role played by women in Rajasthan's Prajamandal movements.
**Answer**:
Women breached feudal *Parda* (veil) barriers to become frontline freedom fighters:
- **Leadership & Arrests**: Anjana Devi Chaudhary was the first woman arrested in Rajasthan (1921); Narayani Devi Verma endured jail with a 6-month-old infant, later founding the Mahila Ashram (1944).
- **Mass Mobilization**: Kishori Devi rallied 10,000 women at Katrathal (1934); Ratna Shastri nurtured female self-reliance at Banasthali Vidyapith.
- **Sacrifice & Governance**: 13-year-old Kalibai gave her life for her teacher at Rastapal; Nagendra Bala became India's first woman Zila Pramukh in 1960.

#### Q12: Explain the significance of the 1942 Quit India uprising in Kota State.
**Answer**:
Kota exhibited one of the most remarkable civilian triumphs during the 1942 Quit India movement:
- **Civilian Coup**: Led by Pt. Nayanuram Sharma's associates (Nathulal Jain, Motilal Agarwal, Abhinna Hari), students and Prajamandal cadres seized the city Kotwali and administrative buildings.
- **Tricolor on Fort**: Confined Maharao Bhim Singh II to his palace, hoisted the National Tricolor on Kota Fort, and ran the city administration autonomously for 3 full days.
- **Peaceful Resolution**: Administration was restored through peaceful negotiations without state military bloodshed.

#### Q14: Trace the origin, clauses, and ideological rift caused by the Gentlemen's Agreement of 1942 in Jaipur state, and analyze how the crisis was resolved.
**Answer**:
The Gentlemen's Agreement of September 1942 illustrates the strategic dilemmas confronting regional Prajamandals during the pan-Indian Quit India upheaval:

1. **Origins & Context**:
   - Following Mahatma Gandhi's "Do or Die" call on 8 August 1942, British authorities arrested top Congress leaders nationwide.
   - In Jaipur, enlightened Prime Minister **Sir Mirza Ismail** sought to prevent direct British military occupation of the state, initiating secret negotiations with Prajamandal President **Hiralal Shastri**.

2. **Core Clauses of the Agreement**:
   - **Prajamandal Pledges**: Agreed **not to initiate civil disobedience or strikes** against Jaipur state, maintaining non-violent cooperation.
   - **State Pledges**: Agreed **not to supply war recruits or state funds to the British**, permitted peaceful anti-British meetings, and promised gradual responsible governance.

3. **The Nationalist Rift: Formation of Azad Morcha**:
   - Militant nationalist cadres accused Shastri of compromising national integrity to appease royal ministers.
   - Led by **Baba Harish Chandra**, **Ramkaran Joshi**, **Hans D. Roy**, and **Gulab Chand Kasliwal**, the dissenters broke away to form the **Azad Morcha (आजाद मोर्चा)** in September 1942.
   - Azad Morcha launched aggressive Quit India processions, courting mass arrests across Jaipur.

4. **Resolution & Legacy**:
   - In **1945**, following the end of World War II, **Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru** visited Jaipur and mediated an honorable reconciliation, merging Azad Morcha back into the main Jaipur Prajamandal.
   - While Shastri preserved institutional gains, Azad Morcha ensured that Jaipur’s patriotic contribution to the national Quit India struggle remained unblemished.
