## 1. Macro Mineral Architecture: "The Museum of Minerals" (*खनिजों का अजायबघर*)

Rajasthan's complex poly-cyclic geological evolution—ranging from Archaean basement granites (Banded Gneissic Complex), Pre-Cambrian Aravalli and Delhi Supergroups, to Mesozoic-Tertiary marine sediments and Deccan basalt sheets—has endowed the state with immense mineral variety. It is classically acclaimed in Indian geosciences as the **"Museum of Minerals" (*खनिजों का अजायबघर*)**.

---

### A. National Standing of Rajasthan in Minerals
- **Number of Minerals**: Ranks **1st** in India in the variety of minerals available (81 types).
- **Non-Metallic Mineral Production**: Ranks **1st** in India in the total production volume of non-metallic minerals.
- **Value of Mineral Production**: Ranks **2nd** in India (after Odisha) in the total value of mineral production (excluding atomic minerals and petroleum).
- **Mining Leases**: Ranks **1st** in India in the number of active major and minor mineral mining leases.

### A. Lead and Zinc (*सीसा-जस्ता — Galena & Sphalerite*)
- **Occurrence**: Always occur together as twin minerals (**Judwa Khanij / जुड़वा खनिज**) along with silver ($Ag$) and cadmium ($Cd$) in metamorphic carbonaceous dolomites.
- **Primary Ore**: Galena ($PbS$) for Lead; Sphalerite ($ZnS$) for Zinc.
- **Major Mining Deposits**:
  1. **Rampura Agucha (*रामपुरा आगूचा — भीलवाड़ा*)**:
     - The **world's richest open-pit zinc deposit** with an extraordinary ore grade of over 14% to 15% zinc-lead.
  2. **Zawar Mines (*जावर — उदयपुर*)**:
     - Historical mine discovered during the reign of **Maharana Lakha (c. 1382–1421 CE)**; recognized as one of the oldest zinc smelting sites in world history (Mochia Magra, Balaria, Zawar Mala).
  3. **Rajpura Dariba & Sindesar Khurd (*राजपुरा दरीबा एवं सिंदेसर खुर्द — राजसमंद*)**:
     - Massive mechanized underground zinc mines operated by Hindustan Zinc Ltd (HZL).
  4. **Kayad (*कायड़ — अजमेर*)**: Deep underground mechanized zinc mine.
  5. **Chauth ka Barwara (*चौथ का बरवाड़ा — सवाई माधोपुर*)**.
- **Smelting Complexes**:
  - **Chanderiya Zinc Smelter (*चंदेरिया — चित्तौड़गढ़*)**: Asia's largest single-location pyrometallurgical zinc-lead smelter (HZL).
  - **Debari Zinc Smelter (*देबारी — उदयपुर*)**: Hydrometallurgical smelter established in 1968.

---

### B. Copper (*तांबा*)
- **National Standing**: Rajasthan ranks **2nd in total copper reserves** (after Jharkhand/MP) and **2nd in production**.
- **The Khetri Copper Belt (*खेतड़ी कॉपर बेल्ट — नीम का थाना / झुंझुनू*)**:
  - An 80-km long mineralized metallogenic belt stretching from Singhana to Raghunathgarh.
  - Mining sections: **Madhan-Kudan, Kolihan, Chandmari, Dariba**.
  - **Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL)** established the Khetri Copper Complex (KCC) at Khetri Nagar in 1967.
- **Other Notable Copper Belts**:
  - **Kho-Dariba (*खो-दरीबा — अलवर*)**: Ancient copper workings in Pre-Cambrian Alwar quartzites.
  - **Pur-Banera & Dariba (*पुर-बनेड़ा — भीलवाड़ा*)**: Polymetallic copper-lead-zinc deposit.
  - **Bhagoni (*भगोनी — अलवर*)**.
  - **Bano ki Dhani (*बानो की ढाणी — सीकर*)** and **Bidasar (*बीदासरा — चूरू*)**.

---

### C. Iron Ore (*लोहा अयस्क — Hematite*)
Rajasthan possesses high-grade **Hematite ($Fe_2O_3$)** and Magnetite deposits concentrated in the North-Eastern Aravallis:
1. **Morija-Banjaol (*मोरीजा-बानोल — जयपुर ग्रामीण / कोटपूतली*)**: Largest hematite iron ore deposit in Rajasthan.
2. **Neemla-Raisalo (*नीमला-राइसलो — दौसा*)**: High-grade hematite deposits.
3. **Dabla-Singhana (*डाबला-सिंघाना — झुंझुनू*)**.
4. **Nathra ki Pal & Thur-Hunder (*नाथरा की पाल एवं थूर-हुंडेर — उदयपुर*)**: Ancient iron mines in Southern Rajasthan.
5. **Pur-Banera & Bigod (*पुर-बनेड़ा — भीलवाड़ा*)**.

---

### D. Tungsten (*टंगस्टन — वोल्फ्रेमाइट / Wolframite*)
- **Physical Property**: Highest melting point of all metals (**$3,422^\circ\text{C}$**); indispensable for defense armour-plating, heavy artillery shells, high-speed cutting tools, and electric incandescent filaments.
- **Major Mining Deposits**:
  1. **Degana (*डेगाना — नागौर*)**:
     - Located at **Revat Hill (*रेवत की पहाड़ी*)**; historically the **largest tungsten mine in India** (operated by Hindustan Zinc Ltd, currently dormant).
  2. **Balda (*बालदा — सिरोही*)**: Significant reserve explored by DMG.
  3. **Nana Karara (*नाना कराब — पाली*)**.

---

### E. Gold & Strategic Atomic Minerals
1. **Gold (*सोना*)**:
   - **Anandpur Bhukiya & Jagpura (*आनंदपुर भूकिया एवं जगपुरा — बांसवाड़ा*)**: Proven commercial reserves exceeding 115 million tonnes of gold ore; recently auctioned for commercial gold extraction.
   - **Dhani Baseri (*धानी बासेरी — दौसा*)** and **Khera-Mundiyawas (*अलवर*)**.
2. **Uranium (*यूरेनियम*)**:
   - **Rohil (*रोहिल — खंडेला, सीकर*)**: Discovered in 2022; second largest deposit in India (after Tummalapalle in Andhra Pradesh); estimated reserves of **12 million tonnes of uranium ore**; leased to Uranium Corporation of India Ltd (UCIL).
   - **Umra (*उमरा — उदयपुर*)**: Historical exploration site.
3. **Beryllium (*बेरिलियम*)**: Used in nuclear reactors; found at Gujarwada (Jaipur), Bandarsindri (Ajmer), and Shikarwari (Udaipur).
4. **Manganese (*मैंगनीज*)**: Confined to **Banswara district** (**Lilwani, Kalakhunta, Talwara, Ghatia, Tambesara**).

### A. Rock Phosphate (*रॉक फॉस्फेट*)
- **Sovereign Industrial Value**: Foundational mineral for chemical fertilizers (Single Super Phosphate - SSP, Di-Ammonium Phosphate - DAP).
- **The Jhamarkotra Deposit (*झामरकोटड़ा — उदयपुर*)**:
  - The **largest and richest rock phosphate deposit in India**.
  - Operates state-of-the-art industrial beneficiation plant set up by **RSMML** to upgrade low-grade phosphate rock into high-grade concentrate ($>31.5\%\ P_2O_5$).
- **Other Deposits**: **Birmania (*बीरमानिया — जैसलमेर*)**, Sallopat (*बांसवाड़ा*).

---

### B. Gypsum (*जिप्सम — हरसोंठ / सेलखड़ी / खड़िया*)
- **Occurrence & Age**: Evaporite mineral formed in Tertiary desert basins.
- **Industrial Applications**: Primary raw material for Plaster of Paris (POP), Portland cement manufacturing, and **chemical reclamation of alkaline/sodic agricultural soils**.
- **Key Deposits**:
  1. **Jamsar (*जामसर — बीकानेर*)**: **Largest gypsum mine in India**.
  2. **Lunkaransar (*बीकानेर*)**.
  3. **Badopal & Rawatsar (*हनुमानगढ़*)**: Subterranean hardpan creating the "Sem problem".
  4. **Mohangarh (*जैसलमर*)** and **Bhadwasi & Manglod (*नागौर*)**.

---

### C. Feldspar, Mica & Asbestos
1. **Feldspar (*फेल्डस्पार*)**:
   - Rajasthan produces **over 95% of India's feldspar**.
   - Epicenter: **Ajmer district (Makrera, Taragarh)**, Beawar, and Bhilwara.
2. **Mica (*अभ्रक*)**:
   - Heat-resistant electrical insulator; primary mining hub: **Bhilwara** (famed as the **"Mica City of Rajasthan / अभ्रक नगरी"**; manufactures heat-resistant mica insulating bricks).
3. **Asbestos (*एस्बेस्टस — मिनरल सिल्क*)**:
   - Fibrous fire-resistant amphibole mineral; found at **Rishabhdeo and Kherwara (Udaipur)** and Salumbar.

## 5. Mineral Policies & Institutional Architecture

### A. Rajasthan State Mines & Minerals Limited (RSMML)
- **Corporate History**: Incorporated as a public sector undertaking in **1974**; Headquarters at **Udaipur**.
- **Four Strategic Business Units & Profit Centres (SBU-PC)**:
  1. **SBU-PC Rock Phosphate**: Jhamarkotra (Udaipur) — Core profit engine.
  2. **SBU-PC Lignite**: Giral, Kasnau-Matasukh (Nagaur/Barmer).
  3. **SBU-PC Gypsum**: Bikaner (Jamsar).
  4. **SBU-PC Limestone**: Sanu (Jaisalmer) — Supplies SMS-grade limestone to Indian steel plants (SAIL, TISCO).

### B. District Mineral Foundation Trust (DMFT)
- Established pursuant to the *Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Amendment Act, 2015*.
- A non-profit trust established in every mining district funded by a mandatory cess ($10\%\text{ to }30\%$ of royalty) paid by mining leaseholders.
- **Statutory Mandate**: **60% of DMFT funds** must be reserved exclusively for high-priority areas: **potable drinking water, healthcare, education, women & child welfare, and environmental rehabilitation** in mining-affected rural habitations.

### C. M-Sand Policy (Manufactured Sand Policy, 2020)
- Officially launched on **25 January 2021** by the Rajasthan Government.
- **Objective**: Provide an eco-friendly, engineered alternative to riverbed sand (*Bajri*) by crushing hard granite and quartz mining overburden into precision-graded manufactured sand (M-Sand), preserving vulnerable river ecosystems.
- Grants M-Sand manufacturing units the statutory status of an **Industry**.

### A. The 4 Petroliferous Basins (Spanning 14 Districts over 1.5 Lakh sq km)

1. **Barmer-Sanchore Basin (*बाड़मेर-सांचौर बेसिन*)**:
   - **Districts**: Barmer, Balotra, Sanchore, and Jalore.
   - **The Mangala Discovery**: On **January 2004**, Cairn Energy (now Vedanta Cairn Oil & Gas) struck commercial crude oil in the **Mangala Field** at Baytu (Barmer).
   - **Commercial Production Inauguration**: Commenced on **29 August 2009**.
   - **Major Oil Fields**: **Mangala, Bhagyam, Aishwarya, Saraswati, Raageshwari** (deep gas), Shakti, Vijaya, Vandana, Guda, Kaameshwari.
2. **Jaisalmer Basin (*जैसलमेर बेसिन*)**:
   - Exclusively situated in Jaisalmer district.
   - Dominated by rich commercial deposits of **Natural Gas**: **Tanot, Dandewala, Gamnewala, Manhera Tibba, Shahgarh, and Chinnewala Tibba**.
3. **Bikaner-Nagaur Basin (*बीकानेर-नागौर बेसिन*)**:
   - Districts: Bikaner, Nagaur, Churu, Sriganganagar, Hanumangarh.
   - Known for heavy crude oil; **Baghewala, Tawariwala**, and ONGC's **Poonam** field.
4. **Rajasthan Shelf / Vindhyan Basin (*विंध्यन बेसिन*)**:
   - Districts: Kota, Bundi, Baran, Jhalawar, part of Bhilwara and Chittorgarh.

---

### B. Pachpadra Oil Refinery & Petrochemical Complex (HRRL)

  - Integrated with a world-scale Petrochemical Fluidized Catalytic Cracker (PFCC) to produce high-value polymers (Polypropylene, Polyethylene) to spawn an adjacent **Petroleum, Chemicals & Petrochemicals Investment Region (PCPIR)**.

---

### C. Lignite Coal Architecture (*लिग्नाइट कोयला — भूरा कोयला*)
- Rajasthan ranks **2nd in India in lignite coal reserves** (after Tamil Nadu Neyveli).
- Formed during the Tertiary Eocene epoch in shallow near-shore lagoons.
- **Major Mining Fields**:
  1. **Bikaner district**: **Palana (*पलाना*)** (oldest deposit, recognized for high calorific value), **Barsingsar** (NLC thermal power project), Gurha, Bithnok.
  2. **Barmer / Balotra district**: **Giral (*गिरल*)** (first lignite-based power plant in Rajasthan with atmospheric fluidized bed combustion), **Kapurdi, Jalipa, Bhadresh**.
  3. **Nagaur / Didwana district**: **Merta Road, Kasnau-Matasukh, Mokala**.

### A. Solar Energy (*सौर ऊर्जा*)
- **Insolation Advantages**: Rajasthan receives over **325 cloudless sunny days per year** with intense solar radiation ranging between **$5.7\text{ and }6.0\text{ kWh/m}^2/\text{day}$**.
- **Solar Potential**: Assessed by the Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (MNRE) at **142 GW (1,42,000 MW)** — the highest in India.
- **Bhadla Solar Park (*भड़ला सोलर पार्क — फलोदी*)**:
  - Located at Bhadla, Phalodi district (formerly Jodhpur).
  - Total Installed Capacity: **2,245 MW** developed systematically across 4 distinct phases.
  - Global Eminence: The **world's largest contiguous operational solar park** by footprint and generation capacity.
- **Other Major Mega Solar Parks**:
  - **Fatehgarh Solar Park** (Jaisalmer) — 1,500 MW.
  - **Nokh Solar Park** (Jaisalmer) — 925 MW (developed by NTPC).

- **Statutory Milestones & Pioneer Landmarks**:
  - *Rajasthan Solar Energy Policy 2019* (set target of 30,000 MW).

  - First Solar Power Project of Rajasthan: **Dhundhar (Mathania, Jodhpur)**.

### C. Institutional Mechanism: RRECL
- **Rajasthan Renewable Energy Corporation Limited (RRECL)**:
  - Formed in **August 2002** under the *Companies Act, 1956*, by amalgamating:
    1. *Rajasthan Energy Development Agency (REDA)*, established in 1985.
    2. *Rajasthan State Power Corporation Limited (RSPCL)*, established in 1995.
  - Functions as the single-window nodal authority for renewable project clearances, land allocation, and green energy corridors.
