Replaced with direct decimal/fractional comparison and common-denominator scaling applied strictly to un-divided factor numerators. Proved the flaw using the counterexample ($x^2 - 9x + 20 = 0 \implies x \in \{4, 5\}$ vs $10y^2 - 130y + 420 = 0 \implies y \in \{6, 7\}$, where true relation is $x < y$, but false root-scaling produced $X \in \{40, 50\}$ vs $Y \in \{6, 7\}$, yielding false $X > Y$).

   * **SI Debt Installment Boundary Warning (P1)**: Clarified that $A$ is the future debt due at maturity (year $n$), not money borrowed today.
