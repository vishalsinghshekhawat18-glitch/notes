## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Context

The initial draft manuscript of `QUANTITATIVE_APTITUDE_MASTER_BOOK.md` was subjected to an exhaustive forensic audit across mathematical accuracy, exam syllabus alignment, pedagogical utility, and typographical integrity.

### Summary of Audit Findings
1. **Mathematical Defects Remedied**:
   * **BODMAS / Precedence Flaw (P0)**: The draft had falsely claimed that division precedes multiplication and labeled $20 \div 5 \times 2 = 8$ as a "fatal exam error". This was completely corrected: multiplication and division share equal precedence (left-to-right), and "of" was properly demarcated as an Indian competitive exam grouping convention ($A \div B \text{ of } C = A \div (B \times C)$).
   * **Modulo 100 Power Flaw (P0)**: The claim that $76 \times 2^k$ ends in the same two digits as $2^k$ was corrected to enforce the strict mathematical condition $k \ge 2$, with the counterexample $k=1$ ($76 \times 2 = 152 \equiv 52 \neq 02$) explicitly highlighted.
   * **Quadratic Sign Table Invariance (P1)**: The claim that negative constant terms in both equations guarantee a "Cannot Be Determined (CND)" relationship was given its rigorous mathematical proof via root sign straddling around zero, with clear domain boundaries.
2. **Curricular Gaps Filled (P1)**:
   * **Number Series & Wrong Number Series**: Completely absent in the draft; fully designed and integrated as dedicated chapters with 12 recurring algebraic/difference patterns and fast recognition heuristics.
   * **Safe Bounded Approximation**: Replaced vague "aggressive rounding" with a 4-step disciplined bounding engine (option spread evaluation, directional shift tracking, and error threshold alerts).
   * **High-Yield Data Interpretation**: Upgraded from elementary single-chart examples to a comprehensive 10-archetype bank including Missing Data Tables, Overlapping Venn Caselets, and Arithmetic DI (Work, Motion, Profit DI).
   * **Data Sufficiency & Quantity Comparison**: Replaced the faulty "Never Solve" slogan with "Do not compute more than necessary to determine sufficiency", added the Value vs. Yes/No decision tree, and created dedicated training for 3-quantity comparisons ($Q_1, Q_2, Q_3$).
3. **Target Calibration & De-Bloating (P1 / P2)**:
   * **Exam Attribution Correction**: Eliminated all false attributions to "RBI Grade B Phase II" (which has no Quant section) and recalibrated strictly to Tier 1 Banking and Regulatory Phase I.
   * **Stripping Pseudo-Rigor & Cognitive Bloat**: Pruned grandiose phrases ("Axiomatic Ontology", "100% Certified", "Unified Field Theory", "Sovereign Mathematical Law") and shifted low-frequency pure math (Wilson's theorem, primitive roots, derangements, coordinate conics) into an Optional Advanced Appendix.
