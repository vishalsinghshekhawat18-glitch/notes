## 1. Audit Severity Classification Standards

* **P0 — Critical (Fatal Defect)**: Mathematical falsehood, invalid shortcut, false theorem, incorrect example calculation, inverted exam statement, or critical flaw that directly causes examination score loss or fundamental misunderstanding.
* **P1 — High (Structural / Exam Alignment Defect)**: Missing high-frequency banking topic, miscalibrated examination target (e.g. CAT/Olympiad distortion), misleading heuristic without boundary conditions, internal narrative-arithmetic contradiction, or unaligned regression assertions.
* **P2 — Moderate (Pedagogical / Efficiency Defect)**: Excessive abstract derivation, redundant duplication of concepts across chapters, weak worked examples, awkward non-instructional numbers, or outdated exam cycle citations.
* **P3 — Minor (Editorial / Formatting Defect)**: Typographical error, inconsistent variable naming, unescaped formatting, non-standard notation, or heading offset.
