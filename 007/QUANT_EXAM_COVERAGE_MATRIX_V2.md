## 3. Prelims vs. Mains Exam Strategy

### Prelims 20-Minute Tactical Allocation (35 Questions)
* **Phase 1: Speed Burst (Minutes 0–5)**:
  * Simplification & Approximation (5 Qs)
  * Quadratic Comparisons (5 Qs)
  * Target: 8–10 questions solved with $100\%$ accuracy.
* **Phase 2: Data Interpretation (Minutes 5–12)**:
  * 1 to 2 standard DI sets (Table, Bar, or Single Pie) (10 Qs)
  * Target: 8–9 questions solved.
* **Phase 3: Number Series (Minutes 12–15)**:
  * 5 questions (Missing or Wrong Series).
  * Strict rule: Skip stubborn series after 40 seconds.
* **Phase 4: Targeted Arithmetic (Minutes 15–20)**:
  * Pick 5–7 arithmetic word problems in high-comfort domains (Partnership, Ages, Percentages, Work, TSD).

### Mains 45-Minute Tactical Allocation (30–35 Questions / 50–60 Marks)
* **Phase 1: Selection & Triage (Minutes 0–3)**: Survey paper, identify 2 approachable DI sets, 1 Caselet, and Data Sufficiency sets.
* **Phase 2: High-Value Sets (Minutes 3–25)**: Execute 2 complete DI sets (10 questions, ~15–20 marks).
* **Phase 3: Analytical Word Problems & Quantity Comparison (Minutes 25–40)**: Solve Quantity Comparisons ($Q_1, Q_2, Q_3$), Data Sufficiency, and selected Arithmetic DI sets.
* **Phase 4: Buffer / Verification (Minutes 40–45)**: Verify edge cases, units, and boundary conditions.
