## 1. Audit Severity Classification Standards

* **P0 — Critical (Fatal Defect)**: Mathematical falsehood, invalid shortcut, false theorem, incorrect example calculation, or critical flaw that directly causes examination score loss.
* **P1 — High (Structural / Exam Alignment Defect)**: Missing high-frequency banking topic, miscalibrated examination target (e.g. CAT/Olympiad distortion or RBI Phase II misattribution), misleading heuristic without boundary conditions.
* **P2 — Moderate (Pedagogical / Efficiency Defect)**: Excessive abstract derivation, redundant duplication of concepts across chapters, weak worked examples, awkward non-instructional numbers.
* **P3 — Minor (Editorial / Formatting Defect)**: Typographical error, inconsistent variable naming, unescaped formatting, non-standard notation.
