## 1. Provenance Classification Taxonomy

To ensure absolute epistemic honesty and prevent the common coaching trap of presenting invented questions as "actual previous year questions", every question archetype in this treatise adheres to one of five explicit tiers:

1. **`[VERIFIED PYQ]`**: Exact examination, year, stage, and shift verified against official question papers or institutional releases.
2. **`[MEMORY-BASED PYQ]`**: Credible memory-based reconstruction corroborated across multiple independent primary candidate recalls immediately post-exam.
3. **`[PYQ-DERIVED]`**: Created by adapting numbers or narrative context from a verified recurring exam archetype while preserving the exact underlying mathematical structure.
4. **`[EXAM-STYLE]`**: Original problem engineered specifically to test the edge-case limits, difficulty, and pacing of contemporary Tier 1 examination patterns.
5. **`[ILLUSTRATIVE]`**: Pedagogical demonstration example used strictly to introduce a mathematical principle, formula, or proof mechanism.
