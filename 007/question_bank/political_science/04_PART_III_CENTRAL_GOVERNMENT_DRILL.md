## FORENSIC TRAP DECONSTRUCTION: High-Yield Insights

1. **Trap on Q.1 (Presidential Vacancy Time Limit)**:  
   *The Trap*: Thinking the vacancy must be filled within 9 months.  
   *The Reality*: Under Article 62(2), an election to fill a vacancy occurring by death, resignation, or removal must be held within **six (6) months**, NOT nine months!

2. **Trap on Q.4 (Governor's Commutation of Death Sentence)**:  
   *The Trap*: Believing Governors have zero powers regarding death sentences.  
   *The Reality*: Under Article 161, a Governor **CAN suspend, remit, or commute** a death sentence! The only power the Governor lacks is the power to grant a **full PARDON** to a death row convict (which is the exclusive prerogative of the President). Statement 2 is false!

3. **Trap on Q.5 (Disqualification Decision Advice Monopoly)**:  
   *The Trap*: Assuming the President acts on the advice of the Prime Minister/Cabinet when disqualifying an MP.  
   *The Reality*: Under **Article 103**, the President must obtain the opinion of the **Election Commission of India** and MUST ACT according to such opinion. The Union Cabinet has zero role!

4. **Trap on Q.9 (Cabinet Committee on Parliamentary Affairs Chair)**:  
   *The Trap*: Candidates assume the Prime Minister chairs all key Cabinet Committees.  
   *The Reality*: The PM chairs Appointments, Economic Affairs, Political Affairs, and Security. The **Cabinet Committee on Parliamentary Affairs is chaired by the Minister of Defence** (or a senior Cabinet Minister, e.g. Home Minister), NOT the Prime Minister!

5. **Trap on Q.11 (Money Bill Exclusions under Article 110(2))**:  
   *The Trap*: Assuming imposition of municipal fines is part of a Money Bill.  
   *The Reality*: Article 110(2) explicitly provides that a bill shall NOT be deemed to be a Money Bill by reason only that it provides for the **imposition of fines or pecuniary penalties, or for the fees for licenses or local taxes**!

6. **Trap on Q.13 (PAC Disallowance Power)**:  
   *The Trap*: Thinking PAC can disallow or veto government expenditure.  
   *The Reality*: The PAC is a post-mortem scrutiny committee. It has **NO executive power to disallow or stop expenditure**; it only reports irregularities to Parliament!
