## FORENSIC TRAP DECONSTRUCTION: High-Yield Insights

1. **Trap on Q.1 (Co-operative Societies: Parliament vs. State Legislature)**:  
   *The Trap*: Thinking Parliament regulates state co-operative societies.  
   *The Reality*: "Co-operative societies" is an exclusive **State subject (Entry 32 of List II)**! Under Article 243-ZI, the **State Legislature** enacts laws for incorporation, regulation, and winding up. Furthermore, Article 243-ZJ caps the maximum number of directors at **twenty-one (21)**, NOT 25!

2. **Trap on Q.2 (State Official Language: State vs. Parliament)**:  
   *The Trap*: Assuming Parliament decides the official language of a State.  
   *The Reality*: Under **Article 345**, the **Legislature of a State** by law adopts any one or more languages in use in the State or Hindi as its official language, NOT Parliament!

3. **Trap on Q.8 (Nominated MP Defection 6-Month Window)**:  
   *The Trap*: Thinking nominated members can never join a political party.  
   *The Reality*: A nominated MP has a **six-month grace period** from the date of taking their seat to join any political party without disqualification! Disqualification triggers ONLY if they join *after* six months!

4. **Trap on Q.9 (The 1/3rd Split Repeal)**:  
   *The Trap*: Candidates still pick "one-third split" based on old pre-2003 questions.  
   *The Reality*: The **91st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003 deleted Paragraph 3** (the 1/3rd split defense). Today, ONLY a **two-thirds (2/3rd) merger** protects members from defection!
