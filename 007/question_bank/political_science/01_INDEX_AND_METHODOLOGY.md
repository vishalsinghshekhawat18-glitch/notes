## 2. The 3-Step Active Recall Solving Protocol

When working through these diagnostic modules:
1. **Blind Solve**: Attempt the questions section by section without looking at the answer keys.
2. **Key Audit**: Cross-verify against the official letter answers provided at the end of each topic.
3. **Trap Autopsy**: For every missed question or lucky guess, read the **Forensic Trap Deconstruction** to identify the precise misleading keyword (*"all" vs. "some"*, *"President" vs. "Parliament"*, *"shall" vs. "may"*, *"Chief Justice" vs. "President"*).
