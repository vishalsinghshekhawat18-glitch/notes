## FORENSIC TRAP DECONSTRUCTION: High-Yield Insights

1. **Trap on Q.1 (Regional Election Commissioner Removal)**:  
   *The Trap*: Candidates assume the Governor of the concerned State must recommend removal.  
   *The Reality*: Under **Article 324(5) second proviso**, Regional Commissioners and other Election Commissioners cannot be removed except on the recommendation of the **Chief Election Commissioner**, NOT the Governor!

2. **Trap on Q.4 (CAG Post-Retirement Disqualification)**:  
   *The Trap*: Thinking the CAG can hold another government chairmanship after retirement.  
   *The Reality*: Under **Article 148(4)**, the CAG is **completely disqualified from holding any further office**, whether under the Government of India or under the Government of any State!

3. **Trap on Q.6 (Attorney General Voting Right)**:  
   *The Trap*: Assuming the Attorney General can vote in Parliament.  
   *The Reality*: Under **Article 88**, the Attorney General has the right to speak and take part in proceedings, but is **NOT entitled to vote** under any circumstances!

4. **Trap on Q.8 (CVC Unique Tenure)**:  
   *The Trap*: Picking 5 years or 6 years based on UPSC/ECI conventions.  
   *The Reality*: The CVC has a **unique 4-year tenure** (or age 65), unlike the 6-year term for UPSC/CAG/ECI or the 3-year term for NHRC!

5. **Trap on Q.9 (CBI Statutory Status)**:  
   *The Trap*: Thinking CBI is a statutory body created by an Act of Parliament.  
   *The Reality*: CBI was established by an executive resolution of the Ministry of Home Affairs in 1963 and derives its investigating powers from the **Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946**. It is NOT a statutory body in itself!
