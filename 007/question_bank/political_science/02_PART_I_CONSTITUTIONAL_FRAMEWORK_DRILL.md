## FORENSIC TRAP DECONSTRUCTION: High-Yield Insights

1. **Trap on Q.2 (1919 vs. 1909 Communal Electorates)**:  
   *The Trap*: Candidates pick option (a) thinking separate communal electorates for Muslims were introduced in 1919.  
   *The Reality*: Muslim electorates were introduced in **1909 (Morley-Minto)**. The 1919 Act merely *extended* them to Sikhs, Anglo-Indians, and Christians. Statement 2 is false!

2. **Trap on Q.6 (Drafting Committee & Constituents)**:  
   *The Trap*: Thinking the Drafting Committee had 8 members.  
   *The Reality*: The Drafting Committee had **seven (7) members**, not eight. Furthermore, representatives were elected from **three** categories (Muslims, Sikhs, General), NOT four. Only statement 3 (strength 389) is correct!

3. **Trap on Q.7 (Provisions in force on 26 Nov 1949)**:  
   *The Trap*: Assuming Emergency provisions took effect immediately.  
   *The Reality*: Under Article 394, only Articles 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (Citizenship), 60, 324 (Elections), 366, 367, 379, 380, 388, 391, 392, and 393 came into force immediately. Emergency provisions (Part XVIII) came into force on **January 26, 1950**!

4. **Trap on Q.10 (Second Schedule Exclusions)**:  
   *The Trap*: Confusing constitutional bodies with dignitary salaries.  
   *The Reality*: The Second Schedule covers President, Governors, Speakers, Judges, and CAG. The **UPSC Chairman is NOT included** in the Second Schedule!

5. **Trap on Q.22 (Article 169 Legislative Council vs. State Ratification)**:  
   *The Trap*: Assuming creating/abolishing a Legislative Council requires state ratification.  
   *The Reality*: Article 169(3) explicitly declares that such a law is **NOT deemed to be an amendment under Article 368**. It requires only a Simple Majority in Parliament without state ratification!
