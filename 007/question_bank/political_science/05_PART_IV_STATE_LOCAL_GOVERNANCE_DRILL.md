## FORENSIC TRAP DECONSTRUCTION: High-Yield Insights

1. **Trap on Q.1 (UT Administrator Appointment)**:  
   *The Trap*: Thinking Parliament appoints the Governor as UT Administrator.  
   *The Reality*: Under **Article 239(2)**, the **PRESIDENT** appoints the Governor of a State as Administrator of an adjoining UT. Statement 1 is false!

2. **Trap on Q.2 (Adjournment vs. Prorogation)**:  
   *The Trap*: Believing the Governor can adjourn the Assembly.  
   *The Reality*: Adjournment of sittings is the exclusive power of the **Speaker / Presiding Officer**. The Governor can only summon, prorogue, and dissolve the House!

3. **Trap on Q.5 & Q.9 (High Court Judge Appointment & Removal)**:  
   *The Trap*: Candidates assume High Court Judges are appointed or removed by the Governor.  
   *The Reality*: High Court Judges are appointed by the **PRESIDENT** (Article 217) and removed ONLY by the **PRESIDENT on an address by Parliament** (Article 217(1) proviso (b)). The Governor has zero power to appoint or remove them!

4. **Trap on Q.7 (No Joint Sitting in States)**:  
   *The Trap*: Searching for a State joint sitting article.  
   *The Reality*: The Indian Constitution provides for a Joint Sitting **ONLY for Parliament (Article 108)**. In States, if the Council disagrees, the Legislative Assembly's will prevails automatically after 4 months!

5. **Trap on Q.9 (HC Judge Pension Charging)**:  
   *The Trap*: Assuming HC Judge pensions come from the State treasury.  
   *The Reality*: The **salary** of a High Court Judge is charged on the Consolidated Fund of the **State**, but the **pension** is charged on the Consolidated Fund of **India** (Article 112(3)(d)(iii))!
