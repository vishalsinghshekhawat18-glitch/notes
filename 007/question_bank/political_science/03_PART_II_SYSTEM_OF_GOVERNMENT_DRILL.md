## FORENSIC TRAP DECONSTRUCTION: High-Yield Insights

1. **Trap on Q.2 (No-Confidence Motion in the Constitution)**:  
   *The Trap*: Candidates assume the Constitution mentions "No-Confidence Motion".  
   *The Reality*: The phrase "No-Confidence Motion" is **NOWHERE mentioned in the Constitution**! The Constitution only states in Article 75(3) that the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha. The procedure for a No-Confidence Motion is governed entirely by **Rule 198 of the Rules of Procedure and Conduct of Business in Lok Sabha**.

2. **Trap on Q.4 (Federal Principles absent in India)**:  
   *The Trap*: Thinking India has separate federal and state courts.  
   *The Reality*: India has an **integrated, single judicial hierarchy** (unlike the US dual court system). Also, Rajya Sabha does not have equal representation of States (unlike the US Senate). Thus, principles 1 and 2 are absent in India!

3. **Trap on Q.13 (Zonal Councils vs. Inter-State Council)**:  
   *The Trap*: Candidates confuse Zonal Councils with the constitutional Inter-State Council.  
   *The Reality*: Zonal Councils are **STATUTORY bodies** created under the *States Reorganisation Act, 1956* (and the North-Eastern Council Act, 1971). They are NOT constitutional bodies, and their recommendations are purely advisory!

4. **Trap on Q.15 & Q.17 (Emergency Approval Windows)**:  
   *The Trap*: Believing all emergencies require approval within 2 months.  
   *The Reality*:  
   - National Emergency (Art 352): **ONE MONTH** (reduced from 2 months by 44th AA).  
   - President's Rule (Art 356): **TWO MONTHS**.  
   - Financial Emergency (Art 360): **TWO MONTHS**.

5. **Trap on Q.16 (Article 358 vs. 359 during Armed Rebellion)**:  
   *The Trap*: Assuming Article 19 is suspended during any National Emergency.  
   *The Reality*: Under the 44th Amendment Act (1978), Article 358 suspends Article 19 **ONLY when the emergency is declared on grounds of War or External Aggression**, NEVER for Armed Rebellion!
