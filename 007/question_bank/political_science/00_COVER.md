## The Epistemic Charter of the Diagnostic Engine

This Question Bank is the **empirical laboratory** of Shelf 007's Political Science curriculum. Mastery of theoretical doctrines and Bare Act articles is tested against **examiner trap vectors**, tricky statutory exceptions, and multi-statement eliminations.

### The Four Pillars of the Diagnostic Engine:
1. **Multi-Statement Elimination Traps**: Questions designed to penalize superficial knowledge of articles by testing hidden sub-clauses, non-obstante provisions, and statutory caveats.
2. **Assertion & Reasoning (A & R) Rigor**: Testing true causal mechanics—confirming whether Reason (R) is merely a true statement or the genuine, legally determinative explanation of Assertion (A).
3. **Negative Exception Radar**: Inoculating against deceptive stems (*"Which of the following does NOT..."*, *"Except which of the following..."*).
4. **Forensic Trap Deconstruction**: Every drill module provides the **official letter answer key** paired with an **Examiner Trap Analysis** explaining why deceptive distractor choices lure candidates into error.
