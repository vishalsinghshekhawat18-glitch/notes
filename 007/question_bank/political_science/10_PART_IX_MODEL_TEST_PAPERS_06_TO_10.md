## MODEL TEST PAPER – 6 & 7 (SELECTED HIGH-YIELD DRILLS)

### Question 6.1 (Article 19 Restrictions Scope)
The right to ‘freedom of speech and expression’ under Article 19(1)(a) is restricted under Article 19(2) on which of the following grounds?
1. Sovereignty and integrity of India
2. Contempt of court
3. Friendly relations with foreign States
4. Protection of minorities
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:
(a) 1, 2 and 3 only  
(b) 2, 3 and 4 only  
(c) 1 and 3 only  
(d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

### Question 6.2 (Anticipatory Emergency Imposition)
Consider the following statements:
1. National Emergency is always imposed throughout the entire nation.
2. Emergency can be imposed not only on the grounds of actual war, external aggression, or armed rebellion, but also in anticipation thereof.
3. Every proclamation of Emergency will cease to operate after one month unless approved by both Houses of Parliament.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only  
(b) 1 and 2 only  
(c) Only 3  
(d) 1, 2 and 3

### Question 6.3 (Joint Sitting Convening vs. Presiding)
Which of the following statements is correct regarding a Joint Session of Parliament?
1. It is convened by the Speaker.
2. It is presided over by the senior-most member of Parliament.
3. It is summoned/convened by the President (Article 108).
Select the correct answer:
(a) 1 only  
(b) 2 only  
(c) 3 only  
(d) None

### Question 6.4 (Attorney General Qualifications)
Consider the following statements with respect to the Attorney-General of India:
1. He is appointed by the President.
2. He must have the same qualifications as are required for a Judge of a High Court.
3. In the performance of his duties, he shall have the right of audience in all courts of India.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  
(b) 1 and 3 only  
(c) 2 and 3 only  
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## FORENSIC TRAP DECONSTRUCTION: Model Tests 6 to 10

1. **Trap on Q.6.1 (Article 19(2) Grounds vs. Minority Protection)**:  
   *The Trap*: Thinking "protection of minorities" is a ground for restricting free speech under Article 19(2).  
   *The Reality*: Article 19(2) contains **eight specific grounds**: Sovereignty and integrity of India, security of the State, friendly relations with foreign States, public order, decency or morality, contempt of court, defamation, and incitement to an offence. **"Protection of minorities" is NOT a ground under Article 19(2)**!

2. **Trap on Q.6.2 (Anticipatory Emergency & Geographic Scope)**:  
   *The Trap*: Believing emergency can only be proclaimed after war has broken out, or that it must apply to the entire country.  
   *The Reality*: Under the 42nd Amendment, the President can declare a National Emergency for the **whole of India OR any part thereof**! And under the explanation to Article 352, it can be declared **in anticipation** before actual occurrence of war or aggression!

3. **Trap on Q.6.4 (Attorney General Qualifications)**:  
   *The Trap*: Assuming the Attorney General must be qualified to be a High Court Judge.  
   *The Reality*: Under **Article 76(1)**, the Attorney General must be qualified to be appointed as a **Judge of the Supreme Court**, NOT a High Court Judge! (The Advocate General must be qualified as a High Court Judge under Article 165).
