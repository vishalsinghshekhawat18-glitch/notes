## Known catalog observations

- The official search index reports a RAS Prelims 2013 paper and a separately labelled version for blind candidates (exam date 26 October 2013).
- The official index reports the 2016 Prelims paper and a final answer key released 16 September 2016.
- RPSC lists two near-duplicate titles for the 2018 Prelims key and multiple near-duplicate listings for 2018 Mains papers. The archive does not expose enough information here to decide whether these are separate language versions or duplicate rows. They remain flagged for binary comparison.
- Six regular Mains cycles are identified in the official archive: 2013, 2016, 2018, 2021, 2023, 2024. Each has Papers I–IV.
- The 2016 Special Mains papers were held 17–18 December 2018 and listed 22 July 2019; keep them separate from regular 2016 Mains.
- RPSC annual report 2012–13 verifies a RAS/RTS Prelims exam on 14 June 2012 and Physics re-exam on 23 August 2012, but its paper is not located in the current indexed paper catalog reviewed.

## Count interpretation

- Core official paper slots listed: **34** (6 core Prelims cycles + 24 regular Mains papers + 4 special Mains papers).
- Additional official listing variants: **2013 blind-candidate Prelims version** and **“RAS Pre 2013” paper dated 31 October 2015**. The latter is kept separate pending verification of its administrative relationship to the 2013 cycle and the relevant answer key.
