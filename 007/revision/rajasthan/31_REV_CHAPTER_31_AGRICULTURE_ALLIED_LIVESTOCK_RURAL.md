## 3. High-Yield RPSC Traps & Elimination Rules

```
                      20th LIVESTOCK CENSUS CONTRADICTIONS
                                       │
            ┌──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┐
            │                                                     │

  ├── Cattle (+4.41%)                                   ├── Camels (-34.69% - Alarming!)
  ├── Buffaloes (+5.53%)                                ├── Donkeys (-71.31% - Steepest!)
  └── Poultry (+82.23% - Massive Explosion!)           └── Sheep (-12.95%)
```

1. **Livestock GVA vs. Crop GVA**:
   - At current prices, **Livestock (49.35%)** generates MORE value than **Crops (42.61%)**!
   - In GVO of livestock products: **Milk is 79.60%**.

2. **Landholding Classification Traps**:
   - Marginal (<1 ha): **40.12%**.
   - Small (1–2 ha): **21.90%**.
   - Marginal + Small combined: **62.02%**.
   - Average size: **2.73 Hectares** (Higher than India's 1.08 ha due to western desert tracts).

3. **Specialized Mandi Locations**:
   - Coriander: **Ramganj Mandi (Kota)** (Asia's largest).
   - Cumin: **Merta City (Nagaur) & Jodhpur**.
   - Garlic: **Chhipabarod (Baran)**.
   - Orange: **Bhawani Mandi (Jhalawar)**.
   - Isabgol: **Bhinmal (Jalore)**.
   - Mehandi: **Sojat (Pali)**.

4. **Farmer Welfare Grant Numbers**:
   - **Kisan Samman Nidhi**: ₹6,000 (Central) + **₹2,000 (State)** = **₹8,000/year**.
   - **Gopal Credit Card**: Up to **₹1 Lakh** interest-free short-term loan.
   - **Kamdhenu Bima**: Up to **₹40,000 per animal** (max 2 milch animals).
   - **Dugdh Sambal**: **₹5 per litre** subsidy.

5. **MGNREGS Days in Rajasthan**:
   - Standard: **125 Days** (100 Central + 25 State-funded).
   - Sahariya, Kathodi & Divyangjans: **200 Days**.

6. **Mahila Nidhi Foundation Date**:
   - **26 August 2022** (Women's Equality Day), modeled on Telangana's *Stree Nidhi*.

## 4. Rapid-Recall Mnemonics

- **National Rank 1 Crops**: **"B-M-O-C-G"**
  - **B** = Bajra
  - **M** = Mustard & Rapeseed
  - **O** = Total Oilseeds
  - **C** = Coarse Grains (Nutri-cereals)
  - **G** = Guar Gum & Garlic/Spices
- **Top 3 Livestock Species in Raj**: **"G-C-B"**
  - **G** = Goats (20.84M - Rank #1)
  - **C** = Cattle (13.90M - Rank #6)
  - **B** = Buffaloes (13.70M - Rank #2)
- **Agro-Climatic Extremes**:
  - Largest = **I-C** (Bikaner, Jaisalmer, Churu)
  - Smallest = **IV-B** (Banswara, Dungarpur, Pratapgarh)
