1. **Critical Rural Employment Law Repeal (1 July 2026)**: MGNREGA, 2005 was **REPEALED and REPLACED** by the **Viksit Bharat—Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 (VB–G RAM G Act, 2025)** on 1 July 2026. The 125 days of wage employment is now a **national statutory guarantee**; do not teach the obsolete "100 central + 25 state" formula!
2. **Special Category 200-Day Entitlement Status**: The earlier 200-day entitlement for Sahariya, Kathodi, and Divyangjans was linked to the old scheme; it is not contained as a general guarantee in the new central Act. Treat as historical unless a current state order is authenticated.
3. **Chief Minister Kisan Samman Nidhi Quantum**: In the 2025–26 Budget, state assistance was increased from ₹2,000 to **₹3,000 per year** (paid in instalments, e.g. fourth instalment: ₹1,000 to 71.79 lakh farmers). Total farmer support is **₹9,000/year** (₹6,000 PM-KISAN + ₹3,000 state).
4. **Headline Animal Insurance Branding**: The operative flagship scheme in Budget 2026–27 is **Mukhyamantri Mangla Pashu Bima Yojana** (42 lakh animals target; ₹100 crore allocation). Do not use the older "Kamdhenu Bima" title as the headline scheme.
5. **Crop Production Rank 1 Details (2023–24)**: Rajasthan is Rank 1 in **Rapeseed-Mustard (43.43%)**, **Bajra (41.34%)**, **Total Oilseeds (23.61%)**, **Coarse Cereals (14.21%)**, and **Guar (88.80%)**.
6. **"Nutri-Cereals" vs "Coarse Cereals" Trap**: Official tables confirm Rajasthan as Rank 1 in **Coarse Cereals** (14.21%), but NOT first in nutri-cereals as a blanket category. Never use them interchangeably!
7. **Rank 2 & Rank 3 Crops**: Rank 2 in **Groundnut (19.91%)**; Rank 3 in **Gram (17.39%)**, **Total Pulses (13.76%)**, and **Soybean (8.96%)**.
8. **Barren & Unculturable Land Percentage**: Correct official proportion in 2024–25 provisional land use is **6.85%** (~23.53 lakh ha), NOT 6.55%.
9. **Net Sown Area Proportion**: Net sown area is **53.02%** (182.09 lakh ha), representing more than half of Rajasthan's reporting area.
10. **Livestock Census Authority**: The Livestock Census is a **quinquennial national enumeration conducted by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD), Government of India**, executed via state machinery. It is NOT conducted by the Board of Revenue, Ajmer!
11. **Livestock Census Vintage**: The latest completed livestock census cited in state accounts is the **20th Livestock Census, 2019** (56.8 million total livestock; Rank #2 in India).
12. **Livestock GVA vs Crop GVA**: In Rajasthan's agrarian economy, **Livestock GVA (~₹2.17 lakh crore) EXCEEDS the crop sector GVA**. Milk alone accounts for **79.60%** of livestock-product GVO.
13. **Agriculture Census Vintage**: Operational holding figures (76.55 lakh holdings, 2.73 ha average size) are from **Agriculture Census 2015–16**, not a 2026 enumeration.
14. **Operational Holding vs Legal Ownership**: An "operational holding" is an economic unit operated by a cultivator; female operational holding (7.75 lakh) does NOT automatically imply legal land ownership/title.
15. **Agro-Climatic Zones Extremes**:
    - **Largest Zone by Area**: Zone I-C (Hyper-Arid Partially Irrigated Western Plain: Bikaner, Jaisalmer, Churu parts).
    - **Smallest Zone by Area**: Zone IV-B (Humid Southern Plain: Banswara, Dungarpur, Pratapgarh, Salumber).
16. **Gopal Credit Card Scheme Limits**: Provides interest-free short-term loans up to **₹1 Lakh** for dairy activities (target: 2.5 lakh families; 2026–27 outcome target: 1 lakh gopalaks, ₹80 crore).
17. **Separate Agriculture Budget Inception**: Rajasthan introduced a dedicated Agriculture Budget for the first time in **2022–23** ("Samriddh Kisan–Khushhal Rajasthan").
18. **Cross-Sector Budget Provision Trap**: Budget Study 2026–27 shows ₹1,19,408.11 crore for "Agriculture, Farmer Welfare & Allied Activities" (with ₹69,422.99 Cr state component). This is a broad cross-sector category, NOT the standalone budget or expenditure of the Agriculture Department!
19. **Rajasthan Mahila Nidhi Launch**: Formally inaugurated on **26 August 2022** (Women's Equality Day) under Rajivika. Specific turnaround times (e.g. "48 hours") are operational product targets, not statutory rights.
20. **e-NAM Mandi Integration Count**: Official portal table records **173 integrated mandis** in Rajasthan (dynamic portal snapshot).
21. **Micro-Irrigation Subsidy Tiers**: Under RajKisan guidelines, drip irrigation assistance provides **70% subsidy for small/marginal farmers** and **50% for other farmers** (subject to category and area ceilings).
22. **Camel Population Crash**: Camels plummeted by **−34.69%** (to 0.213 million) between 2012 and 2019, despite being declared the State Animal (livestock category) in 2014.
23. **Poultry Explosion**: Poultry surged by **+82.23%** (reaching 14.65 million), but is tracked separately from the mammalian livestock aggregate.
24. **Specialized Mandi Centres**:
    - Coriander: Ramganj Mandi (Kota).
    - Oranges: Bhawani Mandi (Jhalawar).
    - Henna (Mehendi): Sojat (Pali).
    - Cumin & Isabgol: Merta City (Nagaur), Jodhpur, and Bhinmal (Jalore).
25. **Myth of "Asia's Largest" Mandis**: Avoid unsourced superlatives ("Asia's largest mandi/finest wool"); RPSC questions evaluate official commodity-district linkages and statutory rules.

## 5. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

```text
FC-01 | Q: What rural employment enactment replaced MGNREGA on 1 July 2026, and what is its statutory guarantee?
      | A: The Viksit Bharat—Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 (VB–G RAM G Act, 2025) replaced MGNREGA on 1 July 2026. It provides a statutory national guarantee of 125 days per rural household per fiscal year.

FC-02 | Q: What is the current annual financial assistance under Chief Minister Kisan Samman Nidhi in Rajasthan?
      | A: ₹3,000 per eligible farmer annually (increased from ₹2,000 in Budget 2025–26), bringing total annual farmer support to ₹9,000 (₹6,000 PM-KISAN + ₹3,000 State).

FC-03 | Q: Which crops/groups placed Rajasthan at Rank 1 in national production in the 2023–24 state review?
      | A: Rapeseed & Mustard (43.43%), Bajra (41.34%), Total Oilseeds (23.61%), Coarse Cereals (14.21%), and Guar (88.80%).

FC-04 | Q: What is Rajasthan's headline livestock insurance scheme in Budget 2026–27?
      | A: Mukhyamantri Mangla Pashu Bima Yojana (target: 42 lakh animals; budget allocation: ₹100 crore).

FC-05 | Q: What is the economic scale of the livestock sector compared to crops in Rajasthan's GSVA?
      | A: Livestock GVA at current prices is approximately ₹2.17 lakh crore, which EXCEEDS the crop sector GVA. Milk accounts for 79.60% of livestock-product GVO.

FC-06 | Q: What are the total reporting area and net sown area percentages in Rajasthan (2024–25 provisional)?
      | A: Total reporting area is 343.43 lakh hectares; Net Sown Area is 53.02% (182.09 lakh ha). Barren & unculturable land is 6.85%.

FC-07 | Q: What are the number of operational landholdings and average holding size in Rajasthan according to Agriculture Census 2015–16?
      | A: 76.55 lakh operational holdings with an average holding size of 2.73 hectares. Small and marginal holdings account for 62.02% of all holdings.

FC-08 | Q: Which agro-climatic zones represent the largest and smallest areas in Rajasthan?
      | A: Largest: Zone I-C (Hyper-Arid Partially Irrigated Western Plain). Smallest: Zone IV-B (Humid Southern Plain).

FC-09 | Q: Which agency conducts the quinquennial Livestock Census in India?
      | A: Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD), Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India (NOT the Board of Revenue, Ajmer).

      | A: Inaugurated on 26 August 2022 (Women's Equality Day) under Rajivika (Rajasthan Grameen Aajeevika Vikas Parishad) to provide credit support to women SHGs.
```
