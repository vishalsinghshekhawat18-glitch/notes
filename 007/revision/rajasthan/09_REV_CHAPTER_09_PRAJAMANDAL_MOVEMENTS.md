## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **First Prajamandal in Rajasthan**: **Jaipur Prajamandal (1931 CE)** founded by Kapur Chand Patni, NOT Mewar (1938) or Marwar (1934).
2. **Prajamandals Founded Outside Their Territories**:
   - **Bikaner (1936)**: Calcutta.
   - **Bharatpur (1938)**: Rewari (Haryana).
   - **Sirohi (1934)**: Bombay.
   - **Jaisalmer (1945)**: Jodhpur.
3. **Signatories to the Gentlemen's Agreement (1942)**: Concluded between Jaipur PM **Sir Mirza Ismail** and Prajamandal President **Hiralal Shastri**.
4. **Founder of Azad Morcha (1942)**: **Baba Harish Chandra**, Ramkaran Joshi, and Hans D. Roy, rebelling against Hiralal Shastri to join Quit India.
5. **First President of Mewar Prajamandal**: **Balwant Singh Mehta** (Manikya Lal Verma was the General Secretary).
6. **The Three "Gandhis" of Rajasthan**:
   - **Gokulbhai Bhatt**: "Gandhi of Rajasthan" (Sirohi).
   - **Bhogilal Pandya**: "Gandhi of Vagad" (Dungarpur).
   - **Master Pyarelal Gupta**: "Gandhi of Chidawa" (Jhunjhunu).
7. **First Princely State to Establish Responsible Government**: **Shahpura (14 August 1947)** under Prime Minister Gokul Lal Asawa.
8. **Martyr of Raisinghnagar (Birbal Day)**: **Birbal Singh Dhaliwal** (martyred 1 July 1946; Birbal Day observed 17 July 1946), NOT Balmukund Bissa.
9. **Rastapal Incident Roles**: **Nana Bhai Khant** was beaten to death; **Kalibai (13-year-old student)** cut the rope dragging teacher Sengabhai and was shot dead by police.
10. **First Political Newspaper in Rajasthani**: **"Aagiwan" (1932, Beawar)** edited by **Jai Narayan Vyas**.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What were the books authored by martyr Sagar Mal Gopa that exposed Jaisalmer’s princely administration?
- **A**: *Jaisalmer Ka Gundaraj*, *Azadi Ke Diwane*, and *Raghunath Singh Ka Muqadma*.

### Card 2
- **Q**: What was the primary difference between the Jaipur Prajamandal and Azad Morcha during the 1942 Quit India Movement?
- **A**: Jaipur Prajamandal signed the **Gentlemen's Agreement** boycotting Quit India; **Azad Morcha** broke away under Baba Harish Chandra to actively launch anti-British satyagraha in Jaipur.

### Card 3
- **Q**: Who was Balmukund Bissa, and where did he achieve martyrdom?
- **A**: Known as the *"Jallianwala / Bhagat Singh of Rajasthan"*, he was a Marwar activist who died on 19 June 1942 following an indefinite hunger strike in Jodhpur Central Jail.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Where was the Seventh All-India States' Peoples' Conference held in Rajasthan, and who presided?
- **A**: Held at **Salotia Ground, Udaipur** (31 Dec 1945 – 1 Jan 1946), presided over by **Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru**.

### Card 5
- **Q**: Who was the first woman in Rajasthan to be arrested and exiled during the freedom struggle?
- **A**: **Anjana Devi Chaudhary** (wife of Ramnarayan Chaudhary) in 1921.
