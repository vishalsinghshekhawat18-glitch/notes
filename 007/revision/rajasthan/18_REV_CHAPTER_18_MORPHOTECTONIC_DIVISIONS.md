## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Gondwanaland vs Tethys Sea Dual Origin**:
   - **Gondwanaland**: **Aravalli Range** and **Hadoti Plateau**.
   - **Tethys Sea**: **Western Sandy Plain** and **Eastern Alluvial Plain**.
2. **The Isohyet Boundary Numbers**:
   - **50 cm Isohyet**: Separates Western Desert from Eastern Rajasthan.
   - **25 cm Isohyet**: Bisects Western Desert into Pure Arid Marusthali (<25 cm) and Semi-Arid Bagar (25–50 cm).
3. **The Most Widespread Sand Dune**: **Parabolic Dunes** are the most widespread across all desert districts, NOT Barkhans!
4. **Dune-Free Tract (*Hamada*) Percentage**: **41.5%** of the desert is dune-free (Pokhran, Ramgarh, Phalodi); dune-covered is **58.5%**.
5. **Chhappan ka Maidan vs Chhappan ki Pahadiyan**:
   - **Chhappan ka Maidan**: Alluvial plain of 56 streams between **Banswara and Pratapgarh** (Mahi Basin).
   - **Chhappan ki Pahadiyan**: Cluster of 56 round granitic hills around **Siwana (Balotra)**.
6. **Aravalli Lengths: Total vs Rajasthan**:
   - Total length: **692 km** (Palanpur, Gujarat to Delhi).
   - Length in Rajasthan: **550 km** (nearly **80%** of total length).
7. **Highest Peaks in Three Aravalli Zones**:
   - **North Aravalli**: **Raghunathgarh (1,055 m)** in Sikar.
   - **Central Aravalli**: **Goramji (934 m)** in Beawar.
   - **South Aravalli & Overall**: **Guru Shikhar (1,722 m)** in Sirohi.
8. **Highest Plateau in Rajasthan**: **Oriya Plateau (1,360 m)** in Mount Abu, NOT Bhorat (1,225 m).
9. **Lashadiya Plateau Updated District**: Now located in **Salumbar district** (formerly Udaipur), east of Jaisamand Lake.
10. **Meteorite Crater Location**: **Ramgarh Crater** is located in **Baran district** (Shahbad Upland), declared India's 3rd recognized meteorite impact structure.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What is the Bhorat Plateau, and what geographical landmark does it represent?
- **A**: A high plateau (1,225 m) stretching between **Kumbhalgarh (Rajsamand)** and **Gogunda (Udaipur)**; represents the continental water divide between the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal drainages.

### Card 2
- **Q**: What is the "Kobar Patti" (Banka Patti), and which districts does it encompass?
- **A**: A hazardous subterranean fluoride groundwater belt between **Nagaur/Didwana and Ajmer**, causing widespread dental and skeletal fluorosis (humped backs) among locals.

### Card 3
- **Q**: What are the geological and biological features of the Lathi Series?
- **A**: A 60-km subterranean paleo-aquifer of the Saraswati river between Pokhran and Mohangarh (Jaisalmer), harboring the sweet-water **Chandan Tube Well** (*Thar ka Ghada*) and protein-rich **Sewan grasslands** (habitat of the Great Indian Bustard).

### Card 4
- **Q**: Trace the Great Boundary Fault (GBF) across Rajasthan.
- **A**: An 800-km structural reverse fault line extending from **Chittorgarh through Kota, Bundi, Sawai Madhopur, Karauli to Dholpur**, separating the Pre-Cambrian Aravallis from the Vindhyan Supergroup.

### Card 5
- **Q**: Distinguish between Bhakar and Girwa in Aravalli geomorphology.
- **A**: **Bhakar** refers to the extremely steep, sharp-edged jagged hills in **western Sirohi**; **Girwa** refers to the saucer-shaped, amphitheater-like ring of hills encircling **Udaipur city**.
