1. **Thermal Fuel Type Trap**:
   - **Suratgarh & Chhabra**: Run on **Bituminous Coal**.
   - **Giral & Barsingsar**: Run on **Lignite Coal**. (Giral is in Barmer; Barsingsar is in Bikaner).

2. **Mahi Hydropower Allocation Trap**:
   - Inter-state water share of Mahi: **55% Gujarat : 45% Rajasthan**.
   - Hydro electricity generated (140 MW): **100% allocated to RAJASTHAN**!

3. **Road Density Metric Trap**:
   - Geographic road density: **97.97 km per 100 sq km** (Much LOWER than India's ~165 km).
   - Per-capita road availability: **4.12 km per 1,000 population** (HIGHER than India's ~3.8 km due to sparse desert distances).

4. **PM-KUSUM Component A First Plant**:
   - Commissioned at **Bhaloji village (Kotputli-Behror / Jaipur)** in April 2021 (1 MW), not in Bikaner or Jodhpur.

5. **First Wind Power Project in Rajasthan**:
   - Commissioned at **Amar Sagar (Jaisalmer)** on **10 April 1999** (2 MW).
   - Devgarh (Pratapgarh) came second on 6 March 2001.

6. **Delhi-Mumbai Expressway vs. DMIC Railway Length**:
   - DMIC / WDFC Railway length in Rajasthan: **548 km (36.4%)**.
   - Delhi-Mumbai Expressway (NE-4) length in Rajasthan: **374 km** across Alwar, Dausa, Sawai Madhopur, Bundi, Kota.

## 5. Model 2-Marker & 5-Marker Rapid Answer Keys

### Q1 (2 Marks): Which districts are traversed by the Delhi-Mumbai Expressway (NE-4) in Rajasthan?
> **Answer**: The Delhi-Mumbai Expressway covers **374 km** in Rajasthan across five districts: **Alwar, Dausa, Sawai Madhopur, Bundi, and Kota** (connected to Jaipur via a dedicated spur corridor from Dausa).

### Q2 (5 Marks): Summarize the institutional structure and functions of the 5 power companies created after unbundling RSEB.
> **Answer**:
> Under the Rajasthan Power Sector Reforms Act 1999, the Rajasthan State Electricity Board (RSEB) was unbundled on **19 July 2000** into:
> 1. **RRVUNL (Generation)**: Operates state-owned thermal, hydro, and gas generation stations (Suratgarh, Kota, Chhabra, Mahi).
> 2. **RVPNL (Transmission)**: State Transmission Utility handling high-voltage grid lines (132 kV to 765 kV) and the Green Energy Corridor.
> 3. **JVVNL (Distribution - Jaipur Discom)**: Serves 12 central/eastern districts (Jaipur, Alwar, Dausa, Bharatpur, Kota, etc.).
> 4. **AVVNL (Distribution - Ajmer Discom)**: Serves central/southern districts (Ajmer, Bhilwara, Nagaur, Sikar, Udaipur, Banswara).
> 5. **JdVVNL (Distribution - Jodhpur Discom)**: Serves 10 western arid districts covering the largest geographical footprint (Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, Bikaner, Barmer, Pali).
