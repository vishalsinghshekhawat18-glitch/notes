## 3. 25 Lethal Exam Traps & Elimination Rules

```
                      RAILWAY DIVISION JURISDICTION ELIMINATION TRAP
                                             │
      ┌──────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┐
      │                                                                             │

      │                                                                             │
  ├── 1. Jaipur Division                                                        └── KOTA DIVISION!
  ├── 2. Ajmer Division                                                         *(Never mark Kota under NWR!
  ├── 3. Bikaner Division                                                         Kota is the sole Raj division
  └── 4. Jodhpur Division                                                         under Jabalpur WCR)*
```

1. **The Kota Railway Division Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Grouping Kota division under North Western Railway (NWR).
   - *Truth*: NWR (HQ Jaipur) has exactly 4 divisions: **Jaipur, Ajmer, Bikaner, and Jodhpur**. **Kota division belongs to West Central Railway (WCR), headquartered at Jabalpur (MP)**.

2. **The Kalisindh Thermal Arithmetic Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Claiming Kalisindh Thermal Power Station (1,200 MW) consists of two 660-MW supercritical units.
   - *Truth*: Kalisindh consists of **two 600-MW subcritical units ($2 \times 600 = 1,200\text{ MW}$)**. Two 660-MW units would total 1,320 MW (like Kawai or Suratgarh Units 7 & 8).

3. **The RAPP-7 Nuclear Commercial Operation Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Stating Rajasthan's operating nuclear capacity is only 1,180 MW from RAPS units 1–6.
   - *Truth*: **RAPP-7 (700 MW indigenous PHWR)** achieved commercial operation on **15 April 2025**, raising operating nuclear capacity to **1,880 MW**. RAPP-8 (700 MW) is currently under construction.

4. **The Mahi Banswara Nuclear Project Status Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Describing Mahi Banswara Nuclear Power Project as "operational" or "commissioned".
   - *Truth*: Mahi Banswara comprises **four 700-MW units (2,800 MW total)** implemented by ASHVINI (NPCIL–NTPC JV). Its **foundation stone was laid on 25 September 2025**; it is under execution, not commissioned.

5. **The Two Incompatible Power Series Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Silently blending the 30 September 2026 renewable portal figure (51.60 GW) with the Economic Review Table 7.1 series (31,556.43 MW).
   - *Truth*: The two datasets use different definitions, perimeters, and reporting dates. Always state the specific source: **31,556.43 MW** is Economic Review Table 7.1 (as of Dec 2025); **51,595.50 MW** is the state renewable investor portal (as of 30 Sept 2026).

6. **The RRECL Predecessor Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Stating RRECL was formed by unbundling RSEB in July 2000.
   - *Truth*: RSEB was unbundled into 5 companies (RVUNL, RVPNL, JVVNL, AVVNL, JdVVNL) on **19 July 2000**. **RRECL was formed later, on 9 August 2002**, by merging **REDA** and **RSPCL** (Rajasthan State Power Corporation Ltd.).

7. **The Classified Road Network vs PWD Length Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Stating Rajasthan's total road network is 1.97 lakh km.
   - *Truth*: As of March 2025 (Economic Review 2025–26), the **total classified road network is 335,306 km**. The **197,051 km** figure represents only the roads maintained by the PWD.

8. **The Road Density Metric Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Stating Rajasthan has a higher road density than India's national average.
   - *Truth*: Rajasthan's geographic road density is **97.97 km per 100 sq km** (significantly LOWER than the national average of ~165 km / 100 sq km due to the vast arid expanse of western districts).

9. **The Delhi-Mumbai Expressway Route Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Believing the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway (NE-4) cuts through the centre of Jaipur.
   - *Truth*: NE-4 traverses eastern/south-eastern Rajasthan (Alwar, Dausa, Sawai Madhopur, Bundi, Kota); Jaipur is accessed via an external spur connectivity road, not through the city centre.

10. **The Expressway vs WDFC vs DMIC Confusion Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Treating the 548-km figure as the length of the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway.
    - *Truth*: The **548-km figure refers to the freight railway / DMIC associated alignment in Rajasthan**. The Delhi–Mumbai Expressway (NE-4) is an 8-lane road highway (~374 km in Rajasthan).

11. **The RSHIP Funding Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Asserting that Rajasthan State Highway Development Project is a single joint World Bank + ADB 4,000-km loan.
    - *Truth*: The **Rajasthan State Highway Investment Program (RSHIP)** is an **ADB multi-tranche facility** (Tranche 2 ~754 km; Tranche 3 ~290 km). Avoid unverified joint-lender shorthand.

12. **The NDB Desert Canal Project Name Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Calling the NDB canal project "JICA IGNP Relining".
    - *Truth*: The project is funded by the **New Development Bank (NDB)** and officially titled **Rajasthan Water Sector Restructuring Project for the Desert Areas**. JICA funds RWSLIP.

13. **The VGF Automatic 40% Grant Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Believing VGF automatically provides a 40% grant to every infrastructure PPP project.
    - *Truth*: VGF provides **up to 40% of Total Project Cost** (typically up to 20% central + up to 20% state) only for eligible, commercially unviable projects selected through competitive bidding.

14. **The Swiss Challenge ₹50-Crore Threshold Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Claiming an entrepreneur can submit a Swiss Challenge proposal of any arbitrary small value under RTPP Act.
    - *Truth*: Rajasthan Finance Department guidelines and FAQ specify a **minimum project threshold of ₹50 Crore** for Swiss Challenge proposals under Rule 79A onward of the RTPP Rules, 2013.

15. **The Mahi Water vs Power Legal Allocation Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Assuming an inter-state river's water-sharing percentage automatically governs the hydro electricity generation allocation.
    - *Truth*: Water sharing and power sharing are legally distinct questions governed by separate bipartite agreements. The 140-MW Mahi Bajaj Sagar station must not be assigned a blanket 100% power ratio without citing the operative agreement.

16. **The Renewable Target Year Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Quoting the target of 30 GW by 2024–25 as active.
    - *Truth*: That target period is in the past. Active benchmarks: **90 GW by 2029–30** (Notified Renewable Policy 2023) and **125 GW by FY 2029–30** (Investor Portal 2026 roadmap).

17. **The Bhadla District Reorganisation Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Marking Bhadla Solar Park as located in Jodhpur district.
    - *Truth*: Following Rajasthan's administrative district reorganization, Bhadla Solar Park is located in **Phalodi district** (capacity: 2,245 MW).

18. **The PM-KUSUM Component Segregation Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Conflating Component A with Component B.
    - *Truth*: **Component A** = 0.5 MW to 2 MW decentralised grid-connected plants on barren land; **Component B** = stand-alone solar agricultural pumps; **Component C** = solarisation of grid-connected pumps and feeders.

19. **The Anta Power Station Ownership Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Marking Anta Gas Thermal Power Station as an RVUNL state plant.
    - *Truth*: Anta Gas Power Station (419.33 MW, Baran) is owned and operated by central PSU **NTPC Ltd.**

20. **The Lignite Plant Fuel Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Assuming Suratgarh or Kota run on domestic Rajasthan lignite.
    - *Truth*: Suratgarh, Chhabra, and Kota run on **bituminous coal** transported from central/eastern coalfields. **Giral (Barmer) and Barsingsar (Bikaner)** run on Rajasthan **lignite**.

21. **The Green Energy Corridor (GEC) Implementing Entity Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Claiming GEC is implemented by JdVVNL.
    - *Truth*: GEC consists of high-voltage transmission lines (765 kV, 400 kV, 220 kV) and grid substations, executed by the state transmission utility, **RVPNL**, with **KfW (Germany)** financing.

22. **The RSTDSP Town Count Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Stating RSTDSP covers all 250+ urban local bodies in Rajasthan.
    - *Truth*: RSTDSP is focused on water supply and sanitation across **at least 14 secondary towns**, with an original ADB loan closing date of **31 May 2028**.

23. **The State vs Located Thermal Distinction Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Quoting "total thermal power in Rajasthan" as identical to "RVUNL thermal power".
    - *Truth*: Total thermal capacity located in the state includes private plants (e.g. Kawai 1,320 MW by Adani) and central shares, exceeding RVUNL's own generation portfolio.

24. **The Capacity vs Generation Unit Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Using "MW" to describe electricity generated over a financial year.
    - *Truth*: "MW" measures instantaneous installed generation capacity; electricity generated is measured in **Billion Units (BU) or Kilowatt-hours (kWh)**. (FY 2025–26 renewable generation: **69.12 BU**).

25. **The Project Status Discipline Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Counting proposed, foundation-stone-laid, and under-construction plants as operating capacity.
    - *Truth*: Rigorously distinguish: *Cabinet approval ≠ Foundation stone (e.g. Mahi Banswara 2,800 MW) ≠ Under construction (e.g. RAPP-8 700 MW) ≠ Synchronization ≠ Commercial Operation Date (e.g. RAPP-7 700 MW)*.

## 5. 10 Active Recall Flashcards

### FC-01: Installed Renewable Energy Snapshot (30 Sept 2026)
- **Question**: What are Rajasthan's installed renewable, solar, and wind capacities as of 30 September 2026, and how do they rank nationally?
- **Answer**: 
  - Total Renewables: **51,595.50 MW (Rank #2 in India)** (~77.53% of installed capacity).
  - Solar: **45,121.44 MW (Rank #1 in India)**.
  - Wind: **5,815.29 MW (Rank #5 in India)**.
  - Renewable Generation (FY 2025–26): **69.12 BU (Rank #1 in India)**.

### FC-02: Conflicting State Power Statistics
- **Question**: Why must the Economic Review Table 7.1 power total not be blended with the investor portal renewable series?
- **Answer**: Economic Review Table 7.1 reports **31,556.43 MW total power capacity up to Dec 2025** using older utility perimeters, whereas the investor portal reports **51,595.50 MW of renewables alone up to Sept 2026**. They use incompatible reporting boundaries and dates and must be cited separately as distinct series.

### FC-03: Nuclear Power Status (RAPS & Mahi Banswara)
- **Question**: What is the current operational capacity of Rawatbhata nuclear station, and what is the status of Mahi Banswara?
- **Answer**: Operating capacity is **1,880 MW** (Units 1–6 = 1,180 MW + **RAPP-7 = 700 MW**, which entered commercial operation on **15 April 2025**). RAPP-8 (700 MW) is under construction. **Mahi Banswara** comprises **four 700-MW units (2,800 MW total)** under ASHVINI JV; its foundation stone was laid on **25 September 2025**.

### FC-04: Kalisindh Thermal Power Station Configuration
- **Question**: What is the correct unit configuration and installed capacity of Kalisindh Thermal Power Station?
- **Answer**: Capacity: **1,200 MW**, consisting of **two 600-MW coal-based units**. (Corrected from false draft claim of two 660-MW units).

### FC-05: RRECL Origin & Formation Date
- **Question**: When was RRECL formed, and which predecessor entities were merged to create it?
- **Answer**: Formed on **9 August 2002** by merging the **Rajasthan Energy Development Agency (REDA)** and the **Rajasthan State Power Corporation Ltd. (RSPCL)**. It is not an unbundled successor of RSEB.

### FC-06: Classified Road Network vs PWD Maintained (March 2025)
- **Question**: What is Rajasthan's total classified road network length, PWD-maintained length, and road density as of March 2025?
- **Answer**: 
  - Total Classified Road Network: **335,306 km** (NH 10,790 km; SH 17,316 km; MDR 18,234 km; ODR 72,822 km; Village Roads 216,144 km).
  - PWD Maintained: **197,051 km**.
  - Geographic Road Density: **97.97 km per 100 sq km**.

### FC-07: Railway Divisions in Rajasthan
- **Question**: Name the 4 operating divisions of North Western Railway (NWR) and identify the zone governing Kota division.
- **Answer**: NWR (HQ Jaipur) comprises **Jaipur, Ajmer, Bikaner, and Jodhpur**. **Kota division belongs to West Central Railway (WCR), headquartered at Jabalpur (MP)**.

### FC-08: Swiss Challenge Procurement Parameters
- **Question**: Under what statutory rules is the Swiss Challenge method regulated in Rajasthan, and what is its minimum threshold?
- **Answer**: Governed under **Rule 79A onward of the Rajasthan Transparency in Public Procurement (RTPP) Rules, 2013**, with a **minimum proposal threshold of ₹50 Crore** for eligible infrastructure sectors.

### FC-09: Multi-Tranche Highway Funding (ADB RSHIP)
- **Question**: What is the formal name and tranche scope of ADB's highway project in Rajasthan?
- **Answer**: **Rajasthan State Highway Investment Program (RSHIP)** funded by **ADB**; Tranche 2 covers ~754 km and Tranche 3 covers ~290 km of State Highways and MDRs. (Replaces false joint WB+ADB 4,000 km claim).

### FC-10: Desert Canal Modernisation (NDB IGNP)
- **Question**: Which multilateral institution funds the relining and restructuring of the Indira Gandhi Canal system, and what is the formal project title?
- **Answer**: Funded by the **New Development Bank (NDB)** under the project title **Rajasthan Water Sector Restructuring Project for the Desert Areas**, focusing on relining the IGNP feeder/main canal to arrest seepage and prevent waterlogging.
