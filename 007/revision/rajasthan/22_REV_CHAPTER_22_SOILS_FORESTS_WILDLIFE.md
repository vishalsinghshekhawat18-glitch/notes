## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Most Widespread Soil Order**:
   - **Entisols** is the most widespread soil order across Rajasthan, NOT Aridisols!
2. **Largest Legal Forest Category**:
   - **Protected Forests (*संरक्षित वन*)** constitute **56.49%** of Recorded Forest Area (RFA).
   - Reserved Forests (*आरक्षित वन*) constitute **37.05%**.
3. **Forest Cover Percentages**:
   - Recorded Forest Area (RFA): **9.60% (32,863 sq km)**.
   - Actual Forest Cover (Canopy $>10\%$): **4.87% (16,655 sq km)**.
   - Combined Forest + Tree Cover: **7.42% (25,388 sq km)**.
4. **Lowest Forest Area vs Lowest Forest Percentage**:
   - Lowest *Absolute Forest Area*: **Churu (77 sq km)**.
   - Lowest *Forest Percentage*: **Jodhpur (0.48%)**.
5. **Soil Health Card Launch Venue**:
   - Launched on **19 February 2015 from Suratgarh (Sriganganagar)**; Slogan: *"स्वस्थ धरा, खेत हरा"*.
6. **Chemical Treatment of Soils**:
   - **Alkaline Soils ($pH > 8.5$)**: Treated using **Gypsum ($CaSO_4 \cdot 2H_2O$)**.
   - **Acidic Soils ($pH < 6.5$)**: Treated using **Lime ($CaCO_3$)**.
7. **Complete 5 Tiger Reserves Chronology**:
   1. Ranthambore (1973)
   2. Sariska (1978)
   3. Mukundara Hills (2013)
   4. **Ramgarh Vishdhari (2022 — 52nd in India)**
   5. **Dholpur-Karauli (2023 — 54th in India)**
8. **Desert National Park Legal Status**:
   - Despite its name containing "National Park", **Desert National Park is a Wildlife Sanctuary**, NOT a declared National Park!
9. **Flying Squirrel vs Blackbuck Sanctuaries**:
   - **Flying Squirrel**: Found in **Sita Mata Wildlife Sanctuary** (Pratapgarh).
   - **Blackbucks**: Found in **Tal Chhapar Sanctuary** (Churu) with sweet *Mothiya* grass.
10. **State Symbols Notification Dates**:

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What are Vertisols, where are they found in Rajasthan, and why are they termed "self-ploughing"?
- **A**: Vertisols are deep black clayey soils rich in montmorillonite clay, found exclusively in the Hadoti Plateau (Kota, Bundi, Baran, Jhalawar). They are called "self-ploughing" because they expand and become sticky when wet, and shrink upon drying to form deep, gaping vertical cracks that naturally aerate the root zone.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Distinguish between Recorded Forest Area (RFA) and Actual Forest Cover in Rajasthan as per ISFR.
- **A**: 
  - **Recorded Forest Area (RFA)**: All land legally registered as forest in state land-records, regardless of tree existence = **32,863 sq km (9.60% of state)**.
  - **Actual Forest Cover**: Lands with a canopy density of $>10\%$ detected via remote sensing satellites = **16,655 sq km (4.87% of state)**.

### Card 3
- **Q**: Name the 5 Tiger Reserves of Rajasthan in chronological order with their notification years.
- **A**: 
  1. **Ranthambore Tiger Reserve** (1973)
  2. **Sariska Tiger Reserve** (1978)
  3. **Mukundara Hills Tiger Reserve** (2013)
  4. **Ramgarh Vishdhari Tiger Reserve** (Bundi — 2022; 52nd in India)
  5. **Dholpur-Karauli Tiger Reserve** (2023; 54th in India)

### Card 4
- **Q**: Trace the history, pod, leaves, and historic martyrdom associated with the State Tree of Rajasthan.
- **A**: **Khejri (*Prosopis cineraria*)**, notified on 31 October 1983. Known as "Kalpvriksha of Rajasthan" / *Shami* / *Janti*. Fruit/pod is **Sangri** (used in Panchkuta curry); leaves are **Loom** (protein-rich fodder). Protected during the **1730 Khejarli Massacre** where **Amrita Devi Bishnoi** and 363 martyrs sacrificed their lives.

### Card 5
- **Q**: Which wetland in Rajasthan is inscribed on the Montreux Record, and why?
- **A**: **Keoladeo Ghana National Park (Bharatpur)**. Inscribed on the Ramsar Montreux Record in 1990 due to ecological character degradation resulting from water shortages and proliferation of invasive weeds (*Paspalum distichum*).
