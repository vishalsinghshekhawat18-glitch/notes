## 3. 25 Lethal Exam Traps & Elimination Rules

```
                       GIRL CHILD & MATERNITY BENEFIT TRAP
                                        │
      ┌─────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────┐
      │                                                                   │

• ₹1.50 LAKH IN 7 INSTALMENTS!                                • ₹50,000 in 6 instalments
• 3.27 Lakh girls benefited up to Dec 2025                    • Older baseline; superseded by Lado
      │                                                                   │
      └─────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────┘
                                        │
                         MATERNITY BENEFIT (SECOND CHILD)
                     **MMMPY**: ₹6,000 IN 3 INSTALMENTS!
                     *(Exam trap: MMMPY is paid in 3 instalments, NOT 5!)*
```

1. **The Lado Protsahan vs Rajshri Scheme Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Presenting Mukhyamantri Rajshri Yojana (₹50,000 in 6 instalments) as the current headline girl-child scheme.
   - *Truth*: The current headline scheme is **Lado Protsahan Yojana (implemented 1 August 2024)**, which provides **₹1.50 Lakh in 7 milestone-linked instalments** (Economic Review 2025–26).

2. **The MMMPY Maternity Instalment Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Claiming Mukhya Mantri Matratva Poshan Yojana (MMMPY) pays ₹6,000 in 5 instalments.
   - *Truth*: The 2025–26 Economic Review explicitly describes MMMPY as **₹6,000 for the second child paid in 3 instalments**. (Central PMMVY covers the first child).

3. **The MAA Coverage Upper Limit Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Writing that Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya (MAA) provides coverage "up to ₹50 Lakh".
   - *Truth*: Official Health Department tender and scheme documents define a **₹25 Lakh family coverage/wallet**. Any claim of ₹50 lakh lacks official gazetted support.

4. **The Right to Health Statute Title Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Citing the statute as the "Rajasthan Right to Health Act, 2023".
   - *Truth*: The formal statutory title in the enacted legislative text is the **Rajasthan Right to Health Act, 2022** (it was debated and passed by the Assembly in March 2023).

5. **The Right to Health Private Hospital Blanket Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Stating that the Right to Health Act allows any citizen to walk into any private hospital for free routine surgery.
   - *Truth*: The Act guarantees **emergency treatment without prepayment** in defined critical conditions (accidental trauma, snake/animal bites, etc.) at qualified facilities, with private providers reimbursed under prescribed rules. It is NOT a universal free-pass for all private elective care.

6. **The VB–G RAM G Rural Employment Transition Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Describing the rural employment guarantee under Rajasthan's MGI Act as "100 days MGNREGA + 25 state days".
   - *Truth*: The **VB–G RAM G Act, 2025** came into force on **1 July 2026**, completely repealing MGNREGA and providing a **125-day national statutory guarantee** for rural households. The old 100+25 formula is legally obsolete.

7. **The MGI Act 150-Day Stacking Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Claiming rural households in Rajasthan now get 150 days (125 central + 25 state).
   - *Truth*: No official state notification authorizes stacking 25 state days on top of the new 125-day central guarantee. Candidates must not invent a 150-day entitlement.

8. **The Social Security Pension Baseline Rate Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Quoting ₹1,000 or ₹1,150 per month as the current pension rate.
   - *Truth*: The 2025–26 Economic Review records **₹1,250 per month** as the current rate for baseline categories (Old Age, Widow/Single Woman, Divyang).

9. **The Statutory Pension Escalation Timing Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Assuming the 15% annual pension increase under the MGI Act occurs on 1 April as a single jump.
   - *Truth*: The Act specifies **two unequal instalments: 5% on 1 July and 10% on 1 January**, subject to at least 12 months from individual pension sanction.

10. **The Free Medicine Inventory Conflation Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Quoting "1,829 free medicines" available under MNDY.
    - *Truth*: The 2025–26 Economic Review lists **1,238 medicines, 428 surgical items, and 156 sutures**. The 1,829 figure is the total combined EDL/NRD inventory across all facility tiers (medicines + consumables).

11. **The School Milk vs Anganwadi Milk Scheme Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Stating that schoolchildren receive milk under "Mukhyamantri Amrit Aahar".
    - *Truth*: Schoolchildren receive milk under **Pannadhay Bal Gopal Yojana** (150 ml for classes 1–5, 200 ml for classes 6–8, 6 days/week). **Mukhyamantri Amrit Aahar** operates in **Anganwadi centres** (for children aged 3–6, launched 14 Dec 2024).

12. **The Palanhar Orphan vs Other Categories Rate Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Stating Palanhar pays ₹2,500/month to all eligible children.
    - *Truth*: Rates are tiered: **Orphan children** receive **₹1,500/mo (0–6 yrs)** and **₹2,500/mo (6–18 yrs)**. **Other categories** (widow/divyang children) receive **₹750/mo (0–6 yrs)** and **₹1,500/mo (6–18 yrs)**. All receive an annual ₹2,000 clothing grant.

13. **The Palanhar Inception Date Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Marking Palanhar Yojana as a post-2020 welfare scheme.
    - *Truth*: Palanhar Yojana was launched on **8 February 2005** by the Government of Rajasthan.

14. **The Shree Annapurna Rasoi Subsidy Breakdown Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Claiming the beneficiary pays ₹10 or that government subsidy is ₹17.
    - *Truth*: The beneficiary pays strictly **₹8 per thali**, and the state government provides a subsidy of **₹22 per thali** (total plate value ₹30). Meal weight: **600 grams**.

15. **The Female Literacy Ranking Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Stating Rajasthan has the *lowest* female literacy in India.
    - *Truth*: According to Census 2011, Rajasthan's female literacy (**52.12%**) is the **SECOND LOWEST** in India (Bihar is lowest at 51.50%). Rural female literacy in Rajasthan is **45.80%**.

16. **The Highest Male Literacy District Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Marking Kota as having the highest male literacy in Rajasthan.
    - *Truth*: **Jhunjhunu holds the highest male literacy at 86.90%** (Census 2011). Kota holds the highest total (76.56%) and female (65.88%) literacy.

17. **The Lowest Literacy District Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Assuming Jalore is lowest in all literacy categories.
    - *Truth*: Jalore is lowest in Total Literacy (54.86%) and Female Literacy (38.47%). However, **Pratapgarh has the lowest Male Literacy at 69.50%** (Census 2011).

18. **The Silicosis Policy Inception Date Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Marking Silicosis Policy as launched in 2015 or 2021.
    - *Truth*: The *Policy on Pneumoconiosis (Silicosis) Detection, Prevention, Control and Rehabilitation* was formally notified on **3 October 2019** (Rajasthan was the 1st state to enact a dedicated policy).

19. **The Silicosis Financial Entitlements Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Stating silicosis compensation is ₹5 lakh upon diagnosis.
    - *Truth*: Entitlements are split: **₹3 Lakh on medical board certification**, **₹2 Lakh to family upon death**, and a **₹1,500 monthly disability pension** for the living patient (+ ₹10,000 funeral aid).

20. **The Anuprati Coaching "₹40,000 Universal Grant" Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Stating every Anuprati student receives a ₹40,000 cash grant in their bank account.
    - *Truth*: Tuition is paid directly to empanelled coaching institutes; the ₹40,000 annual maintenance/hostel allowance is paid only to students living in hostels/rented accommodation outside their home municipal area, subject to annual guidelines.

21. **The Census 2011 Boundary Caution Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Mapping Census 2011 district literacy rankings onto Rajasthan's newly reorganized 50-district map.
    - *Truth*: All official census literacy statistics belong strictly to the **old 33-district boundaries**. Answers must explicitly qualify: *"As per Census 2011 (33-district framework)"*.

22. **The NFHS-5 Time-Vintage Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Citing 54.4% female anaemia as a "2026 statistic".
    - *Truth*: These data points come from **NFHS-5 (survey period 2019–21)**. They illustrate structural health burdens but are not current 2026 measurements.

23. **The RMSCL Institutional Origin Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Claiming RMSCL was established along with the launch of the scheme on 2 October 2011.
    - *Truth*: RMSCL was incorporated earlier, on **4 May 2011**, to build the procurement and warehousing infrastructure before the scheme's launch on 2 October 2011.

24. **The Free Diagnostics Test Count Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Declaring 133 tests as a mandatory universal right at every primary health centre.
    - *Truth*: Diagnostic test packages are tiered: primary health centres offer basic panels (~15–20 tests), community health centres offer intermediate panels (~35–50 tests), and district/medical college hospitals offer full panels (up to 90–133+ tests).

25. **The RGHS vs MAA Beneficiary Separation Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Treating RGHS and MAA as overlapping alternatives for general citizens.
    - *Truth*: **RGHS** is an exclusive scheme for state government employees, pensioners, MLAs, and accredited journalists. General citizens are covered under **MAA (Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya Yojana)**.

## 5. 10 Active Recall Flashcards

### FC-01: Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya (MAA) Parameters
- **Question**: What is the family coverage limit and institutional delivery model of Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya (MAA)?
- **Answer**: 
  - Wallet/Coverage: **₹25 Lakh per family per year** across insurance and trust modes.
  - Features: Cashless hospitalization for eligible listed packages; 132 new packages added in 2025–26; active interstate portability.

### FC-02: Nishulk Dawa & Janch Inception Dates
- **Question**: State the exact launch dates and nodal agencies for Mukhyamantri Nishulk Dawa Yojana and Nishulk Janch Yojana.
- **Answer**: 
  - Free Medicines (MNDY): **2 October 2011** (Gandhi Jayanti); procurement agency is **RMSCL** (incorporated **4 May 2011**).
  - Free Diagnostics (MNJY): **7 April 2013** (World Health Day); operated by the Medical & Health Department.

### FC-03: Rajasthan Right to Health Act Statutory Provisions
- **Question**: What is the formal statutory title and core emergency care mandate of the Right to Health Act?
- **Answer**: 
  - Formal Title: **Rajasthan Right to Health Act, 2022** (enacted 2023).
  - Mandate: Guarantees emergency medical treatment without prepayment in defined critical emergencies at qualified public and private facilities, with private providers reimbursed under prescribed rules.

### FC-04: Lado Protsahan Yojana Structure
- **Question**: When was Lado Protsahan Yojana implemented, what is the total assistance, and how is it disbursed?
- **Answer**: Implemented from **1 August 2024**; provides **₹1.50 Lakh per girl child** disbursed across **7 milestone-linked instalments** tied to birth, vaccination, school admission, and higher education. (Supersedes the older Rajshri ₹50,000 schedule).

### FC-05: Maternity Benefit for Second Child (MMMPY)
- **Question**: What is the cash entitlement and instalment schedule under Mukhya Mantri Matratva Poshan Yojana (MMMPY)?
- **Answer**: Provides **₹6,000 for the birth of the second child**, paid in **3 instalments** (corrected from old draft's 5 instalments) to address maternal and child undernutrition.

### FC-06: School Milk vs Anganwadi Milk Schemes
- **Question**: Contrast the school milk programme with the Anganwadi milk scheme in Rajasthan.
- **Answer**: 
  - Schools: **Pannadhay Bal Gopal Yojana** (hot milk from skimmed milk powder; 150 ml for classes 1–5, 200 ml for classes 6–8; **6 days a week**).
  - Anganwadis: **Mukhyamantri Amrit Aahar** (implemented from **14 December 2024** for children aged 3–6 years).

### FC-07: Minimum Guaranteed Income Act & July 2026 Transition
- **Question**: What are the statutory employment entitlements under the Rajasthan Minimum Guaranteed Income Act, 2023, and how did the central legal framework change on 1 July 2026?
- **Answer**: 
  - Urban entitlement: **125 days** of guaranteed wage employment.
  - Rural entitlement transition: On **1 July 2026**, the central **VB–G RAM G Act, 2025** came into force, repealing MGNREGA and providing a **125-day national statutory guarantee**. The old "100 central + 25 state" formula is obsolete.

### FC-08: Palanhar Yojana Current Monthly Rates
- **Question**: What are the monthly rates and annual grants under Palanhar Yojana as reported in the 2025–26 Economic Review?
- **Answer**:

### FC-09: Silicosis Policy Financial Architecture
- **Question**: When was Rajasthan's Silicosis Policy notified, and what are its primary financial benefits?
- **Answer**: Notified on **3 October 2019** (first in India). Benefits: **₹3 Lakh** upon medical board certification, **₹2 Lakh** to family upon death, **₹1,500/month** patient pension, and **₹10,000** funeral assistance.

### FC-10: Census 2011 Literacy Rates & Extremes
- **Question**: State Rajasthan's total, male, and female literacy rates, national ranking in female literacy, and extreme districts as per Census 2011.
- **Answer**: 
  - Rates: Total: **66.11%**; Male: **79.19%**; Female: **52.12%** (Gender gap: **27.07%**).
  - National Rank: Female literacy is **2nd lowest in India** (ahead of Bihar 51.50%).
  - Extremes: Total & Female Highest: **Kota (76.56% & 65.88%)**; Total & Female Lowest: **Jalore (54.86% & 38.47%)**; Male Highest: **Jhunjhunu (86.90%)**; Male Lowest: **Pratapgarh (69.50%)**.
