1. **Female Literacy Standing Trap**:
   - Rajasthan's female literacy (**52.12%**) is NOT the lowest in India; it is the **SECOND LOWEST** (Bihar is lowest at 51.50%).
   - Rural female literacy in Rajasthan is a meager **45.80%**.

2. **Literacy Extremes Invariant**:
   - Highest Total Literacy: **Kota (76.56%)**.
   - Highest Female Literacy: **Kota (65.88%)**.
   - Highest Male Literacy: **Jhunjhunu (86.90%)** (NOT Kota!).
   - Lowest Total Literacy & Lowest Female Literacy: **Jalore (54.86% & 38.47%)**.
   - Lowest Male Literacy: **Pratapgarh (69.50%)** (NOT Jalore!).

3. **Palanhar Genesis Trap**:
   - Palanhar Yojana was NOT launched recently; it was launched on **8 February 2005** under the Social Justice & Empowerment Department.

4. **Minimum Guaranteed Income Statutory Escalation**:
   - The annual pension increase is **15%**, broken into **two unequal installments**:
     - **5% increase on 1 July**
     - **10% increase on 1 January**.

5. **IGMPY Child Sequence Trap**:
   - Central PMMVY covers the *First Child*.
   - Rajasthan's Indira Gandhi Matritva Poshan Yojana (IGMPY) uniquely covers the **Second Child** (₹6,000).

6. **Shree Annapurna Rasoi Metrics**:
   - Beneficiary cost: Strictly **₹8 per thali**.
   - Government subsidy: **₹22 per thali** (Total cost = ₹30).
   - Food quantity: **600 Grams** (100g dal, 100g sabzi, 300g chapati/millets, 100g rice/khichdi).

7. **Silicosis Assistance Trap**:
   - Total financial package: **₹3 Lakh on diagnosis** + **₹2 Lakh on death** + **₹10,000 funeral assistance**.

## 5. Model 2-Marker & 5-Marker Rapid Answer Keys

### Q1 (2 Marks): What is the core mandate of the Rajasthan Policy on Pneumoconiosis (Silicosis), 2019?
> **Answer**: Notified on **3 October 2019** (first in India), it grants rights-based relief to mine/quarry workers: **₹3 Lakh** on medical certification, **₹2 Lakh + ₹10,000** upon death, monthly widow pension of **₹1,500**, and automatic inclusion of surviving children in the Palanhar Yojana.

### Q2 (5 Marks): Summarize the institutional and technological architecture of the Mukhyamantri Nishulk Dawa Yojana (MNDY).
> **Answer**:
> 1. **Launch**: Launched on **2 October 2011** (Gandhi Jayanti) to ensure zero out-of-pocket expenditure on medicines in public healthcare.
> 2. **Procurement Agency**: Handled by the **Rajasthan Medical Services Corporation Ltd (RMSCL)** through centralized transparent rate contracts.
> 3. **Digital Inventory**: Managed end-to-end via the **e-Aushadhi** software portal, tracking quality testing, batch expiration, and buffer stock across 34 district drug warehouses.
> 4. **Scope**: Completely free provision of **1,800+ medicines, 900+ surgical items, and 180+ sutures** from sub-centres to medical college hospitals.
> 5. **National Role Model**: Formed the structural template for the Government of India's *Free Drugs Service Initiative* under the National Health Mission (NHM).
