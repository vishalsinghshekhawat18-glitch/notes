## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **First vs Last State to Sign Accession**: **Bikaner** signed first (7 Aug 1947); **Dholpur** signed last (14 Aug 1947).
2. **Nomenclature of Matsya Union**: Suggested by **K.M. Munshi**, NOT Sardar Patel.
3. **The "Death Warrant" Epigram Ruler**: Uttered by **Maharawal Chandraveer Singh of Banswara**, NOT Hanwant Singh of Jodhpur.
4. **Inaugurator of Stage 3 (United Rajasthan)**: Inaugurated by **Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru** (18 April 1948 at Udaipur).
5. **Rajasthan Day (30 March)**: Celebrates **Stage 4 (Greater Rajasthan, 1949)**, NOT 1 November (which is Reorganization Day).
6. **The Only "Maharajpramukh" in Indian History**: **Maharana Bhupal Singh of Mewar** (lifetime constitutional post).
7. **P. Satyanarayan Rao Committee Allocations (1949)**:
   - Capital: **Jaipur**
   - High Court: **Jodhpur**
   - Education: **Bikaner**
   - Mining/Customs: **Udaipur**
   - Agriculture/Forest: **Bharatpur**
   - Board of Revenue: **Ajmer**
8. **Dr. Shankar Rao Deo Committee Mandate**: Ascertained that **Bharatpur and Dholpur** public wished to merge with **Rajasthan** rather than UP.
9. **Sirohi Partition in Stage 6**: Merged **excluding Mount Abu and Delwara** (which Sardar Patel merged into Bombay Province). Restored in Stage 7 via **Gokulbhai Bhatt's** agitation.
10. **Territorial Exchange on 1 Nov 1956**: **Sunel Tappa (MP) went to Jhalawar (Rajasthan)**; **Sironj sub-division (Kota, Rajasthan) went to Madhya Pradesh**.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What was the "Dhara Sabha", and who was its Chief Minister?
- **A**: The autonomous 30-member legislative assembly of the centrally administered Category 'C' territory of **Ajmer-Merwara**; headed by Chief Minister **Haribhau Upadhyaya** until its merger on 1 November 1956.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Who was the first Governor of Rajasthan, and when did he take office?
- **A**: **Sardar Gurmukh Nihal Singh**, who took the oath of office on **1 November 1956** upon the abolition of the post of Rajpramukh under the 7th Constitutional Amendment Act.

### Card 3
- **Q**: How many districts existed in Rajasthan immediately following the final reorganization on 1 November 1956?
- **A**: Exactly **26 Districts** (Ajmer became the 26th district).

### Card 4
- **Q**: Who were the three members of the States Reorganisation Commission (SRC) that finalized Rajasthan's map?
- **A**: **Justice Fazal Ali (Chairman)**, **K.M. Panikkar**, and **Hriday Nath Kunzru**.

### Card 5
- **Q**: In which specific stages were the three hereditary Chiefships (*Thikanas*) merged into Rajasthan?
- **A**: (1) **Neemrana**: Stage 1 (Matsya Union); (2) **Kushalgarh**: Stage 2 (Rajasthan Union); (3) **Lava**: Stage 4 (Greater Rajasthan).
