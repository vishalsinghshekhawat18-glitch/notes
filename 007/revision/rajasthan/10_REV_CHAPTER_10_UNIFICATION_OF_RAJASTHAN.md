## 3. Top 10 High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **First vs Last State to Sign Accession**:
   - **Bikaner (Sadul Singh, 7 Aug 1947)** signed first; **Dholpur (Udaybhan Singh, 14 Aug 1947)** signed last.
2. **Nomenclature of Matsya Union**:
   - Suggested by **K.M. Munshi** after ancient Matsya Mahajanapada, NOT by Sardar Patel or N.V. Gadgil.
3. **The "Death Warrant" Quote Ruler**:
   - Uttered by **Maharawal Chandraveer Singh of Banswara** during Stage 2 accession, NOT Hanwant Singh of Jodhpur.
4. **Inaugurator of Stage 3 (United Rajasthan)**:
   - Inaugurated by Prime Minister **Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru** on 18 April 1948 at Udaipur, NOT Sardar Patel.
5. **Rajasthan Day (30 March) Significance**:
   - Celebrates **Stage 4 (Greater Rajasthan, 1949)**, which integrated 75% of Rajasthan's territory, NOT 1 November (which is Reorganization Day).
6. **The Only "Maharajpramukh" in Indian History**:
   - **Maharana Bhupal Singh of Mewar** was the sole person to hold this lifetime constitutional post.
7. **P. Satyanarayan Rao Committee Allocations (1949)**:
   - State Capital: **Jaipur**
   - High Court Principal Bench: **Jodhpur**
   - Education Department: **Bikaner**
   - Mining & Customs Department: **Udaipur**
   - Agriculture & Forest Department: **Bharatpur**
   - Board of Revenue (*Rajswamandal*): **Ajmer**
8. **Dr. Shankar Rao Deo Committee Mandate (1949)**:
   - Ascertained that the public of **Bharatpur and Dholpur** overwhelmingly wished to merge with **Rajasthan** rather than Uttar Pradesh.
9. **Sirohi Partition in Stage 6 & Restitution in Stage 7**:
   - In Stage 6 (Jan 1950), Sirohi merged **excluding Mount Abu and Delwara** (annexed to Bombay Province by Patel). Restored in Stage 7 (Nov 1956) due to **Gokulbhai Bhatt's** tireless satyagraha.
10. **Territorial Exchange on 1 Nov 1956**:
    - **Sunel Tappa** (Bhanpura tehsil, Mandsaur, MP) was **merged into Jhalawar (Rajasthan)**.
    - **Sironj sub-division** (Kota district, Rajasthan) was **given to Madhya Pradesh**.
