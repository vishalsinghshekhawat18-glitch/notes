## 3. Top 10 High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **First Ruler to Sign Accession**:
   - In all of India, V. P. Menon records that the **Gaekwar of Baroda was the first ruler actually to sign**. In Rajputana, **Maharaja Sadul Singh of Bikaner** played a critical early leadership role (signing around 7 August 1947). Dholpur’s signing on 14 August is an exam convention for Rajputana, not proven last in all India.
2. **Nomenclature of Matsya Union**:
   - Proposed by **K. M. Munshi** recalling the ancient Vedic/Mahajanapada Matsya region, NOT by Sardar Patel or N. V. Gadgil.
3. **Four Constituent States of Matsya Union**:
   - The GoI *White Paper on Indian States* strictly lists **four princely states**: Alwar, Bharatpur, Dholpur, and Karauli. Neemrana was an associated small chiefship/thikana, NOT a fifth independent princely state.
4. **Inaugurator of Stage 3 (United Rajasthan)**:
   - Formally inaugurated by Prime Minister **Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru** on 18 April 1948 at Udaipur, NOT by Sardar Patel or N. V. Gadgil.
5. **Rajasthan Day (30 March) vs Reorganization Day (1 November)**:
   - **30 March (Rajasthan Day)** marks Stage 4 (Greater Rajasthan, 1949) when major states (Jaipur, Jodhpur, Bikaner, Jaisalmer) merged.
   - **1 November** marks the final territorial reorganization under the States Reorganisation Act, 1956.
6. **Mewar Financial Settlement Nuance**:
   - Maharana Bhupal Singh was granted a ₹10 lakh privy purse, ₹5 lakh Rajpramukh allowance, and a further **₹5 lakh for religious and charitable purposes** (the claim that this ₹5L was exclusively earmarked for Eklingji temple lacks clause-level verification).
7. **P. Satyanarayan Rao Committee Allocations (1949)**:
   - State Capital: **Jaipur**
   - High Court: **Jodhpur**
   - Education Department: **Bikaner**
   - Mining & Customs Department: **Udaipur**
   - Agriculture Department: **Bharatpur**
8. **Dr. Shankar Rao Deo Committee Mandate (1949)**:
   - Committee comprised **Dr. Shankar Rao Deo, R. K. Sidhwa, and Prabhu Dayal Himmat Singhka**. They ascertained public opinion in Bharatpur and Dholpur through questionnaires and public delegations (not a formal ballot plebiscite), recommending merger with Rajasthan.
9. **Sirohi Partition (1950) & Restitution (1956)**:
   - In Stage 6 (Jan 1950), Sirohi merged into Rajasthan **excluding Abu Road taluka and Delwara**, which were transferred to Bombay Province. They were returned to Rajasthan on 1 November 1956 under the States Reorganisation Act following sustained local agitation led by Gokulbhai Bhatt.
10. **Territorial Reorganization Exchange on 1 Nov 1956**:
    - **Sunel Tappa** (from Bhanpura tehsil, Mandsaur district, Madhya Bharat/MP) was merged into **Jhalawar district (Rajasthan)**.
    - **Sironj sub-division** (an enclave of Kota district, Rajasthan) was transferred to **Madhya Pradesh**.
    - **Ajmer-Merwara** was integrated into Rajasthan as the **26th district**.
