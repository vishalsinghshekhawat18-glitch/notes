## 4. Top 25 Corrected RPSC Prelims Traps & Elimination Rules

```text
                  GSVA SECTORAL HIERARCHY AT CURRENT PRICES (2025–26)
                                           │
             Rank 1: SERVICES (47.71%) ──► Dominant state value creator
             Rank 2: INDUSTRY (26.55%) ──► Edges out Agriculture at current prices!
             Rank 3: AGRICULTURE (25.74%)► Livestock (~₹2.17L Cr) > Crop Sector!
```

1. **GSDP vs GSVA Total Trap**: 2025–26 Advance Estimate GSDP at current prices is **₹18,75,413 crore** (nominal growth 10.24%). The frequently quoted figure of ₹17,12,016 crore is the **GSVA total**, which excludes net product taxes!
2. **Constant Price GSDP & Real Growth**: Constant (2011–12) prices GSDP for 2025–26 is **₹9,81,807 crore** with a real growth rate of **8.66%**.
3. **Net State Domestic Product (NSDP)**: 2025–26 AE NSDP is **₹16,85,118 crore** at current prices (growth 10.48%) and **₹8,59,333 crore** at constant 2011–12 prices (growth 8.90%).
4. **Per Capita Income (PCI) Milestone**: Current-price PCI for 2025–26 AE is **₹2,02,349** (growing at 9.32%), crossing the landmark ₹2 lakh mark. Constant (2011–12) PCI is **₹1,03,189** (growing at 7.76%).
5. **National GDP Base Year Rebase**: MoSPI released the new national GDP series with base year **2022–23** on 27 February 2026. However, Rajasthan’s *Economic Review 2025–26* reports state accounts at **constant 2011–12 prices**. Never make direct comparisons without accounting for this vintage mismatch!
6. **Sectoral GSVA Ordering at Current Prices**: **Services (47.71%) > Industry (26.55%) > Agriculture (25.74%)**. Industry's share is higher than Agriculture at current prices!
7. **Livestock vs Crop Sector Dominance**: In Rajasthan, the **livestock sector GVA (~₹2.17 lakh crore) exceeds the crop sector GVA**. Milk is the single largest contributing commodity to livestock income.
8. **Fiscal Deficit Year Distinction**:
   - FY 2024–25 Fiscal Deficit in Economic Review: **4.26% of GSDP**.
   - FY 2026–27 Budget Estimate: **3.69% of GSDP** (₹79,493 crore). Never cite 4.26% as the 2026–27 figure!
9. **Debt and Liabilities Ratio**: In the 2025–26 Economic Review, total debt and liabilities for FY 2024–25 stand at **₹6,40,844 crore**, which is **37.67% of GSDP** (not 35.70%).
10. **Budget Capital Outlay Growth**: In Budget 2026–27, capital outlay is budgeted at **₹53,978 crore**, up by 36.9% from **₹39,429 crore** in 2025–26 Revised Estimates (replacing older ₹30,727 Cr figures).
11. **GFCF vs Government Capital Outlay**: Gross Fixed Capital Formation (GFCF) measures economy-wide fixed asset creation across both private and public sectors; government budget capital outlay is only public expenditure on assets. They are not identical.
12. **Rajasthan State WPI Base Year**: Rajasthan's state-specific WPI compiled by DES Jaipur uses **1999–2000 = 100** (July 2026 general index: 427.94). It does NOT use 2011–12!
13. **All-India WPI Base Year**: The current All-India WPI published by DPIIT/MoSPI has been updated to **2022–23 = 100**.
14. **CPI-IW Centre Trap (Alwar vs Ajmer)**: In the revised 2016 base series of CPI for Industrial Workers (CPI-IW), the three designated Rajasthan centres are **Jaipur, Alwar, and Bhilwara**. **Alwar replaced Ajmer** in the 2016 base series!
15. **CPI-AL and CPI-RL Base Year Revision**: Labour Bureau revised CPI for Agricultural and Rural Labourers from the 1986–87 base to **2019 = 100** (released June 2025).
16. **CPI-Combined Retail Inflation Base**: The national CPI series (Rural, Urban, Combined) compiled by NSO/MoSPI uses **2024 = 100** (replacing the 2012 base).
17. **Solar Power Installed Capacity Reality**: As of 30 September 2026, Rajasthan's installed solar capacity is **45,121.44 MW (~45.12 GW)** (total renewable capacity: 51,595.50 MW). The old "over 22 GW" figure is outdated.
18. **USD 350-Billion Economy Target Year**: The Budget 2025–26 declared target is **2030** (NOT 2029!).
19. **Employment Mission Year**: The Rajasthan Employment Policy 2026 targets facilitating **15 lakh employment opportunities by March 2029** (distinct from the 2030 GDP target).
20. **Viksit Rajasthan @2047 Frameworks**: Budget 2026–27 articulates **10 development resolutions/pillars**; Economic Review 2025–26 structures its overview around **13 development sectors**. Do not conflate them into a fabricated "7 pillars" list.
21. **PM-KUSUM Component Separation**:
   - Component A: Decentralized grid-connected RE power plants (up to 2 MW) on barren/fallow land.
   - Component B: Standalone off-grid solar agriculture pumps.
   - Component C: Solarization of grid-connected agriculture pumps and feeders.
22. **Effective Revenue Deficit (ERD)**: Defined as **Revenue Deficit minus Grants for Creation of Capital Assets**. It isolates consumption borrowing from capital transfer grants.
23. **Sixteenth Finance Commission Devolution**: Union Government accepted **41% vertical devolution** to states as a group for the award period starting 2026–27. Do not quote individual state percentages without the official 16th FC award table.
24. **SDG India Index 2023–24**: Rajasthan scored **67** (up 7 points from 2020–21), placing it in the **Front Runner** category (65–99 score band). However, this overall status does not imply Front Runner standing in every individual goal.
25. **FRBM Act Benchmark**: Governed by the **Rajasthan FRBM Act, 2005**. Statutory target is 3% fiscal deficit/GSDP, though conditional borrowing windows (power sector reforms, capital investment loans) can cause temporary variations.
