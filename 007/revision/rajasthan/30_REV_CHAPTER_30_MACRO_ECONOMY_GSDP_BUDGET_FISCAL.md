## 3. High-Yield RPSC Traps & Elimination Rules

```
                      GSVA SECTORAL RANKING AT CURRENT PRICES
                                         │
             Rank 1: SERVICES (47.71%) ──► Engine of Value Addition
             Rank 2: INDUSTRY (26.55%) ──► Slightly edges Agri at current prices!
             Rank 3: AGRICULTURE (25.74%)► Crops ~11.5% + Livestock ~11.8%
```

1. **WPI Base Year Trap**:
   - All-India WPI base year: 2011–12.
   - **Rajasthan WPI base year**: **1999–2000 = 100** (Compiled by DES, NOT Labour Bureau).

2. **CPI-IW Designated Centers**:
   - The three centers in Rajasthan are **Jaipur, Alwar, and Bhilwara**.
   - *Trap*: **Ajmer was dropped** and replaced by **Alwar** in the revised 2016 base series.

3. **Per Capita Income Invariant**:
   - Constant (2011–12) prices: **₹1,03,189** (Raj) vs. **₹1,21,968** (All-India).
   - Real growth rate of Rajasthan (7.76%) is **higher** than All-India (6.30%).

4. **The Deficit Quad**:
   - **Revenue Deficit (RD)** = Revenue Expenditure - Revenue Receipts.
   - **Effective Revenue Deficit (ERD)** = RD - Grants for Creation of Capital Assets (Rangarajan Committee).
   - **Fiscal Deficit (FD)** = Total Borrowing Requirement (4.26% of GSDP).
   - **Primary Deficit (PD)** = FD - Interest Payments.

5. **GFCF Ownership Share**:
   - **Private Sector = 77.97%** (Massive lion's share).
   - **Public Sector = 22.03%**.

6. **The $350 Billion Target**:
   - Target deadline: **Year 2029**.
   - Stepping stone toward **"Viksit Rajasthan @2047"**.

## 4. Rapid-Recall Mnemonics

- **The 7 Pillars of $350B Economy**: **"H-S-C-P-G-H-G"**
  - **H** = High Growth (12%+ nominal)
  - **S** = Sectoral Transformation
  - **C** = Capital Formation (GFCF expansion)
  - **P** = Price Stabilization & Cold Chains
  - **G** = Green Growth & Solar Transition
  - **H** = Human Capital (Skill & Health)
  - **G** = Good Governance & RIPS 2024
- **CPI-IW 3 Centers**: **"J-A-B"**
  - **J** = Jaipur
  - **A** = Alwar (Replaced Ajmer)
  - **B** = Bhilwara
