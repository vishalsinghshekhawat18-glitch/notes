## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Founding Leader of Bijolia**: **Sadhu Sitaram Das** founded Phase 1 (1897–1915). Vijay Singh Pathik joined only in **1916**.
2. **Chanwari vs Talwar Bandhai Taxes**:
   - **Chanwari (1903)**: Imposed by Rao Krishna Singh (Rs 5 on daughter's wedding).
   - **Talwar Bandhai (1906)**: Imposed by Rao Prithvi Singh (feudal succession tax).
3. **Uparmal Panch Board Details**: Established in **1917 on Hariyali Amavasya** at Berisal village; **Manna Patel** was elected first Sarpanch.
4. **Newspaper Nationalizing Bijolia**: **"Pratap" (Kanpur)** edited by **Ganesh Shankar Vidyarthi**, NOT *Rajasthan Kesari*.
5. **Rajasthan Seva Sangh Genesis**: Founded in **1919 at Wardha** (Maharashtra); shifted to **Ajmer in 1920**.
6. **Govindpura vs Dabi Martyrs**:
   - **Govindpura (13 July 1923, Bengu)**: Rupa Ji Dhakar and Kripa Ji Dhakar.
   - **Dabi (2 April 1923, Bundi)**: Nanak Ji Bhil and Devi Lal Gurjar.
7. **The Elegy "Arzi"**: Composed by **Manikya Lal Verma** in honour of martyr **Nanak Ji Bhil**, NOT for Bijolia (where he wrote *Panchhida*).
8. **"Dyerism Double Distilled"**: Coined by **Mahatma Gandhi in Young India** for the **Nimuchana Massacre (14 May 1925)** in Alwar, NOT for Mangarh Dham or Jallianwala.
9. **Katrathal Women's Meet Leadership**: **Kishori Devi was the President**; **Durga Devi Sharma was the keynote orator** (25 April 1934, 10,000 women).
10. **The Kudan Incident in British Parliament**: Firing by British Captain Webb on Jat peasants at **Kudan (Sikar, 1935)** was debated in the **House of Commons, London**.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What was the "Bolshevik Agreement" in Rajasthan's peasant history?
- **A**: A 1922 bilateral compromise signed between **Rawat Anoop Singh of Bengu** and the Rajasthan Seva Sangh abolishing cesses, repudiated by Mewar Maharana as dangerous socialism.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Which historic peasant bulletin was published clandestinely by the Uparmal Panch Board in Bijolia?
- **A**: **"Uparmal Danka"** (ऊपरमाल डंका), a handwritten weekly cyclostyled bulletin circulated across villages.

### Card 3
- **Q**: Why was Commander Chhaju Singh labelled the "General Dyer of Rajasthan"?
- **A**: For ordering machine-gun firing and torching 144 houses of peaceful farmers at **Nimuchana (Alwar)** on 14 May 1925.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Which five Jagirs constituted the "Panchpana" in Shekhawati?
- **A**: **Nawalgarh, Mandawa, Dundlod, Bissau, and Khetri / Udaipurwati**.

### Card 5
- **Q**: How was the 44-year-long Bijolia movement finally resolved in 1941?
- **A**: Mewar Prime Minister **Sir T. Raghavachari** and Revenue Minister **Dr. Mohan Singh Mehta** negotiated with Manikya Lal Verma, restoring all confiscated lands and abolishing all 84 cesses.
