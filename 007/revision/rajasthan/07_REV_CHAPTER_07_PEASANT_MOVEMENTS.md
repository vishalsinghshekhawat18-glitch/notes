## 3. Top 10 High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Founding Leader of Bijolia**: **Sadhu Sitaram Das** founded Phase 1 in **1897**. Vijay Singh Pathik took over leadership only in **1916**.
2. **Chanwari vs. Talwar Bandhai Taxes**:
   - **Chanwari (1903)**: Imposed by **Rao Krishna Singh** (Rs 5 on daughter's wedding).
   - **Talwar Bandhai (1906)**: Imposed by **Rao Prithvi Singh** (feudal succession investiture fee).
3. **Uparmal Panch Board Details**: Established on **Hariyali Amavasya in 1917** at Berisal village; **Manna Patel** was elected its first Sarpanch.
4. **Newspaper Championing Bijolia**: **"Pratap" (Kanpur)** edited by **Ganesh Shankar Vidyarthi**, NOT *Rajasthan Kesari*.
5. **Rajasthan Seva Sangh Genesis**: Founded in **1919 at Wardha (Maharashtra)**; shifted to **Ajmer in 1920**.
6. **Govindpura vs. Dabi Martyrs**:
   - **Govindpura (13 July 1923, Bengu)**: Rupa Ji Dhakar and Kripa Ji Dhakar.
   - **Dabi (2 April 1923, Bundi)**: Nanak Ji Bhil and Devi Lal Gurjar.
7. **The Song "Arzi" vs. "Panchhida"**:
   - **Panchhida (पंछीड़ा)**: Written by Manikya Lal Verma to inspire Bijolia peasants.
   - **Arzi (अर्जी)**: Written by Manikya Lal Verma as an elegy for martyr **Nanak Ji Bhil** after the Dabi massacre.
8. **"Dyerism Double Distilled" Quote Context**: Coined by **Mahatma Gandhi in Young India** for the **Nimuchana Massacre (14 May 1925)** in Alwar, NOT for Mangarh Dham or Jallianwala.
9. **Katrathal Women's Conference Leadership (1934)**: **Kishori Devi was the President**; the fiery **keynote speaker was Durga Devi Sharma** (protesting Thakur Man Singh of Sihot for Sotia ka Bas atrocities).
10. **The Kudan Incident in British Parliament**: Police firing by British Captain Webb on Jat peasants at **Kudan (Sikar, 1935)** was debated in the **House of Commons, London**.
