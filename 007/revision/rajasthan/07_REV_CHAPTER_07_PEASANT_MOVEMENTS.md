## 3. Top 10 High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Founding Leader of Bijolia**: **Sadhu Sitaram Das** guided the early phase from **1897**. Vijay Singh Pathik (Bhoop Singh) assumed operational leadership only in **1916**.
2. **Chanwari vs. Talwar Bandhai Taxes**:
   - **Chanwari (1903)**: Imposed by **Rao Krishna Singh** (marriage-linked cess on daughters).
   - **Talwar Bandhai (1906)**: Imposed by **Rao Prithvi Singh** (feudal succession investiture burden passed down to peasantry).
3. **Uparmal Panch Board Details**: Established on **Hariyali Amavasya in 1917** at Berisal village; **Manna Patel** is traditionally identified as its first Sarpanch (Moti Chand appears in later gazetteer records by 1922).
4. **Newspaper Championing Bijolia**: **"Pratap" (Kanpur)** edited by **Ganesh Shankar Vidyarthi**, NOT *Rajasthan Kesari*.
5. **Bal Gangadhar Tilak & Bijolia**: Tilak supported the Bijolia struggle through editorial articles in ***Maratha*** and personal correspondence with Maharana Fateh Singh. **He died on 1 August 1920 and NEVER raised Bijolia in the British Parliament.** (It was the **1935 Kudan firing** that was debated in the British House of Commons).
6. **Govindpura vs. Dabi Martyrs**:
   - **Govindpura (13 July 1923, Begun)**: Rupa Ji Dhakar and Kripa Ji Dhakar.
   - **Dabi (2 April 1923, Bundi)**: Nanak Ji Bhil and Devi Lal Gurjar.
7. **The Song "Arzi" vs. "Panchhida"**:
   - **Panchhida (पंछीड़ा)**: Written by Manikya Lal Verma to inspire Bijolia peasants.
   - **Arzi (अर्जी)**: Written by Manikya Lal Verma specifically as an elegy for martyr **Nanak Ji Bhil** after the Dabi firing.
8. **Nimuchana Casualty Figure Trap**: **800 was the estimated crowd/gathering size**, NOT the number of peasants killed. Mahatma Gandhi characterized the atrocity as *"Dyerism Double Distilled"* in *Young India*.
9. **Katrathal Women's Conference Leadership (25 April 1934)**:
   - **President / Organizer**: **Kishori Devi** (wife of Sardar Harlal Singh).
   - **Keynote / Main Speaker**: **Uttama Devi** (wife of Thakur Deshraj). Durga Devi Sharma was an associated active participant.
10. **The Kudan Debate in London (1935)**: Police firing under Captain Webb on Jat cultivators at **Kudan (Sikar, 25 April 1935)** was formally raised and debated in the **British House of Commons (Hansard)**.
