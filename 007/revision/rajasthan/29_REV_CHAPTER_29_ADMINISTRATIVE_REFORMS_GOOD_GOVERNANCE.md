## 3. High-Yield RPSC Traps & Elimination Rules

```
                      SERVICE GUARANTEE (2011) vs. RIGHT TO HEARING (2012)
                                                │
           ┌────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────┐
           │                                                                         │
   2011 ACT (Service Delivery)                                               2012 ACT (Grievances)
           │                                                                         │
  ├── Notified: 14 November 2011                                            ├── Notified: 1 August 2012
  ├── Official: Designated Officer (DO)                                     ├── Official: Public Hearing Officer (PHO)
  ├── Focus: Specific public services                                       ├── Focus: General citizen grievances
  ├── Disposal: Prescribed service window                                   ├── Disposal: Maximum 15 DAYS
  ├── Penalties: Rs. 250/day up to max Rs. 5,000                            ├── Penalties: Rs. 250 to Rs. 5,000
  └── Deduction: Direct from salary                                         └── Deduction: Direct from salary
```

1. **First State in India Distinctions**:
   - *Right to Hearing Act*: **Rajasthan is #1** (First state in India to enact in 2012).
   - *Public Services Guarantee Act*: **Madhya Pradesh was #1** (2010), Rajasthan was an early pioneer in 2011.
   - *Jan Soochna Portal*: **Rajasthan is #1** (First state in India for proactive digital RTI in 2019).

2. **Jan Aadhaar Number Traps**:
   - Family ID = **10 Digits**.
   - Individual Member ID = **11 Digits**.
   - Adult woman (18+ yrs) = **Mandatory Head of Household**.

3. **Penal Limits (Identical Caps)**:
   - Both the 2011 and 2012 Acts cap the maximum monetary penalty at **Rs. 5,000**, which is compulsorily **deducted from the officer's salary**.

4. **Right to Hearing Timelines**:
   - PHO hearing and decision: **15 days**.
   - First Appeal filing: **30 days**; First Appeal decision: **21 days**.
   - Second Appeal filing: **30 days**; Second Appeal decision: **30 days**.

## 4. Rapid-Recall Mnemonics

- **Sampark 181 Lifecycle**: **"R-M-A-D-V"**
  - **R** = Registration
  - **M** = Moderation
  - **A** = Allocation
  - **D** = Disposal
  - **V** = Verification & Feedback
- **Good Governance 8 Pillars**: **"P-R-T-R-C-E-E-A"**
  - **P** = Participation
  - **R** = Rule of Law
  - **T** = Transparency
  - **R** = Responsiveness
  - **C** = Consensus oriented
  - **E** = Equity & Inclusiveness
  - **E** = Effectiveness & Efficiency
  - **A** = Accountability
