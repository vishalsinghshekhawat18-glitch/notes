1. **Election Modes (Who is Direct vs. Indirect?)**:
   - **Directly Elected**: Ward Panchs, **Sarpanch**, Panchayat Samiti Members, Zila Parishad Members, Urban Ward Councillors.
   - **Indirectly Elected**: Up-Sarpanch, **Pradhan**, Up-Pradhan, **Zila Pramukh**, Up-Zila Pramukh, Municipal Chair/Sabhapati/Mayor.

2. **Gram Sabha Mandatory Meeting Dates (4 Annual Dates)**:
   - **26 January** (Republic Day)
   - **1 May** (Labour Day)
   - **15 August** (Independence Day)
   - **2 October** (Gandhi Jayanti)
   - *Quorum*: **1/10th** of registered voters.

3. **Current Educational Qualification Law**:
   - Class 8/10 pass requirement was **REPEALED and ABOLISHED in February 2019**.
   - Zero educational qualification required today.
   - Functional toilet requirement is **STILL MANDATORY**.

4. **District Planning Committee (DPC) Leadership Trap**:
   - In Rajasthan, the **Zila Pramukh** is the Chairperson of the DPC (under Article 243ZD & Section 121 of 1994 Act).
   - The **District Collector** is the Member Secretary.

5. **PESA (1996) Invariants in Rajasthan**:
   - 100% Chairperson seats (Sarpanch, Pradhan, Pramukh) reserved for **Scheduled Tribes (ST)**.
   - Minimum 50% member seats reserved for STs.
   - Gram Sabha is sole owner of Minor Forest Produce (MFP / MFPs) and must be consulted before land acquisition or minor mineral leasing.

## 4. Rapid-Recall Mnemonics

- **Chronology of State PRI Committees**: **"M-S-V-K-K"**
  - **M** = Mathur Committee (1963)
  - **S** = Sadiq Ali Committee (1964)
  - **V** = Vyas Committee (1973)
  - **K** = Kharra Committee (1990)
  - **K** = Kataria Committee (2000s)
- **Gram Sabha Mandatory Meeting Dates**: **"J-M-A-O"**
  - **J** = 26 January
  - **M** = 1 May
  - **A** = 15 August
  - **O** = 2 October
