## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Epigraphic Foundation of Rao Siha**: Rao Siha died in **Bithu village (Pali)** in 1273 CE, commemorated in the **Bithu Inscription**, NOT at Mandore or Jodhpur.
2. **Mehrangarh vs Bikaner Foundation Dates**: **Mehrangarh (Jodhpur)** was founded on **Jyeshtha Shukla 11, 1459 CE**; **Bikaner** was founded on **Akshaya Tritiya (Vaishakh Shukla 3), 1488 CE**.
3. **The Commanders of Giri-Sumel (1544 CE)**: **Jaita and Kumpa** led the heroic Rathore charge at Giri-Sumel. Do NOT confuse them with Jaimal-Patta (Chittor 1568) or Gora-Badal (Chittor 1303).
4. **Roothi Rani’s Final Resting Place**: Rani Uma De stayed at Taragarh (Ajmer) but performed Sati and has her memorial Chhatri at **Kelwa (Mewar)**.
5. **Nagaur Durbar (1570 CE) Alignment**: Rao Chandrasen **boycotted** and walked out of the Durbar; **Rao Kalyanmal of Bikaner** and Harraj Bhati of Jaisalmer accepted Mughal suzerainty.
6. **Chandrasen’s Financial Strategy**: Chandrasen pawned **Pokhran Fort** to Rawal Harraj of Jaisalmer for 1,00,000 Fadiyas; he did NOT receive funds from Bhama Shah (who financed Maharana Pratap).
7. **Panna Dhai vs Gora Dhai**: **Panna Dhai** sacrificed son Chandan to save Udai Singh of Mewar (1536); **Gora Dhai** sacrificed her child to rescue infant Ajit Singh of Marwar from Delhi (1679).
8. **Resting Place of Veer Durgadas Rathore**: Durgadas died in exile and has his memorial Chhatri on the banks of the **Shipra River in Ujjain (MP)**, NOT in Jodhpur.
9. **Karna of Rajputana vs Karna of Kaliyuga**:
   - **Rao Lunkaran of Bikaner**: "Karna of Kaliyuga" (कलिकाल का कर्ण) by Bithu Suja.
   - **Maharaja Rai Singh of Bikaner**: "Karna of Rajputana" (राजपूताना का कर्ण) by Munshi Devi Prasad.
10. **The Combatants of Matire ki Raad (1644 CE)**: Fought between **Karan Singh of Bikaner** and **Amar Singh Rathore of Nagaur** (over a watermelon vine), NOT between Bikaner and Jodhpur.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What is the historical verdict uttered by Sher Shah Suri after the Battle of Giri-Sumel (1544 CE)?
- **A**: *"For a mere handful of bajra (millet), I had almost lost the empire of Hindustan!"* (Acknowledging how close Jaita and Kumpa came to shattering his imperial army).

### Card 2
- **Q**: Who composed the celebrated Dingal work *Veli Krishan Rukmani Ri*, and what honorific did Dursa Adha bestow upon it?
- **A**: Composed by **Prithviraj Rathore** of Bikaner at Gagron Fort; poet Dursa Adha proclaimed it the **"Fifth Veda and Nineteenth Purana"**.

### Card 3
- **Q**: Which two texts by Muhnot Nainsi form the cornerstone of Rajasthan historiography, and what is *Marwar ra Pargana ri Vigat* commonly called?
- **A**: *Nainsi ri Khyat* and *Marwar ra Pargana ri Vigat*. The latter is termed the **"Gazetteer of Rajasthan"** and the **"Ain-i-Akbari of Rajasthan"** for its meticulous census and revenue data.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Under whose reign and leadership did the historic Khejarli tree sacrifice occur?
- **A**: Occurred in **1730 CE** (Bhadrapada Shukla 10) during the reign of **Maharaja Abhai Singh**; led by **Amrita Devi Bishnoi**, wherein 363 Bishnois sacrificed their lives protecting green Khejri trees.

### Card 5
- **Q**: Which Bikaner monarch established the Anup Sanskrit Library, and which imperial military decoration did Aurangzeb confer upon him?
- **A**: **Maharaja Anup Singh** (1669–1698 CE); conferred the premier imperial honorific **"Mahi Maratib"** (The Insignia of the Royal Fish) for his campaigns at Golconda and Bijapur.
