## 3. Top 10 High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Chand Baori Location Trap**: **Chand Baori is at Abhaneri (Dausa district)**. It is NOT inside Mandore Fort and was not built by a queen of Rao Chunda.
2. **Bikaner City (1488) vs. Junagarh Fort (c. 1589–1594)**: **Bikaner city** was founded by **Rao Bika in 1488 CE**; the stone **Junagarh Fort** was constructed a century later under **Maharaja Rai Singh (c. 1589–1594 CE)**.
3. **Bithu Memorial Scope (1273 CE)**: The Bithu inscription anchors **Rao Siha's memorial and death year (1273 CE)**. It does NOT prove the legendary claims of descent from Jayachandra of Kannauj or the Rashtrakutas.
4. **Giri-Sumel (1544 CE) Tactical Reality**: The Battle of Giri-Sumel was a **tactical victory for Sher Shah Suri**; Jaita and Kumpa fell in a valiant vanguard counter-attack. Sher Shah’s famous remark (*"for a handful of bajra..."*) is a reported chronicle narrative (Abbas Sarwani), not a modern audio transcript.
5. **Jodhpur City (1459) vs. Mehrangarh Evolution**: Rao Jodha founded Jodhpur in **1459 CE** on Chidiyatunk hill. Mehrangarh's surviving palaces (Phool Mahal, Moti Mahal, Sheesh Mahal) are multigenerational constructions spanning the 16th to 19th centuries.
6. **Nagaur Durbar (1570 CE) Outcomes**: **Rao Kalyanmal of Bikaner** and **Harraj of Jaisalmer** accepted Mughal suzerainty; **Rao Chandrasen of Marwar** withdrew to initiate guerrilla resistance in the hills.
7. **Karna of Rajputana vs. Karna of Kaliyuga**:
   - **Rao Lunkaran (Bikaner)**: Styled *"Karna of Kaliyuga"* (कलिकाल का कर्ण) by poet Bithu Suja.
   - **Maharaja Rai Singh (Bikaner)**: Styled *"Karna of Rajputana"* (राजपूताना का कर्ण) by historian Munshi Devi Prasad.
8. **Matire ki Raad (1644 CE) Historical Reality**: A feudal boundary clash between **Karan Singh of Bikaner** and **Amar Singh Rathore of Nagaur** over boundary villages (Jakhania & Silvi). The watermelon vine is a popular folkloric focal point of a wider territorial conflict.
9. **Khejarli Environmental Sacrifice (1730 CE)**: 363 Bishnois led by **Amrita Devi** sacrificed their lives protecting green Khejri trees during the reign of Maharaja Abhai Singh. Note that the **Amrita Devi Bishnoi Wildlife Protection Award** is a national honor instituted by the Government of India.
10. **Veer Durgadas Memorial Location**: Durgadas Rathore spent his final years in exile and died in **1718 CE**. His memorial chhatri stands on the banks of the **Shipra River in Ujjain (Madhya Pradesh)**, NOT in Jodhpur.
