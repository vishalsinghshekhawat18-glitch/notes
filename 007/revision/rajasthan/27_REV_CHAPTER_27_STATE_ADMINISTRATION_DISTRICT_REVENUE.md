## 4. High-Yield RPSC Traps & Revenue Distinctions

1. **Who Abolished and Who Revived Divisions?**:
   - Abolished in **1962** by **Mohan Lal Sukhadia**.
   - Revived on **26 January 1987** by **Harideo Joshi**.
   - 6th Division created in 1987: **Ajmer**.
   - 7th Division created in 2005: **Bharatpur**.
   - Current total: **10 Divisions** (Sikar, Pali, Banswara added in 2023).

2. **Where Do Agricultural Land Disputes Originate?**:
   - *Original Trial Court for Agricultural Tenancy Rights / Partition / Declarations*: **Court of the SDO** (Sub-Divisional Officer) under the Rajasthan Tenancy Act, 1955.
   - *Tehsildar*: Handles boundary disputes, government land trespass, and undisputed mutations under Rajasthan Land Revenue Act, 1956.
   - *Apex Revenue Court*: **Board of Revenue (Ajmer)**.

3. **Key Revenue Registers & Time Intervals**:
   - **Jamabandi (Record of Rights - ROR)**: Prepared every **5 YEARS** (Quinquennial).
   - **Khasra Girdawari (Harvest Inspection)**: Bi-annual:
     - Kharif: Begins **1 October**.
     - Rabi: Begins **1 February**.
     - Zaid: Begins **1 May**.
   - **Agricultural Census & Livestock Census (Pashu Ganana)**: Conducted every **5 YEARS** by the **Board of Revenue, Ajmer**.

4. **Collector's Trinity**:
   - Created in **1772** by **Warren Hastings**.
   - Triad: Collector (Revenue) + District Magistrate (Law & Order under BNSS) + District Development Officer (Welfare & PRIs).

## 5. Rapid-Recall Mnemonics

- **The Five CS Apex Roles**: **"A-C-H-R-D"**
  - **A** = Advisor to Chief Minister
  - **C** = Cabinet Secretary
  - **H** = Head of Civil Services
  - **R** = Residual Legatee
  - **D** = Disaster & Crisis Commander
- **The 3 New 2023 Divisions**: **"S-P-B"**
  - **S** = Sikar
  - **P** = Pali
  - **B** = Banswara (Smallest: 3 districts)
