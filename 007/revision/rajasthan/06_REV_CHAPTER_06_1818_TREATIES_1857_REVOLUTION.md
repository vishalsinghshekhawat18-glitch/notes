## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **First vs Last Treaty Sequence**: Under Lord Hastings, **Karauli** signed first on 9 Nov 1817; **Sirohi** signed last on 11 Sept 1823 (delayed due to Jodhpur's false feudal claims).
2. **States Paying Zero Khiraj**: **Jaisalmer, Bikaner, and Alwar** paid **zero tribute (*Khiraj*)** to the British.
3. **The Non-Participating Cantonments**: **Beawar** (Mer Battalion) and **Kherwara** (Mewar Bhil Corps) did **NOT** take part in the 1857 revolt.
4. **The Slogan "Chalo Dilli, Maro Firangi"**: Raised by the soldiers of the **Jodhpur Legion at Erinpura** (21 August 1857), NOT at Naseerabad.
5. **Bithoda vs Chelawas**:
   - **Bithoda (8 Sept 1857)**: Jodhpur commander Onad Singh killed; Heathcote fled.
   - **Chelawas (18 Sept 1857)**: "Battle of Black and White"; PA Mock Mason beheaded; AGG Lawrence fled.
6. **Iconography of Sugali Mata**: **10 heads and 54 arms**; patron deity of Auwa freedom fighters, now preserved in Bangar Museum, Pali.
7. **Nature of Kota Uprising**: Kota had **no British military cantonment**; it was a civil-military revolution led by **Lala Jaidayal and Mehrab Khan**.
8. **Gun Salute Adjustment**: Kota's salute was **reduced from 17 to 13 guns**; Karauli's salute was **increased from 13 to 17 guns** (for sending 2,000 troops under Madan Pal to liberate Kota).
9. **The Only King Marching Outside His State**: **Maharaja Sardar Singh of Bikaner**, who marched into Badlu (Punjab/Haryana), rewarded with 41 villages of Tibbi pargana.
10. **First Martyr of Rajasthan**: **Amar Chand Bathiya** of Bikaner, hanged at Gwalior on 22 June 1858 for financing Rani Lakshmibai and Tatya Tope.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What was the challenge thrown by Mohammad Ali Beg to British Colonel Abbott at Neemuch on 2 June 1857?
- **A**: *"Did you British remain loyal to your oath when you annexed Awadh? Why then should Indians be bound to oaths of loyalty to you?"*

### Card 2
- **Q**: Who sheltered the 40 British refugees fleeing Neemuch at Doongla village, and where did Maharana Swaroop Singh house them?
- **A**: Sheltered by peasant **Rungaram** at Doongla village; safely escorted to Udaipur, where Maharana Swaroop Singh accommodated them at **Jag Mandir Palace** in Lake Pichhola.

### Card 3
- **Q**: What were the findings of the Major Taylor Commission (1860) regarding Thakur Kushal Singh of Auwa?
- **A**: The Commission **acquitted Kushal Singh** of murder charges, ruling that Mock Mason was killed during open military combat and not through premeditated murder.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Where was Tatya Tope betrayed, who betrayed him, and where was he executed?
- **A**: Betrayed in the Paron forests by his friend **Man Singh Naruka** (Jagirdar of Narwar); publicly **hanged at Shivpuri (MP)** on 18 April 1859.

### Card 5
- **Q**: Who coined the phrase that the Rajput princes acted as "Breakwaters in the storm" during 1857?
- **A**: Governor-General **Lord Canning**, acknowledging that without the princely rulers' active military backing, British rule in India would have collapsed.
