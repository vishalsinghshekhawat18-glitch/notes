## Epistemic Hierarchy Reference
- **Baseline Fact**: Backed by statutory textbooks (RBSE Class 10), official state heritage registers (RHPPA Panoramas for Jambhoji, Dhanna, Mavji, Karni Mata), primary scripture inclusions (Dhanna and Pipa hymns in the *Guru Granth Sahib*), or university press monographs (Oxford 2023, Cambridge 2024).
- **Official Exam Convention**: Standard formulations in RPSC/RBSE syllabi (e.g. the five-peer Panchpir roster, 1485 CE / VS 1542 for Bishnoi code, 29 vs 36 rules, Ramcharan's Shahpura Ramdwara, 4-armed Kallaji).
- **Qualified Claim / Tradition**: Local oral legends (e.g. Mahmud of Ghazni dialogue, 48-minute Chirja duration, "only poet-deity", universal corpse exposure, or single-ruler temple foundations) which must be marked as hagiography rather than documentary fact.

## 3. Top 10 High-Yield Killer Traps

1. **The Panchpir Roster Boundary Trap**:
   - **Panchpir**: **Pabuji, Harbhuji, Ramdevji, Mehaji Manglia, Gogaji**.
   - **Trap**: Including **Tejaji** or **Devnarayanji** in the Panchpir. Although vastly popular across Rajasthan, neither belongs to the five peers.
2. **The Phad Accompaniment Matching**:
   - **Pabuji ki Phad**: Recited with the **Rawanhattha** (bowed string) by Nayak/Bhil Bhopas.
   - **Devnarayanji ki Phad**: Recited with the **Jantar** (plucked string with gourds) by Gurjar Bhopas.
3. **Bishnoi Code (29) vs Jasnathi Code (36)**:
   - **Sant Jambhoji / Bishnoi Panth**: **29 Rules** (*Bees + Nau*), founded at Samrathal Dhora in **1485 CE** (VS 1542).
   - **Sant Jasnathji / Jasnathi Panth**: **36 Rules**, centered at Katariyasar (Bikaner). Never swap these numbers.
4. **Calendar Conversion Error on Jambhoji (1485 vs 1482 CE)**:
   - The foundation of the Bishnoi panth occurred on Kartik Badi Ashtami, **Vikram Samvat 1542**.
   - Converting VS to CE ($1542 - 57$) yields **1485 CE**. (An official RHPPA page has a typographical error printing 1482 CE in parentheses; use 1485 CE in exams).
5. **Karni Mata & Fort Foundation Anachronism**:
   - **Fact**: Karni Mata lived in the 14th/15th century and is traditionally associated with laying foundation stones for Mehrangarh (1459) and the older Bikaner fort.
   - **Trap**: Claiming she laid the foundation of **Junagarh Fort**, which was constructed much later (1589–1594 by Raja Rai Singh).
6. **Sheetla Mata Broken Idol Exclusivity**:
   - **Fact**: Sheetla Mata is worshipped atop Seel ki Doongri (Chaksu) with cold food (**Basoda**) on Sheetla Ashtami.
   - **Trap**: Writing that Sheetla Mata is the "only deity in Rajasthan worshipped as a broken idol" (an unsupported universal).
7. **Ramdevji "Only Poet-Deity" Overstatement**:
   - **Fact**: Devotional tradition attributes the *Chaubis Vaniyan* to Ramdevji.
   - **Trap**: Asserting as fact that he was Rajasthan's *only* poet folk deity.
8. **Rajasthan Saints in the Guru Granth Sahib**:
   - The sacred hymns of two prominent Rajasthan saints are enshrined in the **Guru Granth Sahib**:
     1. **Dhanna Bhagat** (Jat cultivator from Dhuwan Kalan, Tonk).
     2. **Sant Pipa** (Khichi Rajput ruler of Gagron who renounced the throne).
9. **Ram Snehi Sampradaya Branch Founders**:
   - **Shahpura (Principal Seat)**: Founded by **Sant Ramcharan** (Phuldol festival).
   - **Ren (Nagaur)**: Founded by **Sant Dariyavji**.
   - **Singhasthal (Bikaner)**: Founded by **Sant Hariramdasji**.
   - **Khedapa (Jodhpur)**: Founded by **Sant Ramdasji**.
10. **Laldas & Religious Synthesis**:
    - **Fact**: Laldas was a 16th-century saint of Mewat revered by both Meos and Hindus (*Laldas ki Chetavniyan*).
    - **Trap**: Reducing his tradition to rigid sectarian binaries or memorising unverified initiation rituals involving donkeys and blackened faces.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What is the traditional couplet identifying Rajasthan's Panchpir, and which two prominent deities are excluded?
- **A**: *"पाबू हड़बू रामदे, मांगलिया मेहा। पाँचू पीर पधारज्यौ, गोगाजी जेहा॥"* (Pabuji, Harbhuji, Ramdevji, Mehaji Manglia, and Gogaji). **Veer Tejaji** and **Devnarayanji** are excluded from the Panchpir.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Differentiate the musical accompaniment and performer communities of the Phad traditions of Pabuji and Devnarayanji.
- **A**: 
  - **Pabuji ki Phad**: Accompanied by the **Rawanhattha**; performed by **Nayak/Bhil Bhopas**.
  - **Devnarayanji ki Phad**: Accompanied by the **Jantar**; performed by **Gurjar Bhopas**.

### Card 3
- **Q**: What is the exact chronology, founding site, and rule count of the Bishnoi panth established by Sant Jambhoji?
- **A**: Founded at **Samrathal Dhora** on Kartik Badi Ashtami, **Vikram Samvat 1542 / 1485 CE** (RHPPA's parenthetical 1482 CE is a conversion error). Enforces **29 Rules** combining ethical conduct, compassion, and strict protection of green trees and wildlife.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Which two Rajasthan Bhakti saints have their devotional hymns enshrined in the Sikh scripture *Guru Granth Sahib*?
- **A**: (1) **Dhanna Bhagat** (cultivator saint of Dhuwan Kalan, Tonk); (2) **Sant Pipa** (ruler of Gagron/Jhalawar who renounced kingship for devotion).

### Card 5
- **Q**: What are the four canonical branches (*Ramdwaras*) of the Ram Snehi Sampradaya and their respective spiritual founders?
- **A**:
  1. **Shahpura (Main Seat, Bhilwara)**: Sant Ramcharan (Phuldol festival).
  2. **Ren (Nagaur)**: Sant Dariyavji.
  3. **Singhasthal (Bikaner)**: Sant Hariramdasji.
  4. **Khedapa (Jodhpur)**: Sant Ramdasji.
