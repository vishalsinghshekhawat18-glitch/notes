## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Panchpir Exclusion Trap**: **Veer Tejaji** and **Devnarayan Ji** are beloved statewide, but they are **NOT** among the Panchpir! Only **Pabu, Harbhu, Ramdev, Mehaji, and Goga** are the Panchpir.
2. **The Sacred Relic of Harbhu Ji**: There is **no idol** at Bengti (Phalodi); devotees worship Harbhu Ji’s actual wooden bullock handcart (**Chhakada Gadi**).
3. **The "Bismillah" Inscription on a Hindu Shrine**: **Gogamedi (Nohar, Hanumangarh)** was constructed in Islamic tomb architecture by Sultan **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** with the word **"Bismillah"** engraved above the entrance.
4. **The Only Poet Folk Deity**: **Baba Ramdev Ji** is the only folk deity in Rajasthan who was also an accomplished poet, having authored **"Chaubis Vaniyan"**.
5. **The Phad vs Instrument Matrix**:
   - **Pabuji ki Phad**: Recited with **Rawanhattha** by Nayak Bhopas.
   - **Devnarayanji ki Phad**: Recited with **Jantar** by Gurjar Bhopas.
6. **Unique Devnarayan Ji Offerings**: Devotees do not worship a stone idol; they worship **large baked bricks** anointed with **Neem leaves** and curd.
7. **The Longest Devotional Song in Rajasthan**: **Jeen Mata’s Chirja (चिरजा)** (Rewasa, Sikar), sung for 48 minutes by Dhadhi bards, NOT Karni Mata or Shila Devi.
8. **The Only Broken Idol Worshiped in Rajasthan**: **Sheetla Mata** at Chaksu; worshiped with cold, stale food (**Basoda**) on Sheetla Ashtami; vehicle is a donkey.
9. **Rule Number Matrix for Saints**:
   - **Jambhoji (Bishnoi)**: **29 Rules**.
   - **Jasnathji (Jasnathi)**: **36 Rules**.
   - **Charan Das Ji**: **42 Rules**.
   - **Aai Mata**: **11 Rules**.
10. **Dadu Panthi Funerary Practice**: Followers **neither cremate nor bury** their dead; the body is left exposed in the wilderness (*Dadu Khol at Bhairana Hill*) to feed birds and animals.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What are the twin shrines of Goga Ji, and what historical event created them?
- **A**: (1) **Shirshmedi** (Dadrewa, Churu), where his severed head fell while fighting; (2) **Dhurmedi / Gogamedi** (Nohar, Hanumangarh), where his headless torso continued fighting against invaders and fell.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Why was Veer Kallaji Rathore consecrated as the "Four-Armed Deity"?
- **A**: During Akbar's 1568 siege of Chittorgarh, Kallaji hoisted the crippled Jaimal Rathore onto his shoulders; with both warriors wielding two swords simultaneously, they fought as a single four-armed deity against the Mughals.

### Card 3
- **Q**: What historical event took place at Khejarli in 1730 CE, and what famous proclamation was made?
- **A**: Under Maharaja Abhai Singh, axemen came to cut Khejri trees; **Amrita Devi Bishnoi**, her daughters, and 363 Bishnois sacrificed their lives hugging the trees, proclaiming: *"सिर साठे रूंख रहे, तो भी सस्तो जाण"* (Even if one's head is severed to save a tree, it is a cheap bargain).

### Card 4
- **Q**: Why did the disciple Rajjab Ji remain in bridegroom’s attire for his entire life?
- **A**: While on the way to his wedding, the Pathan nobleman Rajjab heard Sant Dadu Dayal preach about spiritual liberation; he renounced marriage immediately and wore his **bridegroom’s robes (*Dulhe ke Vesh*)** for the rest of his life to remind himself of spiritual awakening.

### Card 5
- **Q**: Distinguish between the incarnations of Pabu Ji, Ramdev Ji, and Devnarayan Ji.
- **A**: **Pabu Ji** is revered as the incarnation of **Lakshmana**; **Baba Ramdev Ji** as the incarnation of **Lord Krishna**; and **Bhagwan Devnarayan Ji** as the incarnation of **Lord Vishnu**.
