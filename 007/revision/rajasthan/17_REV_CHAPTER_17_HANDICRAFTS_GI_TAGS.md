## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Blue Pottery Raw Material**: Uses **NO clay/mud**; composed of **quartz powder, glass cullet, Fuller's earth (Multani Mitti), gum, and sodium salt water (Sajji)**.
2. **Kripal Singh Shekhawat's Innovation**: Invented **25 new mineral colors** beyond the traditional two-tone blue-green, founding the **"Kripal Kumbh"** style.
3. **Thewa Art Transmission Invariant**: Thewa art technique is **never taught to married daughters**; it is transmitted exclusively from father to son within the Rajsoni family of Pratapgarh.
4. **Molela Clay Additive**: The red clay from the Banas riverbed is mixed with **donkey dung (*Gadhe ki Leed*)** to provide porosity and prevent cracking during firing.
5. **Sanganeri vs Bagru Print Background**:
   - **Sanganeri**: Printed on **pure white or off-white** backgrounds with delicate floral sprays.
   - **Bagru**: Printed on **dark, earthy red-black** backgrounds using mud-resist *Dabu*.
6. **Ajrakh Print Double-Sided Feature**: Ajrakh is printed on **both sides of the fabric** in perfect geometrical registration, dominated by **indigo blue and crimson red**.
7. **Paper-Thin vs Black Pottery**:
   - **Kagzi (Paper-thin) Pottery**: Handcrafted exclusively in **Alwar**.
   - **Black Pottery**: Handcrafted in **Kota**.
8. **Badla Material & Location**: Badla is a water bottle made of **zinc (*Jasta*)** lined with cloth, manufactured exclusively in **Jodhpur**.
9. **The 2023 GI Tags of Rajasthan (Recent Exam Target!)**:
   - *Nathdwara Pichhwai Craft*
   - *Udaipur Koftgari Metal Craft*
   - *Bikaner Kashidakari Craft*
   - *Bikaner Usta Kala Craft*
   - *Jodhpur Bandhej Craft*
10. **Padma Shri Awardees in Crafts**:
    - **Hisam-ud-din Usta (1986)** $\rightarrow$ Usta Art (Bikaner).
    - **Kudrat Singh (1988)** $\rightarrow$ Minakari (Jaipur).
    - **Mohanlal Kumhar (2012)** $\rightarrow$ Molela Terracotta (Nathdwara).
    - **Mahesh Raj Soni (2015)** $\rightarrow$ Thewa Art (Pratapgarh).

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What are the five ingredients of Jaipur Blue Pottery dough, and why is it unique?
- **A**: Quartz powder, glass cullet, Multani mitti, natural gum, and sodium salt water (*Sajji*); it is unique because **no potter's clay/mud is used**.

### Card 2
- **Q**: What makes Thewa Art technically unique, and which family practices it?
- **A**: The fusion of microscopic 24-carat pure gold filigree onto colored Belgian glass; practiced exclusively by the **Rajsoni family of Pratapgarh** (pioneered by Nathu Ji Soni; Padma Shri Mahesh Raj Soni).

### Card 3
- **Q**: Distinguish between the printing methods of Sanganeri and Bagru prints.
- **A**: **Sanganeri** is printed on an **off-white/cream cotton base** with delicate floral *Bootis* using natural vegetable dyes; **Bagru** uses the **Dabu mud-resist method**, printing on dark, earthy backgrounds with **Hinglu red and Syahi black**.

### Card 4
- **Q**: What is "Kavad" in Rajasthani woodcraft, and where is it manufactured?
- **A**: A portable wooden temple shrine with multi-tiered, illustrated folding doors that are opened sequentially during storytelling; crafted in **Bassi (Chittorgarh)** by Suthar artisans.

### Card 5
- **Q**: Name the five Rajasthani craft traditions granted GI Tags in 2023.
- **A**: (1) Nathdwara Pichhwai Craft, (2) Udaipur Koftgari Metal Craft, (3) Bikaner Kashidakari Craft, (4) Bikaner Usta Kala Craft, and (5) Jodhpur Bandhej Craft.
