## 4. Top 20 Corrected Prelims Traps

1. **Thewa Art Material & Technique**: Pratapgarh; fine gold sheet (~23-carat) etched and set against coloured glass; Mahesh Raj Soni received Padma Shri in 2015. (Do not write "24-carat foil on Belgian glass" as an absolute).
2. **Thewa Transmission Lore**: Traditional knowledge is guarded within the Raj-Soni family guild; avoid asserting a rigid universal customary law that it is "never taught to daughters".
3. **Blue Pottery Composition Trap**: Jaipur Blue Pottery uses **zero clay**; its body is a **quartz/frit-based mixture** (powdered quartz, glass cullet, Multani mitti, borax/flux, gum).
4. **Blue Pottery Revivalist**: **Kripal Singh Shekhawat** (Padma Shri 1974, Shilp Guru) revived the art. The claim that he invented "exactly 25 new shades" is a popular biographical flourish, not an invariant technical requirement.
5. **Molela Terracotta Temper**: Hand-modelled votive relief plaques of local deities (Devnarayanji, Bhairav) made from Banas river clay mixed with **~5–10% donkey dung** temper to prevent shrinkage cracking.
6. **Molela Artisan**: **Mohan Lal Kumhar** received the Padma Shri in **2012** for Molela terracotta.
7. **Bikaner Usta Kala Craft**: Embossed (*Munawwati*), gilded and lacquered decoration on camel hide and palace interiors. Registered GI name is **Bikaner Usta Kala Craft** (2023). **Hisamudin Usta** received Padma Shri in **1986** (notified under lacquer work).
8. **Minakari is NOT a Registered GI**: While Jaipur is world-renowned for enamelling and **Kudrat Singh** received the Padma Shri in **1988**, no separate registered GI named "Jaipur Minakari" exists in the IP India register.
9. **Minakari vs Koftgari Mechanical Difference**:
   - **Minakari**: Coloured mineral enamel fused into precious metal channels (gold/silver).
   - **Koftgari**: Gold or silver wire/foil inlaid into cross-hatched iron or steel surfaces (**Udaipur Koftgari Metal Craft**, registered GI 2023).
10. **Badla Water Flask**: Zinc-bodied water container wrapped in felt/cloth for evaporative cooling in **Jodhpur**; not an exclusive monopoly and guarantees no exact chilled temperature.
11. **Bassi Kavad Status**: Folding portable wooden shrine with hinged doors from Bassi, Chittorgarh; **Chittorgarh Bassi Kawad Craft** is **under Examination** (App 1597), NOT a registered GI.
12. **Kathputli Institution & Wood**: Traditional string puppetry; **Bharatiya Lok Kala Mandal**, Udaipur was founded in **1952** by **Devi Lal Samar**. Puppets are carved from lightweight wood (species varies; not exclusively Adusa wood).
13. **Kota Doria Khat Structure**: Lightweight cotton-silk fabric woven in Kaithoon, Kota; standard square check (*khat*) consists of **14 yarns: 8 cotton and 6 silk**. Registered GI (App 12) valid through 2034.
14. **Sanganeri Print Background**: Fine floral block printing of Sanganer; traditionally often on pale/white grounds, but modern production employs a wide variety of ground colours. Registered GI (App 147).
15. **Bagru Print & Dabu**: Bagru hand block print utilizes the **Dabu mud-resist** technique with an earthy natural palette (alizarin red, iron-jaggery black). Registered GI (App 183).
16. **Barmer Ajrakh has NO GI**: Barmer artisans produce Ajrakh with geometric repeats in indigo and red, but **no separate Barmer Ajrakh GI** exists in IP India; double-sided printing is not universal to all pieces.
17. **Black Pottery Geographic Trap**: Black pottery is not a settled Kota-only fact; an official application exists for **Black Pottery of Sawai Madhopur** (App 1598, under Examination).
18. **Pokhran Pottery has NO GI**: Pokhran (Jaisalmer) terracotta has no registered GI entry in IP India.
19. **2026 Registered GI Additions**:
    - **Mojari Craft of Jodhpur (Rajasthan)** (App 735, registered 28 March 2026).
    - **Rajasthan Ravan Hattha (Musical Instrument)** (App 1225, registered 28 March 2026).
20. **Pending Application $\neq$ Registered GI**: Application numbers for Bassi Kavad, Lehariya, Jaipur Rajai, Lakh Chudiya, Jodhpur Safa, Jaipur Tarkashi, and Nayla Gota Patti represent pending applications, not registered GIs.

## 5. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1: Thewa Art & Chemical Specification
- **Q**: What are the base materials and technique of Thewa Art, which family preserves it, and who is its celebrated modern awardee?
- **A**: Thewa consists of an etched thin **~23-carat gold sheet** mounted against **coloured glass** to create an openwork design. It is preserved through hereditary practice by the **Raj-Soni family of Pratapgarh** (pioneered in tradition by Nathuji Soni). **Mahesh Raj Soni** received the Padma Shri in **2015**. Registered GI: *Thewa Art Work* (App 244).

### Card 2: Jaipur Blue Pottery Composition
- **Q**: Why is Jaipur Blue Pottery described as "clay-free", what are its main ingredients, and who led its twentieth-century revival?
- **A**: It uses **zero conventional potter's clay**; its body is a **quartz/frit-based mixture** composed of powdered quartz, glass cullet, Multani mitti (fuller's earth), borax/flux, and natural gum. It was revived by **Kripal Singh Shekhawat** (Padma Shri 1974, Shilp Guru). Registered GI: *Blue Pottery of Jaipur* (App 66).

### Card 3: Molela Terracotta & Donkey Dung Temper
- **Q**: What is the cultural role and material recipe of Molela Terracotta, and which master craftsman won national acclaim?
- **A**: Hand-modelled relief plaques and figures representing local deities (such as Devnarayanji and Bhairav) used for devotional/ritual purposes. Banas river clay is mixed with **~5–10% donkey dung** as a temper to improve workability and prevent shrinkage cracking during firing. **Mohan Lal Kumhar** received the Padma Shri in **2012**. Registered GI: *Molela Clay Work* (App 67).

### Card 4: Minakari vs Koftgari Differentiation
- **Q**: Disambiguate Minakari and Koftgari across technique, substrate, geographical hub, and GI status.
- **A**:
  - **Minakari**: Application of coloured mineral enamel into engraved grooves of precious metals (gold/silver); centred in **Jaipur**; **Kudrat Singh** (Padma Shri 1988); **NO registered GI**.
  - **Koftgari**: Inlaying gold or silver wire/foil into cross-hatched iron or steel surfaces (weaponry, hilts); centred in **Udaipur**; registered as **Udaipur Koftgari Metal Craft** (GI No. 747, registered 2023).

### Card 5: Bassi Kavad Mechanics & Legal Status
- **Q**: What is a Kavad, where is it crafted, and what is its official GI status?
- **A**: A portable, painted wooden shrine with multiple sequential folding hinged doors used by traditional storytellers to reveal religious episodes; made in **Bassi (Chittorgarh)** by Suthar artisans. Status: **Chittorgarh Bassi Kawad Craft** (App 1597) is **under Examination** in IP India—it is **NOT a registered GI**.

### Card 6: Kota Doria Weaving Anatomy
- **Q**: Describe the fabric composition and the structural signature of Kota Doria.
- **A**: Lightweight gossamer textile combining cotton (warp for strength) and silk (weft for sheen); woven in Kaithoon (Kota). Its signature is the **khat** (square-check grid), traditionally woven with **14 yarns: 8 cotton and 6 silk**. Registered GI: *Kota Doria* (GI No. 12, valid to 2034).

### Card 7: Sanganeri vs Bagru Print Comparison
- **Q**: Compare Sanganeri and Bagru block prints across technique, motif style, and colour palette.
- **A**:
  - **Sanganeri**: Fine floral *bootis* and delicate foliage; traditionally printed often on pale/white grounds (though modern grounds vary); uses vegetable, mineral, and synthetic dyes. Registered GI: *Sanganeri Hand Block Printing* (App 147).
  - **Bagru**: Characterized by the **Dabu mud-resist** technique; earthy colour palette dominated by alizarin red (*hinglu*) and iron-rust black (*syahi*). Registered GI: *Bagru Hand Block Print* (App 183).

### Card 8: Unregistered Famous Crafts Trap
- **Q**: Name four famous Rajasthani crafts that are widely assumed to be GI-tagged but have NO registered product GI in the IP India register.
- **A**:
  1. **Jaipur Minakari** (no registered product GI found).
  2. **Barmer Ajrakh Print** (no separate registered Barmer entry).
  3. **Pokhran Pottery** (no registered GI entry).
  4. **Akola Dabu Print** (no registered GI entry).

### Card 9: 2026 Newly Registered GIs
- **Q**: Which two Rajasthani cultural products were granted GI registration certificates on 28 March 2026?
- **A**:
  1. **Mojari Craft of Jodhpur (Rajasthan)** (App No. 735, Footwear/Handicraft).
  2. **Rajasthan Ravan Hattha (Musical Instrument)** (App No. 1225, Musical Instrument/Handicraft).

### Card 10: Product GIs vs Logo Marks
- **Q**: Why is it legally and taxonomically incorrect to count "Kota Doria Logo" or "Blue Pottery Logo" as additional craft products of Rajasthan?
- **A**: A GI logo registration is a separate intellectual property device mark intended to protect the graphic insignia used by authorized producers. It does not represent a second or distinct craft product; counting it inflates the registered product count.
