## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **The Sahibdin vs Sahibram Distinction (Deadliest RPSC Trap!)**:
   - **Sahibdin**: 17th-century master of **Mewar (Udaipur)** under Jagat Singh I (painted *Ragamala*, *Gitagovinda*, *Arsha Ramayana*).
   - **Sahibram**: 18th-century master of **Dhundhar (Jaipur)** under Ishwari Singh & Pratap Singh (pioneered **Aadmakad life-sized portraits**).
2. **Earliest Dated Illustrated Manuscript**: **Shravaka Pratikramana Sutra Churni (1260 CE)**, painted by **Kamalchandra** at Ahar under Guhil king **Rawal Tejasimha**, NOT Maharana Kumbha.
3. **The Two Celebrated Female Painters**: **Kamala** and **Ilaichi** are master artists of the **Nathdwara Sub-style** (Mewar School), NOT Kishangarh or Bundi.
4. **"Mona Lisa of India" Nomenclature**: Coined by British scholar **Eric Dickinson**, NOT Karl Khandalavala or Anand Coomaraswamy.
5. **Postal Stamp on Bani Thani**: Issued on **5 May 1973** by the Government of India in the denomination of **20 paise**.
6. **The Signature Diagnostic Ornament of Bani Thani**: The **Besari (बेसरी)**, a delicate pearl nose-ring/stud, NOT a necklace or earrings.
7. **"Chandni Raat ki Sangeet Goshthi" Artist**: Painted by **Amarchand (अमरचंद)** of Kishangarh, NOT Nihal Chand.
8. **The School That Systematically Signed Artworks**: **Bikaner Sub-style** — Muslim master artists called **Ustad** inscribed their names, father's names, and precise completion dates on the reverse.
9. **Micro-miniature Painting on Elephant Tusk (Ivory)**: Practiced exclusively in the **Alwar Sub-style** under Maharaja Vinay Singh.
10. **Royal Women Hunting Depiction**: The exclusive hallmark of the **Kota Sub-style** — queens and princesses are depicted shooting lions and wild boars in the jungles.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What was "Chiteron ki Ovari", who established it, and what was its popular local name?
- **A**: An official royal art studio established in Udaipur City Palace by **Maharana Jagat Singh I** (1628–1652 CE); popularly celebrated across Rajasthan as **"Tasveeraan ro Karkhano"** (तस्वीराँ रो कारखानो).

### Card 2
- **Q**: Who was the master artist of the 1605 Chavand Ragamala, and during whose reign was it painted?
- **A**: Master painter **Nasiruddin (Nisardi)**, painted on 10 April 1605 CE at the independent hill capital of Chavand during the reign of **Maharana Amar Singh I**.

### Card 3
- **Q**: What are the twin distinct artistic traditions of Bikaner, and how do they differ?
- **A**: (1) **Usta Art**: Gilded lacquer and gold relief work on treated **camel hide** (pioneered by Hisam-ud-din Usta); (2) **Matheerna Art**: Religious murals and wooden *Patra* book-cover paintings practiced exclusively by the local **Jain community of Bikaner**.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Which painting school is known as the "School of Birds and Animals", and what is its most famous mural monument?
- **A**: The **Bundi Sub-style** (Hadoti School); celebrated for its dense jungle vegetation, lotuses, and fauna. Its masterpiece is the **Chitrashala (Rang Mahal)** commissioned by **Maharao Ummed Singh** inside Garh Palace, Bundi.

### Card 5
- **Q**: Why is the Jaisalmer painting school unique among all Rajasthani schools?
- **A**: It is the **only school in Rajasthan that remained completely untouched by Mughal or British influences** (100% indigenous); its defining portraiture subject is **Moomal**, the princess of Lodharva.
