## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Chhoti Teej vs Badi Teej Dates & Locations**:
   - **Chhoti Teej (Hariyali Teej)**: **Shravan Shukla Tritiya** $\rightarrow$ Celebrated in **Jaipur**.
   - **Badi Teej (Kajli / Satoodi Teej)**: **Bhadrapada Krishna Tritiya** $\rightarrow$ Celebrated in **Bundi**.
2. **Gangaur Without Isar**: Celebrated exclusively in **Jaisalmer** on **Chaitra Shukla Chaturthi** (only Gauri's sawari is brought out).
3. **The Dhinga Gavar "Betmar" Festival**: Celebrated in **Jodhpur & Udaipur** on **Vaishakh Krishna Tritiya**; women in disguise strike unmarried men with canes.
4. **Charan Bathing Taboo at Kolayat**: Charans **never bathe in or visit the Kapil Muni Fair at Kolayat Lake (Bikaner)** because Karni Mata’s foster son Lakhan drowned in the lake.
5. **Baneshwar Fair Rivers Confluence**: The Triveni Sangam is of the **Som, Mahi, and Jakham** rivers (NOT Chambal or Banas).
6. **Tooth vs Nose Ornament Confusion (Top RPSC Trap!)**:
   - **Chup (चूँप)** = **Tooth** ornament (gold pin inserted in tooth).
   - **Chop (चोप)** = **Nose** ornament (clove-shaped nose pin).
7. **Two-Finger Ring vs Thumb Ring**:
   - **Damna (दामणा)** = Ring worn on **two fingers simultaneously**.
   - **Arsi (आरसी)** = Ring worn on the **thumb**, featuring a small mirror.
8. **Son vs Daughter Pomcha Colors**:
   - Birth of a **Son** $\rightarrow$ **Yellow Pomcha (पीला पोमचा)**.
   - Birth of a **Daughter** $\rightarrow$ **Pink Pomcha (गुलाबी पोमचा)**.
9. **Mosar vs Josar**:
   - **Mosar (मौसर / नुकता)** = Post-death funeral feast.
   - **Josar (जोसर)** = Funeral feast hosted by an individual **during their own lifetime**!
10. **"Veli Krishan Rukmani Ri" Authorship & Title**: Composed by **Prithviraj Rathore of Bikaner** at Gagron Fort; hailed by Dursa Arha as the **"5th Veda and 19th Purana"**.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What specific historical event led to the permanent discontinuation of public Gangaur processions in Bundi?
- **A**: During Maharao Budh Singh’s reign, his brother **Jodh Singh drowned in Lake Jait Sagar** while carrying the Gangaur idol, giving birth to the proverb: *"हाड़ा ले डूब्यो गणगौर"*.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Distinguish between the linguistic origin, geographical seat, and traditional composers of Dingal and Pingal.
- **A**: **Dingal** is rooted in Western Rajasthan (Marwari base), characterized by harsh martial consonants, and composed by **Charan** bards for heroic *Veer Rasa*. **Pingal** is rooted in Eastern Rajasthan (Brajbhasha synthesis), characterized by melodious meters, and composed by **Bhat** bards.

### Card 3
- **Q**: What are the four major ear ornaments commonly asked in RPSC exams?
- **A**: **Toti** (टोटी), **Oganiya** (ओगण्या), **Surliya** (सुरलिया), and **Peepal Patra** (पीपल पत्रा).

### Card 4
- **Q**: What is the literary context of Kanhaiyalal Sethia’s "Pāthal aur Peethal"?
- **A**: A dramatic Rajasthani poetic dialogue where **Pāthal** represents **Maharana Pratap** (unyielding hero facing hardship in the Aravalis) and **Peethal** represents **Prithviraj Rathore of Bikaner** (court poet in Akbar's durbar who inspires Pratap not to surrender).

### Card 5
- **Q**: Why is Vijaydan Detha ("Bijji") celebrated in modern Rajasthani folklore?
- **A**: Co-founder of Rupayan Sansthan (Borunda), he compiled the monumental **14-volume Rajasthani folklore compendium "Batān ri Phulwari"**; his short story *Duvidha* was adapted into acclaimed national films (*Duvidha* and *Paheli*).
