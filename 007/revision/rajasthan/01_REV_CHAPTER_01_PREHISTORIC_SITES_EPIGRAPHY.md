## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Ganeshwar District**: Now in **Neem Ka Thana**, NOT Sikar.
2. **Bairat District**: Now in **Kotputli-Behror**, NOT Jaipur.
3. **Tilwara District**: Now in **Balotra**, NOT Barmer.
4. **Kiln-Fired Bricks in Chalcolithic**: Discovered at **Gilund** (Rajsamand), NOT at Ahar or Ganeshwar.
5. **Kalibangan Drainage System**: Made of scooped **wooden logs** (लकड़ी की नाली), NOT baked brick sewers like Mohenjo-daro.
6. **Mother Goddess at Kalibangan**: **Completely absent**; replaced by a row of 7 community fire altars on the Citadel.
7. **Earliest Inscription in Rajasthan**: **Barli Inscription (443 BCE)** near Ajmer, NOT Ghosundi or Bhabru.
8. **Bhabru Edict Present Location**: Preserved in the **Asiatic Society Museum, Kolkata**, NOT in Rajasthan.
9. **Rana Hammir's Title "Vishama Ghati Panchanan"**: Inscribed in the **Kumbhalgarh Prashasti** (1460 CE), NOT the Kirti Stambha Prashasti.
10. **Tatanagar Coin Hoard**: Rairh yielded **3,075 punch-marked silver coins**, NOT gold or copper coins.
