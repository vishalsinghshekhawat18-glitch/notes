## 3. Top 10 High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Capital Sequence Trap**: The safe chronological sequence is **Dausa (early seat) ➔ Amer (long-term capital) ➔ Jaipur (1727 planned capital)**. Do NOT insert Jamwa Ramgarh as a formal successive capital; it was an important religious/cult center (Jamway Mata), not a documented sovereign capital.
2. **Mansab Terminology Trap**: Mughal mansab ranks consist of **zat** (personal status) and **sawar** (cavalry obligation). Writing *"7,000 Jat"* for Raja Man Singh I is a major terminology error; it is **7,000 zat**.
3. **Generational Marriage Trap**: Do not conflate the two Mughal-Kachhwaha royal marriages:
   - **Raja Bharmal's daughter** (Harkha Bai / *Mariam-uz-Zamani*) married **Akbar in 1562** (mother of Jahangir).
   - **Raja Bhagwant Das's daughter** (Maan Bai / *Shah Begum*) married **Prince Salim (Jahangir) in 1585** (mother of Khusrau).
4. **Mariam-uz-Zamani vs. Jodha Bai**: "Mariam-uz-Zamani" was the imperial title of Harkha Bai of Amer. "Jodha Bai" historically referred to **Jagat Gosain (Mani Bai)**, daughter of Mota Raja Udai Singh of **Jodhpur**. Avoid calling the 1562 bride "Jodha Bai" without explaining the historiographical naming confusion.
5. **Barah Kothri System Founder**: Established in regional tradition by **Prithviraj Kachhwaha** (early 16th century) for his twelve sons, NOT by Sawai Jai Singh II.
6. **Jantar Mantar Chronology**: **Delhi (1724 CE)** was the first observatory built. **Jaipur (1728–1734 CE)** is the largest and best-preserved, inscribed as a **UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2010**.
7. **Hurda Conference (1734 CE) Leadership**: **Sawai Jai Singh II** of Jaipur was the prime convenor/organizer; **Maharana Jagat Singh II of Mewar** was appointed the formal President.
8. **Man Sagar Lake vs. Jal Mahal**: Man Sagar Lake was formed by damming a seasonal channel in the Amer landscape; Rajasthan Tourism associates the **Jal Mahal hunting lodge with Madho Singh I**, with restorations under subsequent rulers. They are not a single, simultaneous construction event.
9. **Suraj Mal and the Third Battle of Panipat (1761)**: Suraj Mal did **NOT** fight in the Maratha ranks at Panipat. He advised Sadashivrao Bhau, **withdrew from the coalition before the battle** after his advice was rejected, and subsequently provided extensive humanitarian relief to fleeing Maratha survivors.
10. **Lohagarh Fort Sieges & Ashtadhatu Gates**:
    - Lohagarh repulsed Lord Lake's assaults in **1805 CE**, but was successfully breached and captured in **1826 CE** by Lord Combermere.
    - Jawahar Singh brought the Ashtadhatu gates from the **Delhi Red Fort in 1765 CE**. Tracing them back to Alauddin Khalji's 1303 sack of Chittorgarh is an unverified popular legend (Shah Jahan constructed Delhi Red Fort centuries later in 1638–48).
