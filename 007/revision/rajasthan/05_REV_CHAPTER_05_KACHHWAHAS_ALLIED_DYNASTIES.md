## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Kuldevi vs Ishta Devi of Jaipur Rulers**: **Jamway Mata** is the dynastic **Kuldevi**; **Shila Devi** (installed in Amer Fort by Man Singh I from Jessore, Bengal) is the **Ishta Devi**.
2. **First Capital of the Kachhwahas**: **Dausa** was the first capital (1137 CE by Dulherai), NOT Amer. Amer became capital in **1207 CE under Kakil Dev**.
3. **The True Identity of "Mariam-uz-Zamani"**: Mariam-uz-Zamani was **Harkha Bai** (daughter of Bharmal of **Amber**). *Jodha Bai* was **Mani Bai / Jagat Gosain** (daughter of Mota Raja Udai Singh of **Jodhpur**).
4. **Founder of the Barah Kothri System**: Established by **Prithviraj Kachhwaha** (1503–1527 CE) for his 12 sons, NOT by Sawai Jai Singh II.
5. **Highest Hindu Mansabdar under Akbar**: Only **Mirza Raja Man Singh I** and **Mirza Aziz Koka** held the premier rank of **7,000 Jat and 6,000 Sawar**.
6. **Treaty of Purandar (1665 CE) Fort Division**: Shivaji surrendered **23 forts** to the Mughals, retaining **12 forts**.
7. **Jantar Mantar Chronology**: **Delhi (1724 CE)** was the first built; **Jaipur (1728–1734 CE)** is the largest and UNESCO-listed. Mathura is now in ruins.
8. **Hurda Conference (1734 CE) Leadership**: **Sawai Jai Singh II** was the organizer/planner; **Maharana Jagat Singh II of Mewar** was the formal President.
9. **The Half Sāka (Ardha Sāka) of Jaisalmer (1550 CE)**: **Kesariya took place**, but **Jauhar was not performed** because the Bhatis completely annihilated Amir Ali and won the battle.
10. **Lohagarh Fort's Ashtadhatu Gates**: Originally looted by Alauddin Khalji from **Chittorgarh** to Delhi (1303); brought by **Maharaja Jawahar Singh** from Delhi to Lohagarh in 1765 CE.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What are the 5 storeys of Hawa Mahal (top to bottom), and who was its chief architect?
- **A**: Architect: **Ustad Lal Chand** (1799 CE). Storeys (top to bottom): (5) Hawa Mandir, (4) Prakash Mandir, (3) Vichitra Mandir, (2) Ratan Mandir, (1) Sharad Mandir.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Which couplet composed by Bihari Lal persuaded Mirza Raja Jai Singh to resume state governance?
- **A**: *"नहिं पराग नहिं मधुर मधु, नहिं विकास यहि काल। अली कली ही सौं बंध्यो, आगे कौन हवाल॥"* (Rewarding him with one gold mohur per doha for the 713-doha *Bihari Satsai*).

### Card 3
- **Q**: What is the historical origin of the princely state of Jhalawar, and who created it?
- **A**: Created in **1838 CE by the British** by partitioning Kota to appease the descendants of Kota's brilliant de facto administrator **Zalim Singh Jhala**, installing his grandson **Madan Singh Jhala** (the last princely state formed in Rajasthan).

### Card 4
- **Q**: Why was Lohagarh Fort (Bharatpur) impregnable against British siege artillery under Lord Lake in 1805?
- **A**: It featured massive double mud ramparts that safely absorbed exploding cannonballs without crumbling, surrounded by the deep Sujan Ganga water moat fed by Ruparel and Banganga rivers.

### Card 5
- **Q**: What were the core recommendations and layout principles of Jaipur city (1727 CE)?
- **A**: Designed on 18 November 1727 by Bengali architect **Vidyadhar Bhattacharya** using a gridiron model divided into **9 rectangular sectors (Nau Chowkris)** aligned to the Nav-Graha, with broad 111-ft-wide main avenues intersecting at right angles (*Chaupars*).
