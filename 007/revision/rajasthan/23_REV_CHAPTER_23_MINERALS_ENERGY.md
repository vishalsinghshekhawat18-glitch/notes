## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Wollastonite & Jasper 100% Monopolies**:
   - Rajasthan is the **100% sole producer** of Wollastonite (Belka Pahar, Sirohi) and Jasper (Jodhpur).
2. **World's Richest Zinc Mine**:
   - **Rampura Agucha (Bhilwara)** is the world's richest zinc deposit, NOT Zawar (Zawar is the oldest historic mine, discovered under Rana Lakha).
3. **Uranium Discovery Venue**:
   - Rajasthan's mega uranium deposit was discovered in **2022 at Rohil (Khandela tehsil, Sikar district)** with 12 million tonnes of ore, developed by UCIL.
4. **Tungsten Location Trap**:
   - India's largest historic tungsten deposit is located at **Revat Hill, Degana (Nagaur)**, NOT in Jaisalmer or Bikaner!
5. **Pachpadra Refinery Equity Sharing**:
   - **74% HPCL : 26% Government of Rajasthan**, NOT 50:50!
   - Capacity is **9 MMTPA**.
   - Located in **Balotra district** (formerly Barmer).
6. **Crude Oil Discovery Date**:
   - Mangala oilfield was discovered in **January 2004**; commercial production started on **29 August 2009**.
7. **World's Largest Solar Park**:
   - **Bhadla Solar Park** is located in **Phalodi district** (formerly Jodhpur) with an installed capacity of **2,245 MW**.
8. **First Wind Power Project**:
   - Commissioned on **10 April 1999 at Amar Sagar (Jaisalmer)**; Jaisalmer is celebrated as the "Wind City" (*पंखों की नगरी*).
9. **M-Sand Policy Launch Date**:
   - The Rajasthan M-Sand Policy was officially launched on **25 January 2021** to replace riverbed Bajri with crushed rock sand.
10. **Bansi Paharpur Sandstone Connection**:
    - The pink-red sandstone used in the construction of the **Ayodhya Ram Mandir** was quarried from **Bansi Paharpur (Bharatpur district)**.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What are the four Petroliferous Basins of Rajasthan, and how many districts do they span?
- **A**: 
  1. **Barmer-Sanchore Basin** (Barmer, Balotra, Sanchore, Jalore — Mangala, Bhagyam, Aishwarya).
  2. **Jaisalmer Basin** (Jaisalmer — Natural gas: Tanot, Dandewala, Manhera Tibba).
  3. **Bikaner-Nagaur Basin** (Bikaner, Nagaur, Churu, Sriganganagar, Hanumangarh — Baghewala, Poonam).
  4. **Rajasthan Shelf / Vindhyan Basin** (Kota, Baran, Bundi, Jhalawar, Bhilwara, Chittorgarh).
  - Spans over **1.5 lakh sq km across 14 districts**.

### Card 2
- **Q**: State the corporate structure, capacity, location, and equity sharing of the Pachpadra Oil Refinery.
- **A**: 
  - **Entity**: HPCL Rajasthan Refinery Limited (HRRL).
  - **Equity Ratio**: **74% HPCL : 26% Government of Rajasthan**.
  - **Location**: Pachpadra, **Balotra district**.
  - **Capacity**: **9 MMTPA**.
  - **Standard**: India's first integrated BS-VI greenfield refinery-cum-petrochemical complex with a Petrochemical Fluidized Catalytic Cracker (PFCC).

### Card 3
- **Q**: Why is Bhadla Solar Park internationally renowned, where is it located, and what is its capacity?
- **A**: Located in **Bhadla, Phalodi district**. With a total installed capacity of **2,245 MW** developed across 4 phases, it is recognized as the **world's largest contiguous operational solar park**.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Name the 4 Strategic Business Units (SBU-PC) of Rajasthan State Mines & Minerals Ltd (RSMML).
- **A**: 
  1. **SBU-PC Rock Phosphate**: Jhamarkotra (Udaipur).
  2. **SBU-PC Lignite**: Giral and Kasnau-Matasukh (Barmer/Nagaur).
  3. **SBU-PC Gypsum**: Bikaner (Jamsar).
  4. **SBU-PC Limestone**: Sanu (Jaisalmer).

### Card 5
- **Q**: Distinguish between the metallic mineral deposits of Morija-Banjaol, Khetri, Rampura Agucha, and Degana.
- **A**: 
  - **Morija-Banjaol** (Jaipur Rural): Largest hematite iron ore deposit in Rajasthan.
  - **Khetri** (Neem Ka Thana/Jhunjhunu): Premier copper belt in India (HCL).
  - **Rampura Agucha** (Bhilwara): World's richest open-pit zinc-lead deposit (HZL).
  - **Degana** (Nagaur): Revat Hill, India's largest historic tungsten (wolframite) mine.
