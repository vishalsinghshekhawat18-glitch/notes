## 3. High-Yield RPSC Traps & Elimination Rules

```
                      SELECTION COMMITTEE COMPARISONS
                                     │
           ┌─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┐
           │                                                   │
    RSHRC (4 Members)                                   RSIC (3 Members)
           │                                                   │
  ├── Chief Minister (Chair)                          ├── Chief Minister (Chair)
  ├── Home Minister                                   ├── Leader of Opposition
  ├── Speaker of Vidhan Sabha                         └── 1 Cabinet Minister (Nominated by CM)
  └── Leader of Opposition
```

1. **Who Removes Whom? (Critical Trap)**:
   - *RPSC*: Appointed by **Governor**, Removed by **PRESIDENT**.
   - *SEC*: Appointed by **Governor**, Removed by **PRESIDENT** (like High Court Judge).
   - *RSHRC*: Appointed by **Governor**, Removed by **PRESIDENT**.
   - *RSIC*: Appointed by **Governor**, Removed by **GOVERNOR** (after Supreme Court inquiry).
   - *Lokayukta*: Appointed by **Governor**, Removed by **GOVERNOR** (after legislative address).

2. **Lokayukta Purview Traps**:
   - **NOT Covered**: Chief Minister, Judges of High Court & Subordinate Courts, RPSC Chairman/Members, Assembly Speaker/Dy Speaker, MLAs, Sarpanch and Ward Panchs, Accountant General.
   - **COVERED**: Ministers, Pradhans & Up-Pradhans, Pramukhs & Up-Pramukhs, Mayors & Municipal Board Chairpersons, State Civil Servants, PSUs/Boards Chairmen and employees.

3. **Time Limitation Periods**:
   - **RSHRC**: Cannot inquire into matters older than **1 YEAR** from date of incident.
   - **Lokayukta**: Cannot inquire into matters older than **5 YEARS** from date of occurrence.

4. **Penal Limits under RTI (RSIC)**:
   - Penalty: **Rs. 250 per day**.
   - Maximum Penalty Cap: **Rs. 25,000**.

5. **6th SFC Allocation Formula**:
   - Total Net Own Tax Devolution: **6.75%**.
   - PRIs vs. ULBs Split: **75.10%** : **24.90%** (2011 Census population basis).
   - Within PRIs: Gram Panchayats (**75%**), Panchayat Samitis (**20%**), Zila Parishads (**5%**).

## 4. Rapid-Recall Acronyms & Mnemonics

- **RSHRC Selection Committee**: **"C-H-S-L"**
  - **C** = Chief Minister
  - **H** = Home Minister
  - **S** = Speaker of Vidhan Sabha
  - **L** = Leader of Opposition
- **RSIC Selection Committee**: **"C-L-M"**
  - **C** = Chief Minister
  - **L** = Leader of Opposition
  - **M** = Minister (Cabinet Minister nominated by CM)
- **RPSC Article Sequence**:
  - `315` = Creation of PSCs
  - `316` = Appointment & Tenure (6/62)
  - `317` = Removal (President + SC inquiry)
  - `318` = Regulations & Service conditions
  - `319` = Post-retirement employment ban
  - `320` = Functions
  - `322` = Expenses charged on Consolidated Fund
  - `323` = Annual Report to Governor
