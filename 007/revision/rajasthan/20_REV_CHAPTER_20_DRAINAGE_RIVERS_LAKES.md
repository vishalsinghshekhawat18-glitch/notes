## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Jojari River Uniqueness**:
   - The **ONLY right-bank tributary of the Luni**.
   - The **ONLY Luni tributary that does not originate from the Aravallis** (originates in the flat plains of Pondlu, Nagaur).
2. **Balotra Salinity Transition**:
   - Luni water is fresh/sweet from Nag Pahar all the way to **Balotra (200+ km)**.
   - It turns brackish/saline downstream of Balotra due to subterranean halite soils, capillary leaching, and dyeing effluents.
3. **Longest River Distinctions**:
   - Longest river *flowing through* Rajasthan: **Chambal (965 km)**.
   - Longest river *flowing entirely and exclusively within Rajasthan*: **Banas (480 km)**.
   - Longest purely *inland* river within Rajasthan: **Kantli (100 km)**.
4. **Mahi Tropic of Cancer & Power Sharing**:
   - Mahi cuts the Tropic of Cancer ($23^\circ 30'\text{ N}$) **twice** (inverted 'U' course).
   - In Mahi Bajaj Sagar, capital is shared 45:55 between Rajasthan and Gujarat, but **100% of the 140 MW power is reserved exclusively for Rajasthan**!
5. **Dam Superlatives**:
   - Tallest Dam in Rajasthan: **Jakham Dam (81 metres)** in Pratapgarh.
   - Longest Dam in Rajasthan: **Mahi Bajaj Sagar Dam (3,109 metres)** in Banswara.
   - Largest Dam by Water Storage: **Rana Pratap Sagar Dam** in Rawatbhata (Chittorgarh).
   - Largest Earthen/Mud Dam: **Panchana Dam** (Karauli, fed by 5 rivulets).
6. **Kota Barrage Produces Zero Power**:
   - Of the 4 Chambal dams, **Kota Barrage produces 0 MW of electricity**; it is purely an irrigation diversion structure.
   - **Gandhi Sagar Dam is NOT in Rajasthan** (located in Mandsaur, MP).
7. **The Ayad to Bedach Name Change**:
   - Originates from Gogunda as **Ayad** (nourishing ancient Ahar civilization).
   - Enters **Udaisagar Lake**; downstream of Udaisagar Lake, it is rechristened as **Bedach**.
8. **Rundhit Sarita (Severed River)**:
   - **Banganga** originates at Bairat and historically reached Yamuna, but now terminates in the Bharatpur wetlands before reaching its master stream — classified as a *Rundhit Sarita*.
9. **Salt Grades: Pachpadra vs Didwana**:
   - **Pachpadra**: 98% pure NaCl, supreme edible grade.
   - **Didwana**: High sodium sulphate ($Na_2SO_4$), strictly **non-edible industrial grade** for leather and dye industries.
10. **Updated Districts (50-District Lens)**:
    - **Jaisamand Lake** is now in **Salumbar district** (NOT Udaipur).
    - **Pachpadra Lake** is now in **Balotra district** (NOT Barmer).
    - **Didwana Lake** is now in **Didwana-Kuchaman district** (NOT Nagaur).
    - **Bairat / Banganga origin** is now in **Kotputli-Behror district** (NOT Jaipur).

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What are the three Triveni Sangams associated with the Banas River system?
- **A**: 
  1. **Bigod (Bhilwara)**: Banas + Bedach + Menal.
  2. **Rajmahal (Tonk/Kekri border)**: Banas + Khari + Dai.
  3. **Rameswaram Ghat (Sawai Madhopur)**: Banas + Chambal + Seep.

### Card 2
- **Q**: What makes the Jojari River unique in the hydrology of Western Rajasthan?
- **A**: It is the only tributary of the Luni River that joins from the right bank, the only one that originates from the non-Aravalli plains (Pondlu, Nagaur), and the only one flowing in a north-to-southwest trajectory.

### Card 3
- **Q**: Name the 4 cascading dams of the Chambal Valley Multipurpose Project, their sequence, and their power capacities.
- **A**: 
  1. **Gandhi Sagar Dam** (Mandsaur, MP; 1960; 115 MW).
  2. **Kota Barrage** (Kota, Rajasthan; 1960; 0 MW — pure irrigation).
  3. **Rana Pratap Sagar Dam** (Rawatbhata, Chittorgarh; 1970; 172 MW — largest storage reservoir in Rajasthan).
  4. **Jawahar Sagar Dam** (Kota/Bundi; 1972; 99 MW — peak load pick-up dam).

### Card 4
- **Q**: What is the Raj Prashasti, where is it located, and what records does it contain?
- **A**: The world's largest stone inscription, engraved on 25 polished black marble slabs on the *Nauchowki ki Pal* of **Rajsamand Lake**. Composed by Ranchhod Bhatt Telang in 24 cantos and 1,106 Sanskrit verses, it chronicles Mewar's history from Bappa Rawal to Maharana Raj Singh, including the 1615 Mughal-Mewar Treaty and dam construction expenses.

### Card 5
- **Q**: Compare the salt harvesting methods and chemical nature of Sambhar, Pachpadra, and Didwana lakes.
- **A**: 
  - **Sambhar**: 8.7% of India's salt; harvested via *Kyār* pans and *Reshta* wind crusts by Sambhar Salts Ltd.; edible.
  - **Pachpadra**: 98% pure $NaCl$; highest culinary purity in India; harvested by traditional Kharwal caste using branches of the *Moroli* bush.
  - **Didwana**: High in Sodium Sulphate ($Na_2SO_4$); non-edible industrial brine harvested in pits called *Dêval*; used for leather tanning and chemicals (Rajasthan State Chemical Works).
