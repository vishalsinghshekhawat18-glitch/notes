## 3. 25 Lethal Exam Traps & Elimination Rules

```
                       PM-MITRA MEGA TEXTILE PARKS — ELIMINATION TRAP

      ┌──────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┐
      │                                                                             │
```

1. **The PM-MITRA False Allocation Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Marking Kakani (Jodhpur) as an approved "PM-MITRA Mega Textile Park".
   - *Truth*: The Union Ministry of Textiles selected exactly **7 sites**: Virudhunagar (TN), Warangal (Telangana), Navsari (Gujarat), Kalaburagi (Karnataka), Dhar (MP), Lucknow (UP), and Amravati (Maharashtra). **Rajasthan was NOT selected.** Rajasthan has other state/RIICO textile parks, but ZERO PM-MITRA parks.

2. **The Export Target Year Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Believing Rajasthan's ₹1.5 lakh crore export target is set for "2028" or "2030".
   - *Truth*: The *Rajasthan Export Promotion Policy 2024* formally sets the target of **₹1.5 lakh crore in merchandise exports by 2029**.

3. **The #1 Export Commodity Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Assuming *Textiles* or *Gems & Jewellery* or *Handicrafts* is Rajasthan's largest export.
   - *Truth*: In the published 2023–24 policy mix, **Engineering Goods is Rank #1 at 16.60%**, followed by Gems & Jewellery (11.18%), Metals (9.80%), Textiles (8.80%), and Handicrafts (7.90%).

4. **The MSME Act 2023 Section Reference Trap**:
   - *Trap*: An assertion that the 2023 amendment changed "Section 4" of the MSME Act 2019.
   - *Truth*: The *Rajasthan MSME (Amendment) Act, 2023 (Act No. 10 of 2023)* amended **Sections 6 and 7**, replacing "period of three years" with **"period of five years"**.

5. **The MSME Portal FAQ Inconsistency Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Claiming the portal FAQ and Gazette Act are perfectly harmonized.
   - *Truth*: The Raj Udyog Mitra FAQ contains an internal contradiction—stating in one answer that the exemption lasts 5 years, while describing certificate validity as 3 years in another. The statutory Gazette amendment (Act 10 of 2023) is the controlling legal authority.

6. **The RSIC 4 ICDs Location Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Including **Kota** among the 4 RSIC Inland Container Depots.
   - *Truth*: The official RSIC schedule lists 4 ICDs: **Jaipur (Sanganer), Bhiwadi, Bhilwara, and Jodhpur (Basni)**. Kota does NOT have an operational RSIC ICD in this schedule.

7. **The RIICO Area Count Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Quoting the obsolete "390+" or "350" industrial areas developed by RIICO.
   - *Truth*: RIICO's current official profile records **472 developed industrial areas**.

8. **The 1980 RIICO Bifurcation Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Stating that minerals were separated from RSIMDC directly into "RSMML" on 1 January 1980.
   - *Truth*: On 1 January 1980, RSIMDC bifurcated into **RIICO** and **RSMDC** (Rajasthan State Mineral Development Corporation). RSMDC merged into RSMML much later (in 2003).

9. **The IIP 2025–26 Annual Index Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Memorising an old or single-month figure like 153.29 as the 2025–26 annual index.
   - *Truth*: The DES Rajasthan portal reports the **2025–26 annual General IIP as 158.85** (Manufacturing 187.93, Electricity 168.12, Mining 90.17).

10. **The Dual GSVA Percentage Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Conflating sub-sectoral share of *Total State GSVA* with share *within Industry GSVA*.
    - *Truth*: Manufacturing is **10.76% of Total State GSVA**, but **40.52% within the Industry Sector**. Construction is **9.30% of Total GSVA**, but **35.02% within Industry**.

11. **The RIPS 2024 Blanket Entitlement Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Stating that every investing enterprise in Rajasthan receives 75% SGST reimbursement and 100% exemptions for 7 years.
    - *Truth*: RIPS 2024 is a **tiered, conditional menu**. An investor chooses between Investment Subsidy, Capital Subsidy, or Turnover-linked incentives. Exemption rates and caps vary by scale, sector, location, and package.

12. **The MLUPY Interest Subsidy Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Confusing an "interest subsidy" with an "interest-free loan".
    - *Truth*: MLUPY subsidizes 8% (up to ₹25L), 6% (₹25L–₹5Cr), and 5% (₹5Cr–₹10Cr) of the interest charged by the lending bank. If the bank charges 11%, the borrower still pays the remaining 3%–6%.

13. **The DMIC Distance & District Claims Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Quoting exact claims of "548 km, 36.4%, 22 districts, 58% area, travel time 48 to 14 hrs" as unassailable statutory facts.
    - *Truth*: These exact composite metrics lack current authoritative substantiation. Safe answers identify the strategic alignment with the Western DFC and name the priority project nodes (**KBNIR** and **JPMIA**) with project-status discipline.

14. **The HRRL Pachpadra Status Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Treating project dedication or SCOD as proof of full commercial operation.
    - *Truth*: Dedication was scheduled for **21 April 2026**; SCOD was designated as **1 July 2026**; but sustained commercial ramp-up is a separate operational milestone that must be dated. Capacity: **9 MMTPA refinery + 2.4 MMTPA petrochemicals**; Cost: **₹79,459 Crore**.

15. **The HRRL Equity Ratio Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Marking GoR equity as 50% or 24%.
    - *Truth*: Equity is strictly **74% HPCL : 26% Government of Rajasthan**.

16. **The Crude Oil Ranking False Comparison Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Comparing Rajasthan's crude oil production directly against "offshore Bombay High" to assert Rajasthan is "Rank #2 state".
    - *Truth*: Bombay High is an offshore central facility, not a state. Rajasthan is a leading **onshore crude producer** in India; state rankings must cite dated PPAC production tables.

17. **The Mineral Varieties Count Nuance**:
    - *Trap*: Declaring 81 varieties as the sole correct number and penalising 82, or vice versa.
    - *Truth*: The **DMG portal reports 81 varieties (57 commercially exploited)**, while the **Economic Review 2025–26 uses the phrasing "nearly 82 varieties (57 in commercial production)"**. Acknowledge both official vintages.

18. **The 34 Major Mineral Blocks Auction Milestone**:
    - *Trap*: Assuming Rajasthan auctioned only minor mineral quarries.
    - *Truth*: In FY 2024–25, Rajasthan auctioned **34 major mineral blocks**, reported by the Economic Review as the highest number among all states in India.

19. **The Mines Department Revenue Distinction**:
    - *Trap*: Confusing the full-year target with actual collection.
    - *Truth*: For 2025–26, the revenue target is **₹12,980 Crore**; collections up to December 2025 were **₹6,857.97 Crore** (a 9-month progress figure, not full-year).

20. **The BIP Investment Focus Threshold Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Claiming BIP legally prohibits projects below ₹10 crore from investing in Rajasthan.
    - *Truth*: BIP's published institutional facilitation focus is on proposals **above ₹10 Crore**; smaller enterprises are facilitated by District Industries & Commerce Centres (DICCs).

21. **The RajNivesh Administrative Throughput Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Treating ₹42,693.70 crore approved applications in 2025 as "realised investment".
    - *Truth*: These are **approved investment proposals** (throughput), not completed capital assets or commissioned factories.

22. **The "Export Volume" vs "Export Value" Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Referring to ₹83,704.24 crore as "export volume".
    - *Truth*: ₹83,704.24 crore is **merchandise export value** in rupees. "Volume" refers to physical tonnage or units.

23. **The RFC Act Basis Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Believing RFC was established under a Rajasthan state legislative assembly act.
    - *Truth*: RFC was established under the central **State Financial Corporations Act, 1951** (on 17 January 1955).

24. **The Constant vs Current GSVA Growth Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Stating industry share is higher at current prices than at constant prices.
    - *Truth*: Industry share at **constant 2011–12 prices is 28.21%**, whereas at **current prices it is 26.55%**.

25. **The Project Status Discipline Trap**:
    - *Trap*: Treating proposed industrial clusters (e.g., medical device park, fintech park, ceramic park) as fully functional identical entities.
    - *Truth*: Always distinguish: *Proposed site ≠ Approved master plan ≠ Land acquired ≠ Civil work in progress ≠ Dedicated/inaugurated ≠ Fully operational units paying taxes*.

## 5. 10 Active Recall Flashcards

### FC-01: Industry Sector GSVA Weight (2025–26 AE)
- **Question**: What is Rajasthan's Industry Sector GSVA at current and constant prices, and what are its sub-sector shares?
- **Answer**: 
  - Current prices: **₹4,54,486 Crore (26.55% of total GSVA)**.
  - Constant (2011–12) prices: **₹2,47,872 Crore (28.21% of constant GSVA)**.
  - Sub-sectors (% of total GSVA): Manufacturing **10.76%** (40.52% of sector), Construction **9.30%** (35.02%), Mining **3.30%** (12.42%), Utilities **3.20%** (12.04%).

### FC-02: State IIP Series (2025–26)
- **Question**: What is the base year of Rajasthan's IIP, and what are the 2025–26 annual index values?
- **Answer**: Base year: **2011–12 = 100**. Annual index 2025–26: **General Index = 158.85**; Manufacturing = **187.93**; Electricity = **168.12**; Mining = **90.17**.

### FC-03: RIICO Institutional History & Footprint
- **Question**: Trace RIICO's origin, date of bifurcation, and current number of industrial areas.
- **Answer**: Incorporated **28 March 1969** as RSIMDC; bifurcated on **1 January 1980** into RIICO and RSMDC. Developed industrial areas: **472 areas** (current official profile).

### FC-04: MSME Act Exemption Window & 2023 Amendment
- **Question**: Which sections were amended by the Rajasthan MSME (Amendment) Act, 2023, and what was the change?
- **Answer**: The amendment (Act No. 10 of 2023) substituted "five years" for "three years" in **Sections 6 and 7**, granting eligible new MSMEs a **5-year moratorium** on state approvals and routine inspections upon filing a Declaration of Intent on RajUdyogMitra.

### FC-05: MLUPY Interest Subsidy Structure
- **Question**: State the maximum loan ceiling and interest subsidy tiers under MLUPY.
- **Answer**: Maximum loan ceiling: **₹10 Crore**. Tiers: **8%** subsidy for loans up to ₹25 Lakh; **6%** subsidy for loans above ₹25 Lakh up to ₹5 Crore; **5%** subsidy for loans above ₹5 Crore up to ₹10 Crore.

### FC-06: RIPS 2024 Design & Validity
- **Question**: When was RIPS 2024 notified, until when is it operative, and what are its 3 core pillars?
- **Answer**: Notified **8 October 2024**; operative until **31 March 2029** (or until a new policy). Three priority pillars: **Green Growth, Export Promotion, and Capability Development**. Offers tiered choices between Investment Subsidy (up to 75% SGST), Capital Subsidy, or Turnover-linked incentives.

### FC-07: Merchandise Export Value & Target
- **Question**: What was Rajasthan's merchandise export value in FY 2023–24, and what is the target under the 2024 Policy?
- **Answer**: FY 2023–24 merchandise exports: **₹83,704.24 Crore**. Policy target: **₹1.5 Lakh Crore by 2029** (under Rajasthan Export Promotion Policy 2024).

### FC-08: Top Export Commodities Mix
- **Question**: List the top 5 merchandise export product groups from Rajasthan according to the 2024 Policy.
- **Answer**: (1) **Engineering Goods: 16.60%**; (2) **Gems & Jewellery: 11.18%**; (3) **Ferrous & Non-Ferrous Metals: 9.80%**; (4) **Textiles: 8.80%**; (5) **Handicrafts: 7.90%**.

### FC-09: HRRL Pachpadra Key Parameters
- **Question**: State the joint venture equity, capacity, cost, and 2026 milestones of HRRL.
- **Answer**: Equity: **74% HPCL : 26% Government of Rajasthan**. Capacity: **9 MMTPA refinery + 2.4 MMTPA petrochemicals**. Revised cost: approximately **₹79,459 Crore** (April 2026). Scheduled Commercial Operation Date (SCOD): **1 July 2026**; Dedication planned: **21 April 2026**.

### FC-10: PM-MITRA Mega Textile Park Status
- **Question**: Is there an approved PM-MITRA park at Kakani (Jodhpur)?
- **Answer**: **NO.** The Union Government approved exactly 7 sites across India (Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Gujarat, Karnataka, MP, UP, Maharashtra). **Rajasthan has ZERO approved PM-MITRA parks.**
