## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **High Court Inauguration Date**:
   - Formally inaugurated on **29 August 1949 at Jodhpur**, NOT on 26 January 1950!
2. **First Chief Justice vs First CJI from Rajasthan**:
   - First Chief Justice of Rajasthan HC: **Justice Kamal Kant Verma** (1949).
   - First native Rajasthani (from Jodhpur) to become Chief Justice of India: **Justice R.M. Lodha** (41st CJI in 2014).
3. **High Court Judges: Salary vs Pension Trap**:
   - **Salaries**: Charged on the **Consolidated Fund of the State** (Rajasthan).
   - **Pensions**: Charged on the **Consolidated Fund of India** (Article 112(3)(d)), because High Court judges are liable to inter-state transfer!
4. **Jaipur Bench Abolition & Restoration**:
   - Abolished in **1958** on the recommendation of the **P. Satyanarayan Rao Committee**.
   - Restored and commenced functioning on **31 January 1977** (under Presidential Order dated 8 December 1976).
5. **Retirement Age**:
   - High Court Judges: **62 Years**, NOT 65 Years! (Supreme Court judges retire at 65).
6. **Oath Administration**:
   - High Court Judge takes oath before the **GOVERNOR of the State** (Article 219), NOT the Chief Justice of India!
7. **First Woman Judge of Rajasthan High Court**:
   - **Justice Kanta Bhatnagar** was appointed in **1978**; she later became the 1st Chairperson of the Rajasthan State Human Rights Commission (SHRC) in 2000.
8. **Lok Adalat Award Appeal**:
   - **NO APPEAL LIES against an award passed by a Lok Adalat** in any court; it is final, binding, and deemed to be a civil court decree.
9. **First Gram Nyayalaya Venue**:
   - Inaugurated on **27 November 2009 at Bassi (Jaipur district)** under the *Gram Nyayalayas Act, 2008*.
10. **Death Sentence Confirmation**:
    - A sentence of death passed by a Sessions Judge **CANNOT be executed without mandatory confirmation by at least two judges (Division Bench) of the High Court** under Section 366 CrPC / Section 407 BNSS.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: Trace the history of the Jaipur Bench of the Rajasthan High Court from 1949 to 1977.
- **A**: Initially set up as a temporary circuit bench in 1949; abolished in 1958 on the recommendation of the P. Satyanarayan Rao Committee to centralize the judiciary at Jodhpur; restored as a Permanent Bench on 31 January 1977 under Section 51(2) of the States Reorganisation Act, 1956.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Why is a High Court Judge's salary charged on the State Fund while the pension is charged on the Central Fund?
- **A**: High Court judges serve the state administration during active tenure (hence salary from Consolidated Fund of the State). However, because judges are liable to inter-state transfers across different High Courts under Article 222, their retirement pension is charged on the Consolidated Fund of India to avoid inter-state apportionment conflicts.

### Card 3
- **Q**: What are the core statutory attributes of an award passed by a Lok Adalat?
- **A**: Under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987:
  1. It is legally deemed to be a Decree of a Civil Court.
  2. It is final and binding on all parties.
  3. No appeal lies against it in any court.
  4. Full court fees deposited are refunded upon settlement.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Where was the first Gram Nyayalaya established in Rajasthan, when, and who presides over it?
- **A**: Established on **27 November 2009 at Bassi (Jaipur district)** under the Gram Nyayalayas Act, 2008. Presided over by a **Nyayadhikari**, holding the rank and qualifications of a Judicial Magistrate of the First Class (JMFC).

### Card 5
- **Q**: What is the sanctioned strength of the Rajasthan High Court, and where is its new complex located?
- **A**: Sanctioned strength: **50 Judges** (38 Permanent + 12 Additional). The new High Court complex is located at **Jhalamand, Jodhpur**, inaugurated on **7 December 2019** by President Ram Nath Kovind.
