## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Size Limits on Council of Ministers**:
   - Maximum limit: **15% of Assembly (30 Ministers in Rajasthan)** including the CM.
   - Minimum floor: **12 Ministers** (an absolute number under Article 164(1A), NOT 12%!).
2. **Four Governors Who Died in Harness**:
   - Exactly four Governors died in office: **Darbara Singh (1998), Nirmal Chandra Jain (2003), S.K. Singh (2009), and Prabha Rau (2010)**.
3. **Only Governor Dismissed**:
   - **Dr. Raghukul Tilak** (1981) is the only Governor of Rajasthan to be dismissed by the President.
4. **Pardoning Power Limit (Article 161)**:
   - The Governor can suspend, remit, or commute a death sentence, but **CANNOT pardon a Death Sentence (*मृत्युदंड*)** — power reserved exclusively for the President (Article 72).
5. **Ordinance Lifespan (Article 213)**:
   - An ordinance promulgated by the Governor ceases to operate **6 WEEKS after the reassembly of the Legislative Assembly**, NOT 6 months!
6. **First Elected vs Nominated Chief Minister**:
   - First *Nominated* CM: **Hiralal Shastri** (1949).
   - First *Elected* CM: **Tikaram Paliwal** (1952).
   - Only person *Both Nominated & Elected*: **Jai Narayan Vyas**.
7. **Shortest vs Longest CM Tenures**:
   - Longest: **Mohan Lal Sukhadia** (16 years, 8 months across 4 terms).
   - Shortest: **Hiralal Devpura** (barely **16 days** in Feb–March 1985).
8. **President's Rule Extremes**:
   - Shortest: **1967 (44 days)** under Governor Dr. Sampurnanand.
   - Longest: **1992–1993 (354 days)** under Governor Dr. M. Chenna Reddy.
9. **Evolution of Assembly Seats**:
   - 1952: **160 seats** $\rightarrow$ 1957: **176 seats** $\rightarrow$ 1967: **184 seats** $\rightarrow$ 1977: **200 seats** (frozen till 2026).
10. **Reserved Seats Breakdown**:
    - **SC Reserved**: **34 seats** (17%).
    - **ST Reserved**: **25 seats** (12.5%).
    - General / Unreserved: **141 seats**.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What are the numerical maximum and minimum limits for the Rajasthan Council of Ministers under Article 164(1A)?
- **A**: 
  - **Maximum**: **30 Ministers** (15% of 200 MLAs), including the Chief Minister.
  - **Minimum**: **12 Ministers** (an absolute integer, NOT 12%), including the Chief Minister.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Name the four Governors of Rajasthan who died while serving in office.
- **A**: 
  1. **Darbara Singh** (May 1998)
  2. **Nirmal Chandra Jain** (September 2003)
  3. **Shailendra Kumar Singh (S.K. Singh)** (December 2009)
  4. **Prabha Rau** (April 2010)

### Card 3
- **Q**: Outline the triggers and tenures of the 1st and 4th episodes of President's Rule in Rajasthan.
- **A**: 
  - **1st Episode (1967)**: 44 days (shortest); triggered by a hung assembly post-1967 elections; Governor Sampurnanand, CM Mohan Lal Sukhadia.
  - **4th Episode (1992–1993)**: 354 days (longest); triggered by the dismissal of the BJP state government post-Babri Masjid demolition; Governor Dr. M. Chenna Reddy, CM Bhairon Singh Shekhawat.

### Card 4
- **Q**: What historic constitutional trifecta occurred in Rajasthan in the 12th Legislative Assembly (2003–2008)?
- **A**: For the first time in Indian history, all three apex constitutional positions in a state were held simultaneously by women:
  1. **Governor**: Pratibha Devisingh Patil
  2. **Chief Minister**: Vasundhara Raje
  3. **Vidhan Sabha Speaker**: Sumitra Singh

### Card 5
- **Q**: What is the seat reservation distribution in the 200-member Rajasthan Legislative Assembly?
- **A**: 
  - **Scheduled Castes (SC)**: **34 seats** (17.0%).
  - **Scheduled Tribes (ST)**: **25 seats** (12.5%).
  - **General / Unreserved**: **141 seats** (70.5%).
  - Total strength is frozen at 200 until after the 2026 census (84th Amendment).
