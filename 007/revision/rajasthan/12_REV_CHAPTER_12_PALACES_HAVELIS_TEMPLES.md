## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Shekhawati UNESCO Status Trap**:
   - *Trap*: Claiming Shekhawati havelis are an "inscribed UNESCO World Heritage Site".
   - *Epistemic Fact*: As of **June 2026**, *The Painted Haveli Towns of Shekhawati* is on India's **UNESCO Tentative List** (a preliminary nomination step covering 14 towns), NOT the inscribed World Heritage List.
2. **84-Pillared Cenotaph (Bundi) Dedication**:
   - *Trap*: Stating it was built for a foster-brother.
   - *Epistemic Fact*: Official **Rajasthan Tourism** specifies it was commissioned by Rao Raja Aniruddha in memory of **Deva, his beloved wet nurse**.
3. **Hawa Mahal Builder & Ventilation Physics**:
   - *Trap*: Crediting Sawai Jai Singh II or describing an "adiabatic refrigeration loop".
   - *Epistemic Fact*: Built in **1799 by Sawai Pratap Singh** (architect **Ustad Lal Chand Ustad**). Its 953 openings provided **passive climate-responsive ventilation** and enabled royal women in *purdah* to view street festivals.
4. **Chand Baori vs Raniji ki Baori Dimensions**:
   - *Trap*: Unconditionally calling Chand Baori "the deepest stepwell on Earth" or confusing its stats with Raniji ki Baori.
   - *Epistemic Fact*: Chand Baori has **~3,500 steps** and ~13 tiers; **Raniji ki Baori in Bundi is recorded at 46 metres (150 ft) depth** by Rajasthan Tourism.
5. **Khadin vs Baori vs Tanka**:
   - *Trap*: Treating all traditional water systems as stepwells.
   - *Epistemic Distinction*: A **Khadin** is an *agricultural runoff-harvesting earthen embankment* (pioneered by Paliwals); a **Baori** is a *descending stepped well to groundwater*; a **Tanka** is a *covered cistern for storing rainwater*.
6. **Salim Singh ki Haveli Dating & Stories**:
   - *Trap*: Treating 1815 as a certified single founding year and the dismantled top storeys as verified history.
   - *Epistemic Fact*: Rajasthan Tourism dates it broadly to the **first half of the 18th century**; its ship-like shape (*Jahaz Mahal*) is famous, but the story of royal orders dismantling upper storeys is local lore.
7. **Patwon ki Haveli Cluster History**:
   - *Trap*: Assuming all five mansions were erected simultaneously in 1805.
   - *Epistemic Fact*: Guman Chand Patwa commissioned the first mansion **c. 1805**; the five-mansion cluster evolved over successive decades.
8. **The Gaitore Necropolis Exception**:
   - *Trap*: Assuming every Kachhwaha ruler from Sawai Jai Singh II onwards is commemorated at Gaitore.
   - *Epistemic Fact*: **Sawai Ishwari Singh** is the sole exception; his cenotaph is in the City Palace precinct near **Talkatora / Jai Niwas Bagh** (suicide to escape Maratha debt).
9. **Three "Khajurahos" of Rajasthan**:
   - *"Khajuraho of Rajasthan"*: **Kiradu Temples (Barmer)**.
   - *"Mini Khajuraho / Khajuraho of Hadoti"*: **Bhanddevara Temple (Ramgarh, Baran)**.
   - *"Khajuraho of Mewar"*: **Ambika Mata Temple (Jagat, Udaipur)**.
10. **Ranakpur Pillar Count & Sightline Claims**:
    - *Trap*: Asserting as certified fact that "all 1,444 pillars are completely unique and no pillar ever blocks any sightline from any angle".
    - *Epistemic Fact*: **1,444 pillars** is the official Rajasthan Tourism convention; the universal zero-obstruction sightline claim is an unverified popular exaggeration.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What is the current UNESCO heritage status of the Shekhawati havelis?
- **A**: *The Painted Haveli Towns of Shekhawati* was submitted to India's **UNESCO Tentative List on 1 June 2026** as a serial nomination across 14 towns. It is NOT yet inscribed on the official World Heritage List.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Who was the 84-Pillared Cenotaph in Bundi built for, according to official Rajasthan Tourism?
- **A**: Commissioned by **Rao Raja Aniruddha Singh** in memory of **Deva, his beloved wet nurse** (not foster-brother), featuring 84 stone pillars and an enshrined Shiva lingam on the lower level.

### Card 3
- **Q**: What are the verified facts regarding Hawa Mahal's date, builder, architect, and ventilation design?
- **A**: Built in **1799 CE** by **Sawai Pratap Singh**; architect **Ustad Lal Chand Ustad**; features 5 storeys and **953 openings** (state convention); functioned as a **passive climate-responsive design** allowing royal *purdah* women to observe city pageants.

### Card 4
- **Q**: How does a Khadin differ functionally from a Baori?
- **A**: A **Khadin** is an agricultural runoff-harvesting system using earthen bunds to capture seasonal water and saturate desert soils for crop cultivation; a **Baori** is a deep subterranean stepped well providing direct human access to fluctuating groundwater.

### Card 5
- **Q**: What are the key verified facts for Chand Baori and Raniji ki Baori?
- **A**: **Chand Baori (Abhaneri, Dausa)** dates to the 8th–9th century (Raja Chanda, Nikumbha), featuring **~3,500 symmetrical steps** across ~13 tiers. **Raniji ki Baori (Bundi)** was built in **1699 CE** by **Rani Nathavati Ji** and is recorded at **46 metres depth**.
