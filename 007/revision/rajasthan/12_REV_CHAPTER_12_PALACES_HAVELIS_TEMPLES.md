## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **The Three "Khajurahos" of Rajasthan**:
   - *"Khajuraho of Rajasthan"*: **Kiradu Temples (Barmer)** (Someshwara temple).
   - *"Mini Khajuraho / Khajuraho of Hadoti"*: **Bhanddevara Temple (Ramgarh, Baran)**.
   - *"Khajuraho of Mewar"*: **Ambika Mata Temple (Jagat, Udaipur)**.
2. **Hawa Mahal's Storey Sequence**: Bottom-to-top order: (1) Sharad Mandir, (2) Ratan Mandir, (3) Vichitra Mandir, (4) Prakash Mandir, (5) Hawa Mandir. It has **no foundation**, sitting on a narrow base.
3. **84 vs 80 vs 32 Pillars Chhatri Matrix**:
   - **84 Pillars**: Bundi (Aniruddha Singh for Dhaabhai Deva).
   - **80 Pillars**: Alwar (Vinay Singh for Moosi Maharani & Bakhtawar Singh).
   - **32 Pillars**: (a) Ranthambore (Hammiradeva for Jaitrasimha's 32-yr rule), (b) Mandalgarh (Jagannath Kachhwaha).
4. **The Gaitore Necropolis Exception**: All Kachhwaha rulers of Jaipur are cremated at **Gaitore**, EXCEPT **Sawai Ishwari Singh**, whose cenotaph is in the City Palace compound at **Talkatora** (committed suicide to avoid Maratha humiliation).
5. **Architects of Dilwara vs Ranakpur**:
   - *Vimal Vasahi (Dilwara)*: Architect = **Kirti Dhara** (Patron: Vimal Shah, 1031 CE).
   - *Luna Vasahi (Dilwara)*: Architect = **Shobhana Deva** (Patron: Vastupala & Tejapala, 1230 CE).
   - *Ranakpur*: Architect = **Depaka** (Patron: Dharanashah, 1439 CE).
6. **Ala-Gila / Arayash vs Secco**: *Ala-Gila* (*Arayash / Mora-Kashi*) is **Fresco Buono** (pigments applied onto **wet slaked lime plaster** and polished with agate stones). *Secco* is applied onto dry plaster with glue.
7. **The Two Sons' Memorial Stepwell**: **Raniji ki Baori** in Bundi was built by **Rani Nathavati** in 1699; **Naulakha Baori** in Dungarpur was built by **Rani Premal Devi** in 1586.
8. **Largest Living Fort vs Largest Haveli**:
   - Living Fort: **Chittorgarh**.
   - Haveli with most windows: **Surana Haveli (Churu)** with **1,111 doors and windows** (*Hawa Mahal of Churu*).
9. **Oldest Dated Temple in Rajasthan**: **Sheetleshwar Mahadev at Jhalrapatan (689 CE)**, NOT Osian (8th–9th century).
10. **Oldest Surviving Jain Temple in Western India**: **Mahavira Temple at Osian** (Jodhpur Rural), built during Pratihara King Vatsaraja's reign.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What makes the engineering of the 1,444 pillars in Ranakpur Jain Temple historically unique?
- **A**: Designed by architect **Depaka** in 1439 CE, every single one of the **1,444 pillars** is carved with a distinct motif (no two are identical), and their placement is engineered so that **not a single pillar blocks the direct view** of the central four-faced Adinatha idol from the surrounding colonnades.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Distinguish between the patrons and deities of Dilwara's Vimal Vasahi and Luna Vasahi temples.
- **A**: **Vimal Vasahi** was built in 1031 CE by Vimal Shah and is dedicated to the 1st Tirthankara **Adinatha** (Architect: Kirti Dhara). **Luna Vasahi** was built in 1230 CE by brothers Vastupala and Tejapala and is dedicated to the 22nd Tirthankara **Neminatha** (Architect: Shobhana Deva; features *Derani-Jethani Gokhla*).

### Card 3
- **Q**: Explain the bioclimatic mechanics of Hawa Mahal's 953 Jharokhas.
- **A**: Designed in 1799 by Ustad Lal Chand, the narrow latticed perforations (*Jalis*) utilize the **Venturi effect**: air accelerates through the small openings, causing a pressure drop that cools incoming summer air (adiabatic expansion cooling), ensuring chilled interior rooms.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Why is Chand Baori in Abhaneri celebrated as a masterpiece of geometric hydraulic design?
- **A**: Built in the 8th–9th century by Raja Chand, it plunges **100 feet across 13 storeys** with **3,500 narrow steps** arranged in perfectly symmetrical double-diamond optical illusion grids where descending and ascending steps cannot be duplicated on the same flight.

### Card 5
- **Q**: What are the "Three Khajurahos" of Rajasthan, and in which districts are they located?
- **A**: (1) **Kiradu Temples** (Barmer) = *"Khajuraho of Rajasthan"*; (2) **Bhanddevara Temple** (Ramgarh, Baran) = *"Mini Khajuraho / Khajuraho of Hadoti"*; (3) **Ambika Mata Temple** (Jagat, Udaipur) = *"Khajuraho of Mewar"*.
