## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **The Three "Khajurahos" of Rajasthan Matrix**:
   - *"Khajuraho of Rajasthan"*: **Kiradu Temples (Barmer)** (Someshwara temple) `[Conv]`.
   - *"Mini Khajuraho / Khajuraho of Hadoti"*: **Bhanddevara Temple (Ramgarh, Baran)** `[Conv]`.
   - *"Khajuraho of Mewar"*: **Ambika Mata Temple (Jagat, Udaipur)** `[Conv]`.
2. **Hawa Mahal's Storey Sequence**:
   - Bottom-to-top order: (1) **Sharad Mandir**, (2) **Ratan Mandir**, (3) **Vichitra Mandir**, (4) **Prakash Mandir**, (5) **Hawa Mandir** `[Core]`. It has **no deep basement**, rising from a street-level stone base.
3. **84 vs 80 vs 32 Pillars Cenotaph Matrix**:
   - **84 Pillars**: Bundi (Aniruddha Singh for foster brother Dhaabhai Deva) `[Core]`.
   - **80 Pillars**: Alwar (Vinay Singh for Moosi Maharani & Bakhtawar Singh) `[Core]`.
   - **32 Pillars (Dual)**: (a) Ranthambore (Hammiradeva for father Jaitrasimha's 32-year rule / *Nyaya ki Chhatri*); (b) Mandalgarh (for Mughal commander Jagannath Kachhwaha) `[Core]`.
4. **The Gaitore Necropolis Exception**:
   - All Kachhwaha rulers of Jaipur are cremated at **Gaitore**, EXCEPT **Sawai Ishwari Singh**, whose cenotaph is inside the City Palace precinct at **Talkatora** (committed suicide to avoid Maratha humiliation) `[Core]`.
5. **Architects of Dilwara vs Ranakpur**:
   - *Vimal Vasahi (Dilwara)*: Architect = **Kirti Dhara** (Patron: Vimal Shah, 1031 CE) `[Core]`.
   - *Luna Vasahi (Dilwara)*: Architect = **Shobhana Deva** (Patron: Vastupala & Tejapala, 1230 CE) `[Core]`.
   - *Ranakpur Chaumukha*: Architect = **Depaka** (Patron: Dharanashah, 1439 CE) `[Core]`.
6. **Ala-Gila / Arayash Chemistry**:
   - *Ala-Gila* (*Arayash / Mora-Kashi*) is **Fresco Buono** (pigments applied onto **wet slaked lime plaster** and polished with agate stones) `[Core]`. *Secco* is applied onto dry plaster with organic binders.
7. **The Two Sons' / Consorts' Memorial Stepwells**:
   - **Raniji ki Baori** in Bundi was built by **Rani Nathavati** in 1699 CE `[Core]`.
   - **Naulakha Baori** in Dungarpur was built by **Rani Premal Devi** in 1586 CE `[Core]`.
8. **Living Fort vs Haveli Superlatives**:
   - Largest Living Fort: **Chittorgarh** `[Core]`.
   - Haveli with most windows: **Surana Haveli (Churu)** with **1,111 doors and windows** (*Hawa Mahal of Churu*) `[Conv]`.
9. **Oldest Dated Temple in Rajasthan**:
   - **Sheetleshwar Mahadev at Jhalrapatan (689 CE / VS 746)** `[Core]`, NOT Osian (8th–9th century).
10. **Oldest Surviving Jain Temple in Western India**:
    - **Mahavira Temple at Osian (Jodhpur Rural)**, built during Pratihara King Vatsaraja's reign (8th c. CE) `[Core]`.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What makes the engineering of the 1,444 pillars in Ranakpur Jain Temple historically unique?
- **A**: Designed by architect **Depaka** in 1439 CE `[Core]`, every single one of the **1,444 pillars** is carved with a distinct motif (no two are identical), and their placement is engineered so that **not a single pillar blocks the direct view** of the central four-faced Adinatha idol from the surrounding colonnades.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Distinguish between the patrons, dates, architects, and deities of Dilwara's Vimal Vasahi and Luna Vasahi temples.
- **A**: **Vimal Vasahi** was built in 1031 CE by Vimal Shah (architect: **Kirti Dhara**) and is dedicated to the 1st Tirthankara **Adinatha** `[Core]`. **Luna Vasahi** was built in 1230 CE by brothers Vastupala and Tejapala (architect: **Shobhana Deva**) and is dedicated to the 22nd Tirthankara **Neminatha**; it features the carved *Derani-Jethani ka Gokhla* `[Core]`.

### Card 3
- **Q**: Explain the functional and passive-ventilation design of Hawa Mahal's 953 Jharokhas.
- **A**: Designed in 1799 CE by Ustad Lal Chand `[Core]`, the 953 stone *Jharokhas* and latticed *Jalis* funnel desert breezes through narrow openings to accelerate airflow (chimney effect), creating passive cooling during peak summer heat. Functionally, it enabled secluded royal *Pardanashin* women (*Zanana*) to observe street festivals without breaking purdah.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Why is Chand Baori in Abhaneri celebrated as a masterpiece of geometric hydraulic design?
- **A**: Built in the 8th–9th century by Nikumbha king Raja Chand `[Core]`, it plunges **13 tiers (~65–100 ft)** with **approx. 3,500 narrow steps** arranged in perfectly symmetrical double-diamond optical grids where descending and ascending paths are separated to prevent pilgrim congestion.

### Card 5
- **Q**: What are the "Three Khajurahos" of Rajasthan, and in which districts are they located?
- **A**: (1) **Kiradu Temples** (Barmer) = *"Khajuraho of Rajasthan"*; (2) **Bhanddevara Temple** (Ramgarh, Baran) = *"Mini Khajuraho / Khajuraho of Hadoti"*; (3) **Ambika Mata Temple** (Jagat, Udaipur) = *"Khajuraho of Mewar"* `[Standard RAS Exam Convention]`.
