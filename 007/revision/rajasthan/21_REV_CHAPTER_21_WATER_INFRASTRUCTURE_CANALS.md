## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **IGNP Headworks vs Storage Dam**:
   - IGNP originates at the **Harike Barrage** (diversion weir at the confluence of Satluj and Beas in Punjab).
   - It does **NOT originate at Bhakra Dam** (Bhakra is an upstream storage reservoir on the Satluj).
2. **Zero Irrigation from Rajasthan Feeder**:
   - The first **204 km** of the canal (Rajasthan Feeder) permits **ZERO direct irrigation**; it serves purely as a sealed transit conduit.
   - Irrigation commences strictly at **Masitawali Head (Hanumangarh)**.
3. **The Rawatsar Branch Anomaly**:
   - Of the 9 IGNP branches, **8 branches are on the Right (Western) Bank**, but **Rawatsar Branch is the ONLY branch on the Left Bank**.
   - All 7 lift canals are exclusively on the Left Bank.
4. **IGNP Tail Point Specifics**:
   - Terminal point of the Main Canal: **Mohangarh (Jaisalmer)**.
   - Absolute tail-end of the extended canal command (Baba Ramdev Sub-Branch): **Ghadra Road (Barmer)**.
5. **Narmada Canal Mandatory Micro-Irrigation**:
   - Pressurized drip and sprinkler irrigation is **statutorily mandatory for 100% of the command area**.
   - Traditional flood irrigation is illegal in the Narmada command.
6. **Gang Canal Construction Material**:
   - Built in 1927 with **hydraulic lime-mortar concrete and kankar masonry (*चूना-कंकड़*)**, NOT Portland cement!
7. **The Cause of the Sem Problem**:
   - Waterlogging (*Sem*) in Badopal (Hanumangarh) is caused by an **impermeable subterranean gypsum/calcrete hardpan** at 5–15 m depth, NOT just surplus canal water.
8. **Jhalra Does NOT Harvest Direct Runoff**:
   - A **Jhalra** collects **subterranean seepage (*अंतःस्रवण जल*)** from an adjacent higher lake or tank, NOT surface rainwater runoff directly.
9. **The Tripartite Water Taxonomy**:
   - *Palār Paani*: Direct rainwater runoff.
   - *Pātāl Paani*: Deep groundwater (wells/tube-wells).
   - *Rejāṇi Paani*: Trapped subterranean moisture above the gypsum band, tapped by Kuis.
10. **Modified PKC-ERCP Funding Pattern**:
    - Designated a **National Project** on **28 January 2024** with a **90% Central Government grant : 10% State funding ratio**.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What is the structural difference between an IGNP Branch and an IGNP Lift Canal?
- **A**: 
  - **Branches (शाखाएं)**: Flow westward (Right Bank) by natural **gravity** because the terrain slopes downward (exception: Rawatsar Branch on Left Bank).
  - **Lift Canals (लिफ्ट नहरें)**: Flow eastward (Left Bank); water must be **mechanically lifted by 30 to 60 metres** using electric turbine pumps because the terrain rises toward the Aravalli foothills.

### Card 2
- **Q**: Trace the history, dimensions, and entry point of the Rajasthan Feeder canal.
- **A**: Originates at Harike Barrage (Punjab) at the confluence of Satluj and Beas. Total length: 204 km (170 km in Punjab/Haryana, 34 km in Rajasthan). Enters Rajasthan at Kharakhera village (Hanumangarh) and terminates at Masitawali Head. Zero irrigation is allowed along its length.

### Card 3
- **Q**: What is a Khadin, who developed it, and how does it facilitate crop cultivation without irrigation?
- **A**: Developed in 15th-century Jaisalmer by Paliwal Brahmins. An earthen bund (*Dhora*) impounds monsoon runoff in a valley between gravel mounds. After soaking for 2–3 months and depositing fertile silt, the standing water is released or recedes. The retained subsoil moisture is sufficient to mature a high-yield winter Rabi crop (Wheat, Gram, Mustard) without any subsequent irrigation.

### Card 4
- **Q**: Name the oldest and longest IGNP lift canal, its old name, and its popular title.
- **A**: **Kanwarsain Lift Canal** (formerly *Bikaner-Lunkaransar Lift Canal*). Irrigates Bikaner and Sriganganagar; acclaimed as the **"Lifeline of Bikaner"**.

### Card 5
- **Q**: What are the key terms of the Modified PKC-ERCP Link agreement signed on 28 January 2024?
- **A**: Tripartite MoU between Ministry of Jal Shakti, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh integrating the Parbati-Kalisindh-Chambal (PKC) link with ERCP. Declared a National Project with 90% Central funding : 10% State funding, providing drinking water to 21 eastern districts up to 2051, new irrigation to 2.8 lakh hectares, and recharging 1,487 traditional water bodies.
