## 3. High-Yield RPSC Traps & Elimination Rules (25 Fatal Traps)

1. **National ST Absolute Population Rank Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Rajasthan ranks 6th in India in absolute tribal population."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Rajasthan ranks **FOURTH (Rank #4)** in India with **9,238,534 STs** (Census 2011), trailing only Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Odisha.

2. **ST Proportion within Rajasthan Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "STs comprise 17.5% of Rajasthan's population."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. ST population is **13.48%** (92.39 Lakh out of 685.48 Lakh in Census 2011). SC population is 17.83% (122.22 Lakh). Do not invert SC and ST percentages!

3. **Tribal Rank within Rajasthan Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Bhils are the most populous tribe within Rajasthan, just like nationally."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Nationally, Bhils are the largest ST group. Within Rajasthan, **Mina / Meena is Rank #1 (~47.0%, 43.46 Lakh)**, while the **Bhil group is Rank #2 (~44.4%, 41.00 Lakh)**.

4. **PVTG Exclusivity Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Rajasthan has three PVTGs: Sahariya, Kathodi, and Damor."
   - **Correction**: **FATAL ERROR**. Rajasthan has **EXACTLY ONE** Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group (PVTG): the **Sahariya** (Baran district). Neither Kathodi nor Damor is notified as a PVTG in Rajasthan.

5. **Sahariya Demographic Concentration Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Sahariyas are spread evenly across Hadoti and Mewar."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Sahariyas are almost exclusively concentrated in **Baran district**, specifically in **Kishanganj and Shahbad** tehsils.

6. **Scheduled Areas District Classification Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Scheduled Areas in Rajasthan cover 8 complete districts."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Scheduled Areas cover **3 COMPLETE districts** (Banswara, Dungarpur, Pratapgarh) and **notified PARTIAL areas in 5 districts** (Udaipur, Rajsamand, Chittorgarh, Pali, Sirohi).

7. **Scheduled Areas Notification Vintage Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Scheduled Areas in Rajasthan operate under the 1981 Presidential Notification."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. The 1981 Order was superseded by the **Scheduled Areas (State of Rajasthan) Order, 2018**, bringing 5,697 villages and 70.43% ST population under its ambit.

8. **PESA Sovereignty Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "PESA 1996 grants sovereign legislative autonomy to Gram Sabhas outside state laws."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. PESA provides statutory decentralized self-governance and administrative consultation powers within the constitutional framework of Part IX and the Fifth Schedule; it does not confer independent legislative sovereignty.

9. **Sagri Operative Statute Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Bonded agricultural labour in Rajasthan is currently prosecuted under the Rajasthan Sagri System Abolition Act, 1961."
   - **Correction**: **FATAL ERROR**. The 1961 Act was explicitly **REPEALED** by the **Rajasthan Sagri System Abolition (Repeal) Act, 1976**. The current operative statute is the central **Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976**.

10. **Sati Legislation Dual Chronology Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Sati was banned nationwide by Parliament in September 1987 immediately after the Deorala incident."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. In 1987, Rajasthan passed the state law: **Rajasthan Sati (Prevention) Act, 1987**. The central Parliament enacted the **Commission of Sati (Prevention) Act, 1987 (Act No. 3 of 1988)**, which was brought into force in Rajasthan on **21 March 1988**.

11. **Child Marriage Statutory Definition Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Under PCMA 2006, any person below 18 years is a child."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Under the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006, a child is defined as a **male under 21 years** or a **female under 18 years**.

12. **Harbilas Sarda Association Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Harbilas Sarda was a judge from Jaipur who drafted the Hindu Marriage Act."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Rai Bahadur Harbilas Sarda was a scholar, judge, and legislative sponsor from **Ajmer** who authored the **Child Marriage Restraint Act, 1929 (Sarda Act)**.

13. **Mrityu Bhoj Legislation Scope Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Mrityu Bhoj is regulated and taxed under the Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. It is strictly **prohibited and penalised** under the **Rajasthan Prevention of Mrityu Bhoj Act, 1960**, covering feasts under the names *Nukta*, *Mosar*, and *Chahallum*.

14. **Witch-Hunting Statute Year Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Rajasthan's Witch-Hunting Prevention Act was passed in 1987 alongside the Sati Act."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. The **Rajasthan Prevention of Witch-Hunting Act** was passed in **2015**.

15. **Pal–Dadhvav vs. Neemra Massacre Conflation Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "The Neemra Massacre occurred on 7 March 1922 in Mewar with 1,200 tribal deaths."
    - **Correction**: **FATAL HISTORICAL CONFLATION**. The historical mass shooting on **7 March 1922** under the Mewar Bhil Corps/British forces associated with Motilal Tejawat's Eki movement was the **Pal–Dadhvav Massacre**, occurring at Pal-Chitariya and Dadhvav in the former Idar State, now in **Sabarkantha district, GUJARAT**. Do not invent an unverified "Neemra Massacre" in Rajasthan.

16. **Mangarh Massacre Date & Figure Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "The Mangarh massacre took place on 17 November 1919 and exactly 1,500 Bhils were killed."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Mangarh occurred on **17 November 1913** (6 years before Jallianwala Bagh). The ~1,500 death toll is a government commemorative **estimate**, not a precise census count.

17. **Govind Guru Movement Identity Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Govind Guru led the Eki movement."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Govind Guru founded the **Bhagat Movement** and the **Samp Sabha (1883)**. **Motilal Tejawat** led the **Eki (Bhomat) Movement (1921)** from Matrikundiya.

18. **Halma vs. Begar Semantic Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Halma is a traditional form of feudal forced unpaid labour exacted by Thikanedars."
    - **Correction**: **FATAL INVERSION**. Feudal forced labour is ***Begār*** or ***Baith-Begār***. ***Halmā*** (or *Halmo*) is voluntary, dignified **collective mutual self-help** among tribal villagers.

19. **Dapa Directionality Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Dapa is a form of dowry given by the bride's father to the groom."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. *Dāpā* is **Bride-Price**, meaning cash/goods given by the **groom's family to the bride's father**.

20. **Vallor Dance Instrument Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Vallor dance of the Garasias is accompanied by heavy beating of Chang and Dholak."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Vallor / Valar is famously performed **WITHOUT ANY MUSICAL INSTRUMENT**, driven purely by vocal rhythm and synchronized footsteps.

21. **Garasia Administrative Listing Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Rajput Garasias are included in the ST quota in Rajasthan."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. The official ST entry explicitly states: *"Garasia (excluding Rajput Garasia)"*. Rajput Garasias are excluded from the ST schedule.

22. **Bhil Mina Administrative Category Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Bhil Mina is counted as part of the Mina ST figure in Census 2011."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. "Bhil Mina" (105,393) is an independent, distinct notified entry in the Constitutional Schedule and Census tables, separate from "Mina" (4,345,528).

23. **Excised Stigmatizing Assertions Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Examiners reward mentioning 'Pati Mangna' as divine theft blessings for Kanjars or 'Kukadi rasam' virginity tests for Sansis."
    - **Correction**: **STRICTLY REJECTED**. These represent colonial-era criminalizing stereotypes and unverified sensationalized lore, formally excised from the authoritative master codex. Answers must be objective, sociological, and constitutional.

24. **Paropkarini Sabha Location Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Dayanand Saraswati established the Paropkarini Sabha permanently at Jodhpur."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Paropkarini Sabha was founded under Dayanand's auspices (initially registered at Udaipur in 1883, with Maharana Sajjan Singh as president) and later operated permanently from **Ajmer**, where Swami Dayanand passed away.

25. **ST Sex Ratio Inversion Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Rajasthan's ST sex ratio is lower than the general state sex ratio due to high female infanticide."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Rajasthan's ST sex ratio is **948**, which is **20 points HIGHER** than the overall state sex ratio of **928** (Census 2011).
