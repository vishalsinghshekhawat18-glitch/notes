## 3. High-Yield RPSC Traps & Elimination Rules

```
                       TRIBAL MARRIAGE CLASSIFICATION
                                     │
      ┌──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┐
      │                                                             │
GARASIA UNIQUE MARRIAGE FORMS                                 BHIL MARITAL CUSTOMS
      │                                                             │
├── **Mor-Bandhiya**: Formal with crown & pheras              ├── **Dāpā**: Groom pays Bride-Price
├── **Pahrawana**: Clothes exchange without Brahmin           ├── **Nātrā**: Informal Remarriage
├── **Tānana**: Elopement followed by Dapa payment            └── **Attā-Sattā**: Sister exchange
├── **Melbo**: Leaving bride at groom's house
└── **Khewana**: Running away with lover (+ Jhagda)
```

1. **Tribal Rank Trap**:
   - **Meenas are Rank #1 (~47%)** in population in Rajasthan.
   - **Bhils are Rank #2 (~44%)** (Nationally Bhils are #1, but within Rajasthan Meenas lead).

2. **PVTG Exclusivity**:
   - The **ONLY Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group (PVTG)** in Rajasthan is the **Sahariya tribe** (Baran district).
   - Neither Kathodi nor Damor is classified as a PVTG in Rajasthan.

3. **Valar Dance Instrument Trap**:
   - The Garasia **Vālar Dance** is performed **WITHOUT ANY MUSICAL INSTRUMENT**!

4. **Dhari Sanskar Attribution**:
   - *Dhāri Sanskār* (sifting ash to identify next rebirth) belongs strictly to the **Sahariya tribe**, not the Bhils.

5. **Bride-Price (Dapa) Direction**:
   - *Dāpā* is **Bride-Price**, meaning the **groom's family pays money to the bride's father**, not vice versa.

6. **Halmo vs. Begar Trap**:
   - *Begar* is forced unpaid labor exacted by feudal lords.
   - *Halmo* is voluntary **collective mutual self-help** organized among Bhil villagers.

7. **Sati Ban Sequencing**:
   - First princely state in Rajasthan to ban Sati: **Bundi (1822)**.
   - Jaipur banned it in 1846, Udaipur in 1861.

8. **Paropkarini Sabha Location**:
   - Founded at **Udaipur** on 27 February 1883 by Swami Dayanand Saraswati (later shifted to Ajmer).

## 5. Model 2-Marker & 5-Marker Rapid Answer Keys

### Q1 (2 Marks): What is the significance of the "Kukadi Ki Rasam" among the Sansi tribe?
> **Answer**: *Kukadi Ki Rasam* is a traditional, regressive customary ritual among the Sansi community wherein a newly wedded bride is required to undergo a physical examination to publicly establish her pre-marital virginity and moral chastity before being formally accepted into her matrimonial household.

### Q2 (5 Marks): Explain the three tiers of the traditional caste panchayat of the Sahariya tribe.
> **Answer**:
> The Sahariya tribe resolves socio-cultural and marital disputes through a self-contained, three-tier customary panchayat system:
> 1. **Panchtāi (पंचताई)**: The primary grassroots council having customary jurisdiction over a cluster of **5 contiguous villages**.
> 2. **Ekdāshi (एकदशी)**: The intermediate dispute tribunal exercising jurisdiction across **11 villages**.
> 3. **Chaurāsi (चौरासी)**: The supreme apex general council with jurisdiction across all **84 Sahariya villages** in Baran. It convenes exclusively at the sacred **Valmiki Ashram in Sitabari (Kelwara)**; its rulings on excommunication, penalty, or reconciliation are final and binding.
