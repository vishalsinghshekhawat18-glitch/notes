## 3. High-Yield RPSC Traps & Elimination Rules (25 Fatal Traps)

1. **RSSC Foundation Year Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "The Rajasthan State Sports Council was established on 1 January 1957."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. The official history of the RSSC records its establishment in **1958** (registered under the Rajasthan Societies Registration Act, 1958). Its first residential training camp was held at Mount Abu in 1959.

2. **Out-of-Turn Appointment to RAS Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Olympic and Paralympic gold medal winners from Rajasthan are directly appointed as RAS officers."
   - **Correction**: **FATAL ERROR**. The *Rajasthan Out-of-Turn Appointment to Sports Medal Winners Rules, 2017* do **NOT** list the Rajasthan Administrative Service (RAS). They specify posts such as **Deputy Superintendent of Police (RPS/DySP)**, Deputy Superintendent Jail, Assistant Conservator of Forests (ACF), Assistant Excise Officer, Physical Training Instructor, and Sub-Inspector.

3. **2% Sports Reservation Scope Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Outstanding sportspersons enjoy 2% horizontal reservation across all recruitments in all state departments and PSUs."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. The reservation applies strictly to **2% of direct-recruitment vacancies outside the purview of the Commission (RPSC)**, subject to the definitions and service rules governing specific departments.

4. **Guinness World Record Attribution Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "The Rajiv Gandhi Rural Olympics entered the Guinness Book of World Records as the largest sporting event."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. The 2022 Rural Olympics was certified by the **World Book of Records**, which is a distinct organization from Guinness World Records.

5. **Rural Olympics Participation Exaggeration Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "The inaugural Rural Olympics 2022 had 58 lakh active participants."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Official registration for the inaugural rural edition stood at **~29.8 to 30 lakh players** across 11,341 Gram Panchayats. Unverified aggregate claims of 58 lakh must not be cited as a single-edition factual baseline.

6. **State Game 1948 Declaration Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Basketball was officially declared the State Game of Rajasthan by a gazette notification in 1948."
   - **Correction**: **CHRONOLOGICAL CONFLICT**. No primary government notification from 1948 exists, and the integration of Rajasthan was incomplete in 1948. While Basketball is widely recognized culturally, do not assert "gazette notification in 1948" as a verified official fact.

7. **Sriram Singh Shekhawat Record Status Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Sriram Singh's 800m timing of 1:45.77 at the 1976 Olympics remains an unbroken Asian record."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. The 1:45.77 mark stood as the **Indian National Record for 42 years** until Jinson Johnson broke it in 2018. It is **NOT** a current Asian record.

8. **Apurvi Chandela Olympic Medal Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Apurvi Chandela won an Olympic Bronze in shooting at Tokyo 2020."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Apurvi Chandela won World Cup Golds (setting a World Record 252.9 in 2019) and Commonwealth Games Gold, but has **NEVER** won an Olympic medal.

9. **Avani Lekhara Paris 2024 Record Trap**:
   - *Trap Statement*: "Avani Lekhara won silver in Paris 2024 after her gold in Tokyo."
   - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Avani Lekhara **DEFENDED HER GOLD MEDAL** at Paris 2024 in the Women's 10m Air Rifle Standing SH1 with a Paralympic Record score of **249.7**, making her the first Indian woman to win back-to-back Paralympic golds.

10. **Krishna Nagar Paris 2024 Medal Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Krishna Nagar defended his para-badminton gold at Paris 2024."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Krishna Nagar won Gold at **Tokyo 2020** (SH6 category); he did **not** win a medal at Paris 2024.

11. **Devendra Jhajharia Category Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Devendra Jhajharia competes in the F56 wheelchair javelin category."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Jhajharia competes in the **F46 limb impairment category** (Athens 2004 Gold, Rio 2016 Gold, Tokyo 2020 Silver).

12. **Sundar Singh Gurjar Paris Medal Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Sundar Singh Gurjar failed to medal in Paris 2024."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Sundar Singh Gurjar won **Bronze** in Men's Javelin F46 at Paris 2024, replicating his **Bronze** from Tokyo 2020.

13. **Bajrang Lal Takhar Asian Games Milestone Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Bajrang Lal Takhar won India's first individual rowing gold at the 2006 Doha Asian Games."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Takhar won **Silver** at Doha 2006, and made history by winning **GOLD at Guangzhou 2010** in Men's Single Sculls.

14. **Col. Rajyavardhan Singh Rathore Olympic Event Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Rajyavardhan Singh Rathore won Silver in 10m Air Rifle at Athens 2004."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. He won Silver in **Men's Double Trap shooting** at Athens 2004.

15. **Limba Ram Category & Event Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Limba Ram is a Paralympic archer from Alwar."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Limba Ram is an able-bodied Olympic archer from the tribal belt of **Udaipur (Sarada tehsil)** who competed in three Olympic Games (1988, 1992, 1996).

16. **Kabaddi Academy Location Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Rajasthan's boys' kabaddi academy is located at Jhunjhunu."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. According to the official live RSSC directory, **Boys' Kabaddi academies are located at Karauli and Churu** (Girls at Churu). **Jhunjhunu hosts the Boys' Volleyball academy**.

17. **Cycling Academy Location Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "The state cycling academy is located at Jaipur's SMS Stadium."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. The Boys' Cycling Academy is located in **Bikaner**.

18. **Girls' Basketball Academy Location Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Girls' basketball academy is located at Jaisalmer."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. **Boys' Basketball is at Jaisalmer**; **Girls' Basketball is at Jaipur**.

19. **Chop Cricket Stadium Superlative Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "The Chop stadium near Jaipur is an operational venue and the 2nd largest cricket stadium in the world."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. The Anil Agarwal Cricket Stadium at Chop is a **proposed / under-construction project** with a planned 75,000 capacity. It is not an operational international venue; avoid memorizing unverified world rankings.

20. **Ashtanga vs. Shatkarma Systemic Confusion Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Shatkarmas are the first six limbs of Patanjali's Ashtanga Yoga."
    - **Correction**: **FATAL PHILOSOPHICAL ERROR**. Shatkarmas belong to **Hatha Yoga** (*Hatha Yoga Pradipika*). Patanjali's Ashtanga Yoga consists of **8 Limbs** (Yama, Niyama, Asana, Pranayama, Pratyahara, Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi).

21. **Kapalabhati Dual-Classification Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Kapalabhati is strictly a Pranayama according to classical Hatha Yoga texts."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. In classical Hatha Yoga (*Hatha Yoga Pradipika* II.22), Kapalabhati is explicitly classified as one of the **Six Shatkarmas (cleansing kriyas)**, though modern schools often practice it as a pranayama.

22. **Pratyahara Structural Role Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Pratyahara is the first limb of Antaranga Yoga."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Pratyahara is the **5th and final limb of Bahiranga (External) Yoga**, acting as the vital bridge/transition to Antaranga Yoga (Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi).

23. **Out-of-Turn Achievement Cut-Off Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Any international medal won since independence qualifies for direct appointment under the 2017 Rules."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. The 2017 Rules require achievements to have occurred **after 1 January 2016**, and applications must be filed within **three years** of winning the medal.

24. **Maharana Pratap Award Recipient Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "Maharana Pratap Award is given to veteran coaches for lifetime mentorship."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. Maharana Pratap Award (instituted 1982–83) is awarded to **Athletes / Sportspersons**. The **Guru Vashishtha Award (instituted 1985–86)** is awarded to **Coaches**.

25. **Olympic Cash Incentives Nature Trap**:
    - *Trap Statement*: "The ₹3 crore Olympic cash reward is a constitutional guarantee in Rajasthan."
    - **Correction**: **WRONG**. It is an executive **policy-dependent grant** announced by state government notifications, subject to revision, verification of event category, and domicile criteria.
