1. **State Game Trap**:
   - The State Game of Rajasthan is **BASKETBALL**, officially declared in **1948** (NOT Polo, Cricket, or Kabaddi).

2. **Sports Awards Cash Prize Invariant**:
   - Both **Maharana Pratap Award** (players) and **Guru Vashishta Award** (coaches) carry a cash prize of **₹5,00,000 (₹5 Lakh)** (upgraded from ₹1 Lakh).

3. **Rural Olympics Launch Date & Venue**:
   - Launched on **29 August 2022** (National Sports Day).
   - Flagged off from **Pal / Kankani village (Jodhpur)**.
   - Kho-Kho was contested strictly by **Girls**; Shooting Ball was contested strictly by **Boys**.

4. **Sriram Singh Shekhawat Record**:
   - Finished 7th in 800m at Montreal Olympics 1976.
   - His time of **1:45.77** stood as the **Asian Record for 42 years** (1976 to 2018).

5. **Ashtanga Yoga Classification Trap**:
   - *Bahiranga Yoga*: Yama, Niyama, Asana, Pranayama, Pratyahara.
   - *Antaranga Yoga*: **Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi**.

6. **Kapalabhati Categorization**:
   - In classical Hatha Yoga (*Hatha Yoga Pradipika*), **Kapalabhati is a Shatkarma (purification kriya)**, not a standalone pranayama.

7. **Sports Quota Percentage**:
   - Government jobs in Rajasthan provide **2% horizontal reservation** for outstanding sportspersons.

### Q1 (2 Marks): Name any four events contested in the Rajiv Gandhi Rural Olympic Games.

### Q2 (5 Marks): Summarize the historic athletic achievements and career milestone records of Devendra Jhajharia.
> **Answer**:
> 1. **Athens 2004 Paralympics**: Won Gold in Men's Javelin Throw (F46) with a World Record of **62.15 meters**.
> 2. **Rio 2016 Paralympics**: Won his second Paralympic Gold Medal, shattering his own World Record with a throw of **63.97 meters**.
> 3. **Tokyo 2020 Paralympics**: Clinched the Silver Medal with a personal best throw of **64.35 meters**.
> 4. **Pioneering Honors**:
>    - First Indian para-athlete conferred the **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna (2017)**.
>    - First para-athlete in Indian history conferred the **Padma Bhushan (2022)**.
> 5. **Sports Administration**: Elected unopposed as the **President of the Paralympic Committee of India (PCI)** in March 2024.
