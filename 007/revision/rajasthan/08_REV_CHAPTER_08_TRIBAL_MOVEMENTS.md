## 3. Top 10 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Founding Place of Samp Sabha**: Founded at **Sirohi in 1883 CE**, NOT at Dungarpur, Banswara, or Mangarh.
2. **Birthplace of Govind Guru**: Born at **Bansia / Bedsa village (Dungarpur)** in a **Banjara** family.
3. **Date of Mangarh Dham Massacre**: Occurred on **17 November 1913 (Margashirsha / Ashwin Purnima)**, six years BEFORE Jallianwala Bagh (1919).
4. **Casualties of Mangarh Dham**: Over **1,500 Bhil devotees** were martyred by the Mewar Bhil Corps and British troops.
5. **Caste of Motilal Tejawat**: He was NOT a tribal Bhil by birth; he was born in an **Oswal Jain merchant family** at Koliyari (Jhadol).
6. **Launch Site of Eki Movement**: Consecrated at **Matrikundiya (Rashmi, Chittorgarh)**, celebrated as the *"Haridwar of Rajasthan"*, NOT at Neemra.
7. **"Mewar Ki Pukar" Demands**: Maharana Fateh Singh **accepted 18 demands**; he rejected only **3 demands** (Begar, Forest Rights, and Wild Boars).
8. **Neemra Massacre Details**: Occurred on **7 March 1922**; over **1,200 Bhils and Garasias** were martyred by British Major Sutton’s troops.
9. **Author of "Meena Puran"**: Jain ascetic **Muni Magan Sagar**, who presided over the historic **Neem Ka Thana Convention (April 1944)**.
10. **The "Mukti Diwas" of the Meenas**: Observed on **28 October 1946 at Baghawas**, when 26,000 Chaukidari Meenas simultaneously resigned from their posts.

## 4. Diagnostic Active-Recall Flashcards

### Card 1
- **Q**: What does the word "Samp" mean in the context of Govind Guru’s Samp Sabha (1883)?
- **A**: In the local dialect, **'Samp' (संप) signifies mutual love, fraternity, peaceful solidarity, and unity**.

### Card 2
- **Q**: What were the three demands of "Mewar Ki Pukar" rejected by Maharana Fateh Singh?
- **A**: (1) Total abolition of forced labor (**Begar**), (2) Restoration of customary **Forest Rights** (collecting timber/fuelwood), and (3) Lifting restrictions on killing crop-destroying **wild boars**.

### Card 3
- **Q**: Which military unit was responsible for the massacres at both Mangarh Dham (1913) and Neemra (1922)?
- **A**: The British **Mewar Bhil Corps (MBC)**, established in 1841 with headquarters at Kherwara (Udaipur).

### Card 4
- **Q**: Why was the Jairam Pesha Act (1930) considered intensely degrading to the Meena community?
- **A**: It mandated that every Meena male, female, and child aged **12 and above** report for a daily in-person roll-call (*Haziri*) at the nearest police station under threat of immediate flogging.

### Card 5
- **Q**: When and under whose chief ministership was the Jairam Pesha Act finally and irrevocably repealed?
- **A**: Repealed in **1952** under the democratic government of Rajasthan Chief Minister **Tikaram Paliwal**.
