# REVISION CHAPTER 08: RAY OPTICS, WAVE OPTICS & THE HUMAN EYE

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **Reflection**: $\angle i = \angle r$.
  - Plane mirror: Virtual, erect, $m = +1$, lateral inversion.
  - Min mirror height for full body: $\mathbf{H/2}$.
  - Rotating mirror by $\theta \implies$ Reflected ray rotates by **$2\theta$**.
  - Number of images: $n = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta} - 1$ (if even). Parallel mirrors $\implies \infty$ images.
* **Spherical Mirrors**: $f = R/2$; $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$; $m = -\frac{v}{u}$.
  - Concave ($f < 0$): Dentist mirror, shaving mirror (object between Pole and Focus $\rightarrow$ Virtual, Erect, Enlarged); Headlights (bulb at Focus $\rightarrow$ parallel beam).
  - Convex ($f > 0$): Rear-view vehicle mirror (Virtual, Erect, Diminished, wide panoramic field).
* **Refraction**: Snell's Law $\frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{n_2}{n_1} = \frac{v_1}{v_2} = \frac{\lambda_1}{\lambda_2}$.
  - **Frequency ($f$) remains STRICTLY CONSTANT**; only $v$ and $\lambda$ change!
  - Apparent depth: $n = \frac{\text{Real Depth}}{\text{Apparent Depth}}$ (Pool looks shallow, pencil looks bent).
* **Total Internal Reflection (TIR)**:
  - 2 Mandatory Conditions: (1) Light must travel **Denser $\rightarrow$ Rarer**, (2) Angle $i > \text{Critical Angle } C$ ($\sin C = 1/n$).
  - Diamond sparkles ($C = 24.4^\circ$); Optical fiber (Core $n_1 >$ Cladding $n_2$); Endoscopy; Mirage in desert.
* **Lenses**: $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u}$; Power $P = \frac{1}{f\text{ (m)}} = \frac{100}{f\text{ (cm)}}$ in **Diopters (D)**.
  - Convex lens ($f > 0 \implies +D$); Concave lens ($f < 0 \implies -D$).
* **Dispersion & Scattering**:
  - Prism: Violet deviates most ($\lambda = 400\text{ nm}$); Red deviates least ($\lambda = 700\text{ nm}$).
  - Rainbow: Primary = 2 refractions + 1 internal reflection; Secondary = 2 refractions + 2 internal reflections.
  - Atmospheric Refraction: Twinkling stars; Advance sunrise (2 min) + delayed sunset (2 min) $= \mathbf{4\text{ minutes daily}}$.
  - Rayleigh Scattering: $I \propto \frac{1}{\lambda^4}$. Sky is blue (blue scattered 16x more than red); Sky is black in space (no air); Danger signals are red (least scattered).
* **Human Eye**: Near point $D = 25\text{ cm}$; Far point $\infty$; Cornea (80% refraction); Rods (dim light, Rhodopsin, Vitamin A); Cones (bright light, color vision, Fovea).
  - **Myopia (Near-sighted)**: Image forms in front of retina $\rightarrow$ Corrected by **Concave Lens ($-D$)**.
  - **Hypermetropia (Far-sighted)**: Image forms behind retina $\rightarrow$ Corrected by **Convex Lens ($+D$)**.
  - **Presbyopia**: Aging, weak ciliary $\rightarrow$ **Bifocal Lens**.
  - **Astigmatism**: Corneal irregularity $\rightarrow$ **Cylindrical Lens**.

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Which parameter of a light wave remains completely unchanged when transitioning from air into water?** | **Frequency ($f$)**; only speed ($v$) and wavelength ($\lambda$) decrease. |
| **Q2: Why are danger and vehicle brake lights always made red?** | Red light has the longest visible wavelength ($\lambda \approx 700\text{ nm}$); by Rayleigh's law ($I \propto 1/\lambda^4$), it is **scattered the least** by fog, dust, and air, traveling the greatest distance. |
| **Q3: What are the two essential conditions required for Total Internal Reflection (TIR) to occur?** | (1) Light must travel from an optically **denser to a rarer medium**, and (2) Angle of incidence must **exceed the critical angle ($i > C$)**. |
| **Q4: Why does a person suffering from myopia wear concave lenses?** | In myopia, the eyeball is elongated and light rays converge in front of the retina. A **concave (diverging) lens** spreads rays outward slightly so they focus precisely onto the retina. |
| **Q5: By how many minutes is total daylight extended each day due to atmospheric refraction?** | By **4 minutes** (2 minutes before actual physical sunrise + 2 minutes after physical sunset). |
| **Q6: What is the minimum vertical height of a plane mirror required for a 6-foot tall person to see their entire body?** | Exactly **3 feet** ($\frac{H}{2}$). |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TIR in Water from Air** | "TIR occurs when light enters water from air at an angle greater than $49^\circ$." | **Impossible!** TIR can *only* occur when light travels from a **denser to a rarer medium** (e.g., from water into air). |
| **Sky Color on Moon** | "The sky looks blue during daytime on the Moon." | The Moon has **zero atmosphere**; without air molecules to cause Rayleigh scattering, the sky appears **jet black** at all times. |
| **Prescription Sign Trap** | "A spectacle prescription of $+2.5\text{ D}$ is for a short-sighted person." | Positive power ($+D$) denotes a **Convex lens**, which corrects **Hypermetropia (long-sightedness)**! Short-sightedness (myopia) requires negative power ($-D$). |
| **Rainbow Sun Position** | "A rainbow can be seen facing the Sun at noon." | The observer's back **MUST be facing the Sun**; rainbows are physically impossible at high noon in summer. |
