# REVISION CHAPTER 17: METALS, METALLURGY & ALLOYS

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **Properties & Exceptions**:
  - Liquid at 25°C: **Mercury ($Hg$)** is liquid metal; **Bromine ($Br_2$)** is liquid non-metal.
  - Soft metals: Sodium ($Na$) & Potassium ($K$) cut with knife, float on water, stored under **kerosene**.
  - White phosphorus catches fire at $30^\circ\text{C}$ in air $\rightarrow$ stored under **water**.
  - Melts in palm: **Gallium ($29.7^\circ\text{C}$)** and **Cesium ($28.4^\circ\text{C}$)**.
  - Non-metal exceptions: Diamond (hardest substance); Graphite (electrical conductor); Iodine (lustrous crystals).
* **Chemical Reactivity**:
  - Series: $K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > [H] > Cu > Hg > Ag > Au$.
  - Cold water: $K, Na$ (fire!), $Ca$ (floats due to $H_2$ bubbles).
  - Steam only: $Al, Zn, Fe$ (forms magnetic iron oxide $\text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4$).
  - Amphoteric Oxides: React with both acids and bases ($\mathbf{\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3\text{ and } \text{ZnO}}$).
  - Aqua Regia: $\mathbf{3\text{ parts Conc. HCl} : 1\text{ part Conc. HNO}_3}$ (dissolves Gold and Platinum).
* **Metallurgy**:
  - Ores: Bauxite ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 \cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$), Haematite ($\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$), Copper Pyrites ($\text{CuFeS}_2$), Cinnabar ($HgS$), Galena ($PbS$), Pitchblende ($U_3O_8$), Monazite (Thorium in Kerala).
  - Concentration: Froth Floatation (exclusively for **sulfide ores** using Pine Oil).
  - Extraction:
    - **Calcination**: Heating in **ABSENCE of air** (Carbonate ores: $\text{ZnCO}_3 \rightarrow \text{ZnO} + \text{CO}_2$).
    - **Roasting**: Heating in **EXCESS of air** (Sulfide ores: $2ZnS + 3O_2 \rightarrow 2ZnO + 2SO_2$).
    - Refining: Zone refining (ultra-pure semiconductors $Si, Ge$); Mond process (Nickel).
* **Alloys & Amalgams**:
  - Amalgam = Alloy of Mercury ($Hg$). **Iron ($Fe$) does NOT form amalgam** (stored in iron flasks!).
  - **Brass**: Copper ($70\%$) + Zinc ($30\%$).
  - **Bronze**: Copper ($88\%$) + Tin ($12\%$).
  - **German Silver**: Copper + Zinc + Nickel (**0% SILVER!**).
  - **Stainless Steel**: Iron + **Chromium ($18\%$)** + Nickel ($8\%$) + Carbon ($1\%$) (Chromium forms protective oxide).
  - **Solder**: Lead ($50\%$) + Tin ($50\%$) (Low melting point $\sim 183^\circ\text{C}$).
  - **Duralumin**: Aluminum ($95\%$) + Copper ($4\%$) (Aircraft bodies).
  - **Invar**: Iron + Nickel ($36\%$) (**Zero thermal expansion** $\rightarrow$ surveying tapes, clock pendulums).

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: What percentage of silver is contained in the alloy known as "German Silver"?** | Exactly **0% Silver**! It is an alloy of Copper ($60\%$), Zinc ($20\%$), and Nickel ($20\%$), named purely for its silvery luster. |
| **Q2: Why is liquid mercury transported and stored in iron containers rather than copper or glass?** | Mercury dissolves and forms amalgams with most metals (copper, silver, gold), but **does NOT form an amalgam with Iron ($Fe$)**. |
| **Q3: What are the two primary amphoteric metal oxides that react with both acids and bases?** | **Aluminum Oxide ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$)** and **Zinc Oxide ($\text{ZnO}$)**. |
| **Q4: What is the exact chemical composition of Aqua Regia, and what does it dissolve?** | **3 parts concentrated $HCl$ to 1 part concentrated $HNO_3$ by volume**; dissolves noble metals like **Gold ($Au$) and Platinum ($Pt$)**. |
| **Q5: Which metal is extracted from Bauxite, and which from Monazite sand?** | **Aluminum ($Al$)** from Bauxite; **Thorium ($Th$)** from Monazite sand (abundant on Kerala beaches). |
| **Q6: What is the fundamental difference between calcination and roasting in metallurgy?** | **Calcination** heats ore in the **absence of air** (for carbonate ores); **Roasting** heats ore in **excess of air** (for sulfide ores). |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **German Silver** | "German silver contains 20% silver." | It contains **strictly 0% silver**; composed of Copper, Zinc, and Nickel. |
| **Rustless Stainless Steel** | "Nickel prevents stainless steel from rusting." | **Chromium ($Cr, \ge 11\%$)** forms the invisible, self-healing **Chromium Oxide ($\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_3$)** passivation barrier that blocks rust. |
| **White Phosphorus Storage** | "White phosphorus is kept in kerosene like sodium." | White phosphorus reacts with air and catches fire at $30^\circ\text{C}$; it does not react with water, so it is stored safely **under WATER**. Sodium reacts with water and is stored in **kerosene**. |
| **Iron Reaction with Steam** | "Iron reacting with steam forms Ferric Hydroxide." | Hot iron with steam forms **Magnetic Iron Oxide ($\text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4$)**, releasing Hydrogen gas. |
