## 2. ATOMIC FORMULAS & NUMERICAL CONSTANTS

* **Antibody Molecule:** Monomeric $H_2L_2$ ($2\text{ Heavy} + 2\text{ Light}$ chains joined by $-S-S-$ bridges).
* **CD4+ Threshold for AIDS:** Normal $>1000/\mu\text{L}$; clinical AIDS defined as **$\text{CD4}^+ < 200/\mu\text{L}$**.
* **Diagnostic Tests:**
  * Typhoid $\implies$ Widal Test.
  * Tuberculosis $\implies$ Mantoux Test / CB-NAAT.
  * HIV $\implies$ ELISA (Screening) + Western Blot (Confirmatory).
  * Dengue $\implies$ NS1 Antigen / IgM ELISA / Tourniquet Test.

## 3. CORE EXAM HIGHLIGHTS & PITFALLS

* **Graft Rejection:** Orchestrated strictly by **Cell-Mediated Immunity (T-Lymphocytes)**; suppressed by **Cyclosporin A** (derived from fungus *Trichoderma polysporum*).
* **Immunoglobulin Classes Rapid Diagnostic:**
  * Crosses Placenta: **$\text{IgG}$ ONLY**.
  * Colostrum & Breast Milk: **$\text{IgA}$**.
  * Primary Immediate Response & Heaviest: **$\text{IgM}$** (Pentamer).
  * Anaphylactic Allergy & Histamine Release: **$\text{IgE}$**.
* **Interferons:** Glycoproteins secreted by **virus-infected host cells** to protect neighboring uninfected cells.
* **Malaria Shivering Chills Mechanism:** Caused by synchronous rupture of erythrocytes releasing **Hemozoin** toxic pigment every 48–72 hours.
* **Fertilization Site in Malaria:** The sexual stage and fertilization occur **in the mosquito gut**, NOT in the human body.
* **Humulin Synthesis:** Eli Lilly did NOT synthesize proinsulin; they produced human chains A and B in separate *E. coli* cultures and combined them via chemical disulfide bonds.
* **Bt Toxin Activation:** Requires the **alkaline pH of insect midgut** to solubilize and cleave inactive protoxin into active lethal pore-forming toxin.
