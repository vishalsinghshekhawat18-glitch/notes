## 2. ATOMIC FORMULAS & NUMERICAL RATIOS

* **Genetic Cross Ratios:**
  * Dihybrid Phenotypic: $9 : 3 : 3 : 1$.
  * Monohybrid Incomplete Dominance (Pheno = Geno): $1 : 2 : 1$.
  * Test Cross: $1 : 1$ (Monohybrid) and $1 : 1 : 1 : 1$ (Dihybrid).
* **Genetic Code Metrics:**
  * Total Codons: $64$ ($4^3$).
  * Sense Codons: $61$ (Code for amino acids).
  * Stop / Nonsense Codons: $3$ (UAA, UAG, UGA).
  * Initiator Codon: AUG (Methionine).

## 3. CORE EXAM HIGHLIGHTS & PITFALLS

* **LH Surge:** Triggers **Ovulation** on Day 14; induced by peaking estrogen levels.
* **Corpus Luteum:** Secretes massive amounts of **Progesterone** to maintain pregnancy-ready endometrium; rescued by placental **hCG**.
* **Oogenesis Arrest Points:**
  * Primary oocytes arrest in **Prophase I (Diplotene)** until puberty.
  * Secondary oocyte arrests in **Metaphase II**; completes division *strictly* upon sperm fertilization.
* **Mendel's Exceptions:**
  * Law of Independent Assortment is violated by **Genetic Linkage** (Morgan).
  * Law of Segregation has **ZERO exceptions** in sexual diploid organisms.
* **Bird Sex Determination:** Female is heterogametic (**ZW**), male is homogametic (**ZZ**).
* **Sickle Cell Mutation:** Single base transversion substituting **Valine for Glutamic acid** at position 6 of the $\beta$-chain ($\text{HbS}$).
* **Aneuploidy Signatures:**
  * Down's: $47, +21$ (Simian crease, flat face).
  * Turner's: $45, X0$ (Webbed neck, streak ovaries, sterile female).
  * Klinefelter's: $47, XXY$ (Gynecomastia, eunuchoid male, Barr body positive).
