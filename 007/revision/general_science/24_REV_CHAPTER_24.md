## 1. HIGH-SPEED KNOWLEDGE SKELETON

```
                     HUMAN PHYSIOLOGY I SKELETON
                     
  [Digestion]   ──► Saliva (Ptyalin 30% starch) ──► Stomach (Oxyntic: HCl + CIF; Chief: Pepsinogen)
                    Duodenum: Bile (NO ENZYMES, Emulsification) + Pancreas (Trypsinogen via Enterokinase)
                    Absorption: Glucose (SGLT-1 Na+ active), Fructose (GLUT-5), Fats (Chylomicrons -> Lacteals)
  [Respiration] ──► TV (500 mL) | IRV (3000 mL) | ERV (1100 mL) | RV (1200 mL, NOT on spirometer)
                    Vital Capacity: VC = TV + IRV + ERV (~4600 mL) | TLC = VC + RV (~5800 mL)
                    Bohr Effect: Right shift (Hb unloading O2): High CO2, Low pH, High Temp, High 2,3-BPG
                    CO2 Transport: 70% as Bicarbonate (Carbonic Anhydrase + Chloride Shift)
  [Circulation] ──► SA Node (Pacemaker, 72 bpm) ──► AV Node (Delay) ──► His-Purkinje
                    Cardiac Cycle (0.8 s): Atrial systole (0.1 s) -> Ventricular systole (0.3 s) -> Diastole (0.4 s)
                    Heart Sounds: LUB (AV valves close) | DUB (Semilunar valves close)
                    ECG: P (Atrial Depol) | QRS (Ventricular Depol) | T (Ventricular Repol)
                    Hemostasis: Thromboplastin + Ca2+ -> Thrombin -> Fibrin mesh | Serum = Plasma - Clotting Factors
```

## 2. ATOMIC FORMULAS & NUMERICAL CONSTANTS

* **Dental Formula (Adult):** $\frac{2123}{2123} \times 2 = 32\text{ teeth}$ (Child: $\frac{2102}{2102} \times 2 = 20\text{ teeth}$, no premolars).
* **Cardiac Output Formula:**
  $$\text{Cardiac Output} = \text{Stroke Volume (70 mL)} \times \text{Heart Rate (72 bpm)} \approx \mathbf{5.0\text{ L/min}}$$
* **Pulmonary Capacities:**
  * $\text{Vital Capacity (VC)} = \text{TV} + \text{IRV} + \text{ERV} \approx 4600\text{ mL}$.
  * $\text{Total Lung Capacity (TLC)} = \text{VC} + \text{RV} \approx 5800\text{ mL}$.
  * $\text{Functional Residual Capacity (FRC)} = \text{ERV} + \text{RV} \approx 2300\text{ mL}$.

## 3. CORE EXAM HIGHLIGHTS & PITFALLS

* **Castle’s Intrinsic Factor:** Secreted by gastric **Parietal (Oxyntic) cells**; required for **Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$** absorption in the ileum; loss causes Pernicious Anemia.
* **Bile Juice:** Secreted by liver, stored in gallbladder; contains **ZERO enzymes**! Functions strictly via bile salts (taurocholate/glycocholate) to emulsify lipids and form micelles.
* **Enamel:** Composed of crystalline Calcium Hydroxyapatite; hardest biological substance in mammals.
* **Spirometry Blind Spot:** **Residual Volume (RV)** and **Functional Residual Capacity (FRC)** CANNOT be measured by standard clinical spirometry because RV cannot be exhaled.
* **Erythroblastosis Fetalis:** Strictly occurs when **$\text{Rh}^-$ Mother** carries a second (or subsequent) **$\text{Rh}^+$ Fetus**; prevented by giving RhoGAM (anti-D IgG) within 72 hours of first delivery.
* **Universal Donor & Recipient:**
  * Universal Donor: **$O^-$ (O Negative)** — lacks A, B, and Rh antigens.
  * Universal Recipient: **$AB^+$ (AB Positive)** — lacks anti-A, anti-B, and anti-Rh antibodies.
