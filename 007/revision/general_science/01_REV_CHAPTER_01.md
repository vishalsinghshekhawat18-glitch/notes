# REVISION CHAPTER 01: PHYSICAL QUANTITIES, UNITS & MEASUREMENT

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **Formula of Measurement**: $Q = n \times u$ ($n \propto \frac{1}{u}$).
* **7 SI Base Units**: Metre ($\text{m}$), Kilogram ($\text{kg}$ via Planck $h$), Second ($\text{s}$ via Cs-133), Ampere ($\text{A}$ via $e$), Kelvin ($\text{K}$ via $k$), Mole ($\text{mol}$ via $N_A$), Candela ($\text{cd}$).
* **2 Supplementary Units**: Radian ($\text{rad}$, plane angle), Steradian ($\text{sr}$, solid angle). Both are **dimensionless with units**.
* **Astronomical Order**: $1\text{ Parsec } (3.26\text{ ly}) > 1\text{ Light Year } (9.46 \times 10^{15}\text{ m}) > 1\text{ AU } (1.496 \times 10^{11}\text{ m})$.
* **Micro Units**: Micron ($10^{-6}\text{ m}$), Angstrom ($10^{-10}\text{ m}$), Fermi ($10^{-15}\text{ m}$).
* **Pressure & Power**: $1\text{ atm} = 760\text{ Torr} = 1.01325 \times 10^5\text{ Pa}$; $1\text{ bar} = 10^5\text{ Pa}$; $1\text{ hp} = 746\text{ W}$; $1\text{ kWh} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J}$.
* **Dimensional Constants**: Force $[M L T^{-2}]$; Work/Energy $[M L^2 T^{-2}]$; Power $[M L^2 T^{-3}]$; Gravitational constant $G [M^{-1} L^3 T^{-2}]$.
* **Scalars vs Vectors**: Current and Pressure are **SCALARS** (current violates vector addition; fluid pressure is isotropic).
* **Key Devices**: Altimeter (height/pressure), Barometer (atmospheric pressure), Pyrometer (high temp radiation), Hygrometer (humidity), Crescograph (plant growth), Sphygmomanometer (BP).
* **Error Propagation**: For $Z = \frac{A^p B^q}{C^r}$, $\% \text{ error in } Z = p(\%A) + q(\%B) + r(\%C)$.

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Which physical quantity is dimensionless yet possesses an official SI unit?** | **Plane Angle** (Radian) and **Solid Angle** (Steradian). |
| **Q2: Why is Electric Current classified as a scalar despite having magnitude and direction?** | It does not follow vector addition laws (triangle/parallelogram law); total current at a junction follows simple scalar arithmetic ($\sum I = 0$). |
| **Q3: What is the exact value of the Chandrasekhar Limit and its physical meaning?** | $\approx 1.44\text{ Solar Masses } (M_\odot)$. It is the upper limit for a stable white dwarf star before gravitational collapse. |
| **Q4: Arrange in descending order: 1 Light Year, 1 Parsec, 1 Astronomical Unit.** | **$1\text{ Parsec } (3.26\text{ ly}) > 1\text{ Light Year } (63,241\text{ AU}) > 1\text{ AU } (1.5 \times 10^{11}\text{ m})$**. |
| **Q5: Which universal constant was locked to redefine the SI Kilogram in 2019?** | The **Planck Constant** ($h = 6.62607015 \times 10^{-34}\text{ J}\cdot\text{s}$) measured via Kibble balance. |
| **Q6: How many Joules are in 1 Kilowatt-hour (kWh)?** | **$3.6 \times 10^6\text{ Joules}$** ($3.6\text{ MJ}$). |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Light Year** | Often asked as: "Light year is a unit of: (a) Time (b) Distance (c) Speed of light". | It is strictly a measure of **Distance** traversed by light in vacuum in 1 year ($9.46 \times 10^{15}\text{ m}$). |
| **Pressure** | "Pressure has direction perpendicular to a surface, hence a vector." | Pressure inside a static fluid is **isotropic** (acts equally in all directions) and is mathematically a **scalar**. |
| **Weight** | "My weight is 70 kilograms." | Kilogram is mass ($M$). Weight is gravitational force ($W = mg$) measured in **Newtons ($\text{N}$)**. |
| **Strain** | "What is the SI unit of mechanical strain?" | Strain is ratio of change in dimension to original dimension ($\Delta L / L$). It is **completely unitless and dimensionless**. |
