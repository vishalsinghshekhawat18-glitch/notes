```
                         THE PLANT PHYSIOLOGY MATRIX
                         
  [Water Dynamics]  ──► Water Potential: Psi_w = Psi_s + Psi_p | Dixon & Joly Transpiration Pull
```

## 2. ATOMIC FORMULAS & NUMERICAL CONSTANTS

* **Water Potential Formula:** $\Psi_w = \Psi_s + \Psi_p$ (Pure water $\Psi_w = 0\text{ MPa}$; $\Psi_s$ always negative).
* **Photosynthesis Stoichiometry (Glucose Synthesis):**
  * $C_3$ Pathway: $6\text{ CO}_2 + 18\text{ ATP} + 12\text{ NADPH} \longrightarrow \text{Glucose}$.
  * $C_4$ Pathway: $6\text{ CO}_2 + 30\text{ ATP} + 12\text{ NADPH} \longrightarrow \text{Glucose}$.
* **Biological Nitrogen Fixation Stoichiometry:**
  * $\text{N}_2 + 8\text{e}^- + 8\text{H}^+ + 16\text{ATP} \xrightarrow{\text{Nitrogenase}} 2\text{NH}_3 + \text{H}_2 + 16\text{ADP} + 16\text{P}_i$.
* **Respiratory Quotient (RQ):**
  * Carbohydrates = $1.0$
  * Fats = $0.7$
  * Proteins = $0.8 - 0.9$
  * Organic Acids $> 1.0$ (Malic acid = $1.33$)
  * CAM at night = $0$

## 3. CORE EXAM HIGHLIGHTS & PITFALLS

* **Guttation vs. Transpiration:**
  * Guttation: Liquid water loss via **Hydathodes** driven by positive root pressure during humid mornings; fluid contains inorganic salts.
  * Transpiration: Vapor loss via Stomata driven by negative transpirational pull; pure water vapor.
* **Essential Elements & Diseases:**
  * Central atom of Chlorophyll: **Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$)**.
  * Photolysis of Water: **Manganese ($\text{Mn}$) & Chlorine ($\text{Cl}$)**.
  * Nitrogenase & Nitrate Reductase: **Molybdenum ($\text{Mo}$)** (Whiptail of cauliflower).
  * Auxin Biosynthesis: **Zinc ($\text{Zn}$)** (Little leaf disease).
  * Pollen germination & Ca translocation: **Boron ($\text{B}$)**.
* **Photorespiration Organelles:** Chloroplast $\to$ Peroxisome $\to$ Mitochondrion.
* **Tissue Culture Hormone Ratio:**
  * High Cytokinin / Auxin $\implies$ Shoot formation.
  * Low Cytokinin / Auxin (High Auxin) $\implies$ Root formation.
* **Hormone Antagonisms:** ABA (Dormancy promoter) is antagonistic to Gibberellins (Germination promoter).
