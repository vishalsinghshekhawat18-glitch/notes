# REVISION CHAPTER 12: NATURE OF MATTER & COLLOIDS

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **Classification**: Elements (Metals: $Hg$ only liquid metal; Non-metals: $Br_2$ only liquid non-metal; Metalloids: $Si, Ge, As$). Compounds have fixed mass ratio ($\text{H}_2\text{O} = 1:8, \text{CO}_2 = 3:8$ by mass) with properties different from constituent elements.
* **5 States of Matter**:
  - Solid, Liquid, Gas.
  - Plasma: Superheated ionized gas ($>10^4\text{ K}$, Sun, stars, fluorescent neon tubes). $>99\%$ of visible universe.
  - Bose-Einstein Condensate (BEC): Super-cooled gas near $0\text{ K}$ (Bose-Einstein 1924, synthesized 1995 Cornell/Wieman using $^{87}Rb$). Single macroscopic super-atom.
* **Phase Changes**: Sublimation = Solid $\rightarrow$ Gas directly (Camphor, Ammonium Chloride $NH_4Cl$, Dry Ice solid $CO_2$, Naphthalene, Iodine).
* **Changes**: Physical (reversible, no new substance, melting/boiling); Chemical (irreversible, new substance, rusting, curdling). **Burning candle = BOTH physical and chemical**.
* **Dispersion Systems**:
  - True Solution: $<1\text{ nm}$, homogeneous, no Tyndall effect.
  - Colloid: $1\text{--}1000\text{ nm}$, heterogeneous, **Tyndall effect (scattering)** + **Brownian motion (chaotic zig-zag stabilizes colloid)**.
  - Suspension: $>1000\text{ nm}$, settles under gravity (unstable).
* **Colloid Examples**:
  - Aerosol: Liquid/Solid in Gas (Fog, Clouds, Smoke).
  - Foam: Gas in Liquid (Shaving cream, Soap lather).
  - Emulsion: Liquid in Liquid (Milk, Face cream).
  - Sol: Solid in Liquid (Blood, Ink, Paint).
  - Gel: Liquid in Solid (Jelly, Cheese, Curd).
  - *Gas in Gas is NEVER a colloid (always homogeneous true solution)*.
* **Separation**:
  - Centrifugation: Density differential (Cream from milk, blood pathology).
  - Separating funnel: Immiscible liquids (Oil and Water).
  - Simple Distillation: $\Delta BP > 25^\circ\text{C}$; Fractional Distillation: **$\Delta BP < 25^\circ\text{C}$** (Petroleum refining, Liquid Air separation: $N_2 -196^\circ\text{C}, O_2 -183^\circ\text{C}$).
  - Chromatography: Solubility/adsorption rate (Dyes, ink pigments, drugs in blood).

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Which metal and which non-metal are liquids at normal room temperature (25°C)?** | **Mercury ($Hg$)** is the only liquid metal; **Bromine ($Br_2$)** is the only liquid non-metal. |
| **Q2: Why is the burning of a candle classified as both a physical and chemical change?** | Melting and vaporization of wax is a **physical change** (phase change); combustion of wax vapor into $\text{CO}_2$, $\text{H}_2\text{O}$, and soot is a **chemical change**. |
| **Q3: What colloidal type is milk, and what are its dispersed phase and dispersion medium?** | Milk is an **Emulsion** (liquid fat droplets as dispersed phase, dispersed in water as the dispersion medium). |
| **Q4: Why does a colloidal solution never settle down under gravity like a suspension?** | Continuous, chaotic bombardment by thermal solvent molecules produces **Brownian motion**, which acts as a kinetic stirring engine preventing gravitational settling. |
| **Q5: When is fractional distillation mandatory instead of simple distillation?** | When the difference in boiling points between two miscible liquids is **less than $25^\circ\text{C}$** (e.g., crude petroleum refining, liquid air components). |
| **Q6: Can a mixture of two gases ever form a colloidal system?** | **Never.** Gas molecules mix completely and uniformly at the molecular level, forming exclusively **homogeneous true solutions**. |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gas Mixture Colloid** | "Air is a colloidal aerosol of nitrogen and oxygen." | Gases mix completely into a **homogeneous true solution**. No gas-in-gas colloid can physically exist. |
| **Distillation Threshold** | "Fractional distillation is used when boiling point difference is very large ($>50^\circ\text{C}$)." | Fractional distillation is required when boiling points are **very close ($\Delta BP < 25^\circ\text{C}$)**! |
| **Sublimation Products** | "Dry ice turns into liquid water when warm." | Dry ice is **solid Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$)**; it sublimes directly into gaseous $CO_2$ with zero liquid phase. |
| **Milk Nature** | "Milk is a homogeneous true solution." | Milk is a **heterogeneous colloidal emulsion**; it scatters light (Tyndall effect) and separates via centrifugation. |
