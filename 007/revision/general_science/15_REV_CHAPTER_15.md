# REVISION CHAPTER 15: CHEMICAL REACTIONS, CATALYSIS & REDOX

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **5 Reaction Types**:
  - Combination: Quicklime + Water $\rightarrow$ Slaked lime $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ (Exothermic). White-washing: forms shiny $\text{CaCO}_3$ over 2–3 days with atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$.
  - Decomposition: Thermal ($\text{CaCO}_3 \rightarrow \text{CaO} + \text{CO}_2$); Electrolytic ($2\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \mathbf{2H_2\text{ (Cathode)} + 1O_2\text{ (Anode)}}$, $2:1$ volume ratio!); Photolytic ($AgBr \rightarrow Ag + Br_2$ in black-and-white photography).
  - Displacement: Reactivity series ($K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > [H] > Cu > Hg > Ag > Au$). Iron nail in blue $\text{CuSO}_4 \rightarrow$ pale green $\text{FeSO}_4$ + reddish Cu deposit.
  - Double Displacement: Precipitation ($\text{BaCl}_2 + \text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4 \rightarrow \mathbf{\text{BaSO}_4\text{ white ppt}}$ Barium meal; $Pb(NO_3)_2 + KI \rightarrow \mathbf{PbI_2\text{ yellow ppt}}$).
* **Redox (OIL RIG)**: Oxidation is Loss of e⁻ (increases oxidation number); Reduction is Gain of e⁻ (decreases oxidation number).
  - Substance oxidized = **Reducing Agent**; Substance reduced = **Oxidizing Agent**.
* **Corrosion & Rancidity**:
  - Rust formula: $\mathbf{\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 \cdot x\text{H}_2\text{O}}$ (Hydrated ferric oxide). **Weight of iron INCREASES upon rusting**! Both $\text{O}_2$ and $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ required.
  - Galvanization: Zinc ($Zn$) coating acts as **sacrificial anode** (even when scratched).
  - Copper corrosion: Green basic copper carbonate ($\text{CuCO}_3 \cdot \text{Cu(OH)}_2$); Silver tarnish: Black silver sulfide ($\text{Ag}_2\text{S}$).
  - Rancidity: Oxidation of fats/oils $\rightarrow$ Prevented by **Nitrogen gas flushing** in potato chip bags, antioxidants BHA/BHT.
* **Catalysis**: Lowers Activation Energy ($E_a$). **Cannot change $\Delta H$ or equilibrium yield ($K_{\text{eq}}$)**!
  - Haber process ($\text{NH}_3$): **Iron ($Fe$)** catalyst + Molybdenum ($Mo$) promoter.
  - Contact process ($\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$): **Vanadium Pentoxide ($\text{V}_2\text{O}_5$)**.
  - Hydrogenation of vegetable oils (Vanaspati ghee): **Nickel ($Ni$)** catalyst.
  - Enzymes: Biological catalysts (Ptyalin $\rightarrow$ starch; Pepsin $\rightarrow$ proteins in stomach pH 1.5; Zymase in yeast $\rightarrow$ alcohol).
* **Mole Concept**: $1\text{ Mole} = \mathbf{6.022 \times 10^{23}}$ (Avogadro $N_A$). Molar mass in grams $= \mathbf{22.4\text{ Litres of gas at STP}}$.

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: What is the ratio of volumes of gases collected at the cathode vs. anode during the electrolysis of water?** | **$2:1$** ($2\text{ volumes of Hydrogen}$ at Cathode to $1\text{ volume of Oxygen}$ at Anode). |
| **Q2: Does the weight of an iron nail increase, decrease, or remain unchanged when it rusts?** | **Increases**, because rust ($\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 \cdot x\text{H}_2\text{O}$) incorporates the added chemical mass of atmospheric oxygen and water molecules. |
| **Q3: Why does galvanized iron continue to resist rust even if its zinc coating is scratched?** | Zinc has a higher oxidation potential than iron and acts as a **sacrificial anode**, preferentially corroding to protect the exposed iron beneath it. |
| **Q4: Which catalyst is used industrially for the hydrogenation of liquid vegetable oils into solid vanaspati ghee?** | Finely divided **Nickel ($Ni$)** at $\sim 200^\circ\text{C}$ (or Platinum/Palladium). |
| **Q5: Can a catalyst increase the final equilibrium yield of a reversible chemical reaction?** | **No.** A catalyst speeds up forward and reverse reactions equally, reaching equilibrium faster without altering the equilibrium constant ($K_{\text{eq}}$) or yield. |
| **Q6: Why are potato chip packets flushed with Nitrogen gas during packaging?** | Nitrogen is chemically unreactive and displaces atmospheric oxygen, preventing the **oxidative rancidity of oils and fats**. |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Electrolysis Gas Ratio** | "Oxygen volume is double Hydrogen volume." | **Hydrogen is DOUBLE Oxygen volume ($2:1$)**; water formula is $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ ($2\text{ H}$ atoms per $1\text{ O}$ atom). |
| **Iron Rusting Weight** | "Rust flakes off, so rusted iron weighs less." | Before flaking, chemical combination with oxygen and water makes the **total mass INCREASE**. |
| **Catalyst Thermodynamics** | "Catalysts decrease the enthalpy change ($\Delta H$) of reactions." | $\Delta H$ is a state function and **remains strictly unchanged**; a catalyst lowers only the kinetic **Activation Energy ($E_a$)**. |
| **White-Wash Reaction** | "Quicklime on walls turns white due to calcium chloride." | Slaked lime reacts with atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$ over 2–3 days to form shiny white **Calcium Carbonate ($\text{CaCO}_3$, Limestone)**. |
