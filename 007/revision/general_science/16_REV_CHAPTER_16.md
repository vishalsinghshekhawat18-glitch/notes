# REVISION CHAPTER 16: ACIDS, BASES, SALTS & PH

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **3 Theories**:
  - Arrhenius: Acid $= H^+$ (Hydronium $H_3O^+$); Base $= OH^-$ in water.
  - Brønsted-Lowry: Acid = **Proton Donor**; Base = **Proton Acceptor** (Conjugate acid-base pairs differ by $1\text{ H}^+$).
  - Lewis: Acid = **Electron-Pair Acceptor** ($BF_3, AlCl_3$); Base = **Electron-Pair Donor** ($:NH_3, H_2\ddot{O}:$).
* **pH Scale**: $\text{pH} = -\log_{10}[H^+]$; $\text{pH} + \text{pOH} = 14$ at $25^\circ\text{C}$. Logarithmic scale ($1\text{ unit change} = 10\times$).
  - Blood: **$7.35\text{--}7.45$** (Bicarbonate buffer $\text{H}_2\text{CO}_3 / \text{HCO}_3^-$).
  - Gastric juice: **$1.2\text{--}2.0$** ($HCl$ activates pepsin); Acid rain threshold: **$< 5.6$**.
  - Milk of Magnesia: $Mg(OH)_2$ antacid ($\text{pH} \approx 10.5$).
* **Indicators**:
  - Litmus (Lichen dye, purple neutral): Acid = **Red**, Base = **Blue**.
  - Phenolphthalein: Acid = **Colorless**, Base = **Vivid Pink**.
  - Methyl Orange: Acid = **Red**, Base = **Yellow**.
  - Turmeric: Base turns **Reddish-Brown** (soap on curry stain).
  - Olfactory: Onion/Vanilla/Clove lose scent in **Base**.
* **Natural Acids**:
  - Formic/Methanoic ($HCOOH$): Ant/Nettle sting (neutralized by baking soda).
  - Acetic/Ethanoic ($CH_3COOH$): Vinegar ($4\text{--}8\%$).
  - Citric: Lemon/Orange; Tartaric: Tamarind (Baking powder); Lactic: Curd; Oxalic: Tomato (Kidney stones).
* **Commercial Salts**:
  - Baking Soda: $\text{NaHCO}_3$ (Solvay process). Baking Powder $= \text{NaHCO}_3 + \text{Tartaric Acid}$ (neutralizes bitter carbonate taste, releases $\text{CO}_2$ fluff).
  - Washing Soda: $\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3 \cdot 10\text{H}_2\text{O}$ (Softens permanent hard water).
  - Bleaching Powder: $\text{CaOCl}_2$ (Slaked lime + $Cl_2$, water disinfectant).
  - Plaster of Paris: $\mathbf{\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O}}$ (Gypsum $\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$ heated to strictly **$100^\circ\text{C}$**; above $100^\circ\text{C} \rightarrow$ Dead Burnt Plaster).

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: What is the crucial difference in composition between baking soda and baking powder?** | Baking soda is pure $\text{NaHCO}_3$; Baking powder is a mixture of **$\text{NaHCO}_3$ and an edible acid (Tartaric Acid)** to neutralize the bitter sodium carbonate byproduct. |
| **Q2: What happens if Gypsum ($\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$) is heated significantly above 100°C?** | It loses all water of crystallization, forming anhydrous **Dead Burnt Plaster ($\text{CaSO}_4$)**, which permanently loses the ability to set with water. |
| **Q3: What chemical gives the ant sting its sharp burning pain, and how is it chemically neutralized?** | **Methanoic Acid (Formic Acid, $HCOOH$)**; neutralized by applying a mild base like moist **Baking Soda ($\text{NaHCO}_3$)** or calamine lotion ($ZnCO_3$). |
| **Q4: Why does a curry stain on a white shirt turn reddish-brown when washed with soap?** | Turmeric contains natural curcumin indicator, which stays yellow in acids but turns **reddish-brown in basic solutions** (soap is alkaline). |
| **Q5: What is the primary chemical buffer system that maintains human blood pH between 7.35 and 7.45?** | The **Carbonic Acid - Bicarbonate Buffer System** ($\text{H}_2\text{CO}_3 \rightleftharpoons \text{H}^+ + \text{HCO}_3^-$). |
| **Q6: Why is phenolphthalein colorless in vinegar but pink in washing soda solution?** | Phenolphthalein is an acid-base indicator that is **colorless in acidic solutions** ($\text{pH} < 7$, vinegar) and turns **vivid pink in basic alkaline solutions** ($\text{pH} > 8.3$, washing soda). |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Acid Rain pH** | "Acid rain is any rain with pH below 7." | Natural rain is slightly acidic ($\text{pH} \approx 5.6$) due to dissolved $CO_2$. **Acid rain is defined strictly as rain with $\text{pH} < 5.6$** (due to $SO_2$ and $NO_x$). |
| **Dead Burnt Plaster** | "Heating gypsum to $200^\circ\text{C}$ speeds up POP setting." | Overheating dehydrates it into **Dead Burnt Plaster ($\text{CaSO}_4$)**, which **cannot set at all**! |
| **Bleaching Powder Formula** | "Bleaching powder is Calcium Chloride ($CaCl_2$)." | It is **Calcium Oxychloride / Hypochlorite ($\text{CaOCl}_2$)**, releasing active nascent chlorine disinfectant. |
| **Baking Soda Alone** | "Baking soda alone makes cakes taste sweet." | Pure baking soda decomposes into **Sodium Carbonate ($\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$)**, which tastes **unpleasant, bitter, and soapy** unless neutralized by tartaric acid. |
