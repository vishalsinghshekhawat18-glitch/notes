## 1. HIGH-SPEED KNOWLEDGE SKELETON

```
                     HUMAN PHYSIOLOGY II SKELETON
                     
  [Excretion]   ──► Ammonotelism (Fish) | Ureotelism (Mammals, Urea via Liver Ornithine cycle) | Uricotelism (Birds)
                    Nephron: PCT (100% Glucose reabsorbed) -> Loop of Henle (Counter-current 1200 mOsm/L) -> DCT
                    RAAS: Renin -> Angiotensin I -> ACE -> Angiotensin II (Vasoconstriction + Aldosterone)
                    Osmoregulation: ADH (Aquaporins in Collecting Duct; Lack = Diabetes Insipidus) | ANF (RAAS Antagonist)
  [Nervous]     ──► Action Potential: RMP -70 mV -> Depolarization (VGSC open, +30 mV) -> Repolarization (VGKC open)
                    Synapse: Ca2+ influx -> Vesicle exocytosis -> Neurotransmitter (ACh, GABA inhibitory)
                    Brain: Cerebrum (Cognition) | Thalamus (Relay) | Hypothalamus (Thermostat, Thirst, Hunger)
                    Midbrain (Corpora quadrigemina) | Cerebellum (Balance, Ataxia) | Medulla (Vital autonomic centers)
  [Endocrine]   ──► Receptors: Peptide/Protein -> Membrane (cAMP, IP3, Ca2+) | Steroid/Thyroid -> Nuclear (DNA/Gene)
                    Pituitary: Anterior (GH, TSH, ACTH, LH/FSH, PRL) | Posterior (Oxytocin [Milk let-down], ADH)
                    Thyroid: T3/T4 (BMR; Cretinism, Graves) | Calcitonin (Hypocalcemic) vs PTH (Hypercalcemic)
                    Adrenal: Cortex (Aldosterone, Cortisol) | Medulla (Adrenaline 3F)
                    Pancreas: Insulin (Hypoglycemic, GLUT-4) vs Glucagon (Hyperglycemic)
```

## 2. ATOMIC FORMULAS & NUMERICAL CONSTANTS

* **Glomerular Dynamics:**
  * $\text{Net Filtration Pressure (NFP)} = \text{GHP } (60) - [\text{BCOP } (32) + \text{CHP } (18)] = \mathbf{10\text{ mmHg}}$.
  * $\text{Glomerular Filtration Rate (GFR)} = \mathbf{125\text{ mL/min}} = \mathbf{180\text{ Liters/day}}$.
  * Daily Urine Output: $\approx \mathbf{1.5\text{ Liters/day}}$ ($99\%$ reabsorbed).
* **Nerve Potentials:**
  * Resting Potential (RMP): $-70\text{ mV}$.
  * Threshold Potential: $-55\text{ mV}$.
  * Action Potential Peak: $+30\text{ mV}$.

## 3. CORE EXAM HIGHLIGHTS & PITFALLS

* **Urea Synthesis Site:** Urea is synthesized **EXCLUSIVELY in the Liver** via the Krebs-Henseleit Ornithine cycle; the kidneys only filter and excrete it!
* **Loop of Henle Permeabilities:**
  * Descending Limb: Permeable to water, impermeable to $\text{NaCl}$.
  * Ascending Limb: **Strictly impermeable to water**, actively pumps out $\text{NaCl}$.
* **Hormone Antagonisms Matrix:**
  * Blood Glucose: **Insulin** (Lowers) $\longleftrightarrow$ **Glucagon** (Raises).
  * Blood Calcium: **Calcitonin** (Lowers) $\longleftrightarrow$ **Parathyroid Hormone / PTH** (Raises).
  * Blood Pressure / Sodium: **Aldosterone** (Retains $Na^+$) $\longleftrightarrow$ **ANF** (Excretes $Na^+$).
* **Diabetes Mellitus vs. Diabetes Insipidus:**
  * Diabetes Mellitus: Insulin deficiency/resistance $\implies$ Hyperglycemia + Glucosuria + Ketosis.
  * Diabetes Insipidus: **ADH (Vasopressin) deficiency** $\implies$ Profuse hypotonic polyuria ($15\text{ L/day}$) with **normal blood sugar and zero glucosuria**.
* **Hypothalamus:** Regulates body temperature (thermostat), hunger, thirst, and biological rhythms.
* **Brainstem Components:** Midbrain + Pons + Medulla Oblongata (Cerebellum is NOT part of the brainstem).
