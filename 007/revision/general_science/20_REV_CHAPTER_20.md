## 2. ATOMIC FORMULAS & NUMERICAL CONSTANTS

* **Ribosome Subunits:**
  * Prokaryote ($70\text{S}$): $50\text{S} \text{ (23S + 5S rRNA)} + 30\text{S} \text{ (16S rRNA)}$.
  * Eukaryote ($80\text{S}$): $60\text{S} \text{ (28S + 5.8S + 5S rRNA)} + 40\text{S} \text{ (18S rRNA)}$.
* **Membrane Pump Stoichiometry:**
  * $Na^+/K^+$ ATPase: Hydrolase of $1\text{ ATP} \implies \text{Extrudes } 3\text{ }Na^+ \text{ outward}, \text{imports } 2\text{ }K^+ \text{ inward}$.
* **DNA & Chromosome Count Across Mitosis & Meiosis:**
  * $G_1$: $2n, 2C$
  * $S \text{ & } G_2$: $2n, 4C$
  * Mitotic Anaphase: $4n, 4C$ (centromeres split) $\implies$ Telophase/Cytokinesis: two daughter cells with $2n, 2C$.
  * Meiotic Anaphase I: $2n, 4C$ (homologs separate; centromeres **do not** split) $\implies$ Interkinesis: two cells with $n, 2C$.
  * Meiotic Anaphase II: $2n, 2C$ (centromeres split) $\implies$ Gametes: four cells with $n, 1C$.

## 3. CORE EXAM HIGHLIGHTS & PITFALLS

* **Virchow’s Doctrine:** *Omnis cellula e cellula* (1855) — all living cells arise exclusively from pre-existing cells.
* **Exceptions to Cell Theory:** Viruses (acellular, no protoplasm), Viroids (naked circular ssRNA), Prions (protein-only), Mature Mammalian RBCs (enucleated).
* **Endomembrane System Members:** RER, SER, Golgi, Lysosomes, Vacuoles ONLY. (Mitochondria, Chloroplasts, Peroxisomes are EXCLUDED).
* **Marker Enzymes:**
  * Lysosome: Acid Phosphatase / Acid Hydrolases ($\text{pH} \approx 4.5 - 5.0$).
  * Peroxisome: Catalase ($2\text{H}_2\text{O}_2 \to 2\text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{O}_2$).
* **Microtubule Architecture:**
  * Centriole / Basal Body: $9 + 0$ triplet pattern.
  * Cilia / Eukaryotic Flagella: $9 + 2$ doublet axoneme pattern with Dynein motor arms.
* **Prophase I Sub-stages Mnemonic (LZPDD):**
  * **L**eptotene: Condensation, bouquet stage.
  * **Z**ygotene: **Synapsis**, formation of synaptonemal complex, bivalents.
  * **P**achytene: **Crossing over** via Recombinase multienzyme complex; tetrads visible.
  * **D**iplotene: Dissolution of synaptonemal complex; **Chiasmata** visible.
  * **D**iakinesis: **Terminalization** of chiasmata; nuclear envelope breakdown.
* **Cytokinesis:**
  * Animal: Centripetal furrowing via Actin-Myosin contractile ring.
  * Plant: Centrifugal cell plate formation via Golgi-derived Phragmoplast.
