# REVISION CHAPTER 07: WAVE MOTION, SHM & ACOUSTICS (SOUND)

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **SHM Mechanics**: Restoring force $F = -kx$; Acceleration $a = -\omega^2 x$.
  - At Mean: $x = 0$, $v = v_{\text{max}} = \omega A$, $a = 0$, $\text{KE} = \text{max}$, $\text{PE} = 0$.
  - At Extreme: $x = \pm A$, $v = 0$, $a = \omega^2 A$, $\text{KE} = 0$, $\text{PE} = \text{max}$.
* **Simple Pendulum**: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}$.
  - **Independent of mass** of bob!
  - Girl standing on swing: Center of gravity rises $\rightarrow l \downarrow \implies T \downarrow$ (swings faster).
  - Summer: Rod expands $l \uparrow \implies T \uparrow$ (clock runs slow); Winter: $l \downarrow \implies T \downarrow$ (clock runs fast).
  - Second's Pendulum: Period $T = 2\text{ s} \implies l \approx 1\text{ metre} = 100\text{ cm}$.
* **Wave Properties**: $v = f\lambda$. Transverse (perpendicular, crest/trough, can polarize, solids only); Longitudinal (parallel, compression/rarefaction, **cannot polarize**, solids/liquids/gases).
* **Speed of Sound**: $v = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma P}{\rho}} = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma RT}{M}}$.
  - Hierarchy: $\mathbf{v_{\text{solids}} > v_{\text{liquids}} > v_{\text{gases}}}$ (Steel $\sim 5130\text{ m/s} >$ Water $\sim 1482\text{ m/s} >$ Air $\sim 332\text{ m/s}$).
  - Vacuum: **Zero sound** (requires medium).
  - Temperature: $+0.61\text{ m/s per } 1^\circ\text{C}$ rise ($v \propto \sqrt{T}$).
  - Humidity: Moist air is less dense $\implies$ sound travels faster.
  - Pressure: **Zero effect on sound speed**!
* **Acoustic Spectra**:
  - Infrasound: $<20\text{ Hz}$ (Earthquakes, elephants, whales).
  - Audible: $20\text{ Hz to } 20,000\text{ Hz}$ (Human).
  - Ultrasound: $>20\text{ kHz}$ (Bats, dolphins, SONAR, lithotripsy, ultrasonography).
* **Reflection & Doppler**:
  - Echo: Minimum distance $\mathbf{17.2\text{ metres}}$ (at $20^\circ\text{C}$, persistence of hearing $= 0.1\text{ s}$).
  - SONAR: $d = \frac{v \times t}{2}$.
  - Doppler: Source/observer approaching $\implies$ pitch rises ($f' > f_0$); receding $\implies$ pitch drops ($f' < f_0$).
  - Mach number: $\frac{v_{\text{object}}}{v_{\text{sound}}}$. Supersonic ($>1$) causes **Sonic Boom**.
* **Human Ear**: Stapes is smallest bone; 3 ossicles amplify pressure $20\text{--}30\times$; Cochlea converts fluid waves into electrical nerve impulses.

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Can sound waves be polarized? Why or why not?** | **No.** Sound in fluids is a **longitudinal wave**, and longitudinal waves oscillate parallel to wave motion, making polarization physically impossible. |
| **Q2: What is the minimum distance required from a reflecting wall to hear a distinct echo at 20°C?** | Exactly **$17.2\text{ metres}$** (round-trip distance $= 34.4\text{ m}$ in $0.1\text{ s}$). |
| **Q3: What happens to a pendulum clock in the summer?** | High temperature causes thermal expansion of the metal rod ($l \uparrow$), increasing the period ($T \uparrow$). The clock **runs slow (loses time)**. |
| **Q4: Why does a girl swinging on a swing oscillate faster if she stands up?** | Standing raises her Center of Gravity, shortening the effective pendulum length ($l \downarrow$); since $T = 2\pi\sqrt{l/g}$, the period **decreases**, swinging faster. |
| **Q5: How does increasing atmospheric pressure affect the speed of sound at constant temperature?** | **Zero effect.** Sound speed is completely independent of pressure changes because gas density increases in exact proportion ($\frac{P}{\rho} = \text{constant}$). |
| **Q6: Why can elephants detect distant earthquakes before humans feel ground tremors?** | Earthquakes emit low-frequency **infrasound waves ($<20\text{ Hz}$)** that travel through the ground and are perceptible to elephants, but below human hearing. |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sound in Space** | "Astronauts on Moon shout to be heard through thin lunar air." | The Moon is in **vacuum**. Sound waves cannot propagate without a material medium; astronauts communicate exclusively via **radio EM waves**. |
| **Pressure and Sound** | "Sound travels twice as fast in high-pressure weather." | At constant temperature, **pressure has zero effect** on the speed of sound. |
| **Pendulum Mass** | "A 10 kg brass bob swings slower than a 1 kg plastic bob." | Time period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{l/g}$ is **strictly independent of mass**; both swing with identical periods. |
| **Mosquito vs. Lion** | "A lion's roar has higher pitch than a mosquito buzz." | A lion's roar has massive **loudness (amplitude)** but low pitch (grave); a mosquito's buzz has low loudness but **very high pitch (frequency)**. |
