# REVISION CHAPTER 14: CHEMICAL BONDING & PERIODIC TABLE

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **History**:
  - Dobereiner Triads (Li-Na-K, Cl-Br-I, Ca-Sr-Ba).
  - Newlands Octaves (Failed past Calcium).
  - Mendeleev (1869): Periodic law based on **Atomic Mass**; Predicted Eka-Aluminium (Gallium), Eka-Silicon (Germanium), Eka-Boron (Scandium).
  - Moseley (1913): **Modern Periodic Law** based on **Atomic Number ($Z$)** ($\sqrt{\nu} \propto Z$). 7 Periods, 18 Groups.
* **Blocks**: $s$ (reactive metals), $p$ (metals/non-metals/metalloids), $d$ (transition metals), $f$ (lanthanides $4f$, actinides $5f$ radioactive).
* **Periodic Trends**:
  - Across Period ($L \rightarrow R$): Atomic Radius $\downarrow$ ($Z_{\text{eff}} \uparrow$), Ionization Energy $\uparrow$, Electronegativity $\uparrow$, Metallic character $\downarrow$.
  - Down Group ($T \rightarrow B$): Atomic Radius $\uparrow$ (new shells), Ionization Energy $\downarrow$, Metallic character $\uparrow$, Electronegativity $\downarrow$.
  - **Extremes**:
    - Highest Electronegativity: **Fluorine ($F = 4.0$)** (Pauling scale: $F > O > N \approx Cl$).
    - Highest Electron Affinity: **Chlorine ($Cl > F$)**! (Fluorine $2p$ has high inter-electronic repulsion).
    - Highest Ionization Energy: **Helium ($He$)**; Lowest: **Cesium ($Cs$)**.
* **Chemical Bonds**:
  - Ionic: Complete electron transfer ($NaCl$). High MP/BP, brittle, **insulator in solid state, conducts in molten/solution state**.
  - Covalent: Mutual electron sharing ($H_2, O_2, N_2, CH_4$). $N_2$ triple bond ($946\text{ kJ/mol}$) makes it inert.
* **Hydrogen Bonding**: Only with **Fluorine, Oxygen, Nitrogen ("FON")**.
  - Why $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ is liquid ($BP=100^\circ\text{C}$) while $\text{H}_2\text{S}$ is gas ($BP=-60^\circ\text{C}$): Intermolecular H-bonds in water.
  - DNA double helix: $A=T$ (2 H-bonds), $G\equiv C$ (3 H-bonds).
  - Ice floats: Hexagonal open cage structure expanded by H-bonds.

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Which element has the highest electron affinity (electron gain enthalpy) in the periodic table?** | **Chlorine ($Cl$)**, because Fluorine's ultra-compact $2p$ subshell creates strong electron-electron repulsion, releasing less energy upon electron capture. |
| **Q2: Why is water ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$) a liquid at room temperature while hydrogen sulfide ($\text{H}_2\text{S}$) is a gas?** | Oxygen's high electronegativity and small radius allow it to form strong **intermolecular hydrogen bonds** that hold liquid water together; sulfur cannot form H-bonds. |
| **Q3: Does solid table salt ($NaCl$) conduct electric current?** | **No.** In solid state, ions are locked rigidly in crystalline lattice points; it conducts electricity **only when molten or dissolved in water**. |
| **Q4: What element did Mendeleev predict as "Eka-Aluminium"?** | **Gallium ($Ga$)**, which matched his predicted mass and chemical properties when discovered. |
| **Q5: Which elements are capable of forming hydrogen bonds with hydrogen?** | Strictly **Fluorine, Oxygen, and Nitrogen ("FON")**, due to their high electronegativity and small atomic size. |
| **Q6: Arrange in increasing order of ionic radius: $Al^{3+}, Mg^{2+}, Na^+, F^-, O^{2-}, N^{3-}$.** | **$Al^{3+} < Mg^{2+} < Na^+ < F^- < O^{2-} < N^{3-}$** (Isoelectronic series: more negative charge = larger ionic radius). |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Highest Electron Affinity** | "Fluorine has the highest electron affinity because it is the most electronegative." | **Chlorine ($Cl$) has the highest electron affinity** in the periodic table; Fluorine has the highest *electronegativity*. |
| **Solid Ionic Conduction** | "Salt conducts electricity because it is an ionic electrolyte." | Solid salt is an **absolute electrical insulator**; mobile free ions exist only in the **molten state or aqueous solution**. |
| **Hydrogen Bonding in HCl** | "HCl forms hydrogen bonds because chlorine is electronegative." | **Zero H-bonding in $HCl$**; chlorine's atomic radius is too large. Hydrogen bonds occur strictly with **Fluorine, Oxygen, and Nitrogen**. |
| **Periodic Law Basis** | "Mendeleev organized elements by atomic number." | Mendeleev organized by **Atomic Mass**. **Henry Moseley (1913)** established the Modern Periodic Law based on **Atomic Number ($Z$)**. |
