# REVISION CHAPTER 06: THERMAL PHYSICS & THERMODYNAMICS

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **Heat vs Temp**: Heat is energy in transit ($1\text{ cal} = 4.186\text{ J}$); Temp is average kinetic energy of molecules.
* **Conversion Formula**: $\mathbf{\frac{C}{5} = \frac{F-32}{9} = \frac{K-273.15}{5}}$.
  - Coincidence Point: $\mathbf{-40^\circ\text{C} = -40^\circ\text{F}}$.
  - Absolute Zero: $0\text{ K} = -273.15^\circ\text{C}$ (No negative Kelvin).
  - Triple Point of Water: $273.16\text{ K} = 0.01^\circ\text{C}$ at $611.65\text{ Pa}$.
* **Expansion**: $\alpha : \beta : \gamma = 1 : 2 : 3$. Bimetallic strip uses differential expansion ($\alpha_{\text{brass}} > \alpha_{\text{iron}}$).
* **Anomalous Expansion of Water ($0^\circ\text{C}$ to $4^\circ\text{C}$)**:
  - Water contracts on heating from $0^\circ\text{C}$ to $4^\circ\text{C}$ $\rightarrow$ **Maximum Density at $4^\circ\text{C}$** ($1000\text{ kg/m}^3$).
  - Ice expands by ~9% in volume $\rightarrow$ density $0.917\text{ g/cm}^3$ (Floats on water).
  - Frozen lakes: Ice insulates top; liquid water remains at $+4^\circ\text{C}$ below, sustaining aquatic life.
* **Specific Heat & Latent Heat**: $Q = mc\Delta T$; Water has highest specific heat ($4186\text{ J/kg}\cdot\text{K}$) $\rightarrow$ radiator coolant.
  - Latent heat: $Q = mL$ (Temp stays constant during phase change).
  - Steam burns at $100^\circ\text{C}$ are more severe than boiling water burns due to extra **$540\text{ cal/g}$ ($2.26 \times 10^6\text{ J/kg}$)** latent heat of vaporization!
  - Sublimation: Solid $\rightarrow$ Gas (Camphor, Dry Ice, Ammonium Chloride).
* **Modes of Transfer**:
  - Conduction: Solids, lattice vibrations (Silver best, Wood insulator).
  - Convection: Fluids only, density buoyancy (Sea/land breeze, fridge freezer at top).
  - Radiation: EM waves, vacuum (Stefan's Law $E \propto T^4$; Wien's Law $\lambda_{\text{max}} T = \text{const}$; Kirchhoff's Law: good absorbers are good emitters).
  - Thermos Flask: Double-walled vacuum (stops conduction/convection) + Silvered walls (stops radiation).
* **Laws of Thermodynamics**:
  - Zeroth: Thermal equilibrium $\rightarrow$ defines temperature and thermometer.
  - First: Energy conservation $\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W$ (Adiabatic tube burst $\rightarrow$ rapid expansion cools air).
  - Second: Heat cannot spontaneously flow cold to hot (Clausius); Engine efficiency $\eta < 1$ (Kelvin-Planck); Entropy always increases.
  - Carnot Efficiency: $\eta = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1} = \frac{T_1 - T_2}{T_1}$ (Temperatures in Kelvin).

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: At what temperature do the Celsius and Fahrenheit thermometers read the exact same value?** | **$-40^\circ$** ($-40^\circ\text{C} = -40^\circ\text{F}$). |
| **Q2: Why can fish survive at the bottom of frozen lakes in winter?** | Water reaches **maximum density at $4^\circ\text{C}$** and sinks to the bottom. Ice floats on top and acts as a thermal insulator, keeping the deep water at a liquid $+4^\circ\text{C}$. |
| **Q3: Why are burns caused by steam at 100°C much more severe than boiling water at 100°C?** | Steam carries an extra **$540\text{ calories/gram}$ ($2,260\text{ J/g}$)** of latent heat of vaporization, released onto flesh upon condensing. |
| **Q4: Why does an iron chair feel colder than a wooden chair on a chilly morning?** | Both are at identical temperatures, but iron has a **vastly higher thermal conductivity**, conducting heat away from human skin much faster. |
| **Q5: Which physical law explains why black surfaces absorb heat effectively and glow brightly when heated?** | **Kirchhoff's Law of Radiation** (*"Good absorbers are good emitters"*). |
| **Q6: Why does air escaping from a suddenly burst bicycle tube feel cold?** | It undergoes rapid **adiabatic expansion** ($\Delta Q = 0$); the gas does mechanical work at the expense of its internal energy ($\Delta U < 0$), lowering its temperature. |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kelvin Scale Negative** | "Temperature of interstellar space is $-5\text{ K}$." | The Kelvin scale starts at **Absolute Zero ($0\text{ K}$)**; negative Kelvin temperatures are physically impossible. |
| **Carnot Engine Efficiency** | Using Celsius in $\eta = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1}$. | Temperatures $T_1$ and $T_2$ **MUST strictly be in Kelvin** ($T_{\text{Kelvin}} = T_{\text{Celsius}} + 273.15$). |
| **Cooking in Mountains** | "Water boils at $100^\circ\text{C}$ faster in mountains." | Water boils at a **lower temperature** ($<100^\circ\text{C}$, e.g. $90^\circ\text{C}$) due to lower atmospheric pressure; food takes **longer to cook** in open pots! |
| **Double Sweaters** | "One thick sweater is warmer than two thin sweaters." | Two thin sweaters trap an intervening layer of **stagnant air** (exceptional thermal insulator), providing superior warmth. |
