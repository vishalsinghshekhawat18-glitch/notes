## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **Photoelectric Effect**: Einstein ($E = h\nu - \Phi_0$). Intensity $\implies$ Photoelectric current (number of electrons); Frequency $\implies$ Maximum Kinetic Energy ($K_{\text{max}}$). Cesium has lowest work function ($\Phi_0 = 2.14\text{ eV}$). De Broglie wave: $\lambda = \frac{h}{mv}$.
* **Atomic Models**: Rutherford's $\alpha$-scattering $\implies$ discovery of dense nucleus ($10^{-15}\text{ m}$). Bohr's quantum model $\implies$ angular momentum quantized ($L = \frac{nh}{2\pi}$).
* **Nuclear Physics**: $E = mc^2$ ($1\text{ u} \approx 931.5\text{ MeV}$). Peak binding energy at **Iron-56 ($^{56}Fe \approx 8.8\text{ MeV}$)** $\implies$ most stable nucleus.
* **Radiation Hierarchy**:
  - Alpha ($\alpha, ^4_2He^{2+}$): **Highest ionization**, lowest penetration (stopped by paper).
  - Beta ($\beta, e^-$): Moderate ionization and penetration.
  - Gamma ($\gamma$, photon): **Highest penetration** (requires thick lead), lowest ionization.
  - Half-life: $T_{1/2} = \frac{0.693}{\lambda}$. Fraction remaining $= (1/2)^n$.
  - Medical Isotopes: Cobalt-60 (cancer therapy), Iodine-131 (thyroid), Carbon-14 (fossil dating), Sodium-24 (blood circulation).
* **Fission vs Fusion**:
  - Fission: $^{235}U + n \rightarrow \text{Ba} + \text{Kr} + 3n + 200\text{ MeV}$. Moderator = **Heavy Water ($\text{D}_2\text{O}$) / Graphite** (slows fast neutrons); Control rods = **Cadmium / Boron** (absorbs neutrons).
  - Fusion: Hydrogen $\rightarrow$ Helium in Sun ($>10^7\text{ K}$). Clean energy, 4x more energy per kg than fission (ITER Tokamak).
* **Semiconductors**: Energy gap $E_g \approx 1\text{ eV}$ (Conductors overlap; Insulators $E_g > 3\text{ eV}$).
  - Intrinsic: Pure $Si, Ge$. At $0\text{ K} \rightarrow$ insulator; Temp $\uparrow \implies R \downarrow$.
  - Extrinsic Doping: **Both n-type and p-type are strictly ELECTRICALLY NEUTRAL**!
    - n-type: Pentavalent impurity ($P, As, Sb$), majority carriers = **Electrons**.
    - p-type: Trivalent impurity ($B, Al, In, Ga$), majority carriers = **Holes**.
  - p-n Diode: Forward bias conducts; Reverse bias blocks $\rightarrow$ **Rectifier (AC to DC)**.
  - Optoelectronics: LED (Forward bias, light emission), Photodiode (Reverse bias), Solar Cell (Zero bias, light to electricity).

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: What is the net electrical charge on an n-type semiconductor crystal?** | Strictly **ZERO (Electrically Neutral)**, because the crystal contains neutral atoms with equal numbers of protons and electrons. |
| **Q2: Which radiation has the highest penetrating power and why?** | **Gamma rays ($\gamma$)**, because they are uncharged, high-energy electromagnetic photons that interact weakly with matter, requiring thick lead or concrete to stop. |
| **Q3: In a nuclear reactor, what is the role of heavy water ($\text{D}_2\text{O}$) versus cadmium rods?** | Heavy water acts as a **Moderator** to slow down fast neutrons to thermal speeds; Cadmium rods act as **Control Rods** to absorb neutrons and regulate the chain reaction. |
| **Q4: What element has the most thermodynamically stable nucleus in the universe?** | **Iron-56 ($^{56}_{26}Fe$)**, possessing the peak nuclear binding energy per nucleon ($\sim 8.8\text{ MeV/nucleon}$). |
| **Q5: In photoelectric emission, how does increasing light intensity affect electron kinetic energy?** | It has **ZERO effect on kinetic energy** (which depends strictly on light frequency); increasing intensity increases only the **number of electrons emitted per second**. |
| **Q6: Why is Cesium the metal of choice for photosensitive solar cells?** | Cesium has the **lowest work function ($\Phi_0 \approx 2.14\text{ eV}$)** of all elements, requiring minimal photon energy to eject electrons. |

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Charge on n-type** | "n-type silicon has a negative electrical charge." | It is **strictly electrically neutral**; electrons are merely majority charge carriers within a neutral lattice. |
| **Alpha Radiation Penetration** | "Alpha particles penetrate deepest because they are heaviest." | Alpha particles have the **LOWEST penetration** (stopped by paper) because their mass and charge make them rapidly ionize air and lose energy. |
| **Photoelectric Kinetic Energy**| "Bright high-wattage light ejects faster electrons than dim light of the same color." | Light intensity affects only the **quantity of electrons**; kinetic energy is governed solely by the **frequency / color** of the light ($E = h\nu - \Phi$). |
| **Nuclear Fission Moderator** | "Control rods slow down fast neutrons." | **Moderators** (Heavy water/Graphite) slow down neutrons; **Control rods** (Cadmium/Boron) *absorb* neutrons to prevent runaway reactions. |
