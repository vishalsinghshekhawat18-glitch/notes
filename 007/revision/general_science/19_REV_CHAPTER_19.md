# REVISION CHAPTER 19: EVERYDAY CHEMISTRY, POLYMERS & DRUGS

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **Polymers**:
  - Thermoplastic (linear, soft on heating, recyclable: Polythene, PVC, Nylon, Teflon).
  - Thermosetting (3D cross-linked, char on heating, non-recyclable: **Bakelite** electrical switches, **Melamine** unbreakable crockery).
  - High-yield monomers: Nylon 6,6 (Hexamethylenediamine + Adipic acid); Teflon (Tetrafluoroethylene, non-stick cookware); PVC (Vinyl chloride); Rayon (regenerated cellulose artificial silk).
  - Natural Rubber: cis-1,4-polyisoprene (Isoprene). **Vulcanization** (heating with $3\text{--}5\%$ Sulfur) creates disulfide $-S-S-$ cross-links $\rightarrow$ heat-resistant, tough tires.
* **Soaps & Detergents**:
  - Soap: Sodium/Potassium salt of fatty acid (Sodium Stearate $\text{C}_{17}\text{H}_{35}\text{COONa}$). Saponification byproduct $= \mathbf{Glycerol}$. Common salt ($NaCl$) precipitates soap via common ion effect.
  - Cleansing: **Micelles** (Hydrophobic tail inside oil droplet, hydrophilic head $-\text{COO}^-$ outside in water).
  - Hard Water: Soaps form insoluble sticky **Scum** with $Ca^{2+}/Mg^{2+}$. Detergents (alkyl benzene sulfonates) clean flawlessly in hard water (calcium salts are soluble).
* **Medicines**:
  - Analgesic (pain relief): Aspirin (anti-platelet blood thinner prevents heart attacks), Paracetamol.
  - Antibiotic: **Penicillin** (Alexander Fleming 1928, *Penicillium notatum*).
  - Antiseptic (living tissue) vs Disinfectant (inanimate floors): **$0.2\%$ Phenol = Antiseptic; $1.0\%$ Phenol = Disinfectant**! Tincture of iodine ($2\text{--}3\% I_2$ in alcohol). Dettol = Chloroxylenol + Terpineol.
* **Explosives & Fireworks**:
  - Dynamite: Nitroglycerin adsorbed on Kieselguhr clay (Alfred Nobel 1867). RDX (Cyclonite/Hexogen). Gunpowder ($75\% KNO_3 + 15\% C + 10\% S$).
  - Fireworks colors: **Crimson Red = Strontium ($Sr$)**; **Green = Barium ($Ba$)**; Yellow = Sodium; Blue = Copper.
* **Fertilizers, Cement & Glass**:
  - Urea ($\text{NH}_2\text{CONH}_2$): **$46\%\text{ Nitrogen}$** (highest in solid fertilizers; synthesized by Wöhler 1828, destroyed vital force theory).
  - Portland Cement: Lime $CaO$ ($60\text{--}65\%$) + Silica $SiO_2$ ($20\text{--}25\%$). **Gypsum ($2\text{--}3\%$) RETARDS setting time** (prevents premature flash setting).
  - Glass: Supercooled liquid. Borosilicate (Pyrex with $B_2O_3$) resists thermal cracking. Flint glass (Lead oxide $PbO$) for optical lenses.

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Why is Gypsum ($2\text{--}3\%$) mixed with clinker during cement manufacturing?** | To act as a **setting retarder**, slowing down the rate of hydration to prevent premature flash-setting and provide adequate working time for masons. |
| **Q2: What percentage of nitrogen by mass is present in pure Urea fertilizer?** | Exactly **$46\%$ Nitrogen** ($\frac{28}{60} \times 100 \approx 46.6\%$), the highest concentration among solid nitrogenous fertilizers. |
| **Q3: What chemical elements produce the vivid crimson red and bright green colors in fireworks?** | **Strontium ($Sr$)** produces crimson red; **Barium ($Ba$)** produces bright emerald green. |
| **Q4: Why can synthetic detergents clean effectively in hard water while soaps fail?** | The calcium and magnesium salts of synthetic alkyl benzene sulfonates are **completely water-soluble**, producing zero scum or precipitate. |
| **Q5: What is the chemical difference in concentration between phenol as an antiseptic vs. disinfectant?** | A **$0.2\%$ solution of Phenol is an Antiseptic** (safe on human skin); a **$1.0\%$ solution of Phenol is a Disinfectant** (corrosive to living tissue, used on hospital floors). |
| **Q6: What chemical element is added to natural rubber during vulcanization, and what structural bond does it create?** | **Elemental Sulfur ($S$)**, which forms covalent **disulfide cross-links ($-S-S-$)** between polyisoprene chains, preventing thermal softening and slippage. |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gypsum in Cement** | "Gypsum is added to make cement set faster." | **Exact opposite!** Gypsum is a **retarder** added to **slow down the setting rate**, preventing flash-setting. |
| **Urea Nitrogen Content** | "Urea contains 20% nitrogen." | Urea ($\text{NH}_2\text{CONH}_2$) contains **$46\%$ Nitrogen by mass**, higher than ammonium nitrate or sulfate. |
| **Pyrex Laboratory Glass** | "Pyrex glass resists heat because of added lead oxide." | Pyrex contains **Boron Trioxide ($\text{B}_2\text{O}_3$)**, which minimizes thermal expansion; Lead oxide is used in optical flint glass. |
| **Natural Rubber Monomer** | "Natural rubber is poly-chloroprene." | Natural rubber is **cis-1,4-polyisoprene** (Monomer: Isoprene). Chloroprene polymerizes into *Neoprene* (synthetic rubber). |
