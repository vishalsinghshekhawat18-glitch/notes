
## 2. ATOMIC FORMULAS & NUMERICAL CONSTANTS

* **B-DNA Dimensions:**
  * Diameter: $2.0\text{ nm}$ ($20\text{ \AA}$)
  * Pitch / Turn: $3.4\text{ nm}$ ($34\text{ \AA}$) with $10\text{ base pairs}$
  * Inter-base distance: $0.34\text{ nm}$ ($3.4\text{ \AA}$)
* **Michaelis-Menten Kinetics:**
  * $V_0 = \frac{V_{max}[S]}{K_m + [S]}$; when $[S] = K_m \implies V_0 = \frac{1}{2}V_{max}$
  * Low $K_m \implies$ High substrate affinity; High $K_m \implies$ Low substrate affinity.
* **Caloric Value:**
  * Fats: $9.3\text{ kcal/g}$ (gross), $9.0\text{ kcal/g}$ (physiologic)
  * Carbohydrates: $4.1\text{ kcal/g}$ (gross), $4.0\text{ kcal/g}$ (physiologic)
  * Proteins: $5.65\text{ kcal/g}$ (gross), $4.0\text{ kcal/g}$ (physiologic)

## 3. CORE EXAM HIGHLIGHTS & PITFALLS

* **Reducing vs. Non-Reducing:**
  * Reducing: All monosaccharides (Glucose, Fructose, Galactose) + Maltose, Lactose.
  * Non-Reducing: **Sucrose** (anomeric carbons locked) and Polysaccharides.
* **Essential Amino Acids (PVT TIM HaLL):**
  * Phenylalanine, Valine, Threonine, Tryptophan, Isoleucine, Methionine, Histidine, Leucine, Lysine.
* **Sulfur-Containing Amino Acids:** Cysteine (disulfide bridges) & Methionine.
* **Achiral Amino Acid:** Glycine (no optical activity).
* **Vitamins Fast Recall:**
  * $\text{A}$ (Retinol): Night Blindness, Xerophthalmia.
  * $\text{B}_1$ (Thiamine): Beriberi (Wet/Dry).
  * $\text{B}_3$ (Niacin): Pellagra (Dermatitis, Diarrhea, Dementia).
  * $\text{B}_9$ (Folate): Megaloblastic Anemia, Neural Tube Defects.
  * $\text{B}_{12}$ (Cyanocobalamin, Cobalt): Pernicious Anemia.
  * $\text{C}$ (Ascorbic Acid): Scurvy (Collagen breakdown).
  * $\text{D}$ (Cholecalciferol): Rickets / Osteomalacia.
  * $\text{K}$ (Phylloquinone): Clotting factors II, VII, IX, X activation.
