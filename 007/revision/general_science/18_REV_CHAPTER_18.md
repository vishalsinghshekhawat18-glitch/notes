# REVISION CHAPTER 18: CARBON & ITS COMPOUNDS

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **Carbon Uniqueness**: Tetravalent ($4$ covalent bonds) + **Catenation** (self-linking long chains, bond energy $348\text{ kJ/mol}$). Never forms $C^{4+}$ or $C^{4-}$ ions.
* **Allotropes of Carbon**:
  - Diamond: $sp^3$ tetrahedral, **hardest natural substance**, electrical insulator, high thermal conductor, $n = 2.42$ ($C = 24.4^\circ$ TIR sparkle).
  - Graphite: $sp^2$ hexagonal planar sheets, **electrical conductor** (free mobile $\pi$-electrons), soft/slippery (layers slide on weak Van der Waals), dry lubricant.
  - Fullerene ($C_{60}$, Buckminsterfullerene): Soccer ball cage, 20 hexagons + 12 pentagons.
  - Graphene: Single 2D atomic honeycomb sheet, 200x stronger than steel, ballistic electron conductor.
  - Coal grades (Carbon %): **Anthracite ($90\text{--}95\%$, best, smokeless)** $>$ Bituminous ($75\text{--}80\%$, commercial) $>$ Lignite ($60\text{--}70\%$, brown) $>$ Peat ($<50\%$).
* **Hydrocarbons**:
  - Alkanes: Saturated, $C_n H_{2n+2}$ (Methane $\text{CH}_4$ in CNG/Biogas).
  - Alkenes ($C_n H_{2n}$, double bond) & Alkynes ($C_n H_{2n-2}$, triple bond): Unsaturated, addition reactions. Oxy-acetylene torch ($C_2H_2$) reaches $3300^\circ\text{C}$.
  - LPG: Propane + Butane. Safety odorant = **Ethyl Mercaptan ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{SH}$)**.
* **Functional Groups**: Alcohol ($-OH$, Ethanol $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}$); Aldehyde ($-CHO$, Formalin $= 40\%$ formaldehyde); Ketone ($-CO-$, Acetone); Carboxylic Acid ($-COOH$, Acetic acid); Ester ($-COOR$, sweet fruity odor).
* **Key Reactions**:
  - Esterification: Acetic acid + Ethanol $\xrightarrow{\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4}$ Ethyl Acetate (fruity) + Water.
  - Saponification: Ester + $NaOH \rightarrow$ Soap + Alcohol.
  - Denatured Alcohol: Ethanol poisoned with $5\%$ Methanol ($CH_3OH$) (Methanol oxidizes to toxic Formaldehyde $\rightarrow$ optic nerve damage, blindness).
  - Glacial Acetic Acid: Freezes into ice crystals at **$16.6^\circ\text{C}$**. Vinegar $= 4\text{--}8\%$ acetic acid.

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Why is an artificial chemical odorant added to domestic LPG cylinders, and what is its name?** | Pure LPG (propane/butane) is completely odorless; **Ethyl Mercaptan ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{SH}$)** is added to provide a distinct foul, rotten-cabbage smell to instantly detect dangerous gas leaks. |
| **Q2: Why does graphite conduct electricity whereas diamond is an electrical insulator?** | In graphite, carbon is $sp^2$ bonded with **delocalized free $\pi$-electrons** that drift across hexagonal sheets; in diamond, all 4 valence electrons are tightly locked in rigid $sp^3$ tetrahedral single bonds. |
| **Q3: What makes consuming illicit spurious liquor containing methanol lethal?** | Liver enzymes oxidize methanol into **Formaldehyde ($\text{HCHO}$)** and formic acid, which denatures cellular proteins, attacks the optic nerve causing **permanent blindness**, and causes respiratory death. |
| **Q4: What is the highest quality grade of coal, and what is its carbon percentage?** | **Anthracite Coal**, containing **$90\%$ to $95\%$ carbon**, burning with a hot, clean, smokeless blue flame. |
| **Q5: What are the two types of rings that compose the Buckminsterfullerene ($C_{60}$) cage?** | Exactly **20 Hexagonal rings and 12 Pentagonal rings** (total 32 rings in a soccer ball geometry). |
| **Q6: Why is pure ethanoic acid historically called "Glacial Acetic Acid"?** | Pure anhydrous acetic acid has a melting point of **$16.6^\circ\text{C}$**; in cold winter temperatures, it freezes into solid, sparkling ice-like crystals resembling glacier ice. |

## Diagnostic Test-Maker Trap Matrix

| Concept | The Deceptive Trap | The Ironclad Scientific Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LPG Odor Origin** | "LPG smells pungent because butane has a strong natural smell." | Liquefied petroleum gas is **completely odorless**; the smell comes exclusively from added **Ethyl Mercaptan ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{SH}$)**. |
| **Diamond Heat/Electricity** | "Because diamond conducts heat efficiently, it must conduct electric current." | Diamond conducts heat via **phonons (crystal lattice vibrations)**, but is an **absolute electrical insulator** because it has zero free mobile electrons. |
| **Coal Grade Hierarchy** | "Bituminous is the highest grade of coal." | **Anthracite is the highest grade** ($90\text{--}95\% C$). Bituminous is second ($75\text{--}80\%$), followed by Lignite and Peat. |
| **Fullerene Pentagon Rings** | "Fullerene pentagonal rings fuse with other pentagons." | In $C_{60}$, **pentagons fuse ONLY with hexagons** (Isolated Pentagon Rule); hexagons can fuse with either hexagons or pentagons. |
