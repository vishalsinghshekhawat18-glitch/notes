## 2. ATOMIC FORMULAS & CLASSIFICATION SUMMARY

* **Binomial Nomenclature Rules:**
  * Format: *Genus* (capitalized) + *species* (lowercase), italicized or separately underlined.
  * Tautonyms (*Rattus rattus*) allowed in Zoology (ICZN), forbidden in Botany (ICN).
* **Bacterial Wall Staining:**
  * Gram-Positive: Thick peptidoglycan, Teichoic acids, purple/violet. Penicillin-sensitive.
  * Gram-Negative: Thin peptidoglycan, Outer LPS membrane (Endotoxin Lipid A), pink/red.
* **Fungi Spore Production:**
  * Ascomycetes: Conidia (asexual, exogenous) + Ascospores (sexual, endogenous in asci, usually 8).
  * Basidiomycetes: Basidiospores (sexual, exogenous on basidia, usually 4).

## 3. CORE EXAM HIGHLIGHTS & PITFALLS

* **Mycoplasma (PPLO):** Smallest free-living cells ($0.1–0.3\text{ }\mu\text{m}$); **completely lack a cell wall**; resistant to Penicillin.
* **Cyanobacteria:** Gram-negative oxygenic phototrophs. Heterocysts contain **Nitrogenase** for $\text{N}_2$ fixation (*Nostoc*, *Anabaena*).
* **Diatoms:** Ocean primary producers; cell walls made of indestructible **Silica**; form Diatomaceous Earth.
* **Dinoflagellates:** Red tides (*Gonyaulax*) releasing lethal neurotoxin **Saxitoxin**.
* **Lichens:** Symbiosis between Phycobiont (alga) and Mycobiont (fungus). Pioneer species on bare rock; **Bioindicators of $\text{SO}_2$ air pollution** (absent in polluted cities).
* **Double Fertilization in Angiosperms:**
  * Syngamy ($n + n \to 2n$ Zygote).
  * Triple Fusion ($2n + n \to 3n$ Triploid Nutritive Endosperm).
* **Body Cavity (Coelom) Diagnostic Checklist:**
  * Acoelomate: Platyhelminthes (Tapeworm, Liver fluke).
  * Pseudocoelomate: Aschelminthes / Nematoda (*Ascaris*).
  * Eucoelomate (True): Annelida, Arthropoda, Mollusca, Echinodermata, Chordata.
