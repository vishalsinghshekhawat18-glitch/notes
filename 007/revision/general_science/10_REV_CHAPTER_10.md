# REVISION CHAPTER 10: MAGNETISM & ELECTROMAGNETIC INDUCTION

## 60-Second Concept Retrieval Skeleton
* **Poles & Lines**: Monopoles do NOT exist ($\nabla \cdot \vec{B} = 0$). Field lines form continuous closed loops (Outside: $N \rightarrow S$, Inside: $S \rightarrow N$). Lines **never intersect**.
* **Earth's Magnetism**: Geographic North contains Earth's **Magnetic South**. Angle of Dip: $\delta = 0^\circ$ at equator, $\delta = 90^\circ$ at poles.
* **Magnetic Materials**:
  - Diamagnetic: Feebly repelled, $\chi < 0$ (Bismuth, Copper, Water). Independent of temp.
  - Paramagnetic: Feebly attracted, $\chi > 0$ (Aluminum, liquid $O_2$, Platinum). Curie's Law $\chi \propto 1/T$.
  - Ferromagnetic: Strongly attracted, domain alignment (Iron, Nickel, Cobalt). Above **Curie Temperature $T_c$** ($770^\circ\text{C}$ for Iron), becomes **Paramagnetic**.
  - Soft Iron: High permeability, low retentivity $\rightarrow$ Electromagnet/transformer cores; Steel: High retentivity $\rightarrow$ Permanent magnets.
* **Oersted & Solenoid**: Current produces magnetic field (Right-Hand Thumb Rule). Solenoid interior field is **uniform**: $B = \mu_0 n I$.
* **Lorentz Force**: $\vec{F}_m = q(\vec{v} \times \vec{B}) = qvB\sin\theta$.
  - Perpendicular to motion $\implies$ **Work done by magnetic field is strictly ZERO** (speed and KE constant, direction changes).
  - Fleming's Left-Hand Rule: Electric **Motor** (Converts electrical $\rightarrow$ mechanical; Split-ring commutator maintains direction).
* **Electromagnetic Induction**: Faraday $\mathcal{E} = -N \frac{d\Phi}{dt}$ ($\Phi = BA\cos\theta$ in Webers).
  - Lenz's Law: Induced current opposes cause $\rightarrow$ **Conservation of Energy**.
  - Fleming's Right-Hand Rule: Electric **Generator** (Mechanical $\rightarrow$ Electrical).
  - Transformers: $\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p} = \frac{I_p}{I_s}$. Mutual induction, **AC ONLY** (Zero output and burns out on DC!). Laminated silicon steel core prevents **Eddy Currents**.

## 3-Minute Active Recall Flashcard Drills

| Flash Question | Target Rapid Retrieval Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Can magnetic monopoles exist in isolation like positive and negative electric charges?** | **No.** Cleaving a magnet in half always produces two complete dipoles; isolated magnetic monopoles do not exist in classical physics. |
| **Q2: What is the angle of magnetic dip at the Earth's magnetic equator vs. magnetic poles?** | **$0^\circ$ at the Magnetic Equator** (horizontal field) and **$90^\circ$ at the Magnetic Poles** (vertical field). |
| **Q3: What happens to a piece of iron when heated above 770°C (its Curie Temperature)?** | It loses its spontaneous ferromagnetic domain structure and transforms into a **Paramagnetic material**. |
| **Q4: Why does a pure magnetic field do zero mechanical work on a moving charged particle?** | The magnetic Lorentz force ($\vec{F} = q\vec{v} \times \vec{B}$) acts perpendicular to instantaneous velocity ($\vec{F} \cdot \vec{v} = 0$), altering direction without changing kinetic energy or speed. |
| **Q5: Why cannot an electrical transformer operate on Direct Current (DC)?** | Direct current produces a constant, unvarying magnetic flux ($\frac{d\Phi}{dt} = 0$), so the induced voltage in the secondary coil is strictly **Zero Volts**. |
| **Q6: Why are transformer cores laminated rather than made from a solid iron block?** | To break up and minimize circulating **Eddy Currents (Foucault currents)**, drastically reducing electrical power loss dissipated as waste heat. |

| **Earth Magnetic Poles** | "Earth's Geographic North Pole houses the Magnetic North Pole." | Earth's **Geographic North houses its Magnetic SOUTH Pole**, which attracts the North-seeking pole of compass needles. |
