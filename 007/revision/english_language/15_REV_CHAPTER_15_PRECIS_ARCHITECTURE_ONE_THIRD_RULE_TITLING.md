## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Précis Operational Parameters

| Parameter | Mandatory Requirement | Severe Failure Mode | Penalty Severity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Word Budget** | Exactly $1/3\text{rd} \pm 5\%$ of original word count. | Writing $1/2$ or $1/4$ length. | 2 to 5 mark deduction. |
| **Paragraph Count** | Exactly **one** unbroken, continuous paragraph. | Fragmenting into 3–4 small paragraphs. | Structural score cap. |
| **Perspective / Voice** | Strict **Third Person** (*he, she, they, the author*). | Using First Person (*"In my opinion..."*). | Epistemic invalidation. |
| **Tense Discipline** | Consistent Indirect Speech (Past / Neutral Present). | Mixing direct quotes with shifts in tense. | Grammatical penalty. |
| **Source Purity** | 100% sourced from passage; zero outside facts. | Inserting current affairs data not in text. | Immediate mark loss. |
| **Title Formatting** | Title Case, 3–7 words, no terminal period. | Sentence title or all-lowercase text. | Loss of 3–5 title marks. |

---

### Matrix B: The Six Red-Pen Deletion Filters

| Noise Filter | Textual Manifestation | Surgical Elimination Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Anecdotes** | Personal stories, historical vignettes. | Delete completely; extract underlying principle only. |
| **2. Statistical Data** | Lists of percentages, dates, and currency values. | Compress into qualitative trajectory (*"expanded significantly"*). |
| **3. Rhetorical Questions**| Inquiries designed for dramatic rhetorical effect. | Convert to declarative, matter-of-fact statements. |
| **4. Direct Quotations** | Quoted speech enclosed in quotation marks. | Paraphrase into indirect third-person discourse. |
| **5. Tautologies / Pairs**| Paired synonyms (*"each and every," "fair and just"*). | Retain only the single most accurate term. |
| **6. Digressions** | Illustrative tangents and parenthetical explanations. | Exclude from core argumentative chain. |

## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
Précis Golden Formula: Word Target = Original ÷ 3 (±5% tolerance) 
➔ Single unbroken paragraph ➔ Zero personal bias ("I", "my", "we" = BANNED) 
➔ No outside knowledge ➔ Paraphrase 100% in own words 
➔ Cut the 6 Noises: Anecdotes, Long Stats, Rhetorical Questions, Direct Quotes, Tautologies, Digressions 
➔ Title Algorithm: 3 to 7 words in Title Case (Capitalize Content Words, No Period at End) 
➔ Formula: [Topic] + [Core Dilemma/Focus].
```

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *A passage discusses renewable energy subsidies. The candidate titles the précis: "Why We Should All Invest In Solar Panels Now." What are the errors in this title?*
* **Error**: (1) Uses First Person (*"We"*), (2) Capitalizes short prepositions incorrectly (*"In"*), (3) Formulates a prescriptive campaign slogan instead of an objective academic title.
* **Correction**: *Renewable Energy Subsidies: Fiscal and Environmental Imperatives*

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Question**: *How should a candidate handle an author's direct question: "Can democracy survive without an independent press?" in a précis?*
* **Answer**: Convert the rhetorical question into a direct declarative proposition: *"An independent press is vital for the preservation of democratic governance."*
