## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Paraphrasing Transformation Mechanics

| Transformation Type | Operational Action | Original Text Cliché | Master Paraphrased Equivalent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clause Consolidation** | Merge 2–3 disparate sentences into 1 compound unit. | *Growth slowed. Inflation rose. Rates were hiked.* | *Stagflationary pressures compelled monetary tightening.* |
| **Lexical Substitution** | Replace verbose multi-word phrase with single formal lemma. | *The state gave money to reduce terrible suffering.* | *Fiscal relief **ameliorated** distress.* |
| **Active to Depersonalized**| Remove human actors to achieve academic detachment. | *The researchers found that people spend less money.* | *Empirical evidence indicates declining household expenditure.* |
| **Nominalization Reduction** | Re-activate zombie nouns into concise active verbs. | *The committee came to the conclusion that...* | *The committee concluded that...* |

## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
Zero Verbatim Copying: 3+ matching consecutive words triggers penalty cap 
➔ Word Target: Passage ÷ 3 (Strict ±5% Window) 
➔ Paraphrase Keys: Clause Consolidation | Lexical Compression | Depersonalization 
➔ Anatomy: Exactly 1 continuous paragraph ➔ 3rd person objective voice ➔ Title in Title Case (No Period) 
➔ Eliminate: Metaphors, Analogies, Historical Vignettes, Data lists.
```

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *How should the clause "The farmers were struggling very hard and were unable to pay back their bank loans" be condensed non-verbatim?*
* **Answer**: *"Cultivators suffered from severe debt distress and insolvency."*

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Question**: *Why is a précis title containing a colon (e.g., "Agricultural Reform: Imperatives and Horizons") highly effective?*
* **Answer**: It cleanly separates the central domain (*Agricultural Reform*) from the operational thesis (*Imperatives and Horizons*), fulfilling the academic titling rubric in under 6 words.
