## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: The Four Training Phases

| Phase | Days | Primary Component | Daily Time Budget | Core Daily Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1** | 01–07 | Syntax & Vocabulary | 45 Minutes | 20 Roots + 10 Prepositions + 1 PEEL Paragraph. |
| **Phase 2** | 08–15 | Précis Compression | 50 Minutes | 1 Hindu Editorial Précis (1/3rd budget) + Title. |
| **Phase 3** | 16–22 | Letters & Reports | 50 Minutes | Alternating 1 Formal Letter / 1 Inspection Report. |
| **Phase 4** | 23–30 | Full-Length Sprints | 90 Minutes | Full Mock Paper (Essay + Précis + Letter) on Notepad. |

---

### Matrix B: Deduction Penalty Ledger

| Flaw Detected | Penalty Deducted | Remedial Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Word count >10% off band** | **-3.0 Marks** | Enforce paragraph-level word budgets strictly. |
| **Précis copied verbatim (3+ words)**| **-5.0 Marks** | Force lexical compression and clause merging. |
| **Précis title omitted / sentence** | **-4.0 Marks** | Apply: *[Theme] + [Tension / Horizon]*. |
| **"Respected Sir" or "Your's"** | **-2.0 Marks** | Use: *Sir / Madam* and *Yours faithfully*. |
| **Essay lacks Hegelian Thesis** | **-2.5 Marks** | Apply: *Although [Counter], [Thesis] because [Reason]*. |
| **Single wall of text** | **-4.0 Marks** | Enforce Quad-Paragraph PEEL structure. |

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Question**: *During Phase 4 full-length mocks, how should the 90 minutes be divided?*
* **Answer**: 10 minutes planning/outlining, 30 minutes essay typing, 25 minutes précis drafting/typing, 20 minutes letter/report typing, and a mandatory 5 minutes for proofreading.

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Question**: *What is the penalty for using "Your's faithfully" in an official letter?*
* **Answer**: An automatic deduction of 1 to 2 marks for a severe grammatical/formatting lapse.
