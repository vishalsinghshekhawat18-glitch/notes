## 3. High-Yield Root Decomposition Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Compound Derivative**: *Animadversion*
* **Morpheme Anatomy**: *Animus* (mind/spirit) + *ad-* (to/towards) + *versus* (turned) + *-ion* (noun)
* **Meaning**: A formal turn of the mind against something; harsh criticism, severe censure, or adverse commentary.

#### Card 2
* **Compound Derivative**: *Circumspect*
* **Morpheme Anatomy**: *Circum* (around) + *specere* (to look)
* **Meaning**: Looking carefully in every direction before committing to action; prudent, cautious, and vigilant against risk.

#### Card 3
* **Compound Derivative**: *Exculpatory*
* **Morpheme Anatomy**: *Ex-* (out of, away from) + *culpa* (blame, fault) + *-atory* (serving to)
* **Meaning**: Tending or serving to clear a defendant from guilt, fault, or criminal liability (*exculpatory evidence*).
