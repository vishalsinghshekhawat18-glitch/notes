## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
Call off (CANCEL completely) vs. Put off (POSTPONE to future date) 
➔ Come across (Meet by chance ──► NEVER say "suddenly came across" = TAUTOLOGY) 
➔ Come by (Acquire with difficulty) ➔ Come of (Result from) ➔ Come into (Inherit property) 
➔ Look into (INVESTIGATE forensically) vs. Look after (NURSE/CARE for) vs. Look down on (DESPISE) 
➔ Look forward to ──► DEMANDS GERUND (-ing) 
➔ Break out (Wars, diseases, fires erupt) vs. Break down (Machines fail / humans weep) ➔ Break into (Burglary) 
➔ Bring about (Cause to occur) vs. Bring up (Rear child / raise topic) vs. Bring out (Publish / highlight) 
➔ Give in (Surrender to force) vs. Give up (Abandon habit) vs. Give away (Distribute prizes / betray secret) 
➔ Make out (Decipher visually / understand) vs. Make up (Fabricate lie / compensate loss) 
➔ Put out (Extinguish flame) vs. Put up with (Tolerate / endure) vs. Put down (Suppress rebellion) 
➔ Run out of (Exhaust supplies) vs. Run into (Incur debts / collide) 
➔ Set in (Drought/recession begins) vs. Set aside (Quash court order) vs. Set off (Trigger panic) 
➔ Turn down (Reject proposal) vs. Turn out (Prove to be in end) vs. Turn up (Arrive unexpectedly)
```

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The regional arbitration tribunal called off the final hearing until the expert submissions could be filed.*
* **Error**: Confusing *call off* with *put off*. Since the hearing is deferred until a later condition is met, it is postponed, not cancelled.
* **Correction**: *"...tribunal **put off** the final hearing until..."*

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The judicial bench set off the controversial land acquisition notifications.*
* **Error**: The phrasal verb meaning to annul, overturn, or quash an administrative or judicial order is **Set aside**.
* **Correction**: *"...bench **set aside** the controversial land acquisition notifications."*

#### Card 3
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The statutory ombudsman announced that it would not put down with financial misrepresentation.*
* **Error**: The phrasal verb meaning to tolerate or endure is **Put up with**. *Put down* means to suppress by physical force.
* **Correction**: *"...would not **put up with** financial misrepresentation."*
