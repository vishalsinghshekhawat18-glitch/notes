## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Foreign Constitutional & Statutory Maxims (Latin)

| Maxim | Literal Latin Sense | Practical Governance / Legal Meaning | High-Yield Contextual Exemplar |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **De Jure** | By right / by law | Legally recognized entitlement regardless of practice | *President is the de jure sovereign head of state.* |
| **De Facto** | In fact / in reality | Actual operating reality regardless of legal ordination | *Prime Minister is the de facto executive leader.* |
| **Ultra Vires** | Beyond the powers | Acting outside lawful statutory jurisdiction; invalid | *Executive notification declared ultra vires.* |
| **Intra Vires** | Within the powers | Acting within lawful constitutional authority | *The regulatory statute was intra vires Parliament.* |
| **Prima Facie** | At first sight | Preliminary impression; accepted until disproven | *Prima facie evidence of financial fraud.* |
| **Sub Judice** | Under a judge | Currently before the court; prohibited from public bias | *Parliamentary debate restricted on sub judice cases.* |
| **Quid Pro Quo** | This for that | Reciprocal exchange of mutual benefits / favors | *Investigating quid pro quo in spectrum allocation.* |
| **Sui Generis** | Of its own kind | Unique; in a distinct non-standard class of its own | *The European Union is a sui generis entity.* |
| **Ipso Facto** | By the fact itself | As an inevitable direct result of the stated fact | *Default ipso facto triggers bond rating downgrade.* |
| **Caveat Emptor** | Buyer beware | Buyer alone bears responsibility for checking quality | *Consumer protection laws limit caveat emptor.* |

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The regional council exceeded its authority; therefore, the High Court struck down the statute as intra vires.*
* **Error**: Struck-down regulations that exceed authority are **Ultra Vires** (*beyond powers*). **Intra Vires** means within lawful power.
* **Correction**: *"...struck down the statute as **ultra vires**."*

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The researcher analyzed ancient Ashokan rock pillars using his expertise in calligraphy.*
* **Error**: The scientific decipherment of ancient carved stone inscriptions is **Epigraphy**. **Calligraphy** is modern artistic decorative penmanship.
* **Correction**: *"...expertise in **epigraphy**."*

#### Card 3
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The central government established an aviary to boost commercial beekeeping across tribal areas.*
* **Error**: An *aviary* houses birds. Beekeeping takes place in an **Apiary**.
* **Correction**: *"...established an **apiary** to boost commercial beekeeping..."*
