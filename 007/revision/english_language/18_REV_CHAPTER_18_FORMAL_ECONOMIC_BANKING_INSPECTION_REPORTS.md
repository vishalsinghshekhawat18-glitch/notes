## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Report Architecture vs. Essay & Letter

| Feature | Official Report | Descriptive Essay | Formal Letter |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Fact-finding, audit, compliance, actionable decree. | Reflective analysis of an idea or policy dilemma. | Specific administrative request, query, or grievance. |
| **Header** | Formal Memorandum Block (To, From, Date, File Ref, Sub). | Title only (or Prompt heading). | Full-Block Address, Date, Subject, Salutation. |
| **Internal Layout** | Numbered sections with headings & sub-headings. | Flowing quad-paragraph prose (No numbered headings). | 3-Paragraph cohesive flow without sub-headings. |
| **Data Presentation**| Mandatory Risk Matrices and compliance tables. | Integrated inline as sentences/reports. | Inline factual references (Account No., Date). |
| **Recommendations**| Specific, time-bound, measurable directives. | Broad forward-looking policy vision. | Specific immediate remedial request. |

---

### Matrix B: The Five Inviolable Sections of a Report

| Section Number | Section Title | Primary Function | Core Vocabulary / Phrasing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Terms of Reference** | Establish statutory mandate & audit scope. | *Pursuant to Section..., A targeted inspection was conducted...* |
| **2** | **Executive Summary** | 50-word synthesis of high-level findings. | *The audit flagged critical divergence in asset classification...* |
| **3** | **Forensic Findings** | Thematic catalog of empirical defects. | *Scrutiny of ledgers revealed..., Records failed to demonstrate...* |
| **4** | **Risk Matrix** | Table summarizing vulnerability & exposure. | *Columns: Operational Area, Defect, Risk Level, Exposure.* |
| **5** | **Action Plan** | Directives with deadlines and accountability. | *Management must reclassify within 15 days; Report by [Date].* |

## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
Report Architecture: Header (To / From / Date / File Ref / Subject) 
➔ Sec 1: Terms of Reference (Mandate & Scope) ➔ Sec 2: Executive Summary (50 words) 
➔ Sec 3: Forensic Findings (Categorized by sub-heads: Underwriting, KYC, IT) 
➔ Sec 4: Risk Matrix Table (Area | Vulnerability | Severity | Exposure) 
➔ Sec 5: Remedial Action Plan (Who, What, By When) 
➔ Tone: Objective, evidentiary 3rd Person ("Sampling revealed...") ➔ 0 personal pronouns ("I" = BANNED).
```

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *What statutory section empowers the Reserve Bank of India to conduct inspection of commercial banking branches?*
* **Answer**: **Section 35 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949**.

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Question**: *In a formal inspection report, how should deadlines for remedial actions be specified?*
* **Answer**: Deadlines must never be vague (e.g., *"as soon as possible"*). They must specify exact calendar days or dates (e.g., *"within 15 working days"* or *"by November 15, 2026"*).
