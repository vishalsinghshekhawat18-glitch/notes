## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: The PEEL Architecture

| Stage | Name | Target Function | Ideal Word Budget | High-Yield Opening Markers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P** | **Point** | Declare the singular governing claim of the paragraph. | 12–18 words | *The primary bottleneck lies in... / Crucially, institutional capacity...* |
| **E** | **Explanation** | Unpack the causal, economic, or legal mechanics. | 25–35 words | *This dynamic stems from... / Mechanistically, this operates via...* |
| **E** | **Evidence** | Provide verified empirical data or institutional precedent. | 20–28 words | *According to the RBI Financial Stability Report... / As observed in...* |
| **L** | **Link** | Reconnect back to prompt thesis; bridge to next thought. | 12–18 words | *Consequently, addressing this friction is vital for... / Hence...* |

---

### Matrix B: Precision Academic Signposting

| Rhetorical Need | Recommended Master Signposts | Amateur / Colloquial Phrases (BANNED) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adding a Point** | *Furthermore, Moreover, Concurrently, In addition* | *Also, And plus, One more thing* |
| **Introducing Contrast** | *Conversely, Notwithstanding, On the other hand, Yet* | *But, Anyhow, Even so* |
| **Showing Consequence** | *Consequently, As a corollary, Hence, Inevitably* | *So, Because of this, Resulting to* |
| **Providing Evidence** | *Instantiated by, Substantiated in, Exemplified by* | *Like, Take for instance, As we see* |

## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
Paragraph Rules: One claim per paragraph ➔ Budget: 65 to 90 words (4-6 sentences) 
➔ PEEL Structure: Point (Assertive Claim) ➔ Explanation (Causal Mechanics) 
➔ Evidence (RBI / Survey / Policy precedent) ➔ Link (Dovetail to prompt thesis) 
➔ Dovetailing: End sentence 1 with Concept X ➔ Start sentence 2 with Concept X 
➔ Avoid Wall-of-Text ➔ Double Enter between paragraphs on TCS iON console 
➔ Vary sentence length: Long compound-complex (analysis) + Crisp simple (verdict).
```

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *Identify the error: A candidate writes a single continuous paragraph of 320 words answering the entire essay prompt.*
* **Error**: Severe violation of paragraph architecture and cognitive scannability. It overwhelms the evaluator and obscures the distinct argumentative stages (Introduction, Multi-dimensional Body, Forward-Looking Conclusion).
* **Correction**: Break into a 4- or 5-paragraph structure: Introduction (50 words), Body 1 (80 words), Body 2 (80 words), Body 3 (80 words), Conclusion (50 words).

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Question**: *What is the "Known-to-New" information principle in academic sentence flow?*
* **Answer**: It dictates that a sentence should begin with information already familiar or introduced in the preceding sentence, and conclude with the newly introduced concept, creating a seamless logical chain without abrupt thematic jumps.
