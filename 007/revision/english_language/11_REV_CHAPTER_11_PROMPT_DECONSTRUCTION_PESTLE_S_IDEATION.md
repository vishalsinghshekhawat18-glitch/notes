## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Directive Verb Cognitive Operations

| Directive Verb | Core Intellectual Obligation | Mandatory Compositional Sections | Fatal Failure Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Analyze** | Dissect into components; establish causal mechanics. | Mechanism ➔ Empirical Evidence ➔ Direct Impact. | Pure narrative storytelling without causality. |
| **Critically Examine** | Interrogate claims; unmask structural trade-offs and limits. | Affirmative Case ➔ Counter-Case / Vulnerabilities ➔ Synthesis. | One-sided eulogy or subjective bias. |
| **Discuss** | Multi-faceted exploration of systemic dimensions. | PESTLE-S Dimensions ➔ Interconnections ➔ Balanced Summation. | Monolithic argument restricted to a single angle. |
| **Evaluate / Assess** | Measure empirical reality against stated goals. | Benchmark Metric ➔ Actual Data ➔ Implementation Gap. | Merely summarizing provisions of the act/scheme. |
| **Elucidate** | Render clear, explain architecture, demystify. | Clear Definition ➔ Working Mechanism ➔ Real-world Case. | Launching an aggressive polemical attack on premise. |

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *When an essay prompt instructs: "Assess the impact of Unified Payments Interface (UPI) on retail micro-merchants", what is the primary benchmark to establish?*
* **Answer**: The stated objective of retail financial inclusion and lower transaction friction vs. the empirical realities of merchant discount rate (MDR) disputes, dispute resolution delays, and dependency on private smartphone interfaces.

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *Why is a purely chronological sequence generally poor practice in an analytical essay?*
* **Answer**: A chronological approach turns an analytical evaluation into a descriptive history lesson, failing the demand of the directive verb (which requires thematic and structural analysis of mechanisms, impacts, and solutions).
