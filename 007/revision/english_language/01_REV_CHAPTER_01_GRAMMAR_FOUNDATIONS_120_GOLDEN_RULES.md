## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Subject-Verb Concord Protocols Across Complex Connectors

| Connector Family | Governing Rule | ✅ High-Yield Correct Sentence | ❌ Common Examiner Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Parenthetical Connectors** (*as well as, along with, together with, in addition to, accompanied by, rather than, like, unlike, except, but*) | Verb agrees strictly with the **FIRST SUBJECT**. | *The Chief Auditor, along with his assistants, **was** reviewing the loan sanctions.* | *Matching verb to nearest plural noun (*assistants were*).* |
| **Correlative Pairs** (*either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also, or, nor*) | Verb agrees with the **NEAREST SUBJECT**. | *Neither the Governor nor the executive directors **were** present at the symposium.* | *Matching verb to the initial singular subject (*was*).* |
| **Distributive Selectors** (*each of, one of, either of, neither of*) | Followed by **Plural Noun + SINGULAR VERB**. | *Each of the shortlisted candidates **has** passed the credit assessment.* | *Using plural verb (*have*) after plural noun.* |
| **One of the + Plural Noun + Relative Pronoun** | Relative pronoun refers to plural noun ➔ **PLURAL VERB**. | *He is one of those officers who **have** reformed banking regulations.* | *Using singular verb (*has*) forgetting the antecedent.* |
| **THE ONLY one of the + Plural Noun + Rel. Pronoun** | *Only* restricts antecedent to singular unit ➔ **SINGULAR VERB**. | *He is the **only one** of the officers who **has** received the award.* | *Using plural verb (*have*) ignoring 'only'.* |
| **Fractional / Percentage Quantifiers** (*two-thirds of, 40% of, most of*) | Verb governed entirely by the **noun following "of"**. | • *Half of the credit **was** disbursed.*<br>• *Half of the branches **were** audited.* | *Treating fractions as fixed singular or plural.* |
| **"A number of" vs. "The number of"** | • *A number of* = Many ➔ **PLURAL VERB**<br>• *The number of* = Aggregate figure ➔ **SINGULAR VERB** | • *A number of accounts **are** dormant.*<br>• *The number of accounts **is** increasing.* | *Using plural verb with 'The number of'.* |

### Matrix B: Correlative Conjunctions, Inversion & Negative Polarities

| Trigger Construction | Inversion Requirement | Mandatory Correlative Partner | Critical Prohibition / Exam Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hardly / Scarcely** | **MANDATORY**: *Hardly had + S + V3...* | **WHEN** (or *before*) | ❌ NEVER pair with *than* or *that*. |
| **No sooner** | **MANDATORY**: *No sooner had/did + S + V3/V1...* | **THAN** | ❌ NEVER pair with *when* or *then*. |
| **Lest** | Normal declarative order | **SHOULD** (or bare subjunctive) | ❌ NEVER use *not* (contains intrinsic negative). |
| **Although / Though** | Normal declarative order | **YET** (or comma `,`) | ❌ NEVER pair with *but*. |
| **Between** | Normal declarative order | **AND** | ❌ NEVER pair with *to* (*Between 10 AM to 5 PM* is wrong). |
| **From** | Normal declarative order | **TO** | ❌ NEVER pair with *and* (*From 10 AM and 5 PM* is wrong). |
| **Both** | Normal declarative order | **AND** | ❌ NEVER pair with *as well as*. |

### Matrix C: The Conditional Tense Harmony Engine

| Conditional Category | Condition Clause (*If-Clause*) | Result Clause (*Main Clause*) | Inverted Equivalent (Formal Style) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Type 0 (Universal Truth)** | Simple Present (*If + V1/Vs-es*) | Simple Present (*V1/Vs-es*) | — |
| **Type 1 (Real / Future Open)** | Simple Present (*If + V1/Vs-es*) | *will / shall / can / may + V1* | *Should you require further credit...* |
| **Type 2 (Unreal / Hypothetical)** | Past Subjunctive (*If + WERE / V2*) | *would / could / might + V1* | *Were the Governor to intervene...* |
| **Type 3 (Unfulfilled Past)** | Past Perfect (*If + had + V3*) | *would have / could have / might have + V3* | *Had the statutory auditors inspected...* |

### Matrix D: Verbal Complementation — Gerunds vs. Infinitives

| Verbal Category | Governing Particle / Verb | Required Complement Form | Exemplar Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prepositional Verbals** | *look forward to, with a view to, accustomed to, be used to, averse to, prone to* | **GERUND (V1 + ing)** | *We look forward to **collaborating** with the multilateral lending agency.* |
| **Verbs of Prevention** | *prohibit, prevent, abstain, refrain, debar, deter* | **FROM + GERUND** | *The regulator prohibited the firm **from accepting** deposits.* |
| **Active Causative Verbs** | *make, let, bid, watch, hear, see* | **BARE INFINITIVE (V1)** | *The supervisor **made the cashier verify** the currency bundles.* |
| **Passive Causative 'Make'** | *be made* | **FULL INFINITIVE (to + V1)** | *The cashier **was made to verify** the currency bundles.* |
| **Passive Causative 'Let'** | *be let* | **BARE INFINITIVE (V1)** | *The documents were **let pass** unscrutinized.* |

## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
First-Subject Rule (as well as, along with) ➔ Nearest-Subject Rule (either/or, neither/nor) 
➔ Distributives (each of, one of + Plural Noun + Singular Verb) ➔ One of the + Plural Noun + Rel. Pronoun (Plural Verb) 
➔ The Only one of the (Singular Verb) ➔ Fractions/Percentages (governed by noun after 'of') 
➔ A number of (Plural) vs. The number of (Singular) ➔ Collective Noun (Unanimous = Singular; Divided = Plural) 
➔ Academic nouns ending in -s (Singular, unless modified by possessive) 
➔ Negative Inversion (Hardly, Scarcely, Seldom, Never + Aux + Subject + Verb) 
➔ Hardly/Scarcely + WHEN ➔ No sooner + THAN ➔ Lest + SHOULD (No 'not') 
➔ Conditionals: Type 1 (If + Pres, will + V1) ➔ Type 2 (If + WERE, would + V1) ➔ Type 3 (If + had + V3, would have + V3) 
➔ Dangling Modifier (Participle must modify adjacent noun) ➔ Parallelism (Equal structures across coordinators) 
➔ Illogical Comparison (Compare with that of / those of) ➔ Latin Comparatives (Superior, senior + TO, no 'more') 
➔ Preposition + Gerund (look forward to + V-ing) ➔ Prevention + FROM + Gerund ➔ Causative active (make + bare infinitive)
```

## 3. High-Yield Error Spotting Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Sentence**: *Scarcely the monetary committee had concluded its deliberations than inflation expectations eased.*
* **Errors**:
  1. No inversion after *Scarcely*: Must be *Scarcely **had the monetary committee concluded**...*
  2. Correlative corruption: *Scarcely* pairs with **WHEN**, never *than*.
* **Correction**: *Scarcely had the monetary committee concluded its deliberations **when** inflation expectations eased.*

#### Card 2
* **Sentence**: *The economic performance of India during the global downturn was more superior than western economies.*
* **Errors**:
  1. Double comparative: *Superior* cannot take *more*.
  2. Comparative preposition: *Superior* takes **TO**, not *than*.
  3. Illogical comparison: Comparing *performance* directly to *economies*. Must use **that of**.
* **Correction**: *The economic performance of India during the global downturn was **superior to that of** western economies.*

#### Card 3
* **Sentence**: *Every officer and every branch manager were instructed to not only verify the KYC documents but also recording the biometrics.*
* **Errors**:
  1. *Every...and every...* takes a **singular verb**: *was instructed*, not *were*.
  2. Split infinitive & faulty parallelism: *to not only verify...but also recording* breaches parallel structure.
* **Correction**: *Every officer and every branch manager **was** instructed **not only to verify** the KYC documents **but also to record** the biometrics.*
