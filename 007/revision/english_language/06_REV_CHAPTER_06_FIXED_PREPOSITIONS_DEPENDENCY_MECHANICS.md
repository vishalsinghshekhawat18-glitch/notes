## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Dual-Trigger Bifurcated Prepositional Dependencies

| Head Verb / Word | Preposition A & Target | Preposition B & Target | High-Yield Contextual Contrast |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agree** | **WITH** a person (*agree with him*) | **TO** a plan (*agree to the merger*) | *I agree WITH the Governor, but disagree TO the schedule.* |
| **Die** | **OF** a disease (*died of cholera*) | **FROM** a cause/wound (*died from overwork*) | *He died OF malaria, whereas his aide died FROM exhaustion.* |
| **Part** | **WITH** property/things (*part with shares*) | **FROM** a person (*part from family*) | *He parted WITH his gold, but wept when parting FROM friends.* |
| **Blind** | **IN** an eye (*blind in the left eye*) | **TO** faults/risks (*blind to banking fraud*) | *Though blind IN one eye, he was never blind TO operational risk.* |
| **Differ** | **WITH** a person (*differ with the chair*) | **FROM** an object (*differs from last year*) | *The economist differed WITH his peers; the plan differs FROM 2020.* |
| **Account** | **FOR** money/results (*account for deficit*) | **TO** an authority (*accounts to Parliament*) | *The ministry accounts TO Parliament and must account FOR funds.* |
| **Compare** | **TO** dissimilar things (*life to a dream*) | **WITH** similar things (*compare GDP with UK*) | *Compare this year's harvest WITH last year's.* |

## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
Cope WITH (NEVER 'cope up with') ➔ Comprise (ZERO 'of' in active voice; Consist OF) 
➔ Discuss / Describe / Analyze (ZERO 'about') ➔ Order (ZERO 'for' as verb) ➔ Resemble (ZERO 'with/to') 
➔ Enter room (ZERO 'into' for physical spaces) vs. Enter INTO agreement (legitimate figurative usage) 
➔ Preferable TO (NEVER 'preferable than' or 'more preferable') 
➔ Prevention Verbs (Abstain, refrain, prevent, prohibit, debar) ──► FROM + GERUND (-ing) 
➔ Agree WITH (person) vs. Agree TO (plan/proposal) 
➔ Die OF (disease/malaria) vs. Die FROM (wound/overwork/cause) 
➔ Part WITH (money/things) vs. Part FROM (people) 
➔ Blind IN (physical eye) vs. Blind TO (flaws/defects) 
➔ Accused OF (crime) vs. Charged WITH (crime) 
➔ Apprise OF (inform) vs. Appraise (evaluate value, zero prep) 
➔ Infringe ON / Encroach ON / Insist ON 
➔ Accustomed to / Look forward to / Averse to ──► DEMAND GERUND (-ing)
```

## 3. High-Yield Prepositional Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The regional rural bank was unable to cope up with the transaction volume.*
* **Error**: The phrase *cope up with* is a non-standard Indianism. The only grammatical idiom is **Cope WITH**.
* **Correction**: *"...unable to **cope with** the transaction volume."*

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The compliance officer apprised the management about the audit objections.*
* **Error**: The verb **Apprise** (*to inform*) commands the fixed preposition **OF**, never *about*.
* **Correction**: *"...apprised the management **of** the audit objections."*

#### Card 3
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The executive committee discussed about the budget and ordered for new office servers.*
* **Error**: Both *discussed* and *ordered* (verbs) are transitive and cannot take *about* or *for*.
* **Correction**: *"...**discussed the budget** and **ordered new office servers**."*
