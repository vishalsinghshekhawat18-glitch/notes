## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Introduction Funnel Architecture

| Stage | Name | Target Word Budget | Primary Objective | Fatal Cliché / Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Hook** | 15–20 words | Establish intellectual authority and macro framing. | *"Since ancient times..." / "From the dawn of history..."* |
| **Tier 2** | **Pivot** | 15–20 words | Narrow the broad macro context to the prompt's boundary. | Abrupt jump without logical connective transition. |
| **Tier 3** | **Thesis** | 20–25 words | State definitive stance and argumentative trajectory. | *"In this essay I will discuss both pros and cons..."* |

---

### Matrix B: Conclusion Archetypes: Summary vs. Circular Callback

| Feature | Mechanical Summary (Scores 5/10) | Circular Callback (Scores 9.5/10) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cognitive Approach** | Re-states points A, B, and C already argued. | Reconciles the tension introduced in Paragraph 1. |
| **Linguistic Phrasing** | *"To conclude, as discussed above..."* | *"Ultimately, [Concept X] is not an obstacle, but the bedrock..."* |
| **Forward Horizon** | Ends with a vague, pious hope (*"we hope all will be well"*). | Articulates precise, institutional, forward-looking policy catalyst. |
| **Structural Resonance**| Disconnected terminal paragraph. | Circularly echoes the opening hook's central metaphor. |

## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
Introduction Funnel (50-60 words): Hook (Macro-paradox / benchmark) ➔ Pivot (Bridge to prompt) ➔ Thesis 
➔ Thesis Formula: Although [Counter-position], [Main Stance] because/provided that [Policy Solution] 
➔ Never announce intentions ("This essay will examine...") 
➔ Never use ancient history clichés ("Since time immemorial...") 
➔ Conclusion (45-55 words): Circular Callback ➔ Pick up opening metaphor ➔ Elevate to forward policy horizon 
➔ No repetitive summaries ➔ Ensure last sentence resonates with institutional poise.
```

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *Identify the error: "In this essay, I am going to analyze the causes of inflation and suggest measures to control it."*
* **Error**: Self-referential meta-commentary. It wastes words announcing what you will do rather than asserting a substantive thesis.
* **Correction**: *"While cost-push supply bottlenecks continue to stoke headline inflation, anchoring core inflation requires a judicious blend of monetary tightening and targeted supply-side fiscal interventions."*

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Question**: *How does a "Macro-Paradox Hook" capture the evaluator's attention?*
* **Answer**: It juxtaposes two conflicting or contrasting macroeconomic realities (e.g., surging GDP growth vs. stagnant formal job creation), immediately establishing analytical maturity and real-world awareness.
