## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: High-Yield Homophone & Paronym Contrasts

| Word A | Word B | Precise Semantic Distinction | Common Examiner Trap / Inversion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Allusion** (Reference) | **Illusion** (Mirage / Deception) | *Allusion* = indirect reference or literary citation.<br>*Illusion* = optical or cognitive deception. | *"The policy is an allusion"* ❌ (Must be *illusion*). |
| **Appraise** (Assess value) | **Apprise** (Inform / notify) | *Appraise* = evaluate asset, value, or performance.<br>*Apprise* = inform or notify someone (*apprise of*). | *"Appraise the governor of developments"* ❌ (Must be *apprise*). |
| **Compliment** (Praise) | **Complement** (Complete / pair) | *Compliment* = expression of admiration.<br>*Complement* = element that enhances or completes a whole. | *"The reforms compliment monetary easing"* ❌ (Must be *complement*). |
| **Council** (Governing body) | **Counsel** (Advice / legal barrister) | *Council* = assembly, legislative body, or committee.<br>*Counsel* = guidance, advice, or legal representative. | *"Legal council was consulted"* ❌ (Must be *counsel*). |
| **Discreet** (Prudent / confidential) | **Discrete** (Separate / distinct) | *Discreet* = circumspect, confidential, tactful.<br>*Discrete* = detached, distinct, separate units. | *"The data is divided into discreet buckets"* ❌ (Must be *discrete*). |
| **Emigrate** (Depart homeland) | **Immigrate** (Settle into new nation) | *Emigrate* = exit one's home country (*emigrate from*).<br>*Immigrate* = enter and settle in another (*immigrate to*). | *"He emigrated to England"* ❌ (Should be *immigrated to*). |
| **Eminent** (Distinguished) | **Imminent** (Impending danger/event) | *Eminent* = renowned, prominent, illustrious.<br>*Imminent* = about to occur immediately, impending. | *"An eminent economic collapse"* ❌ (Must be *imminent*). |
| **Judicial** (Relating to judiciary) | **Judicious** (Wise / prudent) | *Judicial* = belonging to court or judge.<br>*Judicious* = displaying sound judgment and wisdom. | *"A judicial allocation of fiscal resources"* ❌ (Must be *judicious*). |
| **Stationary** (Immobile / at rest) | **Stationery** (Writing paper / pens) | *Stationary* (with an **a**) = fixed, unmoving.<br>*Stationery* (with an **e**) = paper, pens, desk supplies. | *"Bought paper from the stationary shop"* ❌ (Must be *stationery*). |
| **Tortuous** (Twisting / winding) | **Torturous** (Excruciatingly painful) | *Tortuous* = serpentine, labyrinthine, convoluted.<br>*Torturous* = involving extreme physical or mental agony. | *"A torturous winding mountain pass"* ❌ (Must be *tortuous*). |

➔ Appraise (Valuate assets/collateral) vs. Apprise (Inform/notify OF facts) 
➔ Compliment (Flattery/praise) vs. Complement (Enhance/complete counterpart) 
➔ Council (Committee/governing cabinet) vs. Counsel (Advice/legal advocate)

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The governor requested the executive directors to appraise him of the systemic liquidity pressures.*
* **Error**: The verb meaning to give information or notify is **apprise** (typically followed by *of*). **Appraise** means to evaluate or assess monetary worth.
* **Correction**: *"...directors to **apprise** him of the systemic liquidity pressures."*

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The research team collected discrete observations, but their analysis was criticized for its torturous explanations.*
* **Error**: Convoluted, winding, or circuitous logic is described as **tortuous** (from Latin *torquere*, to twist). **Torturous** pertains to agonizing suffering or torture.
* **Correction**: *"...criticized for its **tortuous** explanations."*

#### Card 3
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The state assembly adopted a series of prudent and judicial measures to rein in fiscal deficits.*
* **Error**: Measures characterized by wisdom, good sense, and careful discernment are **judicious**. **Judicial** specifically means relating to a court of law or judicial administration.
* **Correction**: *"...series of prudent and **judicious** measures to rein in..."*
