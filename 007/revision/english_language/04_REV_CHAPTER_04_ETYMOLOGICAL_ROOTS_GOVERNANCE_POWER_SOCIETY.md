## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Typology of State Authority (-Cracy & -Archy)

| System of Rule | Classical Morphemes | Exact Sovereign Structure | Antithetical Concept |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Democracy** | *Demos (people) + cracy* | Power exercised by the collective populace | **Autocracy** (one unchecked ruler) |
| **Plutocracy** | *Ploutos (wealth) + cracy* | Rule concentrated exclusively in the wealthy | **Meritocracy** (rule by ability) |
| **Theocracy** | *Theos (God) + cracy* | Rule by religious authorities / divine code | **Secular State** (separation of religion) |
| **Oligarchy** | *Oligos (few) + archy* | Rule by a small self-serving elite cartel | **Democracy** (majority rule) |
| **Kakistocracy** | *Kakistos (worst) + cracy* | Rule by the least competent and most corrupt | **Aristocracy** (rule by the best) |
| **Technocracy** | *Techne (craft) + cracy* | Rule by technical experts and scientists | **Partisan Political Rule** |
| **Anarchy** | *An- (without) + archy* | Total absence of government; lawlessness | **Monarchy / Sovereign Order** |

## 3. High-Yield Examination Flashcards

#### Card 1
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The regional administrative body was criticized for operating as an autocracy of three senior officials.*
* **Error**: An *autocracy* is ruled by **one individual** (*autos*). Rule by a small group of three is an **Oligarchy** (or *Triumvirate*).
* **Correction**: *"...operating as an **oligarchy** of three senior officials."*

#### Card 2
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *These high transport costs mitigated against rural industrialization.*
* **Error**: Confusion between *mitigate* and *militate*. Working as a strong adverse factor against an outcome requires **militate against**.
* **Correction**: *"...**militated against** rural industrialization."*

#### Card 3
* **Diagnostic Sentence**: *The Supreme Court commended the Chief Minister for his judicial decision to allocate emergency flood relief.*
* **Error**: Confusing *judicial* (court-related) with *judicious* (wise, sensible, prudent).
* **Correction**: *"...for his **judicious** decision to allocate emergency flood relief."*
