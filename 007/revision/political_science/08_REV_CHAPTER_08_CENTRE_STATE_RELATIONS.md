## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **The Article 252 Trap**: When two or more states request Parliament to legislate on a State List subject, **the State Legislatures CANNOT amend or repeal the resulting Act**. Only Parliament can amend or repeal it.
2. **The Foreign Borrowing Trap**: State governments in India **CANNOT borrow from foreign countries or global agencies**. They can borrow only within India (Article 293).
3. **The 42nd Amendment Transfer Trap**: The 42nd Amendment transferred 5 subjects **FROM the State List TO the Concurrent List** (Education, Forests, Weights & Measures, Wildlife, Subordinate Courts).
4. **The Article 271 Surcharge Trap**: Surcharges and cesses levied by Parliament are **NOT part of the divisible pool**. They are retained 100% by the Central Government.
5. **The Article 254(2) Presidential Assent Shield**: A State law repugnant to a Central law on a Concurrent subject survives **ONLY IF it received the assent of the President**; Governor's assent is insufficient.
