### Matrix B: Constitutional Comparison: Article 323-A vs. Article 323-B

| Parameter | Article 323-A | Article 323-B |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Subject Scope** | Public service recruitment & disputes ONLY | Specific subjects: Taxation, Land, Foreign Exch, Rent, Labor |
| **Enacting Authority** | **Parliament ALONE** | **Both Parliament & State Legislatures** |
| **Hierarchy of Tiers** | Only one level (CAT / SAT) | A **hierarchy of tribunals** can be established |
| **Territorial Scope** | Union CAT, State SAT, or Joint SAT | Subject to legislative lists (Lists I, II, III) |

### Matrix C: National Green Tribunal (NGT) Key Architectural Facts

| Dimension | Statutory Reality |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Enactment & Date** | *National Green Tribunal Act, 2010* (In force 18 October 2010). |
| **Constitutional Basis** | Enforces the Fundamental Right to a Healthy Environment under **Article 21**. |
| **Bench Composition** | Balanced Two-Member Benches: **1 Judicial Member + 1 Expert Member**. |
| **Benches Locations** | Principal Bench: **New Delhi**; Regional Benches: **Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata, Chennai**. |
| **Included Statutes (7)** | Water Act 1974, Water Cess 1977, Forest Conservation 1980, Air Act 1981, EPA 1986, Public Liability Ins 1991, Biodiversity Act 2002. |
| **Excluded Statutes** | **Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972** and **Forest Rights Act (FRA), 2006**. |
| **Guiding Principles** | Principles of Natural Justice; Sustainable Development, Precautionary Principle, Polluter Pays Principle. |
| **Time Limit & Appeal** | Mandated disposal in **6 months**; Direct appeal lies to **Supreme Court** (within 90 days). |

### Matrix D: Landmark Tribunal Jurisprudence Evolution

| Landmark Judgment | Key Legal Doctrine Established |
| :--- | :--- |
| **S.P. Sampath Kumar (1987)** | Upheld exclusion of High Court under Art 323-A; held CAT is an effective substitute. |
| **L. Chandra Kumar (1997)** (7-Judge Bench) | **Overruled Sampath Kumar**; Articles 226/32 judicial review is **Basic Structure**; exclusion of High Courts void; tribunals are courts of first instance, all decisions subject to HC Division Bench. |
| **Union of India v. R. Gandhi (2010)** | Upheld NCLT/NCLAT; technical members must have judicial standards; executive cannot dominate selection. |
| **Madras Bar Association (2014–2021)** | Struck down executive majorities on selection panels (must be CJI-chaired); struck down short 3-year tenures; directed National Tribunals Commission. |
