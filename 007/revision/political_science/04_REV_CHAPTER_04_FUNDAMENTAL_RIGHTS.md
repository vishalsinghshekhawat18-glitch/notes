## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: The Six Prerogative Writs & Jurisdictional Boundaries

| Writ | Meaning | Purpose | Issued Against | Cannot Be Issued Against |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Habeas Corpus** | "To have the body of" | Release person unlawfully detained | **Both Public & Private** entities | Lawful arrest; Contempt of court; Outside jurisdiction |
| **Mandamus** | "We command" | Compel public authority to perform legal duty | Public bodies, inferior courts, officials | Private bodies; Discretionary duties; President / Governors |
| **Prohibition** | "To forbid" | Stop lower court from exceeding jurisdiction | **Judicial & Quasi-Judicial ONLY** | Administrative bodies; Legislatures; Private entities |
| **Certiorari** | "To be certified" | Quash order of inferior court/tribunal | Judicial, quasi-judicial & **administrative** | Legislative bodies; Private individuals |
| **Quo-Warranto** | "By what authority" | Prevent illegal usurpation of public office | Substantive statutory/constitutional office | Ministerial offices; Private offices |

## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **The Article 20/21 Emergency Trap**: Articles 20 and 21 **CAN NEVER BE SUSPENDED** during a National Emergency under Article 352 (guaranteed by 44th Amendment 1978).
2. **The Ex-Post Facto Civil Fallacy**: Article 20(1) bans retroactive criminal laws only. Parliament **can pass retroactive civil and tax legislation**.
3. **The Double Jeopardy Departmental Trap**: Departmental or administrative disciplinary action followed by a court trial is **NOT Double Jeopardy**. Article 20(2) protects only against multiple trials before a judicial court.
4. **The Minority Scope Trap**: Article 30 rights are available **ONLY to Religious and Linguistic Minorities**. They do not apply to caste or economic minorities.
5. **The Writ Discretion Trap**: High Courts under Article 226 **can refuse** a writ petition if an alternative remedy exists; the Supreme Court under Article 32 **CANNOT refuse** because Article 32 is itself a Fundamental Right!
