## 3. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Mill's Plural Voting**: Mill opposed raw equal adult suffrage; he proposed weighted votes for educated citizens to prevent uneducated majorities from dominating.
2. **Rawls' Lexical Priority**: Basic civil liberties (Principle 1) CANNOT be traded off or compromised for economic gains or even the Difference Principle.
3. **Nozick's Proviso**: Nozick accepts Lockean acquisition ONLY if "enough and as good" is left for others without worsening their baseline condition.
4. **Austinian Sovereignty Fallacy**: Austinian sovereignty cannot exist in a constitutional federation where power is legally partitioned by a written constitution and guarded by judicial review.
5. **Sen's Distinction**: *Niti* is procedural/institutional correctness; *Nyaya* is substantive realized justice in living humans.
