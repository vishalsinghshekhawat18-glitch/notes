## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **The 42nd Amendment Trap**: The 42nd Amendment added **Socialist, Secular, and Integrity**—NOT "Democratic" (which was there since 1949).
2. **The Enforceability Fallacy**: The Preamble is **NON-JUSTICIABLE**. It cannot be enforced in courts of law, nor can an Act of Parliament be struck down purely for violating the Preamble without violating a specific constitutional article.
3. **The Secular Origin Trap**: India did NOT become a secular state only in 1976 when the word was added. The Supreme Court confirmed that Articles 25 to 28 made India fundamentally secular from 26 January 1950.
4. **The Berubari vs. Kesavananda Trap**: Berubari (1960) said NO; Kesavananda (1973) overruled it and said YES, the Preamble IS an integral part of the Constitution.
5. **The Revolution Source Trap**: Justice is from the **Russian Revolution (1917)**; Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity are from the **French Revolution (1789)**.
