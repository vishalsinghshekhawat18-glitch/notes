## 3. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **SPSC Removal Trap**: Governor appoints the SPSC Chairman and members, but **only the President can remove them** (Article 317).
2. **Solicitor General is Not Constitutional**: Only the Attorney General is mentioned in Article 76. The Solicitor General is a statutory/executive post.
3. **ECI Does Not Conduct Local Polls**: Panchayats and Municipalities are managed solely by State Election Commissions (Arts 243K & 243ZA).
4. **JSPSC is Statutory**: A Joint State Public Service Commission is created by an Act of Parliament, not by the Constitution itself.
5. **NCBC Constitutionalization**: The National Commission for Backward Classes was made a constitutional body via the **102nd Amendment Act, 2018 (Article 338B)**.
6. **CAG Audit of Panchayats**: Local bodies are audited on request; CAG's core mandate covers Consolidated Funds of Union and States.
