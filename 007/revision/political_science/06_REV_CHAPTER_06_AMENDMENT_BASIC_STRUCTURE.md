## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: The Three Categories of Constitutional Amendments

| Amendment Type | Parliamentary Majority | State Ratification Needed? | Subject Matters Covered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **The Joint Sitting Fallacy**: A Joint Sitting under Article 108 is **NEVER PERMITTED for a Constitutional Amendment Bill**. If the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha disagree, the bill lapses.
2. **The Presidential Veto Fallacy**: The President **HAS NO VETO** over a Constitutional Amendment Bill. Under Article 368(2) as amended by the 24th Amendment 1971, the President *must* give assent.
3. **The State Legislative Initiation Trap**: State legislatures **CANNOT INITIATE** a constitutional amendment under Article 368. Only Parliament can initiate it.
4. **The Definition Fallacy**: The term "Basic Structure" is **NOT DEFINED OR MENTIONED in the text of the Constitution**. It is an entirely judge-made doctrine created by the Supreme Court in 1973.
5. **The 9th Schedule Blanket Immunity Trap**: Laws in the Ninth Schedule are **NOT immune from judicial review**. Under *I.R. Coelho (2007)*, all laws inserted on or after 24 April 1973 can be struck down if they violate the Basic Structure.
