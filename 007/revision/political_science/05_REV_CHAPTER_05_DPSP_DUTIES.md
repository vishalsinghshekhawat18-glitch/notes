## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **The Tax Duty Fallacy**: The Swaran Singh Committee recommended a duty to pay taxes, but the **Congress Government rejected it**. It is NOT a Fundamental Duty under Article 51-A.
2. **The 31C Overreach Fallacy**: All Directive Principles do **NOT** take precedence over Fundamental Rights. Only laws implementing **Article 39(b) and 39(c)** enjoy immunity against Articles 14 and 19 (*Minerva Mills 1980*).
3. **The National Song Trap**: Article 51-A(a) mandates respect for the **National Flag and the National Anthem**. The **National Song (Vande Mataram) is NOT mentioned** in the text.
4. **The Article 45 vs. 21-A Age Trap**: Article 21-A covers children aged **6 to 14 years** (Fundamental Right). Article 45 covers children **below 6 years** (Early Childhood Care - DPSP).
5. **The Foreigner Duty Trap**: Fundamental Rights under Articles 14, 20, 21, etc., apply to foreigners, but **Fundamental Duties apply EXCLUSIVELY to Indian citizens**.
