## 3. High-Yield Flash Traps (Top 20 Instant Killers)

1. **Oaths of Top Trio**: President (Art 60), VP (Art 69), Governor (Art 159) have oaths in their **own Articles**, NOT in the Third Schedule.
2. **Estimates Committee**: Exactly **30 members, ALL from Lok Sabha**. Zero Rajya Sabha representation!
3. **HC Judge Removal vs Pension**: Removal is by **President**; Pension is charged on **Consolidated Fund of India**; Salary is charged on **State Fund**.
4. **Council Creation / Abolition**: State Assembly requires **Special Majority** (Art 169(1)); Parliament requires only **Simple Majority** (Art 169(3)).
5. **Anti-Defection Merger**: Requires at least **2/3rd members** of legislative party to merge. 1/3rd split is completely repealed.
6. **Nominated MP Defection**: Disqualified if joining a political party **after 6 months** from taking seat. If within 6 months, completely permitted.
7. **Independent MP Defection**: Disqualified if joining **ANY political party at ANY time**.
8. **National Emergency Cabinet Call**: Requires **WRITTEN communication of the Union Cabinet** (PM + Ministers of Cabinet rank), not oral advice from PM alone.
9. **Article 19 Emergency Suspension**: Suspended **ONLY for War or External Aggression**, NEVER for Armed Rebellion (Article 358).
10. **CAG & UPSC Chair Disqualification**: Completely ineligible for any further employment under the Government of India or any State Government after demitting office.
11. **Governor Death Sentence Commutation**: Governor CAN commute, remit, or suspend death sentences (Art 161); only full PARDON is exclusive to President.
12. **Regional Election Commissioner Removal**: Removed ONLY on recommendation of the **Chief Election Commissioner** (Art 324(5)), NOT the Governor.
13. **No-Confidence Motion in Constitution**: Phrase is **NOWHERE in the Constitution**; governed solely by Lok Sabha Rule 198.
14. **Single Electoral Roll (Art 325)**: Only **ONE general roll** for every constituency; zero separate rolls for Lok Sabha vs Vidhan Sabha.
15. **Zonal Councils Genesis**: Created under **States Reorganisation Act, 1956 (Statutory)**; NOT under Article 263.
16. **Trade in Foodstuffs**: **Concurrent List (Entry 33)**, NOT exclusive Union List!
17. **Rajya Sabha PAC Members Rights**: Members of Rajya Sabha in PAC have **EQUAL voting and participation rights** with Lok Sabha members.
18. **Co-operatives Board Cap**: Maximum **21 directors** (Art 243-ZJ), NOT 25; State Legislature enacts laws under Entry 32.
19. **Attorney General Qualification**: Must be qualified to be a **Supreme Court Judge** (Art 76), NOT High Court Judge.
20. **Table of Precedence Governor Rank**: Governors within their respective States rank **4th** (ABOVE Former Presidents, Deputy PM, and CJI)!
