## 4. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Delimitation Challenge Bar**: Delimitation Commission orders cannot be questioned even in the Supreme Court (Article 329(a)).
2. **Election Petitions Go to HC**: Election petitions are heard by the **High Court**, NOT by the Election Commission of India.
3. **No 3-Month Appeal Grace**: Following *Lily Thomas (2013)*, convicted legislators lose their seats **immediately** on conviction; they do not get a 3-month grace period.
4. **Voter Age vs Candidate Age**: Voting age is **18 years** (Art 326); contesting age is **25 years** (Lok Sabha / Assembly), **30 years** (Rajya Sabha / Council), and **21 years** (Panchayat / Municipality).
5. **VVPAT Landmark**: VVPAT introduction was mandated by the Supreme Court in *Subramanian Swamy v. ECI (2013)*.
