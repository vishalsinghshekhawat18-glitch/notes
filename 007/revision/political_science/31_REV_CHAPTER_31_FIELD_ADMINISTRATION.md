## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Cabinet Secretariat vs. Prime Minister's Office (PMO)

| Dimension | Cabinet Secretariat | Prime Minister’s Office (PMO) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Genesis** | 1947 (Successor to Viceroy's Executive Council Secretariat) | 1947 as Prime Minister’s Secretariat; renamed PMO in 1977 |
| **Administrative Head** | **Cabinet Secretary** (Senior-most civil servant, Rank #11) | **Principal Secretary to the PM** (Senior IAS/Cabinet rank) |
| **Constitutional Rule** | Rules of Business under Article 77(3) | Rules of Business under Article 77(3) |
| **Operational Role** | Institutional secretariat to Union Cabinet & Committees | Political, personal, and strategic administrative arm of PM |
| **Key Function** | Prepares Cabinet agendas, records minutes, monitors compliance | Drives high-priority national missions, foreign policy, intelligence |

---

### Matrix B: State Secretariat vs. Executive Directorates

| Parameter | State Secretariat | Executive Directorates (Line Agencies) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Role** | Policy formulation, legislation, budgeting, parliamentary oversight | Executive implementation, technical supervision, field delivery |
| **Head** | Additional Chief Secretary (ACS) / Principal Secretary | Director / Commissioner (Technical specialist / Technocrat) |
| **Nature of Personnel** | Generalist administrators (IAS, Senior State Civil Service) | Subject matter specialists (Doctors, Engineers, Agronomists) |
| **Locus of Work** | State Capital (Seat of Government) | Dispersed throughout state field divisions and districts |

---

### Matrix C: Dual Control System vs. Police Commissionerate System

| Parameter | Traditional Dual Control District | Police Commissionerate City (> 10 Lakh Population) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Administrative Command** | Shared between District Magistrate (DM) and SP | Unified under the **Commissioner of Police (CP)** |
| **CrPC Magisterial Powers** | Vested in District Magistrate & Executive Magistrates | Vested directly in **Commissioner of Police** (ADGP/IGP rank) |
| **Section 144 CrPC** | Promulgated only by the District Magistrate | Promulgated directly by the Commissioner of Police |
| **Accountability Line** | SP reports to DM on law and order; DM reports to Div Commr | CP reports directly to State Government / Home Department |

---

### Matrix D: Sub-District Administrative Topography

| Tier | Administrative Head | Core Statutory Powers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sub-Division** | **Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM/SDO)** | Executive Magistrate (Sec 20 CrPC); Assistant Collector 1st Class; revenue appeals. |
| **Tehsil / Taluka** | **Tehsildar** | Chief of Tehsil; Assistant Collector; attestation of mutations; public demand recovery. |
| **Revenue Circle** | **Girdawar / Kanungo / Inspector** | Supervises 10–20 Patwaris; conducts crop inspection (*Girdawari*); boundary marks. |
| **Village Cluster** | **Patwari / Lekhpal / Talati** | Village land records: *Khasra* (harvest register), *Khatauni* (rights), *Shajra* (map). |
