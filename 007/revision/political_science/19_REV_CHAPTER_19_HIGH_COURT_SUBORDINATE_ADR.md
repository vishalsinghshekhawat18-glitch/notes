## 4. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **HC Judge Resignation Trap**: High Court judges are sworn in by the **Governor**, but submit their resignation to the **President of India**.
2. **HC Strength Trap**: The President determines the strength of High Courts, whereas Parliament determines the strength of the Supreme Court.
3. **No Appeal from Lok Adalat**: Lok Adalat awards are mutually agreed compromise decrees; **no appeal lies to any court**.
4. **Permanent Lok Adalat Power**: Unlike regular Lok Adalats which only facilitate compromise, a **Permanent Lok Adalat can decide the case on merits** if the parties fail to reach an agreement.
5. **District Judge Qualification**: Direct recruitment requires **not less than 7 years** of practice as an advocate (Article 233(2)).
