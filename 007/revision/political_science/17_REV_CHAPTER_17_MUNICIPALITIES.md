## 4. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Cantonment Board Ministry**: Managed by the **Ministry of Defence**, NOT the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs or State Governments.
2. **Industrial Township Exemption**: Under the proviso to Article 243-Q(1), the Governor can exempt an industrial township from having a municipality.
3. **Wards Committee Population**: Minimum population is **3 Lakhs**, not 10 Lakhs. (10 Lakhs is the threshold for Metropolitan Planning Committees under 243-ZE).
4. **DPC vs MPC Fractions**: DPC requires **4/5th** elected members; MPC requires **2/3rd** elected members.
5. **Nominated Municipal Members Cannot Vote**: Under Article 243-R(2)(a), experts in municipal administration nominated to the municipality **do not have the right to vote** in municipal meetings.
