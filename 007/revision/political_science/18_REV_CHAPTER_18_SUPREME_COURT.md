## 4. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Distinguished Jurist Asymmetry**: Eligible for appointment as **Supreme Court Judge** (Art 124(3)(c)), but **NOT for High Court Judge** (Art 217(2)).
2. **SLP Court Martial Bar**: Article 136 does **not** apply to Armed Forces Courts Martial under Article 136(2).
3. **Article 143 Advisory Discretion**: Under 143(1), SC **may decline** to answer; under 143(2) (pre-constitution disputes), SC **must** answer.
4. **Post-Retirement Ban**: Supreme Court judges are barred from practicing in **all courts** in India. (High Court judges can practice in SC and other High Courts).
5. **No SC Judge Impeached**: To date, **no Supreme Court judge has ever been removed** under Article 124(4). (V. Ramaswami in 1993 survived due to abstentions).
