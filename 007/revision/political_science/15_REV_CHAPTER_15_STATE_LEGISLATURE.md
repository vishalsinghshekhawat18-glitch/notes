## 4. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **No Joint Sitting in States**: If Assembly and Council disagree, the Assembly repasses the bill, and after 1 month, it automatically passes. There is **no joint sitting**.
2. **Chairman Membership**: Unlike the Rajya Sabha Chairman who is the Vice-President, the Chairman of the Legislative Council is an **elected member of the Council**.
3. **Article 169 is Not Article 368**: An Act of Parliament abolishing or creating a State Legislative Council is passed by a **Simple Majority** and is not deemed to be an amendment under Article 368.
4. **Co-operatives in Council**: 1/6th nominated by Governor includes "Co-operative Movement", absent in Rajya Sabha nomination categories.
5. **Anglo-Indian Seat Discontinued**: Article 333 (1 Anglo-Indian nominated by Governor) was abolished by the 104th Amendment Act, 2019.
