## 3. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Life/Liberty 48-Hour Clock**: Information affecting a person's life or liberty must be provided within **48 hours**, not 30 days.
2. **Security Agency Corruption**: Even IB, RAW, and BSF **must disclose information** relating to allegations of corruption and human rights violations under the provisos to Section 24.
3. **No Fees if Delayed**: If information is delayed beyond the statutory limit, it must be provided **completely free of cost** under Section 7(6).
4. **Second Appeal Forum**: The First Appeal goes to a senior officer in the same department (FAA); the **Second Appeal goes to the CIC or SIC**, NOT to a civil court.
5. **Civil Court Bar**: Civil courts are barred from entertaining any suit or application in respect of any order made under the RTI Act (Section 23).
