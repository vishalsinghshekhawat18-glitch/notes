## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Special Protective Statutes Overview

| Statute | Target Group | Key Statutory Innovation | Landmark Judicial Ruling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955** | Scheduled Castes (Article 17) | Enforces abolition of untouchability; non-compoundable; automatic election disqualification under RPA. | *State of Karnataka v. Appa Balu Ingale (1993)* |
| **SC & ST (PoA) Act, 1989** | Dalits & Adivasis | Offenses by non-SC/ST only; **Section 18/18A bar on anticipatory bail**; Special Courts; 2015 Chapter IVA victim rights. | *Subhash K. Mahajan (2018)* $\rightarrow$ overturned by *Section 18A*; upheld in *Prathvi Raj Chauhan (2020)* |
| **Forest Rights Act (FRA), 2006** | FDST & OTFD (75 yrs / 3 gens) | Recognizes pre-2005 customary tenure; ceiling **4 hectares**; MFP ownership; **Gram Sabha sole initiating authority**. | *Niyamgiri Case / OMC v. MoEF (2013)* (Gram Sabha veto on mining) |
| **Domestic Violence Act (PWDVA), 2005** | Women in domestic relationship | Hybrid civil-criminal; covers physical, sexual, verbal, economic abuse; **Residence Order (Shared Household)**; Sec 31 breach is criminal. | *Satish Chander Ahuja v. Sneha Ahuja (2020)* (Shared household rights) |
| **POSH Act, 2013** | Women in workplace | Codifies *Vishaka (1997)*; **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)** mandatory for $\ge 10$ workers; 50% women; 1 NGO; LCC for $< 10$. | *Aureliano Fernandes v. State of Goa (2023)* (Strict POSH enforcement) |
| **POCSO Act, 2012** | Children ($< 18$ years) | **Gender-neutral**; mandatory reporting (Sec 19); non-uniform police; **Sec 29/30 reverse burden of proof**; 1-year trial. | *Independent Thought v. UOI (2017)* (Marital rape of minor criminalized) |
| **RPwD Act, 2016** | Differently-abled | Replaced 1995 Act; expanded to **21 disabilities**; **5% education** and **4% employment reservation**; Chief Commissioner. | *Vikash Kumar v. UPSC (2021)* (Reasonable accommodation mandate) |
| **Senior Citizens Act, 2007** | Parents & elderly ($\ge 60$) | Maintenance Tribunal (SDM); ₹10k monthly cap; **Section 23 declares property gift/transfer void** if neglected. | *S. Vanitha v. Deputy Commissioner (2020)* |

### Matrix B: Caste Justice: PCR Act (1955) vs. SC/ST PoA Act (1989)

| Parameter | Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 | SC & ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Constitutional Basis** | **Article 17** (Abolition of Untouchability) | **Articles 15, 17, 21, and 46** |
| **Nature of Offenses** | Religious and social disabilities arising from "untouchability" (entry to wells, temples, shops). | Violent crimes, physical torture, sexual assault, land dispossession, economic boycott, malicious prosecution. |
| **Perpetrator Scope** | Any person who practices untouchability. | **ONLY a person who is NOT a member of SC or ST**. |
| **Anticipatory Bail** | Section 438 CrPC applies normally. | **Explicit statutory bar under Section 18 & Section 18A**. |
| **Court Forum** | Ordinary Judicial Magistrate Courts. | Designated **Special Courts / Exclusive Special Courts** (Session Courts). |
