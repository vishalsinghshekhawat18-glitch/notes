## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **The Zonal Council Constitutional Fallacy**: Zonal Councils are **STATUTORY BODIES** (States Reorganisation Act 1956), NOT constitutional bodies under Article 263.
2. **The Water Dispute Article 131 Trap**: Original suits under Article 131 are barred for inter-state water disputes referred to a tribunal under Article 262; however, the Supreme Court **CAN review awards under Article 136 (Special Leave Petition)**.
3. **The Sikkim Zonal Trap**: Sikkim is **NOT part of the Eastern Zonal Council**; it is the 8th member of the **North Eastern Council (NEC)**, added in 2002.
4. **The Chairmanship Confusion**: The **Prime Minister** chairs the Inter-State Council; the **Union Home Minister** chairs all five Zonal Councils and the North Eastern Council.
5. **The Criminal Execution Fallacy**: Article 261 full faith and credit for direct execution across state borders applies **ONLY to civil judgments**, NOT to criminal convictions.
