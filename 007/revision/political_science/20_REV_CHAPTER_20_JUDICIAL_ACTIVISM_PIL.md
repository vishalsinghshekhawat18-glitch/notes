## 3. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **First PIL Case**: *Hussainara Khatoon (1979)* was the first reported PIL case in India, piloted by Pushpa Kapila Hingorani.
2. **Absolute Liability vs. Strict Liability**: Under *Rylands v. Fletcher (1868)* Strict Liability, exceptions (Act of God, plaintiff's fault) existed. In *Oleum Gas Leak (1987)*, Absolute Liability has **no exceptions whatsoever**.
3. **Excluded Matters**: Service/employment disputes and landlord-tenant disputes **cannot** be entertained as PILs.
4. **Epistolary Jurisdiction Target**: Addressed to Supreme Court (Article 32) or High Courts (Article 226), **not** subordinate courts.
5. **Overreach Distinction**: Activism fills a governance vacuum where rights are violated; Overreach substitutes judicial opinion for executive policy choices or statutory drafting.
