## 4. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Intermediate Tier Exemption**: Only states with a population **not exceeding 20 lakhs** can skip the intermediate tier (e.g., Goa, Sikkim).
2. **SEC Removal**: The Governor appoints the State Election Commissioner, but **cannot remove them**. The SEC is removed exactly like a **High Court Judge**.
3. **No Fresh 5-Year Term**: A Panchayat elected after premature dissolution only serves for the **remainder** of the 5-year term. If the unexpired period is $<6$ months, no election is held.
4. **OBC Reservation is Voluntary**: SC/ST reservation is mandatory in proportion to population; women's reservation is mandatory at $\ge 33\%$; but **OBC reservation is voluntary** under Article 243D(6).
5. **PESA Minor Minerals Scope**: PESA mandates prior recommendation for **minor minerals** (sand, gravel, stone), NOT major minerals (coal, bauxite, iron ore).
