## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Constitutional Framework for Civil Services (Part XIV)

| Article | Constitutional Mandate | Legal Significance / Landmark Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article 309** | Recruitment and conditions of service regulated by legislative Acts | Proviso allows President/Governor to frame statutory service rules. |
| **Article 310** | **Doctrine of Pleasure**: Civil servants hold office at pleasure of President/Governor | Subject to Article 311 safeguards; cannot be terminated arbitrarily. |
| **Article 311(1)** | Subordinate authority cannot dismiss or remove | Safeguard against local/arbitrary victimization. |
| **Article 311(2)** | Mandatory departmental inquiry and reasonable hearing | Natural justice requirement; charges must be communicated in writing. |
| **Article 311(2)(a)** | Exception: Conviction on criminal charge | Departmental inquiry dispensed with automatically. |
| **Article 311(2)(b)** | Exception: Not reasonably practicable to hold inquiry | Disciplinary authority must record reasons in writing. |
| **Article 311(2)(c)** | Exception: In the interest of the security of the State | President/Governor subjective satisfaction (*Tulsiram Patel 1985*). |
| **Article 312** | All India Services (IAS, IPS, IFoS 1966) | Created by Rajya Sabha special resolution (2/3 present & voting). |

### Matrix B: The 7 Supreme Court Directives in *Prakash Singh (2006)*

| Directive No. | Structural Institutional Reform | Core Mechanism / Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Directive 1** | **State Security Commission (SSC)** | Bipartisan oversight body (Home Minister, LoP, Chief Secy, DGP, jurists) to prevent political interference. |
| **Directive 2** | **Selection & Minimum Tenure of DGP** | UPSC empanels 3 senior-most officers; State selects one; **minimum 2-year tenure** regardless of retirement. |
| **Directive 3** | **Minimum Tenure of Field Officers** | Inspector General (IG), SP, and Station House Officer (SHO) get **minimum 2-year tenure** in field. |
| **Directive 4** | **Separation of Investigation** | Bifurcate investigation police from law and order police in all urban areas to professionalize forensics. |
| **Directive 5** | **Police Establishment Board (PEB)** | DGP + 4 senior officers regulate transfers, postings, and promotions below SP rank internally. |
| **Directive 6** | **Police Complaints Authority (PCA)** | State level (headed by retired HC judge) and District level (retired Dist judge) inquire into custodial death/rape. |
| **Directive 7** | **National Security Commission (NSC)** | Federal commission to select and place chiefs of Central Police Organizations and CAPFs with fixed tenure. |
