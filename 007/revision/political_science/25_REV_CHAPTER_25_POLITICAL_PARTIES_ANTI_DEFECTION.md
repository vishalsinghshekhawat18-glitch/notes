## 3. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Split Exemption is Dead**: The 1/3rd split exemption was **abolished** by the 91st Amendment in 2003; only a 2/3rd merger is protected today.
2. **Voluntarily Giving Up Membership**: Does **not** require a formal resignation letter; conduct and anti-party actions suffice (*Ravi S. Naik 1994*).
3. **Nominated vs. Independent Asymmetry**: Independent members cannot join any party; nominated members have a **6-month grace period** to join a party.
4. **Speaker as Tribunal**: In *Kihoto Hollohan (1992)*, the Speaker was held to act as a **Tribunal**, bringing their orders under judicial review.
5. **Whip Condonation Window**: A party has **15 days** to forgive or condone a member's defiance of the party whip.
