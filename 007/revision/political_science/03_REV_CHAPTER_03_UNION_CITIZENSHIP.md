## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **The Article 368 Reorganization Trap**: Altering the name, area, or boundaries of a state does **NOT require an amendment under Article 368**. Article 4(2) explicitly provides that such laws pass by an ordinary **Simple Majority**.
2. **The Berubari Cession Trap**: While creating or diminishing a state under Article 3 needs only a simple majority, **ceding Indian territory to a foreign country CANNOT be done under Article 3**. It strictly requires a **Constitutional Amendment under Article 368** (*Berubari Union 1960*).
3. **The State Veto Fallacy**: State legislatures have **ZERO VETO** over boundary alterations. The President must refer the bill to them, but Parliament can pass the bill even if the State Assembly unanimously votes to reject it.
4. **The US vs. India Presidential Eligibility Trap**: In the US, only a *natural-born citizen* can become President. In India, **both a citizen by birth and a naturalized citizen** are eligible to hold the office of President of India.
5. **The OCI Dual Citizenship Trap**: OCI cardholders do **NOT** have dual citizenship. They are foreign passport holders granted an immigration status; they have zero political rights and cannot vote in Indian elections.
