## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: The Three Constitutional Emergencies Compared

| Dimension | National Emergency (Article 352) | President's Rule (Article 356) | Financial Emergency (Article 360) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trigger Grounds** | War, External Aggression, or **Armed Rebellion**. | Failure of Constitutional Machinery in State (**Art 356 & 365**). | Threat to Financial Stability or Credit of India. |
| **Cabinet Advice** | **Written recommendation of Cabinet** (44th Amend). | Governor's report or otherwise. | General advice. |
| **Approval Window** | **1 Month** (both Houses). | **2 Months** (both Houses). | **2 Months** (both Houses). |
| **Majority Required**| **Special Majority** (>50% total seats + 2/3rds present & voting). | **Simple Majority** (>50% present & voting). | **Simple Majority** (>50% present & voting). |
| **Single Tenure** | 6 Months. | 6 Months. | **Indefinite** until revoked. |
| **Maximum Ceiling** | **Indefinite** (with 6-month approvals). | **3 Years Maximum** (1-year bar rules under 44th Amend). | **Indefinite** (No 6-month renewal needed!). |
| **State Government** | State Govt continues under Central directions. | State Ministry **DISMISSED**. | State Govt continues under financial canons. |
| **State Legislature** | State Assembly continues; Parliament gets concurrent power. | State Assembly **SUSPENDED or DISSOLVED**. | State Assembly continues; Money bills reserved. |
| **Effect on FRs** | Art 19 suspended (358); Other FRs except 20/21 suspended (359). | **ZERO impact on Fundamental Rights!** | **ZERO impact on Fundamental Rights!** |
| **Revocation Rule** | Lok Sabha simple majority resolution can revoke. | President revokes at will. | President revokes at will. |

### Matrix B: Fundamental Rights Suspension: Article 358 vs. Article 359

| Dimension | Article 358 | Article 359 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rights Affected** | Confined **ONLY to Article 19** (6 Democratic Freedoms). | Extends to all specified FRs, **EXCEPT Articles 20 and 21**! |
| **Operation Mechanism**| **Automatic**: Suspends Art 19 immediately on proclamation. | **Presidential Order**: Requires an explicit notification. |
| **Grounds Limitation** | Operates **ONLY during External Emergency** (War/Aggression). | Operates during **BOTH External and Internal (Armed Rebellion)** emergencies. |
| **Territorial Extent** | Entire country automatically. | Pan-India or specific territories specified in order. |
| **Substantive vs Remedial**| Suspends the substantive rights under Article 19. | Suspends the **remedial right to move courts** (rights exist, but cannot be enforced). |

## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **The Fundamental Rights Confusion**: Neither President's Rule (Article 356) nor Financial Emergency (Article 360) affects Fundamental Rights. FRs are affected **ONLY during a National Emergency (Article 352)**.
2. **The Armed Rebellion Article 358 Trap**: Article 19 is NOT automatically suspended under Article 358 during an internal emergency declared on grounds of Armed Rebellion. It suspends **ONLY during War or External Aggression**.
3. **The Article 20/21 Inviolability**: Articles 20 and 21 **CAN NEVER BE SUSPENDED** under Article 359 under any circumstance (44th Amendment Act 1978).
4. **The Financial Emergency Renewal Trap**: Unlike Articles 352 and 356, **Financial Emergency under Article 360 DOES NOT REQUIRE repeated 6-month parliamentary approval**. It continues indefinitely until revoked by the President.
5. **The High Court Exemption**: During President's Rule, the President can dismiss the State Government and dissolve the Assembly, but **CANNOT assume the powers of the State High Court**.
