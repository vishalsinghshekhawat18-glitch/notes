## 3. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Gandhi as Anarchist**: Gandhi is formally classified as a "Philosophical Anarchist" because he regarded the state as an engine of institutionalized violence and envisioned an ultimate stateless society.
2. **Gramsci's Hegemony Forum**: Hegemony operates primarily through **Civil Society** (schools, churches, families, press), while domination/coercion operates through Political Society (police, army, courts).
3. **Indian Socialism Case Law**: *Excel Wear (1978)* established that the Preamble's socialism does not authorize total nationalization or abolish private ownership; *D.S. Nakara (1983)* confirmed it is a blend favoring Gandhian welfare.
4. **Althusser's Apparatuses**: RSA functions by direct coercion/violence; ISA functions primarily by ideology/conditioning.
5. **Guild Socialism**: Led by G.D.H. Cole, it advocates worker self-management organized around trade guilds, rejecting state central planning.
