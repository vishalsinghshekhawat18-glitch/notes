## 3. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **FCRA Receipt Monopoly**: Foreign contributions cannot be received in any local commercial bank account; they must pass through the **SBI New Delhi Main Branch**.
2. **Citizen's Charter Enforceability**: Citizen's Charters are **not legally enforceable** in Indian courts. Statutory enforceability exists only through state Public Service Guarantee Acts.
3. **FCRA 2020 Sub-Granting**: An NGO registered under FCRA cannot transfer its funds to another NGO, even if that second NGO also holds an FCRA registration.
4. **First Public Service Guarantee State**: Madhya Pradesh was the first state in 2010 to pass the *Right to Public Services Act*.
5. **Political Parties vs Pressure Groups**: Pressure groups do not field candidates or contest elections; their role is external influence.
