## 4. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **L. Chandra Kumar Appeal Route**: You **cannot bypass the High Court**; you must challenge Tribunal orders before a Division Bench of the High Court (Arts 226/227) before going to the Supreme Court.
2. **NGT Wildlife Exclusion**: The Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972 is **not** under NGT jurisdiction.
3. **Armed Forces Excluded from CAT**: Armed Forces personnel cannot approach CAT; they have the Armed Forces Tribunal (AFT).
4. **NGT Appeal is Direct to SC**: Unlike administrative tribunals, appeals against NGT orders go **directly to the Supreme Court** (Section 22).
5. **No CPC Technicalities**: Tribunals are guided by Principles of Natural Justice and are not shackled by the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908.
