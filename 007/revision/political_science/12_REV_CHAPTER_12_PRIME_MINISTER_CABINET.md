## 4. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **Collective Responsibility Target**: Ministers are collectively responsible to the **Lok Sabha**, NOT to Parliament as a whole (Rajya Sabha cannot pass a No-Confidence Motion).
2. **Rajya Sabha Ministers Resign**: When a No-Confidence Motion passes in the Lok Sabha, ministers who are Rajya Sabha MPs **must also resign** under collective responsibility.
3. **PM Death vs Minister Death**: PM’s death/resignation **dissolves** the entire Ministry. A minister's death/resignation merely causes a **vacancy**.
4. **Cabinet in 1950**: The word "Cabinet" did not exist in the 1950 Constitution text. It was added to Article 352 by the 44th Amendment in 1978.
5. **Cabinet Committees are Not Constitutional**: They are executive bodies established under the *Rules of Business, 1961*.
6. **No Minimum Cap for Union**: The 91st Amendment prescribes a minimum of 12 ministers for **States** (Art 164(1A)), but specifies **NO minimum number** for the Union Council of Ministers (only a 15% maximum cap under Art 75(1A))!
