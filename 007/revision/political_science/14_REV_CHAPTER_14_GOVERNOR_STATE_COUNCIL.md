## 3. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **SPSC Chairman Removal**: Governor appoints the SPSC Chairman, but **only the President can remove them** (Article 317).
2. **State Election Commissioner Removal**: Governor appoints the SEC, but the SEC is removed in the like manner and grounds as a **High Court Judge** (Article 243K).
3. **Co-operatives in Council**: "Co-operative movement" is an eligible field for the Governor's nominations to the State Legislative Council (Art 171(5)), but **not** for the President's nominations to the Rajya Sabha (Art 80(3)).
4. **Minimum 12 Ministers**: States must have at least 12 ministers under Article 164(1A), but the Union has **no statutory minimum** under Article 75(1A).
5. **No Pocket Veto for Governor**: Recent SC rulings (*Punjab 2023*, *Tamil Nadu 2024*) mandate that the Governor cannot withhold assent indefinitely; if assent is withheld, the bill must be returned to the legislature for reconsideration.
6. **Bihar Removal**: Bihar was removed from the mandatory Tribal Welfare Minister list by the 94th Amendment Act, 2006.
