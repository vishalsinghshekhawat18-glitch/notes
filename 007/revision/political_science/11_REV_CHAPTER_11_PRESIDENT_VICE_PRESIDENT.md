## 4. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **State Legislative Councils (MLCs)**: Never participate in Presidential elections or impeachment.
2. **Nominated MPs Asymmetry**: They do **not** vote in the Presidential election, but they **do** vote in Presidential impeachment!
3. **Census Freeze**: 1971 population baseline holds until the first census post-2026 under the 84th Constitutional Amendment Act 2001.
4. **Veto on State Bills (Article 201)**: The President is **not bound** to give assent even if the State Legislature repasses the returned bill. (Contrast with Parliament under Article 111, where President is bound upon repassage).
5. **No Ordinance During Session**: An ordinance promulgated when **both** Houses of Parliament are in session is void ab initio.
6. **Vice-President's Ex-Officio Salary**: The VP does not receive a salary as Vice-President; he receives salary as Chairman of Rajya Sabha (₹4 lakh/month). When acting as President, he draws the President's salary (₹5 lakh/month).
