## 3. High-Yield Exam Traps

1. **CBI is NOT Statutory**: It was created by an executive resolution in 1963 and derives powers from the *Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946*.
2. **NIA Needs NO State Consent**: Unlike the CBI which requires state general/specific consent, the NIA can investigate scheduled terror offenses anywhere in India without asking the State.
3. **CVC 4-Year Tenure**: The CVC serves a 4-year term, not 3 or 5 years.
4. **NHRC Has No Punitive Power**: NHRC recommendations are purely advisory and cannot enforce compensation directly or punish offenders.
5. **NITI Aayog Has No Grant Powers**: Unlike the old Planning Commission, NITI Aayog cannot allocate financial grants to states.
6. **First Lokayukta State**: Maharashtra in 1971 (Odisha passed the law first in 1970, but implemented later).
