## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Tidal Gravitational Proportion**: Tide-generating force varies inversely as the **CUBE of the distance ($1/r^3$)**, NOT the square of distance ($1/r^2$). That is why the Moon exerts more than double the tidal force of the Sun despite being millions of times smaller.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Deep-Water Wave Speed Dependence**: The speed of a deep-water wave depends **ONLY on wavelength ($L$)**, completely independent of water depth. Conversely, the speed of a shallow-water wave depends **ONLY on water depth ($d$)**, completely independent of wavelength.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Airspace Sovereignty Under UNCLOS**: A coastal state's sovereign control over **airspace** extends ONLY over its **Internal Waters and Territorial Sea (up to 12 NM)**. It does NOT have sovereignty over the airspace of the Contiguous Zone or the EEZ (foreign aircraft enjoy freedom of overflight beyond 12 NM).
- [ ] **Trap 4: Darwin's Reef Theory Validation**: Darwin's subsidence theory was empirically proven by drilling at **Enewetak Atoll** in 1952, which hit basaltic volcanic bedrock beneath $1,283\text{ meters}$ of shallow-water coral limestone.
- [ ] **Trap 5: EEZ Water Column vs. High Seas**: In the EEZ (12–200 NM), foreign vessels enjoy complete **freedom of navigation and overflight**; the coastal state's sovereign jurisdiction applies exclusively to the economic exploitation of living and non-living natural resources.
