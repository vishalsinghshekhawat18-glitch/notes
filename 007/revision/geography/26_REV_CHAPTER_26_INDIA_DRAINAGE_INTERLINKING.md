## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Panch Prayag Tributary Sequence**:
  * **Vishnuprayag** = Alaknanda + **Dhauliganga**
  * **Nandaprayag** = Alaknanda + **Nandakini**
  * **Karnaprayag** = Alaknanda + **Pindar**
  * **Rudraprayag** = Alaknanda + **Mandakini**
  * **Devprayag** = Alaknanda + **Bhagirathi** (Where it becomes the Ganga).
- [ ] **Trap 2: Ganga Tributary Originating in Plains**: The **Gomti River** is the only major tributary of the Ganga that originates in the **alluvial plains (Gomat Taal / Fulhar Jheel in Pilibhit, UP)**, NOT in the Himalayas or Peninsular plateau.
- [ ] **Trap 3: River Crossing Tropic of Cancer Twice**: The **Mahi River** is the **ONLY river in India that crosses the Tropic of Cancer ($23.5^\circ\text{N}$) twice** (first flowing north in Madhya Pradesh/Rajasthan, and then curving southwest into Gujarat).
- [ ] **Trap 4: Perennial Nature of Cauvery**: Unlike other peninsular rivers that dry up drastically in summer, the **Cauvery maintains near-perennial flow** because its upper catchment (Karnataka) receives rain from the **Southwest Monsoon (June–Sept)**, while its lower catchment (Tamil Nadu) receives rain from the **Northeast Monsoon (Oct–Dec)**.
- [ ] **Trap 5: First River Interlinking Project**: The first river interlinking project implemented in India is the **Ken-Betwa Link Project (KBLP)**, NOT the Ganga-Cauvery or Godavari-Krishna link.
- [ ] **Trap 6: Dominant Source of Indian Irrigation**: While Major and Medium canal projects receive the vast majority of public capital expenditure, **Minor Irrigation (primarily private groundwater tube-wells) irrigates $> 65\text{--}70\%$ of India's net cropped land**, NOT major canal networks!
- [ ] **Trap 7: Irrigation Classification Thresholds**: In Indian planning norms, a **Major Irrigation Project has a CCA $> 10,000\text{ hectares}$**, Medium is **$2,000\text{ to }10,000\text{ hectares}$**, and Minor is **$< 2,000\text{ hectares}$**. (Candidates frequently confuse hectares with square kilometers or acres).
