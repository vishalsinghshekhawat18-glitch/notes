## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Cyclones at the Equator**: Tropical cyclones NEVER form between 0° and 5° N/S because the Coriolis force is zero; converging air directly fills the low without establishing cyclostrophic rotation.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Energy Source Comparison**: Temperate cyclones derive energy from **baroclinic instability** (temperature contrasts between cold polar and warm tropical air). Tropical cyclones derive energy from **latent heat of condensation** released from warm ocean waters.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Eye of the Storm Conditions**: The Eye has the **lowest barometric pressure** and **warmest temperature aloft**, but winds are **calm** ($<15\text{ km/h}$) and skies are clear due to descending, compressional warming air. The violent winds reside in the **Eyewall**, NOT the Eye.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Wind Veering vs. Backing**: In the Northern Hemisphere, as a warm front approaches, winds **back** (counter-clockwise: E to SE), but upon frontal passage, winds **veer** (clockwise: SE to SW at warm front, SW to NW at cold front).
- [ ] **Trap 5: Landfall Dissipation**: Tropical cyclones die over land NOT simply because of friction, but primarily because their thermodynamic fuel supply (**latent heat from warm ocean water evaporation**) is abruptly terminated.
