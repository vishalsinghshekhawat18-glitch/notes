## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Kota Barrage Hydropower Output**: The **Kota Barrage produces ZERO megawatt (0 MW) of electricity**. It is exclusively an **irrigation barrage** constructed to divert water into the Left and Right Main Canals. The three power-generating dams of the Chambal project are Gandhi Sagar ($115\text{ MW}$), Rana Pratap Sagar ($172\text{ MW}$), and Jawahar Sagar ($99\text{ MW}$).
- [ ] **Trap 2: Gandhi Sagar Dam Location**: **Gandhi Sagar Dam is NOT located in Rajasthan**; it is located in the **Mandsaur district of Madhya Pradesh**.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Longest River in Rajasthan vs. Longest River ENTIRELY in Rajasthan**:
  * **Longest River flowing through Rajasthan**: **Chambal River ($1,051\text{ km}$ total)**.
  * **Longest River flowing ENTIRELY within Rajasthan**: **Banas River ($512\text{ km}$)**.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Jojari River Distinction**: **Jojari** is the **ONLY tributary of the Luni River that originates outside the Aravallis** (originates in the plains of Pondlu village in Nagaur) and is the **ONLY tributary that joins Luni from its RIGHT BANK**.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Pachpadra vs. Didwana Salt Chemical Type**: **Pachpadra Lake** produces edible high-grade **Sodium Chloride ($98\%\text{ NaCl}$)**. **Didwana Lake** produces non-edible **Sodium Sulfate ($Na_2SO_4$)**, used for industrial leather tanning and dyeing, NOT human consumption.
