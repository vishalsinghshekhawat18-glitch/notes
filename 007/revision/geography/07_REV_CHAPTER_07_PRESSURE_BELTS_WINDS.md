## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

1. [ ] **Coriolis at Equator**: The Coriolis force is ZERO at the Equator ($\sin 0^\circ = 0$), NOT maximum.
2. [ ] **Equatorial Cyclone Absence**: Tropical cyclones CANNOT form within 0°–5° of the Equator because zero Coriolis force prevents vortex rotation.
3. [ ] **Subtropical High Genesis**: Dynamically induced by upper-air subsidence of cooling air, NOT thermally induced.
4. [ ] **Geostrophic Wind Trajectory**: Blows strictly PARALLEL to isobars, NOT across them from high to low pressure.
5. [ ] **Roaring Forties Hemisphere**: Located between 40°S and 50°S in the SOUTHERN Hemisphere, NOT in North America.
6. [ ] **Doldrums Wind State**: Characterized by calm, light winds with dominant vertical convective updrafts, NOT violent storms.
7. [ ] **Tropical Easterly Jet Timing**: Operates ONLY in summer (June–Sept) due to Tibetan Plateau heating; absent in winter.
8. [ ] **Coriolis Velocity Impact**: Coriolis alters ONLY the direction of wind; it CANNOT change wind speed.
9. [ ] **Mediterranean Rainfall Season**: Receives rain in WINTER from the Westerlies, while summers are bone-dry under Subtropical Highs.
10. [ ] **Trade Wind Desert Location**: Trade winds cause hot deserts on the WESTERN margins of continents where they blow offshore.
