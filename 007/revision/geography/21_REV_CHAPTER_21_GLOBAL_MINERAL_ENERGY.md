## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Country with Largest Proven Oil Reserves**: **Venezuela** has the world's largest proven oil reserves (~303 billion barrels, primarily in the Orinoco Heavy Oil Belt), surpassing Saudi Arabia (~267 billion barrels). However, the United States is the #1 **producer** of crude oil due to the shale fracking revolution.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Magnetite vs. Hematite Abundance**: **Magnetite** has the higher iron content ($72.4\%\text{ Fe}$), but **Hematite ($69.9\%\text{ Fe}$)** is by far the most widely mined and industrially consumed iron ore globally.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Hard-Rock vs. Brine Lithium Geography**: The **Lithium Triangle** (Bolivia, Chile, Argentina) extracts lithium from evaporitic salt **brines** (*salars*). **Australia** (the world's largest lithium producer by volume) extracts lithium from **hard-rock spodumene pegmatites** (Greenbushes).
- [ ] **Trap 4: Congo (DRC) Cobalt Monopoly**: The **Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC)** produces over **$70\%$ of the world's mined cobalt**, but over **$75\%$ of DRC's cobalt is shipped to China for refining**.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Coal Classification Carbon Content**: **Anthracite** has the highest fixed carbon ($90\text{–}95\%$) and calorific value, but represents only ~1% of global reserves. **Bituminous coal** is the most commercially mined and utilized coal on Earth.
