## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Suez vs. Panama Lock System**: The **Suez Canal has ZERO locks** (it is an open sea-level cut connecting the Mediterranean and Red Sea). The **Panama Canal relies on a complex freshwater lock system** to lift vessels $26\text{ meters}$ above sea level across artificial Gatun Lake.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Nations Bordering Strait of Hormuz**: The Strait of Hormuz is bordered by **Iran to the north and OMAN (the Musandam Peninsula exclave) to the south**. The United Arab Emirates (UAE) does NOT directly control the narrowest chokepoint waters.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Montreux Convention Authority**: Under the **Montreux Convention of 1936**, Turkey has the sovereign authority to close the Bosporus and Dardanelles straits to warships of belligerent nations during times of war.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Lombok Strait Depth vs. Malacca**: The **Strait of Malacca has a depth limitation of ~20.5 meters (Malaccamax)**. Fully laden ultra-large crude carriers (ULCCs / Capesize ships) that draft deeper than 20.5 meters CANNOT transit Malacca and must divert through the **deep-water Lombok Strait (>250 m deep)**.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Panama Canal Drought Threat**: The Panama Canal is vulnerable to droughts (such as during intense El Niño years) because each ship transit drains over **200 million liters of fresh water** from **Gatun Lake** into the oceans; low lake levels force severe draft restrictions and transit queue backlogs.
