## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Chhappan Hills vs. Chhappan Plain**:
  * **Chhappan Hills (*Chhappan ki Pahadiyaan*)**: A circular cluster of **56 rounded granitic monadnock hills** located in **Siwana (Barmer)** in Western Rajasthan (hosts Nakoda Parbat).
  * **Chhappan Plain (*Chhappan ka Maidan*)**: A fertile alluvial river plain drained by **56 streams / villages** located between **Banswara and Pratapgarh** along the **Mahi River** in Southern Rajasthan.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Highest Plateau in Rajasthan**: The highest plateau in Rajasthan is the **ORIYA PLATEAU ($1,295\text{ m}$)** on Mount Abu (situated just beneath Guru Shikhar), NOT the Bhorat Plateau ($1,225\text{ m}$).
- [ ] **Trap 3: Bhorat Plateau Exact Boundaries**: The **Bhorat Plateau** is situated specifically between **Kumbhalgarh (in Rajsamand)** and **Gogunda (in Udaipur)**.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Most Widespread Sand Dune Type in Rajasthan**: While Barchans are the most famous and dangerous, the **most widespread and numerically abundant sand dune type across Rajasthan is the PARABOLIC DUNE**.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Ramgarh Geological Origin**: The circular Ramgarh structure in Baran district is **NOT a volcanic crater**; it has been scientifically confirmed as a **Meteorite Impact Crater (Astrobleme)**.
