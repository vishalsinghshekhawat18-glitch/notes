## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

1. [ ] **Tropopause Temperature Paradox**: The tropopause is coldest over the Equator (~ -80°C), NOT over the Poles (~ -45°C) due to greater convective height (18 km vs. 8 km).
2. [ ] **Tropospheric Heating**: Heated from below by longwave terrestrial radiation, NOT directly by incoming shortwave solar rays.
3. [ ] **Most Abundant Greenhouse Gas**: Water vapor accounts for 60–70% of natural greenhouse effect, NOT carbon dioxide.
4. [ ] **Thermosphere Sensible Heat**: Gas kinetic temperature exceeds 1,500°C, but near-vacuum density means heat conduction is zero; an astronaut would freeze.
5. [ ] **Maximum Surface Insolation**: Subtropical deserts (Sahara, Thar) receive higher surface insolation than the Equator due to persistent clear skies.
6. [ ] **Planetary Albedo Budget**: 35 units of solar energy are reflected immediately back to space WITHOUT heating Earth or air.
7. [ ] **Inversion Effect on Rain**: Temperature inversions suppress convection and prevent rain; they do NOT cause thunderstorms.
8. [ ] **Valley Inversion Orchard Placement**: Orchards are planted on hillside slopes to escape freezing katabatic air pooling in valley floors.
9. [ ] **Ionosphere Night Radio Layer**: Long-distance nighttime shortwave radio is reflected by the F-layer; the D-layer vanishes at night.
10. [ ] **Latent Heat vs. Conduction**: Latent heat of condensation (19 units) transfers over 3 times more heat from surface to atmosphere than conduction (6 units).
