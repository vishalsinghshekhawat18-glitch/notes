## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Maximum Salinity Latitude**: Maximum open-ocean salinity is NOT at the Equator; it is centered in the **Subtropical High Pressure belts ($20^\circ\text{ to }35^\circ\text{ N/S}$)** where evaporation vastly exceeds precipitation.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Dittmar's Principle Scope**: Dittmar's Principle states that the **relative ratios** of major dissolved ions remain constant, even though the **total absolute salinity** varies substantially from sea to sea.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Arabian Sea vs. Bay of Bengal Salinity**: The **Arabian Sea is significantly MORE saline** ($36‰\text{–}37‰$) than the **Bay of Bengal** ($30‰\text{–}33‰$), because the Bay of Bengal receives colossal freshwater discharge from the Ganges, Brahmaputra, and peninsular rivers.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Freezing Density of Seawater**: Unlike pure freshwater which reaches maximum density at $3.98^\circ\text{C}$ and expands upon cooling, normal seawater ($S > 24.7‰$) becomes **progressively denser all the way down to its freezing point ($-1.9^\circ\text{C}$)**.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Ocean Acidification Ion Depletion**: Ocean acidification does NOT simply mean the ocean becomes acidic (pH is still alkaline at ~8.1); the catastrophic damage occurs because excess $H^+$ ions bind with and **deplete carbonate ions ($CO_3^{2-})$**, depriving corals and pteropods of the building blocks for $CaCO_3$ shells.
