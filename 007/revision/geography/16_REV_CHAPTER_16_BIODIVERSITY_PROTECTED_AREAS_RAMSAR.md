| Hotspot Name | Geographic Territory in India | Endemic Flagship Fauna | Endemic / Characteristic Flora | High-Yield Exam Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Western Ghats & Sri Lanka** | Continuous west coast mountain chain (~$1,600\text{ km}$; 6 states) | **Lion-tailed Macaque**, **Nilgiri Tahr**, Purple Frog | *Strobilanthes kunthiana* (*Neelakurinji*), wild nutmeg | Traverses Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu |

| **Indo-Burma** | NE states south of Brahmaputra + **Andaman Islands** | **Hoolock Gibbon** (India's only ape), Sangai (Eld's deer) | *Nepenthes khasiana* (Pitcher plant), rare orchids | **Andaman Islands fall under Indo-Burma**, NOT Sundaland |

* **Hotspot Criteria (Norman Myers)**: $\ge 1,500$ species of vascular plants as endemics ($>0.5\%$ of global total) AND has lost $\ge 70\%$ of primary native habitat.

### Matrix B: Protected Area Legal Hierarchy (WPA 1972)

| Protected Category | Governing WPA Section | Human Rights & Grazing | Boundary Alteration Authority | Land Ownership |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **National Park (NP)** | Section 35 | **Completely Prohibited**: No grazing, no private rights | **ONLY on recommendation of NBWL** (Headed by PM) | Exclusively Government owned |
| **Wildlife Sanctuary (WLS)** | Section 18 / 26A | **Regulated**: Chief Wildlife Warden may permit limited grazing | **ONLY on recommendation of NBWL** | Exclusively Government owned |
| **Conservation Reserve** | Section 36A (2002 amend) | Buffer/corridor connecting parks; local rights preserved | State Govt after consulting local community | Exclusively Government owned |
| **Community Reserve** | Section 36C (2002 amend) | Traditional community conservation managed by Panchayat | Cannot be denotified without NBWL approval | **Private or Community owned** |

* **WPA 1972 Amendment (2022 / Effective 2023)**:
  * Consolidated into **4 Schedules**: Schedule I (Highest protection), Schedule II (Lesser protection), Schedule III (Protected Plants), Schedule IV (CITES species).
  * **Schedule V ("Vermin") completely repealed**.

## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Andaman vs. Nicobar Hotspot Split**: The **Andaman Islands** belong to the **Indo-Burma Hotspot**, whereas the **Nicobar Islands** belong to the **Sundaland Hotspot**. They are NOT in the same hotspot.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Grazing Rights in National Parks**: Commercial or domestic livestock grazing is **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** in National Parks under Section 35 of WPA 1972. In Wildlife Sanctuaries, limited grazing may be permitted by the Chief Wildlife Warden.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Montreux Record Delisting**: **Chilika Lake is NO LONGER on the Montreux Record**. It was placed on it in 1993 and successfully removed in 2002 after the opening of a new mouth to the sea restored salinity. Only **Keoladeo** and **Loktak** remain on the record.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Schedule V in WPA Amendment 2022**: The old Schedule V (which listed "Vermin" like common crows, fruit bats, mice, and rats that could be hunted freely) was **completely deleted** in the Wildlife Protection Amendment Act 2022.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Biosphere Reserve WNBR Count**: Out of India's 18 designated Biosphere Reserves, only **12 are recognized under UNESCO's World Network of Biosphere Reserves (WNBR)**. The remaining 6 (including Kachchh and Cold Desert) are national designations only.
