## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
Ocean Currents: Wind friction (initiator) + Coriolis (NH right, SH left) + Density (thermohaline) + Continents (gyres) 
➔ Ekman Spiral: Surface moves 45° to wind; Net Ekman Transport is EXACTLY 90° to wind (NH right, SH left) 
➔ Coastal Upwelling: Offshore Ekman transport pulls cold nutrient water to surface (Rich fish, coastal desert) 
➔ Western Intensification: Stommel theorem, Coriolis β-effect ➔ Western boundary currents (Gulf Stream, Kuroshio) narrow, deep, fast 
➔ Eastern boundary (Canary, Peru, Benguela, California) wide, shallow, slow, cold ➔ Creates world deserts: Atacama, Sahara, Namib 
➔ Sargasso Sea: Calm, high salinity (37‰), rootless brown Sargassum weed, no land boundaries (Atlantic Gyre) 
➔ Northern Indian Ocean: ONLY ocean where currents reverse seasonally (Clockwise SW Monsoon in summer, Counter-clockwise NE in winter) 
➔ Thermohaline Conveyor Belt: Broecker 1,000-year cycle ➔ NADW sinks in North Atlantic, AMOC tipping point via Greenland melt 
➔ Normal Pacific: Trade winds pile warm pool in Indonesia, upwelling in Peru, Walker circulation 
➔ El Niño: Trades collapse, warm water sloshes East, Peru warms, fisheries die, SOI negative (Tahiti < Darwin), Indian monsoon drought 
➔ La Niña: Trades supercharged, Peru super cold, SOI positive, Indian monsoon floods 
➔ El Niño Modoki: Central Pacific warm, flanked by cold East/West 
➔ IOD: Dipole Mode Index ➔ Positive (+IOD: Warm West, Cold East) boosts Indian monsoon ➔ MJO: 30–60 day eastward rain pulse
```

## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Direction of Net Ekman Transport**: The surface water moves at $45^\circ$ to the wind, but the **net integrated transport of the entire water column is at $90^\circ$ to the wind** (to the right in the NH, to the left in the SH).
- [ ] **Trap 2: Southern Oscillation Index Sign**: $\text{SOI} = P_{\text{Tahiti}} - P_{\text{Darwin}}$. When pressure at Darwin is higher than Tahiti, the index is **NEGATIVE**, which corresponds to **El Niño**. A **POSITIVE** SOI corresponds to **La Niña**.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Unique Feature of Northern Indian Ocean**: The Northern Indian Ocean is the **only ocean basin in the world where surface currents completely reverse direction** biannually in response to the seasonal reversal of the Southwest and Northeast Monsoons.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Atacama Desert Aridity Engine**: The Atacama Desert is hyper-arid NOT merely because of rain shadows, but primarily because the cold **Peru (Humboldt) Current** stabilizes the air, creates strong temperature inversions, and suppresses all vertical convection.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Positive IOD vs. El Niño**: A **Positive IOD** is beneficial for India; it acts in the opposite direction of El Niño and can completely rescue the Indian Monsoon from an El Niño-induced drought (as occurred in 1997).
