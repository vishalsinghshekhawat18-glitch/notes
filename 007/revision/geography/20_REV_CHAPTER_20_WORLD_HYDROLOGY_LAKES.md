## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: The Only Great Lake Entirely in the USA**: **Lake Michigan** is the only one of the five Great Lakes located **entirely within the territory of the United States**. The other four (Superior, Huron, Erie, Ontario) straddle the international border with Canada.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Deepest Lake vs. Largest Freshwater Area**: **Lake Superior** is the largest freshwater lake on Earth by **surface area** ($82,103\text{ km}^2$), but **Lake Baikal** is the **deepest ($1,642\text{ m}$)** and holds the largest **volume of liquid fresh water** (more than all 5 Great Lakes combined).

- [ ] **Trap 5: Driving Force of Aral Sea Collapse**: The catastrophic drying of the Aral Sea was NOT driven primarily by global climate change; it was an **engineered ecological disaster caused by the Soviet Union diverting the Amu Darya and Syr Darya rivers** to irrigate desert cotton fields.
