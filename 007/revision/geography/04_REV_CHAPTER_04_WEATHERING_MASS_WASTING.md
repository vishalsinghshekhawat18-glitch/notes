## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

1. [ ] **Mass Wasting Fluid Role**: Gravity is the primary mover; water acts purely as a pore-pressure lubricant, NOT as a fluid transport agent.
2. [ ] **Davisian Uplift Premise**: Davis assumed erosion starts ONLY AFTER uplift completely ceases (a major physical fallacy).
3. [ ] **Slump vs. Slide Geometry**: A Slump has rotational movement along a curved concave plane; a Slide has planar movement along a flat plane.
4. [ ] **Chemical Weathering Peak**: Maximum in warm, humid equatorial/monsoon climates; negligible in hyper-arid cold/hot deserts due to lack of moisture.
5. [ ] **Water Freezing Expansion**: Water expands by 9% upon freezing, NOT 25%.
6. [ ] **Monadnock Attribution**: A Monadnock is the residual hill on a Davisian Peneplain; an Inselberg/Bornhardt belongs to Penck/King.
7. [ ] **Karst Chemistry**: Carbonation converts insoluble calcium carbonate into soluble calcium bicarbonate via carbonic acid, NOT oxidation.
8. [ ] **Solifluction Habitat**: Confined to cold periglacial tundra over permafrost, NOT tropical monsoon hills.
9. [ ] **Exfoliation Dome Driver**: Formed primarily by Pressure Release / Unloading of overburden rock, NOT chemical solution.
10. [ ] **Talus Cone Maximum Angle**: Limited by the Angle of Repose (~33°–35°); cannot stand stable at near-vertical angles.
