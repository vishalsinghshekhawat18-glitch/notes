## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text

➔ De Candolle (1874): Megatherms (A) ➔ Xerophytes (B) ➔ Mesotherms (C) ➔ Microtherms (D) ➔ Hekistotherms (E) 
➔ Köppen 2nd Letters: f (no dry season) ➔ m (monsoon) ➔ w (winter dry) ➔ s (summer dry, Mediterranean) ➔ S (Steppe) ➔ W (Desert) ➔ T (Tundra) ➔ F (Frost) 
➔ Köppen 3rd Letters: a (hot summer >= 22°C) ➔ b (warm summer < 22°C) ➔ c (short cool summer) ➔ d (extreme winter < -38°C, Siberia only) ➔ h (hot B: >= 18°C) ➔ k (cold B: < 18°C) 
➔ Dryness Threshold (r): Summer rain = 2T+28 ➔ Even rain = 2T+14 ➔ Winter rain = 2T ➔ P < r/2 is Desert (BW), r/2 <= P < r is Steppe (BS) 
➔ Mediterranean (Cs): Unique summer dry, winter wet via shifting Westerlies, sclerophyll vegetation 
➔ Taiga (D): Confined almost entirely to Northern Hemisphere (no vast landmass in SH 50°–70°S) 
➔ Thornthwaite: P-E / T-E index (1931) ➔ Potential Evapotranspiration & Moisture Index Im = (100S - 60D)/PET (1948) 
➔ Trewartha: 8 months >= 10°C defines Subtropical; 0°C coldest month boundary between C and D
```

## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Absence of D Climates in Southern Hemisphere**: Köppen's **D (Cold Continental / Microthermal)** climates are virtually **ABSENT in the Southern Hemisphere** because there are no extensive landmasses between 50°S and 70°S latitudes; the Southern Ocean dominates.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Mediterranean Rainfall Timing**: In **Cs (Mediterranean)** climate, rain falls in **WINTER** due to the equatorward migration of the Westerlies; summers are **DRY** due to the poleward shift of the Subtropical High Pressure cells.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Af vs. Am Driest Month**: In **Af (Rainforest)**, every single month must receive at least **$60\text{ mm}$** of precipitation. In **Am (Monsoon)**, the driest month receives **$< 60\text{ mm}$**, but the deficit is compensated by enormous monsoon downpours during the wet season.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Tundra Tree Line Limit**: In Köppen's **ET (Tundra)**, the warmest month mean temperature is between **$0^\circ\text{C}$ and $10^\circ\text{C}$**. The **$10^\circ\text{C}$ isotherm of the warmest month** forms the absolute global **polar tree line**; trees cannot grow if the warmest month fails to average at least $10^\circ\text{C}$.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Thornthwaite 1948 Factor 60**: In Thornthwaite's formula $I_m = (100S - 60D)/PET$, surplus ($S$) has a weight of 100 while deficit ($D$) has a weight of only 60. This is because plants can utilize moisture stored in the root-zone soil during dry periods.
