## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Lake Victoria Rift Origin**: **Lake Victoria is NOT located inside a rift valley graben**. Unlike lakes Tanganyika, Malawi, and Albert (which occupy true tectonic grabens), Lake Victoria sits in a shallow structural depression or sag on the plateau between the Eastern and Western rift branches.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Highest Peak in Europe**: The highest peak in Europe is **Mount Elbrus ($5,642\text{ m}$)** in the Caucasus Mountains of Russia, NOT Mont Blanc ($4,809\text{ m}$) in the Alps.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Vosges and Black Forest Horst Type**: The Vosges (France) and the Black Forest (Germany) are **Block Mountains (Horsts)**, and the Rhine River flows between them through a down-faulted **Rift Valley (Graben)**.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Andes vs. Himalayas Metric**: The **Andes** is the **LONGEST** continental mountain range on Earth (~$7,000\text{ km}$); the **Himalayas** is the **HIGHEST** mountain range on Earth.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Great Artesian Basin Aquifer Pressure**: In the Great Artesian Basin of Australia, water rises spontaneously to the surface in artesian wells due to natural **hydrostatic pressure** confined between tilted impermeable strata, requiring no mechanical pumping.
