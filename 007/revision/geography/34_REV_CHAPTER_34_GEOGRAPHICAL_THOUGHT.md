## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Who Coined "Possibilism"**: The term **"Possibilism" was COINED by French historian LUCIEN FEBVRE** in 1925, NOT by Paul Vidal de la Blache (who was the master practitioner/founder of the school).
- [ ] **Trap 2: Traffic Controller Metaphor**: The **"Traffic Controller" analogy** belongs to **GRIFFITH TAYLOR** under **Neo-Determinism (Stop-and-Go Determinism)**. Man is like a traffic officer who alters the rate, but not the direction of progress.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Hartshorne vs. Schaefer Debate**: **Richard Hartshorne** advocated for **Areal Differentiation** as an *idiographic* (particularizing/descriptive) study of regions. **Fred K. Schaefer** attacked this as "exceptionalism" and demanded that geography become *nomothetic* (law-seeking, spatial science).
- [ ] **Trap 4: Space vs. Place in Humanistic Geography**: In Yi-Fu Tuan's framework, **Space** is abstract, geometrical, and open, whereas **Place** is endowed with human meaning, security, memory, and emotional value (**Topophilia**).
- [ ] **Trap 5: Author of "Man is a Product of Earth's Surface"**: This iconic opening sentence was written by **ELLEN CHURCHILL SEMPLE** in *Influences of Geographic Environment* (1911), translating and amplifying Friedrich Ratzel's ideas in the United States.
