### Matrix B: Critical Diagnostic Geometric Contrasts

| Contrast Group | Core Geometric Difference | Diagnostic Physical Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Barchan vs. Parabolic Dune** | Barchan horns point **DOWNWIND**; Parabolic horns point **UPWIND**. | Barchans are free mobile dunes in dry sand; Parabolics are anchored by coastal/semi-arid vegetation. |
| **Stalactite vs. Stalagmite** | Stala**C**tite grows from **C**eiling down; Stala**G**mite grows from **G**round up. | Stalactites have a central hollow feeding capillary tube; Stalagmites are solid with blunt tops. |
| **Roche Moutonnée vs. Drumlin** | Roche Moutonnée has gentle stoss side up-glacier and steep plucked side down-glacier. | Drumlin has steep blunt end up-glacier and tapered tail down-glacier (exact reverse). |
| **Yardang vs. Zeugen** | Yardang develops in **vertically tilted** rock strata parallel to wind. | Zeugen develops in **horizontally stratified** rock beds with resistant caprock. |

## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

1. [ ] **Barchan Horns**: Point DOWNWIND in the direction of the wind, NOT upwind.
2. [ ] **Stalactite vs. Stalagmite**: Stalactites hang from the ceiling; stalagmites grow from the floor.
3. [ ] **Esker Sorting**: Eskers are composed of sorted, stratified sand and gravel deposited by subglacial streams, NOT unsorted till.
4. [ ] **Drumlin Slope Orientation**: Steep blunt end faces up-glacier; tapered tail faces down-glacier (opposite of Roche Moutonnée).
5. [ ] **Tombolo Function**: A sand bar/spit connecting an island to the mainland, NOT a deep marine trench.
6. [ ] **Mississippi Delta Form**: Bird's-foot delta with finger distributaries, NOT an arcuate delta.
7. [ ] **Mushroom Rock Undercutting**: Caused by aeolian sandblasting abrasion in the lowest 1–2 m, NOT groundwater solution.
8. [ ] **Yardangs vs. Zeugen**: Yardangs occur in vertically tilted strata; Zeugen occur in horizontally stratified beds.
9. [ ] **Kettle Lake vs. Tarn**: A Tarn is in a cirque basin; a Kettle lake is formed by melting buried ice blocks in an outwash plain.
10. [ ] **Polje Scale**: The largest karst depression on Earth (hundreds of km²), NOT a small solution sinkhole.
