9. [ ] **Indus Suture Zone (ITSN)**: Demarcates the collision between Indian and Eurasian plates, NOT the Main Boundary Thrust (MBT).
10. [ ] **Red Sea Stage**: Juvenile stage of an expanding new ocean basin, NOT a closing sea.
