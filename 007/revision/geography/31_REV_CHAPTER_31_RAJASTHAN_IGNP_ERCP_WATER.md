* **The Three Water Types**:
  1. **Palar Pani**: Direct sweet rainwater collected from clean catchments.
  2. **Patal Pani**: Deep, mineralized groundwater from deep wells.
  3. **Rejwani Pani**: Perched capillary moisture trapped in intermediate sand layers above gypsum beds.

## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Irrigation Along the Rajasthan Feeder**: **NO IRRIGATION IS PERMITTED along the Rajasthan Feeder ($204\text{ km}$)**. The feeder canal is exclusively a carrier channel designed to transport water from Harike Barrage to Masitawali Head (Hanumangarh), where the Main Canal begins.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Rawatsar Branch Position**: Out of the 9 flow branches of the IGNP, **RAWATSAR BRANCH is the ONLY branch that takes off from the LEFT BANK**. All other 8 flow branches (Suratgarh, Anupgarh, Pugal, Dattor, Birsalpur, Charanwala, Birbal, Sagarmal Gopa) take off from the Right Bank.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Mandated Sprinkler Irrigation**: The **Narmada Canal Project** is the **ONLY irrigation project in India where micro-irrigation (drip / sprinkler) is legally mandatory**. Flood irrigation is prohibited by statute.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Inventors of the Khadin System**: The **Khadin** runoff agriculture system was invented in the **15th century by the PALIWAL BRAHMINS of Jaisalmer**, NOT by modern engineers or state kings.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Palar Pani vs. Rejwani Pani**: **Palar Pani** is pure, direct *rainwater* collected from catchments or rooftops. **Rejwani Pani** is *subsurface capillary moisture* trapped in sand layers above an impermeable gypsum bed, harvested through narrow deep **Kuis (Beris)**.
- [ ] **Trap 6: Warabandi Rotational Scheduling Invariant**: Warabandi is a **strict 168-hour (7 days $\times$ 24 hours)** rotational rationing schedule where an individual cultivator's time slot ($T_i$) is mathematically proportional to their landholding area ($A_i / A_{cca} \times T_{net}$). It is **NOT a demand-responsive or volumetric metering system**; farmers cannot "call for water on demand" when crops wilt.
- [ ] **Trap 7: Participatory Irrigation Management (PIM) Statutory Mandate**: Below canal outlets (chak level), tertiary field distribution and on-farm water management are statutorily transferred to **Water Users Associations (WUAs)** under the **Rajasthan Farmers' Participation in Management of Irrigation Systems (RPIMIS) Act, 2000**, supported by Command Area Development Authorities (CADAs at Bikaner and Kota), NOT operated directly by central or state irrigation engineers.
