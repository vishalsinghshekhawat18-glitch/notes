## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Most Widespread Forest Type in India**: The most widespread forest type in India is the **Tropical Moist Deciduous Forest (~37%)**, NOT Tropical Wet Evergreen or Tropical Dry Deciduous. The two deciduous groups combined account for nearly **$65\%$** of India's forest cover.
- [ ] **Trap 2: State with Highest Forest Percentage**: **Mizoram** has the highest forest cover as a percentage of total land area (**$84.53\%$**) among Indian states. **Madhya Pradesh** has the largest forest cover by **absolute area** (~$77,493\text{ km}^2$), but its percentage is only ~25%.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Junction of North-South and East-West Corridors**: The North-South Corridor (Srinagar to Kanyakumari) and the East-West Corridor (Silchar to Porbandar) intersect at **JHANSI in Uttar Pradesh**, NOT at Nagpur or Delhi.
- [ ] **Trap 4: First Corporatized Major Port**: **Kamarajar Port (Ennore)** in Tamil Nadu is the **only major port in India registered as a corporate company** under the Companies Act; all other 12 major ports are governed as port authorities under the Major Port Authorities Act, 2021.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Sole Riverine Major Port in India**: **Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port (Kolkata-Haldia)** is the **only riverine major port in India**, situated on the freshwater tidal estuary of the **Hooghly River**, approximately $203\text{ km}$ inland from the sea.
- [ ] **Trap 6: Soil Health Card Excluded Nutrients**: Calcium ($Ca$) and Magnesium ($Mg$) are **NOT among the 12 statutory parameters** tested under the Soil Health Card scheme! The card tests 3 primary macro ($N, P, K$), 1 secondary ($S$), 5 micro ($Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B$), and 3 physical parameters ($pH, EC, OC$).
- [ ] **Trap 7: Micro-Watershed Statutory Threshold**: A **Micro-watershed has a geographic area of 100 to 1,000 Hectares**, NOT 1,000 to 10,000 hectares (which is a *Mini-watershed*).
- [ ] **Trap 8: Dominant Engine of Indian Fish Production**: Marine capture fisheries do **NOT produce the majority of India's fish output**. **Inland Fisheries & Aquaculture account for over $75\%$** of total production, while Marine capture represents barely $\sim 25\%$.
