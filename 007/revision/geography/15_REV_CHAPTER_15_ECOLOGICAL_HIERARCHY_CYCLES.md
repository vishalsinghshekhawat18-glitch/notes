## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
Hierarchy: Individual ➔ Population (same species) ➔ Community (different species) ➔ Ecosystem (biotic + abiotic) ➔ Biome ➔ Biosphere 
➔ Ecotone: Junction zone between two ecosystems (Mangroves, Estuaries, Grasslands); high biodiversity via EDGE EFFECT 
➔ Niche: Elton/Hutchinson profession/functional role; Gause Competitive Exclusion: Complete competitors cannot coexist 
➔ Energy Flow: Unidirectional, Lindeman 10% law (90% lost as metabolic heat, capped at 4–5 trophic levels) 
➔ Food Chains: Grazing (GFC: dominant in aquatic) vs. Detritus (DFC: dead matter, dominant in terrestrial forests, 80–90% NPP) 
➔ Pyramids: Energy is ALWAYS UPRIGHT (2nd law thermodynamics) ➔ Biomass is INVERTED IN OCEANS (phytoplankton turnover) 
➔ Numbers is INVERTED in single tree with parasites ➔ Toxins: Bioconcentration (water only) ➔ Bioaccumulation (in 1 organism over age) 
➔ Biomagnification (multiplies up trophic chain, DDT eggshell thinning) ➔ Cycles: Gaseous (C, N; atmosphere reservoir) vs Sedimentary (P, S; lithosphere) 
➔ Nitrogen: Fixation (Rhizobium, Azotobacter) ➔ Nitrification (Nitrosomonas to NO2⁻ ➔ Nitrobacter to NO3⁻) ➔ Denitrification (Pseudomonas, anaerobic) 
➔ Phosphorus: NO gaseous phase; rocks weathering to PO4³⁻, guano return, causes eutrophication 
➔ Succession: Primary (sterile rock) vs Secondary (soil present) ➔ Lichen ➔ Moss ➔ Herbs ➔ Shrubs ➔ Climax Forest
```

## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Aquatic Biomass Pyramid**: The Pyramid of Biomass in an aquatic open ocean is **INVERTED**, NOT upright. The standing biomass of phytoplankton at any single instant is tiny compared to long-lived whales and fishes, but phytoplankton turn over and reproduce in hours.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Pyramid of Energy Exception**: There are **ZERO EXCEPTIONS** to the Pyramid of Energy. It is **ALWAYS UPRIGHT** in every ecosystem on Earth due to the Second Law of Thermodynamics (entropy and heat dissipation).
- [ ] **Trap 3: Nitrosomonas vs. Nitrobacter**: *Nitrosomonas* converts **Ammonia to Nitrite ($NO_2^-)$**; *Nitrobacter* converts **Nitrite to Nitrate ($NO_3^-)$**. They are NOT interchangeable.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Denitrification Soil Conditions**: Denitrification occurs exclusively under **anaerobic (waterlogged/oxygen-deficient)** conditions. Aerating the soil suppresses denitrification and preserves plant-available nitrate.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Phosphorus Atmospheric Phase**: The Phosphorus cycle has **NO gaseous atmospheric component**. It cannot evaporate into the atmosphere under normal conditions, unlike carbon, nitrogen, and sulfur.
