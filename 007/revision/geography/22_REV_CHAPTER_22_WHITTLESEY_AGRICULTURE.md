## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Yield Per Hectare vs. Yield Per Worker**: **Intensive Subsistence Farming** (Monsoon Asia wet paddy) has **VERY HIGH yield per hectare**, but **LOW yield per worker** (due to immense population pressure). Conversely, **Commercial Grain Farming** (US Prairies) has **LOW yield per hectare**, but the **HIGHEST yield per worker** on Earth (due to massive mechanization).
- [ ] **Trap 2: Von Thünen Ring 2 Anomaly**: In modern times, firewood is not located close to cities, but in Von Thünen's original 1826 model, **Timber and Firewood occupied Zone 2** (immediately surrounding market gardening) because wood was the sole building material and fuel source, and was too heavy and bulky to haul long distances by horse cart.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Robusta vs. Arabica Coffee Leaders**: **Brazil** is the world leader in overall coffee and **Arabica** production. **Vietnam** is the world's undisputed leader in **Robusta** coffee production (Central Highlands).
- [ ] **Trap 4: Spring vs. Winter Wheat Planting**: **Spring Wheat** is planted in **Spring** and harvested in **Autumn** (in regions like North Dakota and Canadian Prairies where severe sub-zero winter temperatures freeze the soil). **Winter Wheat** is planted in **Autumn**, survives under winter snow cover, and is harvested in **early Summer** (Kansas, Punjab).
- [ ] **Trap 5: Black Tea World Exporter**: China is the world's largest **producer** of tea (dominated by green tea), but **Kenya** is the world's largest **exporter** of CTC black tea.
