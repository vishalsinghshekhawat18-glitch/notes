## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: True Crustal Boundary**: The true boundary between continental crust (granitic Sial) and oceanic crust (basaltic Sima) is the **Continental Slope**, NOT the continental shelf or shoreline.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Continental Shelf Width**: Continental shelves are NOT uniform in width. They are widest on **passive margins** (e.g., Siberian Shelf ~1,500 km) and virtually absent or paper-thin on **active convergent margins** backed by mountains (e.g., Peru-Chile trench margin).
- [ ] **Trap 3: Guyot Flattening Mechanism**: Guyots were NOT formed flat underwater. Their summits were eroded flat by **wave action at sea level** when they existed as subaerial volcanic islands, before subsequent seafloor spreading and thermal subsidence submerged them.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Submarine Canyon Excavation**: Submarine canyons were primarily carved by **turbidity currents** (dense underwater sediment-slurry flows), though some upper heads were initiated by subaerial rivers during Pleistocene sea-level lowstands.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Deepest Trench in Indian Ocean**: The deepest point in the Indian Ocean is the **Java (Sunda) Trench** ($7,450\text{ m}$), NOT the Carlsberg Ridge or Chagos Trench.
