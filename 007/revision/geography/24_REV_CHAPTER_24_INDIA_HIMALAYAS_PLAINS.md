## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: North-to-South Thrust Boundary Order**: From north to south, the master boundaries are: **ITSZ $\to$ MCT $\to$ MBT $\to$ HFF**. The MCT separates Greater and Lesser Himalayas; the MBT separates Lesser Himalayas and Shiwaliks; the HFF separates Shiwaliks and the Great Plains.
- [ ] **Trap 2: Stream Behavior in Bhabar vs. Terai**: Streams do **NOT** disappear in the Terai; streams **disappear underground in the porous gravels of the BHABAR**, and then **re-emerge at the surface in the TERAI** to create a wet, marshy zone.
- [ ] **Trap 3: Karewa Formations & Saffron**: **Karewas** are lacustrine (lake) sedimentary deposits found specifically in the **Kashmir Valley** (between Greater Himalayas and Pir Panjal), renowned for the cultivation of **Zafran (Saffron)**.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Bhangar vs. Khadar Flood Reach**: **Bhangar** is the *older alluvium* that lies **above the reach of annual floods** and contains calcareous lime nodules called *Kankar*. **Khadar** is the *newer alluvium* in the floodplain that is **inundated by floods every year**.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Location of K2 (Godwin Austen)**: **K2 ($8,611\text{ m}$)** is situated in the **Karakoram Range of the Trans-Himalayas**, NOT in the Greater Himalayas / Himadri.
