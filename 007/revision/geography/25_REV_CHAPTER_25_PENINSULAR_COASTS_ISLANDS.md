## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Highest Peak in South India**: The highest peak in South India / Peninsular India is **ANAMUDI ($2,695\text{ m}$)** in the Anamalai Hills of Kerala, NOT Dodabetta ($2,637\text{ m}$ in Nilgiris).
- [ ] **Trap 2: Geological Affiliation of Meghalaya Plateau**: The **Meghalaya (Shillong) Plateau is part of the PENINSULAR SHIELD**, NOT the Himalayas. It was separated from the Chota Nagpur Plateau by the **Garo-Rajmahal (Malda) Fault Gap**.
- [ ] **Trap 3: 8° vs. 9° vs. 10° Channels**:
  * **$8^\circ\text{ Channel}$**: Separates **Minicoy (India)** from **Maldives**.
  * **$9^\circ\text{ Channel}$**: Separates **Minicoy** from **main Lakshadweep**.
  * **$10^\circ\text{ Channel}$**: Separates **Andaman (Little Andaman)** from **Nicobar (Car Nicobar)**.
- [ ] **Trap 4: Coast of Submergence vs. Emergence**: The **Western Coastal Plain is a Coast of Submergence** (narrow, rocky, natural deep harbors, estuaries). The **Eastern Coastal Plain is a Coast of Emergence** (wide, shallow continental shelf, large prograding deltas).
- [ ] **Trap 5: Satpura Block Mountain Boundaries**: The Satpura Range is a true **Horst (Block Mountain)** bounded by two tectonic rift valleys: the **Narmada River to the NORTH** and the **Tapi River to the SOUTH**. Both rivers flow west through fault grabens, NOT normal erosional valleys.
