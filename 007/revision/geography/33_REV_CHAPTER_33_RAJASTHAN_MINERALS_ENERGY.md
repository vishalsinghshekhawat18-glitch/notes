## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

- [ ] **Trap 1: Degana Tungsten Monopoly**: **Degana (Revat Hill in Nagaur district)** is the **ONLY commercial tungsten mine in India**. It produces wolframite, a strategic defense metal with the highest melting point of all metals ($3,422^\circ\text{C}$).
- [ ] **Trap 2: World's Largest Zinc Smelter vs. Mine**:
  * **World's Richest Zinc Mine**: **Rampura Agucha** in Bhilwara district.
  * **World's Largest Zinc Smelting Complex**: **Chanderiya Smelter** in Chittorgarh district (operated by Hindustan Zinc Ltd).
- [ ] **Trap 3: HRRL Equity Shareholding**: The equity partnership in the Pachpadra Petroleum Refinery is **$74\%$ HPCL and $26\%$ Government of Rajasthan (GoR)**, NOT a 50:50 joint venture.
- [ ] **Trap 4: World's Largest Operational Solar Park**: **Bhadla Solar Park ($2,245\text{ MW}$)** located in **Phalodi district** (formerly part of Jodhpur) is the **world's largest operational solar park by installed capacity**, surpassing Pavagada Solar Park in Karnataka.
- [ ] **Trap 5: Makrana Marble Rock Type**: Makrana marble is a **calcitic metamorphic marble** ($90\text{–}98\%\text{ }CaCO_3$) derived from ancient limestone; green marble from Rishabhdeo is chemically a **serpentinite rock**, NOT true marble.
