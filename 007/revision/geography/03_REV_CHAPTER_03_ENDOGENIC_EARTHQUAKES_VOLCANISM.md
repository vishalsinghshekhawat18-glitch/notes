## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Epeirogenic vs. Orogenic Movements

| Diagnostic Dimension | Epeirogenic (Continent-Building) | Orogenic (Mountain-Building) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direction of Force** | **Vertical / Radial** (Uplift away from center, Subsidence toward center) | **Horizontal / Tangential** (Compression / Tension) |
| **Crustal Structural Deformation** | Gentle regional warping; strata remain near-horizontal | Intense folding, reverse thrusting, metamorphism, nappes |
| **Geomorphic Manifestation** | Raised beaches, marine terraces, submerged coasts (Dwarka) | Fold mountain systems (Himalayas, Alps), Rift valleys (Narmada) |

---

### Matrix B: Fault Types & Stress Mechanics

| Fault Type | Governing Stress | Movement of Hanging Wall | Crustal Length Effect | Real-World Benchmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Normal Fault** | Tension (Extensional) | Moves **Downward** relative to Footwall | Lengthens the crust | Basin & Range (USA), East African Rift |
| **Reverse / Thrust** | Compression | Moves **Upward** relative to Footwall | Shortens the crust | Main Central Thrust (MCT, Himalayas) |
| **Strike-Slip** | Shear (Lateral friction) | Moves **Horizontally** past Footwall | Length conserved | San Andreas Fault (Right-lateral / Dextral) |

### Matrix C: Intrusive Igneous Landforms

| Intrusive Pluton | Geometry & Structural Alignment | Distinguishing Diagnostic Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Batholith** | Discordant, bottomless irregular core ($>100\text{ km²}$) | Largest pluton; exposed in cores of eroded mountain belts (Ranchi granite) |
| **Laccolith** | Concordant, mushroom/dome shape with **flat base** | Arches overlying sedimentary strata into a blister dome (Henry Mts, Utah) |
| **Lopolith** | Concordant, **saucer/bowl-shaped** basin | Sagging central floor due to weight of dense magma (Bushveld, SA) |
| **Phacolith** | Concordant, **lens-shaped** intrusion | Confined to the crest of an anticline or trough of a syncline |
| **Sill** | Concordant, **horizontal tabular sheet** | Injected **parallel** to sedimentary bedding planes (Great Whin Sill) |
| **Dyke (Dike)** | Discordant, **vertical wall-like sheet** | **Cuts across** sedimentary bedding planes (Deccan dolerite dykes) |

## 3. Instant Killer Traps Checklist

1. [ ] **Caldera Origin**: Formed by gravitational collapse into an emptied magma chamber, NOT by an explosive blast blowing off the summit.
2. [ ] **Focus vs. Epicenter**: Seismic waves originate at the subterranean Focus (Hypocenter), NOT at the surface Epicenter.
3. [ ] **Narmada Flow Direction**: Flows westward because it is confined in a faulted structural Rift Valley (Graben), NOT because the plateau slopes west.
4. [ ] **Richter Energy Scaling**: Magnitude 8 releases $\sim 1,000,000$ times more energy than Magnitude 4, NOT double ($31.6^4$).
5. [ ] **Deep Ocean Tsunami**: Ships in deep water sail over a tsunami imperceptibly ($<1\text{ m}$ height); dangerous height develops only at the coast via Shoaling.
6. [ ] **India's Active Volcano**: Barren Island is the ONLY active volcano in South Asia; Narcondam is dormant/extinct.
7. [ ] **Sill vs. Dyke**: A Sill is horizontal and concordant; a Dyke is vertical and discordant.
8. [ ] **Shield Volcano Slope**: Broad and extremely gentle ($2^\circ\text{ to }10^\circ$) due to fluid basalt, NOT steep.
9. [ ] **BIS Seismic Zones**: India has NO Seismic Zone I; BIS maintains only Zones II, III, IV, and V.
10. [ ] **Tsunamis & Strike-Slip Faults**: Strike-slip faults do NOT generate large tsunamis because they involve horizontal shear without vertical water displacement.
