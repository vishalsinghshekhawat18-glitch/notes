## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Core Computational Formulations & Pie Angles

| Operation | Mathematical Identity | Critical Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Percentage Change ($\Delta\%$)** | $\frac{\text{Final} - \text{Initial}}{\text{Initial Base}} \times 100\%$ | Reference baseline follows preposition *"over"* or *"compared to"*. |
| **Percentage Point vs \% Change** | $P_2 - P_1$ vs $\frac{P_2 - P_1}{P_1} \times 100\%$ | $4\% \to 5\% = 1 \text{ percentage point}$ increase, but **$25\%$ relative change**! |
| **Pie Angle Conversion** | $\theta = \% \times 3.6^\circ$ | $100\% = 360^\circ \iff 1\% = 3.6^\circ \iff 1^\circ = \frac{5}{18}\%$. |
| **Dual Pie Chart Rule** | $\text{Absolute} = \% \times \text{Base}$ | **Never compare percentages directly** across two charts with unequal totals! |
| **Line Graph Slope** | $\text{Slope} = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta t}$ | Steeper slope $\implies$ faster velocity of growth. |

### Matrix B: 3-Set Venn Diagram Algebra

| Venn Segment | Mathematical Formulation | Operating Precaution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Union $n(A \cup B \cup C)$** | $\sum n(A) - \sum n(A \cap B) + n(A \cap B \cap C)$ | Standard inclusion-exclusion. |
| **Exactly One Subject Only** | $\sum n(A) - 2\sum n(A \cap B) + 3n(A \cap B \cap C)$ | Excludes all dual and triple overlaps. |
| **Exactly Two Subjects Only** | $\sum n(A \cap B) - 3n(A \cap B \cap C)$ | Removes central intersection triple-count. |
| **At Least Two Subjects** | $\sum n(A \cap B) - 2n(A \cap B \cap C)$ | Exactly two $+$ All three. |

## 3. Top 5 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Dual Pie Naked Percentage Comparison**: Declaring a $30\%$ slice in Year 1 larger than a $25\%$ slice in Year 2 without weighting by total expenditure.
2. **Percentage Point vs Percentage Change**: Stating unemployment changed by $2\%$ when it rose from $6\%$ to $8\%$. It changed by $2$ percentage points, but rose by $\mathbf{33.33\%}$!
3. **Reference Base Inversion**: Dividing by the later year when asked for percentage growth *"over 2020"*. The base is ALWAYS the initial year (2020).
4. **Venn "Only" Category Neglect**: Treating $A \cap B$ as "A and B only". $A \cap B$ contains elements shared by $C$ as well; subtract $A \cap B \cap C$ to isolate "only".
5. **Scale and Multiplier Misread**: Overlooking graph axes labeled *"in Thousands"* or *"in Millions"*, causing a $10^3$ or $10^6$ decimal catastrophe.
