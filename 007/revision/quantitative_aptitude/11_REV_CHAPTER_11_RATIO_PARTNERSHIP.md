## 3. Top 5 Instant Killer Traps

1. **The 3-Term Inverse Ratio Fallacy**: Stating that the inverse of $2 : 3 : 4$ is $4 : 3 : 2$. Correct is $\frac{1}{2} : \frac{1}{3} : \frac{1}{4} = \mathbf{6 : 4 : 3}$.
2. **Work Term Position in Chain Rule**: Putting work $W$ in the numerator. It must always be in the **denominator**!
3. **Coin Count vs Rupee Value**: Multiplying coin counts directly to find total money without weighting by denomination face values ($50\text{p} = ₹0.50$).
4. **Active Partner Salary Deducted Too Late**: Subtracting the working partner's salary from their share instead of the gross total pool.
5. **Componendo-Dividendo Sign Slip**: Writing $\frac{a+b}{a-b} = \frac{c-d}{c+d}$. Both sides must maintain the identical numerator-denominator operation structure.
