## 3. Top 5 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Cycle Residual Rate Trap**: Dividing the leftover work by the average cycle rate instead of dividing by the individual daily rate of the worker whose turn it is.
2. **Work Position in Chain Rule**: Inverting $W$ into the numerator ($M \cdot D \cdot H \cdot W$). Output (roads, books, ditches) is the accumulated consequence and belongs in the denominator.
3. **Three-Term Efficiency Inversion**: Writing inverse of $2 : 3 : 5$ as $5 : 3 : 2$. The correct inverse ratio is $\mathbf{15 : 10 : 6}$.
4. **Phantom Work Sign Error**: Subtracting instead of adding when a worker leaves *before completion*. Add phantom units so all workers appear to work until the finish line.
5. **Wage Allocation Blindness**: Splitting earnings by time spent when efficiencies differ, or by efficiencies when durations differ. True metric is $\mathbf{\eta \times T}$.
