| Question Type | Sufficiency Criterion | Fatal Non-Sufficient Condition |

## 3. Top 5 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Information Leakage / Carryover**: Carrying facts or deductions from Statement (1) into the evaluation of Statement (2). Reset your mind completely before testing (2)!
2. **The "Need to Calculate" Compulsion**: Spending valuable minutes performing long arithmetic when you have already confirmed the system has a unique solution.
3. **The "Definitive NO" Misunderstanding**: Believing that a statement proving $x$ is NOT greater than $0$ is insufficient. A definitive "No" is **fully sufficient**!
4. **Quadratic Sign Blindness**: Assuming $y^2 = 16 \implies y = 4$. Unless stated as positive, $y = \pm 4$ gives two distinct answers, making it **insufficient**.
5. **Dependent System Illusion**: Counting two equations as sufficient without checking if one is a multiple of the other ($2x + 5y = 10$ and $4x + 10y = 20$).
