### Matrix B: Net Effective Percentage Formula Summary

| Operation Pattern | Formula | Critical Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Two Successive Increases** | $+a + b + \frac{ab}{100}$ | Multiplier $(1 + a/100)(1 + b/100)$. |
| **Increase followed by Decrease** | $+a - b - \frac{ab}{100}$ | Sign of product term is negative. |
| **Two Successive Discounts** | $a + b - \frac{ab}{100}$ | Always strictly less than simple sum $(a + b)$. |
| **Equal Increase & Decrease ($\pm x$)** | $-\left(\frac{x}{10}\right)^2\%$ | **Always a net decrease!** E.g. $\pm 20\% \implies -4\%$. |

## 2. 60-Second Retrieval Skeleton

```text
Percentage = (Part / Base) × 100 ➔ Identify the denominator baseline first!
➔ A is R% more than B ➔ B is less than A by: [R / (100 + R)] × 100%
➔ Fraction Shift: Increase of +a/b ➔ Decrease of -a/(b + a)
➔ Constant Expenditure: Price × Consumption = Constant ➔ Price (+1/4) ➔ Consumption (-1/5 = -20%)
➔ Successive Changes: Net % = a + b + (ab / 100)
➔ Equal ±x% change: Net change is ALWAYS a loss of -(x / 10)² %
➔ Population Compound Growth: P_future = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ ➔ P_past = P / (1 + R/100)ⁿ
```

## 3. Top 5 Instant Killer Traps

1. **The Equal Increase-Decrease Zero Trap**: Believing that increasing a price by $10\%$ and then decreasing it by $10\%$ restores the original price. It leaves a **$1\%$ loss**!
2. **The "Of" vs "More Than" Base Mistake**: $A$ is $125\%$ of $B$ means $A = 1.25 B$ ($25\%$ more). $A$ is $125\%$ more than $B$ means $A = 2.25 B$ ($225\%$ of $B$)!
3. **Invalid Votes Subtraction Order**: When candidate gets $54\%$ of valid votes, you must first subtract invalid votes from total votes before calculating the $54\%$ share.
4. **Pass/Fail Threshold Span**: When one student is below pass marks by $-x$ and another is above by $+y$, the mark span is **$x + y$**, NOT $y - x$.
5. **Dimensional Area % Multiplier**: If radius of a circle increases by $20\%$, area increases by $20 + 20 + \frac{20 \times 20}{100} = \mathbf{44\%}$ (NOT $20\%$, because area scales as $r^2$!).
