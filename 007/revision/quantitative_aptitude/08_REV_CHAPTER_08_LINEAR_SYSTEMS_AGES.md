## 1. Core Distinction Matrices

### Matrix A: Linear System Consistency Criteria ($a_1x + b_1y + c_1 = 0$)

| System Type | Ratio Test | Geometric State | Solutions Count |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unique Solution** | $\frac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \frac{b_1}{b_2}$ | Lines intersect at single point | Exactly 1 |
| **Infinite Solutions** | $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{c_1}{c_2}$ | Lines are coincident (identical) | Infinitely Many |
| **No Solution** | $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \frac{c_1}{c_2}$ | Lines are strictly parallel | Zero |

## 3. Top 5 Instant Killer Traps

1. **Parallel vs Infinite Confusion**: For $a_1/a_2 = b_1/b_2$, candidates often forget to check $c_1/c_2$. If the constant ratio also matches, it is NOT parallel—it has infinitely many solutions!
2. **Age Gap Chronology Inversion**: Assuming the age difference changes as years pass. If a father was 24 years older than his son 10 years ago, he is **still exactly 24 years older today**!
3. **Tens Digit Coefficient Omission**: Writing a two-digit number as $t + u$ instead of $10t + u$. $t$ represents tens, so its positional weight is 10.
4. **"Hence" vs "Ago"**: "Hence" means future ($+T$). "Ago" means past ($-T$).
5. **Diophantine Negative Step Oversight**: In $ax + by = c$, as $x$ increases by $b$, $y$ must **decrease by $a$** to maintain the balance.
