## 3. Top 5 Instant Killer Traps

1. **The "Only" Region Confusion in Venn Caselets**: Stating $n(A \cap B) = 15$ means "only A and B is 15". The "only" region is $n(A \cap B) - n(A \cap B \cap C)$.
2. **Footnote Metric Blindness**: Ignoring small footnote: *"Data in thousands"* or *"Values represent % change from previous year"*. Results in answers off by order of magnitude.
3. **Pie Chart Angle vs Percentage Confusion**: Converting $54^\circ$ by multiplying by $100/360 \implies 15\%$, but forgetting that the base for Year 1 is different from Year 2.
4. **Solving Questions Individually from Scratch**: Re-calculating the master table for each sub-question instead of spending 2 minutes upfront to build a single master reference matrix.
5. **Arithmetic DI Formula Amputation**: Trying to solve a Time & Work DI set purely by visual trend estimation without calculating individual worker efficiencies.
