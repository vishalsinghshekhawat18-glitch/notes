## 3. Top 5 Instant Killer Traps

1. **The "Of" vs Multiplication Precedence**: $24 \div 6 \text{ of } 2 = 24 \div 12 = 2$. Writing $24 \div 6 \times 2 = 8$ is incorrect. "Of" binds before division!
2. **Left-to-Right Division Chaining**: $64 \div 8 \div 4 \neq 64 \div (8 \div 4) = 32$. Correct is $(64 / 8) / 4 = 8 / 4 = 2$.
3. **The Vinculum Negative Sign Absorption**: $- \overline{5 - 3} = -(2) = -2$, whereas $-5 - 3 = -8$. The bar functions as parentheses!
4. **Denominator Approximation Direction**: If you round $D$ up ($78 \to 80$), the fraction decreases. If you round $D$ down ($82 \to 80$), the fraction increases.
5. **Decimal Fraction Multiplication Zero Deficit**: $0.05 \times 0.04 = 0.0020 = 0.002$ (4 decimal places). Do not drop zeros before establishing the full decimal offset!
