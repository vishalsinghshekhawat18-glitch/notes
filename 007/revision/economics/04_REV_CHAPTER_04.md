## 2. Multiplier & Fisher Equations

$$\mathbf{\text{Money Multiplier } (m)} = \frac{M_3}{M_0} = \frac{1 + c}{c + r}$$
* $c = \frac{C}{D}$ (Currency-Deposit Ratio): Higher digital adoption (UPI) $\implies c \downarrow \implies m \uparrow$.
* $r = \frac{R}{D}$ (Reserve-Deposit Ratio): RBI hikes CRR $\implies r \uparrow \implies m \downarrow$.

$$\mathbf{\text{Fisher Equation of Exchange}}: \quad M \times V = P \times Y \quad (\text{Money} \times \text{Velocity} = \text{Price} \times \text{Real GDP} = \text{Nominal GDP})$$

## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)
1. **The Vault Cash Fallacy**: Commercial bank vault cash is **EXCLUDED** from Money Supply ($M_1/M_3$). Only money held by the **public** counts.
2. **ATM Withdrawal**: Withdrawing ₹10,000 cash from an ATM has **ZERO immediate impact on $M_1$ and $M_3$** (cash rises by ₹10,000; demand deposits fall by ₹10,000).
3. **Cheques are NOT Legal Tender**: A merchant or creditor can legally refuse a cheque; they cannot refuse RBI banknotes once a legal debt is created.
4. **Inter-Bank Deposits**: Deposits between commercial banks are excluded to prevent double-counting.
