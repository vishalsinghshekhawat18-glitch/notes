### Matrix B: The Pre-IBC vs. IBC Structural Paradigm

| Feature | Pre-IBC Regimes (DRT, SARFAESI, CDR) | Modern IBC, 2016 Regime |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Underlying Philosophy** | **"Debtor-in-Possession"** (Promoter retained control while company was bled dry) | **"Creditor-in-Control"** (Promoter ousted immediately; independent IRP takes over) |
| **Statutory Time Limit** | Indefinite; cases dragged for 5–10 years | **Mandatory 330-Day Outer Limit** (including all litigation; else liquidation) |
| **Who Governs Resolution** | Civil courts and bank debt-recovery forums | **Committee of Creditors (CoC)** consisting of **Financial Creditors only** |
| **Voting Threshold** | Unanimity or varying thresholds | **Strict 66% majority vote of the CoC** |
| **Promoter Re-bidding** | Promoters bought back debt at deep discounts | **Section 29A strictly BARS defaulting promoters** from bidding |
| **Default Threshold** | ₹20 Lakh (DRT) | **₹1 Crore** (raised in 2020 to shield MSMEs) |

## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)
1. **The Agricultural NPA Trap**: Agricultural crop loans do NOT follow the 90-day rule; they require **2 crop seasons overdue** for short crops and **1 season** for long crops.
2. **Operational Creditors in CoC**: Operational creditors (suppliers, vendors) have **ZERO voting rights** in the CoC; only Financial Creditors vote.
3. **Agricultural Land under SARFAESI**: Banks are statutorily barred from seizing or auctioning agricultural land under the SARFAESI Act, 2002.
4. **Provisioning Coverage Ratio (PCR)**: A healthy bank target is **$\text{PCR} \ge 70.0\%$**. Net NPA is calculated by subtracting provisions from Gross NPA.
