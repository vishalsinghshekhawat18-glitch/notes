## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **MSP Crop Count**: The CACP recommends pricing for **23 crops in total** (22 crops under MSP $+$ 1 crop, Sugarcane, under FRP). Coconut is derived from copra.
2. **CACP Advisory Role**: CACP **does NOT fix or notify MSPs**. CACP is merely an advisory body; the **Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA)** chaired by the PM takes the final binding decision.
3. **Government MSP Formula**: The Government's 50% profit margin is applied to **$\mathbf{(A2 + FL)}$, NOT to $C2$**. Candidates frequently assume Swaminathan's $C2 + 50\%$ formula was implemented.
4. **FRP Legal Enforceability**: Sugarcane **FRP is legally enforceable** under the Essential Commodities Act; sugar mills must pay it within 14 days. General MSP has **no statutory backing** (private buyers cannot be prosecuted for purchasing below MSP).
5. **Marginal vs. Small Landholdings**: Marginal is **$< 1$ hectare**; Small is **$1 \text{ to } 2$ hectares**. Together they make up **86.2% of Indian farm holdings**.
