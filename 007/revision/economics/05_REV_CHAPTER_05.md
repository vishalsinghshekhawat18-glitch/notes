## 1. Master Comparative Matrices

### Matrix A: The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) Statutory Anatomy

| Parameter | Statutory Provision & Architecture |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Statutory Charter** | Section 45ZB of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (inserted via Finance Act, 2016) |
| **Total Membership** | **6 Members** (3 Internal RBI officials + 3 External independent experts) |
| **Internal Members** | 1. Governor, RBI (Ex-officio Chairperson)<br/>2. Deputy Governor in charge of Monetary Policy (Ex-officio)<br/>3. One officer of the Bank nominated by Central Board |
| **External Members** | Appointed by Central Government on recommendation of Search-cum-Selection Committee (Cabinet Secretary, RBI Governor, DEA Secretary, 3 experts) |
| **Tenure of Externals** | **4 Years** from date of entering office; **STRICTLY NOT ELIGIBLE FOR REAPPOINTMENT** (§45ZC) |
| **Quorum Required** | **4 Members**, at least one of whom must be Governor or Deputy Governor (§45ZI) |
| **Voting Mechanism** | **One Member, One Vote**. Majority rules. In case of 3–3 tie, Governor has **Casting Vote** (§45ZL) |
| **Meeting Frequency** | Statutorily at least **4 times a year** (conventionally bi-monthly, 6 times a year) |
| **Transparency Standard** | Full minutes, voting record & individual statements published on **14th day** after meeting |

### Matrix B: The Flexible Inflation Targeting (FIT) Rules

| Parameter | Statutory Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Targeting Authority** | Central Government in consultation with RBI (§45ZA) once every 5 years |
| **Nominal Target** | **4.0% Headline Consumer Price Index (CPI-Combined)** |
| **Tolerance Band** | $\pm 2.0\%$ (Lower Limit: **2.0%**; Upper Limit: **6.0%**) |
| **Statutory Definition of Failure** | Average headline CPI inflation is **$>6.0\%$ OR $<2.0\%$ for THREE CONSECUTIVE QUARTERS** (§45ZN) |
| **Consequence of Failure** | RBI submits confidential report to Govt: 1. Causes, 2. Remedial actions, 3. Expected timeline |
| **First Historical Failure** | Triggered in **Q1–Q3 2022** (Russia-Ukraine war commodity shock); report submitted 3 Nov 2022 |

## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)
1. **The WPI Fallacy**: MPC targets **CPI-Combined**, NEVER WPI.
2. **Re-appointment Trap**: External MPC members **CANNOT be re-appointed**.
3. **Failure Timeline Trap**: A monthly breach is NOT failure. Average inflation must breach the 2%–6% band for **3 consecutive quarters**.
4. **Revaluation Gain Dividend**: Unrealized gains in the Currency and Gold Revaluation Account (CGRA) **cannot** be transferred to the Government.
