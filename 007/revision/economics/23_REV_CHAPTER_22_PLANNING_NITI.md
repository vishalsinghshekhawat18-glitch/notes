## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **Statutory Status of NITI Aayog**: NITI Aayog is **NEITHER constitutional NOR statutory**. It was created via a **Union Cabinet Resolution on January 1, 2015**.
2. **Fund Distribution Power**: NITI Aayog does **NOT allocate funds or plan grants to states**. Fund transfers are governed strictly by the **Ministry of Finance** based on the constitutional **Finance Commission's** recommendations.
3. **12th Plan Completion**: The 12th Plan was **NOT terminated in 2014**; it completed its full 5-year cycle until **March 31, 2017**, although NITI Aayog was established on Jan 1, 2015.
4. **Governing Council Composition**: The Governing Council of NITI Aayog comprises the Prime Minister, Chief Ministers of all States and UTs with legislatures, and Lieutenant Governors of other UTs.
