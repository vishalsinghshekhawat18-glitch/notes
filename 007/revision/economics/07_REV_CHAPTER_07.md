## 1. Master Comparative Matrices

### Matrix A: Payments Banks vs. Small Finance Banks (Nachiket Mor Committee, 2014)

| Dimension | Payments Banks (PBs) | Small Finance Banks (SFBs) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Mission** | Small savings, remittances, payments | Small-ticket credit delivery to unserved micro-units |
| **Deposits Permitted** | **Demand Deposits ONLY** (Savings/Current) | **Both Demand & Time Deposits** (CASA, FDs, RDs) |
| **Individual Deposit Cap** | **Capped at ₹2,00,000 per customer** | **No cap** |
| **Lending Activity** | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED FROM LENDING** | **PERMITTED TO LEND** (Focus on MSMEs & small farmers) |
| **Credit Cards** | **CANNOT issue credit cards** (Debit cards OK) | **Can issue credit cards** |
| **Priority Sector Lending**| Not applicable (cannot lend) | **75.0% of ANBC** must go to PSL |
| **Asset Deployment** | Min **75% of demand deposits in G-Secs/T-Bills** | Standard CRR & SLR; **$\ge 50\%$ loans up to ₹25 Lakh** |
| **Minimum Capital** | **₹100 Crore** | **₹200 Crore** |

### Matrix B: Basel III Capital Standards vs. RBI Mandates in India

| Capital Tier / Ratio | Basel III Global Standard | RBI Mandate in India | Function / Loss Absorption |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1)** | 4.5% of RWA | **5.5% of RWA** | Purest equity, absorbs going-concern losses |
| **Minimum Tier 1 Capital** | 6.0% of RWA | **7.0% of RWA** | CET1 + Additional Tier 1 (AT1 perpetual bonds) |
| **Minimum Total Capital (CRAR)** | **8.0% of RWA** | **9.0% of RWA** (12% for PSBs) | Tier 1 + Tier 2 (subordinated debt/provisions) |
| **Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB)**| 2.5% of RWA | **2.5% of RWA (in CET1)** | Mandatory cushion; curbs dividends if breached |
| **Total Capital Requirement + CCB** | **10.5% of RWA** | **11.5% of RWA** | Complete minimum regulatory fortress |
| **Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)** | $\ge 100\%$ | $\ge 100\%$ | High-Quality Liquid Assets for **30-day stress** |
| **Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)**| $\ge 100\%$ | $\ge 100\%$ | Stable funding over **1-year horizon** |

## 2. Systemic Banking Architecture Summary

$$\mathbf{\text{Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)}}: \quad \text{Center: } \mathbf{50\%} \quad \Big| \quad \text{Sponsor Bank: } \mathbf{35\%} \quad \Big| \quad \text{State Govt: } \mathbf{15\%} \quad (\text{PSL Target} = 75\%)$$

## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)
1. **Payments Bank Lending Trap**: Payments Banks **CANNOT lend** and **CANNOT issue credit cards** under any circumstance.
2. **Fixed Deposit Trap in PBs**: Payments Banks can accept **Demand Deposits ONLY** (no FDs/RDs), capped at ₹2 Lakh.
3. **Indian vs. Global CRAR**: Global Basel III minimum is 8.0%; **RBI mandates 9.0%** (11.5% with CCB).
4. **RoA Dropped from PCA**: Return on Assets (RoA) was removed as a trigger in the 2021 PCA revision.
