### Matrix B: The HDI Architecture (Post-2010 Methodology)

| Dimension | Indicator Used | Minimum Goalpost | Maximum Goalpost | Transformation Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Health** | Life Expectancy at Birth | 20 Years | 85 Years | Linear normalization: $\frac{\text{Actual} - 20}{85 - 20}$ |
| **Education** | 1. Mean Years of Schooling (Adults $\ge 25$)<br/>2. Expected Years of Schooling (Children) | 0 Years<br/>0 Years | 15 Years<br/>18 Years | Arithmetic average of normalized MYS & EYS indices |
| **Income** | Gross National Income (GNI) per capita (PPP \$) | \$100 | \$75,000 | **Logarithmic**: $\frac{\ln(\text{Actual}) - \ln(100)}{\ln(75,000) - \ln(100)}$ |

$$\mathbf{\text{Composite HDI}} = \sqrt[3]{I_{\text{Health}} \times I_{\text{Education}} \times I_{\text{Income}}} \quad (\text{Geometric Mean penalizes lopsided development})$$

## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)
1. **The Literacy Myth**: Adult Literacy Rate was **DROPPED** from HDI in 2010. Modern HDI uses MYS & EYS.
2. **GDP vs. GNI in HDI**: HDI uses **GNI per capita in PPP \$**, NOT GDP per capita. (GNI captures actual resident purchasing power).
3. **Logarithmic Income**: Income is log-transformed ($\ln$) in HDI to reflect diminishing marginal utility of money.
4. **MPI Indicator Weights**: A person is NOT poor by counting raw indicators; deprivations MUST be weighted! A child missing school ($\frac{1}{6}$) contributes three times more deprivation than lacking a toilet ($\frac{1}{18}$).
