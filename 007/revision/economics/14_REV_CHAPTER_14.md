## 2. Key Mathematical Identities & Ratios

$$\mathbf{\text{Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR)}} = \frac{\text{Employed} + \text{Unemployed}}{\text{Working-Age Population (15+)}} \times 100$$

$$\mathbf{\text{Unemployment Rate (UR)}} = \frac{\mathbf{\text{Unemployed Persons}}}{\mathbf{\text{Labour Force (Employed + Unemployed)}}} \times 100 \quad \Big[\mathbf{\text{Denominator Trap: NOT Total Pop}}\Big]$$

$$\mathbf{\text{Worker Population Ratio (WPR)}} = \frac{\text{Total Employed Persons}}{\text{Total Population}} \times 100$$

$$\mathbf{\text{Dependency Ratio}} = \frac{\text{Population (0–14)} + \text{Population (60+)}}{\text{Working-Age Population (15–59)}} \times 100$$

## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **Unemployment Rate Denominator**: The denominator for UR is the **Labour Force** (those working or actively looking for work), NOT the general population or working-age population.
2. **Current Weekly Status (CWS) Threshold**: Under CWS, a person is treated as employed if they worked for **as little as 1 hour on any 1 single day** during the 7-day reference week.
3. **Disguised Unemployment Definition**: It does **not** mean total output is zero; it means the **Marginal Productivity of the extra laborer is ZERO ($\mathbf{MP_L = 0}$)**. Removing them leaves total output unchanged.
4. **PLFS Conducting Body**: The Periodic Labour Force Survey is conducted by the **National Statistical Office (NSO, MoSPI)**, NOT by the Ministry of Labour and Employment or the Labour Bureau.
5. **Retrenchment Ceiling**: The Industrial Relations Code raised the prior government permission threshold for retrenchment from **100 workers to 300 workers**.
