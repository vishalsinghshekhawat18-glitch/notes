## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)

1. **Indian vs Western Secularism**: Western secularism enforces strict mutual exclusion (state and religion ignore each other); Indian secularism practices **Principled Distance** (state respects and regulates all religions equally — *Sarva Dharma Sambhava*).
2. **Kymlicka's Minorities Typology**: National minorities (indigenous/conquered) legitimately claim **self-government rights**; voluntary immigrant groups claim **polyethnic rights** (exemptions, cultural protection), not separate self-government.
3. **Classical Language Antiquity**: Criteria requires high antiquity of early texts/recorded history over **1,500 to 2,000 years**, with an original literary tradition not borrowed from another language community.
4. **Indra Sawhney 50% Cap**: In *Indra Sawhney (1992)*, the Supreme Court capped reservations at 50%. In *Janhit Abhiyan (2022)*, the Court held that the **10% EWS quota under the 103rd Amendment** is a valid exception over and above the 50% ceiling.
