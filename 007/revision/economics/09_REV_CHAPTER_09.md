## 3. High-Yield Examiner Traps (Quick Scan)
1. **States & T-Bills**: State Governments **CANNOT issue Treasury Bills**. T-Bills are issued exclusively by the **Central Government**.
2. **Masala Bond Risk**: The **overseas investor bears the currency risk**, shielding the Indian issuer from rupee depreciation.
3. **T-Bill Coupon**: T-Bills have **ZERO coupon payments**; they are issued at a discount to face value and redeemed at par.
4. **Retail Direct**: Individuals can buy G-Secs directly via an RDG account with the RBI without intermediary broker commissions.
