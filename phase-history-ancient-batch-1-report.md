## 5. Canonical Concepts Created (9 Concepts)

1. **`CON-HIST-01`:** *Prehistoric India: Human Evolution, Stone Age Cultures & Food Production*
   - Paleolithic stages (Hathnora hominin skull, Attirampakkam 1.5 Mya Acheulian tools), Mesolithic transition (microliths, Bagor & Adamgarh animal domestication, Bhimbetka rock art), Neolithic revolution (Mehrgarh continuous farming 7000 BCE, Burzahom pit dwellings & dog burials, South Indian ash mounds), Chalcolithic cultures (Ahar-Banas, Jorwe).
2. **`CON-HIST-02`:** *Harappan Civilisation: Chronology, Geography & Burnt-Brick Urban Planning*
   - Tripartite chronology (Early 3300–2600 BCE, Mature 2600–1900 BCE, Late 1900–1300 BCE), cardinal extremities (Manda, Daimabad, Sutkagan Dor, Alamgirpur), gridiron street planning, standard 1:2:4 burnt-brick ratio, Citadel vs Lower Town, covered drainage systems with soak pits, Great Bath of Mohenjo-daro (bitumen waterproofing), and granary food security.
3. **`CON-HIST-03`:** *Harappan Economy, Craft Metallurgy, Script & Long-Distance Trade*
   - Agrarian foundation (Banawali terracotta plow, Kalibangan ploughed field, Dholavira reservoirs, cotton cultivation), lost-wax bronze casting (*Dancing Girl*), steatite bead factories (Chanhudaro, Lothal), binary weight series ($1\dots 640$) and decimal higher units, long-distance trade with Mesopotamia (*Meluhha*, *Dilmun* Bahrain, *Magan* Oman).
4. **`CON-HIST-04`:** *Harappan Society, Material Religion, Seals & Archaeological Interpretations*
   - Material religious iconography: Pashupati seal (Elephant, Tiger, Rhino, Buffalo + 2 Deers), Mother Goddess figurines, Pipal worship, fire altars at Kalibangan & Lothal; logo-syllabic boustrophedon script (375–400 signs, Dholavira 10-sign signboard); egalitarian burial customs without precious treasures.
5. **`CON-HIST-05`:** *Harappan Decline Theories, Ecological Shifts & Late Harappan Phase*
   - Historiographical evaluation of decline: rejection of Wheeler's Aryan invasion; evidence for climatic aridity (pollen cores from Sambhar/Didwana lakes), Saraswati/Ghaggar drying, tectonic shifts, trade collapse; transition to Late Harappan rural cultures (Cemetery-H in Punjab, Jhukar in Sindh, Rangpur in Gujarat).
6. **`CON-HIST-06`:** *Early Vedic Period: Sources, Geography & Rigvedic Pastoral Society*
   - Rigveda Samhita (1,028 hymns, 10 Mandalas), Sapta-Sindhu geography (Saraswati as *Naditama*, rare mention of Ganga), pastoral-nomadic cattle economy (*Gau, Gomat, Gavishti*), egalitarian tribal polity (*Jana, Rajan*), democratic popular assemblies (*Sabha, Samiti, Vidatha, Gana*), high status of women (Upanayana, assembly participation, hymn composers Apala/Ghosha), Battle of Ten Kings (*Dasrajan War* on Parushni/Ravi).
7. **`CON-HIST-07`:** *The Later Vedic Transformation: Iron Technology, PGW & State Formation*
   - Eastward expansion into Ganga-Yamuna Doab (*Aryavarta*), material catalyst of iron metallurgy (*Shyama-Ayas*) and Painted Grey Ware (PGW), shift to sedentary agriculture, rise of territorial *Janapadas*, autocratic kingship (*Samrat*), decline of popular assemblies (Vidatha vanished, women barred from Sabha), 12 royal *Ratnins* (Bhagadugha tax collector, Sangrahitri treasurer), mandatory 1/6th taxation, royal sacrifices (*Rajasuya, Ashvamedha, Vajapeya*).
8. **`CON-HIST-08`:** *Vedic Religious Evolution, Varna Crystallization & Philosophical Transitions*
   - Crystallization of the 4-fold hereditary Varna hierarchy (Purusha Sukta Rigveda X.90), 4 Ashramas (*Jabala Upanishad*), Gotra exogamy, degradation in the position of women; shift from naturalistic deities to Prajapati/Rudra/Vishnu; philosophical revolt of the Upanishads (*Vedanta*), rejection of mechanical sacrifices (*Mundaka Upanishad* "unsteady rafts"), *Atman-Brahman* non-duality (*Tat Tvam Asi*), *Karma, Samsara, Moksha*, origin of national motto *Satyameva Jayate*.
9. **`CON-HIST-09`:** *Early Vedic vs. Later Vedic Comparative Synthesis & Historical Evolution*
   - Master first-principles comparative matrix across Geography, Economy, Metallurgy, Pottery, Political Units, Assemblies, Taxation, Bureaucracy, Social Stratification, Position of Women, Deities, Rituals, and Philosophy; structural contradictions setting the material stage for the 6th Century BCE Second Urbanization and heterodox movements (Buddhism and Jainism).

## 6. Major Uncertainties & Debates Preserved (Anti-Hallucination)

1. **Harappan Script & Governance:** Explicitly labeled as undeciphered and inferential; avoided claiming definitive identification of a priest-king or imperial monarchy.
2. **Harappan Religious Labels:** Flagged that calling the Pashupati figure "Proto-Shiva" is Sir John Marshall's retrospective interpretation, while other scholars view it as a horned shaman or master of animals.
3. **Horse in Harappan Context:** Documented as disputed and marginal (terracotta figurines at Lothal, contested bones at Surkotada) rather than a central domestic animal.
4. **Harappan Decline:** Presented as a multi-causal ecological process rather than manufacturing a single monocausal explanation.
